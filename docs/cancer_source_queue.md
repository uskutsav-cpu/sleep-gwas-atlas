# Cancer-source verification queue

This discovery log keeps public cancer GWAS sources and their remaining
analysis blockers explicit. A verified download is not a licence to invent a
population prevalence, mix ancestries, infer rsIDs, or advance a downstream
readiness gate before the Phase 0 audit passes.

| Trait | Official release | Verified facts | Remaining analysis blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `breast_cancer` | [BCAC overall breast-cancer results](https://www.ccge.medschl.cam.ac.uk/breast-cancer-association-consortium-bcac/data-data-access/summary-results/gwas-summary-associations), [Zhang et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-020-0609-2) | The official BCAC page identifies this as the European-ancestry overall analysis with 133,384 cases and 113,789 controls. Its public 4,764,280,363-byte Google Drive archive was range-validated and has local SHA-256 `4f21dfc33d52753d496cd33756beed6e78e31fd6849fbd5755c406ad2c64e9c8`. It produced 8,623,039 autosomal explicit-rsID records (`breast_cancer.txt.gz` SHA-256 `900a1801455939e4bd170f3475f9f74a6fe78aab860d932da17da24391aeee0a`). The audit counted 669,140 coordinate-only records, 1,232,711 indel/multiallelic records, one injected header, one incomplete record, and 235,914 non-autosomal rows; the extractor fails if any autosomal allele conflict occurs. The registry uses the [NCI/SEER 13.0% US female lifetime risk](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/breast.html) as an explicit liability-conversion approximation. | **Curated with caveat:** the prevalence is US-population, lifetime-risk evidence—not a cohort-specific European BCAC prevalence—so it must accompany every liability-scale interpretation. |
| `prostate_cancer` | [GWAS Catalog GCST006085](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST006085), [Schumacher et al. 2018](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-018-0142-8) | The primary Catalog metadata specifies 79,148 European-ancestry cases and 61,106 European-ancestry controls. Its public B37 release is 2,534,497,125 bytes with local SHA-256 `649921348dcc20f22a86e17bf897a2f8ae93139613a393f492d97e006a923390`. A full-file audit found 20,065,528 data rows: exactly 367 unrecoverable physical records with other than the expected 16 fields, 5,887,673 non-rsID labels, 1,104,805 non-SNP alleles, and 391,229 non-autosomal records. The fail-closed materializer projects the 10 documented fields for 12,681,454 eligible rows and writes every exclusion count plus malformed example line numbers to `prostate_cancer.txt.gz.provenance.json`; it neither invents rsIDs nor reconstructs damaged statistics. The official data dictionary defines rsID, effect allele/frequency, log-OR beta, SE, p-value, and imputation r2. The data dictionary asks users to cite Schumacher et al. and acknowledge PRACTICAL. | **Curated with caveat:** source exclusions are explicit, and the registry uses the [NCI/SEER 13.2% US male lifetime risk](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/prost.html) as a documented liability-conversion approximation, not a cohort-specific European prevalence. |
| `ovarian_cancer` | [GWAS Catalog GCST004462](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST004462), [Phelan et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3826) | The original public EBI `Phelan_Archive.zip` is 3,774,309,689 bytes and has local SHA-256 `47c48d83ac9d6a072961e25021ce3e900b7dabb8401bb4ac498a9741ac8a06ab`. Its supplied data dictionary names 23 chromosome tables, specifies that the association results are in Europeans, and defines `overall` as all-invasive epithelial ovarian cancer. The materializer processed 19,911,988 source rows, retaining 14,936,299 explicit-rsID autosomal SNPs in `ovarian_cancer.txt.gz` (SHA-256 `57a0b17ca9cbd513307d1bacceab279652ed824959fdff0dda83445fe4ee9e10`). It excluded 3,102,828 non-rsID, 1,829,530 non-SNP and 43,331 incomplete rows; 458 retained tags were validated through a complementary strand match. Its `overall_OR` values are documented log-odds ratios, so they are emitted as beta without a second transform. The selected phenotype is 22,406 invasive cases and 40,941 controls; the paper's 25,509 total EOC cases includes a separate low-malignant-potential group. | **Curated with caveat:** the registry uses the [NCI/SEER 1.1% US female lifetime risk](https://seer.cancer.gov/statfacts/html/ovary.html) as a documented liability-conversion approximation, not a cohort-specific European prevalence. |
| `colorectal_cancer` | [GWAS Catalog GCST90255675](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST90255675), [Fernandez-Rozadilla et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01222-9) | The official European-only stratum contains 78,473 cases and 107,143 controls. The 689,720,973-byte GRCh37 TSV reproduced upstream MD5 `9af7cccd0eef180ca55d6bd125699385` and has SHA-256 `5247a9f31a8291d8e3203fba63cf5d8a64bab2d0b8d30a42a61531f418fb92ed`. Its 11,284,768 variants have explicit effect allele, beta, SE, and p-value fields; `rs3094315` at 1:752566 independently confirms GRCh37. EBI records a modified CC BY-NC 4.0 academic/non-commercial licence and I2 <65% filtering. | **Source and schema verified:** materialization, harmonization, and a defensible prevalence citation remain. |
| `lung_cancer` | [GWAS Catalog GCST004748](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST004748), [McKay et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.3892) | The official overall-lung-cancer European stratum contains 29,266 cases and 56,450 controls. The 733,791,985-byte GRCh37 TSV reproduced upstream MD5 `7a112797e42b9b7fd8f10e654363dbfd` and has SHA-256 `94f42820e5950dec4a0210547debe06dbedf1b34968dc51a9f90a2f0cce34105`. Its 7,884,164 variants have explicit effect allele, beta, SE, p-value, and frequency fields; `rs2977608` at 1:768253 matches NCBI GRCh37 and differs from GRCh38 position 832873. | **Source and schema verified:** materialization, harmonization, and a defensible prevalence citation remain. |
| `melanoma` | [FinnGen R9 `C3_MELANOMA_SKIN_EXALLC`](https://finngen.gitbook.io/documentation/r9/data-download), [Kurki et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-05473-8) | The promoted public Finnish register endpoint has 2,993 cases and 287,137 controls excluding all cancers. Its 757,006,258-byte GRCh38 gzip reproduced official MD5 `37b6517ad78745de6bbb4c9393b93b32` and has SHA-256 `fcda650ea871d6ad027b97e7b055303e926091d1f57dea554a070aacbf745db5`. A complete 20,167,616-row audit found zero malformed rows and valid beta, SE, P, and frequency throughout. | **Source/schema complete with caveat:** it is smaller and differently ascertained than the controlled Landi analysis, and the cancer-free control definition must accompany interpretations. A registered hg38-to-hg19 liftover is required before EUR LDSC. |

Where a source imposes terms, this repository records them without accepting
anything on the user's behalf. It keeps only reproducible acquisition
procedures and checksums; downloaded summary statistics remain ignored by Git.
