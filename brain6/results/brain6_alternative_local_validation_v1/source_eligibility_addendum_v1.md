# Pre-outcome source eligibility lock

This addendum supersedes only the tentative long-sleep source-eligibility
judgment in `method_adjudication.md`. It was fixed before reading any real
SUPERGNOVA local covariance output or protected candidate result. The running
insomnia–ADHD calculation and its frozen protocol hash remain intact.

The exact Dashti long-sleep export is missing per-SNP analyzed N, and its
release columns are not bound explicitly to a single association model.
Published descriptions and an SE-scale audit suggest a linear BOLT-LMM model
but do not establish the exact export mapping. A study-wide N argument accepted
by SUPERGNOVA does not cure that source gap. Accordingly the three long-sleep
pairs are `METHOD_INAPPLICABLE`; they will not be run or interpreted as null.

Insomnia–ADHD and insomnia–MDD retain `ELIGIBLE_SECONDARY` status with their
published total-N scalar approximation and explicit meta-analysis limitations.
The source models and signed-Z provenance for those pairs are established.
All five original pairs remain in the denominator of the predeclared
Bonferroni family: 0.05/(5 × 1,693). The 25 candidate and 20 geographic-region
crosswalks will make inapplicability explicit. The LAVA primary failure remains.

The machine-readable lock is `source_eligibility_lock_v1.json`, which records
the SHA-256 of the preceding protocol and the exact per-pair decisions.
