# Frozen five-track candidate lead signs

The complete five-track PLACO master was checked against its source-output hashes. All 27 lead entries across the fixed 25 pair-specific candidates were found exactly once with the expected pair, chromosome, interval, and `TESTED` status. The source Z signs agree between sleep and disorder for every lead in 23 candidates. One lead is discordant in each of the insomnia–ADHD chr5:87.2–88.2 Mb and long-sleep–bipolar chr17:1.3–2.3 Mb candidates. The three-lead long-sleep–schizophrenia chr17 candidate retains all three individual signs.

These are signed source-GWAS Z products, not PLACO effect estimates, local genetic covariance, genetic correlation, replication, or causal direction. `lead_directions.tsv` stores the 27 source rows; `candidate_directions_25.tsv` stores the exact 25-candidate crosswalk. `receipt.json` pins the five source files, candidate identities, code, and both outputs.
