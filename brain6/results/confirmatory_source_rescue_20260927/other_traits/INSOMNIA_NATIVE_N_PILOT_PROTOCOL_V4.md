# Insomnia native-N pilot v4: prospective engineering correction

The v2 four-worker pilot is terminal and immutable. Its worker 2 exited 1 before writing its 22-row result. The v3 postmortem established that locus 950 has zero SNP rows in both the original and native-N shards, and that LAVA raises on this empty input. V4 is a **new, explicitly outcome-aware repair protocol** frozen before its worker-2 outcome. It may reuse the exact completed v2 workers 1, 3, and 4 after hash verification, and may rerun **only worker 2**. V2/v3 paths, receipts, and statuses remain untouched.

V4 keeps the v2 source, 88 candidate-excluded loci, four original partitions, per-SNP N, fixed case-fraction approximation, LAVA 0.1.5, UKB reference, roundoff symmetry patch, `min.K=2`, prune/max-K/block settings, univariate strict gate, and original v2 advancement rule exactly. The sole intended behavioral change is that a receipt-verified zero-row gzip shard with the expected header is emitted as `NOT_RUN` with reason `NOT_RUN_NO_SNPS` before calling LAVA. A missing file, invalid receipt, malformed shard, nonzero-row inconsistency, or real LAVA exception is a `FAILED` execution state. The runner writes all worker-2 rows and a summary before a nonzero failure exit.

Advancement requires **all 88 fixed loci**, zero failures, and either at least 0.10 absolute increase in TESTED fraction over the frozen canonical insomnia results, or at least 0.25 relative reduction in `LOW_LOCAL_H2_UNDERPOWERED` NOT_RUN cells. A positive result permits only consideration of a separately frozen complete insomnia trait-only sensitivity screen. It does not admit the Jansen source to confirmatory bivariate LAVA, change canonical family counts, or promote any PLACO candidate. The source remains sensitivity-only until exact model/case fraction/overlap requirements are satisfied.

Before execution, the controller must verify frozen code/config/protocol/rule/source/README/canonical/reference/selection/materialization hashes, all 88 receipt-bound shards and input-info files, four original worker partitions, and successful workers 1/3/4 output and log hashes. It creates a new exclusive v4 SSD root, records reused outputs as symlinks to immutable v2 files, runs worker 2 once, and writes launch, exit, aggregate, comparison, decision, and v4 receipts. The v2 rule is applied unchanged by the original aggregation routine.
