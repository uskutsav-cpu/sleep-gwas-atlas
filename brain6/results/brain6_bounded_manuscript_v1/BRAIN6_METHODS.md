# Methods and output boundaries

We used the frozen [25-candidate geographic membership](../loci/five_track_cross_pair_region_reconciliation_v1/pair_candidate_members.tsv) and joined it by exact candidate ID to the [canonical LAVA and Yale crosswalk](../lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/BRAIN6_FINAL_REGION_EVIDENCE.tsv). We required exact agreement on candidate, pair, and geographic-region IDs and required all protected promotions to remain `NO`. The source-bound [v6 region table](../exploratory_five_track_v6/geographic_region_evidence.tsv) supplies the 20 geographic aggregates, independent LD and signed-direction diagnostics, and already audited context counts. The deterministic builder is [build_brain6_bounded_manuscript_v1.py](../../scripts/build_brain6_bounded_manuscript_v1.py); [table provenance](table_provenance.json) binds every input, output, and script byte by SHA-256. It refuses a changed existing output.

For replication, the [global pair master](../replication/replication_master.tsv) was attached by trait pair and explicitly labeled global, never locus-specific. The [predeclared Yale candidate lookup](../lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/pair_candidate_replication.tsv) was attached by exact candidate ID. Its source is a phenotype-adjacent ≥10-hour UKB+MVP meta-analysis, and the original long-sleep phenotype is ≥9 hours versus 7–8 hours. The 3/14/2/6 classifications are reused unchanged; no new threshold or nearby-variant proxy was selected from these results. No variant-level SleepChart result was available under the documented [access audit](../lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/LONG_SLEEP_REPLICATION_REPORT.md).

For QTL context, we grouped [published GTEx brain molecular credible-set memberships](../exploratory_five_track_v5/gtex_brain_qtl_credible_sets/candidate_molecular_credible_set_memberships.tsv) by pair-specific candidate, counted distinct candidate SNPs and dataset/credible-set pairs, and reported the maximum published molecular PIP separately for gene expression and LeafCutter splicing. We also counted cached [nominal exact-lead eQTL rows](../exploratory_five_track_v4/lead_eqtl_context/lead_eqtl_associations.tsv), with no significance threshold or causal interpretation. Their `brain_cortex` and `brain_frontal_cortex` labels are **source tissue descriptions**, not tissue enrichment. Published molecular PIP is not GWAS PIP. Neither a published molecular credible set nor a lead eQTL P value substitutes for regional GWAS–QTL colocalization.

We joined exact-lead [Ensembl regulatory features](../exploratory_five_track_v6/lead_regulatory_context/lead_regulatory_features.tsv), [nearest positional genes](../exploratory_five_track_v1/b_annotation/nearest_gene.tsv), and [official Catalog trait-context records](../loci/gwas_catalog_candidate_leads_v1/trait_relevant_context.tsv) by candidate ID. Regulatory features are exact coordinate overlaps; nearest genes and Catalog rows are descriptive. We did not infer a causal gene, tissue, pathway, or shared variant from these annotations.

The [method-readiness audit](../exploratory_five_track_v3/method_readiness_20260927.md) found seven hashed dense GWAS files but no reviewed trait-specific sample-size/case-fraction contract for every source, no verified full regional allele-aligned LD matrices, and no `susieR` or `coloc` in the pinned R runtime. It found no locally bound verified full-cis QTL summary datasets and no reviewed gene weights, enrichment background, pathway family, or multiplicity plan. Thus GWAS SuSiE, trait–trait coloc, GWAS–eQTL/sQTL coloc, tissue/cell-type enrichment, and pathway enrichment are recorded as **not estimable under current verified inputs**. [Explicit method tables](method_tables_addendum.provenance.json) carry their own source hashes and do not invent a PIP, PP.H4, enrichment P value, or negative biological call. These are method states, not adverse test results.

We did not rerun LAVA or change any v3, v2, roundoff, pilot, source, or protected candidate output. The original seven-trait LAVA family, thresholds, and 25 candidate identities retain their prior frozen meaning. Any confirmatory extension must use the [guarded restart procedure](CONFIRMATORY_RESTART.md) after exact-source admission and a prospectively frozen pilot decision.
