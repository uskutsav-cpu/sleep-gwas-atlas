# Limitations and non-estimated analyses

1. The frozen canonical LAVA v3 family failed its predeclared 5% maximum untested-cell QC (3,720 versus 873); it is complete as a run but cannot promote local-rg or final region tiers. Three candidate pairs passing both isolated univariate gates do not change this family decision. No full bivariate family was run.
2. The exact public Dashti long-sleep file lacks native per-variant analyzed N and a direct released-file model manifest. Its GWAS Catalog mirror is byte-identical. Historical constant effective N and the explored constant total N are not admitted substitutes for verified source semantics. The 88-locus linear pilot did not meet its prospective advance rule.
3. Yale's ≥10-hour UKB+MVP long-sleep result differs from the ≥9-hour original phenotype and overlaps UK Biobank. The candidate-level lookup is exploratory and cannot establish independent replication. The six unavailable leads are not inferred from nearby P values. FinnGen directional replication applies only to a global insomnia–ADHD correlation, not any of its six candidate regions.
4. Independent genotype LD and signed-direction QC contain explicit exceptions: six out-of-range raw candidate signed correlations and 24 missing exact frozen-reference alleles. No clipping or backfilling was used. Independent 1000G LD is a geographic/variant diagnostic, not a trait-level sharing test.
5. The existing seven dense GWAS files are hash-bound, but trait-specific N and binary case-fraction semantics have not all passed method-specific review. Full regional, signed, allele-aligned LD and pinned `susieR`/`coloc` are unavailable in the audited environment. GWAS fine-mapping and trait–trait colocalization therefore have no valid current estimate.
6. Published GTEx molecular credible sets and nominal exact-lead eQTL records are incomplete substitutes for full-cis QTL summary statistics and reviewed GWAS/QTL N, build, alleles, priors, and overlap. No GWAS–eQTL or GWAS–sQTL colocalization was estimated; molecular PIP is not GWAS PIP.
7. Nearest genes, prior Catalog associations, exact-lead regulatory overlaps, and source-tissue labels are descriptive. There is no reviewed locus-to-gene weighting, tissue or cell-type background, pathway collection, or multiplicity family for enrichment. No causal gene, tissue, cell type, or pathway is assigned.
8. Candidate selection followed global screens, and the 20 geographic groups can contain multiple pair-specific candidates. Neither the 25 rows nor 20 groups are independent discovery claims. A confirmatory extension must preserve pair identity and account for its prespecified family and selection context.
