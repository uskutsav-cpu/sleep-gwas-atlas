# Bounded Brain6 results for manuscript drafting

**Status: exploratory regional evidence with a failed confirmatory LAVA family.** The seven-trait canonical v3 LAVA family completed all 17,465 intended trait–locus evaluations: 13,745 `TESTED`, 3,720 `NOT_RUN`, and zero numerical `FAILED`. The frozen maximum was 873 `NOT_RUN`; the family is `FAILED_QC_NOT_PROMOTED`. No bivariate local genetic correlation was promoted. Long sleep contributed 1,291 of the `NOT_RUN` cells. [Canonical status and frozen receipt](../lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/BRAIN6_CONFIRMATORY_STATUS.md).

The frozen five-pair PLACO screen retains **25 pair-specific candidates in 20 geographic regions**. The [candidate table](candidate_evidence_25.tsv) preserves each pair row and its canonical local-h² status; the [region table](region_evidence_20.tsv) groups overlapping coordinates only for descriptive display. Three of the 25 pair rows had both strict canonical univariate gates pass, but this cannot rescue the failed family or establish local genetic correlation. All 25 remain without a final shared-locus tier. PLACO P values remain candidate-screen statistics, not proof of a shared causal variant.

Independent genotype LD and signed-direction work has been completed as a bounded diagnostic. Across the candidate-variant table, 2,686 rows were inspected; six raw signed correlations failed range QC and 24 lacked an exact frozen-reference allele. These exceptions remain visible in the [v6 source report](../exploratory_five_track_v6/region_evidence_report.md). All 27 exact lead rows passed signed-LD checks. Five cross-pair independent 1000G genotype LD edges were measured; this supports coordinate reconciliation, not trait-trait colocalization.

The [replication table](replication_25.tsv) separates pair-level from candidate-level evidence. FinnGen ADHD yielded directional replication for the insomnia–ADHD **global** genetic correlation and therefore applies to six pair rows only as global context. No candidate has independent two-trait locus-specific replication. The separate Yale UKB+MVP ≥10-hour versus 7–8-hour file gave 3 exploratory exact-variant P/direction labels, 14 direction-only labels, 2 no-replication labels, and 6 unavailable labels across the 25 rows. Because its phenotype threshold differs and it includes UK Biobank, none is independent confirmation of the original ≥9-hour long-sleep signal. See the [frozen Yale lookup protocol and report](../lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/LONG_SLEEP_REPLICATION_REPORT.md).

For molecular context, exact PLACO candidate variants from six pair rows intersect published GTEx brain eQTL or sQTL credible sets. The [QTL table](qtl_context_25.tsv) preserves candidate-variant counts, distinct molecular sets, source tissues, and **molecular** PIP; it records GWAS PIP and GWAS–QTL colocalization as unestimated. The chr11:61.1–62.1 Mb long-sleep–bipolar geographic region has five candidate variants in a published brain eQTL set, maximum molecular PIP 0.199, and an exact-lead enhancer coordinate overlap. The chr17:43.0–45.3 Mb long-sleep–Parkinson/schizophrenia group has extensive molecular-set overlap, but it contains 1,845 candidate variant rows, so overlap count is strongly affected by candidate density. The chr15 group has three candidate variants in published molecular eQTL sets with maximum molecular PIP 0.0022. These are hypotheses for later source-complete follow-up, not validated shared loci.

The [regulatory and gene table](regulatory_gene_25.tsv) retains positional nearest-gene entries, 24 trait-relevant GWAS Catalog association records, and six Ensembl regulatory feature overlaps at five exact leads. Coordinates and prior trait associations are context only. The [tissue table](tissue_context_25.tsv) names the GTEx brain cortex and frontal-cortex source groups where observed; no tissue, cell-type, or pathway enrichment was estimated. Explicit [fine-mapping](fine_mapping_25.tsv), [trait–trait coloc](trait_trait_coloc_25.tsv), [eQTL/sQTL](eqtl_sqtl_evidence_25.tsv), and [pathway](pathway_20.tsv) tables record observed descriptive context and each unestimated method state. An absent estimate is neither a null association nor a negative biological finding.

**Bounded finding:** the completed work identifies reproducible candidate locations and exploratory molecular or regulatory context for some of them. It does not establish a confirmatory shared local genetic signal, causal variant, gene, tissue, cell type, or pathway. The [claim ledger](BRAIN6_MANUSCRIPT_CLAIMS.md) gives allowed wording for manuscript use.
