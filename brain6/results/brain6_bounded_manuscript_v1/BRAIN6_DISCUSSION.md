# Discussion for a bounded manuscript

The completed Brain6 analysis shows why a candidate map and a confirmatory shared-local-genetics claim must be kept separate. Five locked sleep–brain-disorder PLACO screens yielded 25 pair-specific candidate locations in 20 coordinate groups. Cross-pair reconciliation, independent genotype LD, source-classified replication, published molecular credible sets, exact-lead regulatory coordinates, and prior-study context sharpen the descriptive map. None overrides the frozen canonical LAVA family decision: 3,720 of 17,465 univariate cells were `NOT_RUN`, above the 873-cell ceiling, so the prespecified local-correlation confirmation was withheld.

Long sleep was the largest technical constraint, with 1,291 `NOT_RUN` cells. Source auditing traced its historical summary-statistic `N` to a constant balanced-equivalent value derived from study-level case and control counts, while the native Dashti file has no variant-level analyzed N. The source methods support a primary BOLT-LMM analysis, but the released file lacks the exact per-variant N/model binding required to assert that a new LAVA representation is source-faithful. A prospectively chosen 88-locus, source-verified total-N linear pilot produced 44 `TESTED` loci, unchanged from canonical, and failed its advance criterion. This supports retaining the source blocker rather than selecting a numerically favorable N representation. Even a perfect long-sleep repair alone would leave 2,429 family `NOT_RUN` cells, so additional trait-level repairs would be required.

The descriptive molecular examples are useful for prioritizing what to test after a source-valid rescue. The chr11 long-sleep–bipolar region combines published molecular eQTL-set overlap with an enhancer coordinate overlap. The broad chr17 group contains many candidate–molecular-set intersections but also many candidates, making raw count an especially poor causal ranking. The published molecular posterior probabilities belong to QTL fine-mapping and do not measure posterior support for a GWAS causal variant or cross-trait colocalization. Tissue source labels likewise show where a QTL dataset was measured, not where a trait mechanism acts.

The current manuscript can report a reproducible, exploratory candidate atlas and a transparent failed confirmatory test. A claim of shared local genetic correlation, replicated locus, causal variant/gene, or enriched tissue/pathway awaits its own validated evidence. The [claim ledger](BRAIN6_MANUSCRIPT_CLAIMS.md) and [limitations](BRAIN6_LIMITATIONS.md) define those boundaries explicitly.
