# Brain6 LAVA multi-trait source triage — 2026-09-26

This is a separate, outcome-blinded source feasibility audit. The canonical v3 LAVA family remains `FAILED_QC_NOT_PROMOTED`; its receipts, thresholds, and outputs were not edited. The Track B admission and five-track PLACO candidate loci also remain unchanged. No association statistic, local-h² value, or downstream frailty result was used for the source decisions below.

## MDD: PGC 2025 European release without 23andMe

The public [PGC MDD2025 Figshare release](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/GWAS_summary_statistics_for_major_depression_PGC_MDD2025_/27061255) provides file ID 51487019 and cohort sidecar ID 51486995. Both downloaded files match their published MD5 hashes, recorded in `brain6/config/lava_multitrait_feasibility_v1/mdd2025_source_screen.json`. The source header declares GRCh37 and has position, rsID, effect/non-effect alleles, effect estimates, per-variant effective N, frequency, and imputation information. Its 75-row EUR cohort sidecar totals 412,305 cases and 1,588,397 controls; these cohort sums are descriptive and are **not** substituted for each variant's `NEFF`. Phenotype definitions vary among clinical, electronic health record, questionnaire, and self-report cohorts. The source includes UK Biobank and historical PGC cohorts, so it is a *sensitivity candidate*, not an independent replication of the locked MDD GWAS.

The predeclared source-only screen `mdd2025_source_coverage_v1.json` used exact reference rsID, GRCh37 position, unordered allele equality, IMPINFO ≥0.9, MAF ≥0.01 computed from case/control frequency and counts, NEFF ≥100, and exclusion of palindromic variants. It read no effect, standard error, or P-value column. Of 7,363,302 source rows, 5,615,536 passed and matched the sealed UKB LAVA reference exactly. At least one passing variant covers 2,494/2,495 frozen loci; five loci have fewer than 30. The locked Howard 2019 MDD input has 1,122,235 exact reference overlaps after its own frozen QC, so source coverage is about fivefold higher under **different** QC pipelines. This is a technical opportunity, not a prediction of local-h² eligibility.

**Decision:** MDD2025 passes source identity, format, and reference-coverage triage for a separately predeclared trait-only LAVA pilot. It is not admitted to the canonical v3 family. A future bivariate analysis would also require candidate-specific LDSC overlap matrices because sample overlap with sleep and other disorder GWAS cannot be assumed zero.

## Parkinson disease: GP2 2025 European meta-GWAS

The [GP2 publication and data statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11957085/) link the public summary-statistics download page. The portal lists “GP2 Parkinson's disease 2025 GWAS: European ancestry” with a [summary-statistics archive](https://api.kpndataregistry.org/api/d/7j5797) and [README](https://api.kpndataregistry.org/api/d/HbrExY). The README, captured with SHA-256 `ed5188d2b5029648b5d42ec46121302cbf6fc72f253399e0c60ab6f83e5b4a4a`, describes six summary-statistic files. The EUR-only and combined files use **hg38**, whereas the sealed LAVA loci and reference use **GRCh37**. Its listed columns include effect allele frequency, effect estimate, standard error, and study count, but **no per-variant sample N**. It says the released data were filtered for MAF >0.01, I² <80, and presence in ≥50% of studies. The publication-wide 63,555 cases, 17,700 proxy cases, and 1,746,386 controls cannot be assigned to the EUR-only file or to every variant.

**Decision:** do not feed the GP2 archive directly into frozen GRCh37 LAVA inputs. First obtain an exact file identity and cohort/variant sample-size semantics, and perform a documented allele-aware liftover with a source-specific QC receipt. No GP2 association data have been downloaded for this audit.

## Family gate remains decisive

The unchanged seven-trait 5% ceiling is 873/17,465 `NOT_RUN` cells. The current family has 3,720. The independent arithmetic `lower_bounds.json` proves that even *perfect* replacement of MDD alone is insufficient; the minimal four-trait perfect set is insomnia, long sleep, MDD, and Parkinson disease. The observed continuous-duration candidate has 876 `NOT_RUN` by itself and cannot satisfy the whole-family ceiling even if all six others were perfect. No candidate region has a final LAVA tier, and no downstream gated analysis is authorized by these source checks.
