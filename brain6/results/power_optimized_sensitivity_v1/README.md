# Brain6 sleep GWAS power audit (outcome-blinded, current evidence)

Audit date: 2026-09-26. Scope: Brain6 only. Canonical LAVA v3 is read-only and remains `FAILED_QC_NOT_PROMOTED`. The checksum-verified continuous-duration candidate passed Brain6-only normalization QC, was materialized against all 2,495 frozen loci, and completed its four-shard trait-only local-h² screen. This audit uses the frozen canonical aggregate, its per-trait cause table, and Brain6 GWAS metadata. It does not use or import any frailty/FI sensitivity result.

## Scope correction

The canonical v3 family has seven inputs, but only two are sleep GWAS: insomnia and long sleep. ADHD, bipolar disorder, MDD, Parkinson disease, and schizophrenia are disorder GWAS. The instruction's reference to “seven sleep phenotypes” is therefore corrected to the two actual Brain6 sleep phenotypes.

## Current-trait technical audit

The reportable corrected two-trait table is `current_sleep_trait_audit_complete_v2.tsv`, with canonical SNP-h² and interpolated local-h² quartiles copied from the hash-bound seven-input audit, plus exact chromosome-matched reference-ID overlap. Its provenance is `current_sleep_trait_audit_complete_v2.provenance.json`. The earlier `current_sleep_trait_audit_complete.tsv` is retained as a historical intermediate; `current_sleep_trait_audit.tsv` is the original upstream table. “TESTED” is processable local-h² output, not significant evidence. The strict frozen per-cell gate is p < 0.05/17,465 = 2.8629e-6; it is shown separately. Median and quartiles describe TESTED loci only. Aggregate genome-wide SNP-ID overlap does not replace per-locus min-K eligibility in canonical receipts.

By exact SNP-ID intersection with the sealed LAVA reference, insomnia has 6,061,267/6,077,635 source variants (99.73%) and long sleep has 6,057,783/6,549,769 (92.49%). Source and reference hashes are bound in `canonical_sleep_reference_overlap_v1.provenance.json`; these counts do not establish allele/effect harmonization. Long sleep is the weakest-powered of the two Brain6 sleep phenotypes by objective local-h² processability: 1,204/2,495 loci tested (48.26%) versus insomnia 1,824/2,495 (73.11%), a 24.85 percentage-point gap. Long sleep has 1,291 NOT_RUN (51.74%), of which 1,271 are low-local-h² and 20 fail shared-reference minimum K; there are no other failures. Insomnia has 671 NOT_RUN (26.89%), of which 651 are low-local-h² and 20 fail shared-reference minimum K. The pattern points primarily to local-h² support, with the same smaller reference-K burden for both traits. Strict univariate p-threshold passes are 1 long-sleep locus and 0 insomnia loci; this stricter count is not the same as processability. Neither result promotes local-rg analysis.

## Candidate search and predeclared decision rule

Before considering downstream associations, define a materially promising candidate as at least a 10 percentage-point gain in loci passing the frozen strict local-h² p gate (p<2.8629e-6) across the same 2,495 frozen loci (at least 250 additional gate-passing loci). Processability (a finite local-h² result) and strict-gate eligibility are reported separately. This strict-gate improvement only qualifies a candidate for a full sensitivity run; canonical thresholds, the 5% family missingness ceiling, and all downstream gates remain unchanged. A 10-point improvement alone does not pass canonical family QC.

The continuous Dashti 2019 sleep-duration GWAS is a scientifically related supplement candidate: N=446,118 EUR, GRCh37, same 24-hour self-report study as the canonical >=9-hour tail. The continuous measure and the long-sleep binary tail are different estimands and share UK Biobank participants, so it must not silently replace long sleep or be treated as independent replication. The primary article confirms N=446,118 for continuous duration and 34,184 cases versus 305,742 controls for >=9-hour sleep. Summary statistics are listed through the GWAS Catalog (GCST007561). The source archive (`77ebce3f…d7c8`) and extracted member (`b1115038…fdda0`) were SHA-256 verified; Brain6 normalization retained 6,344,850 unique variants after QC, and reference matching found 6,093,758 (96.04%) across the sealed panel. All 2,495 frozen loci were materialized and screened in four disjoint shards under the canonical p threshold and unchanged min-K/local-h² settings. Results: 1,619 loci had finite local-h² output, 876 were `NOT_RUN` (all low local h²), zero failed, and 7 passed the strict univariate gate. Relative to canonical long sleep, finite-output processability improved by 415 loci (+16.63 percentage points) and low-local-h² `NOT_RUN` decreased by 395 (31.08%), but strict-gate passes increased only from 1 to 7 (+6 loci, +0.24 percentage points). This falls below the predeclared +250-locus (+10-point) promotion criterion; no full bivariate sensitivity family was launched. No new local-rg results exist.

The Austin-Zimmerman 2023 EUR >=10-hour candidate is a closer binary phenotype but has 15,962 cases and 382,950 controls (effective N≈61,293), about half the canonical long-sleep effective N≈122,985; its stricter threshold further narrows the phenotype. It is not a power improvement. The exact author-listed EUR file's genome build/field semantics and participant overlap also remain unverified. The larger Jansen 1.33M insomnia meta-analysis is not usable as a replacement because the full meta-analysis summary statistics are not publicly available; Brain6 already uses its available UKB-only release.

Watanabe et al. 2022 report a 2,365,010-person European insomnia meta-analysis (593,724 cases; 1,771,286 controls). Its primary data statement makes the full UKB GWAS and only the top 10,000 23andMe SNPs public; full 23andMe data require qualified access, and the full combined meta-analysis is not available. The public UKB component (N=386,988) is essentially the same cohort and sample size as Brain6's current UKB-only insomnia GWAS (N=386,533), so it does not constitute a meaningful power upgrade. It is not used as a replacement.

An Austin-Zimmerman et al. 2025 medRxiv preprint reports a larger continuous-duration analysis across UKB and the Million Veteran Program (646,218 in Results; 648,125 in Abstract/Introduction). The quantitative duration construct is relevant, but the MVP question explicitly includes naps and its response categories differ from UKB. A fresh primary-source check on 2026-09-26 confirms the record remains a preprint: [medRxiv v1 states](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.05.19.25327902v1) that study-produced data will be available on reasonable request after peer-reviewed publication, and [PubMed indexes it as a preprint](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40475156/). The [current GWAS Catalog sleep-duration search](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/search?query=sleep+duration) shows the 2023 Austin-Zimmerman paper but no 2025 accession; no independently downloadable 2025 summary-statistics file, build, variant schema, or effect fields could be verified. UKB source overlap with canonical long sleep and the FI GWAS (which uses UKB plus Swedish TwinGene) is known at cohort level; exact participant intersections are unknown. Despite the larger reported N, this candidate fails the current usability and ancestry/reference-compatibility gates. Do not treat it as an available replacement; reconsider only if a checksum-verifiable file is released with documented fields and a defensible ancestry-specific subset.

## Recommendations and runtime summary

- Insomnia: **KEEP** the current UKB-only GWAS; no accessible, technically qualified equivalent with independently verified greater local-h² eligibility is established.
- Long sleep: **SUPPLEMENT WITH SENSITIVITY** only as a completed, outcome-blinded trait-only diagnostic. Do not promote a full bivariate sensitivity: the candidate adds 6 strict-gate loci, below the predeclared 250-locus threshold. Keep the canonical >=9-hour result unchanged; do not describe the continuous trait as a replacement, validation, or independent replication.
- The complete six-candidate comparison is `candidate_replacement_screen_complete_v2.tsv`, with provenance in `candidate_replacement_screen_complete_v2.provenance.json`; the older candidate table remains preserved as history. Runtime/result summary: 5 candidates reviewed in the original screen, with the device-sleep exclusion and completed Dashti screen incorporated in the six-row final table; 2 potentially higher-N related continuous-duration candidates identified; 1 candidate independently acquired, checksum-verified, normalized, and reference-matched; 1 trait-only local-h² screen completed across 2,495/2,495 loci; 0 candidates met the predeclared full-sensitivity promotion criterion; 0 full bivariate sensitivity families launched/completed; 0 new local-rg cells or estimates; 0 downstream loci eligible for follow-up. The current power-screen evidence supports increased processability for a related continuous estimand, while strict local-h² gate eligibility remains sparse. No downstream biology was run because canonical v3 failed family QC and no candidate passed its objective promotion rule.
