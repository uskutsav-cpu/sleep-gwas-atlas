# External blocker: source-valid exact long sleep

The public Dashti ≥9 h versus 7–8 h file has no per-variant analyzed N, `NMISS`, case counts, or model identifier. An independent GWAS Catalog copy is byte-identical. The study methods and a coauthored paper support primary BOLT-LMM statistics, while LAVA's binary path reconstructs logistic Wald effects from the supplied Z, per-SNP N, and case proportion. Neither constant `N_eff=122,985.40891841166` nor constant total `N=339,926` is established as the actual sample count for every SNP or as a justified BOLT-to-LAVA transformation. The prospective 88-locus linear diagnostic did not improve primary testability. No exact-phenotype richer public release or rerun was verified in the ledger.

**Frozen arithmetic:** canonical LAVA has 3,720 NOT_RUN cells against a maximum of 873. Long sleep accounts for **1,291**. Even if every other trait became perfectly TESTED, the family would retain 1,291 NOT_RUN, **418 above** the maximum. A passing family needs at least 2,847 cells repaired overall. Even perfect long sleep alone leaves 2,429, so at least 1,556 additional cells from other traits would still be needed. The source issue is a necessary blocker, not the only family issue.

**Blocked:** admission of a new exact long-sleep confirmatory input; prospective full-family repair; candidate-region promotion; all downstream work that depends on passed confirmatory LAVA. Existing frozen v3, roundoff, v2, receipts, hashes, and pilot outputs remain intact. Exploratory phenotype-adjacent lookups and source-first audits of other binary traits can proceed independently.

No email was sent. A local documentation search found no evidence of an existing Brain6-approved UK Biobank application or technically available authorized individual-level environment. An authorized investigator may verify their institutional UK Biobank access; without that, an exact rerun requires the normal UK Biobank application route. No credentials or restricted participant data were accessed.
