# Prospective exploratory long-sleep lookups (v1)

Frozen before the full Yale-file lookup. This branch cannot enter the confirmatory LAVA source slot. The 2023 Austin-Zimmerman UKB+MVP European meta-analysis contrasts **≥10 h with 7–8 h**, excludes obstructive sleep apnoea, includes UK Biobank participants, and therefore differs from and overlaps the Dashti ≥9 h source. Its public [Yale file](https://medicine.yale.edu/lab/gelernter/stats/) is genome-wide GWAS summary statistics with `rsid,CHR,BP,A1,A2,Freq1,N,Effect,P`, not PRS weights. The preserved Brain6 geography comprises 20 regions and 25 pair-specific candidates (26 distinct lead variants). No candidate, region, or source is selected by these results.

Use only exact rsID **and** GRCh37 chromosome/position matches for variant lookup. Compare `A1/A2` to Dashti `ALLELE1/ALLELE0` and reverse the Yale sign if effect alleles swap. Exclude allele mismatches, ambiguous A/T and C/G variants, and any lead absent from the original file from direction claims. Report exact P and the smallest descriptive P in each prespecified region. The latter does not establish LD proxy support; no LD proxy classification is made without a verified reference calculation.

For each pair-specific candidate, classify an aligned exact lead with P < 0.05/25 and concordant sign as `EXPLORATORY_EXACT_VARIANT_REPLICATION`; otherwise a region with minimum P < 5e-8 as `EXPLORATORY_LOCUS_REPLICATION`; otherwise a present aligned lead with concordant sign as `EXPLORATORY_DIRECTION_ONLY`; otherwise a present aligned lead as `NO_REPLICATION`; otherwise `UNAVAILABLE`. These are descriptive evidence labels in a phenotype-mismatched, overlapping cohort. Preserve every raw P, alignment, and missing status. Neither statistical threshold nor outcome can change frozen confirmation or promotion.

Source: [Austin-Zimmerman et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41249-y), [official Yale download page](https://medicine.yale.edu/lab/gelernter/stats/). Script: `brain6/scripts/build_exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1.py`.
