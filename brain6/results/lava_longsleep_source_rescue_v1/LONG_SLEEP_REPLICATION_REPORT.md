# Exploratory phenotype-adjacent long-sleep evidence

The analysis was [frozen before lookup](exploratory_replication_protocol_v1.md). The downloaded [Yale/Austin-Zimmerman 2023 EUR UKB+MVP file](https://medicine.yale.edu/lab/gelernter/stats/) has SHA-256 `0e1c2701b4b7e0c008308b28cf85741eb565785677c9ce4b2ee7c5b0537b2964`. Its 7,152,485 variants have columns `rsid, CHR, BP, A1, A2, Freq1, N, Effect, P`; all rows report `N=390570`. The [paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41249-y) reports 445,966 EUR participants across cohorts and defines long sleep as **≥10 h versus 7–8 h** after excluding sleep apnoea. The file's constant `N` is reported as a file fact, not interpreted as exact participant count or used for LAVA. The cohort includes UK Biobank, so this is phenotype-adjacent overlapping evidence, **not independent replication** or a confirmatory replacement.

All **20** frozen geographic regions had at least one Yale variant. Among the **25** preserved pair-specific candidates (26 distinct leads), 20 distinct exact rsID + GRCh37-position matches were found, with allele alignment against the original Dashti file. The predeclared candidate labels were: **3** `EXPLORATORY_EXACT_VARIANT_REPLICATION`, **14** `EXPLORATORY_DIRECTION_ONLY`, **2** `NO_REPLICATION`, **6** `UNAVAILABLE`; **0** `EXPLORATORY_LOCUS_REPLICATION`. The smallest P across any prespecified region was `9.185e-06`, above the descriptive locus threshold `5e-8`. The three candidate-level exact-variant labels occur at `rs199443` (longsleep–Parkinson, P `5.432e-05`), `rs62054437` (one of three longsleep–schizophrenia leads, P `0.0001173`), and `rs6466056` (longsleep–schizophrenia, P `6.13e-05`); their aligned effects agree with the Dashti sign. These labels only mark the prespecified P < 0.002/direction rule in overlapping, differently defined data.

The complete [25 candidate rows](exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/pair_candidate_replication.tsv), [26 lead lookups](exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/lead_variant_lookups.tsv), [20 geographic rows](exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/geographic_region_replication.tsv), and [hash/scan provenance](exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/provenance.json) preserve unavailable leads, actual P values, and allele status. No LD proxy calculation was performed, so a nearby minimum P must not be called an LD proxy.

[SleepChart 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10524-5) uses >8 h versus 6–8 h in UK Biobank and reports REGENIE v4.1 for its binary sleep GWAS. Its [portal](https://labs-laboratory.com/sleepchart/gwas/long) links Synapse `syn70524096`; anonymous Synapse metadata were visible, but the file download endpoint returned HTTP 403, so there is **no SleepChart variant-level replication result**. A permitted Synapse account or author-hosted open mirror would be needed to add this separate exploratory source. [PGS Catalog score PGS019923](https://www.pgscatalog.org/score/PGS019923/) was inspected to the scoring-file header: its `effect_weight` is a score weight with no GWAS P/SE/N, and was not analyzed as a GWAS.

The canonical LAVA family and all protected slots are unchanged. None of these exploratory observations affects source admission or candidate promotion.
