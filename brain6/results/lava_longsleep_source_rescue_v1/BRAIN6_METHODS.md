# Methods for the terminal source-rescue audit

The exact phenotype was frozen from the [Dashti 2019 primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08917-4), supplement/methods, and official release README, then SHA-bound in [contract v1](phenotype_contract_v1.freeze.json). Original Broad and EBI ZIPs were SHA-256 compared; the raw ZIP member was streamed once for header, row count, missing-token count by field, chromosome distribution, and native sample-size/model fields. Historical Brain6 harmonizer code and QC receipt were traced to calculate and identify constant balanced-equivalent N. The installed LAVA 0.1.5 namespace was compared at R-function level to pinned upstream source and traced through `read.sumstats.file`, `get.input.info`, `process.locus`, `process.binary`, and `univariate.test`; equations and references are in [LAVA semantics](LAVA_SAMPLE_SIZE_SEMANTICS.md).

The earlier 88-locus pilot was prospectively selected without PLACO candidate intervals, split into four workers for each predeclared arm, audited against terminal receipts/hashes, and adjudicated by its frozen 10-percentage-point advance rule. We reused its terminal result rather than rerunning it. Full-family projected bounds were calculated from the frozen 3,720 NOT_RUN and 873 ceiling before any full rerun; none was authorized.

For exploratory replication, [v1 lookup rules](exploratory_replication_protocol_v1.md) were committed before the full Yale file scan. All 7,152,485 rows of the public ≥10 h UKB+MVP EUR meta-analysis were scanned. We required matching rsID and GRCh37 position, exact nonpalindromic allele alignment, and used the original Dashti effect to assess sign. We kept raw exact P and minimum P within each frozen geographic region; exact-variant labels used P < 0.05/25 and concordant direction. The descriptive regional threshold was 5e-8. No LD proxy inference, independent-cohort claim, or confirmatory promotion was made. [Machine-readable provenance](exploratory_long_sleep_replication_v1/provenance.json) binds the source hashes, row count, N values, and label counts.

Source searches used official study, GWAS Catalog, Yale, Synapse, PGS Catalog, institutional, and primary publication pages. The source ledger records verified identity and remaining unknowns; no candidate was admitted on a secondary citation alone. Local project documentation was searched for approved UK Biobank access, without accessing credentials or restricted data.
