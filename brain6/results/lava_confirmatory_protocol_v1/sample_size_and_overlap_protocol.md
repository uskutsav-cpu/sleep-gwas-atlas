## LAVA implementation facts and interpretation

The pinned LAVA source snapshot is commit `e729a245f7b6923967a96804fbf5246eadf2d6c6` of [LAVA](https://github.com/josefin-werme/LAVA/tree/e729a245f7b6923967a96804fbf5246eadf2d6c6), captured byte-for-byte under `source_docs/`. Its README defines summary-statistics `N` as **number of samples**, and its input-info `cases` and `controls` define case fraction. `get.input.info` sets `prop_cases = cases/(cases+controls)`; `process.locus` passes each SNP's `N` and that fraction into `process.binary`, while the locus-mean `N` enters sampling variance. `process.binary` explicitly sets `N.case = N * case.prop` and genotype counts from `N` and reference allele frequency. Input-info total `N` does not replace per-SNP sumstats `N`. Thus `N` and case fraction form a joint model and cannot be chosen independently by whichever increases local-h² testability. The [LAVA overlap vignette](https://github.com/josefin-werme/LAVA/blob/e729a245f7b6923967a96804fbf5246eadf2d6c6/vignettes/sample_overlap.md) permits an LDSC-intercept-derived sampling correlation when overlap is unknown; it does not make an old source-pair matrix portable to changed cohorts.

For a directly analyzed case-control cohort with ordinary logistic GWAS, use the source-documented per-variant actual total `N_i = cases_i + controls_i` only when the input-info case fraction accurately represents those analyzed variants. Because LAVA accepts one trait-wide fraction, substantial variant-wise case-fraction variation or heterogeneous cohort mixture requires a documented approximation and sensitivity assessment before admission. A source that supplies only study-wide counts cannot have them assigned to every SNP without evidence that all SNPs used those samples. `N_eff = 4 N_case N_control/(N_case+N_control)` is the sample size of a **balanced-equivalent** study, not its literal total. A meta-analysis sum of cohort effective sizes may differ from effective size computed after pooling counts. For such a source, `N_eff` with fraction 0.5 is a candidate effective-cohort model only if source methods and LAVA methodology support that specific representation; `N_eff` with the observed aggregate fraction, or counts-summed total with aggregate fraction, is not automatically valid. Unsupported transformations remain inadmissible even if a diagnostic pilot looks strong.

For continuous traits, establish whether reported `N` is observed per-variant participants, a fixed analyzed cohort, effective `N`, or a mixed-model proxy; record phenotype transformation, covariates, and scale. Do not map mixed-model effective sample size to literal `N` without source/method justification. Missing per-variant `N` can be filled with a fixed `N` only with source evidence that variant sample size is effectively fixed after QC, and a predeclared exclusion/sensitivity rule for missingness.

The [PGC MDD2025 release](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/GWAS_summary_statistics_for_major_depression_PGC_MDD2025_/27061255) reports per-variant `NEFF`, `NCAS`, and `NCON`, with 75 heterogeneous European cohorts including UK Biobank; its [publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11829167/) describes balanced-equivalent effective sample size. A read-only source-row audit found `NEFF / (4*NCAS*NCON/(NCAS+NCON))` from 0.8593 to 0.9529. The prior 111-locus pilot used `NEFF` with a fraction from summed cohort cases/controls. This combination lacks an established joint LAVA interpretation, so **MDD2025 status is SCIENTIFIC_HOLD**. Its 108/111 numerical `TESTED` result is diagnostic only. Do not promote or launch a full MDD2025 rescue until a source- and method-supported representation is independently established. Predetermined same-locus sensitivity runs may compare `NEFF`/0.5, `NEFF`/aggregate fraction, and `NCAS+NCON`/aggregate fraction to expose model dependence, but may not select among them by testability or association outcome. The method-supported choice must be decided first; otherwise the source is inadmissible.

## Source admission, specified before pilot outcomes

For each candidate, store original URL/accession, access date, publication, license/access terms, exact file hash, source README/methods snapshot hash, cohort list, phenotype definition, ancestry, cases/controls, total and per-variant `N` semantics, summary-statistic model and effect scale, transformation and covariates, genome build, imputation/reference, variant IDs, allele/effect conventions, and known/possible cohort overlap with *each* paired Brain6 source. Missing fields are explicit `UNKNOWN`, never inferred from a filename. Prefer original consortium releases, GWAS Catalog, FinnGen, published UK Biobank and major consortium releases, in that order of documentation strength. A changed phenotype, ancestry, proxy-case definition, or source cohort must be labeled as a sensitivity rather than silently treated as identical. Require a source-supported mapping of the GWAS statistic to LAVA's `STAT`, `N`, and case fraction before association-level pilots. Reject candidate sources with unresolved critical `N`/binary-model semantics, inaccessible terms, incompatible trait meaning, or no auditable build/allele mapping. A technically coverable file is not scientifically admitted.

The locked original seven source cards are historical canonical inputs. A rescue rerun must re-audit every input whose semantic assumption or cohort covariance matters; it cannot declare a replacement valid just because the original workflow used a similar convention. Preserve original cards and source files. No candidate-specific or downstream locus result is consulted during admission.

## Input and pilot QC

Stream large files to `/Volumes/Extreme SSD` in a new versioned directory. Verify published checksum where available, then record SHA-256. For each admissible source, establish an allele-aware GRCh37 coordinate and reference mapping; verify effect/non-effect alleles against the sealed UKB reference, strand flips and ambiguous variants, duplicate policies, INFO/MAF filters, finite P/beta/SE/Z consistency, `N` ranges/meaning, row counts, reference intersections, and per-locus shared SNP counts. Preserve input, settings, software, source, and QC hashes. Do not alter the sealed reference or historical runs.

Use a source-only coverage screen first, with no association columns. Then run a small, predetermined trait-only pilot across representative loci sampled from the frozen locus index (including both canonical `TESTED` and `NOT_RUN` strata, but excluding all 25 pair-specific candidate identities from selection). Freeze the list, settings, binary `N` model, case fraction, and acceptance criteria before reading pilot outcomes. Compare technical failure rates, shared-SNP counts, local-h² validity and numerical stability to the same canonical loci. The pilot can reject an input or justify broader feasibility estimation; it cannot revise the source's `N` model. Use uncertainty on projected residual `NOT_RUN`, not an optimistic point estimate, to decide whether a multi-trait full run is plausible. Never launch an MDD-only full run as a family rescue: even perfect MDD repair leaves 3,131 `NOT_RUN`.

## Pair-specific sample overlap

Maintain a pair-by-pair cohort intersection ledger for `insomnia__adhd`, `insomnia__mdd`, `longsleep__scz`, `longsleep__bipolar`, and `longsleep__parkinson`. For any changed source or material sample-composition change, estimate fresh pair-specific sampling covariance from correctly munged, source-matched cross-trait LDSC intercepts, with diagonal intercepts from the same source versions, or obtain a method-supported known overlap covariance. Record LDSC versions, reference, regression SNP list, h²/intercept quality, intercept SE, positive-definiteness and matrix hash. Do not reuse the frozen original matrices for a replaced pair; do not assume zero overlap from incomplete cohort lists. A trait-only pilot does not require a pair matrix; bivariate LAVA does. If no defensible covariance can be obtained, leave that pair blocked and do not promote it.

## Terminal decision

Only after all admitted sources, source-specific `N` representations, overlap matrices, harmonization settings, trait list, LD reference, thresholds, and hashes are frozen in a new pre-run receipt may the complete seven-trait rescue run. Resume verified shards without overwriting them. Audit all 17,465 cells and all worker receipts. Apply the original family gate exactly; bivariate and promotion logic runs only after legitimate whole-family PASS_QC. A failed gate yields a versioned residual cause matrix and another prospectively justified repair iteration. Retain this protocol v1 and every historical result unchanged.
