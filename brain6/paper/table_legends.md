# Table legends

**Table 1. GWAS source metadata.** Frozen source registry and archived replication provenance for the unique GWAS in the selected Brain6 tracks, augmented with a derived availability overlay. Original-source archive/container paths and checksums are distinct from harmonized dense-input paths and checksums; the frozen `gwas_master.tsv` is unchanged. Acquisition dates remain unknown without dated receipts, and the table is checksum-bound by its provenance record. Missing files and unresolved metadata are stated explicitly.

**Table 2. Locked global map.** All 72 sleep × brain disorder rg results extracted from the original atlas. `original_BH_FDR_q` and significance status are inherited from the 396-test family.

**Table 3. Heritability and QC.** Sleep and disorder h² estimates, standard errors, LDSC intercepts, SNP overlap, and pair QC fields present in the source atlas. These fields do not imply local sharing.

**Table 4. Post-atlas deep-follow-up selection.** Existing reviewed primary selections, eligibility, source availability, and overlap caveats. Alzheimer's remains `NO_ELIGIBLE_PAIR`.

**Table 5. Replication coverage.** The archived FinnGen R13 directional replication for insomnia–ADHD and explicit non-attempted status for the remaining selected pairs. `NOT_ATTEMPTED` must not be interpreted as a negative replication. The separate published context for long sleep–schizophrenia is in Supplementary Table S18 and is not admitted as independent replication.

**Supplementary Table 16. Interim headline-claim sensitivity coverage.** A checksum-bound, interim ledger of the current headline claims and sensitivity status, including the partial four-pair PLACO MHC candidate filter. `NOT_RUN` and `IN_PROGRESS` remain explicit. It is not a complete robustness assessment, a full-family result, or a final freeze.

**Supplementary Table 17. Software, resources, and versions.** Runtime, package, reference, operating-system, and repository-commit snapshot for the current analysis environment. `NOT_INSTALLED`, `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`, and `NOT_RUN` entries are explicit and do not represent negative scientific results.

**Supplementary Table 18. Published long-sleep–schizophrenia context.** A published European-ancestry estimate is compared descriptively with the locked Brain6 estimate. The sleep definitions and contributing cohorts differ, UK Biobank overlap is possible, and the exact PGC input crosswalk is unresolved; this is not admitted as independent replication.

Tables S6–S15 are not generated; Table S16 is available as an explicitly interim sensitivity-coverage table. See `supplement.md` for the exact stage-coverage reason.
