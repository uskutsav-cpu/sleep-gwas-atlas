**Table S21 — Partial PLACO signed reference-LD audit:** `../results/loci/placo_candidate_signed_ld_partial.tsv`, with provenance in the adjacent `.provenance.json`. **Table S22 — Partial PLACO candidate grouping after LD range QC:** `../results/supplement/table_S22_partial_placo_valid_ld_rebuild.tsv`. It summarizes the separately versioned range-filtered rebuild; full candidate rows, merged intervals, and pair-specific invalid-edge records are in `../results/loci/placo_candidate_variants_valid_ld_partial_v2.tsv`, `placo_candidate_loci_valid_ld_partial_v2.tsv`, and `placo_candidate_ld_range_exceptions_partial_v2.tsv`. The rebuild produces 25 candidate lead signals rather than the prior 21, while the 19 broad merged intervals are unchanged. The unusually frequent range exceptions make lead independence unresolved; neither table is a complete five-track family or a final independent-locus set. Provenance and reference/input hashes are in `../results/loci/placo_candidate_valid_ld_partial_v2.provenance.json`; rebuild with `make -C brain6 placo-valid-ld`.

**Table S23 — Partial PLACO effect signs and signed-LD consistency:** `../results/supplement/table_S23_partial_placo_signed_effect_ld.tsv`; candidate-level rows and provenance are in `../results/loci/placo_candidate_signed_effect_ld_partial_v1.tsv` and the adjacent `.provenance.json`. **Table S27 — Pair-context univariate audit:** `../results/supplement/table_S27_lava_pair_context_univariates.tsv` summarizes the receipt-verified baseline and separate roundoff pair-context outputs against the canonical trait-only v3 values; all 36,819 source rows and hashes are recorded in `../results/lava/lava_pair_context_univariates_v1.tsv` and adjacent provenance.
