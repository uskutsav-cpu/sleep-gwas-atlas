# Data availability

The global 72-pair table and figures are derived from the repository's locked atlas results and are included under `brain6/results/global/`. The selected GWAS metadata and source URLs are recorded in `brain6/manifests/gwas_master.tsv`; derived archive and harmonized-input availability is recorded in `brain6/manifests/gwas_external_availability.tsv` and Supplementary Table S1. All seven selected dense source files named by the reviewed source cards have been verified against their expected size and SHA256 on the mounted external research archive (six exact archive matches and one Parkinson's gzip source whose decompressed bytes match the frozen source checksum). The 21 normalization, pair-join, pair-index, and chunk-preparation receipts have passed validation; detailed provenance is retained in `brain6/manifests/locked_dense_input_audit.tsv`, `brain6/qc/source_archive_audit.tsv`, and external scratch. The raw and harmonized files are not redistributed in this repository. Their acquisition dates remain unknown where no dated receipt exists. The four PLACO+ v3 pair outputs are published with pair-level QC PASS and independently audited five-track q values; the protected Track B output is absent, so the PLACO family remains incomplete. The corrected canonical LAVA v3 family completed with all 17,465 cells verified, but 3,720 `NOT_RUN` cells exceeded the frozen 873-cell limit; its immutable decision is `FAILED_QC_NOT_PROMOTED`. The separate roundoff run remains incomplete. Neither PLACO nor LAVA is presented as a promoted final Brain6 analysis in this global-map manuscript. The archived FinnGen R13 ADHD replication estimate and ingestion provenance are retained under `results/track_b/replication/` and summarized in `brain6/results/replication/`.

No statement is made that all underlying GWAS data are redistributed or available from this package. Access conditions and source URLs must be checked at the authoritative source before reacquisition. This global-map manuscript package is not a complete data release; completed preparation receipts do not imply that downstream Brain6 analyses are complete.
