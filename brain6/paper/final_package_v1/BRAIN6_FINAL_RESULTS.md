# Brain6 final results: transparent secondary-validation paper

## Prespecified primary endpoint and candidate universe

The locked global atlas reports 35 significant entries in the selected 72-row display, inheriting the correction from the original 396-test family; the 72 rows were not corrected as a new family. The completed five-track PLACO screen generated **25 pair-specific candidates in 20 overlapping geographic regions**. These are association-screen candidates, not 25 independently confirmed shared loci. Their identities and geographic grouping are preserved in the [candidate](BRAIN6_FINAL_CANDIDATES.tsv) and [region](BRAIN6_FINAL_REGIONS.tsv) tables.

The prespecified seven-trait canonical LAVA v3 family contains **17,465 trait–locus cells: 13,745 `TESTED`, 3,720 `NOT_RUN`, and zero numerical `FAILED`**. Its frozen maximum was 873 `NOT_RUN`; the decision is `FAILED_QC_NOT_PROMOTED`. No bivariate local genetic correlation or protected candidate was promoted. The [complete canonical-status copy](BRAIN6_FINAL_LAVA.tsv) preserves every cell and explicitly records `NOT_ADMITTED` rescue. Three isolated candidate pairs passed both **strict** univariate local-h² gates, as recorded in the [bounded 25-candidate source table](../../results/brain6_bounded_manuscript_v1/candidate_evidence_25.tsv); the family decision prevents a confirmatory local claim. The [canonical source report](../../results/lava_longsleep_source_rescue_v1/BRAIN6_CONFIRMATORY_STATUS.md) and [family projection](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/phase5_family_projection_v2/summary.json) remain the primary endpoint receipts.

## Source repair and secondary local covariance

The source-first audit found that the exact ≥9-hour versus 7–8-hour Dashti long-sleep release lacks a released per-SNP analyzed-N field and an explicit mapping from its exported statistics to one model. Its public mirror and nine local containers did not resolve that contract. Long sleep alone contributes **1,291** canonical `NOT_RUN` cells, exceeding the entire 873-cell ceiling even if every other trait were repaired perfectly. The unique smallest hypothetical perfect-repair set is long sleep, insomnia, Parkinson disease, and MDD; it would leave 533 cells. These are arithmetic upper bounds, not achieved recoveries. The [source ledger](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/TERMINAL_BLOCKER_AND_RESTART.md) records each remaining trait's model/N issue.

The corrected insomnia native-per-SNP-N pilot reused three receipt-verified workers and reran only the zero-row worker partition. Locus 950 is correctly `NOT_RUN_NO_SNPS`, not `TESTED` or a swallowed failure. Across the unchanged 88-locus selection, the v4 result was **47 `TESTED`, 41 `NOT_RUN`, zero `FAILED`**, versus 44/44/0 canonical: three additional tested loci, a 3.41 percentage-point gain. Its low-h² `NOT_RUN` reduction was 6.98%. Both improvements were below the frozen advance alternatives of 10 percentage points or 25% relative, so the terminal decision was `NO_MATERIAL_PILOT_IMPROVEMENT`; no full insomnia replacement was admitted. The [v4 receipt and result](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/other_traits/INSOMNIA_NATIVE_N_PILOT_V4_RESULT.md) leave validated family recovery at zero and projected `NOT_RUN` at 3,720.

A separately frozen SUPERGNOVA analysis supplied a **secondary same-source local-covariance check**, not a substitute LAVA endpoint or independent cohort replication. It retained all five pairs and 1,693 non-MHC blocks per pair in the family denominator (`0.05/8,465 = 5.906674542232723e-06`). Only insomnia–ADHD and insomnia–MDD met the prospective source-eligibility gate; each completed all block slots using four workers. Across them, 3,304 blocks were estimated, 61 were low-SNP, and 21 were low-rank. The three long-sleep pairs were `METHOD_INAPPLICABLE` for 5,079 block slots and were never computed. Three estimated blocks passed the family threshold: one insomnia–ADHD block at chr11:112,459,489–114,257,728 and two insomnia–MDD blocks at chr6:100,630,147–102,636,772 and chr11:112,459,489–114,257,728. **None overlaps a protected pair-specific candidate interval.** Of 25 candidates, 12 are `NOT_SUPPORTED` under this method and 13 are `METHOD_INAPPLICABLE`; **0/25** are `SUPPORTED_FWER`. The [full block table](BRAIN6_FINAL_ALT_LOCAL_BLOCKS.tsv), [candidate crosswalk](BRAIN6_FINAL_ALT_LOCAL_VALIDATION.tsv), and [method result](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/RESULT.md) give all numerical and inapplicable states.

## Exploratory locus and molecular results

Numerically invalid stored LAVA LD matrices stopped the prespecified SuSiE-RSS route: **0/32** reviewed matrices passed the frozen LD gate. A separately frozen, single-causal-variant approximate Bayes-factor analysis evaluated 28 of 50 candidate–trait units. It produced **24 model-conditional 95% credible sets**, four units without sufficient posterior association, nine input-QC holds, and 13 long-sleep model holds. Credible-set sizes ranged from 3 to 1,445 SNPs (median 53); these are exploratory conditional posteriors, not confirmed causal-variant PIPs. [Full 50-row table](BRAIN6_FINAL_FINE_MAPPING.tsv).

Six insomnia–ADHD candidate intervals qualified for full single-signal trait–trait coloc H0–H4 calculations. At frozen `p12=10^-5`, three had descriptive ABF-model PP.H4 ≥0.80: chr3:117.14–118.14 Mb (0.9207), chr5:103.45–104.45 Mb (0.9911), and chr7:113.57–114.57 Mb (0.8432). At the prespecified lower `p12=10^-6`, only the chr5 interval remained ≥0.80 (0.9174); the chr3 and chr7 values fell to 0.5373 and 0.3496. The other 19 pair candidates were not estimated: six for dense-input QC and 13 for long-sleep source semantics. These ABF findings do not provide multi-signal or LAVA confirmation. [Coloc table](BRAIN6_FINAL_TRAIT_COLOC.tsv), [prior sensitivity](BRAIN6_FINAL_TRAIT_COLOC_PRIOR_SENSITIVITY.tsv), and [method audit](../../results/brain6_exploratory_finemap_coloc_v2/RESULTS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).

Official eQTL Catalogue release-7 brain panels supported **four actual component-GWAS–QTL single-signal tests** at one insomnia–ADHD interval, using the ADHD component only: three eQTL and one sQTL. Their maximum PP.H4 was **0.0824**; none supported a shared GWAS–QTL variant under the frozen priors. No pair-level GWAS–QTL coloc was estimated. Published molecular credible-set membership in six pair rows and six exact-lead Ensembl regulatory-feature rows across five candidates remain descriptive coordinate evidence, as do nearest genes and GWAS Catalog context. [eQTL](BRAIN6_FINAL_EQTL_COLOC.tsv), [sQTL](BRAIN6_FINAL_SQTL_COLOC.tsv), [regulatory](BRAIN6_FINAL_REGULATORY.tsv), and [functional report](../../results/brain6_exploratory_functional_v1/REPORT.md).

No candidate has source-verified, phenotype-compatible, nonoverlapping **two-trait locus replication**. FinnGen supplies directional *global pair* context for insomnia–ADHD; the Yale long-sleep lookup uses an adjacent ≥10-hour phenotype and UKB-overlapping data. Neither is independent locus replication. [Replication table](BRAIN6_FINAL_REPLICATION.tsv).

The full 20-region positional enrichment failed its prospective matched-control gate in two regions (four and seven eligible anchors versus the required ten), so it has no full-set p-values. A separately frozen **18-region/21-candidate sensitivity** generated 10,000 accepted nonoverlapping matched null sets and tested all 54 GTEx v8 bulk tissues and 1,680 Reactome pathways. None reached BH q<0.05 (minimum tissue q=0.11339; minimum pathway q=0.24264). No cell-resolved or regulatory-feature enrichment was estimable from a reviewed panel/background. These are positional subset results and cannot be extrapolated to all 20 regions. [Tissue](BRAIN6_FINAL_TISSUE_CELLTYPE.tsv), [pathways](BRAIN6_FINAL_PATHWAYS.tsv), and [enrichment report](../../results/brain6_exploratory_enrichment_v3/REPORT.md).

## Final classification

The explicit pair-specific classes are **A `PRIMARY_LAVA_CONFIRMED`: 0; B `ALTERNATIVE_LOCAL_VALIDATION_SUPPORTED`: 0; C `PLACO_PLUS_REPLICATION_SUPPORTED`: 0; D `EXPLORATORY_MULTIOMIC_SUPPORTED`: 0; E `UNSUPPORTED`: 25**. Class E means that none of the predefined A–D positive gates was met. It does not assert absence of association or biology; exploratory ABF, molecular overlap, and annotation values remain separately reported. Geographic-region class is the highest member pair-specific class, never an assertion that all merged members share that class. The [claim ledger](BRAIN6_FINAL_CLAIMS.md) distinguishes primary, secondary, exploratory, descriptive, and unsupported wording.
