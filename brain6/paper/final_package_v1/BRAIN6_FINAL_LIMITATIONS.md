# Brain6 limitations and interpretation boundaries

1. **Primary family QC failed.** Canonical LAVA was numerically complete as a 17,465-cell accounting exercise, but 3,720 cells were `NOT_RUN` against a frozen maximum of 873. Zero numerical `FAILED` cells do not turn untested cells into valid tests. No bivariate local-rg family or protected promotion was authorized.
2. **Exact source semantics limit confirmatory rescue.** The Dashti ≥9-hour versus 7–8-hour long-sleep release lacks per-SNP analyzed N and an exact export-model mapping. Its public mirror is byte-identical; alternate sleep phenotypes do not replace the protected slot. Insomnia has source per-SNP N but no variant-specific case fraction or exact regression command; its fixed-fraction native-N pilot missed its predeclared advance rule. Parkinson proxy cases, MDD missing variant N, and PGC/ADHD meta-analytic effective-count conventions remain unresolved for LAVA substitution. Projected validated recovery is zero.
3. **Secondary method is same-source and partly inapplicable.** SUPERGNOVA used the same discovery GWAS, a 503-person EUR LD panel, and published scalar total N for eligible meta-analyses. Cohort-level participation varies. The three long-sleep pairs had no admissible method-specific N/model mapping and were held before outcomes. Its 12 candidate `NOT_SUPPORTED` labels mean only that no overlapping estimated block passed the prospectively fixed family threshold; 13 candidate results are inapplicable. This is not independent-cohort replication, a proven absence of local covariance, or a LAVA-equivalent endpoint.
4. **Candidate selection and geography constrain inference.** Five PLACO screens selected the 25 candidates, and several candidates share a geographic group. The 20 merged rows cannot be counted as independent replications; their displayed class is the highest member's pair-specific class. PLACO significance does not imply a common causal SNP or biological pleiotropy. Signed lead Z products describe source association directions only. Among 12 secondary-method-eligible candidates, reference-aligned lead-to-best-block direction could be compared for four (all concordant); eight lacked an aligned comparison and 13 long-sleep candidates had no local direction. The [direction audit](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/candidate_direction_audit.tsv) preserves missingness.
5. **Fine-mapping and trait coloc are model conditional.** None of 32 stored LAVA LD matrices passed the fixed SuSiE numerical gate. The ABF alternative assumes at most one causal variant per trait in each interval and does not condition multiple signals. Its credible-set PIPs are neither SuSiE PIPs nor confirmed causal-variant probabilities. Three default-prior trait-coloc H4 values met a descriptive 0.80 gate, but two fell below it at the lower prespecified `p12`; none is a confirmatory shared-variant result. Nine trait-unit input-QC holds and 13 long-sleep model holds remain explicit.
6. **QTL evidence is limited to one component and four tests.** eQTL Catalogue regional slices cover four brain contexts, but only the ADHD component at one candidate passed all predeclared gates. The highest QTL PP.H4 was 0.0824. The insomnia component and pair-level QTL sharing were not estimated; a low component H4 does not exclude all regulatory mechanisms. QTL `sdY` was estimated, and single-signal assumptions and variant matching add uncertainty. Published molecular credible-set membership is a QTL result, not a GWAS fine-map or coloc result.
7. **Replication and annotation remain descriptive.** FinnGen supplies global pair-direction context, while Yale's adjacent long-sleep phenotype overlaps UKB. No phenotype-compatible, cohort-disjoint, two-trait regional replication was demonstrated. Exact-lead regulatory features, nearest genes, source-tissue labels, and prior Catalog records are coordinate and literature context, not causal gene or tissue assignments.
8. **Enrichment is a subset sensitivity.** Two of 20 regions lacked the required matched-control pool, so no valid full-20 enrichment P-values were calculated. The separately frozen 18-region set contains 21 of 25 pair candidates; its 54 tissue and 1,680 pathway tests are positional and multiplicity-corrected, with no q<0.05. Exclusion relates to regional matching feasibility, so subset p-values cannot be generalized to all candidates. No reviewed cell-resolved or regulatory-feature universe/background supported those additional tests.
9. **Scope of final class E.** `UNSUPPORTED` means no predefined A–D positive evidence gate was met in this frozen package. It is not an assertion of biological absence, no global association, no PLACO association, or no future value. The exploratory measurements remain reported without promoting them into the frozen primary tier system.
