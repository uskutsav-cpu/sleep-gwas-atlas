# Brain6 discussion

The atlas identifies **prespecified candidate locations for follow-up**, while its prespecified local-genetic confirmation failed a family-wide testability gate. This distinction determines the paper's main claim. The completed global and PLACO screens support genetic-association candidates across the selected sleep–disorder pairs. They do not, by themselves, establish local genetic correlation, a shared causal variant, or a causal pathway at any protected region.

The LAVA failure is informative about method readiness, not evidence that all candidate regions lack shared genetics. The exact long-sleep public release has no direct per-variant analyzed-N field or explicit exported-model contract, and long sleep contributes 1,291 untested canonical cells. The repaired insomnia source yielded three additional tested loci in its prospectively fixed pilot, yet the gain missed both advance rules. Consequently, combining promising isolated loci or favorable pilot outcomes would have violated the frozen family decision. The unchanged 3,720 `NOT_RUN` projection is the defensible primary result.

The secondary local-covariance branch reached a complete **methodologically applicable** endpoint for insomnia–ADHD and insomnia–MDD. Three blocks elsewhere in the genome passed the five-pair correction, showing that the method could return stringent signals. None intersected a protected PLACO candidate. This is a bounded lack of secondary candidate support under the chosen method and inputs, rather than a genome-wide null or independent replication failure. The three long-sleep pairs were declared method-inapplicable before local outcome review; their missing p-values cannot be interpreted as negative results. The secondary method uses the discovery GWAS and a study-total N approximation, so even an overlapping block would have remained secondary evidence, not a LAVA rescue or cohort-independent validation.

Exploratory downstream analyses supply specific hypotheses without changing the classification. Single-signal ABF conditional posteriors produced 24 credible sets, but the available LD failed the SuSiE route and the long-sleep source semantics held 13 trait units. The chr5:103.45–104.45 Mb insomnia–ADHD trait-coloc interval retained high PP.H4 under a lower prespecified shared-variant prior; the chr3 and chr7 model-support intervals did not. This prior sensitivity and the unverified single-causal-variant assumption preclude a strong shared-variant claim. At a different insomnia–ADHD locus, four actual ADHD-component brain QTL coloc tests had maximum PP.H4 0.0824, so the published molecular credible-set and regulatory overlaps remain positional leads rather than proof of an effector gene. No candidate has independent two-trait locus replication.

The 18-region matched positional sensitivity found no GTEx bulk tissue or Reactome pathway at its respective 5% FDR threshold. Because two regions failed the original 20-region control gate, this does not test the full candidate universe. It also cannot adjudicate cell types, regulatory activity, or causal genes. The strongest biological conclusion is therefore restrained: selected sleep and brain-disorder GWAS share broad genetic signals and nominate specific PLACO regions for future exact-source, independent-cohort, and multi-signal follow-up; **none of the 25 protected pair-specific candidates currently satisfies a primary, secondary local, independent replication, or multiomic support class**.

The path to a stronger claim is concrete. An exact-phenotype long-sleep source with variant-keyed analyzed N and explicit released-model documentation, together with source-compatible overlap and meta-analysis handling for the other high-burden traits, could enable a newly frozen complete LAVA family. Independent phenotype-matched cohort summary statistics would permit genuine regional replication. Dense, well-conditioned ancestry-matched LD and complete cis-QTL datasets could support multi-signal fine-mapping and coloc. Any such work would be a new versioned analysis with new receipts and prospective gates; the current failed primary endpoint and exploratory results remain historical facts.
