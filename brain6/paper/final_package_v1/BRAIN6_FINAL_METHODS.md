# Brain6 final methods and frozen decision boundaries

## Design, families, and protected units

The study carried forward the inherited 396-test atlas correction to its locked 72-row global display. Five preselected sleep–disorder pairs entered the completed five-track PLACO screen. Its frozen lead-significance, reference matching, clumping, and geographic reconciliation defined 25 **pair-specific** candidates in 20 geographic regions. A geographic merge is a display unit and never changes the pair-specific statistical identity. The [PLACO lock](../../config/placo_family_v3/family_lock.json), [shared-locus rule](../../config/shared_locus_rule_v1.json), [protected candidate table](../../results/loci/placo_five_track_candidate_loci_v1/candidate_loci.tsv), and [locked global table](BRAIN6_FINAL_GLOBAL.tsv) are SHA-256-bound in the final provenance.

The prespecified primary local method was canonical LAVA v3 over seven traits and 2,495 GRCh37 loci, yielding 17,465 intended trait–locus cells. The frozen family allowed at most 873 `NOT_RUN` cells. A cell with no usable SNPs or insufficient local heritability is `NOT_RUN`, not a tested null. Family QC precedes any bivariate local-rg correction or candidate promotion. The exact [canonical configuration](../../config/lava_family_canonical_v3.json), [complete cell table](BRAIN6_FINAL_LAVA.tsv), and immutable SHA-256 in [package provenance](BRAIN6_FINAL_PROVENANCE.json) bind this decision. No baseline, v2, roundoff, or sensitivity result was spliced into the canonical family.

## Source-first LAVA rescue

We reconstructed the seven-trait failure matrix and direct-source field contracts before reading new pilot outcomes. The target required at least 2,847 recovered cells to reach the frozen 873 ceiling. Candidate replacement sources were assessed for exact phenotype, cohort, ancestry, build, alleles, association model, variant-specific N or case/control semantics, LD compatibility, and sample overlap. The [trait ledger](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/other_traits/SOURCE_FIRST_LEDGER.md), [long-sleep public ledger](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/public_longsleep/SOURCE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md), and [local archive audit](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/local_archive/LOCAL_ARCHIVE_AUDIT.md) retain classifications and source hashes. Dashti's exact ≥9-hour versus 7–8-hour release was never replaced with ≥10-hour, >8-hour, continuous duration, or PRS data.

For insomnia, the original Jansen release directly labels per-SNP `N` and the A1 odds ratio. The prospectively fixed 88-locus native-N pilot retained source, LD reference, N meaning, locus selection, and four worker partitions. The v4 runner special-cased only a receipt-confirmed empty locus as `NOT_RUN_NO_SNPS`; malformed input and true runtime errors remained failed with nonzero process exits. Three successful v2 workers were reused by verified receipt; only worker 2 reran in a new output root. Its original materialization receipt was linked into a separately labeled aggregation repair, which reproduced the first aggregate hashes byte-for-byte. The unchanged decision compared native N with canonical under either ≥10 percentage-point tested gain or ≥25% relative low-h² `NOT_RUN` reduction, with zero worker failures. [Pilot protocol/result](../../results/confirmatory_source_rescue_20260927/other_traits/INSOMNIA_NATIVE_N_PILOT_V4_RESULT.md). No full confirmatory LAVA rescue was launched because the validated recovery projection remained 3,720 and the exact long-sleep N/model contract was unavailable.

## Prospectively selected secondary local covariance

The separate [method adjudication](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/method_adjudication.md) evaluated HDL-L, ρ-HESS, and SUPERGNOVA against available signed summary statistics, cohort overlap, sample-size requirements, and LD resources before inspecting local outcomes. SUPERGNOVA was selected for **observed-scale local covariance** from signed Z and a 503-person 1000 Genomes EUR GRCh37 PLINK reference. Its source is bound to the implementation and exact patch in the [frozen protocol](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/protocol_freeze.json). The reference was restricted to common, nonpalindromic, unique rsIDs, interpolated with a genetic map, and split by chromosome after a combined-map cM reset was found invalid in a pre-result runtime attempt. The original failed combined-reference log was preserved.

The [pre-outcome source-eligibility lock](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/source_eligibility_lock_v1.json) admitted only insomnia–ADHD and insomnia–MDD. Three long-sleep pairs were `METHOD_INAPPLICABLE` because a scalar study total accepted by software cannot establish the exact released model or per-SNP analyzed-N/export mapping. The two eligible pairs used published study-total N as an explicitly limited method-specific approximation, preserving meta-analysis heterogeneity; this is **not** a LAVA N admission. The analysis covered all 1,693 non-MHC Berisa–Pickrell EUR blocks per pair with four workers per eligible pair, sequentially. A block required at least 120 shared SNPs; low-SNP and low-rank blocks remained distinct non-estimates. The two-sided covariance P threshold was fixed at Bonferroni `0.05/(5 × 1,693)=5.906674542232723e-06`, including all three inapplicable pairs in the family denominator. A protected candidate could be `SUPPORTED_FWER` only if a prespecified overlapping block passed that threshold and its full eligible pair run passed receipt and completeness QC. Correlation was descriptive only when local h² estimates permitted it. The [8,465-row block table](BRAIN6_FINAL_ALT_LOCAL_BLOCKS.tsv), [pair-specific crosswalk](BRAIN6_FINAL_ALT_LOCAL_VALIDATION.tsv), and [method receipt](../../results/brain6_alternative_local_validation_v1/run_provenance.json) preserve all statuses and software/source hashes. This is independent **methodological** assessment of the same discovery GWAS, not independent-cohort replication or primary LAVA confirmation.

## Fine-mapping, colocalization, and functional context

For each of 50 candidate–trait units, the LD-first SuSiE-RSS gate checked symmetry, diagonal/range, and positive semidefiniteness of the available LAVA matrices; none of 32 assessed matrices passed. No SuSiE PIP or SuSiE-coloc posterior was produced. A distinct [pre-outcome ABF protocol](../../results/brain6_exploratory_finemap_coloc_v2/RESULTS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md) used source beta/SE and fixed single-causal-variant assumptions, prior and QC gates. The 95% credible sets and variant PIPs are conditional on that model; 0.80 was the prespecified association/description gate. Trait–trait coloc used `coloc.abf` 5.2.3 with full H0–H4 reporting, frozen `p12=10^-5`, and sensitivity at `10^-6` and `5×10^-5`. Input-level allele, density, and source-model holds remained `NOT_RUN`; absence of an estimate was never converted to zero posterior. [Independent ABF audit](../../results/brain6_exploratory_finemap_coloc_v2/integrity_provenance.json).

For molecular tests, four official eQTL Catalogue release-7 GTEx brain contexts were bounded before outcome inspection: cortex eQTL, cortex LeafCutter sQTL, frontal cortex eQTL, and frontal cortex LeafCutter sQTL. GRCh37 candidate windows were lifted to GRCh38 and variants matched by unique position and unordered alleles; raw GWAS source N/case-control fields and QTL `AN/2` were used. Each numerical test required at least 500 shared variants, a QTL P≤5×10^-8 and a component-GWAS P≤5×10^-8. `coloc.abf` 5.2.3 used `p1=p2=10^-4`, `p12=10^-5`; all H0–H4 values were kept. These are component-GWAS tests, with estimated QTL `sdY` and a single-signal assumption; they do not establish pair-level GWAS–QTL sharing or causal genes. Published molecular credible sets, nearest genes, exact-lead Ensembl features, source-tissue labels, and GWAS Catalog records were joined separately as coordinate context. [QTL protocol and receipts](../../results/brain6_exploratory_functional_v1/PROTOCOL.md).

Independent two-trait locus replication required exact phenotype compatibility, regional evidence for both traits, verified cohort disjointness, and source review. Global FinnGen correlation and the UKB-overlapping Yale ≥10-hour long-sleep lookup were not promoted to this status. For enrichment, the original frozen 20-region matched-background gate required at least ten eligible controls per region; two regions failed, so no full-set P values exist. A separately frozen 18-region sensitivity used exact-width, same-chromosome, LAVA-block-span, pair-specific PLACO tested-density and gene-count matching; 10,000 accepted nonoverlapping null sets; 54 GTEx v8 bulk tissues; 1,680 Reactome pathways; and separate Benjamini–Hochberg corrections. It is a positional subset analysis, not cell-type or causal-gene inference. [Enrichment protocol](../../results/brain6_exploratory_enrichment_v3/PROTOCOL.md).

## Evidence classes and reproducibility

Candidate classes were assigned by positive gates in order: A `PRIMARY_LAVA_CONFIRMED` only through the frozen primary rescue/promotion workflow; B `ALTERNATIVE_LOCAL_VALIDATION_SUPPORTED` only through an eligible, fully audited secondary FWER block; C `PLACO_PLUS_REPLICATION_SUPPORTED` only through a verified independent compatible two-trait regional replication; D `EXPLORATORY_MULTIOMIC_SUPPORTED` only through source-supported shared molecular/regulatory evidence beyond coordinate overlap; E `UNSUPPORTED` if no A–D gate was met. The frozen primary failure makes A impossible here. Positional QTL credible-set or regulatory overlaps alone cannot trigger D. The [deterministic final builder](../../scripts/build_brain6_final_package_v1.py) enforces anchor hashes, source identities, all 25/20 memberships, family counts, and source statuses; it writes into a temporary sibling before atomic publication. The [independent audit](../../scripts/audit_brain6_final_package_v1.py) checks hashes, rows, status counts, tiers, and document receipts.
