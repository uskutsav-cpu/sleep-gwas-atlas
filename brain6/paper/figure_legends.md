# Figure legends

**Figure 1A. Global sleep–brain genetic correlations.** Signed rg estimates for the 12 locked sleep/circadian traits and six target disorders. Color scale is centered at zero and clipped at ±0.5 for legibility.

**Figure 1B. Effect-size magnitude.** Absolute rg is encoded by color; each cell is annotated with its sign and rg estimate.

**Figure 1C. Original-family significance overlay.** Signed rg as in Figure 1A. Rings indicate significance under the original 396-test atlas family (inherited q<0.05); no new 72-test correction was calculated.

**Figure 1D. Sleep-trait profiles.** Disorder-specific rg profiles across the 12 sleep traits. Lines are descriptive summaries of the global map.

**Figure 1E. Disorder-profile comparison.** Pearson similarity of six disorder rg profiles across 12 sleep traits, displayed in descriptive average-linkage order. The clustering is exploratory and does not establish a disorder taxonomy.

**Figure 2. External global genetic-correlation check for insomnia–ADHD.** The locked atlas discovery estimate is compared with the archived FinnGen R13 estimate using pointwise normal 95% confidence intervals. Both estimates use the same insomnia GWAS; FinnGen provides the external ADHD cohort. This is directional concordance, not fully independent two-trait replication. Finnish founder-population and phenotype differences apply, and individual-level overlap was not verified. Machine-readable inputs and output hashes are in `../results/replication/figures/fig_replication_insomnia_adhd.provenance.json`.

**Supplementary Figure 1. Partial PLACO candidate intervals.** Candidate intervals defined by frozen 500-kb lead flanks, with overlapping same-pair regions merged, are shown by pair and chromosome for the four available pair-QC-passed PLACO outputs. Color encodes the best lead-signal PLACO P value; point area reflects LD-clumped candidate-variant count. The across-pair family is incomplete because the protected insomnia–ADHD Track B output is unavailable. These are descriptive PLACO-only candidates, not a complete-family independent-locus result, fine-mapped variants, or causal mechanisms. Source table, family lock, plotting code, and output hashes are bound in `../results/placo/figures/figS_partial_placo_candidate_intervals_v1.provenance.json`.
