# Translational validation

Public FinnGen R13 data supported four locked variant/pair checks at alpha 0.05/4. Three exact GRCh38 REF/ALT objects passed independent raw-JSON hash, identity and field checks. R13 uses REGENIE v3.3; native source P values are retained, while Wald intervals are approximate diagnostics.

- Chr11 rs7105462, clinical insomnia–MDD: conjunction P 0.00275816; Bonferroni-four P 0.01103265.
- Chr5 rs77960, clinical insomnia–ADHD: conjunction P 0.000346761; adjusted P 0.001387043.
- Chr6 rs9485410, clinical insomnia–MDD: conjunction P 0.0316417, above 0.0125.
- Chr11 insomnia–ADHD: ADHD association fields are null. This is NOT_ESTIMATED, not a biological null.

These are co-associations at fixed prior-selected variants under registry phenotypes. They do not establish the same causal variant, regional colocalization, phenotype-matched replication or participant independence. Discovery insomnia uses UKB self-report; FinnGen insomnia uses clinical F51.0/G47.0 diagnoses. Disorder definitions, within-FinnGen overlap and ancestry/cohort differences remain explicit.

Full summary statistics require a source form. That path was not bypassed: only advertised public-browser variant queries were used. Browser filtering/completeness does not admit dense-region analyses.

No authorized participant-level psychiatric dataset, independently validated predictor, symptom/subtype interaction or functional perturbation exists. No causal MR or therapeutic claim was added. The new evidence improves validation of a known association example, without establishing a new psychiatric mechanism or clinical utility.
