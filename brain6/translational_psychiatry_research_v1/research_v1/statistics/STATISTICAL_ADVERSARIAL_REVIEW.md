The contrast bound is `Var(X-Y)=s1²+s2²-2Cov(X,Y) ≤ (s1+s2)²`. Hence `2*Phi(-abs(X-Y)/(s1+s2))` is the maximum two-sided normal Wald P over all admissible sampling covariances. Bonferroni across a prospectively fixed or explicitly retrospective full family remains conservative irrespective of contrast dependence. Estimated SEs make this asymptotic; low heritability, unstable ratio estimates, biased marginal rg, differing phenotypes or cohorts can invalidate interpretation. It supports source-defined parameter differences, not conditional causal architecture or disorder-specific loci.

The new source-specific implementation concern is materially stronger than arithmetic replay of existing P values. It is also a reason to **withhold promotion**, not to erase historical positives or declare the underlying psychiatric relationships absent. The highest-value next work is source restoration and native full-family inference review. Strong biological or clinical conclusions remain unsupported by this statistics-only work.
