# Primary literature and current-version audit through 7 October 2026

The new public supplements materially change the prior novelty assessment. **22 of the 25 protected PLACO pair-specific windows contain a previously published lead for the same labeled trait pair.** This includes all six insomnia–ADHD and all six insomnia–MDD windows, three long-sleep–bipolar windows and seven long-sleep–schizophrenia windows. The three long-sleep–Parkinson windows remain unresolved; absence from this targeted extraction is not evidence of novelty. Candidate A insomnia–MDD and Candidate B insomnia–MDD were also already reported. Candidate A insomnia–ADHD remains unresolved as a same-pair regional claim.

The machine comparison uses frozen **pair-specific** intervals, not merged geographic display windows. [NOVELTY_MASTER.tsv](NOVELTY_MASTER.tsv) has 25 protected candidates plus the three new A/B pair slots; 24 are KNOWN_SAME_PAIR_REGION and four remain unresolved. Coordinate inclusion establishes prior regional reporting; it does not establish identical causal signals or validated source harmonization.

## Jia et al., SLEEP 2025

[DOI 10.1093/sleep/zsae209](https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/zsae209), PMID39243390. Published online September 2024, 2025 journal issue. Ordinary article access failed in the prior audit, but the publisher's publicly supplied free article and its explicitly linked Supplementary Tables S1–S16/Figures S1–S2 are now legitimately accessible. No authentication or bypass was used. Exact source acquisition URLs and hashes are in the receipts.

Supplement S3 row132 reports insomnia–MDD **rs7105462**, chr11:112912048, PLACO P=4.97e-8, inside A. S7 row132 reports ABF H4=.008; its best-causal SNP is rs61902811 with SNP.PP.H4=.075. S3 row138 reports **rs9485410**, chr6:101261007, P=4.60e-9, inside B. S7 row138 H4=.650, best-causal SNP rs12213967 with SNP.PP.H4=.088. Low A H4 and moderate B H4 are not proof of absence of shared signals under different assumptions, but they prevent describing prior regional overlap as established shared causation.

S1 identifies binary long sleep from Dashti2019, PMID30846698, with 34,184 cases and 305,742 controls. These match the original Brain6 long-sleep source ledger: self-reported >=9 h versus 7–8 h controls. S3 includes all ten protected long-sleep bipolar/SCZ windows. Nine windows have at least one exact Brain6 lead rsID; the bipolar chr15 prior rs17598603:85200211 differs from Brain6 rs1051168:85200520 while lying in the same window. The exact long-sleep phenotype labels/source improve compatibility evidence; source N/model eligibility for a new analysis remains a separate gate.

Jia S3/S7 do not report alleles. A/B positions match official Ensembl GRCh37 mappings, but these are multiallelic rsIDs. All ten selected long-sleep prior leads additionally have exact published-coordinate agreement with newly acquired official Ensembl37 records; the complete build-check file now has27 study/lead checks. Original Brain6 insomnia source and current reference genotypes establish G/A for A and T/C for B; Jia's specific biallelic representation is still unreported. The report preserves this limitation rather than treating rsID identity as complete allele attestation.

## Lin et al., Mammalian Genome 2026

[DOI 10.1007/s00335-026-10232-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00335-026-10232-5), PMID42115503, published May2026. Main text remains subscription-limited; both explicitly linked public supplements were acquired. Supplementary Methods explicitly identify FUMA/GRCh37. Table6 reports nine insomnia–ADHD and ten insomnia–MDD loci with alleles and boundaries. All twelve protected Brain6 insomnia windows recur regionally; five of six ADHD lead rsIDs recur exactly. The remaining ADHD chr5 lead is Lin rs171697 rather than Brain6 rs2431108.

Table8 reports single-causal ABF results for the MDD loci. Selected H4 values range from .429 at rs4341827 to .989 at rs30266; rs1597044 has H4=.749 and rs2121708 as its top causal SNP. No INS–ADHD rows were found in Table8, despite Table6 INS–ADHD loci; no absent posterior was invented. Source Table1 ADHD PMID30478444/year2022 conflicts with its 38,691/186,843 counts, which match Demontis2023 (PMID36702997). This inconsistency does not invalidate the observed published locus recurrence, but it blocks uncritical source-release attestation from the table alone.

## Schipper et al., updated preprint

[DOI 10.1101/2025.10.18.25338281](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.18.25338281). Official medRxiv version API identifies v1 (20October2025) and v2 (11March2026). V2 Data6 row24 reports INS–DEP LAVA at chr11:112755447–113889019: 1,355 SNPs, 126 PCs, rg=.871195, CI .60495–1, P=1.65998e-7. Data7's **right-hand** table row22 reports the same window with 2,991 SNPs: H1=.547999, H3=.445575, H4=.00642659. The different left-hand row22 is another locus and must not be substituted. Main-text family-size/alpha arithmetic and Table1 insomnia source/N bibliographic inconsistencies are flagged in the adversarial source review, not silently corrected. This overlapping-source preprint is prior art, not Brain6 replication.

## Xue et al. and wider literature

[DOI 10.1093/sleep/zsaf317](https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/zsaf317), PMID41065713. The accessible publisher abstract describes twelve psychiatric traits, MiXeR/conjFDR/ASSET and cell-type work, with 70 shared loci/97 candidate SNPs. Full methods and exact supplementary loci remain inaccessible through the ordinary public route. This is an active limitation on all unreviewed locus novelty assessments; the abstract cannot exclude overlap with A/B/C or Parkinson candidates.

Five focused, date-bounded Europe PMC queries were completely paginated: 102,22,51,231 and154 indexed hits, 488 unique metadata records. Query completion closes earlier truncation for these exact queries; it does not certify exhaustive worldwide literature coverage. Twenty-three priority records have explicit dispositions; remaining records are title/abstract triage, not full-text reviews. Source metadata and current primary supplements were prioritized where they can change novelty or source eligibility. [Search ledger](novelty/query_completion.tsv) and [screen dispositions](novelty/priority_screen_dispositions.tsv) preserve these limits. Earlier public GWAS Catalog accession/rsID metadata are retained in the prior acquisition cache; absence from that catalog is never used as novelty evidence.

Related global sleep/psychiatric overlap, neurodevelopmental annotations, proteomics and cell-type claims already exist. A general atlas or nearest-gene/neurodevelopment narrative is not a first mechanistic discovery. The feasible remaining contribution is rigorous comparison of regional covariance, genuine multi-signal colocalization, phenotype sensitivity and failures of validation assumptions. Whether that adds enough scientific value requires actual analysis outcomes and human statistical-genetics review.
