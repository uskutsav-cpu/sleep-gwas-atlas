Classification is PHENOTYPE_SENSITIVITY. Discovery–FinnGen participant independence is UNVERIFIED without a complete cohort manifest and overlap check. Neither geographic separation nor an LDSC intercept establishes independence. Case comorbidity/shared controls within FinnGen are not assumed absent.

Primary test at each slot is an intersection–union test, `P_conjunction = max(P_native_insomnia, P_native_outcome)`, testing association with both clinical endpoints at the same fixed biallelic variant. Familywise alpha .05 is Bonferroni divided by all four planned slots: .0125. This test does not require independence between the two trait statistics or the four slots. It supports only two fixed-variant associations, not shared causality, replicated regional covariance, colocalization, or a molecular mechanism. No sign concordance relative to discovery is an endpoint because published Jia allele orientation is absent; native ALT directions and approximate Wald confidence intervals are descriptive.

Response identity must match chromosome, GRCh38 position, REF and ALT. Every slot requires one exact native phenotype row for each trait, finite beta, positive finite SE, native 0<P<=1 and 0<=ALT AF<=1. Missing exact point, absent phenotype, failed identity, missing/invalid fields or inaccessible source yields NOT_ESTIMATED; no missing row becomes P=1, zero effect, or a biological null. All four slots remain in the denominator. No proxy substitution, SNP scan or family redefinition follows a missing or negative result.
