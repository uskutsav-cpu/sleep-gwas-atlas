# Novelty and competition

The audit fully paginated five focused Europe PMC searches: 488 unique records, with 23 title/abstract-prioritized competitors. Accessible primary texts, supplements and preprint updates were inspected. This bounded search does not prove worldwide exhaustiveness. The exact locus/source/build crosswalk is in `research_v1/NOVELTY_MASTER.tsv`.

Zu 2026 already reports the chr5 insomnia–ADHD region and rs77960. Actual 503-person European reference genotypes show r² = 0.995480603 between Brain6 rs2431108 T>C and published rs77960 G>A. Population-specific checks also show strong tagging. Different rsIDs do not establish a distinct signal.

Jia 2025 reports insomnia–MDD rs7105462 at GRCh37 chr11:112912048 and rs9485410 at chr6:101261007. Its reported single-signal H4 values are 0.008 and 0.650. Schipper v2, dated 11 March 2026 and not peer reviewed, reports an insomnia–depression region contained within candidate A: local rg 0.8712 and P 1.66e-7, but H4 0.00643 and H3 0.44557. Regional sharing and shared-variant identity remain separate questions.

Lin's accessible supplement covers all six protected insomnia–ADHD and all six insomnia–MDD intervals. Jia supplies prior same-labeled long-sleep signals for all three bipolar and all seven schizophrenia intervals. Thus 22 of 25 protected intervals have prior regional evidence; phenotype/build qualifications remain in the crosswalk. The three Parkinson intervals remain unresolved, not novel by absence of a match.

The new native chr5 result strengthens a known association model. The covariance counterexample is a source-specific methodological concern; its general novelty and empirical impact have not been established.
