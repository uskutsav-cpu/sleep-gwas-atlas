# Adversarial review

Three agents and the integrating agent challenged source identity, novelty, statistical assumptions, native implementations, alleles, priors and claims. These are separate automated review passes, not independent human peer review.

Resolved issues include chr5 lead tagging; prior signals in newly acquired Jia/Lin/Schipper supplements; the source-specific moment/weighted-variance mismatch; scalar-N invariance; palindromic harmonization sensitivity; live JSON/R API differences; REGENIE release semantics; missing browser fields; and source-specific ADHD redistribution restrictions.

Critical checks use separate implementations: standard-library contrast tails, explicit distinct-SNP Bayes factors, retained coloc R functions, dense Gaussian quadratic forms, raw FinnGen JSON, independent Pearson correlations and native PIP/credible-set/prior reviews. Reports and exact residuals are in `research_v1/statistics`, `sources` and `ld`.

Unresolved questions require human statistical-genetics review: empirical covariance calibration and overlap uncertainty; binary/single-N RSS approximations; LD coverage and finite-reference uncertainty; cohort deduplication; phenotype-matched two-trait replication; functional causality; and whether the methodological contribution is sufficiently novel and consequential for publication. Numerical concordance cannot answer these questions alone.
