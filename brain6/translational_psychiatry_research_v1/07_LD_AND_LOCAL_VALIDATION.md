# LD and local validation

Three public EBI 1000 Genomes v5b GRCh37 regions were acquired through verified HTTP 206 byte ranges: 26,443,591 genotype bytes plus 754,117 metadata bytes. Current indexes match retained hashes. Headers, REF/ALT coding, variant order, allele frequencies, the 503 European samples and pedigree/release corrections were checked.

The chr5/6/11 matrices contain 2,694/4,878/4,627 variants and rank 502. All pass finite-value, unit-diagonal, symmetry, bounded-correlation and PSD gates, with 100 independent Pearson pair checks per region. Small normalized genotype factors reconstruct the signed matrices; full matrices are local, with hashes retained. These new matrices do not change the historical zero-of-32 decision.

Exact insomnia and ADHD discovery archives matched their frozen hashes. Raw counts are 10,862,567 and 6,774,224, distinct from old filtered counts. ADHD's source README prohibits public redistribution of its result files; new raw inputs remain local.

Candidate C admits 2,185 common variants. Median approximate effective N is 312,492.7 for insomnia and 128,213.8 for ADHD. Native RSS consistency estimates are 0.0140861 and 0.0091722, with zero predeclared allele-switch flags. All eight fixed SuSiE fits converge and produce one qualifying 95% credible set per trait. Primary coloc-SuSiE H4 is 0.9915682295, 0.9216295123 under the lower prior and 0.9983021935 under the higher prior. All fixed model-size/large-N sensitivities retain high support.

These are reference-qualified, same-discovery exploratory posteriors for a known region. They do not prove a unique causal variant, first discovery, effector gene or independent replication. Binary and single-N approximations, a finite reference, incomplete coverage, cohort composition and post-selection remain limitations.

No new canonical LAVA family was admitted. Insomnia association in A/B does not meet the locked strong-association fine-mapping gate. The SUPERGNOVA reassessment demonstrates an estimator/variance mismatch and normal-tail limitations under its truth-known model. The three historical positives remain recorded candidates with uncertified calibration; synthetic inflation factors never replace empirical SEs or P values.
