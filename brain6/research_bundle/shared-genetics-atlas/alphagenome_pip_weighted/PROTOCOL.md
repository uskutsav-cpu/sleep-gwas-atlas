# Descriptive PIP-weighted AlphaGenome accessibility summaries

Post-hoc exploratory comparison using only the 425 previously selected variants and their saved JBrowse predictions, under the approved non-commercial AlphaGenome terms; no predictions are used to train a model. For each region–trait pair with an admitted exploratory single-signal credible set, normalize the available per-variant conditional ABF PIPs over the selected union only, then report the weighted mean absolute 501-bp adult frontal-cortex DNase ALT-versus-REF log₂ summary, the corresponding unweighted selected-variant mean, selected PIP mass, the variant with greatest product of PIP and absolute score, and that variant's share of the weighted sum. Do not report PIP-weighted summaries for region–trait pairs whose association gate did not admit a credible set. Count and exclude missing PIPs as unavailable, not zero; fail on invalid nonempty PIPs, missing or invalid predictions for eligible variants, and zero-sum weights. These are descriptive combinations of quantities from different models; they are neither calibrated functional-effect probabilities nor gene scores, formal tests, independent evaluation or genome-wide estimates. Conditional PIPs assume a single causal signal and depend on prior and data; scores are predicted signal summaries, not observed effects. The selection, model version, tissue, window, pseudocount, LD, omitted variants, mixture of cohort sources and potential winner's curse constrain interpretation. Report the new denominator as selected-union PIP sum, not unconditional probability of causality. Preserve all values and hashes; do not infer direction of disease risk from ALT–REF prediction sign.
