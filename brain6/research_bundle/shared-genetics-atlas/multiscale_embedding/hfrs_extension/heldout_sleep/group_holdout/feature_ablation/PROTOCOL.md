# Held-out-group feature ablation for the frailty-correlation prototype

After inspecting earlier embedding results, compare the existing six-feature ridge model with six leave-one-feature-out versions, removing ADHD, MDD, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, Alzheimer disease or Parkinson disease one at a time. Use exactly the prior 12 sleep traits, three frailty targets, source grouping, outer held-out groups, inner group-held-out folds, training-only feature standardization, λ grid and tie rule. Refit and reselect λ for every ablation and target; do not use held-out outcomes for training or λ selection. First reproduce the six-feature predictions from the earlier saved run. Report per-outcome and pooled RMSE, MAE, change relative to the six-feature fit and out-of-range predictions, along with all 252 held-out predictions and selected λ values.

This is a post-hoc model-stability diagnostic, not an independent experiment, feature importance test, biological attribution or clinical risk model. Only 12 correlated sleep phenotypes are available, cohorts and participants overlap across groups, features themselves require already-estimated sleep–brain genetic correlations, source uncertainty/covariance is not modeled, and no AlphaGenome predictions are used as training features. Comparing six correlated ablations across three outcomes is not six independent tests; do not attach p-values or treat changes in RMSE as evidence of genuine generalization. There are no individual-level prediction outcomes, and long-sleep coding and source-reference issues remain.
