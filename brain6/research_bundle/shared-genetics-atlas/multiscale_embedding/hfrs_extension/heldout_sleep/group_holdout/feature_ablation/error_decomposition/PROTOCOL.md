# Post-hoc ablation error decomposition

After inspecting the ablation results and individual prediction errors, use the existing 252 held-out predictions without refitting to decompose each drop-one-feature model's change in squared error relative to the all-feature model. Join exactly by sleep trait, held-out group and outcome. Sum differences across the three outcomes within each previously defined held-out sleep group and across groups; confirm that the sum divided by 36 equals the difference in pooled mean squared error. Report negative and positive contributions and both counts, without treating traits or cells as independent observations.

For the particularly large observed improvement when Parkinson disease is omitted, calculate error changes separately for the previously defined duration and accelerometer groups, and for the remaining singleton groups. These groups and the feature were examined before this follow-up. Such a decomposition cannot establish that a feature is useful or harmful, and a reduced-group evaluation is not a new held-out experiment or an independent validation. Do not select a final model, conduct a significance test, or infer biology from these small correlated groups. Retain the uncertainty and long-sleep phenotype concerns of the parent analysis.
