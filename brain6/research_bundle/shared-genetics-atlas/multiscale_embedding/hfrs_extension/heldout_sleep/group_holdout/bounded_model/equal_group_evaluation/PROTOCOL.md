# Equal-group evaluation sensitivity

The existing held-out comparison has eight sleep groups of unequal size: six singletons and two groups of three. Without refitting or selecting a new model, calculate squared and absolute errors from the saved 36 predictions, first averaging across the sleep traits in each held-out group (separately per frailty target), then assigning the eight groups equal weight. Report equal-group RMSE as sqrt(mean(group mean squared error)), equal-group MAE, original pooled metrics, and per-group losses for the bounded ridge, unbounded ridge, kNN3 and training-mean models. Verify there are 12 unique traits, eight groups, three targets and no missing predictions. Use the same pre-existing post-hoc predictions and group choices, so this is a descriptive sensitivity, not independent validation, an inferential test or feature importance. Unequal group sizes, shared participants and related traits remain.
