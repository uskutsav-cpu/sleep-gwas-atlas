# Input uncertainty: simultaneous one-SE perturbation

For each previously held-out sleep group and frailty target, reconstruct the bounded ridge fit using its selected lambda and frozen training rows and features. Hold the fitted model and all training values fixed; vary each of the held-out sleep trait's six brain correlation inputs independently within its reported point estimate ± one reported standard error. Calculate the minimum and maximum prediction over the resulting six-dimensional box. Because the linear predictor is monotone under tanh, extrema follow from the signs of the coefficients; check the formula by enumerating all 64 corners. Report the most sensitive cells and median ranges by target, and check whether perturbed inputs remain within the mathematical correlation range.

The simultaneous ±SE box is a hypothetical stress range, not a 68% confidence interval or a joint probability statement. It assumes no distribution and does not model covariance, uncertainty in training rows, coefficient estimation, source sample overlap, trait coding or model selection. If any input endpoints fall outside [−1,1], label them and do not silently clip. Source SEs may reflect different cohorts and SNP support. This is post hoc and cannot demonstrate clinical utility, causal features or independent generalization. Use no AlphaGenome outputs.
