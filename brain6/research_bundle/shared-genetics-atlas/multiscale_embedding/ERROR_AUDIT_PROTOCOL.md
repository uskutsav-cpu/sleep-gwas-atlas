# Post-hoc embedding error audit

Before viewing subgroup results, use the 960 saved masked-cell predictions and 96 source cells. Join by exact sleep/outcome, verify each source cell appears once per repeat, and summarize errors overall, in the six brain-outcome columns, in FI and FFS separately, and with long sleep excluded. For each group report ordinary RMSE, median absolute error, and root mean squared error divided by the reported correlation SE per cell. The latter scales by sampling SE but does not incorporate prediction uncertainty or covariance and must not be interpreted as a calibrated Z test or chi-square statistic. The dependent repeats are not independent observations, and this post-hoc audit is descriptive without a hypothesis-test claim. Also report the effect of clipping rank-two predictions to [−1,1], which is a post-hoc model modification, not a new fitted or externally validated model. Preserve original predictions. Do not mix in or train on AlphaGenome outputs.
