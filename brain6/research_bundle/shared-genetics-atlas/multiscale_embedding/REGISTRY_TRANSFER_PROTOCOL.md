# Registry-insomnia source-shift diagnostic

Written before evaluating this comparison. For the FI and FFS outcomes only, take the ten pre-existing masked-cell predictions for the UKB insomnia row from each of the four joint-model approaches (outcome-column mean, original rank-two, additive and multi-start rank-two). No model is refit. Compare those predictions with the saved R13 registry-insomnia–FI and registry-insomnia–FFS LDSC point estimates on the 1,134,953 common-support SNPs. Report all prediction values, means, ranges and residuals against both the UKB matched-support target and the R13 target; summarize average prediction residual and root mean squared error per outcome and method. This is a post-hoc source-shift stress test with two outcomes, not a test of predicting a new trait from features.

The model never used FinnGen insomnia to learn a representation; applying the UKB insomnia representation to R13 requires an unvalidated assumption that the phenotype and effects transfer. The R13 sleep source differs in population and ascertainment, and its FI and FFS outcome GWAS are reused from training; exact cohort overlap and reference-population match remain uncertain. The ten masked models reuse the same 96 original estimates, and their predictions are not independent replicates. Target SE describes uncertainty in the observed LDSC estimate, not prediction uncertainty; no confidence interval or p-value for the residual is justified here. Do not claim independent two-trait replication, causal direction or clinical prediction. Verify that every prediction was actually held out and that the reference file matches the previously audited R13 exact JSON. AlphaGenome outputs are not training features.
