# Joint sleep, brain and frailty embedding: exploratory protocol

Written before fitting. Join the existing 72 sleep-by-brain-disorder LDSC estimates with 24 sleep-by-FI/FFS estimates from the archived 12-trait paired support table, matching sleep trait labels. Verify one record per pair and complete 12×8 coverage; record source paths, exact input hashes and both source tables in the result. Labels alone do not establish identical GWAS versions, and supports differ across analyses; the matched FI–FFS inputs share within-sleep support, whereas brain cells come from the Brain6 global atlas. This is a conceptual mixed-source matrix, not a fully harmonized or covariance-adjusted joint LDSC estimate. Long-sleep source/model semantics and some input QC remain unresolved. Explicitly retain them as uncertainty, do not use the embedding to repair gates.

Fit a rank-two SVD for a descriptive map and use the original embedding's fixed unweighted ridge ALS, 10 repeated random five-fold masked-cell assignments, column-mean and additive baselines. All comparisons are exploratory, post hoc, and reuse 96 estimated correlations; repeated folds are dependent. The folds retain other pairs from each trait, so they do not test new-trait or independent-cohort generalization. Scores capture squared matrix magnitude or masked-cell error, not variance of a phenotype, causal interactions, predictive risk or independent evidence. Analyze no AlphaGenome predictions in model fitting. Save all source rows, reconstructions, cell predictions, configurations and hashes. Do not ascribe a biological meaning to axis signs or proximity.
