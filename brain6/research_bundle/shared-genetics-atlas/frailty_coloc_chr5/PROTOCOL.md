# Exploratory chromosome 5 FI–insomnia colocalization

Written before extracting or inspecting this analysis's chromosome 5 results. The selected region is the existing Brain6 insomnia–ADHD candidate chr5:103447968–104447968 GRCh37. This is a third, post-selected locus after the separate chr3/chr7 analysis and is not a preregistered replication family. The FI includes sleep items, and exact cohort overlap and cross-population LD differences remain uncertain. Single-signal ABF colocalization cannot resolve multiple signals and does not identify a gene or prove causation.

Use the exact FI and FinnGen R13 insomnia raw files and the same source/allele matching and numerical procedure as [the two-window protocol](../frailty_coloc/PROTOCOL.md): FI is GRCh37, R13 is GRCh38; extract GRCh38 chr5:104000000–105300000 to cover the lifted window. Map uniquely on the same chromosome and positive strand using the hg38-to-hg19 chain, then require rsID, position, two unique nonpalindromic SNV alleles, valid beta and SE, MAF≥1% in both, aligned allele-frequency difference ≤0.15, and no duplicate matched key. Keep a region only if at least 500 variants match. Require the known rs2431108 GRCh38 chr5:104612267 to lift to GRCh37 chr5:103947968.

Use coloc 5.2.3 original `approx.bf.estimates` and `combine.abf` functions; FI prior SD 0.15 under SD=1, registry insomnia log-OR prior SD 0.2, p1=p2=10⁻⁴ and p12 values 10⁻⁵ and 10⁻⁶. Report H0–H4, conditional shared-variant probabilities and credible-set size, plus effect-prior SD 0.1 and 0.3 sensitivity for both traits. Report source precision, regional coverage and assumptions. A three-trait mechanism cannot be inferred by combining two separate pairwise coloc outputs.
