# Fried-score readiness at the Brain6 chromosome 5 region

Of 2,337 previously matched FI–R13 insomnia variants, 2,306 (98.7%) have one Ye FFS record matching the rsID, reported chromosome/position and unordered alleles; 31 have no valid match. The FFS file provides no allele-frequency field, so this is not an FFS frequency-QC pass. A separate exact-file, full-reference comparison was independently rerun: 1,162,055 variants shared chromosome, position and rsID with the pinned GRCh37 map, with 216 allele-pair mismatches. This strongly supports the file using GRCh37 coordinates, while not verifying every record or effect direction. No FFS colocalization posterior was calculated.

The source p-value and the p recomputed from beta/SE differ in these records: the median absolute log10 difference is 0.0149, the 95th percentile is 0.0703 and the maximum is 0.1481. A difference above 0.01 occurs for 1,487 of 2,306 records. This is compatible with source rounding or different association models; it does not prove an error. BOLT-LMM distinguishes an infinitesimal-model beta/SE approximation from other association statistics, and the exact FUMA field mapping is not author-confirmed. The [FUMA input guide](https://fuma-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/snp2gene/prepare_input_files.html) treats an `A1` header as the effect allele by default, and the BOLT-LMM manual defines its standard `ALLELE1` as the effect allele. These conventions support interpreting the exact FUMA-formatted file as A1-signed, but the author did not explicitly map the renamed columns in a sidecar. The original project review further infers an effect on the ordinal 0–5 score; the exact applied scaling remains unconfirmed. Do not substitute a Wald p for the reported p without further source documentation.

For descriptive context, the source FFS p-values at rs2431108, rs1363103 and rs13164188 are 5.0×10⁻⁹, 9.1×10⁻¹³ and 8.0×10⁻¹³, respectively. These are **marginal FFS associations**, not corrected joint tests, independent two-trait replication or shared-causal evidence. They were inspected after the region and markers were selected. The FFS–R13 insomnia association and colocalization remain unestimated here.

[Ye et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10651618/) published score counts for values 0–5 in the 386,565-person discovery sample: 231,629; 110,651; 31,561; 9,564; 2,801; and 359. Those counts imply a raw-score mean of 0.5574 and population SD of 0.8148. This does not establish whether the released GWAS coefficients are on that raw scale, nor does it validate a quantitative-effect prior. The original model output, harmonization and coefficient scale must be obtained before calculating an interpretable posterior.
