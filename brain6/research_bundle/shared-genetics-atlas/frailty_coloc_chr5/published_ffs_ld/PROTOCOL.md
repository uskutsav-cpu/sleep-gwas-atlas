# Reference LD for published FFS lead and chr5 candidates

Post-hoc follow-up prepared after identifying the prior FFS lead rs7703746. Query the 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 EUR panel for rs7703746 and the three existing chr5 reference-LD candidates. Calculate phased, pairwise-complete haplotype r and r² for rs7703746 against rs1363103, rs13164188 and rs2431108, bootstrapping individuals 2,000 times. All variant coordinates and counted alleles must match exact source rsID and allele-pair records. Archive reference query and panel provenance without individual genotypes.

This is an ancestry-reference sensitivity, not the GWAS sample LD; FIN is not separately tested here. Candidate selection is post-hoc and linkage does not establish a shared causal variant or independent replication. The inferred FFS effect allele and its phenotype scale remain author-unconfirmed. A reference-panel LD coefficient is not a trait effect or a gene assignment.
