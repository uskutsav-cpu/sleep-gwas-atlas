# Prior FI discovery and FFS replication context at chromosome 5

The highest conditional H4 candidate in the post-selected FI–registry-insomnia analysis, **rs1363103**, is not a new frailty-index locus. [Atkins et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8441299/) already reported it in their UK Biobank–TwinGene FI meta-analysis at GRCh37 chr5:103917837, with T as the effect allele, beta approximately +0.019, SE 0.0034 and p≈2.2×10⁻⁸. The current matched FI row uses the same T allele with beta +0.0191, SE 0.0034 and p=2.228×10⁻⁸. This concordance reflects the **same underlying FI GWAS**, not an independent replication. A later [frailty instrument table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11872387/) reports the opposite counted allele C and beta −0.0191, consistent after the allele is reversed; it also reuses the Atkins FI GWAS.

The new FI–R13-insomnia single-signal analysis is a different pairwise analysis whose H4 is sensitive to priors; it does not convert the earlier FI association into a validated joint locus or prove a shared causal variant. Compare the [full chromosome 5 results](RESULTS.md), the separate insomnia–ADHD coloc and reference LD before making any cross-phenotype claim. The original FI paper's UKB and TwinGene components have a mixed-sign direction for rs1363103 in the published table; this flags heterogeneity as a follow-up question and does not establish either study's effect as wrong.

Separately, [Ye et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10651618/) reported an independent HRS analysis of the **FFS phenotype**: 22 of 37 discovery lead SNPs were eligible or proxied, 18 had concordant directions, and three had individually FDR-significant FFS associations in HRS. Their sign test and PRS analysis concern FFS on its own; the inspected source does not establish that rs1363103, rs13164188 or rs2431108 is one of those three, nor does it replicate both insomnia and frailty at a shared causal variant. Do not count all 37 as independently replicated or transfer the phenotype-side replication to the cross-trait analysis.

A closer [Ye 2023 source check](../replication/ye_2023/REVIEW.md) identifies rs7703746 as an already-published FFS discovery lead in this region and crosswalks the HRS figure’s three gene labels to discovery-table rows. The figure uses gene labels and some HRS tests use proxies, so those labels do not verify exact HRS-tested SNP identities or joint sleep–frailty replication.
