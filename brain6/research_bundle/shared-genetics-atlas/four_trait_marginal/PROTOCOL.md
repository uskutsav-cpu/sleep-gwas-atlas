# Four-trait marginal threshold screen

Protocol written before examining this four-trait screen. Traverse the exact 1,138,262-row aligned, common HapMap3 sleep/FI/FFS support and join the existing filtered FinnGen R12 HFRS source by unique rsID and unordered single-base alleles; same-rsID allele matching is not a complete reference-aware cross-build check. Keep missing, duplicate, allele-mismatched and invalid HFRS associations as explicit missing statuses, not p=1 or negative evidence. The three aligned sources must match in row order, SNP and effect allele; validate uniqueness, counts and finite Z. HFRS presence and eligibility are checked for every support marker. Label HFRS effects with their source alleles and align to the common A1 only on direct or reversed match.

Use a full 4 × 1,138,262 SNP–trait denominator with Bonferroni cutoff 0.05/(4×1,138,262). For the three rounded LDSC Z values use the normal-tail upper bound based on |Z|−0.0005; for HFRS use source-reported p-values and acknowledge their finite precision. Save all markers with at least one observed crossing; if HFRS is unavailable preserve its explicit status alongside the observed crossing pattern. Obtain chromosome and GRCh37 position from the harmonized FI source for these selected markers only, requiring unique rsID and allele compatibility; compare FI chromosome with HFRS chromosome where possible and hold mismatches. This is post-hoc marker-level prioritization, not locus clumping, trait specificity, two-trait replication, PLACO or enrichment-based conjunction FDR. It does not include HFRS variants absent from the common sleep/FI/FFS support and cannot prove genetic absence. Record source hashes, all coverage and counts; no participant-level data are used.
