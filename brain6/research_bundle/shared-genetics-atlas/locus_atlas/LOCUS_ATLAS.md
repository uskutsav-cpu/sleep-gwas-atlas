# Locus-level evidence atlas

This table is an integration of existing Brain6, AlphaGenome, GTEx and sleep–frailty candidate results. It is **not a new association analysis**. `brain6_six_region_atlas.tsv` has one row for each selected insomnia–ADHD candidate region, with separate trait–trait coloc probabilities, primary and alternative local status, candidate representatives, PIPs and selected GTEx brain eQTL records. Region column boundaries are the final merged geographic-region boundaries in GRCh37; candidate identifiers retain the original 1-Mb analysis interval and can differ slightly. Variant representations are explicitly GRCh38. The 425 variants are the union of trait-specific conditional ABF credible sets, not a genome-wide or independently confirmed causal set.

The ordering is an exploratory follow-up order based on the documented coloc sensitivity and molecular context, **not a validated ranking of causal loci**. All six inherit Brain6's `UNSUPPORTED` class because none met the frozen positive validation gates; that does not assert absence of association. The original LAVA family failed QC and no new result rescues it. No pair has verified independent two-trait locus replication. The eQTL status field is the project’s existing GWAS–QTL colocalization status; newly queried exact-variant GTEx associations are separate and must not be confused with colocalization. An empty GTEx cell here only means none returned among the 20 selected representatives, not among all 425 variants or all possible genes.

## Frailty candidate comparison

`frailty_six_candidate_atlas.tsv` lists six published insomnia–FI candidates that also met a post-selected MVP-insomnia/HFRS max-p × 124 descriptive benchmark. In order of increasing max-p, their positional labels are FOXP2 (rs12536335), MAD1L1 (rs28528997), NEGR1 (rs2815764), NCAM1 (rs2155292), RP11-384F7.1 (rs817509), and PCDH8P1 (rs1909650). The direction and allele records, marginal p-values, FFS lookup and full caveats are in the source tables. Five of these six have multiallelic reference representations; the Song source build is assumed rather than verified. In particular, rs2155292 has FFS p=0.54 and rs1909650 FFS p=0.097, so the HFRS findings cannot be called six shared FFS associations. Nearby genes are positional labels; genetic correlation, shared marginal association and shared causal variant are different claims. These candidates were selected after inspection and exact cohort independence is unknown.

## Reproduction

`build_locus_atlas.py` reads the 25 frozen candidate rows, the selected-variant gene-context table, GTEx significant-pair output, the existing 425-variant pilot, the published 124-candidate cross-source lookup, the six positional-gene annotations and the copied FFS marginal lookup. `input_manifest.tsv` records hashes of these inputs; `manifest.tsv` hashes outputs and code. The full [GTEx audit](../variant_context/gtex_v8/GTEX_QTL_REVIEW.md), [AlphaGenome pilot](../../../alphagenome_pilot/results/jbrowse_expanded/EXPANDED_REPORT.md), [published-local audit](../local_evidence/LOCAL_FRAILTY_AUDIT.md) and [124-candidate screen](../evidence/song124_mvp_hfrs_followup.md) retain methods and source limitations.
