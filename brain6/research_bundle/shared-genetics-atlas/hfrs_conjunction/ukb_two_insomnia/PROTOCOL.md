# UKB HFRS conjunction with two insomnia definitions

Pre-result protocol, 28 October 2026. Use the three existing ordered, common-support summary files from the UKB HFRS source comparison: UKB HFRS, questionnaire insomnia, and registry insomnia. The common support was selected before this screen and contains 1,020,621 SNPs. Verify the recorded SHA-256 checksums and read the entire gzip streams in lockstep, requiring identical unique rsIDs and matching unordered non-palindromic A/C/G/T allele pairs. For each SNP, separately test association with UKB HFRS and each insomnia source by max(p_HFRS, p_insomnia). Reserve all 2 × 1,020,621 SNP–pair slots in the family, including any invalid rows as p=1. Use normal-tail probabilities from the saved Z scores, subtracting 0.0005 from absolute insomnia Z to conservatively account for the three-decimal stored values, and 10⁻⁹ from absolute UKB HFRS Z to guard its displayed precision; floor at zero. Save the top 100 per source, all Bonferroni crossings, counts, hashes, and global Bonferroni, BH and BY results. Verify family sizes and source ordering, and recheck ranked candidates separately.

This is an exploratory post-selected, HapMap3 common-support intersection–union scan; it is not PLACO+, enrichment-based conjunctional FDR, dense genome-wide coverage, or a test of common causal variants. The family is distinct from earlier FinnGen-HFRS and FI/FFS families, which were already examined. If source marginal tests are valid, max-p remains a valid conjunction p-value under sample overlap. BH dependence assumptions are not established; BY permits arbitrary dependence for valid p-values. Corrected markers are not independent loci. Different insomnia and HFRS phenotypes, unverified ancestry and cohort overlap, source Z rounding, UKB HFRS sample-N discrepancy, and original association-model calibration limit interpretation. Compare threshold counts rather than interpreting threshold differences as evidence for phenotype-specific genetic effects.
