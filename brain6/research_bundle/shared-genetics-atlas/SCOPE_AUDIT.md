# Requested scope: evidence and outstanding work

Status reviewed 27 October 2026. This is a checkpoint, not a claim that all requested analyses are complete. “Available” describes an artifact or a bounded analysis, not scientific confirmation of the underlying mechanism.

The [recovered Lee reporter results](variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/RESULTS.md) provide published allele and element statistics for 27 selected variants, including two chromosome 5 emVars. A [NUDT12 gene-link review](variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/GENE_LINK_RESULTS.md) and [brain-QTL check](variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/nudt12_brain_qtl/RESULTS.md) distinguish the computational hypothesis from weak astrocyte measurements. In the separate ADHD-prioritized region, the [rs62369151 reporter and QTL follow-up](variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/rs62369151_brain_qtl/RESULTS.md) does not establish causal mediation or repair LAVA.

A [2026 U.S. POINTER review](component_exclusion/us_pointer_2026/REVIEW.md) adds randomized-intervention precedent for a frailty outcome with sleep components omitted. It does not provide a matched sleep-excluded FI GWAS or estimate genetic sleep–frailty overlap.

A [prior frailty ML comparison](multiscale_embedding/TSENG_PRIOR_ML.md) distinguishes Tseng's individual-level classification from the project's trait-matrix embedding. Combining genetics and ML for frailty is already published; independent sleep-specific evaluation remains open.

The [explicit trait-argument diagnostic](conditional_enrichment/conjfdr_readiness/r_implementation/explicit_trait_guard/RESULTS.md) identifies an additional input guard defect in the inspected R implementation. The previous simulations did not use that branch; their calibration concern remains unexplained.

A [LINC03051 model-summary check](variant_context/linc03051_laddr_2026/model_summary_lookup/RESULTS.md) verifies reported model attributes for eight selected sleep–ADHD RNA-feature comparisons. It does not verify weight-file identity, causal mediation or independent disease replication.

A [fixed-marker LD truncation sensitivity](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/pc_threshold/RESULTS.md) shows that the FI variance in locus 464 changes sign across retention thresholds. This post-selected diagnostic does not rescue the original local-correlation family.

The [component-band follow-up](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/pc_threshold/component_bands/RESULTS.md) attributes the sign change arithmetically to the additional projected components relative to the sampling term, without establishing a statistical cause or repair.

A [FinnGen immune-cell molecular lookup](variant_context/rs62264764_finngen_immune/RESULTS.md) shows no credible-set molecular-QTL hit for rs62264764 in the public browser and only weak displayed marginal tests. It does not address brain effects or establish independent disease replication.

A [chromosome 5 immune-cell lookup](variant_context/chr5_finngen_immune/RESULTS.md) finds no browser credible-set molecular hit at rs2431108 and one post-selected nominal expression row; the displayed feature's identity and broader testing family remain unresolved. The original genetic colocalization and failed local gates are unchanged.

A [published falls follow-up](replication/chr5_falls_2020/RESULTS.md) reports rs2431108 in a UK Biobank discovery and two smaller cohorts, with nominal same-direction evidence in Rotterdam but not B-PROOF. Falls are distinct from the requested frailty phenotypes, and this is not independent replication of insomnia–ADHD sharing.

A [reference comparison](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/RESULTS.md) finds a reproducible out-of-range LD entry and a negative eigenvalue in a sampled locus-464 submatrix. The numerical issue's cause and its effect on LAVA are unverified; no local gate is rescued.

In a [one-marker removal test](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/pair_removal/RESULTS.md), the selected FI variance remained negative after removing either member of that pair. This is not a validated correction or proof of the failure's cause.

A [whole-locus LD spectrum check](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/full_spectrum/RESULTS.md) confirms widespread numerical departures from correlation-matrix constraints in the selected extracted reference. The original 216 retained eigenvalues are positive; no causal link to FI failure or validated repair has been shown.

A [PSD-adjustment sensitivity](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/psd_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) demonstrates divergent outcomes under two altered LD matrices. Neither supports promotion of the local result or a change to the failed gates.

A [same-marker 1000 Genomes reference sensitivity](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/eur_lava_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) gives a positive FI variance in a 503-person European-labeled panel and a negative value using the UKB reference. Reference mismatch and limited panel size prevent choosing the correct estimate or claiming a local correlation.

A [reference-resampling diagnostic](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/eur_lava_sensitivity/bootstrap/RESULTS.md) shows substantial changes in retained components and estimated variance when the small reference is resampled. Its replicates are not independent cohorts or calibrated intervals.

A [population-holdout sensitivity](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/eur_lava_sensitivity/population_holdout/RESULTS.md) retains positive FI variance after excluding each reference population in turn. These overlapping, smaller panels are not independent replications or evidence of a specific ancestry effect.

A [direct LAVA univariate check](local_evidence/song_lava_receipt/cross_trait/marker_overlap/replay/reference_comparison/eur_lava_sensitivity/lava_runtime/RESULTS.md) reproduces the public-panel FI calculation and tests insomnia using original UKB allele frequencies. Its uncorrected selected-locus values are not a validated bivariate local result or family-gate rescue.

A [selected-region HDL-L pilot](local_evidence/hdll_pilot/RESULTS.md) returned positive insomnia–FI estimates across hypothetical overlap settings, with a trait-order check. This alternative uses the same trait data, is post-selected and uncorrected, and does not rescue LAVA or establish a validated local family.

A [published walking-pace comparison](component_exclusion/walking_pace_published/RESULTS.md) documents prior genetic correlations with insomnia and ADHD, including after BMI adjustment. This physical-function phenotype contains no insomnia item, but is not the requested matched, item-excluded frailty score.

A [registry-insomnia physical-function LDSC pilot](component_exclusion/physical_function_ldsc/RESULTS.md) uses walking pace and grip separately; only grip meets its two-test correction. Neither is an item-excluded frailty composite, and source coding and population limitations remain.

A [common-marker insomnia-source sensitivity](component_exclusion/physical_function_ldsc/insomnia_source_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) finds a paired walking-pace correlation difference across questionnaire and registry insomnia; the grip contrast does not meet its two-contrast correction. Cohort, phenotype and reference differences remain inseparable.

The [five-outcome extension](component_exclusion/physical_function_ldsc/insomnia_source_sensitivity/frailty_extension/RESULTS.md) compares FI, FFS, HFRS, walking and grip on the same marker support, with five paired source contrasts and ten individual tests. Its different correction families and source limitations are explicit; it does not estimate item-excluded frailty.

A [public UKB HFRS source comparison](global_audit/ukb_hfrs_public/RESULTS.md) adds a 2×2 HFRS-source and insomnia-source sensitivity with paired contrasts. The released UKB file's reported N differs from the article and release description; matched-cohort patterns do not identify the cause or establish independent replication.

A [UKB HFRS dementia-weight exclusion sensitivity](global_audit/ukb_hfrs_public/dementia_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) compares both insomnia sources with original and modified HFRS on common support. The correlations are similar, but this is not sleep/exhaustion-item deletion and the source N discrepancy persists.

A [full-source UKB HFRS lookup of all 425 ranked Brain6 variants](variant_context/brain6_ukb_hfrs/RESULTS.md) provides candidate-level results in both HFRS scores. Chromosome 7 shows the clearest signal, while rs62264764 does not pass the post-selected 850-slot benchmark. This does not establish independent joint replication, a causal variant or gene, or a resolution of the failed local QC.

A [425-candidate MVP-insomnia/Finnish-HFRS comparison](variant_context/brain6_mvp_finngen_hfrs/RESULTS.md) finds post-selected joint marginal evidence at rs62264764 and rs1476535; their related ADHD-symptom follow-ups remain weak. The tested SNPs are correlated and no independent three-trait causal locus is established.

An [expanded three-phenotype selected-candidate screen](variant_context/brain6_three_trait_candidate/RESULTS.md) matches 418 variants in MVP insomnia, Finnish HFRS and childhood ADHD symptoms; no max-p result crosses its 425-slot benchmark. This is not a genome-wide scan or a null finding for shared biology.

A [chromosome 7 UKB HFRS–registry-insomnia coloc pilot](frailty_coloc/ukb_hfrs_chr7/RESULTS.md) favors distinct dominant signals in the tested single-signal models, with substantial HFRS-only posterior and uncertainty in HFRS effect-prior scale. It does not rule out secondary sharing or repair local QC.

A [same-marker questionnaire versus registry insomnia sensitivity](frailty_coloc/ukb_hfrs_chr7/insomnia_source_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) yields different, prior-sensitive HFRS coloc posteriors. The shared UK Biobank cohort makes the unverified independence assumption questionable in the questionnaire pairing, and the source differences cannot be isolated.

A [hypothetical overlap-correlation stress test](frailty_coloc/ukb_hfrs_chr7/insomnia_source_sensitivity/overlap_stress/RESULTS.md) finds no HyPrColoc two-trait cluster at tested positive correlations of 0.05 and higher. The true correlation is unknown; this is sensitivity evidence, not a corrected result or a null conclusion.

A [chromosome 5 HFRS–insomnia–ADHD pilot](frailty_coloc/chr5_hfrs_three_trait/RESULTS.md) retains the earlier high pairwise sleep–ADHD coloc on common support; HFRS was not returned in the tested three-trait cluster. HFRS priors and cohort dependence remain unresolved, so this is not proof of no sharing or a local-QC rescue.

A [chromosome 3 HFRS–insomnia–ADHD pilot](frailty_coloc/chr3_hfrs_three_trait/RESULTS.md) returns an independence-based cluster but drops HFRS under tested hypothetical positive dependence. The actual overlap, HFRS scale and secondary signals remain unresolved, and no independent three-trait causal locus is established.

A [cross-source HFRS candidate comparison](variant_context/hfrs_cross_source/RESULTS.md) finds 46 correlated variant records meeting a post-hoc two-source benchmark in two selected regions. It tests frailty associations only; cohort independence, source calibration and shared causal variants remain unverified.

A [LINC03051–LSAMP experimental review](variant_context/linc03051_lsamp_function/REVIEW.md) adds a previously published lung-cell RNA-stability hypothesis; the paper's panel references and isoform mapping require care, and no neuronal or variant-mediated mechanism is established.

A [reporter-barcode one-base diagnostic](variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/barcode_audit/one_base/RESULTS.md) rules out a one-substitution match in the scanned files for four candidates. The mismatch and published assay-QC reconstruction remain unresolved; no negative functional result follows.

A [two-recheck input inventory](data_manifest/README.md) verifies the saved GWAS, LD-score and code files against existing receipts and checks runtime versions. It does not authenticate original GWAS processing or lock the complete execution environment.

A [public NHANES phenotype pilot](component_exclusion/nhanes_public/phenotype_pilot/RESULTS.md) estimates same-person FI–adapted-Fried and sleep–FI correlations, including item-excluded working scores. It uses approximate unvalidated constructions, overlapping physical questions and a mobility-related skip pattern; it does not provide the requested item-excluded genetic analysis.

A [sleep-specific NHANES sensitivity](component_exclusion/nhanes_public/phenotype_pilot/sleep_specific/RESULTS.md) separates sleep-only and sleep-plus-fatigue deletions and checks a second sleep question. The same cohort, access-related disclosure and unvalidated FI scoring limit interpretation; no genetic result follows.

A [shared-physical-item sensitivity](component_exclusion/nhanes_public/phenotype_pilot/shared_items/RESULTS.md) finds lower same-sample correlations after altering both provisional NHANES scores. The differences change score content and do not isolate causal or measurement contributions.

The [NHANES item-mapping follow-up](component_exclusion/nhanes_public/item_mapping/RESULTS.md) documents a cycle-specific visit-frequency boundary and unobserved weight-loss intentionality. The later pilot makes explicit working assumptions, but its scoring has not been validated against the source authors' implementation.

A [two-insomnia UKB HFRS conjunction screen](hfrs_conjunction/ukb_two_insomnia/RESULTS.md) has no corrected crossings over its combined 2,041,242 common-support tests. It is a conservative max-p screen, not PLACO+, enrichment-based conjunctional FDR or a comprehensive local-correlation analysis.

A [completed mixed-signal simulation](conditional_enrichment/conjfdr_readiness/r_implementation/synthetic_ld_overlap/mixed_signal/RESULTS.md) finds false conjunction-FDR crossings with and without smoothing in all 20 datasets; max-p controls recovered fewer synthetic shared blocks. It is an artificial scenario with ten seeds per setting, not a real-data false-discovery estimate or proof of general calibration.
