# Can the current reporter subset benchmark AlphaGenome ranking?

**Not yet.** The selected Siraj neural-derived SK-N-SH data have 38 assay rows for 32 Brain6 variants; six variants occur in two libraries. All rows join to the saved frontal-DNase prediction and both disease fine-mapping PIPs. Two rows pass the source allele-skew FDR threshold, but **neither also passes the simple row-level activity screen** used here. We have no independently validated true-negative labels.

That leaves no positive class under this conservative proxy, so AUROC and precision–recall are not meaningful for it. The proxy requires skew FDR<0.05 and at least one allele-activity Bonferroni p<0.05; it is **not** a reconstruction of the authors' full expression-modulating-variant classification. Nonsignificant rows are not proven negatives. The 38 rows are not 38 independent variants, and the 32 variants come from five selected, LD-correlated regions rather than a representative genome-wide sample.

The assay measures short-construct reporter activity in a neural-derived cell line; the project prediction is a 501-base summary of predicted adult frontal-cortex DNase change. They are different cell contexts and molecular readouts. We have not established whether the assay or overlapping datasets were used in AlphaGenome training, so the set cannot be called a held-out model benchmark. No model was trained on AlphaGenome outputs, in keeping with the user's authorized non-commercial terms and the [provider's stated restrictions](https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphagenome#terms).

To test whether the ranking adds value, a future evaluation needs a larger, rights-compatible assay dataset with published, reproducible activity and allele-effect QC; appropriately measured negative controls; frozen variants, baselines and metrics; and chromosome- or LD-block-separated evaluation with documented training-data overlap. Compare predictions with association strength and fine-mapping baselines without training on restricted outputs. Even then, assay classification would assess the chosen molecular readout, not a causal sleep–ADHD mechanism or clinical risk.

A [two-region expanded assay scan](../regional_reporter_readiness/RESULTS.md) identifies one candidate positive and three near-zero comparisons under explicit analyst-defined proxy rules. That remains too small and selected for a reliable model benchmark and does not establish author-defined labels.

The [pre-result plan](PROTOCOL.md), [join code](audit.py), [38 joined rows](joined_rows.tsv) and [receipt](receipt.json) record the counts and hashes. The source-specific results and design limitations are described in the [assay review](../../variant_context/siraj_2026_mpra/RESULTS.md). The join checks unique rsIDs against the earlier exact allele/build-matched extraction; it does not independently revalidate the assay, reference mapping or author labels. No performance metric or predictive model was fit.
