# rs12659965 in a childhood ADHD-symptom GWAS

The public [childhood ADHD-symptom GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02295-y) contains one exact GRCh38 G/A row for rs12659965. The **A-allele beta is −0.00657** (SE 0.00272; nominal p=0.01575). A normal-approximation 95% interval is −0.01191 to −0.00124 on the study's symptom-score scale. This is a post-selected, uncorrected result in a quantitative childhood symptom measure, not a clinical ADHD diagnosis.

The A-allele estimate is negative in both this symptom source and the previously queried FinnGen R13 registry-insomnia source (beta −0.0280, p=2.635×10⁻⁵). The numerical max of their marginal p-values is **0.01575**. This is an unadjusted, post-selected comparison after many variants and datasets were inspected, not a confirmatory family-wise result. It cannot establish the same causal variant, a gene or a direction of causation, and the two beta magnitudes use different phenotype scales. The marker is moderately linked to the existing chromosome 5 lead and should not be counted as a separate independent locus.

The symptom-study authors report no participant overlap with the diagnostic ADHD discovery GWAS used for the earlier Brain6 follow-up, but rs12659965 itself was absent from the archived Brain6 ADHD regional input, so its discovery effect and direction are unavailable here. Exact overlap and relatedness across all sleep and symptom cohorts remain unaudited. The [previous reporter and FFS context](../RESULTS.md), source allele conventions, multiple testing and failed local QC continue to limit interpretation. This is related-phenotype consistency, **not verified independent replication of an insomnia–clinical-ADHD locus**.

The [pre-query protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [query](query.py), [all raw rows and metadata receipt](receipt.json), [arithmetic verification](verification.json), and [run log](run.log) preserve this result. The indexed GWAS file and index were accessed remotely without whole-file hashes; the saved source metadata hash and exact G/A match are recorded. A direct Wald calculation reproduces the reported p-value. Neither this arithmetic check nor the association establishes causal mediation or validates the reporter model.
