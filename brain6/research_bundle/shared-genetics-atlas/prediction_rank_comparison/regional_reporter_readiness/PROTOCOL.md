# Expanded reporter benchmark feasibility

Before examining rows, search the published Siraj OL33 SK-N-SH differential-expression table for biallelic SNVs in the two previously defined Brain6 GRCh37 windows: chr3:117142005–118142005 and chr5:103447968–104447968. Read the complete selected ZIP member by validated byte range, verify its ZIP CRC and gzip completion, retain all rows in the two windows, and count unique variants, duplicate library rows, missing data and availability of activity/skew evidence. Record source and checksums. This is a selected two-region assay subset, not an unbiased genome-wide sample.

For a **feasibility proxy**, a candidate positive row requires source skew FDR<0.05 and at least one allele with positive activity log2 fold change and Bonferroni activity p<0.05. A candidate near-zero row requires at least one such active allele and an unadjusted normal 95% interval for log2 skew entirely inside [−0.25,+0.25]. Do not call these author-defined emVars or established biological negatives; the latter is a chosen practical-equivalence margin with unadjusted confidence intervals, not proof of no effect. Report whether both proxy groups exist; do not fit a model or calculate AUROC before independent source QC, duplicate handling, precise allele/build mapping, training-overlap and LD review. No AlphaGenome output will be used to train another model.
