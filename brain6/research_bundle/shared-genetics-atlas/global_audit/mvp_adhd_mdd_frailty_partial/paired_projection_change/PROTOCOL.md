# Paired contrast of genetic projections

Before calculating these contrasts, compare the already computed insomnia–frailty partial genetic correlations given ADHD alone with those given both ADHD and MDD. Use the same previously validated LDSC 200-block deletions for all component correlations, apply each projection to each block, and calculate the paired jackknife SE of the two-minus-one projection difference. Verify both individual point estimates and jackknife SEs against their saved results and require valid positive-definite four-trait matrices for all blocks. Report all three differences, approximate normal intervals, and Bonferroni p-values for this post-hoc three-contrast family, not the broader atlas. A contrast between two estimands does not establish mediation, an individual-level diagnosis effect, causal attenuation, interaction or a pure physical-frailty component. Source overlap, existing sample-size and intercept limitations, selection after earlier results, and score content remain.
