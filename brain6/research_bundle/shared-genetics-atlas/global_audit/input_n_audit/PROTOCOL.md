Compare hashes to the existing rerun receipts, inspect original source descriptors and relevant preparation logs when available, and distinguish constant/per-variant N from its meaning. Merely seeing a constant or a numerical resemblance does not establish whether N is total, effective, per-variant, or correct. Do not change inputs or recompute disease-scale heritability. Flag any log-labelled observed h² whose N convention cannot be supported and preserve genetic correlations separately. No independence, liability-scale or clinical conclusions follow.

After seeing the N distribution and the raw ADHD header, select the first valid archived ADHD-munged rsID at each of its five most frequent N values without regard to GWAS p-value. Before looking up those rows in raw data, scan the full archived raw ADHD file for matching IDs and unordered allele pairs; compare the munged N to 4*Nca*Nco/(Nca+Nco). This limited deterministic audit tests an arithmetic hypothesis, not all rows or provenance.
