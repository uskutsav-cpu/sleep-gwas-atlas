# Brain6 insomnia against FI, FFS and HFRS on common support

Post hoc extension of the two-frailty paired analysis, after its results were known. The previously aligned HFRS source and the FI/FFS sources share identical ordered SNP and allele keys. Subset HFRS in source order to the frozen 1,099,360-key FI input used in the previous runs; require unique exact ordered SNP/A1/A2 matches and finite Z and positive N. Confirm source and retained FI hashes against prior receipts and save the HFRS input on the SSD. Verify HFRS output keys exactly match all three frozen inputs. Using the identical LDSC software and European reference, run the HFRS–original-insomnia pair with unconstrained intercepts and `--print-delete-vals`; retain earlier FI/FFS results. Require identical post-merge SNP counts, 200 delete blocks, identical insomnia h² delete vectors and each reconstructed jackknife SE within LDSC rounding precision. If these fail, withhold paired contrasts.

Compute HFRS−FI, HFRS−FFS and FI−FFS from printed four-decimal r_g, with paired delete-block SEs, unadjusted 95% normal intervals and a Bonferroni correction over these three post-hoc contrasts. Also Bonferroni-correct the three pairwise association p-values for this bounded family. This correction does not cover other atlas analyses. Document phenotype differences, unknown study overlap, possible HFRS ancestry/reference mismatch, high HFRS h² intercept and use of total N. The source support was selected for earlier R13–HFRS analyses; it is not a comprehensive scan. Do not infer causality, independence, a local genetic correlation, or an item-excluded phenotype.
