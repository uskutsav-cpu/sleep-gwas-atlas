# Brain6 and frailty: genome-wide LDSC covariance-intercept audit

Post hoc follow-up of the chromosome 7 FI–insomnia–ADHD model's unverified independence assumption. Use the same archived Brain6 insomnia and ADHD munged inputs and native FI and FFS munged inputs previously used in the atlas's same-data LDSC rechecks, with the same European LD-score reference. Perform unconstrained bivariate LDSC for Brain6 insomnia–FI, Brain6 insomnia–FFS, and ADHD–FI. Verify exact input hashes against the earlier input receipts and record each full log. Compare the fitted cross-trait intercepts with their standard errors and interpret them as LDSC model quantities, not participant counts, trait correlations, direct input to HyPrColoc, or local null correlations. Different pairs may use different SNP supports. Report the genetic correlations as exploratory pairwise estimates, with Bonferroni correction for these three post-hoc tests only. Preserve negative/null intercept estimates and do not force an intercept or turn them into evidence of independence. FI contains insomnia and tiredness; FFS contains exhaustion. Estimates remain subject to allele, phenotype, overlap, ancestry and LD-score assumptions. This does not establish independent replication, replace the sleep-component-excluded GWAS, fix LAVA QC, or validate PLACO+.
