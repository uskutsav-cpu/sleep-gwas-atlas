# Paired-jackknife sensitivity for the three shared-support correlations

After obtaining the three same-support point estimates, re-run the identical three LDSC pairs with `--print-delete-vals`, retaining the original first-trait ordering and the same 200 blocks. Each output supplies delete-block totals for both trait heritabilities and genetic covariance. Reconstruct each deleted r_g as covariance divided by sqrt(h²_1 h²_2); require finite positive heritabilities, 200 values for each, aligned input SNP/allele ordering, matching row counts, and numerically identical shared-insomnia h² delete vectors and point estimate across pairs. Check each reconstructed jackknife SE against the corresponding printed LDSC SE and, for FI and FFS, against the saved native exact deletion estimates. Use paired delete differences to estimate the SE of HFRS−FI, HFRS−FFS, and FI−FFS with sqrt((B−1)/B sum((delete−mean(delete))²)). Construct normal 95% CIs and two-sided p values, and Bonferroni-correct this post-hoc three-contrast family by three. These SEs condition on the support, LD-reference, overlap model and sample-size inputs; they do not test whether differences are causal or clinically meaningful. A repeated shared-trait delete vector and common SNP ordering are strong checks but do not prove full cohort or LD fit. The R13 insomnia and HFRS source share FinnGen recruitment, and the HFRS h² intercept is high. If any numerical, support, or shared-block check fails, withhold paired contrasts.
