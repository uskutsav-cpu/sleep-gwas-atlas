# Registry-insomnia correlations with FI, FFS and HFRS on a common support

After discovering the prior four-trait support used in the UKB FI sensitivity, re-execute the three pairwise LDSC analyses of FI, FFS and HFRS against the archived S registry-insomnia file on that shared 1,134,953-marker input. Use the archived standard LDSC implementation and the available project EUR LD scores and weights, with unconstrained intercepts; put each frailty trait first, as in the existing native FI–S and FFS–S records. Hash each input and all 22 reference LD-score and M files, script and output logs. Check that the two prior FI–S and FFS–S results and SNP counts match saved native records to reported precision. The HFRS–S run is a same-source sensitivity and may share FinnGen participants; inspect its heritability and genetic-covariance intercepts. For this bounded new analysis, compute Bonferroni p across these three correlations, capped at 1, but do not replace the prior project-wide 16- or 396-test corrections or call this prespecified confirmation. Pairwise r_g contrasts require their covariance; don't treat SEs as independent or claim a significant difference without a matched paired-jackknife analysis. All sources' sample sizes are approximations where per-variant N is unavailable; phenotype and ascertainment differences, field overlap, ancestry and cohort intersections remain unresolved. Genomic correlation is not within-person phenotypic correlation or causation.
