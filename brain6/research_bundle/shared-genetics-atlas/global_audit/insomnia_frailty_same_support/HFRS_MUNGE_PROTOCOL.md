# HFRS filtered-to-munged audit

For every selected HFRS marker in the 1,134,953-marker same-support input, match its rsID uniquely to the archived `HFRS_HM3.tsv` filtered source, compare unordered nonpalindromic alleles (including valid strand complement and swap) and align the source beta sign to the final effect allele. Reconstruct the signed normal Z from the source p and aligned sign using a stable inverse survival function, then check agreement with the final munged Z to 0.000501, accounting for the three-decimal storage. Check N equals the source N, finite p and beta, duplicate IDs, and full source hashes; preserve mismatch counts and a bounded sample of errors. This audits the final file relative to the project's *filtered* source and tests the stated p-to-Z munging convention; it does not authenticate the upstream raw HFRS allele definition, independent cohorts, per-variant N, model, ancestry or participant overlap. Do not interpret p-derived Z as necessarily beta/SE or claim sample independence.
