# Exploratory conditional genetic correlations: insomnia, ADHD and frailty

Before obtaining ADHD–frailty results, use the ten previously verified common-marker inputs to estimate ADHD–FI, ADHD–FFS and ADHD–HFRS by LDSC with 200 block deletions and the same European reference. Reuse the existing MVP-insomnia–ADHD and MVP-insomnia–frailty full and delete-block values, on the same 991,707 SNPs. Verify all input hashes, equal marker counts, reconstructed individual jackknife SEs and matching ADHD h² delete vectors across runs to support paired block alignment. For each score compute the correlation of genetic components of insomnia and frailty after linear projection on ADHD: (r_IF − r_IA*r_AF)/sqrt((1−r_IA²)*(1−r_AF²)). Derive a conditional block-jackknife SE and approximate normal interval using joint delete vectors; require positive finite denominators and positive-semidefinite three-trait correlation matrices for the point estimate and all blocks. Report all three results or all failed gates, and a three-comparison Bonferroni correction without implying atlas-wide control. These are exploratory summary-statistic correlations, not regression on individual-level ADHD diagnosis, gene-by-sleep interaction, mediation, direct causal effects or independent replication. Different score content and cohorts, overlap, sample-size and LD-reference issues, and elevated LDSC intercepts remain. The input and outcome values have already been inspected; no AlphaGenome output is used as model training data.
