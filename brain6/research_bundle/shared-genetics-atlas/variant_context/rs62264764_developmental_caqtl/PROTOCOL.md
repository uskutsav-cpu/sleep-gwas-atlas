The archive is 6.3 GB; inspect the ZIP central directory using HTTP range requests and retrieve only the two 3-chromosome inner gzip members to the SSD, validating the ZIP member CRC and gzip integrity. The complete ZIP archive will not be downloaded or verified against its whole-archive MD5. Verify source coordinates, allele semantics and phenotype annotation before interpreting values; identify all tested candidate-marker records and peak positions, retain source-level counts and selected rows, and apply a simple exploratory multiplicity correction for the stated candidate-marker/peak family where appropriate. A marker's caQTL is an association, not a functional perturbation, colocalization or proof of disease mediation; primary human neural progenitors and their differentiated neurons are not adult frontal cortex.
