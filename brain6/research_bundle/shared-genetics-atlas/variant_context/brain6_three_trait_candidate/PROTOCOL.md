# Selected-candidate three-phenotype follow-up

Written before retrieving childhood ADHD-symptom results for the 425-variant set. The set and MVP insomnia–FinnGen HFRS findings were selected and examined in earlier work, including individual symptom results for some variants. This is a retrospective sensitivity, not a prospectively independent replication family. Use the prior 425-row MVP/HFRS result table and retrieve the public harmonized childhood ADHD-symptom-only GWAS GCST90568440 through its indexed GRCh38 file. Query one bounding interval per selected Brain6 region. Match each candidate by rsID, chromosome, position and non-palindromic REF/ALT allele pair, with unique matches and finite beta, SE, p and effect allele frequency. Retain missing, duplicate and invalid rows. Align symptom beta to the same candidate ALT used in the MVP and HFRS table. Save query bounds, result counts and hashes; indexed queries do not independently verify the full remote file or index checksum.

For each candidate with all three marginal p-values, calculate max(p_MVP insomnia, p_FinnGen HFRS, p_childhood symptoms). This is a conservative intersection–union test of association with all three traits if the three source marginal p-values are valid; it does not require their independence. Multiply by the full 425-candidate denominator and cap at one, retaining missing candidates as non-testable slots. Check direction alignment descriptively only. Report all 425 rows, correction and the number of matching traits. Such a selected-family benchmark does not control the full earlier search, establish a shared causal variant or gene, or prove independent clinical ADHD replication. Childhood symptom counts are not ADHD diagnoses; source ancestry, exact participants and relatedness, allele mapping, imputation/QC, differing trait models and regional LD remain limitations. Do not treat a non-crossing as evidence of no effect, and do not use AlphaGenome outputs for model training.
