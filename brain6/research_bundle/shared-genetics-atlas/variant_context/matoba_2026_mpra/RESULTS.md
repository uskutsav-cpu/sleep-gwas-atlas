# Public neural-progenitor MPRA lookup

[Matoba et al., Nature Neuroscience 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02454-2) measured reporter activity of cortical-structure-associated variants in human neural progenitor cells at baseline and under Wnt stimulation. This is a freely accessible functional resource, not a sleep or ADHD replication study. Its supplementary data include separate chromatin-QTL candidate and cortical-structure GWAS variant tables.

The seven previously selected candidate rsIDs (rs62264764, rs62264767, rs2431108, rs77960, rs416223, rs1476535, rs4916723) did not appear as exact text in any data sheet of Supplementary Tables 2 or 3. The caQTL sheets use coordinate identifiers, so nine previously documented chromosome–position combinations, spanning both available GRCh37 and GRCh38 positions for the two chromosome 3 candidates and GRCh38 positions for five other candidates, were also probed; none matched. Table 2's assembly was not independently resolved, and both builds were not checked for every candidate. The exact query and row counts are in [lookup receipt](lookup_receipt.json). Non-matches mean only that these identifiers or coordinates were not found in the examined tables; they are not evidence that these variants lack regulatory activity. A subsequent regional scan of the coordinate-bearing activity sheets found no rows in broad intervals around the four selected regions; see the regional coverage in the receipt. These bounds encompass the selected leads under their documented builds, but the study table assembly was not independently resolved, and absence within these bounds does not rule out distant LD proxies. No LD calculation was made for this resource.

[Supplementary Table 2](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41593-026-02454-2/MediaObjects/41593_2026_2454_MOESM3_ESM.xlsx) and [Supplementary Table 3](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41593-026-02454-2/MediaObjects/41593_2026_2454_MOESM4_ESM.xlsx) were downloaded to `/Volumes/Extreme SSD/brain6-matoba-mpra/`; their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the receipt. [Code](lookup.py) uses openpyxl in the bundled workspace Python. Assay signals would represent transcription from reporter constructs, not endogenous chromatin accessibility or proof of gene mediation; this lookup does not rescue failed LAVA QC or independently replicate a shared genetic locus.
