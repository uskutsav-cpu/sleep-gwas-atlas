# Public neural-progenitor MPRA source

[Lee et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11890967/) report a human neural-progenitor MPRA of variants selected from eight psychiatric disorders. Their supplementary Table S1B is described as allele-level MPRA activity; the public [GEO record GSE244011](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE244011) also lists processed count matrices and a variant–barcode mapping file, and the [authors' code](https://github.com/thewonlab/crossdisorder-MPRA) is public. This is a potentially useful experimental complement to computational AlphaGenome predictions; neural progenitors are not mature brain tissue, and reporter activity alone does not demonstrate a disease mechanism.

The GEO supplementary directory listing was reachable anonymously. The article lists a 9.8 MB supplementary workbook with allele-level statistics, but a direct download attempt on 2026-10-28 returned an HTML access-check page instead of a workbook. The workbook was not analyzed. However, an earlier [screen of the public count matrices](crossdisorder_mpra/RESULTS.md) found 42 of 425 ranked candidates, including rs4916723; rs62264764 was not among the matches. Matrix presence is not proof of a significant allele effect or passage of study QC. Provisional models and raw-barcode reconciliation have unresolved differences, documented in that screen. A new [cross-assay comparison](crossdisorder_mpra/CROSS_ASSAY_COMPARISON.md) joins those results to another public assay. Do not interpret a missing match or inaccessible table as a negative assay result. The attempted response was saved on the SSD at `/Volumes/Brain6Work/NIHMS2043764-supplement-7.xlsx`, but despite its name it is HTML and is not a valid workbook.

A separate [McAfee 2023 neural-progenitor library lookup](mcafee_2023_mpra/RESULTS.md) found no exact rsID match among the 425 ranked candidates or 1,548 previously selected chromosome 3 variants. Its schizophrenia-selected assay therefore adds no direct experimental result for those markers; a non-match is not evidence of absent function.

The publisher-hosted workbook was subsequently recovered. A [published-table comparison](crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/RESULTS.md) matches 27 candidates, including two published chromosome 5 `emVar` records; it does not establish disease causality or rescue LAVA QC.
