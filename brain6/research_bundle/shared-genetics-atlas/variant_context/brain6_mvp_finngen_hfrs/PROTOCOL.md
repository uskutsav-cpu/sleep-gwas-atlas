Written before extracting the 425 candidates from these two full sources. The 425 variants were previously selected through Brain6 sleep–ADHD analyses; several individual source findings and proxies were already reviewed. This is a post-selected sensitivity, not a preregistered replication family. Scan the previously checksum-audited MVP European-ancestry insomnia and FinnGen HFRS files in full, verify each against the earlier recorded source MD5 and hash the files, and match by rsID, exact GRCh38 chromosome and position, and the non-palindromic REF/ALT pair in the ranked candidate list. Require a unique exact match and finite valid source p and effect data; align the MVP log-odds and HFRS beta to the candidate ALT. Retain missing, ambiguous and filtered rows in the full 425-candidate table. These are source-specific marginal associations; allele and build harmonization does not prove a shared causal variant.

For candidates eligible in both sources, use max(p_MVP,p_HFRS), the intersection–union p-value for association with both traits if both marginal tests are valid, without requiring independent cohorts. Include all 425 original slots, including missing candidates, in the numerical Bonferroni benchmark 425×max(p), capped at one. Report all candidates and directions separately. Since both regions and source analyses have already been selected, the benchmark does not correct all previous exploration or establish a confirmatory discovery. Source phenotype definitions, ancestry, sample overlap, study ascertainment, effect-scale differences and variant QC remain concerns; the p-value does not test whether effects have the same direction or reflect the same causal SNP. Compare with the earlier UKB HFRS screen descriptively only and preserve the clinical ADHD-symptom follow-up. Reuse of source participants and unresolved relatedness preclude an unqualified claim of independent two-trait or three-trait replication.
