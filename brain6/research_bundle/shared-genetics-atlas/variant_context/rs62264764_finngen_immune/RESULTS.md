# rs62264764 in a public immune-cell molecular atlas

On 28 October 2026, the public [FinnGen CASCADE browser](https://cascade.finngen.fi/variant/chr3_117920728_G_A) identified the candidate as rs62264764, chr3:117920728 G>A on GRCh38. The [published study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2) measured single-nucleus RNA and chromatin accessibility in peripheral blood immune cells. Its RNA atlas included 1,108 donors; the chromatin atlas included 1,093 paired donors. These are immune cells, not brain, and total atlas donor counts are not the sample size of every cell-type test.

The browser says this variant has **no CASCADE classification or credible-set molecular-QTL hit**, while showing marginal associations. For the displayed LSAMP expression feature, all 14 cell-type rows had nominal p≥0.094; the lowest displayed value was NK L2 p=0.094 (β −0.075, SE 0.045). A second displayed feature, AC068633.1, had four rows, all p≥0.181. Three selectable chromatin-peak features had 1, 3 and 9 displayed rows respectively; all displayed p≥0.104. These are rounded browser values, selected after the variant was prioritized; the rows share donors and are not independent experiments. The allele-effect convention and feature-specific sample sizes were not independently verified here, so do not infer effect direction across datasets.

The page also reports no GWAS colocalizations for this variant. That is a **browser-filtered result**, not proof of no association in every immune cell, no tested colocalization in all traits, or absence of a brain regulatory effect. The browser's lack of a fine-mapped hit does not imply that every possible molecular feature was tested or measured with adequate power. The previously reported [brain-cell LINC03051 eQTLs](../rs62264764_brain_xqtl/RESULTS.md) use different tissues, donors and assays and are not contradicted by this immune-cell lookup. Neither source establishes which gene mediates insomnia or ADHD, and the immune result does not validate the AlphaGenome frontal-cortex DNase prediction.

## Scope and reproducibility

We used the public interface rather than downloading restricted data: searched rs62264764, checked the variant page, expanded all LSAMP rows, selected both offered eQTL features and all three offered caQTL features, and recorded the displayed ranges above. The page has a downloadable table option, but the underlying unrounded dataset and full search universe were not archived or independently validated; this is a manual, time-stamped browser observation. The browser's credible-set filter, cell-type availability, multiple-testing rules, allele convention and participant overlap should be checked before using these results for formal comparisons. No ADHD association or independent sleep–ADHD replication was estimated.
