# Brain6 variant screen in public UKB HFRS files

Written after checking the existing selected candidate inventory and prior HapMap-restricted HFRS file, but before inspecting any full-source candidate results. The existing list contains 425 unique variant IDs selected from six Brain6 insomnia–ADHD candidate regions and prior credible-set analyses. It is post-selected and not a genome-wide or independent replication set. Stream the complete authors' original and dementia-excluded UKB HFRS releases separately, verify the publisher MD5s, byte counts and gzip completion, and record SHA-256; do not save the full archives or delete data.

For each candidate, match the source by unique rsID, the previously mapped GRCh37 chromosome/position, and non-palindromic SNP REF/ALT allele pair, assuming the prior liftover's allele orientation carries over. Record all matches and mismatches, including alternate source records; exclude ambiguous or duplicate exact matches, invalid numbers, MAF<0.01 or INFO<0.9. Report source A1, A2, N, effect, SE, p and INFO, and derive a sign aligned to the candidate ALT only when the exact allele mapping is valid. Check BETA/SE against the published p. Source-specific allele convention and GRCh37/GRCh38 mapping still require verification beyond this position/allele crosswalk. The previously documented 407,643 versus 407,463 N discrepancy remains unresolved.

Report both HFRS p-values for every eligible candidate, as well as missing and excluded entries. Calculate a conservative Bonferroni benchmark over all 850 candidate–score slots, including missing entries, capped at one. This is a post-selected numerical screen, not a guaranteed post-selection family error rate. Original and dementia-excluded HFRS are overlapping same-cohort measures; p-value differences do not establish an effect difference without the covariance of the estimates. No HFRS-only result shows both sleep and ADHD association, shared causal variation, independent replication, a causal gene or AlphaGenome validation. Nearby genes and predicted molecular effects remain hypotheses. Compare the named rs62264764 and chr5/chr7 anchors as descriptive rows without promoting them because of selection.
