# Measured reporter activity at rs40465

The public [neuropsychiatric regulatory-variant study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-023-01533-5) tested the chromosome 5 variant at **GRCh37 5:103981726** in multiple cell contexts. Its supplied constructs identify reference **T** and alternate **G**; the atlas independently maps this coordinate and allele pair to **rs40465**, GRCh38 5:104646025 T>G. This is one of the previously ranked variants in the Brain6 chromosome 5 insomnia–ADHD region, not rs62264764. In the existing regional analysis, it had insomnia and ADHD approximate PIPs 0.0397 and 0.00934, respectively, conditional on the relevant fine-mapping models. Its adult-frontal AlphaGenome DNase prediction was +0.02584 log2 ALT/REF in the selected 501-bp window; that prediction is not the same measurement as reporter expression.

We matched this coordinate to 13 cell-context worksheets in the authors' [complete MPRA summary-statistics supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41588-023-01533-5/MediaObjects/41588_2023_1533_MOESM8_ESM.xlsx). None of those 13 reported study-adjusted FDR values was below 0.05; the smallest was 0.1933 in differentiated SH-SY5Y cells (nominal p=0.0420). For astrocytes the reported p=0.5094, FDR=0.8254; for day-2 and day-4 neurons p=0.8565 and 0.8118, with FDR=0.9930 and 0.9452. See all [cell results](cell_results.tsv). The P-NPC worksheet omitted row identifiers and could not be matched reliably; no conclusion is drawn for it. Cell models are heterogeneous and were examined after candidate selection. A nonsignificant result is not proof of no effect, and we do not interpret the direction of logFC without separately verifying the authors' allele contrast convention.

The exact rs40465 T/G coordinates match the prior atlas, and the source's raw construct names confirm both alleles were represented. This supports a measured follow-up for this *one candidate*, not a claim about rs62264764 or a shared sleep–ADHD mechanism. The linked chromosome 5 lead rs2431108 is 33,758 bp away in GRCh37; a lookup of the prior 1000 Genomes reference returned no record at rs40465, so LD with that lead has not been established. A reporter assay measures activity of isolated constructs and does not by itself demonstrate an endogenous chromatin effect, identify a target gene, or independently replicate GWAS associations. It does not resolve failed LAVA quality gates.

A subsequent exact-variant screen converted the 425 previously ranked Brain6 variants to GRCh37, then matched the authors' raw construct labels and alleles. Only rs40465 matched; the other 424 had no matching raw construct coordinate in the supplied counts. The [coverage table](ranked_screen.tsv), [code](screen_ranked.py), and [receipt](ranked_screen_receipt.json) record the inputs, chain and workbook hashes. This is a limited screen of this study, not evidence about variants in other assays; matching coordinates and alleles alone does not establish causal effects.
