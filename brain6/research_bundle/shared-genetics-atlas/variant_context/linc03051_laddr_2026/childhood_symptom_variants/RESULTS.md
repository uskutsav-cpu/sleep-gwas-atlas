# Childhood ADHD-symptom follow-up at selected chromosome 3 markers

All six selected RNA/depression and sleep–ADHD markers matched the public [childhood ADHD-symptom GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02295-y) by GRCh38 position, rsID and allele pair. Their symptom p-values ranged from **0.228 to 0.492**; none was nominally below 0.05. The LINC03051 molecular lead rs13073833 had p=0.259, the insomnia xTWAS lead rs1456193 had p=0.492, and rs62264764 had p=0.437. [All six rows](results.tsv) include aligned estimates and standard errors.

The symptom beta signs all agree with the corresponding Brain6 diagnostic ADHD discovery betas after allele alignment. The associations are weak, however, and the markers are linked; six matching signs are not six independent confirmations. Effects are measured on different scales—childhood symptoms versus diagnostic ADHD log odds—and must not be directly compared in magnitude. Weak results do not prove that the variants or region have no ADHD effects, especially across different phenotypes and cohorts.

The symptom study reports that this symptom-only GWAS does not overlap the Demontis 2023 diagnostic ADHD discovery sample. That is author-reported participant nonoverlap, not an independent audit of relatedness or overlap with every sleep and molecular dataset. The six markers and this source were examined after prior candidate and gene selection; the comparison is **not a confirmatory family-corrected replication**. It does not test the RNA feature, provide an independent sleep result, or establish a shared causal variant. The nominal nucleus-accumbens RNA–ADHD xTWAS result from the older diagnostic source remains a hypothesis rather than a replicated finding.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [lookup code](lookup.py), [remote-query receipt](receipt.json) and [verification](verification.json) document the exact rows and calculations. Remote GWAS Catalog data and its index were queried by range and not whole-file hashed. The [source review](../../../replication/childhood_symptoms/RESULTS.md) records the discrepancy between published and metadata sample sizes and other limitations. No AlphaGenome outputs were used for training, and no LAVA quality gate was changed.
