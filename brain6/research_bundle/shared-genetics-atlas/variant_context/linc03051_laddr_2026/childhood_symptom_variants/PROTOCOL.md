# Childhood-symptom lookup for selected RNA and disease variants

Written before this lookup. Query six previously selected chromosome 3 variants—the two published depression–RNA top variants, two molecular leads, rs1456193 and rs62264764—in the independent childhood ADHD-symptom summary data GCST90568440 using its indexed harmonized GRCh38 file. Require exact coordinates, unique rsID and allele-pair matching; align the symptom beta to the effect allele in the archived Brain6 diagnostic ADHD input using the previously verified mapping. Preserve missing and ambiguous results as unavailable and retain raw returned rows.

The childhood symptom paper reports participant nonoverlap with the Brain6 diagnostic ADHD source; individual identity, relatedness and sleep-cohort overlap are not independently audited. This is not a replication of the RNA feature or sleep association. The six variants, gene and symptom dataset were selected after extensive inspection, so no factor-of-six adjustment captures the full selection. Report p-values and directions descriptively without asserting a confirmatory discovery. A marginal variant association does not establish causal sharing or identify a gene, and the original LAVA gate remains failed.
