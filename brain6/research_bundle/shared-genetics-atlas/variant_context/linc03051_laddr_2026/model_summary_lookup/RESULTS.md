# LINC03051 RNA-model summary check

The authors' [public LaDDR model-summary archive](https://zenodo.org/records/19924423) contains entries for all eight previously selected sleep-associated LINC03051 RNA features. The selected method, SNP count and model cross-validation statistics agree with the corresponding older ADHD association rows, allowing for the latter's rounded R² values. This strengthens the evidence that the two result tables describe the same labeled modeling configurations; it does **not** prove their underlying weight files are byte-identical or that their GWAS are independent.

For the post-selected nucleus-accumbens residual feature **PC14**, the profile reports a BLUP model cross-validation R² of **0.072** and p=7.0×10⁻⁵; the associated ADHD row reports the same values. The model's 420 SNPs are the reported SNP count, not a count of people. The other seven selected methods have profile R² values from 0.074 to 0.220. These statistics describe the molecular-feature prediction model in its training data, not prediction of ADHD, insomnia or clinical risk. The [FUSION implementation](https://github.com/gusevlab/fusion_twas/blob/master/FUSION.compute_weights.R) computes its model performance from cross-validated predictions using adjusted R²; the exact archived computation and model-selection uncertainty have not been independently reproduced here. Values are selected after finding associations, and cannot be generalized to other genes or tissues.

The model check does not turn PC14's nominal, selected ADHD association into independent replication, and its previously reported sleep–RNA PP4 of 0.565 is not evidence for a three-trait shared variant. We still lack the model weights for a new 2023 ADHD xTWAS and cannot infer the RNA feature's biological mechanism, causal gene or variant from its R².

## Reproducibility and limits

[Lookup code](lookup.py) uses HTTP byte ranges to read six complete ZIP members; Python's ZIP reader checks their CRCs, and [the receipt](receipt.json) records individual SHA-256 hashes. The 142,716,444-byte archive was not fully downloaded and its publisher MD5 was not independently checked. CRC validates consistency of extracted members with the retrieved ZIP directory, not the full publisher archive's authenticity. [Selected profiles](selected_profiles.tsv) and [verification code](verify.py) join model method, SNP count, R² and p-value to the eight previously archived ADHD rows; exact p-values agree, while three displayed R² values agree within 0.005 because of rounding. Published model summaries do not include the actual weight files and cannot establish causal relationships.
