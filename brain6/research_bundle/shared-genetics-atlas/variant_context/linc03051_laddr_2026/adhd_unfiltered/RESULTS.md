# LINC03051 RNA features: public ADHD follow-up

The public [Pantry Portal](https://pantry.pejlab.org/download) provides unfiltered xTWAS tables for an older European PGC ADHD GWAS. We matched **all eight** LINC03051 RNA-feature/tissue combinations previously selected from the published insomnia xTWAS results. The identifiers and listed model attributes—including the molecular lead, prediction method and reported model statistics—agree across each matched pair; we have not independently compared the model-weight files byte for byte.

The strongest ADHD follow-up among the eight is **residual RNA feature PC14 in nucleus accumbens**, with ADHD xTWAS z=+3.264 and p=0.00110; the corresponding previously reported insomnia xTWAS z=+6.056 and p=1.40×10⁻⁹. The authors' insomnia–RNA coloc PP4 for this feature is **0.565**, and the ADHD table gives no coloc posterior for the matched rows. The other seven matched ADHD p-values range from 0.0600 to 0.1031. [All eight comparisons](matched_models.tsv) are provided.

This is a **post-selected, nominal follow-up** after the insomnia features and region were examined. Eight tests do not capture the broader search and selection history, so no confirmatory multiplicity-adjusted claim is made. A matched feature with two associations does not establish that the same variant drives both traits, that the feature mediates either trait, or that rs62264764 affects this molecular phenotype. Similar z signs are descriptive and depend on the shared feature's orientation; they are not a measured allele-specific mechanism. The lower PP4 for this feature does not prove distinct signals, and absence of an ADHD coloc posterior is missing evidence, not a negative result. The seven weak ADHD results do not rule out effects given differing power and models.

The portal labels this source `PGC_ADHD_EUR_2017`, with 53,293 individuals and 19,099 cases in its [source metadata](adhd_metadata.tsv). It cannot be treated as independent replication of the later Brain6 diagnostic ADHD discovery: [Demontis et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914347/) explicitly incorporated summary statistics from earlier European PGC cohorts. Exact overlap and data lineage for this portal file remain to be verified, and the insomnia source and GTEx training tissues also overlap or may overlap earlier project analyses. The source GWAS was harmonized and imputed to a GTEx reference; imputation can affect apparent precision. Sample ancestry, GWAS definitions, LD and age differences limit comparisons.

The result creates a testable **nucleus-accumbens LINC03051 RNA-feature hypothesis**, not a demonstrated sleep–ADHD pathway or causal gene. Follow-up needs independent sleep and ADHD data, dense molecular QTLs for the specific residual feature, overlap-aware multi-signal colocalization and allele-specific experiments. The prior study's single-trait sleep association and this same-source ADHD follow-up should not be counted as independent confirmation.

A [public model-summary check](../model_summary_lookup/RESULTS.md) confirms matching method, SNP count and cross-validation statistics for all eight selected labels, within displayed rounding. For the selected nucleus-accumbens feature, the molecular model's cross-validation R² is 0.072; it is not an ADHD or insomnia prediction score. Weight-file identity remains unverified.

A [variant-level childhood symptom follow-up](../childhood_symptom_variants/RESULTS.md) found no nominal associations among the six selected chromosome 3 markers. This related phenotype and post-selected lookup do not directly replicate the RNA feature or rule out diagnostic ADHD effects.

The [archive lookup](lookup.py) streamed all 419,344,197 bytes and 400 members and saved [165 matching gene rows in a receipt](receipt.json), with a computed SHA-256; no publisher archive checksum was found, so the digest is provenance rather than independent authentication. [Comparison code](compare.py) and [receipt](comparison_receipt.json) verify model attributes and reproduce the eight-row table. The underlying archive was not retained, and no AlphaGenome output was used to train a model. The source repository requests citation of [Barbeira et al.](https://doi.org/10.1101/814350) for its harmonized GWAS data.
