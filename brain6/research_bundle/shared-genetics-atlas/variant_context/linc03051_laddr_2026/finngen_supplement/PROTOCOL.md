# Public FinnGen molecular-colocalization supplement lookup

This post-hoc lookup was planned after the LINC03051–insomnia xTWAS and older ADHD feature results were inspected. Retrieve the 2026 Munro et al. article supplement from Europe PMC and inspect Table S3 for ENSG00000239268. Preserve the source's tissue, feature, FinnGen endpoint and CLPP, and match any records to the eight previously selected insomnia RNA features by tissue and phenotype identifier. Record source hashes and all matching rows.

The article says Table S3 contains selected credible-set colocalizations with CLPP >0.01 from FinnGen R12, not all attempted tests. Its CLPP is a sum of products of two fine-mapping posterior inclusion probabilities; it is not coloc H4. Absence of an endpoint or gene row cannot distinguish untested data, missing fine maps, insufficient power or a result below threshold. Treat the endpoint as a different phenotype and pairwise molecular comparison; do not infer a common causal variant across insomnia, ADHD and another trait, or independent replication. Record that the GTEx RNA data overlap the earlier molecular analysis. No AlphaGenome output is used for model training.
