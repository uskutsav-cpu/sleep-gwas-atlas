# Reference LD for selected LINC03051 follow-up variants

After inspecting the published results, compare the two reported top shared variants from the FinnGen depression–RNA supplement with rs62264764 and the sleep xTWAS lead rs1456193. Also include the two molecular lead variants used by the earlier highlighted RNA models. Use Ensembl's GRCh38-to-GRCh37 mapping, require a unique forward-strand mapping and exact listed REF/ALT pair in the UCSC 1000 Genomes Phase 3 VCF, and calculate phased-haplotype r² among EUR samples from the existing 503-person panel. Save mapping responses, source variants, coverage and pair counts. If a variant fails exact matching, record it as unavailable rather than substitute a proxy.

This is a post-selected, small reference-panel LD description. It is not an analysis in the actual FinnGen, PGC or GTEx cohorts, a multi-signal colocalization, independent replication or a gene-mediation test. Ref/alt orientation and genome builds are explicit; signed correlations in this comparison count the stated ALT alleles, but a sign does not establish disease-risk direction. Do not infer weak or strong causal sharing from a single pairwise LD value. The remote VCF and index are accessed by region and cannot be whole-file checksum-verified in this lookup.
