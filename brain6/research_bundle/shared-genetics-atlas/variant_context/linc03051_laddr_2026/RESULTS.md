# Prior ML-derived RNA phenotypes at the rs62264764 region

[Munro et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2026.06.005) used LaDDR to summarize RNA-sequencing coverage as latent gene-level phenotypes, then combined GTEx molecular models with GWAS using FUSION xTWAS and coloc. Their article highlights an insomnia association for **LINC03051** in anterior cingulate cortex. This is important prior work for Brain6: a data-driven brain RNA–insomnia hypothesis for this gene has already been published. A latent RNA feature is not automatically total gene expression, and an xTWAS association does not prove that changing the gene causes insomnia.

The authors' public [GTEx hit tables](https://zenodo.org/records/19924423) contain **eight selected LINC03051–UKB insomnia rows**: three data-driven features (DDP) and five residual data-driven features (rDDP). The table reports the same best GWAS marker, GRCh38 chr3:117918850 T>C, for each. This is **rs1456193**, previously checked in Brain6 and in an independent [reporter-assay lookup](../siraj_2026_mpra/RESULTS.md). In the project's 503-person European 1000 Genomes reference, rs1456193 and rs62264764 have r²≈0.821. That is reference-panel tagging, not proof that either variant is causal. rs1456193's reporter comparison in one SK-N-SH library did not detect allele skew; a nonsignificant transformed-cell assay does not negate the tissue-specific RNA result or rule out an effect in another context.

| Published RNA feature and tissue | xTWAS p | Reported coloc PP4 | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| DDP PC8, anterior cingulate (BA24) | 4.04×10⁻⁹ | 0.861 | The highlighted gene–insomnia result; PP4 is a model-dependent regional shared-signal posterior. |
| DDP PC10 / PC2, frontal cortex (BA9) | 3.01×10⁻⁸ / 4.22×10⁻⁸ | 0.938 / 0.981 | Different latent features from overlapping tissue/donor resources, not independent replications. |
| rDDP PC2 / PC9, hippocampus | 9.39×10⁻⁹ / 1.30×10⁻⁸ | 0.943 / 0.952 | Residual features model RNA coverage after the study's six known modalities; they are not a specific transcript mechanism without further characterization. |

The remaining three rDDP sleep rows have reported PP4 0.761, 0.660 and 0.565; [all eight rows](sleep_hits.tsv) are retained. The coloc posteriors are the authors' outputs for molecular phenotype–insomnia comparisons, not a new analysis here or a three-way insomnia–ADHD–RNA colocalization. Their priors, sample overlap, signal multiplicity and LD assumptions require direct review before causal interpretation. The high reported PP4 values do not identify rs1456193 or rs62264764 as the causal variant. The previous Brain6 single-nucleus **total-expression** pilots favored distinct dominant signals; these model different molecular phenotypes, tissues and donors and cannot be treated as direct contradictions of LaDDR's RNA-coverage features.

A [source-code review](method_review/RESULTS.md) identifies the selected xTWAS threshold, an intended European LD reference, Z-based approximate coloc inputs and implicit package defaults in the public workflow. The actual execution environment and priors remain unverified.

The GWAS tag is `UKB_1200_Sleeplessness_or_insomnia`, not an ADHD GWAS. [GWAS metadata](gwas_metadata_insomnia.tsv) identifies a UK Biobank source with 337,119 participants; its metadata omit an ancestry label for this row. Brain6's discovery insomnia data also include UK Biobank, and both this molecular study and earlier project GTEx analyses use GTEx v8, so exact participant overlap is not independently verified and this is not independent two-trait replication. The adjacent LSAMP and LINC03051 gene labels remain hypotheses; the study does not establish a direct variant effect on either gene or a sleep–ADHD pathway.

The source hit tables were filtered by their authors at TWAS p<5×10⁻⁸. We found no LINC03051 rows in their knowledge-driven hit table; that is absence from a prefiltered table, not evidence of no total-expression association or a complete census of all tests. Five reported PP4 values above 0.8 among these selected rows are not a multiple-testing correction for our post-hoc search. Rows, tissue labels and model types should not be counted as independent discoveries.

A [public unfiltered ADHD follow-up](adhd_unfiltered/RESULTS.md) matches all eight sleep-selected RNA features. One nucleus-accumbens residual feature has a nominal ADHD association; its sleep–RNA PP4 is 0.565 and ADHD colocalization is unavailable. The older ADHD source may overlap cohorts used in the later Brain6 source, and this selected comparison is not established as independent replication or evidence of a shared causal mechanism.

A [FinnGen R12 supplement lookup](finngen_supplement/RESULTS.md) finds published RNA–depression credible-set colocalizations for two tissue/feature identifiers also appearing in the selected insomnia results. These are separate pairwise analyses, use CLPP rather than coloc PP4 and do not establish shared insomnia–ADHD–depression causality.

A [comparison with archived Brain6 single-signal PIPs](fine_mapping_comparison/RESULTS.md) finds much smaller conditional disease PIPs for the four selected RNA/depression leads than for the two highlighted insomnia–ADHD markers. Single-signal and LD limitations prevent a definitive shared-versus-distinct causal conclusion.
