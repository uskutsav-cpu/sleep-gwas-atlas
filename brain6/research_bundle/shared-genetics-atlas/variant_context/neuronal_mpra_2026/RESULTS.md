# Neuronal reporter assay: access and candidate lookup

Salomon et al. (2026), [preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13405209/), report a lentiviral massively parallel reporter assay in induced excitatory neurons, with sequence-level allelic effects. The preprint identifies [IGVFDS1419ZPHD](https://data.igvf.org/construct-library-sets/IGVFDS1419ZPHD/) as its data accession. A reporter assay measures expression from inserted sequence fragments, outside their normal genomic context; it does not directly measure endogenous chromatin accessibility or establish a disease mechanism.

The linked [neuronal analysis set](https://data.igvf.org/analysis-sets/IGVFDS2228LNRT/) has a released reporter-experiment file, IGVFFI9183PYQJ, containing replicate-level RNA/DNA data and per-oligo activity. Downloaded to the SSD at `/Volumes/Extreme SSD/brain6-igvf/IGVFFI9183PYQJ.tsv.gz`; SHA-256 `f5de5a2dbd4418307d6bdbc69de20d378f44bcabe319653b4ad930ee3919df6d`. A text search found none of rs62264764, rs62264767, rs2431108, rs77960, rs416223, rs1476535 or rs4916723. However, the file uses oligo identifiers rather than necessarily listing rsIDs, so this does **not** establish that these variants were untested.

The portal labels its linked variant-effects file IGVFFI8255MSFZ as **in progress** and its download returned HTTP 403; an older variant-effects file is marked **revoked**. Consequently no variant-level effect, significance test, negative result, or replication claim is inferred from this source. Revisit once a released variant mapping and effect table are publicly accessible, verify assembly and alleles, and compare the prespecified leads. The assay is independent functional evidence, not an independent replication of a two-trait genetic association.
