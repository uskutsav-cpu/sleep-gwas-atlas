# Chromosome 5 cell-specific expression–GWAS colocalization pilot

Post hoc follow-up of rs4916723 after seeing its Brain6 ADHD fine-mapping and brain xQTL results. Use the frozen GRCh37 Brain6 region chr5:87185500–88185500 and the two regional insomnia and ADHD files authenticated against the AlphaGenome pilot provenance. Lift each variant independently with the pinned hg19-to-hg38 chain; require a unique same-chromosome positive-strand mapping. The lifted endpoints are chr5:87889683–88889683, inclusive. Test two preselected context/target combinations from the aging-brain ROSMAP combined single-nucleus data: astrocyte LINC02060 (ENSG00000250156) and oligodendrocyte progenitor TMEM161B-AS1 (ENSG00000247828). Extract all tested SNP associations in this window from NG00184.v1 rather than significance-filtered results; validate the complete chromosome 5 archives and tabix indices using publisher MD5, the association orientation and sample metadata, and selected exact-variant statistics against portal results. Only use association records for the chosen gene and cell context.

Match GWAS and QTL records by uniquely lifted position and exact unordered nonpalindromic SNV allele pair; align GWAS beta to ALT and require finite betas, positive SE and 0<ALT frequency<1. Exclude ambiguous or duplicate keys. The GWAS sources lack allele frequency, so this does not independently verify frequency compatibility; require at least 500 matched markers per fit. For each of two targets and two GWAS, run single-signal coloc ABF with quantitative expression effect prior SD 0.15 and case-control GWAS SD 0.20; vary p12 1e-5 and 1e-6, with p1=p2=1e-4. Report H0–H4 for all pairs and matched/available counts. Check posterior arithmetic independently. Treat this as a selected exploratory model, with no FDR-controlled discovery claim; the two cell types and two GWAS are not independent. Do not infer mediation, gene function, validated causal SNP or sleep–ADHD sharing from expression–GWAS pairwise results. Existing prior estimates, sample overlap, ancestry, source coverage, missing variants, LD mismatch and multiple signals remain limitations. The selected window may truncate the cis/TAD region. Do not use AlphaGenome outputs in the model, retain PGC-derived matched rows on the SSD, and do not label this a LAVA rescue. Cite NG00184 and required NIAGADS acknowledgment.
