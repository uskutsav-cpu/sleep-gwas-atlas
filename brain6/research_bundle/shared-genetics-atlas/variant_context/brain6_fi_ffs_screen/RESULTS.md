# FI, FFS and HFRS at 425 selected Brain6 variants

All **425 previously selected Brain6 variants** matched exact rsID, GRCh37 position and allele-pair rows in each full archived FI and FFS source. This is a bounded look at the existing candidate set, not a genome-wide frailty search. At a numerical threshold of 0.05/850 for 425 variants × two outcomes, **129 FI rows** and **71 FFS rows** cross. All 71 FFS rows also cross for FI and lie in the selected chromosome 5 region near rs2431108; the 58 FI-only threshold patterns lie mainly in chromosome 7, with one in chromosome 14. These are correlated **SNP rows**, not independent loci. A threshold pattern is not proof of trait specificity.

For a separate three-score comparison, all 425 candidates matched the previously checksum-verified UKB HFRS extraction by ID, coordinate and alleles. **None** passes the post-hoc `425 × max(p_FI,p_FFS,p_HFRS) < 0.05` numerical benchmark; the smallest max-p is 0.00092 at rs10262103, giving 0.391 after multiplying by 425. If the three marginal p-values are valid, max-p is a conservative test of association with all three traits without requiring independent samples; it does not test whether they share a causal variant. The 850-slot FI/FFS screen and 425-slot three-score max-p screen are separate families, not a joint or atlas-wide correction.

Candidate selection and many prior analyses preceded these screens, so neither numerical benchmark provides confirmatory family-wise error control for the broader project. FI contains sleep and fatigue; FFS includes exhaustion; HFRS has a hospital-diagnosis definition. UKB contributes to all three sources and individual overlap is unverified. Source populations, effect scales and uncertainty differ. The findings do not establish an independent sleep–ADHD–frailty locus, target gene, shared causal variant, item-excluded genetic association, or resolution of failed local LAVA gates. A missing crossing is not evidence of no effect.
