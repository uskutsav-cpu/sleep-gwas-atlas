# Chromosome 5 common-support pairwise sensitivity

This is a post-selected, descriptive comparison, not a new three-trait colocalization model. Intersect the previously matched testis RP11-6N13.1 expression–insomnia and expression–ADHD tables by exact GRCh38 variant representation, and assert the eQTL statistics and alleles agree. Use this identical intersection to rerun three pairwise single-signal ABF coloc comparisons: expression–insomnia, expression–ADHD, and insomnia–ADHD. Use SD 0.15 for expression and 0.20 for each case–control GWAS; p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-5 or 1e-6. Compute the H4-conditional SNP distributions and 95% sets at the default effect priors. Report set overlap and conditional leaders. SNP probabilities are conditional on H4 and cannot be multiplied across comparisons; common leaders do not imply a three-trait posterior or establish causality. Comparisons reuse the same regional source data and testis eQTL, and do not independently replicate any finding or rescue failed LAVA QC. They are subject to single-signal and prior assumptions, source/LD/coverage uncertainty, sample overlap, lack of source allele frequencies and tissue limitations. The common-support set is selected through GTEx availability and matching and is not the complete regional GWAS support. No AlphaGenome prediction enters the model.

Post-hoc follow-up after seeing that the conditional sets differ: query 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 phased EUR genotypes at all variants in any of the three conditional 95% sets. Require unique positive-strand liftover and exact rsID, position, and allele-pair match; calculate all pairwise phased r² using 503 EUR reference participants and pairwise-complete calls. This small reference is not study-matched; r² cannot establish causality, signal identity, conditional independence or three-trait sharing. The selection is post hoc; no new test or correction is implied.
