# Chromosome 5: dense GTEx expression–GWAS assessment

This exploratory follow-up is selected after reviewing the Brain6 trait–trait coloc, AlphaGenome predictions and exact-variant GTEx associations. It does not validate LAVA or establish a causal gene. Analyze GTEx v8 nominal cis-expression statistics for RP11-6N13.1 (ENSG00000251574) in testis, where rs2431108 and rs77960 have significant-pair records, and frontal cortex BA9, an exploratory brain tissue. Extract the lifted positive-strand GRCh38 interval chr5:104112267–105112267 corresponding to Brain6's GRCh37 chr5:103447968–104447968 window. All nominal rows, not only significant associations, are required. The selected 1-Mb window may truncate the gene's cis region.

Use the previously frozen Brain6 insomnia and ADHD regional source files, hash-check against the earlier provenance, and lift unique positive-strand coordinates using the pinned chain. Match unique exact non-palindromic SNP position/allele-pair records, align GWAS effect to QTL ALT, and exclude invalid statistics and ambiguous/duplicate matches. Brain6 inputs lack frequencies; the match does not validate allele orientation independently. Test two tissues × two GWAS using single-signal coloc only if at least 500 variants match. Use p1=p2=1e-4 and p12 of 1e-5 and 1e-6; use effect prior SD 0.15 for expression and 0.20 for case–control GWAS and additional 0.1 and 0.3 SD for both. Report every pair and all hypothesis posteriors, including unavailable comparisons, counts and coverage, with no multiple-testing discovery claim. GTEx tissues, brain, testis and both GWAS are not independent experiments. The method does not resolve multiple signals and the expression gene may not be the causal effector. Testis results cannot be extrapolated to brain. Compare exact-variant expression p-values with GTEx significant-pair records where matched. Independent cohorts, sample overlap, ancestry, missing variants, prior assumptions, and tissue relevance remain limitations. Predictions are not used in the statistical model.
