# Chromosome 5 lead in the FinnGen immune-cell atlas

On 28 October 2026 we searched rs2431108, the lead in the stronger shared insomnia–ADHD region, in the public [CASCADE variant browser](https://cascade.finngen.fi/variant/chr5_104612267_T_C). It identifies chr5:104612267 T>C on GRCh38, matching the allele and position in the project's earlier [follow-up](../../replication/six_lead_joint/RESULTS.md). The atlas measures expression and chromatin in peripheral immune cells, not brain; the [study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2) describes 1,108 RNA and 1,093 paired chromatin donors across the overall atlas, not necessarily per cell-type test.

The browser reports **no CASCADE classification or credible-set molecular-QTL hit** for this variant and no displayed marginal caQTL hits. Its marginal eQTL section displays 11 cell-type rows; the lowest nominal p is 0.015 for CD4 T cells (displayed β +0.060, SE 0.025), with no credible-set probability shown. The displayed table did not identify the expression feature by name, so we do not assign it to a gene. An illustrative Bonferroni calculation based only on these 11 displayed rows gives 0.165 for the smallest rounded p-value. This is a post-selected subset, not a comprehensive correction across the larger project, and the rows share donors and include overlapping cell categories. Effect-allele and feature-specific sample-size conventions were not independently verified; no risk-direction comparison is made.

The page additionally displays a **Siraj et al. reporter-assay summary**, labeled tested but inactive, with aggregate p=0.093. This is the same published assay family previously examined in the [project's reporter review](../siraj_2026_mpra/RESULTS.md), not a new independent experiment. A reporter construct is not a direct endogenous chromatin perturbation; its result does not establish absence of an effect in a different cell type or developmental stage. The page reports no GWAS colocalizations for this variant in its displayed filtered set, which is not evidence that all traits were tested or that all regional signals lack colocalization.

Neither the nominal immune expression row nor the filtered negative results identify a gene or mechanism for sleep or ADHD. The project's chromosome 5 single-signal colocalization remains encouraging under its model, while the local quality gates remain failed. This targeted lookup is not independent two-trait replication and does not resolve the [prior brain-cell QTL limitations](../chr5_brain_xqtl/RESULTS.md).

## Scope and reproducibility

We used the browser's rsID search, expanded all 11 marginal expression rows and read its summary and reporter sections. P-values and estimates are rounded, manually transcribed browser outputs; underlying full statistics, the identity of the displayed molecular feature, source sample overlap and full test coverage were not independently validated. The 11-row benchmark is descriptive and cannot support a discovery claim. No raw individual data were accessed or saved.
