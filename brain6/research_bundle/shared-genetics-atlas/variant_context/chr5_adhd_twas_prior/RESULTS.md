# Chromosome 5 ADHD transcriptome context

A 2026 [Abrishamcar et al. preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12934864/) used MetaBrain tissue expression models and the Demontis 2023 ADHD GWAS to test gene-level associations. Its cortex table reports omnibus TWAS p=2.59×10⁻⁷ for **MEF2C** and p=3.71×10⁻⁷ for **TMEM161B**, below the authors' stated 2.7×10⁻⁶ per-tissue threshold. These are model-based associations of genetically predicted expression with ADHD, not direct measurements of a Brain6 allele's effect or evidence about insomnia. The authors' conditional gene-level analysis also reports both genes in the same broad chromosome 5 region. Coordinate build for that printed region was not established in this check; shared gene names alone do not establish identity of the underlying signals.

The preprint's separate cortex molecular-colocalization table reports TMEM161B maximum PP4=0.0235 with no colocalizing credible-set pair in the reported fit. The absence of MEF2C from that table should not be interpreted as a negative test: the authors report results only where their coverage and signal requirements were met. These results qualify the gene hypotheses rather than prove or exclude mediation. TMEM161B is a distinct gene from the antisense transcript **TMEM161B-AS1** implicated by the project's selected cell-specific QTL lookup.

The preprint explicitly reuses the 2023 PGC ADHD GWAS, which is also the Brain6 discovery source, so its TWAS and QTL analyses are **not independent ADHD-cohort replication**. It is a preprint and has not been established here as peer reviewed. Brain6's chr5:87 Mb rs4916723 region is strongly ADHD-prioritized but has low insomnia–ADHD shared-signal posterior under the tested priors; it is distinct from the high-colocalization chr5:103 Mb region. No result here establishes a sleep–ADHD shared causal variant, the target gene of rs4916723, or a FOXP2–MEF2C interaction in this locus. The existing [brain-cell QTL comparison](../chr5_brain_xqtl/dense_coloc/RESULTS.md) and [gene hypothesis review](../../pathway_hypotheses/GENE_PATHWAY_REVIEW.md) use different assays, tissues and models and cannot be equated with this TWAS.

Evidence is taken from preprint Tables 1–3 and its Methods; the author-reported p-values and posterior are not recalculated here. Independently ascertained ADHD and sleep data, harmonized multi-signal molecular analysis and direct perturbation remain needed.
