# FinnGen–LINC03051 statistic and prior sensitivities

The conclusion that the FinnGen fit is dominated by the **expression-only hypothesis** remains under the tested numerical sensitivities, but the exact posterior depends on assumed effect and sharing priors. These checks do not validate the model or exclude a secondary shared signal.

On the same 2,441 variants, the largest absolute difference between FinnGen beta/SE Z and the signed, two-sided normal Z reconstructed from its reported p-value is **0.0000151**. For the brain-cell QTL, the median difference is **0.00191**, the 95th percentile is **0.163**, and the maximum is **0.530**. At rs9829042 (chr3:117956015 A>G), the strongest matched QTL marker, beta/SE gives Z = 11.612 while the reported p-value corresponds to normal Z = 11.082. A later [distribution diagnostic](qtl_t_distribution/RESULTS.md) found that a t distribution with approximately 673 degrees of freedom closely reproduces the selected QTL p-values. This is consistent with the study's stated TensorQTL method, though the source run's actual degrees of freedom and covariates were not independently recovered. It should not be interpreted as evidence of an allele error.

With the original disease-effect prior SD 0.20 and p12 = 10⁻⁵, the original beta/SE fit gives H1 = 0.7655 and H4 = 0.1183. Substituting p-derived Z values while retaining the reported SE-based variance gives H1 = 0.7664 and H4 = 0.1173 for the QTL, or H1 = 0.7655 and H4 = 0.1183 for FinnGen. Replacing both gives H1 = 0.7664 and H4 = 0.1173. **These p/SE combinations are hybrid numerical stress tests, not a validated source likelihood or proof of correct inference.** Other posterior components, especially very small H0/H2, also change; see the full table.

With beta/SE and p12 = 10⁻⁵, changing the disease-effect prior SD from 0.10 to 0.30 changes H1 from 0.6284 to 0.8294 and H4 from 0.1913 to 0.0857. With p12 = 10⁻⁶, H4 ranges from 0.0231 to 0.00929. These are assumed prior settings, not confidence intervals or probabilities that LINC03051 causes insomnia. The original and FinnGen insomnia sources differ in phenotype, sample, ancestry and power; the region is selected, and the model allows only one signal per trait.

The computations use the [earlier matched data](../matched.tsv); the reported QTL statistic discrepancy requires source-method review. AlphaGenome was not an input, the failed LAVA gates remain failed, and no gene or disease mechanism is established.
