# Post-fit reported-statistic and effect-prior sensitivity

On the identical 2,441 matched variants, compare reported FinnGen p-values with two-sided normal p-values reconstructed from beta/SE, and likewise check the QTL p-values. Evaluate how results change when replacing each FinnGen Z magnitude by its two-sided p-derived normal magnitude while retaining the sign from beta and the original SE-derived variance. Apply the same substitution for QTL as a 2×2 descriptive stress test. This p/SE hybrid is not the source likelihood or a validated coloc method; it tests sensitivity to inconsistent statistic representations. Use exact source p values strictly between zero and one, retain all variants, report any exclusions, and use stable log probabilities.

Repeat standard beta/SE ABFs across disease-effect prior SDs 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3, keeping QTL SD 0.15, p1=p2=1e-4, and p12 1e-5 or 1e-6. These priors are hypothetical and do not establish calibration. Compare H0–H4, and independently check the baseline against the previously saved R posteriors. The region and source choice were previously examined; these are post-hoc numerical sensitivities, not independent validation, a causal gene or a repair of local QC.
