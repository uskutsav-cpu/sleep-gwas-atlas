# Same-marker FinnGen insomnia sensitivity for excitatory LINC03051 eQTL

Written before inspecting FinnGen regional results. Start with the previously validated excitatory-cell LINC03051–Brain6 insomnia matched dataset and its recorded hash; this is a selected subset, not the full eQTL cis window. Verify the archived FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA compressed SHA-256 against its existing provenance record. Use the publisher's tabix index to obtain the genomic interval and validate index MD5 against publisher metadata. Match unique non-palindromic chromosome, GRCh38 position and exact allele pairs to the existing table; align FinnGen beta and ALT frequency to the molecular ALT allele. Exclude duplicates and invalid statistics; require at least 500 matches. Retain the existing Brain6 and FinnGen insomnia beta and standard errors on exactly the same matched SNP support and compare single-signal eQTL–GWAS ABF posteriors with the earlier priors: quantitative expression SD 0.15, case-control SD 0.20, p1=p2=1e-4 and p12 in {1e-5, 1e-6}. Compare source frequencies descriptively; the QTL and Finnish cohorts need not have the same ancestry or sample.

A source-sensitivity comparison is not an independent QTL experiment or independent two-trait causal replication. Ascertainment, cohort ancestry, sample overlap, effect scale and power differ; the selected region and matched support can truncate causal signals; one-signal colocalization cannot rule out secondary sharing. The QTL's remote regional source is partially retrieved and not fully hashed; maintain previous provenance and do not infer mediation, causal gene, validation of AlphaGenome, or local-QC rescue. No AlphaGenome outputs enter the fit. Record source hashes, matching counts, priors and all posterior components; independently check posterior arithmetic against the saved R implementation.
