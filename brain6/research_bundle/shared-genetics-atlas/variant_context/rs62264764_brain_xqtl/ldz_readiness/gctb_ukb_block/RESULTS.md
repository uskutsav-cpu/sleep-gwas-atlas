# Public GCTB LD reference: feasibility and numerical check

**The extracted matrix failed the pre-specified positive-semidefinite check and is not admitted for fine-mapping or LD–Z diagnosis.** The public [GCTB resource](https://gctbhub.cloud.edu.au/software/gctb/) describes a 13-million-variant, blockwise full LD reference calculated from 10,000 unrelated UK Biobank participants of European ancestry. Its 206 GB archive cannot fit on the available disk. Using HTTP ranges, we streamed the complete compressed SNP-index member and the complete chromosome 3 block 341 member, and verified each against the CRC recorded in the ZIP central directory. The full archive was neither downloaded nor independently authenticated; CRC is an error check, not a cryptographic publisher signature. The [GCTB source](https://github.com/jianzeng/GCTB/blob/master/scr/eigen.cpp) describes row-wise lower-triangular float storage. The [archive inventory](archive_inventory.json), [SNP receipt](snp_receipt.json), and [matrix receipt](matrix_receipt.json) record retrieval details.

The reference matches **1,547 of 1,548** earlier variants using GRCh37 position and exact allele pairs; none of the matched alleles is A/T or C/G strand-ambiguous. The analysis aligns correlations to the earlier ALT allele. The unmatched variant is excluded, not filled with zeros. The raw matched diagonal ranges from 0.9998660 to 0.9999307. After scaling by the square roots of diagonal entries, the matrix is symmetric with unit diagonal, but its smallest eigenvalue is **−0.000348**, and **108 eigenvalues are below −10⁻⁶**. The cause is not established. This violates the [protocol](PROTOCOL.md); close-to-one diagonals and small negative eigenvalues cannot simply be assumed harmless. The [QC receipt](qc_receipt.json) documents the checks; [raw matched matrix](../../../../PUBLICATION.md#external-dependencies) (external target: `matched_ld.npz`), [aligned SNPs](matched_snps.tsv), and [normalized matrix](../../../../PUBLICATION.md#external-dependencies) (external target: `strand_safe_ld.tsv.gz`) preserve the material for review.

The normalized matrix gives r² ≈ **0.99923** between rs62264764 and rs62264767, descriptively consistent with the earlier public ADSP pairwise estimate of 0.99919. This is a pairwise tagging observation, not validation of the entire matrix, a disease replication, or proof of a causal variant. Median absolute ALT-frequency difference versus the 503-person 1000 Genomes reference is 0.0109 (maximum 0.0586), which does not establish compatibility with the actual QTL or GWAS cohorts.

No new LD–Z, SuSiE or coloc fit was run on this matrix. The diagnostic script is deliberately stopped at the quality gate. Any repair or shrinkage would require a separately justified protocol, sensitivity analysis, validation and appropriate statistical review; a repaired matrix would still be external to the brain-QTL, ADHD and Finnish insomnia cohorts and may overlap UK Biobank discovery samples. The earlier local-correlation and multi-signal limitations remain in force. No AlphaGenome output was used in this check.

A later [post-failure repair sensitivity](REPAIR_SENSITIVITY_RESULTS.md) compared two numerical adjustments with the earlier reference. Neither adjustment establishes study-matched LD; the ADHD LD–Z mismatch remained substantial. No new fine-mapping or colocalization result was admitted.
