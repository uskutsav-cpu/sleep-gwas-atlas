# ADHD study-coverage signature audit

**The ten large ADHD residuals share a source direction-string pattern, but this does not prove the reason for their mismatch.** In the post-hoc audit, all 1,547 ADHD variants matched unique source records by rsID, build-37 position and allele pair, and reproduced ALT-aligned effect and standard error. All source direction strings had 13 characters. At the third position, 48 variants had a question mark. Under both GCTB numerical adjustments, all 10 variants with absolute standardized residual above 5 were among those 48; the other 38 were not above 5. Among the 1,499 without a question mark at that position, none exceeded 5. Under the earlier 1000 Genomes reference, 5 of the 48 and 1 of the other 1,499 exceeded 5.

The [release README](https://ipsych.dk/fileadmin/iPSYCH/PGC/README_ADHD_meta_Jan2022.pdf) describes `Direction` as cohort effect directions and `Nca`/`Nco` as variant-specific case and control counts. The [public METAL source](https://github.com/statgen/METAL/blob/master/metal/Main.cpp) uses `?` as a default when a study has no entry; we have not verified the exact software version or encoding used for this release. We therefore treat the shared symbol as a **possible missing-study signature**, without assigning it a named cohort or claiming to know participant overlap. Other characters, including `0`, were not treated as missing.

These are correlated variants in a locus selected for follow-up. Both the position and residual threshold were inspected post hoc; the counts are not independent observations or a calibrated association test. The other 38 variants with the same symbol and no large residual show that the pattern alone does not predict an outlier. Differing study coverage is a stronger, testable lead for diagnosing the summary-statistic covariance, alongside reference LD and other sources of model mismatch; it is not established as the cause. A next analysis would require documented cohort order and per-study or pairwise sample overlap, ideally study-specific summary statistics and matched LD. Sample counts alone cannot recover that covariance.

The [protocol](ADHD_DIRECTION_PROTOCOL.md), [anonymized pattern counts](adhd_direction_audit.json), [code](adhd_direction_audit.py) and [verification](direction_verification.json) document the audit. Source effect signs and original records were not reproduced. The failed local-correlation gate and the lack of validated multi-signal colocalization remain unchanged; no variant was removed or flipped.
