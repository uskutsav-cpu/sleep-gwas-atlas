# Post-hoc study-coverage signature audit

Following the observation that ten large ADHD LD–Z residuals have lower `N_AUDIT`, inspect the original summary statistics' `Direction` and case/control counts for all 1,547 analyzed variants. The [release README](https://ipsych.dk/fileadmin/iPSYCH/PGC/README_ADHD_meta_Jan2022.pdf) identifies Direction as the direction of effects in included cohorts and Nca/Nco as case/control counts with information. First record observed characters and lengths, then describe binary question-mark position patterns, without assigning positions to named cohorts. Published [METAL source](https://github.com/statgen/METAL/blob/master/metal/Main.cpp) uses `?` as its default for absent study data; whether that exact version or logic produced this release is not independently established, and `0` is not treated as missing. The reported patterns are a proxy for possible study availability, not verified participant overlap.

Use the source SHA-256 already independently checked, unique rsID, chromosome 3, GRCh37 position and exact allele set. Group `|standardized residual| > 5` and `<= 5` for each of the three existing references; report anonymized position masks, counts, effective-N and INFO summaries, with all masks rather than only favorable groups. Both the grouping and diagnostic cutoffs are post hoc, variants are correlated and no significance test, independent replication or causal conclusion is appropriate. Source `INFO` is described by the publisher as a weighted average among contributing cohorts, not necessarily comparable genotype quality in a single cohort. Do not distribute original effect signs or source rows. Neither cohort identities nor subject overlap can be reconstructed uniquely from the available aggregate counts and masks; do not remove variants or fit a colocalization model based on this exploration.
