# Within-European reference sensitivity for three selected LD pairs

The three pairs with the largest earlier between-panel differences include **low-frequency variants** in the 503-person 1000 Genomes European-labeled reference. Splitting its five constituent population labels produces highly unstable r² estimates, which makes a simple pooled-reference comparison harder to interpret.

For the first pair, chr3:117835650 T>G and chr3:117858571 G>A, the observed dosage r² ranges from **0.159 to 1.000** across five groups of 91–107 people. The first variant's ALT frequency ranges from 0.0047 to 0.0606 across those groups: at the low end, one ALT allele was observed among 214 chromosomes. In that group, **1,085 of 3,000** bootstrap resamples were monomorphic and had undefined r². Intervals reported in [the table](population.tsv) use only defined replicates; for low counts they can be misleading and do not describe a stable population distribution.

The other two selected pairs also contain low-count subgroups and undefined bootstrap replicates. These groups are small and the pairs were chosen **after** the largest differences were observed. Sampling, ancestry structure, relatedness, genotype quality and the definition of LD can all influence results. This does not establish an ancestry-specific biological effect, a significant between-population difference, or which reference matches the QTL, insomnia or ADHD donors. ADSP's larger r² is not assumed to be an average of these groups. The specific rs62264764–rs62264767 lead pair is **not** one of the three pairs tested here.

[Protocol](POPULATION_PROTOCOL.md), [code](population.py), [all 15 group-pair results](population.tsv), and [receipt](population_receipt.json) record group sizes, ALT frequencies, point estimates, valid and monomorphic bootstrap counts. The original panel and marker hashes and pooled dosage values were verified; the remote VCF and index were not fully hashed. Bootstrapping resampled 3,000 individuals per group with replacement using seed 20261028; the intervals are descriptive within-panel summaries, not post-selection-corrected tests. The public ADSP source suppresses weak pairs, and this analysis does not create a complete LD matrix, validate multi-signal colocalization or repair LAVA gates.
