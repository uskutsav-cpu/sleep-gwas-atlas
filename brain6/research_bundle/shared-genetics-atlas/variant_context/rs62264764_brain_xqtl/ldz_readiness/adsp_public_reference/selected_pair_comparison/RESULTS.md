# Selected rs62264764-region LD pairs: public ADSP and 1000 Genomes

Among 42 **post-selected** markers chosen from earlier LD–Z diagnostic outliers and the two lead variants, the public ADSP R5 file returned exact position-and-allele records for **50 of 861 possible pairs**. For these returned pairs, the median absolute difference in r² versus the existing 503-person 1000 Genomes European-labeled reference was **0.034**; eight differed by at least 0.10 and three by at least 0.20. The largest observed difference was 0.272: r² = 0.706 in the smaller reference and 0.434 in ADSP for chr3:117835650 T>G and chr3:117858571 G>A.

This is a targeted comparison of **available, threshold-selected pairs**, not a representative estimate of reference error or proof that either panel matches the GWAS or QTL donors. The ADSP publisher retains only eligible pairs with |R| ≥ 0.2; the other 811 have no unique exact returned record and are **unknown, not zero**. Variants were chosen because earlier diagnostics were extreme, so this analysis cannot provide a population-wide LD discrepancy or calibrate those diagnostics. The two references also differ in sample size, ancestry composition, ascertainment, relatedness and potentially LD statistic definitions; the 503-person reference's genotype dosage correlation and the publisher's reported LD r² should not be assumed identical estimands. No signed correlation or allele-effect orientation is inferred from r².

A [post-selected resampling sensitivity](BOOTSTRAP_RESULTS.md) found that two of the three largest ADSP values fell below the 1000 Genomes within-panel percentile interval. This is not a significance test or proof of why references differ. A [within-panel dosage-versus-haplotype check](METRIC_RESULTS.md) shows smaller metric differences for the same pairs, without establishing which ADSP mode was used.

The previously verified rs62264764–rs62264767 row appears identically in this extraction. Its ADSP r² is 0.99919, supporting near-perfect tagging in that panel; it does not identify a causal variant. The observed differences provide a reason to retain the existing reference-mismatch concern, but do not explain the LD–Z outliers, repair the failed LAVA gates, demonstrate biological mediation or justify substituting a sparse ADSP matrix into SuSiE. Appropriate study-matched LD and validated multi-signal modeling remain needed.
