# Exploratory ADSP–1000 Genomes LD-pair comparison

Before examining ADSP pair-level values, select up to 10 variants with largest absolute previously computed LD–Z residual separately in each of oligodendrocyte and excitatory LINC03051 eQTL, insomnia GWAS, and ADHD GWAS, plus rs62264764 and rs62264767. Ties use lexical variant ID; union variants once and record the reasons. These are post-selected diagnostic outliers, not an unbiased variant or genome sample. The existing residuals were calculated in the 503-person European-labeled 1000 Genomes reference; selection does not establish a problem with any given marker.

Stream the complete public ADSP R5 NHW chromosome 3 compressed file, check length, MD5 and SHA-256 against the prior independently obtained source receipt, and require successful complete gzip decompression. Extract all rows with both positions in the selected set; uniquely match GRCh38 positions and reference/alternate alleles to the retained 1000 Genomes marker set, exclude duplicate and ambiguous pairs. Compare reported ADSP r² and the squared 1000 Genomes dosage-LD correlation for matched available pairs. Report their absolute differences, number above prespecified 0.1 and 0.2 descriptive thresholds, and pair coverage. These metrics are postselected descriptive diagnostics, not significance tests, error rates, or independent validation. Differences between dosage and publisher LD calculation, finite 503-person sampling, ancestry, ascertainment, relatedness, allele semantics and rounding can all matter.

The public file suppresses |R|<0.2 and may omit variants for other reasons; unreturned pairs must never be assigned correlation zero or treated as missing at random. Analyze only returned matched pairs, report denominator and exclusion causes. Do not use this sparse comparison as a full study-matched LD matrix, SuSiE model, LAVA repair, causal mechanism or trait replication. Do not use AlphaGenome scores.

An initial text-pattern filter was interrupted before completing because it was CPU-bound; its partial output is retained and is not analyzed. The restarted filter reads the two numeric position columns directly and is checked against synthetic rows before use. The same full-source checksum and gzip completion gates apply.
