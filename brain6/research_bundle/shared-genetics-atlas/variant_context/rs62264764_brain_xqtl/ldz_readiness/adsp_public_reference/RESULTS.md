# rs62264764 and rs62264767 in a larger public ADSP LD reference

The public ADSP R5 European-labeled reference reports **r = 0.99959 and r² = 0.99919** for the two chromosome 3 variants, using a panel described as 26,042 individuals. The record identifies GRCh38 chr3:117920728 G>A (rs62264764) and chr3:117923158 A>C (rs62264767). This corroborates the earlier finding of r² = 1.0 in the 503-person 1000 Genomes EUR reference: both panels show very strong tagging. The estimates are panel observations, not independent replications of an insomnia or ADHD association.

The reported R is as supplied by the source; precise dosage/effect orientation should be verified before using it for signed-effect modeling. Very high LD does not identify which variant affects a molecular phenotype, establish LSAMP or LINC03051 as the effector, or demonstrate the same LD in the discovery, Finnish or brain-QTL participants. The panel is ascertained for Alzheimer-disease genetics; ancestry, relatedness, sample overlap, and LD are not necessarily matched to the studies of interest. The panel size is the publisher's group count, not a verified per-pair complete-genotype count.

The full 5,043,829,695-byte compressed chromosome source was streamed without being stored. Its MD5 matched the publisher manifest and gzip decompression completed successfully; SHA-256 was recorded. One row matched the requested pair and its coordinates and allele labels matched the target definitions. The reported r² is consistent with the rounded R. The [previous 1000 Genomes calculation](../../../../replication/rs62264764_mvp/RESULTS.md) gives its reference samples and haplotype limitations.

This is a thresholded pairwise file: only |R| ≥ 0.2 entries are supplied for eligible variants. It cannot reconstruct the full signed LD matrix needed for the multi-signal model; the [reference assessment](REFERENCE_ASSESSMENT.md) describes the access and matrix limitations. No local LAVA quality gate is rescued and no AlphaGenome prediction is validated by LD alone.
