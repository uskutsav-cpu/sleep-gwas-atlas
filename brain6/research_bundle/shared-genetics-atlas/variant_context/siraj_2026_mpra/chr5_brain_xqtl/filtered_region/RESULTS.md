# Local context for the rs12658032 brain methylation association

A [FunGen-xQTL regional query](https://xqtl.niagads.org/download/region/chr5%3A104400000-104700000) returned 391 HMT-significant rows. Restricting to the same Knight-ADRC parietal-cortex CpG `cg24905254` yielded **131 selected variant records**. The original rs12658032 row matches the earlier exact-variant download. One of several tied strongest returned markers is **rs1442112** (GRCh38 chr5:104452850 C>T), source p=6.52×10⁻¹¹ and source FDR=7.90×10⁻⁹. These associations concern the same CpG and cohort, so they are correlated evidence, not 131 independent replications.

In 503 European-labeled 1000 Genomes reference individuals, rs1442112 has r²≈**0.289** with rs12658032 and r²≈**0.242** and **0.241** with the sleep–ADHD markers rs2431108 and rs77960, respectively. The 95% individual-bootstrap intervals are 0.253–0.325, 0.209–0.276 and 0.209–0.274. The reference panel is not study-matched; the strongest *returned* marker need not be the true molecular causal variant, and the query window may exclude stronger markers elsewhere. These LD estimates make a simple identical-lead interpretation less secure, but they do not establish different causal variants or exclude secondary shared signals.

The regional download is **significance-filtered**, not a complete set of tested variants; it cannot support unbiased dense colocalization or a reliable comparison of posterior causal signals. Neither a gene target nor methylation mediation of insomnia or ADHD has been established. The source p-value versus normal-tail discrepancy for rs12658032 remains as documented in the [exact-variant report](../RESULTS.md). The observed target and lead were selected after prior analyses, and the source FDR does not correct the full project search.
