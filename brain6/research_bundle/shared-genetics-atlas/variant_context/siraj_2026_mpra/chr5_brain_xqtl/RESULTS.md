# rs12658032: filtered brain molecular-QTL lookup

The public [FunGen-xQTL variant lookup](https://xqtl.niagads.org/variant/rs12658032) returned **one methylation QTL and no expression-QTL rows** for rs12658032. The reported association is for CpG `cg24905254` in bulk parietal cortex from Knight-ADRC, at GRCh38 chr5:104484889–104484891. The study-assigned annotation is AC099520.1; it is an annotation of a methylation target, **not an expression association with that gene**. The ALT G allele has source-reported methylation beta −0.002917 (SE 0.000587), nominal p=1.013×10⁻⁶ and source-reported FDR=4.966×10⁻⁵. The portal reports the SNP as 83,635 bp from the target. The downloadable portal table contains only its filtered significant associations; no returned expression row does not establish that expression was tested, absent, or unaffected.

The source's Z equals beta/SE to numerical precision. However, its reported p differs from a two-sided standard-normal tail calculated from that Z (6.61×10⁻⁷); the source test distribution or other processing was not independently verified. We retain the source p and FDR without treating the discrepancy as an error or recalculating its correction. The source FDR is not a project-wide correction for the post-selected candidate, tissues, targets and earlier searches. The [atlas preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13308301/) reports 419 samples for its Knight-ADRC parietal methylation dataset, but that is not a verified per-variant sample size for this row.

This is a measured statistical association with methylation in a bulk aging-brain sample, distinct from the predicted frontal DNase change and reporter skew. It does not demonstrate that the variant changes AC099520.1 expression, mediate either phenotype, share the sleep–ADHD causal signal, or resolve the activity and cell-context discrepancies. The tested marker was selected after previous association and assay analyses; associated markers in linkage are not independent discoveries. Confirming a mechanism requires dense methylation and GWAS summary statistics, study-appropriate LD and overlap checks, multi-signal colocalization and targeted perturbation.

[Downloaded source row](source.tsv), [verification code](verify.py) and [receipt](receipt.json) preserve the exact lookup and the statistical discrepancy. See the [reference-LD analysis](../chr5_lead_ld/RESULTS.md) and [developmental chromatin lookup](../chr5_developmental_lookup/RESULTS.md) for complementary evidence and limits. The failed LAVA quality gate remains unresolved.

A [filtered regional and LD follow-up](filtered_region/RESULTS.md) found stronger methylation markers at the same CpG that have moderate reference LD with the sleep–ADHD leads. The filtered rows cannot support dense colocalization or distinguish causal signals.

A subsequent [unfiltered methylation comparison](unfiltered_readiness/RESULTS.md) shows that the p-value difference is closely explained by an empirically inferred t distribution with 370 degrees of freedom. Exploratory single-signal models favor distinct dominant methylation and disease signals, subject to prior, window and model limitations.
