# rs12658032: developmental chromatin and gene-expression context

For the post-selected chromosome 5 candidate **rs12658032** (GRCh38 chr5:104568525 A>G), a complete scan of the already retrieved [Aygün et al. cortical developmental caQTL files](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-023-02959-0) found seven compatible variant–peak rows in neurons and seven in progenitors. The smallest nominal p values were **0.1467** and **0.1812**, respectively. No row was nominally below 0.05; a descriptive Bonferroni correction across the 14 returned rows gives 1.0 for each. This family was selected after the reporter result and does not account for the broader research search. The provided peak coordinates do not contain the variant under an assumed zero-based half-open interpretation. These are locus-level variant–peak associations in selected Model1A data, not direct experimental perturbations.

This targeted null result does not prove the variant has no chromatin effect: source selection, finite sample size, cell conditions, peak conventions and incomplete testing matter. The neuron and progenitor data reflect developmental cultures rather than adult frontal cortex or the SK-N-SH reporter cell line. The finding does not validate the AlphaGenome prediction, resolve the discordant reporter libraries or show that the variant cannot affect another cell type.

In the existing [GTEx testis expression–GWAS follow-up](../../chr5_dense_qtl/RESULTS.md), the same G allele has a nominal association with RP11-6N13.1 expression (slope −0.141, p=0.000151), a result from testis rather than brain. In that prior **single-signal** expression–GWAS analysis, rs12658032 is not in either conditional 95% shared-variant set and receives very small conditional weights. These are model-dependent values: the association may reflect linkage, models can miss secondary signals, and no gene-mediated sleep or ADHD mechanism has been established. Its substantial reference LD with the trait leads and the reporter library disagreement are described in the [LD follow-up](../chr5_lead_ld/RESULTS.md).
