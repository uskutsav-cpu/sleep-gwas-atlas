# Neural-progenitor assay candidates: chromosome 3 LD

Before examining LD, use the four chromosome 3 variants in the previously saved selected 425-variant Lee matrix screen (rs16827974, rs56376353, rs56385956, rs73187934). Match their recorded GRCh37 position, rsID and allele pairs plus rs62264764 in the same indexed 1000 Genomes Phase 3 reference used in the prior European LD analysis. Calculate pairwise phased haplotype r² using the A allele for rs62264764 and the source alternate allele for each candidate, with 2,000 within-person bootstrap resamples per pair; record exact counts and nonmatches. Classify r²≥0.8 as a descriptive strong-LD benchmark, not evidence of causality. Verify point values independently from aggregated haplotype counts and check panel hash; save no sample-level genotypes.

The earlier Lee allele models are provisional and the authors' full QC and published results were not reproduced. A matrix entry, low or high LD, or a provisional p-value alone does not establish a measured regulatory effect at rs62264764 or a shared sleep–ADHD signal. The 503-person EUR reference and remote VCF are not necessarily matched to the discovery studies; phase is estimated. Treat this as a post-selected prioritization exercise, not independent replication, a genome-wide survey or a basis to promote a gene. AlphaGenome outputs will not be used for training.
