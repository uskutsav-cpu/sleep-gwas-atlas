# One-base barcode mismatch diagnostic

Before scanning, use the four variants whose two alleles had mapping labels but no exact raw-label matches in the prior 42-candidate audit: rs185260, rs325485, rs4916723 and rs62369151. Construct all single-substitution neighbors of their recorded 20-base A/C/G/T barcode labels, and scan the same 44 gzip count files inside the public GEO archive. Report distinct mapped labels, distinct observed raw labels, count-file coverage and read totals by candidate and allele. Exact and reverse-complement mismatches were already investigated; do not infer that a one-base neighbor arose from a particular original barcode without additional evidence.

Random 20-base sequences can also lie one substitution apart, and correlated files repeatedly measure the same library; do not treat matches as independent replicates or evidence of the correct sample/construct assignment. Retain collisions when one observed label is a neighbor of more than one mapped label or tag. This diagnostic cannot reconstruct the authors' barcode filters, statistical result or target-gene effect and does not test rs62264764, which is absent from the matched count-matrix set. Verify source hashes against the earlier receipt and inspect each raw file to completion.
