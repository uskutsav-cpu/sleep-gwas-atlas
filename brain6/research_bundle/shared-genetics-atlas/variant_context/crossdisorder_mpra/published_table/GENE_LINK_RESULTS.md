# A circadian-related gene hypothesis from the published MPRA mapping

The authors' [Table S4](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0092867424014351-mmc5.xlsx) maps **rs6874138 to ENSG00000112874, NUDT12**, using an activity-by-contact (ABC) model. [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/gene/83594/) identifies this human gene as nudix hydrolase 12. The matching table record uses GRCh37 chromosome 5 position 103,899,596; the workbook does not include allele columns in this mapping sheet. In its GRCh38 annotation, NUDT12's closest gene boundary is about 1.0 Mb from the variant, so this is a long-distance computational link rather than nearest-gene annotation. The table does not supply a score or variant-specific perturbation result for this link.

This link suggests a **testable RNA-processing and circadian-regulation hypothesis**. A separate [experimental study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.11.108) found human NUDT12 can remove NAD caps from RNA and reported increased circadian clock transcripts in the liver of mice lacking Nudt12. Those mouse liver results do not show that rs6874138 regulates NUDT12, that the same process occurs in human neurons, or that it mediates insomnia or ADHD. The [Lee reporter result](RESULTS.md) and the ABC gene assignment come from the same study and are not independent validations of one another. rs6874138 is moderately linked to the prior insomnia–ADHD lead in the small reference panel; it is not established as the causal disease variant.

Neither rs6874138 nor rs325485 appeared in the authors' selected Meta-QTL or excitatory-neuron QTL mapping sheets; rs325485 also had no matching row in their combined gene-mapping sheet. These are **absent rows in selected tables**, not proof that either variant lacks a QTL or target gene. The previously analyzed one-megabase regional windows do not by themselves settle the distant NUDT12 link. A direct test would measure allele-specific endogenous enhancer activity and NUDT12 expression in suitable human neural cells, followed by independent sleep and ADHD genetic analyses with study-matched LD. No effect direction, gene mediation or LAVA rescue is inferred.

A subsequent [public brain-cell QTL lookup](nudt12_brain_qtl/RESULTS.md) found nominal astrocyte NUDT12 records for both variants, with p=0.855 and p=0.862, respectively, and no matching oligodendrocyte-progenitor records. Both astrocyte records are outside the standard cis window but inside the source's TAD-enhanced window. This weakens support from these particular astrocyte expression measurements, without ruling out another cellular context or validating the ABC prediction.

The workbook's ZIP CRCs were checked and its SHA-256 recorded; no publisher digest was available. No clinical interpretation follows from this candidate hypothesis.
