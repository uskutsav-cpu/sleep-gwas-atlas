# Published reporter comparison for four selected candidates

**rs62369151 is a candidate for functional follow-up in the ADHD-prioritized chromosome 5 region.** It is the only one of the four pre-existing cross-assay matches with source-reported allele-effect FDR below 0.05 in both the Lee neural-progenitor table (0.0114) and the Siraj SK-N-SH OL42 table (0.0442). The candidates were selected before this comparison, the tests have different families and assay conditions, and these values are not a combined or project-wide corrected test.

Lee labels this variant `MPRA-Allelic`; its element-level FDR is 0.226 and the element-class cell is blank, so the published table does not classify it as an `emVar`. In Siraj's OL42 row, both allele activity p-values after that study's Bonferroni correction equal 1.0; its allele-skew FDR alone does not satisfy the stated activity criterion. Allele-effect signs are not compared because orientation has not been independently reconciled. The assays are reporter constructs in different cell models; the results do not establish an endogenous enhancer effect, a target gene, or a sleep–ADHD causal mechanism.

In 503 European-labeled 1000 Genomes individuals, the post-selected variant has phased haplotype **r²=0.833** with rs4916723, the region's ADHD-prioritized lead. The reference is not matched to the GWAS or assay donor, and tagging does not prove causality. The existing Brain6 single-signal model assigns rs62369151 an ADHD PIP of approximately 0.0427 and insomnia PIP of 0.000132, versus 0.884 and 0.000243 for rs4916723; these are prior model-conditional scores, not replication or experimental probabilities. This region is distinct from the stronger chromosome 5 insomnia–ADHD colocalization.

A [brain-cell expression lookup](rs62369151_brain_qtl/RESULTS.md) finds that both linked markers have associations with LINC02060 in astrocytes and TMEM161B-AS1 in oligodendrocyte progenitors. The earlier regional single-signal model nevertheless favored distinct dominant ADHD and expression signals; the variant-level associations do not establish gene mediation or validate the reporter mechanism.
