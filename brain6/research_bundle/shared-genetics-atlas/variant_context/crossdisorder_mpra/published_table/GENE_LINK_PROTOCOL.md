# Published reporter-variant gene-link audit

After finding rs325485 and rs6874138 as published emVars, inspect the same article's publisher-hosted Table S4, which lists variant–gene mappings. Validate the XLSX archive and exact table descriptions and record all matching records for these two preselected rsIDs in S4A (Meta-QTL), S4B (excitatory neuron single-cell QTL), and S4C (ABC, chromatin-loop and Meta-eQTL links). S4A/B describe GRCh38 coordinates and alleles; S4C describes GRCh37 positions but lacks alleles. Match position and rsID in each respective build and require allele-set agreement where supplied. Report each method distinctly and do not interpret an absent row as no effect or a failed experiment. Search all rows, not a selected subset, and record worksheet sizes and ambiguities.

Check human gene identifiers against a documented external or local source; do not infer target genes from proximity or conflate gene-mapping methods with trait colocalization. eQTL, ABC or loop links were selected for the authors' cross-disorder emVars, not independently for sleep–ADHD. If matching outputs have multiple genes, retain all. No QTL p-value or score is valid as a causal gene-mediation test or direct AlphaGenome validation; do not interpret association or reporter direction unless the allele conventions are established.
