# Reporter-effect direction: conditional orientation audit

A post-hoc check provides evidence for, but does not conclusively establish, the allele direction of the published Lee et al. reporter log-fold changes. The authors' archived [analysis pipeline](https://github.com/thewonlab/crossdisorder-MPRA) labels the first 22 count-matrix columns REF and the next 22 ALT, uses an `alt` indicator in `~alt + batch`, and reports coefficient 2. Under that design, the coefficient is ALT relative to REF. Across all **27** previously matched candidates, the published logFC equals the project's v1.24-like reconstruction to a maximum absolute difference of **1.40×10⁻¹⁴**. The GRCh37 source labels and recorded GRCh38 alleles have the same order for those candidates.

Conditional on the count-matrix labels, construct mapping and the archived pipeline matching the actual published analysis, this supports interpreting the published logFC as ALT relative to REF for these matches. This is not an independent biological replication: both calculations use the same released count data. The workbook description itself specifies A1 and A2 for the GWAS but does not explicitly define the MPRA logFC numerator. Earlier barcode-to-construct checks remain unresolved for some variants, including rs325485 and rs62369151, and matching unordered alleles or a model coefficient does not independently validate the underlying construct labels. Do not treat the conditional direction as a confirmed endogenous effect or as a GWAS risk direction.

A later [rs6874138 raw-count diagnostic](../../barcode_audit/rs6874138_raw/RESULTS.md) found incomplete reconciliation of the exact mapped ALT barcode reads with aggregate counts, especially for RNA. Sample order and preprocessing remain unverified, so the conditional direction should not be promoted to independently verified construct orientation.

For the two previously identified active, allele-dependent reporter elements, the conditional ALT-relative-to-REF reporter log₂ effects are **+0.875** at rs325485 and **+0.581** at rs6874138. Their existing adult frontal DNase model summaries for a 501-bp window are −0.0157 and −0.00330, respectively. Those signs describe different assays, cell states and contexts, and are sensitive to the orientation and window definitions; they do not establish a model error, a measured DNase effect, causal gene mediation or a sleep–ADHD–frailty mechanism. The selection was post hoc and cannot support a performance benchmark. No AlphaGenome outputs were used to train a model.

The [27-row comparison](comparison.tsv), [audit script](audit.py), and [input hashes](receipt.json) record the numerical and script checks. Source reporter FDR, assay selection, and barcode limitations are detailed in the [published-table review](../RESULTS.md) and [barcode audit](../../barcode_audit/RESULTS.md). A pre-result protocol was not recorded for this exploratory comparison.
