# Post-hoc extreme-LD–Z-flag removal sensitivity

After reviewing the prior pilot's kriging diagnostics and before fitting this sensitivity, remove the **union** of variants with |kriging standardized residual| >5 in any of the eQTL, insomnia or ADHD diagnostic tables. This threshold and the analysis itself are post hoc, not a pre-established calibration or allele-flip criterion; flagged variants may reflect real signals or reference mismatch. Apply the same remaining variant set to every trait and the same 1000 Genomes EUR LD submatrix. Do not flip alleles or claim excluded variants are wrong. Do not remove only variants that improve a preferred result.

Recompute original single-signal ABF and SuSiE-RSS fits with exactly the original settings, 10 effects, fixed seed, median previously used scalar Ns and fixed residual variance; also repeat ADHD at the previously used minimum N_AUDIT. If converged and credible sets exist, report all eQTL–insomnia and eQTL–ADHD credible-set pairs at p12=1e-5 and 1e-6, along with credible-set membership, convergence, purity and new kriging diagnostics. Compare numeric results to the original full support. Report the exact union, whether rs62264764 or the dominant eQTL lead was removed, all failures and hashes. These are diagnostic sensitivities, not corrected or independent tests. This removal does not validate sample size, study-matched LD, molecular colocalization or the failed LAVA QC; if meaningful signal changes occur they indicate instability, not which model is correct. No AlphaGenome outputs are used in fitting.
