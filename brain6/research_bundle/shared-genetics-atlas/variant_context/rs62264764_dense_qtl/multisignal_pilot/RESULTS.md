# Exploratory multi-signal pilot: adrenal LSAMP and insomnia/ADHD

**Status: NOT ADMITTED as validated molecular colocalization.** We ran a SuSiE/coloc pilot allowing multiple signals per trait, but the available LD is a small out-of-sample reference and the original study-specific LD and fully verified per-variant model semantics are unavailable. A later [source-frequency audit](frequency_qc/RESULTS.md) recovered frequency fields from the original files and verified the ADHD N_AUDIT formula. LD–Z diagnostics contain substantial outliers. The failed LAVA local-correlation gate remains failed; this pilot is not a local-correlation result or independent replication.

The reference QC retained **1,548 of 1,556** shared, allele-matched variants (99.5%) using 503 European-labeled 1000 Genomes participants. The signed dosage LD matrix is symmetric with unit diagonal. Its numerical rank at tolerance 10⁻⁸ is **467**, below the theoretical 502 maximum for 503 centered participants; no eigenvalues were below −10⁻⁸. The matrix is necessarily singular because it has more variants than reference people, and a small mixed-European panel need not represent the exact GWAS or GTEx samples. Eight variants were excluded by the pre-specified genotype/frequency checks. A later [original-source frequency comparison](frequency_qc/RESULTS.md) found close frequencies for the selected variants, but cannot validate study LD or eliminate allelic and model uncertainty.

Using SuSiE-RSS with up to 10 effects and the specified external-LD setting, the fits converged for adrenal *LSAMP*, insomnia, and both ADHD sample-size settings. The model returned **one** qualifying 95% credible set each for the eQTL (three variants) and insomnia (75 variants). rs62264764 is in the insomnia set; rs6804394, the prior adrenal lead, is in the eQTL set. The two sets do not contain each other's named markers. The ADHD fits returned **no qualifying credible set** at either the median or minimum N_AUDIT setting; this does not mean ADHD has no regional association or no shared signal. Convergence and credible-set purity are necessary computational checks, not proof that the fitted signals or their coverage are calibrated.

These preliminary posterior values favor distinct *dominant* signals under their respective models and chosen priors, consistent with the earlier lead-variant LD check. They do **not** establish distinct causal variants, reject a secondary shared signal, or prove that *LSAMP* has no role. Although the fitting model permits multiple signals, it produced only one qualifying set for each of the two compared traits; this run has not resolved whether additional shared signals exist. No ADHD–eQTL coloc posterior was available because no ADHD credible set was returned.

SuSiE's estimated LD–Z inconsistency parameter was approximately 0.0655 for the eQTL, 0.0168 for insomnia, and 0.0236 for ADHD. The [kriging diagnostic](kriging_summary.tsv) found maximum absolute standardized deviations of 5.40, 6.60 and 11.27, respectively; the counts above 3 and 5 are **post-hoc descriptive flags**, not pre-calibrated error thresholds. These patterns may reflect reference mismatch, sampling, real signals, or harmonization issues; they do not identify the cause or prove an allele flip. In particular, the ADHD N_AUDIT ranged from 64,699 to 128,214 across retained variants. SuSiE accepts one scalar N, so median and minimum settings were used as a sensitivity; neither establishes the true per-variant sample sizes or cures study–reference mismatch. GTEx had a median allele-count-derived N of 233, with some variation. The different source populations, genotype/association models, possible participant overlap, and selected window all constrain interpretation.

The second posterior uses [p12=10⁻⁶](coloc_insomnia_1e-06.tsv). `runtime.txt` records R 4.6.1, SuSiE 0.14.2 and coloc 5.2.3 from the existing project library. The whole remote VCF was not hashed. A separately implemented [verification](verification.json) checked matrix formatting and four single-signal posterior calculations; this is not validation of SuSiE, LD compatibility or biological inference. No AlphaGenome output was used to fit these models. Appropriate study-matched LD, independently confirmed source-frequency semantics, correct effective sample sizes, multi-signal sensitivity, independent cohorts and expert statistical review are required before a stronger conclusion.

A [post-hoc extreme-flag removal sensitivity](outlier_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) removed 10 variants and left the qualifying eQTL and insomnia credible sets unchanged, with very similar H4. Remaining LD–Z outliers and source/reference limitations mean this does not validate the result; ADHD still had no qualifying credible set.
