# Original-source frequency and sample-size audit

All **1,548** pilot variants had unique matches in both archived original ADHD and insomnia GWAS files by rsID, GRCh37 position and unordered allele pair. After orienting each effect to the pilot's ALT allele, source log(OR) and SE reproduced the pilot values within numerical tolerance. Both compressed files matched SHA-256 values recorded by the project's earlier harmonization QC. This verifies the relationship to the currently accessible source bytes, not all aspects of the original analysis or the exact samples behind the reference panel.

The ADHD source's `FRQ_U_186843` is documented as A1 frequency in controls in the [original PGC README](https://ipsych.dk/fileadmin/iPSYCH/PGC/README_ADHD_meta_Jan2022.pdf). After aligning A1 to ALT, its median absolute difference from the 1000 Genomes EUR reference frequency was **0.0028**; the maximum was **0.0564**. One variant exceeded 0.05 and none exceeded 0.10. For the 22 variants flagged by the post-hoc ADHD kriging |standardized deviation| > 3, the median difference was **0.0052** and the maximum **0.0437**.

The insomnia source column is labeled `MAF`; assuming that label denotes minor-allele frequency, the median absolute difference from reference minor-allele frequency was **0.0103**, the maximum **0.0531**, and three variants exceeded 0.05. For the 35 post-hoc insomnia kriging |standardized deviation| > 3 flags, the median difference was **0.0064** and the maximum **0.0296**. This MAF-only comparison cannot orient the effect allele; the source label's semantics still need independent documentation.

For all 1,548 ADHD variants, the stored N_AUDIT equals **4 × Nca × Nco / (Nca + Nco)** computed from the original reported variant counts, within 1.5×10⁻¹¹ numerical difference. This verifies how the audit field was constructed; it does not establish that one scalar effective N is an exact input for SuSiE in the meta-analysis. The insomnia source's per-variant N and the pilot's fixed N_AUDIT are distinct quantities and were not equated here.

The close frequency matches reduce concern about a large frequency-orientation mismatch in these selected non-palindromic variants, but they **do not explain or resolve the LD–Z outliers**. Reference and study allele frequencies may share upstream reference information, and small population differences, source models, LD structure, relatedness, genuine signals, missingness and imputation can affect the diagnostic. The thresholds and outlier groups are descriptive and post hoc; SNPs are correlated and the groups are not independent tests. No variants were flipped, removed or refitted based on this inspection, and the multi-signal pilot remains **NOT ADMITTED** as validated molecular colocalization.
