# Reference LD among chr3 eQTL and disease lead variants

This post-hoc diagnostic follows the already observed single-signal posterior results. Within the selected adrenal LSAMP–insomnia and LSAMP–ADHD matched files, select the smallest eQTL p-value, the smallest beta/SE-derived GWAS p-value in each trait, and rs62264764. Verify that the eQTL lead is the same in both trait-specific sets and identify each SNP by unique exact GRCh38 variant and allele pair. Map through the documented hg38-to-hg19 chain to the 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 EUR VCF on the positive strand and require unique exact rsID/position/allele matches. Calculate signed phased-haplotype r and r² for each pair among 503 EUR-labeled reference people with allele indices matching the same GRCh38 ALT allele; bootstrap participants 2,000 times with fixed seed and record all counts, source hashes and exclusions. Frequencies and LD are reference-panel summaries, not the GTEx or GWAS cohort LD, conditional association, multi-signal fine-mapping, independent replication, or tests of gene mediation. If any identifier/allele or genotype validation fails, report it rather than guessing. Selection follows the results and has no inferential significance.
