# Conservative SNP-level conjunction check

Across 1,138,262 common HapMap3 variants and both insomnia–FI and insomnia–FFS pairs (2,276,524 tests), **zero variants passed** the two-pair Bonferroni threshold of 2.1963×10⁻⁸ by the conservative max-p intersection–union test. The smallest max-p was approximately 2.5033×10⁻⁵ for rs6596578 in both pairs; its two-pair Bonferroni-adjusted value is 1. No top-ranked variant is a corrected discovery. `top50_per_pair.tsv` lists the 50 smallest max-p values per pair to make the result inspectable; these are ranked variants, not independent loci or validated candidates.

At each marker, max(p_insomnia, p_frailty) tests the conjunction null that at least one trait has no association. If both input marginal tests are valid, it is conservative without requiring independence of the two studies or of SNPs. The full two-pair Bonferroni denominator was retained. This is **not conjFDR or PLACO+** and cannot demonstrate one shared causal variant, identify effector genes, or determine the direction of a causal relationship. It is much less sensitive than methods that borrow information from the distribution of association statistics, so no threshold crossings do not imply no true shared variants. The scan is restricted to the existing common HapMap3 support, excludes HFRS, uses p-derived Z rounded to three decimals and depends on the original association models and valid marginal p-values. To conservatively account for Z rounding, normal tail p-values were computed from abs(Z)−0.0005, floored at zero. The FI contains sleep items; cohort overlap and ancestry differences remain relevant to interpretation.

The pre-result protocol is in `PROTOCOL.md`, code in `screen.py`, complete summary and input checksums in `summary.json`, and passing rows in `hits.tsv` (header only). The separate PLACO+ screen uses a different joint statistical model and must not be interpreted as confirmed by this result. For the intersection–union principle see [the intersection–union test description](https://academic.oup.com/genetics/article/210/1/25/6088059); for PLACO+ see [Park and Ray 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xhgg.2025.100501).

## FDR sensitivity across the same conjunction family

Both Benjamini–Hochberg (BH) and Benjamini–Yekutieli (BY) corrections at 0.05 returned **zero** SNP–pair rejections; the smallest BH and BY adjusted p-values were both 1.0. BY permits arbitrary dependence across valid input p-values, whereas BH is reported descriptively because its dependence conditions were not established. Validity of the source marginal tests still matters. This sensitivity analysis corrects max-p conjunction tests; it is **not** the distinct enrichment-based [conjunctional FDR method](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31520123/). Details, all family inputs, and the adjustment code are in `FDR_PROTOCOL.md`, `fdr_summary.json`, `fdr.py`, and `top50_fdr.tsv`. The BH/BY adjusted-p formula was checked against R `p.adjust` on a small example with tied and boundary p-values. See [Benjamini and Yekutieli 2001](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1013699998) for the dependence assumptions.
