# Post-hoc combined insomnia–FI, FFS and HFRS conjunction family

The previously completed FI/FFS and HFRS screens were corrected as separate families. As a post-hoc harmonized sensitivity, we combine their **hypothesis slots**, not their independent loci, into one family: 2,276,524 insomnia–FI/FFS SNP–pair slots plus 1,158,908 insomnia–HFRS slots, totaling **3,435,432**. The resulting Bonferroni threshold is 1.4554×10⁻⁸. There are **no Bonferroni, BH or BY rejections** at 0.05, derived from the saved full-screen receipts; this is not a new dense genome-wide scan.

The smallest reported max-p across the two screens is 8.23247×10⁻⁶ from the HFRS screen. The two screens use different HapMap3 support and source precision: FI/FFS use conservatively adjusted three-decimal Z scores on common three-trait support, while HFRS uses displayed marginal p-values on its broader insomnia support, padding 353 unavailable matches as p=1. Each marker–pair is a separate hypothesis, even when the same marker appears in both groups; no assertion of disjoint cohorts or independent variants is needed. This combined family was formed after inspecting the individual results and should be treated as descriptive post-hoc sensitivity.

## Why the BH and BY results follow

Let group sizes be m₁ and m₂. Both previously computed BH procedures had zero rejections at 0.05 on their complete padded families. If a pooled BH rejection occurred at rank k, with k₁ and k₂ entries from the two groups among the first k, at least one group has kᵢ/mᵢ ≥ k/(m₁+m₂). Its kᵢth smallest p-value would then be no greater than the pooled kth p-value and hence would meet that group's own BH threshold, contradicting its reported zero rejections. This holds with ties and arbitrary dependence. BY thresholds are no larger than BH thresholds, so the pooled BY procedure also has no rejections. Bonferroni has none because the combined minimum exceeds its cutoff. This argument verifies the numerical implication of the prior receipts; it does not independently rerun their source parsing or establish BH's FDR control under arbitrary dependence.

The max-p is a conservative intersection–union test only when both marginal p-values are valid. HFRS printed p-value precision, source quality, ancestry, sample overlap and phenotype differences still matter. FI contains sleep-related items and FFS includes exhaustion; HFRS is hospital-ascertained. No crossing does not establish absence of shared biology, and the procedure does not test local correlation, a common causal variant, PLACO+ or enrichment-based conjunction FDR. The [FI/FFS screen](../RESULTS.md), [HFRS screen](../../hfrs_conjunction/RESULTS.md), and [combined receipt](receipt.json) contain the provenance and qualifications. `check.py` reproduces the arithmetic and hashes the two inputs.
