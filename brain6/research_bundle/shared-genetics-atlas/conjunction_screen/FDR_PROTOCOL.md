# FDR sensitivity analysis for conjunction tests

Written before inspecting the FDR results. Apply BH and Benjamini–Yekutieli (BY) at nominal 0.05 to the same two-pair family of 2,276,524 conservative max-p SNP-level conjunction tests described in `PROTOCOL.md`. BH is descriptive because LD and cross-pair dependence do not automatically satisfy its required dependence conditions. BY is the arbitrary-dependence sensitivity analysis, provided all marginal p-values are valid. These are FDR corrections of intersection–union p-values, not the published conditional/conjunctional FDR method that estimates enrichment between traits. Both count SNP–pair hypotheses, not independent loci. Follow the exact correction over all hypotheses, even though only ranks and summaries are saved. The calculation is post hoc, and source limitations remain: HapMap3-only coverage, rounded p-derived Z, ancestry and marginal calibration. Report counts and the smallest adjusted p, without making causal or replication claims.
