# Tracing a selected variant through the evidence graph

Run `python3 outputs/shared-genetics-atlas/evidence_graph/trace_variant.py rs62264764` from the workspace root. The JSON output separates direct edges from paths that begin with **one reference-LD edge**. It preserves the source, allele context and evidence type. It does not score or rank paths. The saved [`rs62264764_trace.json`](rs62264764_trace.json) and [`rs1476535_trace.json`](rs1476535_trace.json) are examples; an unlisted variant may simply not have been selected for this graph.

For rs62264764, direct records include the two single-signal credible-set memberships, positional overlaps including LSAMP, and GTEx v8 adrenal LSAMP eQTL association. The latter is a measured gene-expression association in adrenal tissue; the queried brain-tissue results do not establish the same association, and it is not a trait–QTL colocalization. A one-hop path goes through the selected rs817509 marker: in the 503-person 1000 Genomes EUR reference, counted rs62264764 A and rs817509 G have r²≈0.703. The rs817509 source lookup gives MVP insomnia p=2.858×10⁻⁵ and FinnGen HFRS p=1.206×10⁻⁷, with a post-selected max-p ×124 value of 0.00354 in the separate candidate screen. Its FFS marginal p=0.0099 and allele convention is inferred.

This path is **not** a direct rs62264764–HFRS association, independent replication of both traits, a causal link through LSAMP, or evidence that the two markers share one causal variant. The frailty build was assumed for the candidate lookup, rs817509 has multiallelic reference representations, sample independence is unknown, and LD in the study cohorts can differ from the small reference panel. The original LAVA quality gates remain failed. The graph contains selected records only, including genes and QTL queries chosen in part using AlphaGenome results; review governing output terms before any model-training use. See [full locus evidence](../cross_project_ld/CROSS_PROJECT_LD.md), [GTEx review](../variant_context/gtex_v8/GTEX_QTL_REVIEW.md) and [candidate screen](../evidence/song124_mvp_hfrs_followup.md) for the complete provenance and limitations.
