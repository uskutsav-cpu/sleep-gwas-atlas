# Dementia-excluded hospital frailty and insomnia: published-lead follow-up

The [published HFRS GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12350161/) analyzed an alternative hospital frailty score with dementia weights removed, as well as the full score. This provides an actual **dementia-component sensitivity**, not the requested sleep- or fatigue-excluded FI or exhaustion-excluded Fried score. The original authors selected 42 independent leads in FinnGen for the dementia-excluded score; their independence criterion concerns HFRS LD, not independent insomnia evidence.

We looked up all 42 author-designated lead rows in existing FinnGen R13 insomnia statistics. **28** had a unique exact rsID, chromosome, position and non-palindromic SNP allele-pair match. Four rows lacked an rsID, eight had strand-ambiguous alleles, and two were non-SNVs; these 14 are unassessed rather than negative. The supplement does not explicitly label its coordinate build in the inspected table, so GRCh38 was assumed from the FinnGen release and checked against exact R13 coordinates and alleles; source-specific build confirmation remains desirable. The full list and source values are in [lead follow-up](lead_followup.tsv).

| Reported lead | Insomnia p (R13) | HFRS without dementia p (published FinnGen) | Insomnia p × 42, capped | Published UKB HFRS-without-dementia p | 
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rs11062161, chr12 | 0.000450 | 1.48×10⁻⁸ | 0.0189 | 0.369 |
| rs7277140, chr21 | 0.000629 | 4.28×10⁻⁸ | 0.0264 | 0.122 |

Both matched variants have the same estimated direction for the listed HFRS alternate allele in FinnGen insomnia and the dementia-excluded HFRS estimates. Their estimated effects are on different trait scales. The two numerical benchmark crossings are **post-selected exploratory candidates**, not validated shared loci or corrected claims: R12 and R13 share participants, the HFRS variants were selected in overlapping data, and the lookup followed earlier investigations. Multiplication by all 42 published lead rows is a conservative numerical screen but cannot guarantee post-selection error control. The reported UKB p-values concern HFRS without dementia alone, not independent two-trait replication; neither of these two HFRS UKB p-values is below 0.05. Seven of the 28 matched variants have nominal insomnia p<0.05, but selection and LD preclude treating them as independent discoveries. Neither a marginal match nor a gene label establishes a shared causal variant, effector gene or pathway.

The source's 109-code HFRS includes R40, “somnolence, stupor and coma,” weight 2.5 (supplement ST18 row 18). It is a broad hospital diagnostic category, not a direct measure of insomnia or an item that proves sleep-specific overlap. Excluding dementia therefore cannot be described as removing sleep-related diagnoses, fatigue, or shared ascertainment. We did not calculate an insomnia–HFRS-without-dementia genome-wide genetic correlation; full dense summary statistics for that exact phenotype were not established in the examined public sources. No sleep-item-excluded GWAS or local-correlation QC repair was produced.

## Reproducibility

The pre-result [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [source workbook](../../PUBLICATION.md#external-dependencies) (external target: `published_supplement.xlsx`), [script](analyze.py), complete [table](lead_followup.tsv) and [receipt](receipt.json) preserve the exact source rows, allele coding and hashes. The supplied workbook is the authors' [Supplementary Information](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs43587-025-00925-y/MediaObjects/43587_2025_925_MOESM1_ESM.xlsx). The source R13 file matched the previously recorded SHA-256 and all 21,327,010 rows were scanned. Run the script with the bundled Python and openpyxl, retaining access to the original source path shown in the script. FinnGen should be acknowledged in any publication using these data.
