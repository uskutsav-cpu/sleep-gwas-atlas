# Published frailty-factor variant crosswalk

For a descriptive prior-evidence crosswalk, select all rsIDs and GRCh37 coordinate/allele pairs from the completed FI/FFS diagnostic candidate position table, plus previously documented Brain6/FI/FFS anchor variants rs62264764, rs817509, rs2431108, rs7703746 and rs1476535. Collapse identical variant specifications, retaining membership; refuse conflicting specifications. Stream each of the seven full published Foote latent-factor TSVs from the GWAS Catalog FTP site without storing complete files. Compute MD5 and SHA-256 over the exact downloaded bytes and compare MD5 to the publisher metadata before accepting a complete stream. Require the expected header, parse every row, and preserve source rows for selected IDs. Separate exact coordinate and unordered allele matches, missing IDs and mismatches; no missing observation implies a null effect because input GWAS and Q-filtering affect inclusion. Records with multiple exact matches are ambiguous. Preserve the original allele and statistics; do not infer direction harmonization from rsID alone. Report publisher factor-level p values as descriptive prior associations, with the study's seven-factor Bonferroni 7.14e-9 threshold clearly distinguished from post-hoc selected variants; do not claim conditional independence, shared causality, independent replication or a component-excluded phenotype. The general factor incorporates insomnia, and the factors share contributing GWAS data. No genomic-SEM output or AlphaGenome model output is used for model training.
