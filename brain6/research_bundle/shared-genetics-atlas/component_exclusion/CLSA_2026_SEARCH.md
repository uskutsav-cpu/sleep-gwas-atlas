# Canadian frailty-component GWAS: access assessment

A 2026 [CLSA frailty GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13103308/) reports secondary GWAS analyses of weakness, slowness, low activity, weight loss and exhaustion separately in an overall European-ancestry sample of 23,105 participants. These components could inform sensitivity analyses, but none is an exhaustion-excluded composite frailty GWAS. The reported sample and covariates differ from the UK Biobank analyses; the results cannot be treated as a matched leave-one-component-out score.

The paper's data-availability statement directs researchers to CLSA access for eligible applicants. It does not identify a freely downloadable full genome-wide summary-statistics file in that statement. Its publicly accessible paper and supplementary PDF are not evidence that full GWAS statistics are anonymously available. We did not obtain a suitable account-free dataset from this report; further repository and supplementary-material checks could change that assessment. This study was not used in the analyses.

The atlas had already reviewed this study in more detail, including a [catalog and CLSA archive search](borhan_2026/REVIEW.md); that earlier audit found a publication listing but no verified anonymous summary-statistics download.
