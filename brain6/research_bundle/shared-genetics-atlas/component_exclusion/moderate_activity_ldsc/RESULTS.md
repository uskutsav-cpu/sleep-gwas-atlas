# Moderate physical activity and insomnia: exploratory genetic correlations

The [Neale Lab public LDSC manifest](https://nealelab.github.io/UKBB_ldsc/downloads.html) identifies an anonymously downloadable GWAS for UK Biobank [field 884](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/showcase/field.cgi?id=884): days per week of at least 10 minutes of self-reported moderate physical activity, excluding walking. This is an activity-related physical trait, not a composite physical frailty score or a GWAS in which exhaustion was removed. The source is the primary both-sexes ordinal analysis. The [direction audit](DIRECTION_AUDIT.md) finds that its published categories increase from 0 to 7 days and its signed Z statistics follow the alternate allele. This supports interpreting a higher activity category as more activity days, conditional on the documented processing; participant-level data were not independently reconstructed.

| Insomnia source | LDSC r_g (SE) | p |
|---|---:|---:|
| Questionnaire | 0.0625 (0.0355) | 0.0785 |
| Registry | 0.0041 (0.0430) | 0.9236 |

Both estimates are imprecise and neither reaches p < 0.05; this does not establish absence of shared genetics. The project-wide multiple-testing burden is larger than these two exploratory tests. Their difference has not been tested with paired jackknife uncertainty, and different cohort composition and ascertainment preclude causal interpretation. The questionnaire and activity GWAS both include UK Biobank, with unknown exact overlap. Finnish ancestry in the registry source may fit the general-European LD reference imperfectly; its observed-scale h² intercept was 1.0769 (SE 0.0097). The activity observed-scale h² was 0.0403 (0.0023) with intercept 1.0110 (0.0077); cross-trait intercepts were -0.0367 (0.0067) and 0.0075 (0.0060), respectively. Intercepts do not identify the cause of overlap or bias.

The complete 8,297,969-byte source was downloaded into memory twice, fully decompressed, and SHA-256 `fffebe514901e7c09b242365f3a327d0a73d4df6729d64fb903f45a4eb7ad5be` matched across both runs and a third audit; no publisher checksum was located, so this establishes internal consistency rather than external authenticity. It contained 1,096,648 unique SNPs, no duplicate IDs or invalid numeric/allele rows, and constant N=343,943, a study-level value rather than independently verified per-variant N. For each insomnia input, 1,020,637 SNP IDs were shared, all matched allele pairs directly or by strand and none was palindromic; LDSC retained all 1,020,637. Reference build and ingest are inherited from the source and previous project inputs rather than independently reconstructed from raw UKB genotypes. The LDSC logs include a compression warning when reading the archived insomnia inputs, whose gzip streams were previously independently checked.
