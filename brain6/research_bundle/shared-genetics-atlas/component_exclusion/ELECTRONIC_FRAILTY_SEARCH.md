# Electronic frailty indices: sleep and fatigue screening

A targeted search for an anonymously downloadable electronic-frailty GWAS did not identify a suitable full, signed sleep- and fatigue-excluded summary-statistics release. This is a limited search, not proof that no such release exists.

One potential alternative, the [electronic screening index of frailty (e-SIF)](https://academic.oup.com/ageing/article/51/7/afac161/6637440), explicitly lists **sleep disorders** and **dyspnoea or fatigue** among its 42 clinical conditions. The source describes construction and validation of the clinical index, not an item-excluded genome-wide association dataset. Thus it does not supply the requested genetic outcome, and its electronic-health-record format alone does not remove sleep or fatigue overlap. It should not be conflated with the separate hospital frailty risk score or the 49-item UK Biobank FI used elsewhere in this project.

A valid future candidate must have an explicit item inventory, complete signed per-variant GWAS summary statistics, ancestry/build and effect-allele information, model and sample-size details, and a documented account-free access route. A matched within-cohort FI-minus-sleep-and-fatigue or Fried-minus-exhaustion GWAS remains the stronger test. Even such a score would not establish a purely physical or causal mechanism on its own.
