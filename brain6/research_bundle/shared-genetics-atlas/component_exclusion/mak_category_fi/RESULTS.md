# A published modified FI that removes insomnia and fatigue

[Mak et al. 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10410014/) performed GWAS for 11 frailty indices, each excluding one category of deficits. One published outcome removes **eight mental-wellbeing items, including insomnia and fatigue**. This is an important prior example of a sleep-item-excluded frailty GWAS and a potential source for the project's requested sensitivity analysis. It is not, by itself, a genetic-correlation result for insomnia and frailty.

The study's Supplementary Table 4 lists the eight UK Biobank FI items: self-rated general health, fatigue, insomnia, feeling depressed, self-described nervous personality, severe anxiety or panic attacks, feeling lonely, and sense of misery. Supplementary Table 16 labels a modified FI excluding those eight items and states that GWAS and biomarker Mendelian-randomization analyses were performed for the category-excluded indices. The original 49-item list would nominally leave **41 items**; individual denominators and missingness rules must be verified in the original analysis. Removing six other items at the same time changes the broader construct, so any difference from the full FI could not be attributed uniquely to insomnia or fatigue.

## What is and is not available here

The study's [version 1 Figshare release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24591573.v1) lists 19 files, including full FI and physical-frailty GWAS plus random-half and non-metabolomics subsets. No filename explicitly identifies the mental-wellbeing-excluded FI. This is a check of **one release's file inventory**, not proof that the statistics are absent elsewhere or that file contents can be inferred fully from their names. Supplementary Table 16 contains biomarker MR estimates, not the per-SNP modified-FI statistics required for LDSC. No insomnia–modified-FI genetic correlation has been estimated here.

Rechecked 29 October 2026 using the [public Figshare API record](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/24591573): it still reports version 1, last modified 20 November 2023, and the same 19 listed names. The exact `fi_nomental.fastGWA` name identified in the author-code audit is not in that inventory. This recheck does not rule out another repository or a differently named file.

A useful next step is to obtain the full modified-FI and corresponding full-FI signed GWAS, together with phenotype coding, ancestry, allele/build conventions, per-variant N and QC. Check whether the full and modified GWAS actually use the **same individuals and model**; matching nominal sample sizes do not prove this. Then run source- and LD-quality checks, matched-support LDSC and paired uncertainty estimates against independently characterized insomnia GWAS. To isolate insomnia and fatigue specifically, separately constructed same-sample FI-minus-insomnia and FI-minus-insomnia-and-fatigue GWAS are still needed. The existing [score-construction protocol](../CONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md) describes those planned phenotypes. None of these comparisons alone proves causation or a purely physical frailty mechanism.

The [data request draft](DATA_REQUEST_DRAFT.md) is prepared for review and has **not** been sent. No summary statistics were requested or downloaded for the modified FI in this check. The existing Mak FI–physical-frailty analysis uses the original FI and is a different comparison.
