# Falls versus insomnia: physical-outcome LDSC pilot

Specified before downloading the falls summary statistics or inspecting results. The [Neale Lab UK Biobank field 2296 resource](https://nealelab.github.io/UKBB_ldsc/h2_summary_2296.html) contains an ordinal falls-in-the-last-year GWAS for 360,344 unrelated European-ancestry participants. Falls is a sleep- and exhaustion-item-free related outcome, not an exhaustion-excluded Fried score or a direct test of physical frailty. Obtain its openly linked LDSC-formatted file to the external SSD working volume; record URL, bytes, SHA-256, gzip completion, sample sizes, allele conventions and unique-marker diagnostics. Confirm the coded direction from source documentation or label it unresolved; do not assume it is equivalent to the 2020 binary falls GWAS.

Using the existing local LDSC and European LD reference, compute unconstrained-intercept bivariate LDSC with the original Brain6 questionnaire-insomnia and FinnGen R13 registry-insomnia inputs. Check matched alleles, SNP counts, warnings and univariate results before interpretation. Report the two model results and a descriptive Bonferroni factor of two; this is a post-hoc family and does not correct prior atlas searches. The two sleep cohorts, sample definitions, ancestry and overlap differ; UKB is shared by falls and the questionnaire insomnia source, while FinnGen and falls are distinct sources with overlap/relatedness unverified. A genetic correlation is not a phenotypic correlation, causal effect, validated local result, item-excluded frailty GWAS or independent cross-trait locus replication. Do not delete files.
