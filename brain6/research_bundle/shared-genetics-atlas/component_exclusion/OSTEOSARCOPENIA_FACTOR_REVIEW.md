# Osteosarcopenia factor as a physical-function comparator

Reviewed 28 October 2026. [Lai et al. 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00335-026-10245-0) describe a genomic-SEM factor using appendicular lean mass, bone mineral density, handgrip strength, walking pace and fracture. None of those five labels explicitly names sleep or exhaustion. The factor is an aggregate of genetic associations, not an individual-level four-component Fried score; a lack of those labels does not prove the inputs are independent of sleep, fatigue, sample overlap or related health conditions. It cannot answer the requested same-person item-deletion question.

The publisher's freely accessible [supplementary DOCX](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs00335-026-10245-0/MediaObjects/335_2026_10245_MOESM1_ESM.docx) was read in memory (1,848,831 bytes; SHA-256 `4bffccab2cc2acb5b574fbf0b9f626f04eec2b520ccbbc5a6f9273467af89643`). Supplementary Table S1 identifies five source accessions: `ebi-a-GCST90000025`, `ukb-b-8875`, `ukb-b-10215`, `ukb-b-4711`, and `ebi-a-GCST90038703`. These accession labels require verification against the source definitions and releases before reuse; no assumption of independence from the atlas inputs is justified. The paper's data statement says the analyzed datasets are public but does not provide a URL for the newly generated full factor GWAS. A [GWAS Catalog v2 publication query](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?pubmed_id=42166073&size=100) returned zero studies; that does not rule out availability elsewhere.

Supplementary tables raise material questions before adopting the factor even if its full statistics are found. The reported single-factor fit has CFI 0.9765 but SRMR 0.1643 and chi-square p 7.81×10⁻⁹. Table S2 reports fracture SNP heritability 0.0008 (SE 0.0008), while Table S5 reports standardized factor loadings for bone density −0.0245 (SE 0.0218) and fracture 0.0759 (0.1226), versus 0.6189 (0.0353) for lean mass, 0.8069 (0.0372) for grip and 0.3079 (0.0216) for walking. These published figures suggest the fitted factor is dominated by the muscle/function inputs and does not strongly represent its bone indicators; they do not alone establish a model error. Table S6 prints N=41,931,477 for its listed SNPs, far above the stated sizes of the five input cohorts; its meaning is not documented in the accessible supplement and must not be treated as a unique participant count or used as LDSC N without clarification.

Table S6 is a selected risk-locus table, not verified complete genome-wide output. It is unsuitable as a replacement for unbiased genome-wide genetic-correlation analysis, and selected matches would not establish independent sleep–frailty replication. The full article was subscription-limited in this check, so other details may be available there. The existing [sarcopenia-factor feasibility analysis](sarcopenia_public_candidate/ldsc_feasibility/RESULTS.md) remains a separate, provisional result and is not this paper's factor.
