GWAS summary statistics for the five components of physical frailty, including weight loss, exhaustion, low physical activity, slow walking speed, and low grip strength. Conducted by using Regenie software v3.5, please see: https://rgcgithub.github.io/regenie/

   - 'Low_Physical_activity': 34406 cases and 371518 controls
   - 'Slow_Walking_speed': 30846 cases and 375531 controls
