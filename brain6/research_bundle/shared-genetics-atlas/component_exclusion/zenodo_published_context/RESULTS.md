# Context for the public frailty-component GWAS

The [open Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/14011550) provides separate UK Biobank GWAS files for five physical-frailty components. Its depositor, Jiajin Chen, and the authors listed in its README match the author list of the later peer-reviewed [Chen et al. heart-failure study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-025-01734-2), whose published title differs from the README's proposed citation. This establishes a useful lead for provenance, not that every detail of the released component GWAS is documented by the article.

In the article's **observational frailty phenotype**, exhaustion was based on reporting tiredness or lack of energy more than half the days or nearly every day over the preceding two weeks. That is distinct from a direct insomnia question, but it can still overlap conceptually with sleep disturbance. The article analyzed 49,530 participants with cardiovascular disease for heart-failure outcomes; the Zenodo README separately reports 47,139 exhaustion cases and 349,680 controls for its component GWAS. These counts describe different analyses, and cannot establish that the article's operational definition, ancestry restrictions, covariates, effect coding or precise samples apply to the released GWAS.

The article says its **composite physical-frailty GWAS** used for Mendelian randomization came from [Ye et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00771-z). That published composite, the article's observational frailty score, and the five Zenodo component files must not be presented as one newly documented matched analysis. The article does not identify the five Zenodo files in its data-availability statement. Its linked [public repository](https://github.com/JiajinChen/PRS-HF/tree/0e106c47b0a50e462ec5c4d049b5d872caf056a3) contained heart-failure PRS coefficient files and a README when checked; the tree did not provide component-GWAS construction code. A repository filename inventory does not rule out code elsewhere.

The remaining evidence gap is the same: obtain the component GWAS's actual phenotype coding, ancestry and GWAS model, allele convention and per-variant N, and assess overlap and LD before using it for a genetic correlation. The file is an exhaustion GWAS, **not** a matched Fried score with exhaustion removed. The original [whole-file and reference audit](../zenodo_full_exhaustion_audit/RESULTS.md) and [frequency/model diagnostics](../zenodo_frequency_model_audit/RESULTS.md) do not establish those missing details. No new sleep–frailty genetic association, causal claim or item-exclusion result follows from this literature lookup.
