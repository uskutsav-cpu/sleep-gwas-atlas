# Physical-frailty source availability check

**29 October targeted follow-up:** An [electronic frailty index screen](ELECTRONIC_FRAILTY_SEARCH.md) found that e-SIF explicitly includes sleep disorders and dyspnoea or fatigue; no suitable full, signed item-excluded GWAS was identified in that limited search.

**28 October follow-up:** The [hospital-score code audit](HFRS_SLEEP_CODE_AUDIT.md) identifies R40 (somnolence, stupor and coma) in the original 109-code HFRS. The existing HFRS GWAS should not be described as a sleep-free physical-frailty score. A search of public Figshare and Zenodo frailty GWAS releases did not identify a full signed GWAS of the requested physical score with sleep and exhaustion removed; search coverage is not exhaustive.

The [GWAS Catalog v2 check](CATALOG_V2_AUDIT.md) found 11 studies under the existing FI's exact frailty-measurement ontology code, with none labeled as the required four-component score; searches of three relevant publication IDs returned no records. Catalog annotation and coverage limit this negative result.

A [2026 osteosarcopenia genomic-factor review](OSTEOSARCOPENIA_FACTOR_REVIEW.md) identifies five physical input traits but no verified full factor summary-statistic release. Published model fit, weak bone-related loadings and unclear factor N require investigation before it could be used as a comparator; it is not an item-excluded Fried score.

Checked 27 October 2026. This assessment records which data can support the requested analyses; none of the sources below is a same-person FI-minus-sleep GWAS.

| Source | Available evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| [Chen's Zenodo component release, record 14011550](https://zenodo.org/records/14011550) | Five files named for the Fried components; README records Regenie v3.5 and case/control totals. The record is version 1.0, last modified 30 October 2024. The linked [Chen et al. paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11357-025-01734-2) analyzes 49,530 cardiovascular-disease participants and references Ye's earlier overall frailty GWAS; it does not establish the generation and full metadata of each component file. | Continue to hold the five files for cross-trait inference until assembly, alleles, ancestry, QC, covariates, model/effect scale, cohort overlap and file-level lineage are documented. A phenotype-description match is not enough to inherit Ye's metadata. A draft request for those details is in `UNSENT_METADATA_REQUEST.md`; it has not been sent. |
| [Tseng et al. EHR-based FI and Fried phenotype](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10828416/) | The study describes a 52-item FI based on diagnoses, laboratory values and physiological reports, and a separate Fried case–control phenotype. Its FI item table reviewed in the existing literature map did not explicitly name sleep or fatigue. Its study design, item list, age range and measurement differ from Atkins' 49-item questionnaire FI. The [publisher's data statement](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11357-023-00874-7) points to UK Biobank project 54423; it does not provide a full downloadable GWAS summary-statistic link. | A potentially useful alternate-phenotype comparison if fully documented signed summary statistics become available. It is not an item-deletion experiment, a pure physical frailty measure, or independent of other UKB-based studies. Its published significant loci alone cannot support whole-genome LDSC or unbiased locus replication. |
| [Atkins FI](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/acel.13459) | UKB complete-case 49-item FI plus TwinGene meta-analysis; the UKB GWAS score was quantile-normalized. Existing project summary statistics are for the original score. | Supports the existing genome-wide comparison, but cannot reconstruct component-excluded associations from aggregate beta/SE alone. |
| [Ye Fried score](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10651618/) | Overall 0–5 FFS GWAS in 386,565 UKB participants of European descent, with a separate HRS polygenic-score analysis. | Supports an overall FFS comparison, not independent two-trait locus replication or a four-component GWAS. Do not assume a downloaded five-component release is the same study. |

The next actionable route is either to obtain source documentation or to derive scores and run GWAS on appropriately approved individual-level data. `CONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md` describes the latter, including the consequences of changing the phenotype and complete-case sample. Do not silently substitute published top hits, a field-level proxy, or a different frailty index for the requested matched analyses.

A [public NHANES observational route](nhanes_public/REVIEW.md) pairs FI and adapted Fried constructions and identifies shared physical questions that could inflate their comparison. An [exploratory survey-weighted pilot](nhanes_public/phenotype_pilot/RESULTS.md) uses approximate working scores with explicit assumptions. It supplies neither a validated reconstruction nor a UKB same-sample item-excluded GWAS.

[Foote et al. 2025 genomic-SEM factors](foote_2025_metadata/REVIEW.md) are another public multivariate comparator with documented factor metadata, not same-person component-excluded GWAS. The general factor includes insomnia and the published factor files are Q-filtered; their use requires an explicit overlap and analysis-suitability audit.

The published [dementia-excluded HFRS GWAS lead follow-up](hfrs_without_dementia/RESULTS.md) provides a distinct component sensitivity and exploratory insomnia lookup, but it is not a sleep-excluded FI/FFS GWAS, an independently replicated two-trait result, or a genome-wide genetic-correlation estimate.

A new [sampled prefix audit](zenodo_prefix_audit/RESULTS.md) supports GRCh37 coordinates for 20 checked exhaustion variants and finds matching positions/alleles at most of the same variants in the other component prefixes. This is a limited build clue, not validation of the full files, effect convention, phenotype or ancestry; the inference hold remains in place.

The [full exhaustion archive audit](zenodo_full_exhaustion_audit/RESULTS.md) verifies the published compressed-file checksum and finds GRCh37 position agreement for all 1,020,541 rows with IDs in the existing HapMap reference. It records 873 allele-pair mismatches and 861 repeated matched rsIDs. The [four remaining physical-component audits](zenodo_full_physical_audit/RESULTS.md) now also verify publisher checksums and find coordinate agreement for roughly 1.02 million reference-ID rows per file. Each has 873–874 allele-pair mismatch rows and 861–862 repeated reference IDs. These checks do not establish effect convention, model or cohort metadata, so the inference hold remains.

A [follow-up allele audit](zenodo_duplicate_audit/RESULTS.md) shows that 862 mismatch rows have a same-rsID source row with the reference allele pair, while 11 have no such companion. This points to the importance of allele-specific joins; it does not resolve the cohort, model or phenotype metadata.

A [prefix frequency and standard-error check](zenodo_frequency_model_audit/RESULTS.md) finds compatibility with a simple case–control scale and broadly similar allele frequencies to a small European reference in selected chromosome 1 variants. It does not establish the generating model or phenotype and does not lift the inference hold.

A further [Mak et al. category-excluded FI source audit](mak_category_fi/RESULTS.md) confirms a published GWAS of an FI without eight mental-wellbeing items including insomnia and fatigue. The modified GWAS is not identified by filename in the checked release, and the published biomarker-MR table cannot substitute for full per-SNP statistics. This is a broader category removal, not the requested insomnia-only sensitivity.

The [older-adult low-grip-strength GWAS sensitivity](older_weakness/RESULTS.md) supplies a publicly accessible physical component with no explicit sleep or exhaustion item. Both questionnaire- and registry-insomnia comparisons have completed with verified full-source checksums. Low grip strength is one component, not the requested item-excluded composite.

A [2026 musculoskeletal–cardiometabolic factor check](cardiometabolic_factor_2026/REVIEW.md) found that its frailty input is the existing sleep-containing Atkins FI; the factor is not an item-excluded physical-frailty GWAS.

A [2026 Whitman et al. sleep-and-aging preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13370888/) is relevant context but does not supply the requested physical-frailty GWAS: its newly described outcome GWAS is for the brain-imaging aging measure DunedinPACNI, while its full publicly linked FinnGen–MVP data concern sleep disorders. The paper's data-availability statement does not identify a full summary-statistics release for a sleep- and exhaustion-excluded physical-frailty score. Its conclusions about sleep and aging should not be treated as an independent validation of the present frailty analysis. Checked 29 October 2026.

A [Lithuanian component-GWAS source audit](gineviciene_2025/REVIEW.md) checked the actual public genotype archive and lean-mass GWAS workbook: the archive has no nonmissing PLINK phenotype values, and the workbook's reported variants all have p < 0.05, so it is not an unbiased genome-wide input. The matched exhaustion-deleted score and full physical-component summary statistics were not established from these files. The paper's broader data-availability statement does not resolve that gap.

[Flores-Flores et al. (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13536796/) provide a directly relevant observational precedent: they compared a four-criterion physical frailty definition excluding exhaustion with the full five-criterion definition, retaining a three-criterion cutoff. Their 1,029-person Lima study is not a GWAS, and its dataset is available on request. Its threshold choice and resulting case counts inform the [construction protocol](CONSTRUCTION_PROTOCOL.md), but do not supply a genetic correlation or a public matched GWAS.

A [6 November reconstruction audit](identifiability/LINEAR_ROUTE.md) distinguishes the valid same-sample raw-linear coefficient route from unidentified transformed or binary item deletion. It also records a targeted Ma et al. source check: the main article uses the full exhaustion-containing Fried phenotype and identifies restricted UKB data; no account-free full modified-score GWAS was verified.

The [complete Ma et al. supplementary-workbook audit](ma_2025/REVIEW.md) checked all 26 sheets from an account-free publisher download, with matching hashes across two retrievals. These sheets provide phenotype definitions and exposure analyses rather than a full SNP-level modified-frailty GWAS; they were not added to genetic estimation or ML training.
