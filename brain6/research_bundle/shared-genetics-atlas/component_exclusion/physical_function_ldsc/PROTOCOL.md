# Physical-function and registry-insomnia LDSC feasibility

Written before downloading or inspecting the physical-function summary statistics or any results. Use the Neale Lab's published Round 2 UK Biobank LDSC-ready files for self-reported usual walking pace (field 924) and inverse-normalized left hand grip (field 46_irnt). These are two individual physical-function measures, not composite FI or FFS, and are not matched item-deleted frailty GWAS. Compare each to the existing FinnGen R13 registry-insomnia S input using the project's available European LD scores and unconstrained cross-trait and heritability intercepts. The UK Biobank–FinnGen pairing is distinct from the previous published walking-pace–insomnia comparison but exact participant and relatedness overlap is unverified.

Retain the unmodified source files on the SSD, hash them and read their gzip streams through completion. Check source headers, allele representation, identifiers, sample-size semantics and observed variant coverage against the existing S input before running. Do not infer sign for grip or walking until source coding and effect allele conventions are understood. Do not run if sources lack the required LDSC fields, fail integrity, or have unresolved variant/allele mapping. Inspect reported heritabilities, standard errors, intercepts, number of retained markers and warnings. Report both outcomes and any failures, with Bonferroni correction over these two exploratory pairwise tests, not as correction of the overall project analysis. Avoid comparing the two r_g estimates without paired uncertainty on common support and clearly distinguish phenotype, ancestry, ascertainment and models. Existing common support is already selected and potential cross-population reference mismatch remains.

No result proves causality, a frailty-specific mechanism, locus-level overlap or successful sleep/exhaustion-item deletion. A correlation can arise through other traits, not only directly measured physical capacity. The physical outcomes and source LD reference may overlap with existing FI/FFS data; registry insomnia is not equivalent to questionnaire insomnia. Maintain all archived source files and do not delete data.
