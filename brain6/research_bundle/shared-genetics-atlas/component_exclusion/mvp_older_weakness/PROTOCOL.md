# Clinical insomnia and older-adult weakness: exploratory LDSC

Before this run, compare the existing audited MVP European-ancestry clinical-insomnia GWAS against the publicly downloadable Jones et al. low-grip-strength GWAS for adults aged at least 60. Use the existing streamed, checksum-verified construction of weakness on the prior questionnaire/physical-function common marker set and the audited MVP stream anchored to its own ADHD/HapMap set. Both streams must reach completion and produce unique receipts matching prior full-source SHA-256 hashes and counts; an incomplete stream or a failed fit is not accepted. Use European reference LD scores, unconstrained intercepts and report retained markers, genetic correlation, h², intercepts and warnings. This post-hoc single comparison is exploratory in the context of many prior atlas analyses; do not present its nominal p as project-wide controlled. No formal contrast with previous insomnia sources is planned, because the paired block uncertainty and shared support are not established. The outcome is one component, not a modified Fried composite; source model, phenotype, case-count and per-variant sample-size limits remain. Unknown overlap, MVP rounded odds-ratio direction and reference fit constrain interpretation. No causal, clinical or independent-replication claim; no AlphaGenome training data.
