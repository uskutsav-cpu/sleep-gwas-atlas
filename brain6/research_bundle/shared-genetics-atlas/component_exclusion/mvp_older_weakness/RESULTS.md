# Clinical insomnia and older-adult low grip strength

The exploratory LDSC comparison of the MVP European-ancestry clinical-insomnia GWAS and the public Jones et al. older-adult low-grip-strength meta-analysis estimated **genetic correlation r_g = 0.1747 (SE 0.0451, logged p = 0.0001)** on 992,358 matched, valid-allele SNPs. The positive direction means genetic effects associated with greater insomnia liability tend to align with those associated with low grip strength, conditional on the source coding and harmonization. This is one physical component, not a frailty score with exhaustion or sleep removed. It is not a causal effect, individual prediction, independent replication, or evidence of a specific shared variant or biological mechanism. This single comparison is post hoc within a much wider project search; its nominal p-value is not a project-wide multiplicity correction.

Weakness observed-scale h² was 0.0702 (SE 0.0053), with intercept 0.9879 (0.0099); LDSC reported a negative ratio, usually associated with genomic control. MVP observed-scale h² was 0.0502 (0.0033), with elevated intercept 1.1309 (0.0093). The cross-trait intercept was −0.0020 (0.0062); it does not establish participant independence. No formal difference from the [questionnaire and registry-insomnia comparisons](../older_weakness/RESULTS.md) was tested: their marker support, ascertainment and cohort composition differ, and paired uncertainty was not calculated.

The source meta-analysis combines multiple cohorts and includes UK Biobank. Its total and case counts have inconsistencies between paper passages; a constant effective N of 157,559.68 is a working approximation and does not reflect verified per-variant cohort contributions. The MVP source uses p-derived signed Z based on rounded odds-ratio directions, excludes directions rounded to one and eligible duplicate IDs, and uses an approximate constant effective N. Participant or relative overlap and LD-reference fit remain uncertain. See the [weakness source audit](../older_weakness/RESULTS.md) and [MVP audit](../../replication/mvp_finngen_genomewide/RESULTS.md).
