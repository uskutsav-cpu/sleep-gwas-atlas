# GWAS Catalog v2 frailty-source check

Checked 28 October 2026 using the [GWAS Catalog's supported REST API v2](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/docs/programmatic-access/rest-api/). The v1 endpoint used in the previous Canadian-study check returned 410 because v1 was retired; that failure is not a negative search result.

As a positive control, [GCST90020053](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies/GCST90020053) returns the known Atkins frailty-index study, annotated EFO_0009885, “frailty measurement.” A [query for this exact ontology identifier](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?efo_id=EFO_0009885&size=100) returned 11 records on one page: the HFRS study GCST90707291; Foote's seven factor studies GCST90624046–GCST90624052; Ye Fried score GCST90295968; and frailty-index studies GCST90020053 and GCST007716. None is labeled as a sleep- and exhaustion-excluded four-component physical frailty score. The original FI and Fried studies include sleep/tiredness or exhaustion respectively; the general Foote factor includes insomnia; the HFRS has a somnolence-related diagnostic category. Residual factor scores are not equivalent to recalculating a four-component physical score.

Separate v2 study queries for publication identifiers [42020437](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?pubmed_id=42020437&size=100) (Borhan), [37523034](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?pubmed_id=37523034&size=100) (Tseng), and [37184129](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?pubmed_id=37184129&size=100) (Mak) each returned zero records. The same query form returned five records for the [documented positive-control publication 35241825](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?pubmed_id=35241825&size=100). Zero results only mean no matching records were returned by these particular queries at this time; they do not prove the studies or their summary statistics are unavailable elsewhere. Catalog curation and phenotype annotation may be incomplete. The API's full-summary-statistics flag is catalog metadata, not verification that a file is suitable or accessible.

This bounded search does not provide a new dataset or permit an item-exclusion analysis. No new genetic correlation is claimed. See [the source inventory](SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md) and [Canadian study review](borhan_2026/REVIEW.md) for other routes and limitations.
