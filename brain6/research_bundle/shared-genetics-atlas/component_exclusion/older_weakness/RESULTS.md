# Older-adult low grip strength and insomnia: exploratory results

Both prespecified comparisons completed. The public [Jones et al. low-grip-strength meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7844411/) defines weakness using grip-strength cutoffs in adults aged 60 or older; it does not include an explicit sleep or exhaustion item. It measures one physical component, not a four-component frailty score or an item-deletion experiment.

On 1,020,637 retained SNPs, LDSC estimated genetic correlation with questionnaire insomnia **r_g = 0.1693 (SE 0.0379, p = 7.83×10⁻⁶)**. The low-grip univariate observed-scale h² was 0.0701 (SE 0.0052), with intercept 0.9880 (0.0099); the cross-trait intercept was 0.0227 (0.0063). These are exploratory results, not causal effects, independent replication, or a sleep-independent frailty mechanism. The questionnaire and weakness GWAS both include UK Biobank, and exact participant overlap is unknown; the intercept alone cannot establish overlap. The source includes multiple cohorts, variable variant coverage, and inconsistent sample totals in the publication. A constant effective N of 157,559.68 computed from the catalog's aggregate case/control counts is an approximation, not verified per-variant effective N. The general-European LD reference and the GWAS's genomic control and meta-analysis may affect calibration.

The entire 377,834,760-byte source was streamed, with MD5 `10ed84976ef644b1ac7ceb06cd34e2dc` matching the publisher metadata; SHA-256 `ddd0f1f45fc8e72494200b8e6fc0f72a4fdd219ec559060e11372f1be61d5b9c`. All 9,457,422 rows were read; 1,020,637 matched the prespecified reference, common input set and allele criteria; no eligible duplicate or invalid numeric row was recorded. The [receipt](receipt_retry_questionnaire.json), [log](retry_questionnaire.log), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), and `stream.py` document the calculation. Matching a reference and checksum does not validate sample-size conventions or establish a causal allele.

The registry-insomnia comparison gave **r_g = 0.1708 (SE 0.0482, p ≈ 0.0004)** on the same 1,020,637 SNPs. The registry observed-scale h² was 0.0201 (SE 0.0016), with intercept 1.0769 (0.0097); the cross-trait intercept was 0.0028 (0.0059). The registry source and general-European LD reference may differ in ancestry composition. Both nominal p-values pass the prespecified two-comparison Bonferroni threshold of 0.025, but the project-wide multiple-testing burden is broader and these results are exploratory. Similar point estimates do not establish equivalence: the analyses did not retain the paired jackknife values needed to test their difference, and cohort dependence is not established. The registry run independently streamed all 377,834,760 bytes and matched the same publisher MD5 and SHA-256, with the same number of retained SNPs and no eligible duplicates or invalid matched rows; see its [receipt](receipt_retry_registry.json) and [log](retry_registry.log).
