# Older-adult low-grip-strength feasibility protocol

Specified before examining these results. Stream the published GCST90007526 EWGSOP low-grip-strength GWAS, with publisher MD5 verification, without storing the full archive. Join exact rsID, GRCh37 chromosome, position and unordered non-palindromic alleles to the previously established common-support insomnia/physical-function marker set. Remove invalid numeric values and fail on duplicated eligible IDs. Construct signed Z from beta/SE, preserving the publisher's effect allele. Run LDSC against the archived questionnaire and registry insomnia inputs with the general-European LD reference and unconstrained intercepts. Confirm the stream completes and receipt is written before interpreting a fit. Keep both logs and all warnings. Do not label this a four-component frailty or sleep-item-excluded frailty GWAS.

Study-level effective N is 4 × 48,596 × 207,927 / 256,523, used solely as a constant working approximation because per-variant N and case counts are not provided. The source is a 22-cohort meta-analysis with varying per-variant cohort contributions; constant effective N and LD reference are strong approximations. UK Biobank is included, so questionnaire overlap is plausible; overlap and ancestry/reference differences are not quantified. FinnGen registry N and model conventions also remain uncertain. If h2, intercept or fit suggest failure, do not report the genetic correlations as reliable. Any results are exploratory, not causal or an independent replication, and are not a replacement for the item-excluded composite.

The [source paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7844411/) has internal sample-size inconsistencies: its abstract, main results and catalog give 256,523 total and 48,596 cases, while one introductory passage says 46,596 cases and its methods say 254,894 total. The working N uses the catalog and main-results numbers; their agreement does not resolve this discrepancy or establish per-variant counts.
