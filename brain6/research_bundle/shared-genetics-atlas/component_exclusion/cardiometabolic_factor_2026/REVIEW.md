# 2026 musculoskeletal–cardiometabolic factor: source suitability

The 2026 study [“Unraveling ‘F’ factor”](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13440131/) presents a genomic-SEM factor using six GWAS inputs: coronary artery disease, type 2 diabetes, hypertension, bone mineral density, frailty, and telomere length. Its Table 2 identifies the frailty input as **GCST90020053**, the Atkins frailty-index GWAS already examined in this atlas. That index includes sleep and tiredness items; see the [source availability audit](../SOURCE_AVAILABILITY.md). The model therefore uses a sleep-containing frailty GWAS and is not a physical-frailty GWAS with sleep and exhaustion removed. It also incorporates cardiometabolic and other nonphysical inputs. Do not treat its factor as a clean item-exclusion sensitivity or infer that the input's sleep contribution is absent.

The article refers to public input GWAS; that does not by itself establish an anonymously downloadable, documented full factor GWAS suitable for our correlation analysis. We did not verify a full factor release, assess its model fit, or run a new analysis. This is a targeted source exclusion, not a claim that no other suitable GWAS exists. The existing [Foote multivariate-factor review](../foote_2025_metadata/REVIEW.md) documents a separate public release and why its residual factors likewise are not matched item-excluded phenotypes.
