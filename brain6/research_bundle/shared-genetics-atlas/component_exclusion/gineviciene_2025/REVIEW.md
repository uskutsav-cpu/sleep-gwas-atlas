# Lithuanian frailty-component study: public-data feasibility

Checked 29 October 2026. [Ginevičienė et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13394718/) describe Fried frailty and separate association analyses of grip strength, gait speed, exhaustion, activity, weight loss and lean mass index (LMI). The paper reports 204 participants, 181 successfully genotyped, in a community sample aged 65 or older. The overall Fried phenotype includes exhaustion; the physical components are separate traits, not a four-component score.

The paper's data-availability statement names two Figshare records for genotyping and GWAS/EWAS data. A more specific inspection shows why this is not yet an analyzable matched score:

* [Figshare record 28845761](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Genotyping_data_of_the_project_titled_Genome_epigenome_ir_telomere_length_features_of_sarcopenia_and_frailty_/28845761) lists a PLINK genotype archive, a log, and `GWAS for LMI in elderly.xlsx`. The 46,859,509-byte workbook's MD5 was `c58c6d9b4427fc9b16c223fe4bc1faae`, matching the deposited metadata. Its `dataset` sheet has 584,739 data rows with chromosome, rsID, position, two allele columns, imputation information, p value, beta and SE. All reported p values in the scan were at or below 0.0499999 (minimum 1.86053e-7), consistent with selection on nominal significance rather than a full genome-wide release. The study reports over 6 million imputed variants; the workbook is not suitable as an unbiased full-genome LDSC input. A simple numeric validity scan flagged 38,701 rows for missing/non-numeric values or nonpositive SE, which were not resolved individually. The workbook concerns LMI, not the item-excluded frailty score, and its allele/effect convention was not verified for harmonization. It remains at `/tmp/brain6-gineviciene-lmi-57027965.xlsx`.
* The anonymously downloaded `GEPITEL.zip` (19,723,809 bytes) had MD5 `5a01fa78c023ffb96c6eaaab4c6aad32`, matching the Figshare metadata. Its archive listing contains only `.bed`, `.bim`, and `.fam` genotype files. The `.fam` has 192 rows, all with PLINK phenotype value `-9` (missing); that row count is not the paper's reported count of 181 successfully genotyped participants and should not be assumed to be the analysis sample. No participant-level phenotype or sample details are reproduced here.
* [Figshare record 28874378](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Summary_raw_EWAS_Ginevi_ien_et_al_2025_Exp_Physiol_xlsx_zip/28874378) identifies methylation and LMI data, not the physical-component genetic summary statistics. File names and metadata alone cannot prove that no other release exists.

**Decision:** The checked materials do not establish public signed per-SNP statistics for a matched exhaustion-deleted score or its physical frailty component outcomes, and the genotype archive alone cannot reconstruct that score. Its small, selected sample also limits statistical power. Do not treat isolated significant grip findings, raw genotypes without paired component phenotypes, or the selectively reported LMI file as a substitute. The downloaded genotype archive was inspected only at archive/aggregate-metadata level; no reidentification or individual-level analysis was attempted. The temporary copy remains at `/tmp/brain6-gepitel-53914253.zip` because deletion was not authorized.
