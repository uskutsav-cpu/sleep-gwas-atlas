# Physical genetic factor: feasibility protocol

Written 1 November 2026 before these joint physical-trait correlations were inspected. Test a workaround using documented Neale Lab GWAS for usual walking pace (924), inverse-normalized left grip (46_irnt), and self-reported days of moderate activity (884). None explicitly asks about sleep or exhaustion. The outcome is existing FinnGen registry insomnia. The three indicators measure different physical behaviors and function; the genetic factor, if identifiable, is not a person-level Fried frailty score or a matched exhaustion-deletion GWAS. Activity may be behaviorally and genetically distinct; these inputs can still be influenced by sleep, illness, BMI and other factors.

Match all four files to the same nonpalindromic SNP and allele-pair support; require published-input hashes documented in earlier analyses. Run all six pairwise unconstrained LDSC estimates with common blocks, retaining delete values, and check intercepts, h², overlap, signal, source QC and matrix admissibility. Before interpreting any factor, check whether all three physical correlations have the signs needed for a real, consistently oriented one-factor model, whether implied standardized loadings and uniquenesses are within [0,1], and whether the three insomnia correlations agree with a common-factor projection within their paired block-jackknife uncertainty. With three indicators, their correlation matrix alone is just-identified and cannot establish model fit; the fourth trait supplies only an exploratory overidentifying check. If any variance, bound, uncertainty or convergence gate fails, report failure and do not force a factor or choose indicators after observing results. State the correlated-residual and sampling/LD assumptions, do not claim causation or independent validation, and do not use AlphaGenome output to train a model.

Prefer full multivariate LDSC and an established genomic-SEM implementation if available. If unavailable, pairwise common-support LDSC and a transparent analytic projection may assess feasibility; it is not a replacement for a properly fitted, validated genomic SEM. Require an ancestry-appropriate reference and treat Finnish–general-European mismatch and uncertain overlap cautiously. If a full factor summary GWAS and matched individual-level validation cannot be established, retain the request for genuine item-excluded data.
