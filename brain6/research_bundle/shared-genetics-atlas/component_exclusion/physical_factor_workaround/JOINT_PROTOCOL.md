The factor assumes no covariance between insomnia and indicator-specific residuals; it is a correlation-moment model, not full multivariate LDSC/Genomic SEM or a person-level frailty GWAS.

These refits account for loading estimation but condition on the estimated weighting covariance and prior model/indicator selection.
