# Component prefix: reference frequency and standard-error diagnostics

Use the 20 exhaustion variants selected in the previous prefix build audit, without selecting by effect, frequency, or outcome. Query the GRCh37 1000 Genomes Phase 3 European panel and count the allele named `ALLELE1` at each exact rsID/position/allele-pair match. Compare that frequency with A1FREQ from each of five component prefixes at the same variant; record absent, mismatched, duplicate, and missing genotype calls. Summarize signed and absolute frequency differences per component, but do not interpret them as estimated ancestry, independently validated allele coding, or a study-matched reference.

Separately, for each component's complete lines in its stored compressed prefix, use the depositor's reported case/control counts to calculate the descriptive ratio `SE^2 × 2 × f × (1−f) × ncase × ncontrol / (ncase+ncontrol)`. Under a simplified independent-sample logistic score approximation with Hardy–Weinberg genotype variance and no covariate or imputation effects, this ratio is near one. The approximation is not a test, proof of logistic analysis, reconstruction of sample counts, per-variant quality, calibration or effect-allele convention. Exclude f at zero or one and non-finite/nonpositive SE and report counts. Do not fit genetic correlations or modify the inference hold on this basis. Save the exact scripts, source prefixes, reference sample hash, counts and output hashes.
