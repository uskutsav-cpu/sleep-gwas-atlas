# Complete-score selection audit

Post-hoc plan before examining these results. Reconstruct the pilot's primary FI/Fried eligibility exactly and retain the full positive-mobile-exam-weight survey design. Within adults aged at least 60, report the unweighted count and survey-weighted proportion included in the primary complete-score domain. Compare included and excluded groups on survey-weighted mean top-coded age, percentage female, percentage reporting difficulty walking without equipment (PFQ054), percentage reporting any recent sleep symptom on DPQ030, and percentage reporting ever telling a health professional about trouble sleeping (SLQ050).

For each outcome, restrict to valid responses separately and report unweighted response count and survey-linearized 95% interval; do not infer missing responses. Use cycle-specific strata and nested PSUs with the same 4-year examination weights as the main pilot. Directly recalculate each weighted point estimate. These are descriptive comparisons, not independent cohorts, causal effects, population rates for excluded institutionalized people or multiplicity-adjusted tests. Completion itself requires valid DPQ030, so excluded participants without that answer are absent from the corresponding comparison; differential missingness remains unresolved. SLQ050 concerns lifetime disclosure, not insomnia diagnosis. No participant-level output will be saved.
