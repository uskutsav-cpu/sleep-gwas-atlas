# Age- and sex-adjusted phenotype sensitivity

Recorded before results. On the exact primary complete-score sample of 2,433 NHANES 2011–14 examined adults aged 60+, compare the weighted correlation of the approximate FI and adapted Fried score before and after linear adjustment for top-coded age and the binary RIAGENDR coding supplied in the survey. Also repeat for FI43 and FFS2, the previously defined reduced scores, on the same respondents. These are weighted partial correlations of residuals, not causal effects, diagnoses or genetic correlations; adjustment is linear and does not establish adequacy of confounder control.

Use the full positive-weight survey design, combined-cycle weights, cycle-specific strata and nested PSUs. Derive each correlation as a function of weighted first and second moments, with survey-linearized covariance and a numerical delta-method gradient. Use t intervals with design degrees of freedom. Verify point estimates separately with weighted least-squares residuals and compare selected gradients across two step sizes. Record estimates and direct paired adjusted-minus-unadjusted differences with 95% intervals; do not report multiplicity-adjusted claims from this post-hoc analysis. Check input hashes before running and save no participant-level outputs. Existing complete-score selection, mobility skip, provisional FI and adapted Fried coding, top-coded age and reduced-score construct changes remain limitations.
