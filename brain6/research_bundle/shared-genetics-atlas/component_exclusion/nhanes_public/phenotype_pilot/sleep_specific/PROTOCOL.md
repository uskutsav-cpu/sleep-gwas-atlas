# Sleep-only and separate-question sensitivity

Post-hoc follow-up, specified before examining its outputs. Recompute the original pilot and retain its primary same-person sample and survey design. First compare the FI–DPQ030 sleep-symptom correlation for the full working FI, FI without DPQ030 alone, and FI without DPQ030 plus DPQ040. Report paired differences in correlation with survey-linearized uncertainty on the **same** sample; the two differences include rescaling and correlated deficits and are not estimates of independent causal effects of the deleted items.

Then use [SLQ050](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2011/DataFiles/SLQ_G.htm), whether a respondent has ever told a health professional about trouble sleeping, as a separate binary sleep-related question, coded yes=1/no=0, unknown/refused missing. Restrict to the original primary domain with valid SLQ050 and estimate full, sleep-removed and sleep-plus-fatigue-removed FI correlations and paired full-minus-removed contrasts on that same subset. A binary/continuous Pearson correlation is a point-biserial association. SLQ050 measures lifetime disclosure to a clinician and depends on health access; it is not diagnostic insomnia or an independent cohort, and the same FI contains healthcare-use items. Report unweighted N, weight and design, missingness, linearized confidence intervals and every planned contrast. No p-values, multiple-testing claims or causal interpretations; this is a selected exploratory family, not independent replication or genetic inference.
