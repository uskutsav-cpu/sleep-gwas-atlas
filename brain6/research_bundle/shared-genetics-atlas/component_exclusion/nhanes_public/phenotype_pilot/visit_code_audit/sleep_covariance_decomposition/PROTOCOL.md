# Working FI–sleep covariance decomposition

Written after viewing the corrected NHANES results and before this analysis. Use the same two-cycle survey design, corrected working scores and complete-score age ≥60 domain as the existing pilot. Copy the corrected scoring script to this directory, change only its output path and add the per-person number n of observed FI components to its in-memory dataset. Verify source hashes and reproduce the earlier 2,433-person domain and reported FI–sleep correlation. No person-level records are exported.

On the domain with observed sleep item S and n ≥40, let R be the working FI without S. The exact score identity is FI = S/n + (1−1/n)R. Decompose survey-weighted Cov(FI,S) into Cov(S/n,S) and Cov((1−1/n)R,S), checking the identity per person and the weighted covariance sum. Use the complete mobile-examination survey design and Taylor linearization for each covariance, calculating 95% t intervals with the design degrees of freedom. Compare direct weighted arithmetic with survey results. Report n distribution and absolute covariance terms; do not call the first term a causal effect or the change from item removal, and do not claim the terms are uncorrelated, independent, a variance explained, or additive components of a correlation. Varying denominators and other item correlations matter. Scores and sample selection remain provisional, and this is observational rather than genetic.
