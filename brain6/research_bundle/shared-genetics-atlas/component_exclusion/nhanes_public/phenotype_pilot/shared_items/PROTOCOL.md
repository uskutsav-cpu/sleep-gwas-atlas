# Shared physical-question sensitivity

Post-hoc plan before inspecting this comparison. On the original primary NHANES pilot's **same complete-score participant domain**, compare survey-weighted Pearson correlations for: (1) the full approximate FI versus full adapted Fried score; (2) FI without the shared quarter-mile and lifting questions versus the adapted score without those two questions; (3) FI additionally without sleep and fatigue versus that three-component score; and (4) FI without sleep, fatigue, the shared physical questions, BMI and grip versus the adapted score without its two shared questions and its BMI/weight component. The last adapted score has only two components and is an exploratory mathematical sensitivity, not a valid Fried frailty score.

The BMI/weight component of the full adapted score uses the same measured BMI that enters the FI; the FI grip threshold also uses BMI. Even after removal of exact shared PFQ questions, BMI and correlated measures remain shared. Removing the weight component as well in comparison (4) avoids that direct BMI-input overlap but changes both scores substantially and does not prove absence of measurement overlap or biological independence. All FI means divide by the number of observed retained items, with the original full FI ≥40-of-49 criterion and both sleep items observed; all participants remain the same as the prior primary domain.

Report all four correlations with 95% survey-linearized intervals, plus a paired full-minus-comparison-(4) contrast calculated from the joint survey covariance. Distinct denominators, content, reliability and scaling change simultaneously, so the difference cannot be attributed uniquely to shared questions or interpreted causally. Scores are unvalidated reconstructions, the sample excludes people missing the original five components, and these selected intervals are not corrected for the broader project. No genetic or clinical inference and no individual-level export are permitted.
