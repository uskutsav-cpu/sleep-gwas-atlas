# NHANES 2017–March 2020 item availability and intentional-weight-loss skip

Before inspecting the public data, check whether the later NHANES pre-pandemic combined release can support a sleep/exhaustion-excluded physical-component comparison. Download the CDC demographic, sleep, weight-history, physical-activity and depression XPT files into memory only, record their bytes and SHA-256, and join on unique SEQN. In respondents aged 60 or older, count observed weekday sleep duration, the two recreational activity items, DPQ040 energy, and self-reported current and one-year-prior weights after codebook exclusions. Separately count those with a reported weight loss of at least 5% of their prior weight and the availability and coding of WHQ060 intentionality. CDC documentation states WHQ060 is skipped unless reported loss is at least 10 pounds; its absence must not automatically be treated as unintentional loss. Count how many 5%-loss respondents do and do not have a usable answer.

This is a data availability and missingness audit, not a frailty prevalence estimate or test of sleep–frailty association; report unweighted sample counts only. No participant-level data are saved. The released files are observational public-use survey data, not genotype data or an item-excluded GWAS. Do not claim to reproduce the published study's scoring or results.
