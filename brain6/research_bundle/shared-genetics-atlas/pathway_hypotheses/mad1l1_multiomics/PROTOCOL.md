# MAD1L1 methylation-instrument reference LD check

Before inspecting linkage results, take the three published genetic instruments for cg19624444–ADHD from Qian et al. Supplementary Table S2a and the single previously selected sleep–frailty candidate rs28528997. Use Ensembl GRCh38 variant representations, independently verify each positive-strand hg38-to-hg19 lift and rsID/alleles in the 1000 Genomes Phase 3 reference. For all three instrument–candidate pairs, calculate phased haplotype r and r² among European-superpopulation reference individuals, with 2,000 individual bootstrap resamples and fixed seed. Use the two reference VCF alleles; do not guess which allele is the methylation effect allele, and do not infer directions of cross-trait effects from the signed r. Record build, alleles, counts, input hashes and exclusions. This is post-hoc marker-tagging context, not a test of colocalization, mediation, shared disease causation, independent replication or LD in the actual source cohorts. Preserve unverified or multiallelic representations as caveats.
