# Exact-variant GTEx lookup for two linked MAD1L1 markers

Query GTEx v8 significant single-tissue eQTL and sQTL endpoints for the exact reference-panel-matched GRCh38 A>G allele representations of rs28528997 and rs56305291. Record all returned genes, tissue labels, p-values, NES, URLs, response hashes and pagination; retain unsuccessful requests as failures, not null results. The rs28528997 dbSNP/Ensembl record also has a T allele; this is an A>G-only query. Single-tissue significant-pair output uses GTEx's gene-specific selection thresholds; individual nominal p-values are not project-wide adjusted p-values. Variant-level QTL association and two linked markers in the same GTEx donors do not independently replicate each other, establish gene mediation or substitute for dense GWAS–QTL colocalization. This is a post-hoc selected lookup with no enrichment testing or inference of tissue specificity from an absent record.
