# Connecting the existing sleep, ADHD and frailty evidence

This post-hoc crosswalk joins the 27 Brain6 candidates with uniquely matched published neural-progenitor reporter records and four additional previously selected leads to the existing AlphaGenome predictions, FI/FFS/UKB hospital-frailty screens, MVP insomnia, Finnish hospital-frailty and childhood ADHD-symptom follow-up. The 31 unique variants are not independent loci. The [table](crosswalk.tsv), [join script](crosswalk.py) and [input hashes](receipt.json) preserve the source values. The joins check the same variant ID, region and the saved GRCh38 allele representation across the predicted and three-trait files; the FI/FFS/HFRS rows inherit the earlier source-level allele and coordinate checks. They do not validate upstream GWAS models or establish shared causal alleles.

The prior three-phenotype screen matched 418 of 425 candidates and found **no** variant crossing its 425-slot max-p benchmark for MVP insomnia, Finnish HFRS and childhood ADHD symptoms. That symptom phenotype differs from clinical ADHD, and failure to cross a threshold does not prove no effect. The maximum-p test addresses marginal associations, not one shared causal variant; all candidates were post-selected and the broader project testing burden is larger. A small p-value from one phenotype cannot be combined with an assay hit or a nearby gene to claim three-trait mediation. UKB overlaps multiple discovery inputs, exact overlaps across sources are not fully resolved, and LD causes correlated hits.

The most discriminating next experiments would be: (1) harmonize effect alleles, fine-map multiple signals using matched LD and obtain molecular colocalization in the implicated cells; (2) perturb rs325485/rs6874138 and appropriate control alleles in neural progenitors and mature neurons, measuring endogenous accessibility and NUDT12 and other plausible targets; (3) test rs1476535 and rs62264764 in relevant brain-cell contexts rather than assuming gene overlap establishes regulation; and (4) evaluate frailty with an independently characterized sleep/exhaustion-excluded outcome when available. These are proposed tests, not established mechanisms. AlphaGenome predictions remain research-only model outputs; no model was trained on them, and the local-correlation quality gates remain failed.
