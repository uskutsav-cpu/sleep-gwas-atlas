# FOXP2 exact-variant tissue bridge

Join the previously saved rs1476535 GTEx v8 significant-pair records to existing AlphaGenome frontal-cortex prediction and existing clinical ADHD–MVP insomnia–FinnGen HFRS follow-up. Check GRCh38 coordinate, REF/ALT and gene identifier, ensure all GTEx results use ALT allele T, and retain all returned tissue records. Compare the signs of expression associations and disease GWAS on the common allele and report the original scales. GTEx's significant-pair endpoint is selected by source thresholds; absent tissues are unknown, not proven null. Tissue and trait effects are not mathematically comparable and differing signs are not formal tissue-interaction tests. Association does not establish shared causal variants, mediation, gene function in a tissue or mechanism of frailty. The model prediction measures local DNase track changes, not expression. All selections and analyses are post hoc, and no AlphaGenome data are used to train models.
