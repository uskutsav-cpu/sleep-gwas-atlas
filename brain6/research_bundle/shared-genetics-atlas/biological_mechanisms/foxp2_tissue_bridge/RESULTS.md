# FOXP2: tissue-specific molecular evidence at rs1476535

The previously selected rs1476535 **T** alternate allele (GRCh38 chr7:114430980 C>T) has GTEx v8 significant-pair FOXP2 expression records in three non-brain tissues. The stored normalized effect sizes are −0.266 in pituitary (nominal p=1.98×10⁻⁶), +0.380 in adrenal gland (p=2.54×10⁻⁵), and −0.174 in subcutaneous adipose (p=4.94×10⁻⁶). GTEx normalized expression effects are not fold changes. Opposite signs across tissues are a reason to investigate tissue context, not a formal test of a tissue-by-allele interaction. The GTEx endpoint supplies source-selected significant pairs and did not return a brain record for this variant; absence from this endpoint does not prove there is no brain effect.

The same T allele has positive estimated effects in the saved MVP insomnia, FinnGen clinical ADHD, and FinnGen hospital-frailty follow-ups. The corresponding p-values are 1.20×10⁻⁷, 0.367 and 1.46×10⁻⁸, respectively; the ADHD estimate is imprecise and does not supply convincing clinical ADHD evidence. The MVP beta is derived from a rounded odds ratio. These trait effects use different outcome scales from the GTEx expression effects; same or opposite signs do not establish a pathway, causal direction or shared causal variant. The five-trait/frailty and seven-marker screens were retrospectively selected and are not confirmatory replications. The [prior three-trait model](../../frailty_coloc/chr7_three_trait/RESULTS.md) and [clinical follow-up](../../replication/clinical_three_trait/RESULTS.md) preserve their own different samples and limitations.

The saved AlphaGenome/JBrowse adult frontal-cortex DNase ALT-versus-REF summary is −0.0207 at 501 bp. It is a predicted local accessibility-track change, not FOXP2 expression, not a GTEx measurement and not a direct validation of the pituitary or adrenal eQTLs. The independent FOXP2 promoter experiment reviewed [here](../../variant_context/foxp2_promoter_2026/REVIEW.md) used non-neuronal cells and did not test rs1476535. FOXP2 lies in the region, but these observations do not establish that this marker regulates FOXP2 in disease-relevant cells or mediates sleep, ADHD or physical frailty. HFRS includes a somnolence-related diagnostic code; it is not an item-excluded physical-frailty outcome.

A defensible follow-up would obtain dense tissue-matched molecular QTL and disease GWAS, evaluate multi-signal colocalization with appropriate LD and sample-overlap assumptions, and perform allele-specific perturbation in relevant cells with endogenous FOXP2 and accessibility readouts. Pituitary or adrenal investigation is a hypothesis suggested by the measurements, not evidence of a demonstrated hormonal pathway.

The [tissue crosswalk](tissue_crosswalk.tsv), [code](run.py), [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), and [input receipt](receipt.json) use the existing project sources and match exact GRCh38 REF/ALT and FOXP2 gene ID. The [GTEx source audit](../../variant_context/gtex_v8/GTEX_QTL_REVIEW.md) explains source thresholds and provenance. No new data were obtained and no AlphaGenome outputs were used for model training.
