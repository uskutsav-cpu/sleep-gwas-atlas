# Experimental and AlphaGenome evidence: candidate-level integration

Using only existing project data, this analysis joins the 27 selected variants uniquely matched to Lee's published neural-progenitor reporter tables to saved AlphaGenome/JBrowse frontal-cortex predictions and single-signal GWAS PIPs. A second crosswalk covers the four previously matched Lee–Siraj reporter candidates. Variant identity and GRCh38 reference/alternate alleles were checked against the saved prediction and window tables; saved window hashes match. The [27-row crosswalk](lee_27_crosswalk.tsv), [four-row crosswalk](crosswalk.tsv), [scripts](lee_crosswalk.py) and [receipts](lee_27_receipt.json) make the joins reproducible. This is a post-selected descriptive comparison, not a performance benchmark, test of concordance, or model training. The two published reporter studies use different cells and testing families; their allele directions have not been harmonized here.

**rs62369151: reporter evidence without a stable predicted DNase direction.** In the ADHD-prioritized chr5 region, Lee reports allele FDR 0.0114 and Siraj OL42 reports allele-skew FDR 0.0442. Neither result independently establishes an endogenous regulatory effect: Lee's element was not classified as an emVar, Siraj's activity criterion was not met, and the original barcode reconstruction has unresolved discrepancies. Its predicted adult frontal DNase ALT-versus-REF log₂ summaries are **+0.0251 (101 bp), +0.00074 (501 bp), and −0.00225 (2,001 bp)** with offset 1. These are the same saved model output summarized at different widths, not independent experiments or significance tests. The small window-dependent values do not establish agreement or disagreement with either reporter assay; assays, cell models, DNA context and orientation differ. The ADHD conditional PIP is 0.0427, insomnia PIP 0.000132; the more strongly ADHD-prioritized regional lead rs4916723 has reference r²≈0.833 with it, not proof of a shared causal variant.

Measured aging-brain expression associations link both rs62369151 and rs4916723 to **LINC02060 in astrocytes** and **TMEM161B-AS1 in oligodendrocyte progenitors**, but a prior regional single-signal coloc favored distinct dominant ADHD and expression signals. These molecular associations do not prove that either gene mediates the reporter or disease associations. Details, donor dependence and test-family limits are in the [cell-specific QTL analysis](../../variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/rs62369151_brain_qtl/RESULTS.md).

**rs325485 and rs6874138: active allele-dependent reporter elements in a separate chr5 region.** Lee labels both `emVar` in neural progenitors, with allele FDR 2.21×10⁻¹³ and 3.85×10⁻¹³, respectively. Their 501-bp predicted DNase summaries are −0.0157 and −0.00330; rs6874138 changes sign at 2,001 bp (+0.00053). These are not calibrated probabilities, and reporter signs were not oriented for comparison. The moderate reference LD of these variants with the region's lead and their low model-conditional PIPs do not establish which variant drives either trait. An ABC model links rs6874138 to **NUDT12**, a target hypothesis, not direct proof; the original donor/barcode limitation remains for rs325485. See the [published reporter audit](../../variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/RESULTS.md).

**rs62264764: strong prediction, distinct molecular evidence.** Its adult frontal DNase prediction remains relatively large under the previously examined window settings, while aging-brain associations link the locus to **LINC03051** expression in oligodendrocytes and excitatory neurons. These QTLs measure expression, not the predicted accessibility change; single-signal regional expression–GWAS comparisons favored distinct dominant signals in the examined cell types. They do not establish LINC03051 or nearby LSAMP as the causal sleep or ADHD gene. See the [window sensitivity](../../alphagenome_window_sensitivity/RESULTS.md) and [brain-cell review](../../variant_context/rs62264764_brain_xqtl/RESULTS.md).

A later [conditional allele-direction audit](../../variant_context/crossdisorder_mpra/published_table/allele_direction_audit/RESULTS.md) matches published reporter logFC to the archived REF/ALT model for all 27 selected candidates. Construct and barcode provenance remain unresolved for some variants, so the orientation is supported rather than conclusive; reporter and DNase signs measure different contexts.

The current evidence prioritizes allele-specific perturbation in disease-relevant human cell types with endogenous accessibility and transcript readouts, alongside independent GWAS and multi-signal QTL colocalization. No evidence here establishes a physical-frailty mechanism, causal mediation, independent two-trait locus replication, or a clinical prediction. Local-correlation quality gates remain failed. AlphaGenome outputs are research predictions used under the previously authorized non-commercial terms, not training labels.
