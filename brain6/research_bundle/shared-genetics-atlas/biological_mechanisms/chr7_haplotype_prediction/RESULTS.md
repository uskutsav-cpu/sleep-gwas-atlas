# Chromosome 7: allele-aligned frailty, insomnia and predicted accessibility

Two linked variants that lead the existing FI–FinnGen-insomnia single-signal model have **opposite predicted local frontal DNase changes**, even after aligning both to the alternate alleles associated with higher values in the two source GWAS. Their predictions describe different genomic windows around two variants about 4.7 kb apart; they are not measurements of the same enhancer or a test of whether their molecular effects oppose each other.

| Marker and GRCh38 allele | FI beta for ALT | Registry insomnia beta for ALT | Predicted ALT/REF DNase log₂, 501 bp | Conditional SNP probability given H4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rs10953765 G>A | +0.0194 | +0.0314 | −0.0267 | 0.2154 |
| rs1456031 T>C | +0.0187 | +0.0317 | +0.0122 | 0.2151 |
| rs1476535 C>T | +0.0154 | +0.0191 | −0.0207 | 0.000000607 |

The original FI–insomnia coloc reports rs1456031 using T, the reference allele; its signed betas were flipped to C for this comparison. The rs10953765 ALT A and rs1456031 reference T have signed reference LD r=−0.982 (r²=0.964) in 503 European-labeled 1000 Genomes participants. Thus A and C track the same haplotypic direction in that reference, not two independent discoveries. The reference is not the GWAS sample and is not validation of a causal haplotype. The signs of these predicted windows remain opposite across the saved 101-, 501- and 2,001-bp summaries with offset 1; rs1476535 is negative in each. Different widths are resummaries of the same model output, not replication.

The SNP probabilities in the table are **conditional on H4**, not probabilities that a variant is causal. Overall FI–registry-insomnia H4 changes from 0.587 to 0.125 when the shared-variant prior changes from 10⁻⁵ to 10⁻⁶. The model assumes one signal per trait, the FI includes sleep and fatigue, and sample overlap and population LD are unresolved. rs1476535 has modest reference LD with the two conditional leaders (r² about 0.14); this does not prove distinct causal signals. Its stronger UKB hospital-frailty association and low conditional SNP probability do not establish a shared three-trait gene. The saved [three-trait model](../../frailty_coloc/chr7_three_trait/RESULTS.md) found an FI–insomnia cluster under one setting, but no three-trait cluster; results changed under assumed study-correlation values.

AlphaGenome predicts sequence-dependent tracks; it does not measure allele-specific accessibility, identify an effector gene, or predict a person's clinical risk. The local predictions do not prove FOXP2 mediation, which cell type is responsible, causal direction, or a physical-frailty mechanism. Future tests should measure the two variants individually and jointly at their endogenous loci in relevant cells, with controls, and use ancestry-matched LD and independently characterized sleep and frailty data.
