# MVP insomnia and FinnGen clinical ADHD genome-wide LDSC feasibility

Post-hoc sensitivity combining the existing MVP European-ancestry insomnia summary statistics with the archived Finnish R13 clinical ADHD LDSC input. The MVP source reports per-variant p and rounded odds ratio but no SE; construct Z as sign(log(OR)) × Φ⁻¹(1−p/2), using the reported p rather than reconstructing a Wald SE from the incongruent reported confidence interval. Exclude p=0, invalid p/OR, and OR=1 when its direction is lost to rounding; report counts. Use per-row effective N = 4 cases × controls/(cases+controls) as a working binary approximation, requiring finite counts and document their distribution. This is not a claim that this N reproduces SAIGE's true effective N or per-variant genetic information.

Anchor to the existing GRCh37 HapMap3 reference map and the archived ADHD SNP set. Lift each unique SNP position to GRCh38 using the saved hg19-to-hg38 chain; require a unique mapping to the same chromosome, same rsID, exact mapped position and non-palindromic allele pair. Handle reverse mappings by complementing reference alleles. Reject and count ambiguous, invalid and duplicate eligible matches. Independently hash both inputs against the frozen recorded SHA-256, read full compressed MVP source to completion and record resulting counts. Run unconstrained LDSC against the existing general-European reference, and report h², intercept, SNP count, warnings and any failure. The two GWAS are separately named cohorts but independence and relatives are not proven, phenotype definitions differ, and FinnGen LD may not match EUR LD scores. A prior source audit found MVP confidence intervals and p-values inconsistent under simple Wald reconstruction, so p-based Z does not validate beta precision or source calibration. Positive result is genetic association, not causal, local, clinical prediction or proof of joint variant replication. Do not modify raw inputs or train on AlphaGenome outputs.
