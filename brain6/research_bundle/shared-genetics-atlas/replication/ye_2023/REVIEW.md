# Ye 2023 FFS discovery and HRS follow-up

[Ye et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11357-023-00771-z) discovered 37 conditionally independent Fried Frailty Score signals in UK Biobank and examined 22 eligible or proxied discovery signals in HRS. The article reports concordant effect directions for 18 of 22, with three individually significant under its 5% FDR criterion. These are **FFS-only** results, not joint insomnia–frailty replication. The HRS PRS result is a separate aggregate analysis, not replication of every individual variant.

The three colored HRS-significant points in the authors' [Figure 2A](https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1007%2Fs11357-023-00771-z/MediaObjects/11357_2023_771_Fig2_HTML.png) carry the labels **NRXN1**, **BSN**, and **RIOK3,C18orf8**. Their [supplementary discovery table ST1](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1007%2Fs11357-023-00771-z/MediaObjects/11357_2023_771_MOESM2_ESM.xlsx) contains one COJO discovery lead with each corresponding nearest-gene label: rs2287234, rs71080535 and rs303762, respectively. This is an informative label crosswalk, **not verified variant-level replication**: the figure itself labels genes, not rsIDs, and the article says some HRS observations used proxies. The inspected supplementary workbook did not provide an HRS SNP-level replication table, so the tested HRS variant identities, proxy LD, exact alleles and individual HRS p-values remain unverified. Gene labels are annotations, not proof of causal genes.

A fourth ST1 row identifies **rs7703746** (chr5:103907337, source table's coordinate) as a previous UKB FFS discovery lead, near the atlas's FI–insomnia chr5 follow-up. Its discovery-table reported p is 6.98×10⁻¹³ and source `refA` is G. The project FI–FinnGen-insomnia match contains the same rsID and chr5 position with A as the counted allele, FI beta −0.0153, FI p=3.875×10⁻⁶, insomnia beta −0.0266972 and insomnia p=6.3972×10⁻⁵. These are marginal associations from different analyses; reversing the counted allele makes the point estimates positive for G, and the FFS G effect is +0.0131507. An [adapter audit](allele_adapter/RESULTS.md) confirms its agreement with the published COJO discovery row and supports the convention across 37 discovery rows. The FI is the original FI with sleep items. This is a prior FFS association and post-hoc same-region context, not proof that the phenotypes share the same causal signal, independent replication of the joint association, or evidence that rs7703746 was one of the three HRS-significant points. The FI–insomnia single-signal coloc assigned it conditional H4 probability 0.01066, conditional on H4; that value is not an unconditional probability of causality.

`discovery_rows.tsv` transcribes the four relevant ST1 rows, with exact Excel row numbers and source columns. `receipt.json` records hashes of the retrieved figure and workbook. The workbook was inspected read-only; its discovery rows do not contain HRS-level association results. Obtain the authors' HRS variant/proxy statistics and allele definitions before asserting variant-level replication.

A [reference LD check](../../frailty_coloc_chr5/published_ffs_ld/RESULTS.md) found r² about 0.53–0.54 between rs7703746 and each of the three previously reviewed chr5 candidates in 503 EUR reference individuals. This moderate post-selected LD is not evidence of a shared causal signal.
