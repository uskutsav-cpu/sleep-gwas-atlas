# Childhood ADHD-symptom lookup of three Brain6 candidate variants

Before viewing this source's variant results, select the previously examined rs62264764 (chr3:117920728 G>A), rs77960 (chr5:104628884 G>A) and rs1476535 (chr7:114430980 C>T) in GRCh38; these were chosen from the earlier 20-variant context and represent three post-selected regional candidates. Retrieve the official GWAS Catalog GCST90568440 harmonized indexed summary data for childhood ADHD symptoms only, not GCST90568441, which combines symptom and diagnosis results. Store the publisher metadata and one bounded indexed query per selected coordinate. Require the same GRCh38 coordinate and exact unordered allele pair, unique biallelic nonpalindromic SNPs, valid effect/other alleles, finite beta/SE/p and SE>0. Match allele direction to the already documented Brain6 source A1, or report unavailable; do not select proxies after seeing the outcome. Record missing, ambiguous and mismatched records. Retain exact query URL, response and metadata hashes, and say if full file MD5 was not independently verified.

Report each symptom beta, SE, p, oriented direction and descriptive p×3 benchmark, with all three requested variants in the denominator even if unavailable. The cohort and age/measurement differ from clinical ADHD; the three variants were selected after prior findings. A passed threshold is an association with a related symptom phenotype, not independent replication of diagnosed ADHD or a sleep–ADHD locus. Cohort and relatedness overlap with both original ADHD and sleep samples remain unverified; verify cohort membership and participants before claiming independence. Do not use mixed diagnosis+symptom GCST90568441 as independent evidence because it includes diagnosis discovery data. No colocalization, new local genetic correlation or LAVA gate is inferred. No AlphaGenome result is treated as association evidence or used as model input.
