# Childhood ADHD-symptom follow-up at three Brain6 variants

A post-selected lookup in the 2025 childhood ADHD-symptom GWAS found **rs77960** associated with symptom scores in the same allele direction as the Brain6 diagnostic ADHD source (nominal p=0.00606). The chromosome 3 rs62264764 and chromosome 7 rs1476535 symptom lookups were not statistically persuasive. This is evidence for a **related symptom phenotype**, not verified two-trait sleep–ADHD locus replication or evidence of a causal variant.

| Variant | Aligned effect allele | Diagnostic ADHD discovery beta | Childhood symptom beta (SE) | Symptom p | Descriptive p×3 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| rs62264764 | A | −0.06470 | −0.00291 (0.00374) | 0.437 | 1.000 |
| rs77960 | A | +0.07321 | +0.00808 (0.00295) | 0.00606 | 0.0182 |
| rs1476535 | C | −0.07000 | −0.00119 (0.00271) | 0.660 | 1.000 |

Diagnostic betas are log-odds estimates; symptom betas are on the quantitative study's score scale and must not be compared numerically as if the traits and models were identical. Signs are aligned to the same effect allele; exact position and allele pair matched in all three cases. The p×3 column multiplies by the three markers chosen before this lookup. It is a **descriptive post-selection benchmark**, not a guarantee of error control for the broader Brain6 candidate search, other outcomes, or choices made before this follow-up. Weak p-values do not prove absence of effects, and same-direction estimates can occur by chance.

## Independence and phenotype

[Van der Laan et al. 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02295-y) explicitly state that their childhood symptom GWAS and the Demontis 2023 diagnostic ADHD GWAS had **no sample overlap**. The diagnostic source has the same 38,691 cases and 186,843 controls and cohort labels as the Brain6 ADHD source, making the authors' statement relevant to this comparison. This is author-reported participant nonoverlap; individual-level identity, relatedness and possible overlap with the separate sleep discovery GWAS were not independently audited here. The symptom GWAS uses population measures in children, not a new clinical ADHD case–control GWAS. Thus rs77960 is a useful independent-sample *related-phenotype* consistency signal on the ADHD side, but sleep-side independent replication, the full candidate-family correction and shared-causal colocalization remain unestablished.

We queried [GCST90568440](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST90568440), the symptom-only dataset. We did **not** substitute GCST90568441, the combined symptoms-and-diagnosis meta-analysis that includes the diagnostic source. The paper reports 70,953 unique symptom participants, whereas the downloaded harmonized metadata lists a sample-size field of 73,483; the meaning of this difference has not been resolved. Neither figure is a verified per-variant N. The catalog metadata declares GRCh38 and European ancestry. Independent data access, source QC, imputation, relatedness, phenotype details and exact study overlap need additional review before a replication claim.

## Reproducibility and limits

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) fixed the three markers and lookup rule before viewing the returned variant records. [Results](selected_variants.tsv), [publisher metadata](metadata.yaml), [bounded-query receipt](query_receipt.json), [query code](query.py) and [verification](verification.json) preserve exact representations, raw returned rows, allele alignment and source hashes. Independent arithmetic reproduced the source p-values from beta/SE within 1.4×10⁻¹⁵ and checked the p×3 calculation; this does not validate the GWAS model. Queries used the indexed [harmonized Catalog file](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90568001-GCST90569000/GCST90568440/harmonised/) remotely; the whole compressed file and index were not downloaded or hashed. A missing marker would have counted in the three-test denominator, but all three were present. This lookup did not itself compute a sleep-side follow-up, local genetic correlation or colocalization. A later [retrospective FinnGen insomnia integration](../symptom_r13_joint/RESULTS.md) shows same-direction rs77960 evidence in both follow-up phenotypes, but cohort independence and post-selection multiplicity remain unresolved. No AlphaGenome score entered the test, and the failed LAVA QC remains unresolved.
