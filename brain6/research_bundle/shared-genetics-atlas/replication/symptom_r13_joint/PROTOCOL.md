# Retrospective FinnGen insomnia and childhood ADHD-symptom comparison

This is a post-hoc integration: the three childhood-symptom results and the rs77960 FinnGen value were already inspected before this protocol. Use the three previously selected markers in the symptom lookup and the saved exact regional FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA extractions; assert extraction SHA-256 matches recorded receipts and require the exact unique GRCh38 rsID, coordinate, nonpalindromic allele pair, valid p, beta and SE. Align the FinnGen alternate-allele effect to the previously reported Brain6 A1, and report the source trait-specific association and direction, not cross-phenotype effect-size comparison. For each of the three variants compute max(p_sleep,p_symptom), and multiply by 3 capped at 1 as a numerical benchmark only. This is an intersection–union p-value under valid marginal p-values, even with dependent samples, but selection after seeing data and the larger earlier candidate family preclude claiming confirmatory multiple-testing control or independent discovery. Include missing markers in the denominator.

Document the study authors' report of no participant overlap between their symptom GWAS and the Brain6 diagnostic ADHD GWAS, but distinguish ADHD symptoms from clinical diagnosis and do not claim verified participant or relatedness independence across all discovery and follow-up cohorts. The FinnGen registry sleep phenotype differs from the original sleep measure and its exact overlap with the discovery sleep cohort is unverified. Previous R13 analyses reused the same FinnGen data. Results cannot prove causation, shared causal variant, independent two-trait replication, or a passed LAVA gate. No AlphaGenome outputs enter the calculations.
