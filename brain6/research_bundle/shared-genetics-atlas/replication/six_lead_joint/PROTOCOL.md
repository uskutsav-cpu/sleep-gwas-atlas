# Six original Brain6 insomnia–ADHD regional leads: retrospective cross-source screen

Before retrieving these six lead-marker results as a family, use exactly the one PLACO lead in each of the six previously frozen Brain6 insomnia–ADHD regions, as listed in BRAIN6_FINAL_CANDIDATES.tsv. This expands the previous three post-selected representative markers; the chromosome 5 lead is rs2431108, not rs77960. However, at least the rs2431108 FinnGen insomnia value and nearby rs77960 in both follow-up sources were already examined. Consequently this is a retrospective expansion, not a blinded or fully pre-registered replication test, and it does not correct for the broader original Brain6 search.

Require each region's original insomnia and ADHD files to match their frozen provenance hashes, each lead to be unique and agree on GRCh37 chromosome/position and nonpalindromic allele pair, and a unique positive-strand GRCh37-to-GRCh38 lift. Align discovery betas to the original insomnia effect allele. Retrieve exact coordinate, rsID and allele-pair matches from the locally authorized FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA file (verify its entire compressed SHA-256 against the existing source provenance receipt while streaming all rows) and from GWAS Catalog GCST90568440, the childhood-symptom-only harmonized indexed data. Do not use the combined symptom+diagnosis dataset. Exclude duplicates, ambiguous alleles, missing or invalid values, and report all statuses; preserve all six leads as the denominator whether or not available. Validate source beta/SE and p range, frequency if available, and align both follow-up betas to the discovery insomnia effect allele; ADHD discovery beta is independently aligned as well. Full source-frequency agreement with discovery is not possible because frozen regional inputs omit it. Do not substitute proxies.

For each lead compute max(p_sleep,p_symptom), then min(1,6*max-p) as a six-lead numerical benchmark. Under valid marginal p-values, max-p is an intersection–union p-value even with correlated follow-up samples; post-selection, prior marker lookups and uncertainty about independence mean the multiplied values are not confirmed FWER-controlled replication evidence. Evaluate matching effect directions separately. Report actual p-values, signs, all source exclusions and limitations. The authors report no participant overlap between their symptom GWAS and the 2023 diagnostic ADHD GWAS; this is not independent identity or relatedness verification. Registry insomnia differs from the original insomnia phenotype, and exact sleep cohort overlap remains unverified. The phenotype on the ADHD side is childhood symptoms, not an independent clinical diagnosis. No causal or gene assignment, local-correlation claim, LAVA rescue, or AlphaGenome-driven test is supported.
