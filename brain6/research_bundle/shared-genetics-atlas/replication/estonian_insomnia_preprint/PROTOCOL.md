# Estonian clinical-insomnia supplementary lookup

Inspect supplementary tables from the [clinical sleep-disorder GWAS preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.21.25340447), which states its discovery meta-analysis includes UKB, FinnGen and MVP and reports follow-up in the Estonian Biobank. Obtain the public Europe PMC supplementary archive; check archive and workbook integrity, publisher-provided member digest if available, sheet descriptions, trait definitions, build, alleles and selection procedures before comparing results with Brain6's six original lead loci. Record the complete searchable set and all exact coordinate/allele matches and nonmatches. Do not assume the 35 selected clinical-insomnia lead variants or any restricted table are complete regional summary data; absence is not a negative association. A nearby hit or different allele is not the same variant, and any follow-up lookup is post-selected.

Estonian clinical registry insomnia and Brain6 questionnaire insomnia differ in phenotype. The preprint's discovery combines cohorts that overlap Brain6's available sources, and Estonian follow-up independence from every Brain6 discovery sample is not independently audited. These are sleep-only data and cannot independently replicate ADHD, a joint locus, colocalization, molecular mechanism or LAVA. Do not claim peer review, statistical correction of the selected post-hoc lookup, or clinical utility.

After inspecting ST5 and observing that some rows overlap the broader Brain6 ranking, extend the lookup post hoc to all 425 ranked candidate variants using exact GRCh38 position, rsID and allele set. The 425 are selected credible-set variants; ST5 is itself restricted to preprint lead follow-ups. Report raw source p-values, not a confirmatory correction over the incomplete, selected intersection. Preserve source A1 and A0 and do not infer cross-study effect directions without a separate harmonization audit.
