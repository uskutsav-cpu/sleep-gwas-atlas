# Clinical-insomnia effect differences: exploratory sensitivity

For the chromosome 5 lead rs2431108, the MVP and FinnGen aligned C-allele log-odds estimates are approximately +0.0115 and +0.0281. Their difference is −0.0166. Assuming independent estimates and an approximate standard error reconstructed from the MVP confidence limits, the difference's Wald 95% interval is −0.0362 to +0.0029 and its nominal p-value is 0.0955. **The differing source p-values do not by themselves establish different effects**, and this interval does not demonstrate equivalence.

The actual dependence between source estimates is unknown. Under hypothetical correlations of −0.2, 0, +0.2 and +0.5, the CI-derived nominal difference p-values are respectively 0.128, 0.0955, 0.0625 and 0.0186. The +0.5 assumption is a stress test, not an estimated correlation or a claim about shared participants. In the separately computed p-derived standard-error sensitivity, the corresponding values are 0.127, 0.0948, 0.0619 and 0.0183. Across five matched leads, both SE reconstructions and the four assumed correlations, the smallest nominal p-value was 0.0183; multiplying by six gives 0.110. The sixth lead has no matched FinnGen record and stays in the six-lead denominator. This multiplier is a descriptive benchmark, not a project-wide or preregistered correction for the extensive earlier selection and sensitivity analyses.

The MVP source SE fields were missing, so neither SE reconstruction is verified. In particular, assuming the published OR limits are 95% Wald limits gives an approximate MVP p=0.00178 at rs2582894, whereas the source p=0.000933; at rs62264767, the corresponding values are 0.000112 and 0.0000499. Source testing methods, rounding and interval semantics need investigation before these can be interpreted as calibrated heterogeneity tests. The p-derived SE assumes a normal Wald p-value and is partly defined by that p-value, so its reproduction of the source p-value is not independent validation. Different sleep definitions, ancestry, ascertainment and possible overlap or relatedness further constrain comparison. The calculations do not establish replication of both sleep and ADHD, a shared causal variant or gene, or resolve LAVA QC.
