# MVP summary-statistic CI versus p-value diagnostic

Specified after the six lead estimates showed a discrepancy. Stream the first 100,000 rows of the same frozen GWAS Catalog-style MVP insomnia gzip, without storing the full source. Check whether source OR, OR confidence limits and two-sided p-values are numerically consistent with log-OR Wald tests, assuming the reported bounds are 95% log-Wald limits. Restrict the diagnostic to rows with finite positive OR and ordered positive CI, 1e-8 <= p <= 0.05, and absolute log OR >= 0.005 to limit rounding artifacts. Record counts, p-value-bin distributions of source z/CI-implied z, and bounds around the observed finite rounding precision; do not treat the first 100,000 rows as a random or independent genome-wide sample. Calculate whether the reported confidence interval contains 1 and whether p<0.05. Retrieve the public source and scientific methods where possible. A systematic discrepancy may reflect different estimators or test statistics; it does not by itself identify the cause or prove that source p-values or confidence intervals are wrong. It does not turn reconstructed standard errors into validated values.

After the broader scan, check the previously reported rs2582894 example against four-decimal rounding: evaluate all corners of ±0.00005 for the reported OR and bounds, assuming ordinary nearest rounding and a 95% log-Wald interval. This is a numerical sensitivity to rounding alone, not a reconstruction of the study's actual test or CI method.
