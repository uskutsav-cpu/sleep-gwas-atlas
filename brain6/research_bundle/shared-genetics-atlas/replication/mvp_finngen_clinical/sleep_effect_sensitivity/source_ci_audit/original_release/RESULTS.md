# Original MVP insomnia release: selected-row provenance

The original public [dbGaP neurological archive](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs002453/phs002453.v1.p1/analyses/GIA/phs002453.MVP_R4.1000G_AGR.GIA.PheCodes_Neurological.analysis-PI.MULTI.tar.table_of_contents.txt) contains the European insomnia file `Phe_327_4.EUR.GIA.dbGaP.txt.gz`. Its [source metadata](source_metadata.txt) reports 78,566 cases, 329,572 controls, and a SAIGE mixed-model analysis adjusted for age, sex and ten genetic principal components. The original member contains odds ratios, confidence limits and p-values, **but no standard-error column**; the missing SE in the saved file cannot be repaired from this release alone.

All six regional leads and rs62264764 appeared once in the original member. For all seven, chromosome, position, effect allele, allele pair, OR, confidence limits, p-value and case/control counts exactly matched the previously saved records. This strengthens the provenance of these selected rows and shows that the selected OR-limit versus p-value discrepancy was already present in the original dbGaP member. The two files are not independent evidence; seven matching rows do not prove every row is identical.

The cause of the difference between source p-values and interval-implied Wald p-values remains unverified. The metadata does not specify the statistical procedure used for the OR limits or how it relates to the p-value; it should not be inferred merely from the SAIGE label. The [earlier diagnostic](../RESULTS.md) and [cross-source sensitivity](../../RESULTS.md) retain their limitations.

The 180.6-GB archive was not downloaded or verified. Its published table of contents was used to locate the 578,841,528-byte BGZF member, and HTTP ranges and tar-header size, name and checksum were checked. The complete member was streamed without saving it; 33,772 BGZF member CRCs were checked, a SHA-256 was recorded, and the standard 28-byte BGZF EOF marker was independently confirmed at the member end. An initial parser that handled only the first concatenated gzip member failed its completion check and produced no accepted results; the corrected parser handled all members. The [retrieval code](extract.py), [metadata](source_metadata.txt), [source rows](original_rows.json), [retrieval receipt](receipt.json), [comparison code](verify.py), and [field comparison](comparison.json) document the checks. The recorded compressed-member hash is not a publisher-provided checksum.
