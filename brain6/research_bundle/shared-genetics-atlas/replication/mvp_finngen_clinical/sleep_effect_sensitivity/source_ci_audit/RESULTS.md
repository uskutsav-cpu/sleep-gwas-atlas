# MVP insomnia: confidence-limit and p-value audit

The discrepancy found in the six-lead comparison is also present in a wider **descriptive** scan. Among the first 100,000 rows of the saved MVP insomnia file, 7,316 met the protocol's numerical filters. Assuming the reported OR bounds are 95% log-Wald confidence limits, 1,384 of those eligible rows have a source p-value below 0.05 while their reported interval includes 1. These are correlated, ordered variants from a non-random portion of the file; the fraction is not an estimate of a genome-wide error rate.

For rs2582894, the source gives OR 0.9753, bounds 0.9601–0.9907 and p=0.0009328. Under a 95% log-Wald assumption, the interval implies p≈0.00178. Even allowing each four-decimal OR and bound to vary within ±0.00005 for ordinary rounding, the implied p ranges from approximately 0.00168 to 0.00188. Thus ordinary four-decimal rounding alone does not explain this example **under that assumed interval construction**.

The numerical mismatch does not establish an error. A confidence interval and p-value can arise from different procedures, and the exact original source estimators, confidence-limit convention and any processing are not established here. The public [MVP phenotype inventory](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/document.cgi?phd=8759&study_id=phs002453.v1.p1) reports the same 78,566 cases and 329,572 controls for a European-ancestry insomnia Phecode; this supports the source identification but does not prove file lineage. The [MVP gwPheWAS publication](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12857194/) describes a GPU implementation of SAIGE, while a [dbGaP analysis description](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/analysis.cgi?pha=12946&study_id=phs002453.v1.p1) describes SAIGE for a different phenotype. Neither documents exactly how the saved insomnia file's OR limits and p-values were produced.

Consequently, retain the original reported p-values as source results and treat the [MVP–FinnGen effect-difference calculations](../RESULTS.md) as uncalibrated sensitivities. Their two reconstructed SEs are not interchangeable or confirmed source SEs. These findings do not establish true effect heterogeneity, invalidate the original associations, or resolve cohort dependence.

[Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [100,000-row audit code](audit.py), [scan receipt](receipt.json), [rounding code](rounding_check.py), and [rounding receipt](rounding_receipt.json) document the selection, source path, calculations and assumptions. The source was streamed from the existing SSD file; the whole-file SHA-256 is from an earlier complete verification, not recomputed in this bounded scan.

An [original dbGaP source check](original_release/RESULTS.md) found exact matches for all seven selected rows, including the reported p-values and limits. The original release has no standard-error column, and its metadata does not explain the discrepancy.
