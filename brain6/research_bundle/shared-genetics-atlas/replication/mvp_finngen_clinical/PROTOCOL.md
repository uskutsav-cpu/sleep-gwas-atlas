# Retrospective MVP insomnia–FinnGen ADHD cross-source comparison

This protocol is written after the separate MVP and FinnGen lead-level results were examined; it is not a preregistered replication analysis. Join the six frozen Brain6 regional leads from the existing exact-marker MVP insomnia and indexed FinnGen R13 clinical ADHD tables by rsID, GRCh38 chromosome and position, and discovery effect allele. Reject mismatches, retain unavailable markers in the family, and calculate max(source sleep p, source ADHD p) and min(1, 6 × max-p) as a descriptive numerical benchmark. Preserve source effects and report each phenotype on its own scale. The separately prioritized rs62264764 is a seventh, already examined, correlated marker and must not be included in the six-lead denominator; describe it only as an exploratory separate comparison to its previously stored MVP insomnia result.

Max-p gives a valid intersection–union test if each marginal p is valid, without requiring independence of the two traits. The sixfold multiplier does not correct the broader prior candidate discovery or subsequent selection and is not a confirmatory adjusted result. Direction, phenotype and cohort identity/relatedness remain limited; do not treat distinct datasets as proven independent by name alone, assume comparable effect scales, infer a shared causal allele, or repair failed local QC. Save input hashes and code. No AlphaGenome model output enters the calculation.
