# Family-based ADHD study: replication-source assessment

[Justice et al. 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00439-026-02840-7) analyzed ADHD in two family cohorts (1,538 and 631 individuals). They report using an independent 2019 PGC ADHD polygenic score and found it associated in both cohorts. This is useful evidence about ADHD polygenic architecture, but a genome-wide score association does **not** independently replicate a particular Brain6 variant or a shared sleep–ADHD locus. Whether these cohorts are independent of the 2023 Brain6 diagnostic discovery source and all proposed sleep sources needs separate documentation; independence from the 2019 score alone does not establish that.

The article reports 258,399 variants after overlap and QC in its family-based meta-analysis and presents selected associations. Its [data-availability statement](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00439-026-02840-7) says family cohort deposits to dbGaP were pending consent/approvals and accession numbers at publication. This check did not identify full variant-level association statistics to test the six Brain6 leads. This is an availability observation, not proof such files do not exist elsewhere.

The publisher's [supplement](../../PUBLICATION.md#external-dependencies) (external target: `source_supplement.docx`) was downloaded and its Word-document text checked for the six Brain6 regional leads plus rs62264764 and rs77960. None of the eight IDs occurred in that text. This does not establish that they were tested or null: the supplement contains selected tables and figures rather than a verified complete GWAS. FOXP2 appears in a table of **previously published** ADHD GWAS gene sets, not a family-cohort variant result. A MEF2C text occurrence is in a schizophrenia reference gene list; it is not a family-cohort MEF2C finding. Image text was not searched.

The [extraction script](check_supplement.py) and [receipt](receipt.json) record source URL, checksum, ZIP integrity and substring counts. More complete authorized data, allele/build and model checks, a frozen candidate family and source-verified cohort independence are necessary before a locus replication test. None of this resolves the failed local QC gate or establishes independent two-trait replication.
