# Two chromosome 3 variants across reference populations

Before examining results, query rs62264764 and rs62264767 from the same 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 UCSC indexed VCF as the prior European analysis. Match rsID, position and allele pair, retain phased diploid calls with both alleles in the specified contrasts, and calculate phased-haplotype allele frequencies and r² within each of the five reference super-population labels. Resample individuals 2,000 times within each group for percentile intervals, with a fixed seed; retain exclusions, URL, sample counts, panel hash and per-group counts, but no genotypes or sample identifiers. Verify the EUR point estimate against the previous calculation and independently recalculate frequency and r² from aggregated haplotype counts.

This post-hoc reference comparison is not an association study, causal-variant resolution or a measure of clinical risk. The broad labels and small group sample sizes do not represent every individual or population. Phase and frequency uncertainty, incomplete whole-file provenance and study/reference mismatch remain; no source GWAS is assumed to have those reference LD values. If a variant is rare or monomorphic, report that r² and its interval are unavailable rather than treating it as zero. Do not use AlphaGenome scores to train a model.
