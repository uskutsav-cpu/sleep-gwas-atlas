# rs62264764 replication planning scenarios

This is a post-selection planning calculation, not a replication result or a calibrated prediction of FinnGen performance. Use the rs62264764 original Brain6 ADHD log-odds estimate and European reference ALT frequency in the existing source-verified pilot. Assume a simple additive logistic model, unrelated participants, no covariates, equal allele frequency in cases and controls, genotype variance 2f(1-f), and small effects. Under the null-information approximation SE(beta) = sqrt(4 / (N_eff*2f*(1-f))), N_eff=4*Ncase*Ncontrol/(Ncase+Ncontrol). A two-sided Wald z has unit variance and mean beta/SE. Compute normal-tail power for alpha=0.05 and an illustrative Bonferroni alpha=0.05/6, with the discovery beta scaled by 0, 0.5, 1 and 1.5, f fixed at 0.1640159, and Ncase=5,559 with either 100,000 controls or infinitely many controls. The 5,559 is an endpoint descriptive count, not a verified GWAS case count; both study sizes are hypothetical. Also solve for the effective N and infinite-controls case count yielding 80% power at the observed beta and each threshold. Record code, inputs and hashes. A six-locus family is an illustrative assumption, not a frozen replication family. These calculations cannot establish actual power under case ascertainment, ascertainment differences, imputation, relatedness, covariates, participant overlap, allele frequency differences, LD, winner's curse or heterogeneous effect sizes. Do not multiply marginal powers into joint power without a joint model; the two-trait replication probability is at most either marginal power. No phenotype is measured, no new p-value estimated, and no LAVA gate is rescued.
