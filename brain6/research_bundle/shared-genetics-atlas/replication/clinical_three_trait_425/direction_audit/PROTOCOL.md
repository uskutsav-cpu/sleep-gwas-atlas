# Clinical ADHD discovery direction and source arithmetic

For each of the 425 candidates, join the saved Finnish clinical ADHD follow-up to its original Brain6 discovery ADHD summary. Require exact rsID, GRCh38 variant and region; validate both source alleles against the reference/ALT pair and orient the discovery beta to GRCh38 ALT. Preserve missing records and zero or invalid effects as unavailable. Tabulate same-direction counts overall and by region, together with follow-up p-value thresholds descriptively; candidates and regions were selected post hoc, and LD makes SNP rows dependent. Do not use binomial significance or call sign agreement independent replication. As a separate source arithmetic diagnostic compare reported two-sided follow-up p-values with the normal approximation from beta/SE, acknowledging source rounding and method-specific p-values. This checks consistency, not calibration or original source lineage. No predictions or AlphaGenome model outputs are used for training.
