# Clinical ADHD follow-up: discovery-direction audit

After aligning to the same GRCh38 ALT allele, **407 of 419** candidates with usable original ADHD discovery effects and FinnGen clinical ADHD follow-up effects had matching signs. This is descriptive context for the [three-trait screen](../RESULTS.md), **not 407 independent replications**. The candidates were selected using the original data and are correlated within six regions; sample and family overlap are not fully resolved. The remaining 12 usable rows have opposite signs, and one additional FinnGen-matched marker, rs6891820, lacks a saved discovery ADHD beta. The five variants without a FinnGen exact match remain missing.

The six regions have different direction patterns: all matched rows agree in the selected chromosome 3, chromosome 11 and both chromosome 5 regions, while 58 of 68 agree on chromosome 14 and 64 of 66 on chromosome 7. These rows do not form independent observations, and sign matching alone does not establish a common causal variant or gene. A small or opposite follow-up effect does not prove a difference between studies; no heterogeneity or formal replication test is asserted.

For all 420 FinnGen-matched rows, a two-sided normal p-value reconstructed from the saved beta and standard error was close to the source-reported p-value: the largest absolute difference in log₁₀(p) was approximately 1.60×10⁻⁵. This is an arithmetic and rounding check under a normal approximation, not a test of the source model's calibration, original full-file parsing, cohort independence or discovery coding. The analysis is post hoc and does not change the finding that none of 419 complete insomnia–clinical-ADHD–HFRS rows crossed the selected-set benchmark. Hospital frailty is not an item-excluded physical-frailty GWAS.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [variant-level output](candidate_directions.tsv), [regional summary](region_summary.tsv), [code](run.py) and [input-hash receipt](receipt.json) document the joins and calculations. The source and ALT allele pairs were checked across builds using the existing reference-verified crosswalk; the historical source's provenance caveats remain in the [FinnGen lookup](../../finngen_adhd_raw_lookup/RESULTS.md). No AlphaGenome output was used to train a model.
