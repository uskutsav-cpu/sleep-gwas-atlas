# Clinical ADHD, insomnia and hospital frailty across 425 selected candidates

The existing FinnGen R13 clinical ADHD source yielded exact GRCh38 rsID and allele matches for **420 of 425** previously selected Brain6 candidates. Of those, **419** also had matched MVP insomnia and Finnish hospital-frailty results. None of the 419 complete rows crossed the post-hoc numerical benchmark `425 × max(p_insomnia, p_ADHD, p_HFRS) < 0.05`. The smallest observed maximum p-value was 0.00448 at rs12705982, which gives a capped benchmark of 1.0. This marker was selected after inspecting the result and is not a validated three-trait locus.

All 425 original slots remain in the benchmark denominator; five lack an exact clinical ADHD match and six lack at least one of the three source matches. Missing rows are not treated as negative associations. **The non-crossing result does not prove an absence of shared biology.** The max-p intersection–union test concerns marginal associations with all three traits, conditional on valid source p-values; it does not test a common causal variant, effect direction, mediation or gene interaction. The candidate set and source analyses were already selected and investigated, so the 425-slot benchmark does not correct the broader project search or establish a prospective discovery.

This complements the previous [425-candidate childhood ADHD-symptom screen](../../variant_context/brain6_three_trait_candidate/RESULTS.md), which used a quantitative symptom measure rather than clinical ADHD. It also extends the earlier [seven-marker clinical comparison](../clinical_three_trait/RESULTS.md). Different phenotype definitions, cohort power and participant overlap mean that results cannot be compared as a controlled test of clinical versus symptom traits. Clinical ADHD and HFRS both come from FinnGen and may share participants; independence from discovery studies and relatedness were not established. HFRS is hospital-based and includes a somnolence-related diagnostic category, not a physical-frailty GWAS with sleep or exhaustion removed. MVP sleep effects use rounded source odds ratios. Finnish ancestry and LD, sample differences and source model calibration remain material.

The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [425-row table](results.tsv), [query and join code](run.py), [interval/input-hash receipt](receipt.json) and [arithmetic and earlier-lead verification](verification.json) preserve the analysis. Queries used a previously verified publisher index and a pinned object generation. A prior independent whole-source compressed-byte scan matched the historical MD5, but neither this analysis nor that scan completely decompressed the full BGZF source; indexed results and input identity do not prove source-model validity. Five overlapping previously queried clinical leads matched in p-value and ALT-aligned beta. No AlphaGenome output was used for model training, and failed local-correlation gates remain unchanged.

A [discovery-direction audit](direction_audit/RESULTS.md) finds matching signs for 407 of 419 usable candidate rows, but their strong LD and post-selection make this a descriptive count, not independent replications. Follow-up beta/SE values are arithmetically consistent with the reported p-values under a normal approximation.
