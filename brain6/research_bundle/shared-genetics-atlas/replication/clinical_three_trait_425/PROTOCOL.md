# 425-candidate clinical ADHD, insomnia and hospital-frailty follow-up

After inspecting the prior seven-marker clinical follow-up and childhood-symptom screen, retrospectively query the same pinned public FinnGen R13 clinical ADHD source across the six existing 425-candidate regions, using the previously verified publisher index. Join the previously fully scanned MVP insomnia–FinnGen HFRS candidate table by exact rsID, GRCh38 coordinate and non-palindromic REF/ALT pair. Require unique matches and finite p, beta, SE and frequency, retain missing or invalid rows, and align ADHD ALT effects to the candidate ALT. Record interval queries, byte hashes, source generation and input hashes. Indexed reads do not independently verify the complete source; the prior compressed whole-source MD5 stream was checked, but complete BGZF decompression and original ingestion were not verified.

For all 425 candidate slots report max(p_MVP insomnia, p_FinnGen ADHD, p_FinnGen HFRS), multiplying by 425 as a numerical post-hoc Bonferroni benchmark with missing slots kept in the denominator. If marginal p-values are valid, max-p tests association with all three traits without requiring independence, but it cannot test shared causality or confirm replication. Clinical ADHD and HFRS may share FinnGen participants, and exact overlap with all discovery cohorts is unknown. ADHD phenotype differs from childhood symptoms, MVP insomnia differs from questionnaire/registry insomnia, HFRS includes a somnolence-related code and is not a sleep-excluded physical-frailty composite. Traits, ancestry and ascertainment differ, and MVP odds ratios are rounded. Region/marker selection and prior investigation make all inference exploratory, and the benchmark does not correct the broader project. No AlphaGenome outputs are used for training.
