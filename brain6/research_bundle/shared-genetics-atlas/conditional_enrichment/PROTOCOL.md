# Conditional p-value enrichment diagnostic

Prepared before this analysis. Use the same ordered 1,138,262 common HapMap3 rows for FinnGen R13 registry insomnia and FI/FFS. For each pair, in both directions, stratify one trait by |Z| corresponding to two-sided p ≤1, 0.1, 0.01 or 0.001; report the fraction of selected markers whose other-trait p ≤0.05, 0.01 or 0.001. All p-values are approximate normal tails from aligned Z rounded to three decimals. Validate full row, allele and finite-value identity. Baseline p≤1 includes all rows. These descriptive conditional distributions are not conditional FDR, conjFDR, calibrated locus tests, or independent marker counts.

For intuition, simulate 1,000,000 independent pairs per trait pair with the observed PLACO correlation-subset correlation, once with unit marginal variance and once with the estimated genome-wide variance parameters. Use a fixed seed and the same simulated Gaussian draws for both scenarios. The unit-variance and inflated-variance zero-mean normals are artificial controls, not inferred true genetic nulls: the fitted correlation can reflect genetic correlation and other dependence, and estimated genome-wide variance includes polygenicity and model artifacts. These simulations omit LD, phenotype ascertainment, sample-relatedness and selection, and small conditional tails have high Monte Carlo uncertainty. No confidence intervals or p-values treating observed SNPs as independent are appropriate. Do not subtract simulation fractions to make discoveries or conclude biological specificity from enrichment.

Report raw numerator, denominator and fraction for each combination, along with pair, direction, source and hash. No locus selection, multiplicity claim or prediction-model training is performed. Published-style enrichment conjFDR requires its own validated implementation, reference LD/pruning, independent assumptions and calibrated analysis.
