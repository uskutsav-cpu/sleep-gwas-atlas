# Common-support conditional comparison of FI, FFS and HFRS

Prepared before this comparison. Join FinnGen R13 insomnia, UKB/TwinGene FI and UKB FFS aligned HapMap3 Z scores to the existing FinnGen R12 HFRS filtered p-values and GRCh37 HapMap3 reference coordinates. Require a unique rsID and matching unordered SNP alleles at all joins, valid source p-values, consistent chromosome and valid autosomal coordinates; report every exclusion. Use only markers with all four trait values so differences are on identical support. HFRS source p-values and the other three normal p-values from rounded Z have different precision; rsID/allele consistency across builds does not replace reference-aware liftover or source-model validation.

For each frailty phenotype, calculate the fraction of selected markers with insomnia p≤0.01 among markers with that frailty p≤0.001. These conditioning groups differ and were chosen after earlier results; their fraction differences are not genetic correlations or causal phenotype contrasts. For FI−FFS, FI−HFRS and FFS−HFRS, use paired delete-one-physical-block jackknife SEs with separate 5 Mb and 10 Mb chromosome bins, retaining covariance from shared markers. Include selected, joint and baseline counts per bin and a descriptive normal 1.96-SE interval; no formal null test, FDR or locus call is made.

Physical bins do not guarantee independent observations, especially for long-range LD. The three studies have different populations, power, phenotype definitions, source methods, sample overlap and data precision. FI includes a sleep item; FFS includes exhaustion; HFRS uses hospital codes. R13 insomnia and R12 HFRS overlap FinnGen participants. These differences can influence conditional distributions independently of underlying biological sharing. No independent two-trait replication or effect of excluding phenotype components is established.
