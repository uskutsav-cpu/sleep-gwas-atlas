# Block-jackknife sensitivity of conditional fractions

Post-hoc sensitivity prepared after the descriptive enrichment analysis. Join the exact common-support insomnia, FI and FFS Z tables to the pinned GRCh37 HapMap3 reference by unique rsID and matching unordered allele pair. Retain autosomal valid coordinates, count missing/duplicate/mismatching records, and report the resulting denominator. Identify 5 Mb and, separately, 10 Mb non-overlapping physical chromosome bins. For each frailty pair and both directions, consider target p≤0.01 and conditioning p≤0.001, using the original normal-tail approximations from the three-decimal Z values. Compute the baseline fraction, conditional fraction, their difference and ratio; leave out one occupied physical block at a time and calculate the usual delete-one-block jackknife standard error for the difference. Record the largest fraction of conditional markers in a single block and a 1.96-SE interval only as a normal-approximation descriptive sensitivity.

Physical windows are not independent biological units and neither 5 Mb nor 10 Mb ensures that all LD falls within blocks. Unequal block sizes, heterogeneous SNP density, genetic correlations across regions, selection on both traits, population structure, sample overlap, rounding, fitted parameters and the post-hoc thresholds limit the jackknife's inferential meaning. The interval does not test a genetic null, validate PLACO or conjFDR, identify shared causal variants, prove biology or provide independent replication. Save all block totals and hashes to enable examination and recomputation.
