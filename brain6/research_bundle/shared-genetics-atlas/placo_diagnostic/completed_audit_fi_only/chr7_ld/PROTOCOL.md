# Post-hoc chr7 FI diagnostic LD comparison

The FI scan selected seven threshold-crossing SNPs on chr7 after a questionable PLACO+ calibration check. Use the 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 European reference to compare their phased haplotype LD with the lowest-p selected chr7 SNP rs2396766 and with previously studied Brain6 rs1476535 and the prior sleep–frailty candidate rs12536335. Select these comparisons from prior context, not to infer independent loci from their number. Require exact coordinate, rsID and biallelic contrast and report missing/mismatching reference records, sample counts and reference alleles; retain pairs only where both valid genotypes exist. Use paired EUR individuals, no individual genotypes in outputs, and 1,000 individual bootstrap draws for descriptive r² intervals. The 503-person EUR panel includes FIN individuals and is not the study LD; the bootstrap reflects panel sampling only. rs1476535 and rs12536335 are themselves in strong reference LD, so neither is independent replication. This is not local genetic correlation, causal fine-mapping, gene evidence or evidence that FI–insomnia diagnostic threshold crossings are valid at genome-wide alpha.
