# Chr7 reference-LD sign consistency

After the FI diagnostic and the single-signal FI–insomnia coloc were both inspected, compare the two diagnostic anchors with the two highest conditional-H4 markers. Use the already saved EUR reference-panel signed haplotype LD and the FI–R13 insomnia matched regional statistics. For each SNP align the two source betas to the reference-LD counted allele only if the same or reversed unique two-base allele pair matches. Then record, separately for each trait, whether the sign of beta1×beta2 agrees with the sign of reference r; retain missing and zero values explicitly. Preserve source hashes, both original effect alleles, aligned betas, standard errors and p-values, and the exact reference r.

The sign relationship is a heuristic expectation in a simple single-signal setting, not a formal causal test. Reused association data and post-selection, allele uncertainty, measurement overlap, multiple causal signals and population-specific LD can all affect it. Sign agreement across LD-linked markers is not independent replication, evidence of a causal variant or gene, a validated PLACO p-value, a three-trait colocalization, or proof of effect magnitude or clinical risk.
