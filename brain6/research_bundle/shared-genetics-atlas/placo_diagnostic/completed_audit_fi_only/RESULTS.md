# FI–registry-insomnia PLACO+ diagnostic: completed FI pair

The FI pair completed 1,138,262 HapMap3 markers and passed the row, input-allele and numerical-output audit, including exact agreement with all 2,191 previously calculated controls. There were 16 SNP-level crossings of the predeclared two-pair Bonferroni numerical threshold 0.05/(2×1,138,262) = 2.1963×10⁻⁸. This is a post-hoc diagnostic, **not a validated discovery list**: the one-trait-only fixed-mean stress tests showed elevated rejection at the nominal level, and the simulation cannot verify calibration at this stringent threshold. Association models, sample overlap, ancestry and rounding remain material. The FI contains sleep-related items.

All 16 markers have unique matching source rsID and alleles for both traits. When the same fitted nuisance parameters are held fixed, all 16 remain below the numerical threshold when recalculated using source-p-derived Z values and when using source beta/SE-derived Wald Z values. That is a precision sensitivity, not independent replication or formal recalibration. In the Wald sensitivity, the largest p is 2.15×10⁻⁸, close to the 2.1963×10⁻⁸ cutoff. Source FI beta and SE have limited precision, and a model-derived p need not equal a Wald p.

The minimum source max(p_insomnia,p_FI) across the 16 is 4.46×10⁻⁵, so **none** approaches the same threshold under the conservative intersection–union max-p test. PLACO+ and max-p make different assumptions and test statistics; neither difference nor numerical stability establishes a shared causal variant. The lowest PLACO+ value is 1.92×10⁻⁹ for rs11876458, with source insomnia p=4.46×10⁻⁵ and FI p=1.47×10⁻⁸. Its positional match is chr18:39289536 GRCh37. All source positions are joined through unique rsID and allele matches; they are not an independent cross-build or multiallelic audit.

Seven markers fall in chr7:114304563–114336114, six in chr18:39256431–39314744 and three in chr22:41418154–41476337 (GRCh37). These are three chromosome-specific clusters by proximity, **not** three independently established loci. The chr7 interval lies within the broader Brain6 FOXP2-containing window, but physical overlap alone does not establish the same causal signal, an effector gene or a sleep–ADHD–frailty mechanism. Appropriate LD analysis, full-precision and source-model checks, dense multi-signal colocalization and independent two-trait replication remain necessary. The original FFS output failed gzip validation; the audited replacement is described in the [combined diagnostic](../completed_audit/RESULTS.md).

`completed_audit.json`, `audited_threshold_crossings.tsv`, `candidate_source_matches.tsv`, `candidate_statistic_sensitivity.tsv`, and `candidate_positions.tsv` contain the checks and results; `source_hashes.json` identifies the source bytes. `map_candidates.py` performs the position join. The [protocol](../PROTOCOL.md), [null stress review](../NULL_SIMULATION_REVIEW.md), [precision sensitivity](../PRECISION_SENSITIVITY.md), and [FFS integrity incident](../ffs_recovery/INCIDENT.md) describe the broader limitations.

A subsequent [chr7 reference-LD check](chr7_ld/RESULTS.md) finds strong linkage among the seven selected diagnostic markers, but r² only 0.081–0.110 with the selected Brain6 and prior frailty anchors in 503 EUR reference individuals. A broad shared window therefore cannot be used as evidence that these selected markers tag the same signal.

A [published-candidate crosswalk](prior_literature/RESULTS.md) identifies rs12671330 as already reported by Song et al. and present in the Brain6 insomnia credible-set union; this is not independent replication. [Chr18 and chr22 reference LD](chr18_chr22_ld/RESULTS.md) confirms substantial linkage within those selected groups, without validating distinct loci.

[Position-only Ensembl context](positional_annotation/RESULTS.md) places four chr7 markers within FOXP2, with three just outside; position does not establish an effector gene. The chr18 and chr22 markers do not overlap a gene body in the queried GRCh37 annotation.

A [chr7 coloc crosscheck](coloc_crosscheck/RESULTS.md) finds r²≈0.71–0.75 between the diagnostic anchors and the two highest conditional H4 markers in the EUR reference panel. Both analyses reuse the same traits and remain sensitive to their respective assumptions; this is not independent replication.
