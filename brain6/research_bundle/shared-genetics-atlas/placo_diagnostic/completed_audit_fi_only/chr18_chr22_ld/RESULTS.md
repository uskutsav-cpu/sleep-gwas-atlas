# Reference LD within the chr18 and chr22 FI diagnostic groups

In 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 EUR reference samples, the five other selected chr18 markers have r²=0.803–0.856 with rs11876458, the smallest-PLACO-p chr18 marker. On chr22, rs4820425 and rs7290458 have r²=0.981 and 0.620, respectively, with rs926914, the smallest-PLACO-p chr22 marker. Pairwise sample counts are 502–503; the chr22 rs4820425 contrast excludes one individual with another allele or unavailable genotype. Exact rsID, position and allele matches were required. `chr18_pairs.tsv`, `chr22_pairs.tsv` and the accompanying receipts retain counted alleles, sample counts and individual-bootstrap intervals.

Together with the [chr7 reference comparison](../chr7_ld/RESULTS.md), these checks show why 16 threshold-crossing markers should not be counted as 16 independent loci. The comparisons use post-selected anchors and a small, broad EUR reference, not study-specific Finnish or UKB/TwinGene LD. They do not validate the PLACO threshold, establish independent loci, identify causal variants, or establish shared sleep–frailty biology. The [pre-comparison protocol](PROTOCOL.md) and `calculate.py` document the analysis.
