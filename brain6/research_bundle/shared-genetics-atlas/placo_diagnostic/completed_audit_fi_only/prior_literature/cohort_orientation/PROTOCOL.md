# Atkins cohort allele-orientation diagnostic

Post-hoc investigation after finding that the public cohort file and the paper's rs2396766 directions may disagree. Use the publicly released Atkins individual-cohort association file and README for four previously selected variants: rs2396766 and rs1363103, both printed as UKB/TwinGene +− in the article's Table 2; rs12671330, a Song and Brain6 candidate; and rs1456031, a leading conditional coloc candidate. Join exact unique rsID, coordinate and unordered allele pair to the local FI meta-analysis extract. Preserve the published file's EA/OA, cohort betas, SEs, p-values and frequencies, and the local meta effect allele.

Compare two explicit interpretations: the README's declared common EA for both cohort effects and, as a sensitivity, flipping the TwinGene beta and frequency to the UKB EA. For each, compute an approximate inverse-variance fixed-effect pooled beta and a two-study heterogeneity p from the difference in cohort betas. Compare these with the already published, rounded meta estimates, directions and heterogeneity p. The paper used METAL with genomic control; these simple calculations do not reproduce its exact model, quantify QC or prove author intent. Different population allele frequencies and rounding are possible; closeness to published values may provide evidence for an adapter but cannot overrule source documentation without author confirmation. All results reuse the same cohorts, contain sleep-related FI components, and do not provide independent replication or evidence for a sleep–frailty causal variant.
