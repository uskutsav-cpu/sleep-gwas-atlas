# Prior FI discovery and reported heterogeneity at rs2396766

The chr7 FI–insomnia PLACO diagnostic marker **rs2396766** was already a genome-wide FI lead in [Atkins et al. 2021, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8441299/): GRCh37 chr7:114318071, effect/other A/G, effect-allele frequency rounded to 0.47, meta-analysis beta rounded to +0.020, SE 0.0033 and p=1.2×10⁻⁹. The current source has A/G, beta +0.0201, SE 0.0033 and p=1.22×10⁻⁹. These are matching statistics from the **same FI study**, not independent replication of an FI association. The paper labels FOXP2 by location, which does not establish that FOXP2 mediates the FI or insomnia association.

The paper's Table 2 prints cohort direction **+−**, defined in its footnote as UK Biobank and TwinGene, and prints a heterogeneity p-value of **0.07** for this variant. Its narrative also describes this locus as having “significant heterogeneity.” Those two statements are not obviously consistent at a conventional 0.05 cutoff: the table's rounded 0.07 is above 0.05, whereas opposite point-estimate signs do not by themselves establish a statistically significant difference. We do not resolve the discrepancy from the displayed table. The direction discordance and the article's own caution warrant examining exact cohort-specific association files, allele harmonization and scale before relying on a pooled direction. Do not silently convert this into evidence of a homogeneous effect or an independently replicated locus.

The new FinnGen registry-insomnia association and post-hoc FI–insomnia coloc are different analyses. Neither establishes joint replication, a shared causal variant, or absence of sleep-item contribution to FI. The [diagnostic](../RESULTS.md), [prior Song crosswalk](RESULTS.md), and [reference LD](../coloc_crosscheck/RESULTS.md) preserve their distinct limitations.

The public cohort files are now available and have been examined. A [cohort-orientation sensitivity analysis](cohort_orientation/RESULTS.md) shows that flipping the TwinGene fields would approximately match the paper's directions and heterogeneity p, but conflicts with the source README; author clarification remains necessary.
