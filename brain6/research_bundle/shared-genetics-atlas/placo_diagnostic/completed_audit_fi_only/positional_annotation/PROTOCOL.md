# Position-only annotation of FI diagnostic markers

For the 16 post-selected FI–registry-insomnia diagnostic markers, query Ensembl's GRCh37 gene-overlap endpoint for each of the three chromosome intervals spanning selected positions plus a 250 kb flank. Verify returned chromosome and GRCh37 assembly. Record every gene whose gene-body coordinates overlap a marker and, separately, the nearest protein-coding gene body returned within the fetched window (ties retained). Distance is zero for overlap, otherwise the distance in base pairs to the nearest gene-body boundary. Keep unlabeled and noncoding genes as returned. Preserve source response bytes, URL, hash and the source marker-table hash. Empty results and genes beyond the bounded windows are not negative functional evidence; transcript-specific effects, liftover, strand, regulatory activity and gene regulation are not measured by a gene-body overlap. Coordinates, candidate selection and model limitations are those of the FI diagnostic; no claim of a causal gene, validated PLACO association, independent replication or pathway enrichment follows from this annotation.
