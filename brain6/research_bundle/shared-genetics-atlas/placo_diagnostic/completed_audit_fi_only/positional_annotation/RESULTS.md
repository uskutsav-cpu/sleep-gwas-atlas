# Reference gene context for FI diagnostic markers

The 16 post-selected markers were compared with gene-body coordinates returned by Ensembl's GRCh37 REST service. Four chr7 markers—rs2396766, rs1378771, rs12705971 and rs12705973—overlap the FOXP2 gene body in this annotation. The other three chr7 markers, including published candidate rs12671330, lie 216–2,287 bp beyond the annotated FOXP2 boundary. In the queried annotation, none of the six chr18 or three chr22 markers overlaps a gene body.

The closest **returned protein-coding gene bodies** for the chr18 markers are PIK3C3, at distances of about 220–279 kb; for chr22, the returned closest coding genes are RBX1 or EP300, about 11–56 kb away. The query covered each selected chromosome interval plus 250 kb, so a returned gene more than 250 kb from a particular marker is not guaranteed to be its nearest genome-wide. These distances are to the nearest boundary of the whole gene body, not to a transcription start site or enhancer target. GRCh37's annotation version differs from newer gene releases.

Position alone does not assign an effector gene or pathway. In particular, FOXP2 overlap does not establish that these variants regulate FOXP2, that a predicted effect occurs in a relevant cell type, or that FI, insomnia and ADHD share a causal variant. The [reference LD check](../chr7_ld/RESULTS.md) finds weak tagging of the earlier selected Brain6 anchor, and the underlying FI PLACO result remains a calibration-limited diagnostic. The non-overlap of other markers with annotated gene bodies does not imply no regulatory effect.

`marker_context.tsv` includes each marker, all returned overlapping gene bodies, and the nearest returned protein-coding gene and distance. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md), script, raw Ensembl responses and `receipt.json` preserve query windows and hashes. Functional assignment would require appropriate tissue-specific molecular QTL, multi-signal colocalization and direct perturbation.
