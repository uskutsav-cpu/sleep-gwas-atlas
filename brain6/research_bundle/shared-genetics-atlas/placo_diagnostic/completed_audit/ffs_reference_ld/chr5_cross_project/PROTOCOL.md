# Chromosome 5 post-selected cross-project LD protocol

Compare every one of the 27 insomnia–FFS PLACO+ selected chr5 markers with four previously documented anchors: FI–insomnia conditional H4 leaders rs1363103 and rs13164188; insomnia–ADHD Brain6 candidate rs2431108; and prior published FFS lead rs7703746. Include identity comparisons and explicitly flag them. Source anchor rsID, GRCh37 position, and allele pair from the existing published-FFS LD analysis. Use the same 1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 phased samples, reported separately for all 503 EUR samples and their FIN subset; these are nested samples, not independent replications. Require an exact rsID, coordinate and two alleles in a single reference record; exclude missing, unphased and third-allele genotypes pairwise. Report all pairwise r and r² and sample counts, not solely the strongest pairs. Record missing targets and input hashes. The reference, post-selection, inherited association statistics and LD cannot establish common causality or independent replication. No population comparisons or specific biological mechanisms are prespecified; FIN is a small sensitivity panel.
