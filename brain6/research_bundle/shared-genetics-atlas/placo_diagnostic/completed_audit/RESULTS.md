# Completed FI and FFS PLACO+ diagnostic

The recovered FFS run completed and its two chunks and combined output passed gzip integrity checks. The full row audit matched 1,138,262 aligned markers per trait pair and all 2,191 stored control calculations in each pair. All 82 threshold-crossing SNP–pair rows had unique rsID and allele matches in the original insomnia and corresponding frailty source files. This establishes computational consistency, not statistical calibration or independent validation.

At the numerical threshold 0.05/(2 × 1,138,262) = 2.1963 × 10⁻⁸, there were 16 FI and 66 FFS marker-level crossings. With the same fitted nuisance parameters, all 16 FI and all 66 FFS rows remained below threshold using signed Z values derived from the original reported p-values. Using beta/SE Wald Z values instead, 16 FI and 63 FFS rows remained below; FFS markers rs132902, rs1810460 and rs5995992 crossed above the threshold. Model-derived p-values need not equal Wald p-values, and the exact BOLT model mapping still needs confirmation. A subsequent [allele-adapter audit](../../replication/ye_2023/allele_adapter/RESULTS.md) supports the FFS A1 convention for the published discovery rows. None of the 82 passes the same threshold using the conservative maximum of its two original marginal p-values: the smallest such max-p is 4.46 × 10⁻⁵ for FI and 2.50 × 10⁻⁵ for FFS.

The lowest FFS diagnostic PLACO+ p is 3.29 × 10⁻¹¹ at rs6596578; its insomnia and FFS source p-values are 2.50 × 10⁻⁵ and 2.2 × 10⁻¹¹, respectively. This is not proof of a shared causal variant. The FFS markers form five chromosome-specific groups by proximity, using a 500 kb maximum gap: 27 on chr5:103810310–104078233, 9 on chr11:57434122–57641883, 4 on chr16:28977020–28993049, 14 on chr17:65832576–65887896, and 12 on chr22:41487218–41949259. Coordinates are those of the FFS source and match the project's GRCh37 FI reference by unique rsID, alleles and position; this is not a comprehensive multi-allelic or independent build audit. Proximity groups are not independent loci; LD and fine-mapping remain to be evaluated. FI-specific checks and prior literature context are [here](../completed_audit_fi_only/RESULTS.md).

Interpret all crossings as **post-hoc diagnostic leads**, not validated discoveries. The one-trait-only fixed-mean null stress tests showed elevated rejection at nominal alpha, and the simulations do not establish calibration at the genome-wide threshold. Sample overlap, ancestry and phenotype construction matter; FI contains sleep-related items and FFS includes exhaustion. No component-excluded GWAS, independent joint replication, or causal mediation is demonstrated. PLACO+ and max-p have distinct assumptions and their difference alone cannot establish pleiotropy.

The machine-readable audit, source matches, sensitivity estimates, positions and reproducible position join are in this folder. The [recovery incident](../ffs_recovery/INCIDENT.md), [protocol](../PROTOCOL.md), and [null stress review](../NULL_SIMULATION_REVIEW.md) record methods and limitations.

A [reference LD follow-up](ffs_reference_ld/RESULTS.md) shows that many FFS candidates are strongly linked, with three chr5 reference mismatches and a possible but sensitivity-dependent chr22 connection to FI candidates. Graph components are not independent loci.

A subsequent [numerical tail stress test](../tail_stress/RESULTS.md) found substantial per-marker excess rejection at the screening threshold under specific fixed-effect one-trait-only Gaussian models. Its assumptions are not known to match the real GWAS, so it does not quantify the actual false-discovery rate.

A [post-selected cross-source follow-up](../cross_source_followup/RESULTS.md) matched 80 of 82 diagnostic SNPs in MVP insomnia and FinnGen HFRS; none passed a conservative 82-row numerical max-p benchmark. This evaluates different phenotypes and is not a direct FI/FFS replication or PLACO calibration test.
