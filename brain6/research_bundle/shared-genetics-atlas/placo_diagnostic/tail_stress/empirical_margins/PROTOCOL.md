# Marginal Z-tail descriptive model check

Before examining counts, stream the three existing allele-aligned 1,138,262-marker HapMap3 support files and check their recorded SHA-256 hashes, SNP and allele-order equality, uniqueness and squared-Z cap. For each insomnia–FI and insomnia–FFS pair, use the same parameter-estimation subset that excludes SNPs with both |Z|-derived p-values below 10⁻⁴. For each trait marginal separately, record the mean, variance, and counts exceeding absolute Z thresholds 3, 4, 5 and 6. Compare these counts with the expectation under a centered normal distribution with the pair's previously fitted marginal variance; record expected counts even where less than one. Verify calculations using a second pass or independently aggregated totals, and preserve exact input hashes and subset counts.

This is a deliberately simple **descriptive** normal-reference comparison, not an estimate of PLACO+ type-I error: the empirical subset may include true single- and two-trait signals, the fitted variance is estimated from these same SNPs, the doubly significant exclusion alters the distribution, and LD makes counts dependent. The PLACO+ method specifies a mixture of sub-null distributions and its VarZ parameter is not a claim that the entire selected GWAS marginal is normally distributed. Rounded Z scores, sample overlap, heterogeneous per-variant N, source model and allele uncertainty further limit interpretation. Do not use deviations to label candidates false positives, correct p-values, or claim evidence that PLACO+ is invalid. Neither apparent fit nor lack of fit validates or rescues local correlation results.
