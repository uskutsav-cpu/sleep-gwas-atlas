# Product-statistic fixed-effect tail stress

This post-hoc sensitivity asks how often the pinned PLACO+ rule would cross its numerical cutoff under an explicit one-trait-only, fixed-noncentrality Gaussian data-generating model. It is not a model of empirical GWAS architecture or a formal correction. For each FI/FFS frozen VarZ and CorZ parameter set, find the absolute product threshold at which the original PLACO+ p equals 0.05 and the two-pair Bonferroni cutoff 0.05/2,276,524. For each threshold, compute the probability P(|Z1 Z2| ≥ threshold, |Z1|≤sqrt(80), |Z2|≤sqrt(80)) when (Z1,Z2) have unit variances, correlation equal to the fitted CorZ, mean (μ,0), and μ=0,1,2,3,4,5,6. Include the exact scan exclusion in this probability. Integrate the conditional normal tail over Z1 on the finite permitted interval, using stable upper-tail calculations and two disjoint sign intervals; check symmetry when the nonzero mean is on the other trait and negative μ. Verify the nominal μ=2 result against the existing 1,000-draw fixed-mean simulation with a Monte Carlo tolerance and check quadrature accuracy by tighter integration and a separate reparameterization. Record the product cutoff, probabilities, integration errors, and values relative to the stated threshold. Estimated probabilities are for one null marker with parameters treated as fixed; they are not real-data per-marker p-values, genome-wide family-wise error rates or a discovery re-ranking. LD, covariance estimation, variable GWAS sample sizes and unknown true noncentralities are not modeled. Do not infer a calibration bound from this finite grid.
