# PLACO+ input-statistic sensitivity on the fixed control sample

Written before running the full control-sample analysis; a 30-marker-per-pair smoke test had already been used to check execution. Use the existing 2,191-marker BLAKE2b-selected sample and its audited source matches. For each of FI and FFS, calculate three PLACO+ values with identical full-support fitted nuisance parameters: (1) original three-decimal p-derived Z, (2) signed Z reconstructed from the original reported p, and (3) aligned beta/SE. Use the exact numerical routine and zero/extreme gates from the full screen. Report failures, absolute log10-p changes, the fraction of threshold changes at the descriptive 0.05 level, and actual ranges, not significance or empirical genome-wide calibration. The p-derived and Wald statistics may represent different source models, especially FFS BOLT-LMM; their differences are sensitivity observations rather than evidence that one is correct. Fixed nuisance parameters are held constant across constructions and are not re-estimated for the Wald version; results are not a fully calibrated Wald rerun. The sample is selected independently of p but is not known null. Sample size is insufficient for discovery-level calibration. Do not alter or interrupt the running full screen.
