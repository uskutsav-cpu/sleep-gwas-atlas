# PLACO diagnostic candidates: MVP insomnia and hospital-frailty follow-up

This post-selected comparison examines the 82 numerical threshold-crossing SNP–pair rows from the existing FI/FFS PLACO+ diagnostic in a different pair of phenotypes: MVP clinical insomnia and FinnGen hospital frailty (HFRS). It is **not direct replication of FI or FFS**, not a test of shared causal variants, and not confirmation of PLACO calibration. Sample independence from the original studies has not been verified. The [protocol](PROTOCOL.md) was recorded before inspecting follow-up results.

Of the 82 rows, **80** had one eligible exact rsID, lifted position and non-palindromic allele-pair match in each source. Two, rs4703307 and rs40465, were not uniquely matched in the MVP file and are unavailable, not negative results. Among the 80 eligible rows, **none** has max(p_MVP, p_HFRS) × 82 below 0.05. The smallest value is **0.368** at FI diagnostic candidate rs12705982 (MVP p=0.0001836; HFRS p=0.00448425). The multiplication uses all 82 original rows, including unavailable rows, as a descriptive benchmark; it is not an adjusted discovery claim. Fifty-six eligible rows have uncorrected max-p below 0.05; because the variants were selected and many are in LD, these are not 56 independent signals. Of the 80, 79 have the same sign in the two follow-up sources after allele alignment; effects and phenotypes differ and this count is not a formal directional-concordance test.

The absence of corrected crossings does not rule out shared biology, especially given differences in frailty definition, power, ancestry and variant coverage. HFRS is a hospital-code risk score, not an FI or Fried physical score. Candidate selection and earlier rounds of follow-up mean the numerical 82-test multiplication does not guarantee error control conditional on the selection. P-values are marginal, and max-p requires valid source tests; neither same-sign effects nor a marginal conjunction can identify a common causal variant. The PLACO diagnostic's one-trait-only tail stress and local QC failures remain unresolved.

[Full rows](followup.tsv) report source p-values, aligned effects and frequencies, match counts and exclusions; [code](analyze.py), [chain](../../PUBLICATION.md#external-dependencies) (external target: `hg19ToHg38.over.chain.gz`), and [receipt](receipt.json) document the computation. The input files were checked against previously recorded digests: HFRS MD5 and compressed MVP SHA-256. The supplied liftOver chain is from [UCSC](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg19/liftOver/hg19ToHg38.over.chain.gz), with its SHA-256 in the receipt. The exact two-allele source matches do not establish that a variant has no additional alleles in other references. Both source frequency estimates use the harmonized candidate allele; all matched pairs differ by less than 0.15 in allele frequency, which is a descriptive check, not evidence of ancestry matching. MVP odds ratios are rounded; aligned log-odds signs are descriptive.
