# HDL-L selected-region feasibility and sensitivity

Written before inspecting HDL-L output. The published HDL-L UKB reference block `chr3.44` has BIM positions 47,598,663–50,380,661 (GRCh37), broadly overlapping the previously inspected LAVA locus 464; windows and estimands are not identical. Obtain its BIM and LD eigenvalue members from the CC BY 4.0 [author archive](https://zenodo.org/records/14825987), verify the entire small BIM archive's published MD5 and each selected ZIP-member CRC, and record SHA-256 digests; do not download the 1.83-GB LD archive in full. Pin the public HDL.L source to Git commit `cadb27c9aaa0e64a06a480cf2b0390a38f4f1d85` and record its digest. Load R dependencies on the SSD; do not allow the function to delete any existing file (`output.file=''`).

Use the saved FI and original insomnia chromosome 3 summary inputs with effect alleles and signed Z, the published reference defaults and the single selected block. Before interpreting any run report its variant and allele coverage, sample sizes, reference build, overlap assumptions and all fitting errors. Run three explicitly hypothetical overlap settings N0=0, 50,000 and 100,000 solely as a sensitivity; these are not estimates of shared participants and the LDSC intercept must not be converted directly into N0. Record all outputs, warnings, boundary estimates and failures. Neither selected-block p-values nor confidence intervals are adjusted for the genome-wide family or earlier selection. No result will override the failed original LAVA gate or be declared replicated or causal.

HDL-L and LAVA have different random- versus fixed-effect targets and different LD partitions. The [HDL-L paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02123-3) reports performance under its simulations; a [subsequent critique](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202507.0966) disputes aspects of the comparison and reports sensitivity of both methods to small reference panels. Neither alone validates the selected analysis. A full application requires sample-overlap information, appropriate LD, calibrated null controls, systematic family-wide correction and independent validation.

After the first three settings returned, a follow-up check was specified before its execution: reverse FI and insomnia at N0=50,000 and compare the correlation and p-value, and the corresponding univariate fields, as a software symmetry diagnostic. A match would not validate model assumptions or sample overlap; a mismatch would be retained and investigated.
