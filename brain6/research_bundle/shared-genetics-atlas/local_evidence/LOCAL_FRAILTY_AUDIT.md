# Published and project-local insomnia–frailty evidence

## Published 3p21.31 region

[Song et al. 2024](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2024.1358996/full) reported a ρ-HESS local signal at chromosome 3p21.31. Its Supplementary Table S2 contains a single reported row: **chr3:47,727,212–49,316,972**, 967 SNPs, `local_rhog` **0.00028557** (SE 0.0000645), p **9.45×10⁻⁶**. The main-text endpoint is garbled; the supplement supplies the integer 49,316,972. We extracted the row from the downloaded supplement and recorded its hash. A two-sided normal calculation using the displayed rounded estimate and SE gives z≈4.427 and p≈9.54×10⁻⁶, consistent with the printed p after rounding; this checks arithmetic, not the original analysis or its assumptions. The article describes a Bonferroni correction for the number of regions, but the inspected row alone does not state the family size or adjusted p. Do not use this as an already adjusted p-value. The exact coordinate build of the authors' table remains unverified here.

**Terminology:** the column is named `local_rhog`. The [ρ-HESS documentation](https://huwenboshi.github.io/hess/local_rhog/) describes its similarly named primary output as local genetic **covariance**; a normalized local genetic correlation additionally requires both local SNP-heritability estimates. Song’s text calls the signal a local genetic correlation, but the supplement does not supply those local heritabilities or identify a post-processing normalization. Therefore treat the printed quantity as `local_rhog` as reported, consistent with covariance, and do not compare 0.00028557 numerically to a dimensionless genome-wide r_g. The exact paper pipeline would be needed to resolve this definitively.

Song et al. used the Atkins UKB/TwinGene FI and UKB insomnia summary data, so this publication shares the FI source with the project and potentially participants with the project's UKB insomnia source. The comparison is not independent replication of the region. Their global LDSC estimates were 1.14 with an unconstrained intercept and 0.61 with constrained intercepts. A point estimate above 1 is outside the feasible range of a true correlation and signals instability; the difference in intercept choices and cohort overlap require investigation, not selective reporting of the preferred number. The precise ρ-HESS sample-overlap correction and settings were not established from the cited methods passage. Rho-HESS can require overlap and phenotypic-correlation inputs; see its documentation.

Song also reported rs34290943 and rs10865954 in this region. Our project's separately assembled MVP-insomnia/FinnGen-HFRS lookup gives, respectively, max(two marginal p-values) 0.00401 and 0.3328; the former becomes 0.497 after multiplication by the original 124-candidate family size, and the latter is 1 after capping. These are post-selected comparisons of different insomnia and frailty definitions, not tests of local correlation; neither satisfies that exploratory two-trait threshold. The reported allele/build and cohort-overlap caveats in `../evidence/song124_mvp_hfrs_followup.md` apply. A weak result does not prove absence of regional sharing, and a different linked causal variant could produce a regional signal.

## Project local analysis

The archived FI–sleep LAVA campaign completed all 29,940 slots. Its post-remount audit validated receipts but reported 5,125 negative-variance process failures, 252 no-reference-SNP failures and seven other process failures. All 12 frozen 1% trait-level failure gates failed. Valid receipt accounting is not proof of valid scientific inference. The archived negative-variance diagnostic and source/reference checks did not establish a corrected cause or calibrate a replacement family, and no local result was promoted. The [project's new-local eligibility record](../evidence/NEW_LOCAL_ANALYSIS_PROTOCOL.md) remains `NOT_ADMITTED`.

Brain6's same-source SUPERGNOVA sensitivity does not validate frailty regions: it analyzed eligible Brain6 pairs and was not an independent cohort. Running a new local method on the frailty pair requires source-specific signed dense statistics, an appropriate ancestry-matched LD panel, defensible handling of sample overlap, frozen regions and family, local heritability and numerical gates, independent statistical review, and a calibration pilot. A nominal local result from a replacement method must not be retroactively used to pass the failed LAVA family. The PLACO+ screen tests variants on HapMap3 support; it is not a local genetic-correlation analysis.

A [common-marker eligibility check](common_marker_eligibility/RESULTS.md) finds only 586 rsIDs shared between the new 991,707-marker MVP input and the 2,741 markers in the selected failed local replay. It is a name-only intersection, not allele-validated local support or a rescue of the failed quality gates.

A [partition and gate audit](partition_and_gate_audit/RESULTS.md) matches the published interval to one of 1,703 standard European LD blocks and calculates an illustrative correction conditional on that exact family. The actual paper family and overlap correction remain unverified. It also reconciles the saved insomnia LAVA failure categories without treating untested loci as nulls.
