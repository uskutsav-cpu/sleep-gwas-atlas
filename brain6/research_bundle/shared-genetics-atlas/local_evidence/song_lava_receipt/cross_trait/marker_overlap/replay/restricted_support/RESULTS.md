# Fixed-input marker-support sensitivity for locus 464

We replayed FI–insomnia after restricting its eligible markers to the 2,452 SNPs used by the sleep-apnea–FI replay. The insomnia and FI summary-statistic values, FI sample size, insomnia overlap input, locus, reference, software and other processing settings were retained. The restricted calculation used 185 LD components and yielded a positive FI genetic-variance diagonal of **4.8878×10⁻⁷**, identical to the saved sleep-apnea replay at displayed precision. Its FI local estimate before capping was **9.0424×10⁻⁵**; FI was not dropped in this diagnostic. The original, wider 2,741-marker insomnia run had 216 components and a negative FI diagonal of −4.1914×10⁻⁹.

This controlled replay shows that changing support to this specific nested marker set and recomputing the LD decomposition is **sufficient to reproduce the observed FI variance difference under these fixed inputs**. It does not identify an individual responsible marker or establish the biological or statistical cause of the original negative estimate. The reduced support was chosen after inspecting the results, and it is not a validated repair: fewer markers and a positive estimate are not evidence of better calibration. The original locus failure and all 12 family QC failures remain unchanged. Neither a bivariate local correlation nor a scientific association claim was produced.

The [pre-run protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [R code](run.R), [output](restricted.json) and [independent comparison](verify.py) retain the test and check the exact SNP set, FI input sample size, projected-statistic sum and variance terms against the previous replay. [Verification](verification.json) hashes the small outputs; the [parent replay](../RESULTS.md) records the pinned runtime, reference hash and historical comparisons. The sleep-apnea and restricted insomnia FI diagonal and projected-signal sum agree within the check tolerance, but the broader code, reference provenance and calibration have not been independently validated.

A [20-subset random-support diagnostic](../random_support/RESULTS.md) found one negative and 19 positive FI variance estimates with the same marker count. The apnea-derived support had fewer LD components and a larger FI estimate than the 20 random masks; this is not a calibrated significance test or a reason to select that support.
