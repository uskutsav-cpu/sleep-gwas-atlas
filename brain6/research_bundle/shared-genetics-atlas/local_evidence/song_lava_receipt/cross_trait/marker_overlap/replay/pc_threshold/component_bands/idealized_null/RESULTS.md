# An idealized check of the failed FI variance

The saved FI estimate at locus 464 is negative but extremely close to its failure boundary. Its 216 projected squared values average **99.9266%** of the estimated sampling term; holding the projections fixed, a hypothetical **0.0734% reduction** in that term would make the variance exactly zero. This is an arithmetic illustration, **not a proposed correction**: the term is estimated from the data, and its correct value is unknown.

To understand the scale of the component-band observation, we compared the saved sums with a deliberately simplified model in which projections are independent zero-mean Gaussians and the estimated sampling term is treated as fixed and known. Under those assumptions, the 216-component standardized sum is 215.84 on 216 degrees of freedom, with a lower-tail probability of 0.510. The post-selected 74-component band added between 98% and 99% retention has a standardized sum of 37.89 on 74 degrees of freedom, with a lower-tail probability of 0.000155.

**Those are toy-model probabilities, not calibrated p-values for the real data.** The band, region and retention thresholds were selected after inspecting results; the sampling term is estimated, the local signal may be nonzero, and the reference LD approximation, ancestry or sample structure may change the distribution and dependence of projections. Consequently this calculation does not distinguish random variation from reference or input mismatch and does not prove an error. In particular, a near-boundary estimate is not evidence that an arbitrary change to the sampling term is justified. The full locus reference also fails exact correlation-matrix properties in the [prior audit](../../../reference_comparison/RESULTS.md).

No bivariate local correlation was estimated and the original quality gates remain failed.
