# Idealized component-noise diagnostic

Before calculating the reference numbers, use the previously saved 216-component FI replay and its 143–216 component band. Assume hypothetically that each standardized projection is independent standard Gaussian, with the saved FI sampling term treated as fixed and known, and all true local effects are zero. Under that deliberately simplified model, total squared projections divided by the sampling term follow a chi-square distribution with degrees of freedom equal to the number of projections. Calculate the 216-component and selected 74-component lower-tail probabilities and the fractional sampling-term reduction that would move the observed 216-component variance to zero, without modifying or rerunning LAVA.

These model comparisons are post-selected descriptive diagnostics, not calibrated p-values for the LAVA inputs: the sampling term is estimated, LD is approximate, effects need not be zero, and reference and study LD or sample structure can differ. The selection of the locus, band and thresholds was data-dependent. Do not interpret a small tail as proof of LD mismatch, justify discarding a component band, or use it to rescue the failed family gates. Independently verify the arithmetic and hash the stored inputs.
