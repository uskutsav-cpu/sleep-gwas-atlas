# Chromosome 5 HFRS, insomnia and ADHD: exploratory common-signal pilot

Written before extracting the HFRS window or inspecting these results. Use the previously examined Brain6 chr5:103447968–104447968 GRCh37 region and its frozen questionnaire-insomnia and ADHD summary inputs. Stream the public UKB HFRS source to completion and verify its publisher MD5; retain only regional rows. Match unique rsID, exact position and non-palindromic allele pairs across all three sources, keeping HFRS A1 as the common effect allele. Require finite betas, positive SE, HFRS MAF≥0.01 and INFO≥0.9, and at least 500 shared variants. The two Brain6 files do not include allele frequencies, so their frequency compatibility with HFRS cannot be checked; report all unmatched rows and source hashes. The HFRS file's 407,643 versus published 407,463 sample-count discrepancy remains unresolved.

On the same support calculate pairwise single-signal coloc H0–H4 for insomnia–ADHD, HFRS–insomnia and HFRS–ADHD, with p1=p2=10⁻⁴, p12 in {10⁻⁵,10⁻⁶}, HFRS raw-effect prior SD in {0.05,0.15,0.30,0.60,1.50}, and sleep/ADHD log-odds prior SD 0.2. The HFRS scale and appropriate prior are unverified, so these are illustrative, not selected primary estimates. Independently verify pairwise posterior arithmetic. The original Brain6 region's reported high PP4 used its original support; a common-support sensitivity is not new replication or confirmation of that earlier fit.

Separately fit the installed HyPrColoc v0.0.2 single-signal model for all three traits with p1=10⁻⁴, conditional prior 0.02, regional/alignment thresholds 0.5 and default identity LD. Probe HFRS–insomnia working residual correlations 0, ±0.001, +0.05, +0.1 and +0.3, with other working correlations zero, `sample.overlap` matrix of ones, and the independent path at zero. These are hypothetical effective correlations, not observed overlap or corrections. Record every cluster, warning, error and unreturned fit, and verify near-zero continuity when possible. The model's conditional prior and probabilities are not directly equivalent to the pairwise coloc priors.

The region was selected after many prior analyses, sample overlap and relatedness across all three cohorts are not verified, UKB HFRS and questionnaire insomnia share the UKB cohort, HFRS scale is uncertain, SNP coverage is selected, and LD and multiple causal signals are not modeled. Posteriors are model-conditional, not multiplicity-corrected p-values, and no output establishes a causal variant or gene, independent replication, a sleep/exhaustion-excluded frailty score, clinical prediction, or rescue of failed LAVA QC. Do not use AlphaGenome outputs in model fitting or delete data.
