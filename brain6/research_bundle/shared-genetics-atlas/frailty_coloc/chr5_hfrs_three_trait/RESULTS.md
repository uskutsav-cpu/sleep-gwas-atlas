# Chromosome 5: UKB hospital frailty, insomnia and ADHD

On **2,359 common variants** in the previously selected chromosome 5 region, the original insomnia–ADHD single-signal colocalization remains strong under the two tested shared-variant priors. The three-trait HyPrColoc pilot returned an **insomnia–ADHD cluster without HFRS** in every tested hypothetical correlation setting. These are model-dependent results; they do not establish that HFRS has no shared signal, a causal variant, or independent three-trait replication.

| Pairwise model | H4 at p12=10⁻⁵ | H4 at p12=10⁻⁶ | Other important posterior |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Insomnia–ADHD | 0.9911 | 0.9174 | H3=0.0089 / 0.0826 |
| HFRS–insomnia | 0.3318 | 0.0473 | Insomnia-only H2=0.5208 / 0.7426 |
| HFRS–ADHD | 0.3061 | 0.0422 | ADHD-only H2=0.5413 / 0.7471 |

HFRS rows use an illustrative raw-effect prior SD of 0.15, with log-odds prior SD 0.2 for the two disease traits and p1=p2=10⁻⁴. The HFRS phenotype scale and appropriate prior are unverified: varying its SD from 0.05 to 1.50 changes HFRS–insomnia H4 from 0.447 to 0.062 at p12=10⁻⁵, and from 0.0748 to 0.00652 at p12=10⁻⁶. The HFRS–ADHD result shows a similar prior dependence. The minimum matched HFRS p was 7.21×10⁻⁵, compared with ≈1.97×10⁻⁹ for insomnia and ≈2.47×10⁻¹³ for ADHD. H2 is the disease-trait-only hypothesis in these HFRS pairings; it is not evidence that an HFRS effect is absent. The insomnia–ADHD estimates reproduce the earlier Brain6 reported values on this support, not an independent cohort result.

The separate HyPrColoc model returned an insomnia–ADHD cluster with posterior **0.9780** and candidate rs77960 under all six tested HFRS–insomnia working correlations (−0.001, 0, +0.001, +0.05, +0.1, +0.3). **HFRS was not in the returned cluster.** The same posterior across settings is expected when the selected cluster excludes HFRS and the other working correlations are fixed at zero; it is not evidence that true cohort overlap has no effect. The actual residual correlations are unknown. This method uses a different conditional prior and output from pairwise coloc, so its cluster posterior should not be equated with H4. A non-returned HFRS cluster does not rule out a shared secondary signal or an effect below available power.

The source window was selected after prior analyses. HFRS and questionnaire insomnia both use UK Biobank; exact overlap and relatedness across all traits are unverified, while the independence baseline assumes independent association statistics. The correlation stress values are hypothetical and the model uses identity LD and at most one causal variant per trait. Different sources, prior choices and incomplete support can affect these estimates. They are not multiple-testing-corrected discoveries, gene assignments, AlphaGenome validation, a sleep/exhaustion-excluded frailty result, or a repair of the **failed LAVA quality gates**.

## Inputs and verification

The complete public UKB HFRS gzip passed its published MD5 and was streamed without retaining the full file. Of 3,787 regional HFRS rows, 2,714 passed the stated allele, frequency and INFO filters; 263 lacked a unique insomnia match and a further 92 lacked a unique ADHD match, leaving 2,359. The two Brain6 inputs have no allele frequencies, so source frequency alignment cannot be verified. HFRS rows report N=407,643 while the publication describes 407,463 participants; the discrepancy is unresolved. These checks support file matching, not upstream GWAS calibration or correct priors.

A separate Python implementation reproduced all 22 pairwise posterior vectors within 2.9×10⁻¹⁵. Six HyPrColoc runs returned without recorded warnings, and the near-zero correlated settings matched baseline cluster membership and displayed posterior. These checks verify arithmetic and software continuity, not biological validity or overlap correction.
