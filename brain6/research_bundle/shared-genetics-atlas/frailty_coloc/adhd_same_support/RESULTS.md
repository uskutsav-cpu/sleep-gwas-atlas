# Chromosome 3 FI–ADHD and insomnia: common-support sensitivity

In the previously selected chromosome 3 region, a single-signal model on 1,496 common variants was **uninformative about whether FI shares a causal signal with ADHD**. The ADHD-only hypothesis dominates; the data and model provide little support for an FI signal on this particular support. This is not evidence that sharing is absent.

| FI paired with | H0: neither | H1: FI only | H2: other trait only | H3: distinct | H4: shared | H4 with p12=10⁻⁶ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ADHD (Brain6 source) | 0.0178 | 0.00062 | 0.9152 | 0.0316 | 0.0348 | 0.00359 |
| FinnGen R13 insomnia | 0.8891 | 0.0308 | 0.0760 | 0.00263 | 0.00147 | 0.000147 |
| Original Brain6 insomnia | 0.00274 | 0.000095 | 0.9302 | 0.0322 | 0.0347 | 0.00359 |

Columns H0–H4 use p1=p2=10⁻⁴, p12=10⁻⁵; the last column changes p12 only. FI is trait 1, and all three pairs use exactly the same 1,496 variants. FI's minimum reconstructed Wald p on that support is 2.42×10⁻⁵; ADHD, FinnGen insomnia and original insomnia have respective minimum reconstructed Wald p-values of 2.38×10⁻⁷, 3.03×10⁻⁴ and 2.13×10⁻⁸. These minima are selected from correlated markers, not corrected tests. FI effects and SEs are rounded, and the corresponding reconstructed p-values need not equal the original source p-values. On all 2,385 FI–FinnGen matched variants in the prior chr3 analysis, FI's minimum supplied p is 2.15×10⁻⁵. The difference between insomnia models cannot be attributed to a particular cause: phenotype definition, cohort, sample size, models and ancestry differ.

The 1,496 markers are the intersection of the prior 2,385-variant FI–FinnGen set and 1,548-variant pilot support, matched on rsID, GRCh37 coordinate and allele pair. The pilot support was itself filtered through selected GTEx and reference variants; it is not complete regional GWAS support. Both FI and insomnia source sets have already undergone their own filters. The shared support makes the three comparisons more comparable, but cannot recover missing causal variants. The ADHD and original-insomnia sources are related to the earlier Brain6 analysis, and none of these pairwise analyses is a three-trait colocalization or independent replication. FI includes sleep-related items; participant overlap and population and phenotype differences remain concerns. The selected region and comparisons are post hoc; priors and the single-signal assumption matter, and multiple signals may be present. Prior LD–Z outliers in the original pilot further prevent interpreting its multi-signal fits as validated. This analysis does not rescue failed LAVA QC or assign a gene or mechanism.

[Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [aggregate posterior results](posteriors.tsv), [R implementation](run.R), and [independent arithmetic and input-hash verification](verification.json) are included. The verification reproduces the posterior calculations to 3.4×10⁻¹⁶, but does not validate the statistical assumptions. The code reads existing local source-derived files; the PGC ADHD release's noncommercial and redistribution restrictions still apply. No AlphaGenome output was used in these calculations.
