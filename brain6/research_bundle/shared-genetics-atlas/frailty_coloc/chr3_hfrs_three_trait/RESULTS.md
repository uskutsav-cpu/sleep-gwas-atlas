# Chromosome 3: HFRS, insomnia and ADHD near rs62264764

On **2,340 common variants** in the previously selected rs62264764 region, a single-signal model gives prior-sensitive shared-signal support for the three pairwise trait comparisons. A separate three-trait model returns a cluster under its independence assumption, but that cluster **changes to insomnia–ADHD when a modest positive hypothetical HFRS–insomnia correlation is introduced**. The actual correlation is unknown. These results do not establish an independent three-trait causal variant or an effector gene.

| Pairwise model | H4 at p12=10⁻⁵ | H4 at p12=10⁻⁶ | H3: distinct signals, respectively |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Insomnia–ADHD | 0.9207 | 0.5373 | 0.0779 / 0.4546 |
| HFRS–insomnia | 0.8271 | 0.3236 | 0.1101 / 0.4307 |
| HFRS–ADHD | 0.7738 | 0.2549 | 0.1425 / 0.4693 |

The HFRS rows use an **illustrative**, unverified raw-effect prior SD of 0.15; insomnia and ADHD use log-odds prior SD 0.2, with p1=p2=10⁻⁴. At p12=10⁻⁵, varying the HFRS prior SD from 0.05 to 1.50 changes HFRS–insomnia H4 from 0.819 to 0.567 and HFRS–ADHD H4 from 0.767 to 0.483. Lowering p12 reduces H4 further. These probabilities are conditional on a one-signal model and selected variants, not corrected discoveries or evidence that the priors are calibrated. Minimum matched p-values were 8.62×10⁻⁵ for HFRS, ≈2.13×10⁻⁸ for insomnia and ≈2.38×10⁻⁷ for ADHD. HFRS p-values at rs62264764 and rs62264767 were 0.000127 and 0.000116, respectively. The insomnia–ADHD H4 values reproduce the earlier selected-region result on this support, not an independent cohort result.

In the separate HyPrColoc analysis, the independence path returned **HFRS, insomnia and ADHD** with model posterior **0.5587** and candidate **rs62264767**; the fraction attributed to that candidate was 0.1530 conditional on the cluster. At hypothetical HFRS–insomnia effective correlations of +0.05, +0.1 and +0.3, the returned cluster contained **insomnia and ADHD only**. Settings −0.001 and +0.001 approach the independence result. The working correlations are not measurements of cohort overlap; the package's conditional prior differs from the pairwise coloc p12 priors. Identical insomnia–ADHD output at positive settings is expected after HFRS is dropped and the other working correlations are fixed at zero. It does not prove that real sample dependence is harmless or that HFRS lacks a secondary shared signal.

rs62264767 is **not** rs62264764. They had r²=1.0 in a [503-person European reference](../../replication/rs62264764_mvp/RESULTS.md), which is not study-matched LD or proof of an identical causal allele. The three-trait candidate fraction is not an unconditional variant-causality probability. Neither model validates the AlphaGenome prediction, establishes LSAMP or LINC03051 as a target, or supersedes the earlier [childhood ADHD-symptom follow-up](../../replication/childhood_symptoms/RESULTS.md), where rs62264764 p=0.437. No independent sleep–ADHD–frailty replication or failed LAVA quality gate is established.

## Inputs, checks and limits

The complete public UKB HFRS gzip passed its publisher MD5 and was streamed without retaining the full archive. Of 3,641 regional rows, 2,634 passed the specified HFRS filters; 175 lacked a unique insomnia match, 118 more lacked a unique ADHD match, and one HFRS key was duplicated, leaving 2,340. These are sequential exclusions. The two Brain6 inputs lack allele frequencies, so cross-source frequency alignment is unverified. HFRS rows report N=407,643, whereas the publication describes 407,463; the reason remains unknown. Both HFRS and questionnaire insomnia use UK Biobank, and exact overlap and relatedness across all traits are not established.

The selected locus, unknown residual correlation, unverified HFRS effect scale, identity-LD approximation in HyPrColoc and one-causal-variant assumption limit inference. A separate Python calculation reproduced all 22 pairwise posterior vectors within 2.0×10⁻¹⁵. All six three-trait fits returned without recorded warnings, and near-zero settings produced the same cluster with close posteriors. These checks verify arithmetic and software continuity, not calibration or causality.
