# Exploratory frailty–insomnia colocalization protocol

These two regions were selected after inspecting prior insomnia–ADHD and frailty evidence; this is a post-selection, hypothesis-generating analysis, not an independent validation or a correction of the failed LAVA analysis. The FI meta-analysis includes sleep-related items. Cohort overlap, ancestry differences and multiple independent signals may affect results.

Use dense FI summary statistics on GRCh37 and FinnGen R13 insomnia summary statistics on GRCh38. Examine fixed 1-Mb GRCh37 intervals: chromosome 3, 117142005–118142005; chromosome 7, 113571035–114571035, centered on previously examined frailty markers. Lift FinnGen positions with the documented hg38-to-hg19 chain. Retain unambiguous, unique rsIDs at the same lifted chromosome and position, with single-base nonpalindromic A/C/G/T alleles in direct or reversed order. Require finite betas, strictly positive SEs, allele frequencies between 0.01 and 0.99 and absolute aligned frequency difference no greater than 0.15. Exclude duplicates and report counts. Align FI A1 effects with FinnGen ALT effects. Assess coverage of eligible FI variants and exclude a region with fewer than 500 matched variants from inference. Missing variants and differing variant density remain potential biases.

Compute standard single-signal Wakefield approximate Bayes factor posteriors using beta and squared SE, with FI as a standardized quantitative trait (effect prior SD 0.15) and insomnia as case–control (log-odds prior SD 0.2). Use p1=p2=1e-4 and test p12=1e-5 and 1e-6. Report H0–H4, the conditional H4 top variants and the 95% conditional credible set; H4 is conditional on the model and priors, not a causal probability proven by experiment. Use the independently obtained coloc 5.2.3 reference implementation directly for the default approximate Bayes factors and hypothesis combination; report any modifications for sensitivity analysis. Assess alternate effect-prior SDs 0.1 and 0.3 for both traits. No clinical conclusions, multi-signal resolution, or genome-wide claims follow. Source: https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/reference/approx.bf.estimates.html and https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/reference/coloc.abf.html.
