# FOXP2 region: post-selected FI–insomnia–ADHD common-support pilot

Written before the comparison: the original Brain6 insomnia–ADHD region and the FI–FinnGen insomnia region share GRCh37 chr7:113571035–114571035. Start from the previously allele-aligned FI–FinnGen insomnia data, then match each of the two frozen Brain6 source files by unique rsID, chromosome 7, position and unordered nonpalindromic biallelic allele pair. Exclude duplicates, missing/mismatched keys and nonfinite/invalid beta and SE; align both Brain6 A1 effects to the FI effect allele. Require the frozen Brain6 source SHA-256 from the AlphaGenome provenance and record all source hashes and match counts. The source GWAS files do not have allele frequency, so no independent FI–Brain6 frequency concordance is possible. The initial FI set was itself selected by FinnGen insomnia availability and QC; the resulting support is not all variants in the region. Keep source-derived variant-level analysis inputs on the external SSD and do not distribute PGC ADHD summary data. Require at least 500 common variants.

On identical support, run HyPrColoc 0.0.2 at pinned commit 0348bbdd977be731d82e4efda115a9bed63cd44f using the documented compiler patch; FI is a quantitative trait and both Brain6 GWAS are binary. Explicitly assume independent study statistics despite unknown or overlapping samples, with default branch-and-bound, prior.1=1e-4, prior.c=0.02 and thresholds 0.5; repeat prior.c=0.01 and 0.005 and thresholds 0.9 at prior.c=0.02. Use the documented package example as a smoke test. For interpretive context also run pairwise single-signal ABF coloc on the same support for FI–insomnia, FI–ADHD and insomnia–ADHD, with case–control effect-prior SD 0.20 and FI SD 0.15, p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-5 or 1e-6. Preserve all results and do not multiply pairwise posteriors or claim joint replication from them.

These are exploratory and post-selected models. FI includes insomnia/tiredness, study overlap and ancestry differ, the FI meta-analysis involves uncertain TwinGene effect-allele interpretation, FI betas/SE are rounded, the source GWAS lack frequencies, and the window and available support can miss relevant variants. HyPrColoc and ABF assume at most one causal variant per trait, and the former's independence approximation is unverified; no study-matched LD or multi-signal model is used. Nearby region annotations do not prove FOXP2 is an effector gene. Output must not be called independent replication, a calibrated genetic-local-correlation result, an item-excluded frailty analysis, clinical evidence, or a rescue of failed LAVA QC. AlphaGenome outputs do not enter these models. Low posterior or no returned cluster does not exclude a secondary shared signal.

Post-hoc after seeing the cluster leader: query phased 1000 Genomes Phase 3 EUR reference r² between rs12671330 and the prior FOXP2 Brain6/frailty anchors rs1476535 and rs12536335, plus the prior FI–FinnGen insomnia conditional-H4 leaders rs10953765 and rs1456031. Require exact GRCh37 rsID, position and allele-pair matches, include pairwise-complete phased individuals, and report missing matches. This describes LD in a small reference, not study-matched LD, signal independence, causation or replication.
