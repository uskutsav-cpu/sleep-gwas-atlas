# Cross-trait frailty and psychiatric manuscript: provenance and method audit

Before extracting the deposited source, compare the 2025 Zenodo record and the university-hosted PDF for title, authors and file identity. Check publication metadata against Crossref by exact title without assuming search failure proves nonpublication. Read methods, phenotype source, FI–sleep results, candidate loci and availability of supplements. Distinguish a prior claim from an independently verified result. Review the paper's use of LDSC intercept, genotype QC and colocalization in the context of summary-statistic limitations, and compare its FI definition with the atlas. Record source hashes and extraction code. Do not classify sleep disorder as insomnia without endpoint confirmation; do not claim independent replication, a sleep-excluded FI, causality or validated PLACO calibration.
