# Marginal-association prioritization protocol

Written before calculating this screen on 27 October 2026. For the three existing aligned HapMap3 sources (FinnGen R13 insomnia, Atkins FI and Ye FFS), test each of 1,138,262 SNPs against each of three traits, correcting the full family of 3,414,786 SNP–trait tests using Bonferroni (0.05/m). Use the conservative upper bound on p-values computed from three-decimal Z with abs(Z) reduced by 0.0005 and floored at zero. Require complete ordered SNP and allele matches and no duplicate SNPs. Save all SNPs with at least one passing marginal p, reporting all three marginal p upper bounds and signs, and classify the pattern of threshold crossings. Separately join the FI source's GRCh37 positions by unique rsID; unresolved or duplicate mappings stay missing. Record input hashes.

This is a post-hoc common-HapMap3 screen, not dense GWAS discovery. A category in which only one trait passes is called 'one-trait threshold crossing', not a trait-specific genetic effect: failure to cross a threshold may reflect power and sample differences and is not evidence of absence. Markers in linkage disequilibrium are correlated, so marker counts do not count independent loci. No gene, causal variant, direction of causation, or independent replication is established. No HFRS data enter this screen; source rounding, cohort overlap, phenotype overlap and association-model calibration remain relevant.
