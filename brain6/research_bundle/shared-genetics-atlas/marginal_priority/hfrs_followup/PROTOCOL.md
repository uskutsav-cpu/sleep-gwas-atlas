# HFRS follow-up of selected marginal signals

Prepared before this follow-up lookup. Take all 368 previously identified insomnia/FI/FFS SNPs from the pre-existing three-trait common-support marginal screen. Look up unique same-rsID HFRS filtered HapMap3 records, require chromosome and unordered biallelic A/C/G/T allele match, and align the HFRS effect to the earlier counted allele only when these checks pass. Preserve missing, duplicate, conflicting and invalid matches as explicit statuses; do not replace them by null association. The prior table's positions are GRCh37 and the HFRS filtered source originated from a GRCh38 release; matching rsID and allele pairs is not by itself a complete reference-aware build or multiallelic audit.

As a descriptive comparison, classify each of the four marginal p-values at 0.05/(4×1,138,262), using the previously calculated conservative Z-rounding bounds for the first three traits and the source-reported HFRS p-value. The chosen 368 markers were selected using a less stringent three-trait threshold, so the table contains every first-three-trait crossing at the four-trait cutoff, but it does not include HFRS-only discoveries elsewhere. The four-trait threshold is a conservative scale for this lookup, not a claim that a complete four-trait screen was performed or that the displayed HFRS p-value has exact precision. No selection-adjusted independent replication is inferred from same-cohort or correlated GWAS. Record count patterns only for matched markers and avoid claims of biological specificity: nonsignificance can reflect power, phenotype differences, and uncertain precision. The 368 markers are not independent loci; perform LD and independent sample follow-up before locus claims. This is not PLACO or enrichment-based conjunction FDR.
