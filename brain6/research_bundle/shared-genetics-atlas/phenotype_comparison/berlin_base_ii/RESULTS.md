# A published within-person FI–Fried-score comparison in BASE-II

In [Vetter et al. 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-025-01233-7), Supplementary Figure 3 shows the correlation of the SPRINT-BASEed deficit-based frailty index with the numeric Fried frailty score in the Berlin Aging Study II. Reading the two plotted cells, the **phenotypic Pearson r was 0.38 at baseline** and **0.53 at follow-up**, in the first imputed dataset of 1,083 participants. The follow-up was on average 7.4 years later. The figure's caption cites r=0.53 as an example without explicitly identifying the time point; the heatmap shows that value belongs to the follow-up panel B, while panel A gives 0.38. This was verified visually, not inferred from the caption alone.

These are descriptive within-person correlations in one imputed dataset, not pooled multiple-imputation estimates, genetic correlations or a statistically tested change over time. The study's supplement reports five imputations under a missing-at-random assumption, and 6.5% missing values across the complete set of study variables; that percentage is not necessarily the missingness of either frailty score. Its deficit index and Fried score are adapted measures in an older Berlin cohort and are not the precise Atkins FI or Ye FFS instruments, samples or GWAS. Observed associations can partly reflect shared components and the cohort's composition. The reported n=1,083 is the first imputed dataset's size, not a verified complete-case count. No confidence intervals are given for these two heatmap values here.

The values complement the separate [UK Biobank categorical comparison](../PHENOTYPIC_VS_GENETIC.md), not a substitute for the exact continuous phenotypic correlation in the project's participants. The project's FI–FFS LDSC r_g=0.7534 (SE 0.0199) describes a different genetic estimand and population; neither the magnitude difference nor the later rise in BASE-II establishes genetic or causal effects. Supplementary Figure 3 is on page 17 of the [published supplement](https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3646481_7/component/file_3702012/content); `receipt.json` records the source-file hash and extraction details.
