# Agreement of published FI and Fried categories

Using the [published UK Biobank 3×3 table](https://bmcmedicine.biomedcentral.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s12916-020-01822-4) of 383,845 participants, 60.62% fall on the category diagonal when Fried robust/prefrail/frail is aligned with FI robust/mild/moderate-or-severe. Given the observed category prevalences, expected diagonal agreement from the marginal distributions is 50.35%. Unweighted Cohen κ is **0.207**; linear-weighted κ is **0.251**; quadratic-weighted κ is **0.321**. These are new calculations from the published aggregate table, not values attributed to the original authors.

The weights encode different penalties for a one- versus two-category difference. A paired-category multinomial bootstrap gave descriptive 95% percentile intervals of 0.204–0.210, 0.248–0.254 and 0.317–0.324, respectively. They assume independent sampling from the observed table and do not address participant selection, family structure, missingness or uncertainty in category definitions. The category schemes have different thresholds and meaning, so a diagonal cell is an approximate category match, not agreement against a shared clinical gold standard. Weighted κ additionally assumes the selected ordinal spacing. The κ values should not be subtracted from the separately estimated FI–FFS genetic correlation or equated with correlation of the continuous scores.

The [prior phenotypic review](../PHENOTYPIC_VS_GENETIC.md) reports the same table's ordinal Spearman rho and explains cohort differences and measurement overlap. `agreement.tsv`, `calculate.py`, `verify.R` and `receipt.json` retain the weights, bootstrap seed and source hash; an independent R calculation reproduces all three kappas.
