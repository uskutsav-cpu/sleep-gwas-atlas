# Published category agreement sensitivity

Prepared before this calculation. Reuse the published Petermann-Rocha UK Biobank 3×3 category count table and label the Fried robust/prefrail/frail categories 0–2 and the FI robust/mild/moderate-or-severe categories 0–2. Compute exact diagonal agreement, the agreement expected from observed marginal proportions, unweighted Cohen kappa, and linear- and quadratic-weighted kappas with weights `1−|i−j|/2` and `1−((i−j)/2)^2`. Add a 10,000-replicate multinomial paired-category bootstrap with fixed seed for descriptive percentile intervals, assuming independent sampling from the reported table. Report all inputs and formulae. Use category names and distributions as published; the FI and Fried thresholds are not a common diagnostic gold standard, and weights encode an arbitrary ordinal spacing. Prevalence, categorization, participant sampling, family clustering and missingness affect agreement; bootstrap intervals do not correct these concerns or recover continuous FI/FFS measures. These kappas are phenotypic category comparisons only, not genetic correlation, clinical accuracy, biological causality or independent replication of GWAS findings.
