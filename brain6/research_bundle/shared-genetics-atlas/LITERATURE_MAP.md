# Literature map for sleep, brain disease and frailty

Compiled 27 October 2026 as a replacement map after the requested proposal PDF could not be located and the user asked to disregard it. This is **not** a claim about the contents of Zunpeng's 15-source review, an exhaustive systematic review, or a guarantee of novelty. It distinguishes primary association studies, method papers and model predictions.

| # | Source | What it contributes | Key constraint for our use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Jansen et al., 2019: insomnia GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-018-0333-3) | Large discovery study, 1.33 million people across sources; reference for insomnia loci. | Brain6 uses a UKB-only public subset; don't substitute the combined sample size or treat reused UKB as independent. |
| 2 | [Demontis et al., 2023: ADHD GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10914347/) | ADHD genetic discovery source. | ADHD GWAS association is not an insomnia mechanism. |
| 3 | [Zu et al., 2026: ADHD and sleep traits](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42297780/) | Longitudinal adolescent sleep/ADHD and genetic overlap; already reports rs62264764, rs77960 and rs1476535. | Shared source cohorts mean rerunning similar data is not independent replication; coloc is model-dependent. |
| 4 | [Xue et al., 2026: insomnia and psychiatric disorders](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41065713/) | Conjunction FDR and ASSET, gene sets and 36 brain cell types across 12 disorders; GABAergic signaling and cortical neuron hypotheses. | Aggregate pathway/cell results cannot be assigned to any specific Brain6 variant without further evidence. |
| 5 | [Insomnia PRS in Brazilian ADHD families](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38846582/) | Phenotype-based study of an insomnia PRS and ADHD in a separate setting. | Nominal association and sample/ancestry differences are not validation of our selected score or shared locus. |
| 6 | [AlphaGenome, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10014-0) | Sequence-based predictions of regulatory molecular outputs and variant effects. | Predictions are not causal disease probabilities, measured expression effects or clinical predictions. |
| 7 | [AlphaGenome Atlas preprint, 2026](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.16.26363192v1.full) | Precomputed variant impact evidence and scores. | Preprint; score is broad rather than a tissue-specific gene assignment; output terms govern permitted uses. |
| 8 | [Atkins et al., 2021: FI GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8441299/) | UKB plus TwinGene GWAS of deficit accumulation. | Broad FI includes sleep-related items; cohort and item composition matter. |
| 9 | [Ye et al., 2023: Fried score GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10651618/) | Five-component physical frailty score in UKB and independent phenotype-side follow-up. | Exhaustion remains an overlapping component; a published single-trait replication is not replication of a cross-trait causal variant. |
| 10 | [Mak et al., 2025: hospital frailty GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-025-00925-y) | FinnGen hospital-code-based HFRS GWAS with UKB replication and proteomics. | Hospital ascertainment and registry diagnoses define a different construct from FI and FFS. |
| 11 | [Tseng et al.: multimodal FI and Fried phenotype](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10828416/) | UKB EHR-based FI and Fried phenotype analysis with multimodal modeling. | The published item table does not explicitly name sleep/fatigue, but full coding and accessible full GWAS data remain unverified; not the same-component-exclusion experiment. |
| 12 | [Song et al., 2024: insomnia–frailty genetics](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnagi.2024.1358996/full) | Prior global/local and cross-trait results including 3p21.31 and rs10865954. | Pre-empts first-discovery claims; overlap, rg sensitivity and local-statistic meaning need attention. |
| 13 | [Zhu et al., 2022: sleep and frailty transitions](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12916-022-02557-0) | Removed insomnia symptoms from an observational FI and analyzed sleep/frailty transitions. | Observational and not a component-excluded genetic GWAS; residual confounding possible. |
| 14 | [Petermann-Rocha et al., 2020: FI and Fried phenotype in UKB](https://bmcmedicine.biomedcentral.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s12916-020-01822-4) | Published within-person FI–Fried categorical cross-tab in 383,845 participants. | Category correlation reconstructed here is not continuous FI–FFS correlation; UKB may overlap our GWAS. |
| 15 | [Welstead et al., 2020: frailty instruments longitudinally](https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/162825199/1_s2.0_S0531556520304034_main.pdf) | Independent cohort context for phenotypic FI–Fried relationships, including baseline rho=0.43. | Different measures, age and population; sample size for each correlation not explicitly established here. |
| 16 | [Bulik-Sullivan et al., 2015: cross-trait LDSC](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26414676/) | Genome-wide common-variant genetic correlation from summary data. | Estimates depend on valid GWAS, LD reference and model assumptions; r_g is not the correlation between measured phenotypes. |
| 17 | [Shi et al., 2017: ρ-HESS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5673668/) | Local covariance and correlation framework. | Differentiate covariance from correlation and account for LD, overlap and local h² uncertainty. |
| 18 | [Giambartolomei et al., 2014: coloc](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1004383) | Bayesian regional shared-variant hypotheses. | Single-signal assumptions and priors matter; high H4 is not proof of a causal gene. |
| 19 | [Park and Ray, 2026: PLACO+](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40913314/) | Cross-trait composite-null testing with correlated statistics. | Requires genome-wide data and appropriate assumptions; our small simulation and rounded HapMap3 scan do not establish stringent-level calibration. |
| 20 | [GTEx v8 methods](https://gtexportal.org/home/methods) | Measured tissue-specific QTL context and variant–gene pair thresholds. | Exact-variant association does not show that the same causal variant drives either GWAS or that the tissue is causal. |
| 21 | [Liu et al., 2025: FFS and schizophrenia](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41537-024-00550-5) | Applied global and local correlation, pleioFDR/conjFDR, and molecular follow-up to Ye’s FFS GWAS and schizophrenia; reports prior cross-disorder frailty loci. | This is not insomnia–frailty replication; it reuses the Ye FFS source. Do not claim that applying conjFDR to a frailty–brain disorder pair is itself novel. |
| 22 | [Frailty, chronic pain and rheumatoid arthritis preprint, 2026](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.30.26356940v2.full) | Reports multi-phenotype frailty and chronic-pain regional analyses, including FOXP2-region follow-up. | Preprint; different phenotypes and methods, and FOXP2 proximity is not proof of a sleep–ADHD–frailty mechanism or independent replication of this project. |
| 23 | [Foote et al., 2025: multivariate frailty genetics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-025-02269-0) | Genomic SEM of 30 frailty deficits, including insomnia and tiredness; one general and six residual factors, with 408 reported loci and external prediction analyses. | Pre-empts broad novelty claims for multivariate frailty-domain genetics. The general factor includes insomnia; latent factors are not matched sleep-excluded FI or exhaustion-excluded FFS. See the [release and metadata review](component_exclusion/foote_2025_metadata/REVIEW.md). |
| 24 | [Vetter et al., 2026: Berlin Aging Study II](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-025-01233-7) | Within-person adapted deficit FI–Fried Pearson correlations of 0.38 at baseline and 0.53 at follow-up in the first imputed dataset. | Different cohort and instruments; not the project GWAS participants or pooled imputation estimates. The figure, not its caption alone, identifies the time points. |
| 25 | [Mak et al., 2023: metabolic underpinnings of frailty](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13868) | FI and modified physical-frailty GWAS in UK Biobank enable a same-study construct sensitivity; the study also ran category-excluded FI GWAS. | The main released FI retains sleep items and FP retains exhaustion. The [mental-wellbeing-excluded FI](component_exclusion/mak_category_fi/RESULTS.md) removes insomnia, fatigue and six other items; its full SNP-level file was not identified in the reviewed release. Source overlap prevents independent two-trait replication. See the [paired audit](frailty_sensitivity/mak2023_cross_study/RESULTS.md). |

| 26 | [Qian et al., 2026: adolescent multi-omics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44220-026-00660-2) | Integrates blood QTL with insomnia and psychiatric GWAS; reports a MAD1L1 methylation–ADHD MR association. | A gene-level link to a selected sleep–frailty candidate is a hypothesis, not variant-level colocalization or independent two-trait replication. |

| 27 | [Gao et al., 2025: six sleep traits and FI MR](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12708208/) | Bidirectional MR across six sleep phenotypes with the Atkins FI meta-analysis. | Reuses the FI source; reverse FI–short sleep p=0.015 does not meet the paper’s stated 0.00833 six-test threshold. See the [correction audit](component_exclusion/prior_sleep_mr_2025/REVIEW.md). |

| 28 | [Watanabe et al., 2022: insomnia GWAS meta-analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-022-01124-w) | Large insomnia study reported gene-level LSAMP association before the 2026 sleep–ADHD study. | Gene-level association does not establish that rs62264764 regulates LSAMP, and related sources can overlap. See the [LSAMP review](variant_context/rs62264764_lsamp_prior/RESULTS.md). |

| 29 | [Abrishamcar et al., 2026: ADHD brain TWAS preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12934864/) | Reports cortex gene-level ADHD associations for MEF2C and TMEM161B. | Reuses the discovery ADHD GWAS; molecular colocalization and gene-target assignment remain model-dependent. See the [chromosome 5 review](variant_context/chr5_adhd_twas_prior/RESULTS.md). |

| 30 | [Liang et al., 2026: neuronal activation multiomics](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42348686/) | Human neuron activation, cell-specific QTL and allele-specific chromatin provide a possible molecular follow-up source. | The relevant tables were not accessible in this execution context; no project variant result was obtained. See the [source assessment](variant_context/neuronal_activation_2026/RESULTS.md). |
| 31 | [Che et al., 2025: FI, FFS and sleep MR](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11812435/) | Bidirectional MR compares both FI and FFS with four sleep outcomes, including insomnia. | Same frailty GWAS; no reported multiplicity correction; insomnia → FFS is nominal and MR-PRESSO detects pleiotropy. See the [scope and correction audit](component_exclusion/prior_two_frailty_mr_2025/REVIEW.md). |
| 32 | [Zhou et al., 2025: frailty and nine mental traits](https://doi.org/10.1192/bjo.2024.835) | Bidirectional FI MR, additional Fried phenotype analyses and overlap-aware sensitivity include insomnia. | Shared frailty sources and mental-health items limit independence and causal interpretation; no matched item deletion. See the [prior-study review](component_exclusion/prior_mental_frailty_mr_2025/REVIEW.md). |

| 33 | [Borhan et al., 2026: Canadian ordinal frailty phenotype](component_exclusion/borhan_2026/REVIEW.md) | A separate Canadian cohort, ordinal Fried phenotype and individual-component GWAS add phenotype and replication-design context. | The combined phenotype includes exhaustion; individual-component tests are not a four-component item-excluded GWAS. The article directs researchers to controlled CLSA data access. |

| 34 | [Munro et al., 2026: LaDDR RNA phenotypes](variant_context/linc03051_laddr_2026/RESULTS.md) | Prior data-driven GTEx brain RNA–insomnia xTWAS includes LINC03051 and model-dependent molecular colocalization. | UKB and GTEx sources may overlap prior Brain6 data; latent RNA features do not by themselves establish total-expression changes, ADHD sharing or causal mediation. |

| 35 | [Timmins et al., 2020: walking-pace GWAS](component_exclusion/walking_pace_published/RESULTS.md) | Reports genetic correlations of self-reported pace with insomnia and ADHD, including after BMI adjustment. | A sleep-free physical-function trait is not a sleep-excluded frailty score; the insomnia source includes UK Biobank. |

For project-specific numeric values, source overlap, locus evidence, gene annotation and open analyses, use [RESEARCH_ATLAS.md](RESEARCH_ATLAS.md) and its linked receipts. The paper list alone does not validate proposed novelty: systematic up-to-date searching and source-specific replication are still needed.

- [Varvas et al. 2026 adult ADHD-symptom preprint](replication/adult_adhd_symptoms_2026/REVIEW.md): preliminary domain-specific genetic associations, including FOXP2-annotated and ARNTL-annotated signals; exact Brain6 variant correspondence and cohort independence are unverified.

A [2025 deposited cross-trait frailty–sleep manuscript](literature_review/frailty_psychiatric_2025/RESULTS.md) describes related FI–sleep-disorder analyses, but its claimed journal details conflict with the publisher volume listings and its analysis tables were not obtained. Treat its publication status and results as unverified.
