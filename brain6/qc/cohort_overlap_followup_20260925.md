# Brain6 cohort-overlap follow-up: long sleep–bipolar disorder

**Audit date:** 2026-09-25. **Scope:** source-level cohort membership only; no participant-level data or identity checks were performed.

The locked long-sleep exposure is Dashti et al. 2019 (UK Biobank; PMID 30846698). The selected bipolar outcome is Mullins et al. 2021 (PGC3 all-bipolar GWAS; PMID 34002096). Mullins et al. report receiving bipolar summary statistics from five external cohorts, including UK Biobank, in addition to 52 PGC cohorts. This is direct evidence that both selected source GWAS contain UK Biobank data. The pair is therefore classified `DOCUMENTED` for cohort-level overlap in `manifests/cohort_overlap.tsv`.

The source reports 1,454 bipolar cases and 58,113 controls in the UK Biobank component; those are the case/control counts for that external component, not an estimate of the number of participants shared with the selected sleep GWAS. The exact person-level intersection and LDSC sampling covariance remain unknown. No overlap correction, rerun, or change to global estimates was made. The frozen post-atlas pair-selection file was not modified.

Sources:

- Mullins et al. 2021, primary methods and cohort description: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8192451/
- Dashti et al. 2019, primary sleep-duration GWAS: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405943/
- Independent published report identifying 1,454 cases and 58,113 controls in the UK Biobank component of the Mullins GWAS: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9541703/
