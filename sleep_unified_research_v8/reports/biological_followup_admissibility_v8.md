# Independent biological follow-up admissibility assessment

Reviewed 11 October 2026. Scope: one completed exploratory FinnGen R13 registry-insomnia × MVP GIA EUR abdominal-pain result and whether one local or molecular follow-up is scientifically justified with existing inputs. No new estimator, locus scan, package installation, body download, investigator contact or historical-file mutation was performed. Raw inspection was limited to file presence/size and literal headers; no favorable region or variant was selected from outcomes.

## Decision

**Do not launch local genetic correlation, fine-mapping or molecular colocalization now. Retain the completed global source-transport result as an honest biological use case.** The new fit supplies a legitimate, qualified global question; the old statement that there are zero eligible new primary signals should not be mechanically treated as permanently prohibiting a separately specified exploratory study. However, a global correlation does not identify a regional association, a shared causal variant or a molecular target. Neither a numerical locus nor a gene/tissue has been specified from independent evidence for this pair. Existing dense bodies are present, but the regional summary-statistic variance contract, suitable signed LD for methods that require it, and molecular-feature contract have not been established.

This decision is about present scientific evidence, not lack of software receipts, and is not evidence that local effects or molecular sharing are absent. The work does not need another controller or extensive reference-calibration campaign to finish the present resource-paper use case.

## What the actual result establishes

The independently reviewed pair gives rg=0.39912075217856924, SE=0.050695938374874, nominal 95% CI 0.2997585388013542–0.49848296555578436, P=3.4669408448782324e-15, across 815,347 allele-valid SNPs. All 200 deletion ratios were finite and positive; their independently reconstructed uncertainty agreed with stock LDSC. This supports positive shared common-variant association structure conditional on the adopted source and LD assumptions. It does not determine where sharing occurs.

FinnGen registry-coded insomnia is the new sleep source. MVP abdominal pain is the historical outcome, and previous results informed its selection. Cohort, phenotype ascertainment and source change together. Thus this is selected exploratory source/construct transport, not independently replicated two-trait discovery, a clinical-versus-questionnaire difference test, disease specificity, a causal sleep-to-pain effect or a molecular mechanism. The new evidence belongs to this project; a field-level novelty claim is unestablished.

**Do not overstate the role of LD or N.** `coloc.abf` can analyze dense regional beta and varbeta without an LD matrix, under at most one causal variant per trait. With supplied case-control beta/variance it does not universally require the LDSC effective-N convention. It also does not require identical LD structures across populations. Thus absent signed LD or a shared constant N is not a universal reason to prohibit all colocalization. The present obstacles to an LD-free fallback are the unresolved MVP variance semantics, no defensible fixed region, no justified single-causal model, and no molecular-feature inputs. A favorable ABF posterior must not be obtained by choosing a simpler model to avoid these gaps. [Official coloc data and model guidance](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/articles/a02_data.html).

For multi-signal colocalization, SuSiE requires an appropriate signed LD matrix for each study and a checked alignment with its effect statistics. Simple ABF can conceal additional signals even when its prior sensitivity looks favorable; the developer's demonstration illustrates why single-signal validity cannot be assumed. [Official coloc multi-signal guidance](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/articles/a06_SuSiE.html).

For LAVA, a null-compatible global cross-trait intercept does not prove zero overlap. A protocol must adopt a justified overlap/noise model, not set overlap to zero because its P is nonsignificant. Current LAVA authors warn that the small 1000G reference can inflate type-I error and bias local h² and recommend their larger UKB reference for European analyses. UKB reference availability still does not establish Finnish matching or valid pair-specific inference. [Official LAVA documentation](https://github.com/josefin-werme/LAVA).

## Finite future question and stopping rules

If regional follow-up is reconsidered, the finite scientific question should be: **within one externally defined genomic region selected without consulting this MVP outcome's regional results, do dense registry-insomnia and abdominal-pain statistics support shared association structure rather than distinct signals?** Molecular extension requires one separately justified gene/feature and context; it must not be added by looking for whichever tissue gives the highest posterior. No numerical region or variant is designated in this assessment because no independent locus evidence was established; assigning one now would fabricate specificity.

Before regional output access, freeze one region's exact build/coordinates and external selection evidence, both source releases, retained dense variant universe, uncertainty route, ancestry/LD treatment, overlap assumptions, one method with fixed priors/diagnostics, and all-result reporting. A region defined from one trait's source can be explored, but the selection process must be disclosed; it is not a genome-wide confirmatory finding. Molecular follow-up additionally needs a fixed feature and compatible full regional QTL statistics with accession, tissue/cell definition, sample size, ancestry, build and permitted use.

Stop without a shared-signal claim if beta/variance semantics cannot be defended; dense coverage or the relevant peak is missing; allele/build/order is ambiguous; required signed LD is unsuitable; local heritability is uninformative; fine-map diagnostics fail; single-causal assumptions are contradicted; molecular inputs are absent; or posterior support is weak/prior-dependent. A failed/weak locus is retained, not replaced with a more favorable region, tissue, method or prior. Lack of signal/power is not evidence of distinct mechanisms or biological absence. A source-schema or bounded regional feasibility extraction could be a next preparation step if its purpose is to close one of these specific gaps; it is not permission to launch a genome-wide scan.

Show the three estimates and marginal intervals, the actual phenotype definitions and source reuse. A useful researcher decision is: the positive global result prioritizes a sleep–pain genomic question for appropriately designed regional or independent-cohort follow-up, but does not justify naming a gene or claiming insomnia causes abdominal pain. Demonstrating this distinction, plus uncertainty and source contracts, is a biological interpretation/use case. It is not a molecular-discovery panel. It can strengthen an integrated resource paper without manufacturing a mechanistic story or promising Cell Genomics acceptance.

Essential present work: incorporate the reviewed estimate, source-defined figure and limits into the qualified release and manuscript materials; have scientific reviewers assess the integrated claims. Optional, contingent work: one regional analysis after the stated data gaps close. Unjustified work: broad LAVA/fine-map/QTL scans, historical-result imports, repeats of the clean global fit, and new execution infrastructure without a concrete defect.
