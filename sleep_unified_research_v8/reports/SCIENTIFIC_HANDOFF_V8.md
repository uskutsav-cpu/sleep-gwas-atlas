## Central contribution and limits

The defensible current direction is one integrated, source-aware genomic association resource with demonstrated interpretation safeguards and an exploratory source-transport use case. Existing association tables alone do not establish a new biological discovery. Added value must be assessed against existing resources and actual researcher use, not the number of calculations or software receipts. Cell Genomics includes research resources and analyses in its [official scope](https://info.cell.com/cell-genomics-registration); acceptance is an editorial and peer-review decision, not an execution milestone.

## Material source-direction finding

Independent discrepancy review inspected five fixed cross-resource cases and all 25 published Table 1 controls from de Lange 2017. After excluding one indel and two palindromic SNVs, all 22 eligible controls conflict with the assumed disease-risk Effect-per-Allele1 convention. They cover 12 chromosomes and both risk-allele orders. Retained HM3 controls preserve the conflict; numerical reproduction therefore does not validate disease direction. The original conversion/effect contract remains unavailable. The findings do not establish author error, HGA error or a universal all-row correction.

The separate V8 candidate retains all 396 core record positions but withholds rg, z and both signed interval bounds for the 36 records using these three outcomes. Their unchanged SE/P/q/threshold/classification metadata are explicitly historical and cannot support biological interpretation. The source-direction hold is mandatory in current retrieval. Original tables remain immutable. No blind sign edit, changed BH family or source-wide correction was made. Restoring these directions requires a defensible uniform source contract; until then they remain excluded from signed conclusions. The private source-owner clarification packet is prepared and has not been sent.

## New empirical evidence

One separately specified exploratory native estimate paired FinnGen R13 registry insomnia with historical MVP GIA EUR abdominal pain: rg=0.39912075217856924, SE=0.050695938374874, nominal 95% CI=0.2997585388013542–0.49848296555578436, P=3.4669408448782324e-15, 815,347 allele-valid SNPs. Independent arithmetic reproduced the full ratio, uncertainty from all 200 actual deletion ratios, Z, P and interval. No obvious deletion instability appeared. The completed native fit was not rerun.

This is new project evidence from a sleep-source extension, not a first-ever association or fully independent two-trait replication. Historical outcome selection/reuse, CI95/log-Wald reconstruction, constant effective N, HM3 fallback, approximate Finnish-to-EUR LD and unverified cross-enrollment remain attached to the estimate. There is no calibrated source-difference test. Related clinical/habitual sleep prior art limits conceptual novelty. The new three-row source-defined figure supports an honest biological interpretation/use case, not a molecular mechanism.
