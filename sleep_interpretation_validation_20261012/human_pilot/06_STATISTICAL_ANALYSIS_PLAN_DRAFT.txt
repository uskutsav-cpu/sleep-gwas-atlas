STATISTICAL ANALYSIS PLAN — PROSPECTIVE HUMAN FEASIBILITY PILOT DRAFT
Status: NOT_STARTED / NO_DATA. Lock investigator version/date before responses.

Population/design
Target 8–12 independent eligible consented researchers, exact count and window prospectively fixed. Frozen twelve tasks, six per workflow, complementary allocations and task orders are unchanged. Planned P01–P12 are placeholders. No participant substitution based on score. Human participants, consent and scores cannot be substituted with AI responses. All tasks are purposive; results do not generalize to arbitrary research tasks or all platforms.

Primary endpoint and paired estimand
For each retained participant i, S_iq and S_ip are the counts of tasks fully supported by conservative agreement of both independent scorers in integrated_query and plain_tables. Each score is divided by SIX ASSIGNED TASKS, not by completed or assessable tasks. D_i = S_iq/6 - S_ip/6; count difference S_iq-S_ip may also be shown. Report every retained participant's two totals and D_i, their empirical distribution, mean/median and range only after actual data exist. Report the count of evaluable participant pairs; tasks are clustered within participant and are not independent people. No confirmatory p-value, superiority threshold or favorable minimum effect defines pilot success. Any supplementary inferential analysis requires prospective justification addressing participant and task dependence; do not improvise one after outcomes.

Fully supported rule
Every required original-key factual unit must be correct and no material unsupported causality/clinical-equivalence/replication/difference claim can be present. Both scorers must independently agree on full support for conservative primary credit. Semantic equivalence is accepted; original numeric rg/SE/CI tolerance is 0.0005 absolute; family/threshold classifications exact. Independent scores, unit disagreement and unresolved status remain visible. Separate adjudicated scores are secondary and never overwrite independent scores or primary totals.

Missingness and withdrawal — original protocol preserved
Unanswered, incomplete, withdrawn-task and scorer-unresolved responses are NOT fully supported in the six-task denominator. Record their distinct reasons. A task withdrawal is not an entire participant withdrawal. Entire participant withdrawal remains in recruitment/flow accounting; consent determines retention of already collected records. If approved consent permits retention, retain authorized observations and apply original missingness rules transparently. If consent requires deletion, delete relevant data/linkage as approved and omit that participant from paired score summaries, retaining only allowed nonidentifying flow information. Do not fabricate scores or all-zero participant records to replace erased data. Report the number omitted, consent-governed reason and resulting balance, without disclosing identity. No observed-score-dependent exclusion.

Failures/deviations
Pre-response instrument failures affecting both workflows are reported separately and excluded only under documented outcome-blind amendment before any answers; this unchanged packet makes no exclusion or replacement. An unresolved newly discovered correctness issue requires investigator assessment before execution, especially exact MVP source/CI uncertainty for T04 historical retrieval; no task/key is amended. Any future amendment is distinct and prospectively reviewed. In-session access issues, timer failures, interruptions and deviations are logged and missingness retained. Conditional correctness among completed/assessable tasks can be a clearly labeled secondary descriptive summary with its exact denominator; it cannot replace the six-task primary endpoint.

Other descriptive summaries
Report task-by-workflow completion, fully supported counts, factual-unit errors, unsupported claim categories, independent scorer agreement/disagreement, order and prior familiarity under approved administrative collection. Shared source/interpretation learning, condition/order imbalance and residual scorer unblinding remain limitations. Secondary human elapsed times use approved start/stop rules. Unfinished tasks have censored time and completion status; report them separately and do not pool them as completed answers. Do not infer human time from tool/model latency. No additional participant questions are added.

Flow and reporting
Report approached, eligibility/declined if allowed, consented, started, completed, withdrawn, data retained/deleted and actual independently scored participants. None of these counts is presently observed. Fixed recruitment window governs stopping regardless of results. Report null/unfavorable outcomes and instrument failure modes. Feasibility success means executable logistics and observable failure modes, not a favorable score. A later confirmatory study requires a new power/sample-size rationale and protocol. No figures or statistical results are prepared until genuine responses exist.
