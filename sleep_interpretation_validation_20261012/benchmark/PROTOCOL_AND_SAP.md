# Sleep GWAS Atlas interpretation benchmark: protocol and statistical analysis plan

Protocol version 1.0, 12 October 2026. This plan is written before final model responses. The benchmark is an interpretation and retrieval evaluation of a fixed resource, not a new GWAS analysis, an audit of raw GWAS validity, or a clinical decision instrument. No LDSC, harmonization, controller, native transport or source data refitting is authorized by this protocol.

## Scientific question and estimands

The primary comparison is B versus C: does a source-aware joined interface improve correct factual interpretation relative to disjoint conventional tables containing the same evidence? Both conditions carry precisely the same canonical fields, including source definitions, missingness, marginal uncertainty, frozen testing families, QC flags, source-direction holds, and the new uncertainty hold. C is the actual current `query_atlas.py` output, accompanied by authentic conventional-resource rows. B renders the same information as separate tables and scope notes without combining estimate and phenotype fields in a row. Canonical reconstruction must give exact equality for every packet before execution.

A is a practical conventional-resource comparison: the authentic Human GWAS ATLAS v20191115 metadata and GC snapshots, all label candidates recorded by the existing source-aware crosswalk, and ordinary resource documentation. A is not a synthetic impoverished copy of C. Snapshot numeric data are read from the cached original files, with original GC line numbers and source-file hashes retained privately. The landing page was cached on 10 October 2026; ordinary documentation was checked on 12 October 2026 and is explicitly contemporary rather than asserted to be a 2019 documentation freeze. A has different sources, coverage and estimator qualifications. Its exact-source abstentions are correct answers. No B/C result is required as A gold when A cannot support it. An A accuracy ceiling is therefore not evidence that A offers equal discovery coverage. A-versus-C findings are descriptive and cannot isolate representation.

Main-benchmark estimand: the difference C minus B in strict all-required-fields-correct task rate across the 60 final items, giving each task equal weight. This is exact retrieval/admissibility correctness on a finite instrument, not a validated measure of broad scientific interpretation. For models executed in this study, average task differences within each model first; report both model-specific results and a pooled descriptive mean. Provider/family generalization is unavailable in this Codex-only setting.

## Inventory, sampling and exclusions

There are exactly 60 final tasks and six development tasks. Ten final strata contain six items each: uncertainty; source-defined phenotype; sample overlap; provenance; testing families; QC; direction; historical validation; causal overclaim; and missingness/uncalibrated contrasts. The last stratum has three table-absence items and three fitted-specification contrasts. QC has two h2-Z failures, two intercept failures and two both-pass records. Direction has three hold and three ordinary records. Validation has two exact-source uncertainty holds and four other historical outcome-side checks. Testing-family items span core, extension and validation equally.

Selection seed is 20261012. Within declared strata and eligibility states, order records by SHA-256 of seed, stratum and exact record key. No estimate sign, magnitude, SE, P, q or association threshold determines ranking or post hoc replacement. Hold/QC states define declared strata only. Selection is finite and deterministic; it is a convenience sample from a frozen atlas, not a random sample of all possible scientific questions. It is deliberately balanced by failure mode, not by favorable atlas effects.

The 12-item human pilot remains untouched, and its private scoring key must never be read. Only its public `tasks.json` and the six V8 demonstration outputs are used for exclusions. Exact demonstrated pair records and the demonstrated linked discovery pair are excluded. Metadata-only pilot question themes are excluded from exact question reuse. Related source constructs can recur across newly selected distinct records; this dependence is recorded. Development and final records do not overlap. Repeated source GWAS, related outcomes and repeated qualification facts make tasks dependent.

## Source validity and gold

The source-qualified candidate preserves historical TSV values. Thirty-six bowel-source records remain on signed biological interpretation hold. Five validation records using exact MVP accession GCST90479148, and the separate exploratory artifact, lack authenticated deposited CI confidence level/construction and remain withheld from reliable inference. The benchmark uses those facts without converting historical threshold flags into cleared findings. The exploratory artifact is not selected as a final task. Full-precision reproduction does not establish raw source validity.

Gold is condition-specific and private on the external SSD. It contains field-level provenance, exact table/row/key references, protected conventional extract pointers, and numeric tolerances. Mechanical equality and script-derived keys are not scientific adjudication. An independent scientific reviewer must verify the task wording, source identity, every held-record interpretation and the draft gold before final scoring is certified. Record disagreements and their resolution without consulting final model responses. Do not tune questions, gold or tolerance to responses. If a genuine source/gold defect is found after execution, preserve original gold and responses, issue a versioned correction, and report both original and corrected analyses with affected item IDs.

A gold permits abstention for exact atlas facts not authenticated by the conventional snapshot. Nulls are required for unavailable exact-source numeric or metadata fields. Conservative statements that a causal/intervention/supported-independence/novelty/contrast claim is not established are scored false/NOT_ESTABLISHED under prespecified field-specific A policy. `independent_samples_supported` is a sufficiency judgment and is false when the packet supplies no support. `fully_independent_two_trait_replication` describes actual linked records and is null when those exact records cannot be authenticated. Query identifiers are unverified selectors, not observed source evidence. Six provenance tasks request authentic conventional-row retrieval separately, where such a row exists. Record A's exact-source support coverage, supported factual-field yield, valid abstention rate, and unsupported-substitution rate separately. Never call correct A abstention an error or silently score A against B/C numeric values.

Closed response schemas use allowed enums, booleans, nullable exact strings and numbers. Numeric correctness uses `abs(response-gold) <= max(1e-6, 1e-5*abs(gold))`; integers must be exact. Numeric precision, structural validity, and interpretation must be reported separately. Schema/field checking is automatic; semantic correctness is adjudicated from the supplied evidence rather than asserted valid by the scorer alone.

## Model access and execution

Accessible agents are Codex model aliases from OpenAI only. Model aliases, requested reasoning settings, observed host, returned model identifiers, provider, execution timestamps, response hashes, packet hashes and tool availability must be recorded. Distinct Codex model aliases are separate model variants, not independent providers or established independent model families. Do not claim three providers or families. Exact pinned backend version, effective temperature and sampling seed may be unavailable; record these as unknown/uncontrollable rather than invent values. Do not discover or expose credentials.

Model settings and prompt text are chosen using only the six development items and frozen before final execution. Development performance is exploratory and excluded from final estimates. Final packets are supplied one task-condition at a time in fresh, isolated sessions with no private gold, sibling answers, other condition packets, or external browsing/tool access. A solver may receive only its selected packet and the shared response instructions/schema. The harness must not give a solver access to the filesystem containing private gold. Execution in an agent with uncontrolled filesystem/tools is a disclosed protocol deviation and cannot be claimed as a clean blinded experiment.

The intended main execution is three accessible model variants, each across all 60 tasks in all three conditions, with one prespecified run per cell: 540 response cells. The initial feasibility amendment below is a distinct 90-cell subset execution, not completion of that main target. Verify actual model alias availability before registering variants; this is a target, not a claim that those cells exist. Freeze alias list and run count before responses. More repetitions require an amendment written before inspecting final outcomes. Do not silently substitute an unavailable model or retry until a desirable answer appears.

Use deterministic task order from seed 20261012, and cycle the six condition permutations across task/model blocks to balance condition order. Preserve first responses. Only transport failure, empty response or an interrupted session may be retried once, with original and retry retained and the reason recorded; an invalid but complete JSON answer is not a transport failure. Report time and token costs when actually exposed by the host; otherwise mark unknown. Do not impute latency from commentary or invent a deterministic temperature/seed.

## Outcomes, scoring and dependence

Primary task score is 1 only when all required fields are correct and the closed JSON schema is valid; otherwise 0. All tasks receive equal weight. Field fraction is secondary, with required fields equally weighted within an item. Report status accuracy, numeric accuracy among applicable numeric fields, structural validity, and stratum scores as secondary outcomes. Nullable fields remain meaningful: unavailable exact-source facts are scored as abstentions, never as numeric zero.

Prespecified unsafe interpretation indicators are: claiming causal direction or an intervention effect; interpreting missing/blank signed estimates as null effects; claiming biological absence or global novelty from table absence; claiming independent samples/fully independent two-trait replication without support; treating a historical threshold flag as sufficient clearance; clearing signed interpretation of a direction-held row; clearing reliable inference of an uncertainty-held row; or claiming a calibrated fitted-specification contrast. Only explicitly requested closed fields can trigger these automated indicators. Narrative or omitted-field interpretation is not inferred from a keyword heuristic.

Tasks share source GWAS and related outcomes; 180 or 540 answers are not independent biological observations. Report exact paired task differences within each model. For dependence sensitivity on the eventual full benchmark, use a prespecified two-way source-cluster bootstrap: assign independent Exp(1) weights to unique sleep-source and outcome-source clusters, multiply their weights for each item, preserve B/C pairing and average item differences over fixed executed models. Use 10,000 replicates, analysis RNG seed 20261012, and 2.5/97.5 percentile limits. Report cluster counts and note finite, few-cluster limitations; this interval is an exploratory dependence sensitivity, not a provider-population confidence interval. Also report the unweighted finite-task effect and a leave-one-sleep-source-out/leave-one-outcome-source-out range. Avoid treating repetitions or model aliases as independent provider families. Do not calculate a confirmatory significance claim from a naive item-independent t test.

All secondary comparisons, including A, strata, model-by-condition patterns, field fractions and unsafe interpretation rates, are descriptive. No multiplicity-adjusted discovery claim will be made from these exploratory endpoints. Publication wording must identify the finite benchmark, condition-specific answerability, model aliases, shared OpenAI provider, information parity, gold review status, and any execution deviations.

## Freeze and release

The protocol, SAP, task inventory, code, source hashes, solver packet hashes and gold hash must be timestamped and hashed before the first final response. Protocol, task inventory and the independently reviewed provisional gold version are frozen jointly before final responses. Mechanical agreement remains provisional while independent human scientific adjudication is pending. The model-registration freeze is separate and must precede the first final response. The construction agent has generated zero model answers. Protected HGA source bodies, numeric extracts, solver evidence packets and gold remain on the external SSD; the repository stores authored tasks, metadata, code and protocol only until source-specific redistribution rights are established.

The benchmark supports evaluating interpretive safety and representation. It cannot prove atlas source validity, clinical value, broad utility across scientific users, independent-provider robustness, or performance on unobserved tasks. No human pilot instrument or manuscript artifact is modified by this protocol.

## Pre-response amendment: bounded Codex-only feasibility

Amendment 1, 12 October 2026, replaces the proposed nine cumulative 60-task agent trajectories with fresh per-cell agents for an initial bounded feasibility experiment. Exactly ten final tasks are selected: one per stratum, the minimum SHA-256 of `20261012|feasibility|task_id`. They remain final tasks, not development data. Selection is made before any final response, without outcome inspection. Freeze the complete 60-item inventory and all packets/gold, these ten IDs, the model registry and the 90-cell schedule together before execution.

The registered aliases are `gpt-6.1-sol`, `gpt-6-sol`, and `gpt-6-luna`, each provider OpenAI, with requested reasoning effort medium. Effective backend version, temperature and random seed are unknown or uncontrollable. Each selected task is run once in A/B/C for each model, creating 90 planned cells. Use a fresh `fork_turns=none` agent for every cell. Task order is deterministic from the frozen seed and schedule; cycle the six condition permutations across task/model blocks. No context reset or compaction is planned within a single bounded cell. If context exhaustion, compaction or interruption occurs, preserve the event and do not silently restart; a retry is allowed only under the already specified transport/interruption rule and must preserve the first attempt. No complete invalid answer is retried. Timing and token counts remain unknown unless the host exposes them.

Each fresh solver is instructed to read only its one assigned packet and write only its own response file. It receives no private gold, other-condition packet, other solver response, development answer or external source. The callable Codex agent cannot technically disable tools/filesystem or pin the backend, so this is instruction-only isolation. Record actual accessible settings, assigned path, read/write access declarations, and observable tool traces; self-reported access declarations do not independently prove isolation. An unobservable trace is recorded as unknown rather than verified. Do not claim technical blinding or isolation simply because an instruction was issued.

Per-cell packets are complete; no truncation or summarized evidence may replace the original packet. The construction manifest records the largest serialized packet size. If a host cannot receive/read a complete packet, mark that cell unevaluable, preserve failure evidence, and do not fabricate a smaller proxy. A and B/C have intentionally different coverage; B/C retain exact canonical information parity.

The feasibility primary descriptive endpoint is strict exact retrieval/admissibility correctness over the ten frozen tasks, paired C minus B within each executed model. Report per-cell outcomes, model aliases, all 90 planned cells, completion, instruction adherence evidence, packet completeness, and protocol deviations. Source reweighting may be shown only as a fixed-response sensitivity, not uncertainty about agents, providers, backend sampling, or interface superiority. Do not present ten-task performance as 60-task benchmark results or use favorable direction/significance as a continuation criterion.

Prespecified continuation rule: complete and preserve all 90 attempted cell records where host capacity permits; do not inspect performance to alter tasks, gold, tolerance or prompts. Hold the main 540-cell execution if complete packets cannot be processed, if solver access to private gold/other condition evidence is observed, or if required response/attempt provenance cannot be preserved. Otherwise, the full execution still requires a written resource/isolated-execution registration before its first additional response. Ten feasibility tasks already exposed cannot be represented as new blind observations in a later run; preserve first responses and distinguish any reruns. Continued work may be justified by successful execution/provenance feasibility, not positive atlas effects. The main 60-task benchmark remains pending until actually executed.
