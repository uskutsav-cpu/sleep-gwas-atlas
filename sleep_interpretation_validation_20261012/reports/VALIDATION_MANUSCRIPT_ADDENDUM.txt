Sleep GWAS Atlas validation addendum
Completed Codex feasibility and pending researcher pilot
12 October 2026

This addendum supplies completed computational results for the integrated source-
qualified manuscript. It is one validation component of the same resource paper.
It does not add a new biological association, an independent replication claim,
human-subject results or a validated general interpretation advantage.

Methods for computational feasibility
We constructed 60 final scientific retrieval/admissibility tasks and 6 development
tasks, with 10 predefined evidence strata. A deterministic, prospectively frozen
subset selected one final task per stratum. Each of these 10 tasks was evaluated
under three conditions by three requested Codex aliases, gpt-6.1-sol, gpt-6-sol
and gpt-6-luna, all from OpenAI. The resulting 90-cell schedule, complete packets,
prompts, source hashes, provisional condition-specific key, numeric tolerances
and scoring software were frozen before final responses. Requested reasoning was
medium, with a fresh session for each cell and balanced condition order. Backend
version, effective temperature/seed, response latency, tokens and cost were not
authenticated. Different aliases are model variants within one provider.

Condition A supplied authentic cached Human GWAS ATLAS v20191115 data and ordinary
documentation. It did not authenticate any of the selected exact target-source
records. Exact-source abstention was therefore a legitimate response, while
supported conservative non-establishment judgments had field-specific false or
NOT_ESTABLISHED codes. Condition B supplied the same canonical evidence as C in
disjoint conventional tables; C supplied the joined source-aware query output.
Information parity was independently checked before execution. A/C comparisons
have different evidence coverage and cannot identify a representation effect.

The primary score required all requested fields to agree with the frozen key
within prespecified numerical tolerances and the closed schema to be valid.
Secondary descriptive outcomes included field agreement, numeric/schema/status
correctness, explicitly requested unsafe interpretation indicators and appropriate
abstention. This finite instrument evaluates exact retrieval and admissibility,
including formatting and instruction adherence. It is not a validated measure of
general scientific interpretation or realistic open-ended resource use.

Solvers were instructed to read only their assigned packet and write their own
answer, without gold, other conditions or external sources. The environment did
not technically disable filesystem/tools, and independent access traces were
unavailable. All complete first answers were preserved. The first three cells
reported initial read-output truncation followed by full rereads before answering;
that recovery is self-reported. A capacity-related failed launch on cell 48 was
preserved and followed by its one allowed transport retry. No complete answer
was rerun or repaired. Returned packet hashes on cells 36 and 44 disagreed with
registration; both were preserved and excluded from primary scoring as unevaluable
provenance. The frozen scorer received 88 unchanged eligible envelopes, while the
execution record retains all 90 completed cells and the separate failed launch.
Matching hash metadata does not independently authenticate actual read access.

Results for computational feasibility
All 90 scheduled cells produced definitive response envelopes. The primary
provenance-eligible analysis included88: all 60 B/C responses and 28 A responses.
Both provenance exclusions were condition A responses from gpt-6-luna.

For each alias, all 10 paired C-minus-B strict-score differences were zero.
Both presentations reached the ceiling: B 30/30 and C 30/30. There was no observed
matched-evidence accuracy improvement in this subset. This finding does not prove
equivalence or exclude benefits on harder tasks, natural scientific workflows,
efficiency or human use. No confidence interval or significance test is reported
for the ten-task feasibility. The tasks share7 sleep-source and 10 outcome-source
identifiers, and model answers are not independent biological observations.

All 88 eligible responses were schema-valid and status-correct. No explicitly
requested unsafe-interpretation indicator was triggered. All A disagreements were
null values where the frozen rubric required false or NOT_ESTABLISHED; these were
conservative coding disagreements rather than unsupported factual substitutions.
Condition A correctly abstained on 78/78 required exact-target factual fields and
retrieved9/9 supported conventional factual fields, with zero unsupported target
substitutions. Its exact target-source support coverage remained0. These results
cannot support a general claim that the atlas improves scientific accuracy over
conventional resources. Closed-field safety scores do not certify every free-
prose statement or all conceivable interpretation errors.

An independent AI statistical reviewer reconstructed the outcomes and verified
that the eligible projection omitted only the two mismatched-hash cells without
altering any retained envelope. Assigned-packet comparison of those two original
answer objects gives gpt-6-luna A 5/10 if they are added as an explicitly unverified
linkage sensitivity; B/C remain unchanged. This does not clear their provenance
or replace the primary analysis. A distinct AI scientific reviewer assessed the
actual responses and notices. These reviews are not human scoring certification
or independent-provider replication. Accountable human adjudication remains
pending. Full results, exclusion ledger and reviews accompany this addendum.

Human researcher pilot
The original 12-task pilot, target8–12 participants, allocation and private key
remain unchanged. We prepared protocol, information/consent and recruitment
drafts, administration/allocation plans, de-identified response and scorer forms,
data management, statistical analysis and reporting templates. No recruitment,
consent, participants, human responses or human utility outcomes are reported.
Institutional/consent determination, recruitment authority, investigator, dates,
two qualified independent human scorers and approved storage/privacy arrangements
remain necessary. The original evidence-schema manifest mismatch and MVP-linked
historical task caveat require a documented pre-execution investigator disposition.
The small pilot will support feasibility and descriptive effects if authorized;
it cannot establish definitive superiority. Favorable CSAIL reviews are user-
reported, with no obtained review reports or institutional authorization.

Interpretation and remaining validation
The completed feasibility supplies genuine new observations of model responses,
including a negative matched-evidence comparison and provenance failures. It adds
no new validated genetic relationship, causal mechanism or fully independent
two-trait replication. The supported resource contribution is source-keyed
integration of historical estimates with phenotype/release identity, QC, original
testing families, validation dependence, uncertainty and mandatory interpretation
holds. Demonstrated resource capabilities are distinct from measured user benefits.

The complete 60-task inventory is prepared but its full 540-cell execution is not
completed. A design-only, outcome-independent decision recommended finishing the
registered feasibility without automatic expansion: more supplied exact-key
tasks cannot repair construct-validity, access-control or human-validation limits.
Any full finite-instrument continuation needs its own registration, preserves the
first90 responses and adds450 new cells; it must not tune final tasks after these
results. Broader interpretation claims need an independently designed, adjudicated
holdout study with realistic ambiguity/multirow retrieval and adequate controls.

The uncertainty of exact MVP source GCST90479148 remains unauthenticated. Five
historical validation records and the separate exploratory fit remain held from
reliable inference; historical values are preserved. Thirty-six bowel-source
signed interpretations remain held. These qualifications remain in the resource
and manuscript irrespective of AI scores. The paper must retain negative evidence,
distinguish outcome-side checks from independent replication, and avoid first-
discovery or general utility claims unsupported by the bounded resource benchmark.
