# Nature Communications requirements and current scientific gaps

Official pages consulted2026-10-10. The journal target is one integrated paper; this table does not claim editorial approval or readiness.

| Requirement | Verified policy and research implication | Current project state |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific advance | Articles must offer an important disciplinary advance. Editors assess novelty, impact, conceptual/methodological contribution and readership before external review. | Method novelty, identifiability and validation-source reviews are complete. No substantive new method, admitted new primary or qualifying new advance is established; see02. |
| Coherence and originality | Related submissions/in-press work must be disclosed; conceptual overlap matters. | One paper only. Historical atlas and extension are foundation; their associations are not relabeled discoveries. Related-publication declarations require authors. |
| Article preparation | Initial formatting is flexible. Guidance: main text ideally5000 words; title≤15 words; abstract≤200 words without citations; methods typically<3000; legends≤350; references generally≤70; up to10 display items subject to length. | The current NO-GO decisions are recorded; qualified manuscript preparation is active. Existing23 figures preserved; selected main figures must serve one supported argument. |
| Data and code | State access conditions, source identifiers, rights/restrictions, versions and parameters. Central custom code must be accessible; enduring deposition is required. | Aggregate tables/scripts available locally. Large third-party GWAS/reference/runtime bodies remain local; source-specific rights and final permitted Git publication remain unfinished. |
| Reporting summary | Life-science design/analysis details require a reporting summary. Exact sample sizes, repeated-source structure, tests, uncertainty, exclusions and analytical assumptions must be transparent. | Existing source/N/testing-family/QC tables provide technical evidence. A new Nature-specific crosswalk will distinguish verified facts, omitted original-source information and human declarations. |
| Ethics and authorship | Human-data ethics, authors' contributions, funding and conflicts require accurate declarations; third-party permissions remain the authors' responsibility. | Applicable institutional/source determinations and author details are requested, not invented. |
| AI transparency | Nature's current framework allows supported reasoning with verifiable human oversight and disclosure; scholarly accountability remains human. AI-produced core reasoning cannot be presented as human-derived. | Actual AI assistance is recorded. Automated adversarial reviews are internal checks, not journal peer review, empirical replication or human approval. Final accountable human verification is pending. |

The first scientific stopping gate is novelty and identifiability. Standard full covariance propagation is an established solution, not an invented method. A fixed-denominator V_Stand export does not establish calibrated correlation uncertainty. New expensive fits or realistic-LD simulations require a finite admitted protocol explaining the uncertainty they resolve and the result that would change the decision. They must not conflict with active V4 source processing.
