# Scientific decision for the integrated study

Decision recorded 2026-10-10: **NO-GO for new-method development and for Nature Communications submission on the present evidence.** Continue the essential V4 source reconstruction and prepare one accurately qualified manuscript, figures and reproducibility package for accountable human review. The decision concerns demonstrated scientific advance and validation; it does not stop authorized computational completion or manuscript preparation.

Failure to establish a new method does not rule out an important biological or resource contribution. Fully independent two-trait replication is also a claim-specific requirement, rather than a universal journal rule. The current resource has not demonstrated its field-level advance beyond historical reproduction, and the proposed measurement/transport claims lack validation appropriate to those claims. This is the basis of the present submission decision.

The four finite methodological candidates were joint genetic-correlation contrasts, weak-heritability inference, latent reliability correction and cohort/measurement transport. Independent primary-literature and mathematical reviews do not establish a substantive new solution. Full sampling-covariance propagation is established. Genetically structured measurement error and pure measurement/cohort effects are not identified by the present summary moments. Weak denominator inference is a real limitation, but a distinct improvement over strong existing methods has not been demonstrated. No new method, simulation campaign or benchmark experiment is admitted.

The predefined biological alternative is a two-outcome source/construct contrast: continuous questionnaire duration versus Jones 2019 main-period device duration, each with exact FinnGen R13 reflux or COPD. A formal dependent contrast may be untested in the inspected prior tables. Nevertheless, named questionnaire/device–COPD marginal comparisons and related reflux/tail/clinical findings already exist. An untested contrast is not by itself an important advance. Exact source/clinical/reference contracts, valid joint sampling covariance, prospective precision and compatible independent validation are unmet. Historical results were seen; selection cannot be called wholly outcome-blind. **No new primary contrast is admitted.** No further outcome or method alternatives will be added to rescue the result.

The strongest established route, if a future scientifically justified contrast passes admission, is inference from unstandardized joint genetic moments and a valid sampling covariance, propagating the full nonlinear Jacobian or recomputing the contrast under verified common genomic deletions. Current fixed-denominator `V_Stand` scaling and separately numbered 200-element delete arrays are insufficient. A standard implementation correction would not constitute methodological invention.

## Evidence that could change the scientific decision

A methods decision would change only with a precise identifiable target, a demonstrated failure of strong established baselines and a substantive prespecified improvement beyond standard covariance propagation, supported across appropriate data designs. A biological decision would change only with a clinically meaningful question distinct from published comparisons, verified source/construct/reference contracts, valid calibrated joint uncertainty, adequate prespecified precision and validation appropriate to the claim. A pure measurement claim additionally requires an anchored or crossed measurement/cohort design. Storage capacity, software receipts or additional marginal associations do not supply these missing observations.

Nature Communications readiness additionally requires final accountable human scientific approval, complete source rights and institutional/authorship declarations. The present qualified manuscript is for review, not a recommendation to submit or an assurance of journal acceptance.
