# Methodological novelty and identifiability review

Review date: 2026-10-10. Authoritative scope: one integrated Nature Communications discovery/methods study, with Atlas V4 preserved. This is a finite review of four candidates, not a methods manuscript or an exhaustive proof that no future method is possible. Existing sleep-literature and phenotyping audits were reused; unchanged full papers and historical outcome tables were not reanalyzed. No fits, simulations, GWAS downloads or V4 edits were performed.

**Decision: NO_GO_FOR_NEW_METHOD.** None of the four candidates currently demonstrates an important, identified inferential target inadequately addressed by established approaches and supported by the available data. Use established joint-moment inference for an admitted biological question. A correction to correlation uncertainty can be necessary for validity while remaining standard methodology. This decision does not interrupt the authorized V4 campaign or rule out one integrated biological paper if its independent scientific gates pass.

## Exact targets and finite candidate decisions

Let S be the genetic variance/covariance matrix for specified observed phenotype definitions, and V the sampling covariance of its estimated unique elements. Define r(A,B)=S_AB/sqrt(S_AA S_BB). These quantities concern the GWAS populations, genetic architecture assumptions and marker/reference set; they are not clinical risk correlations.

**C1 — Shared-trait dependent contrasts.** The estimand is Δ_D=r(Q,D)−r(M,D), or equivalently a shared-sleep contrast r(S,D1)−r(S,D2), with a prespecified phenotype/outcome family. Sampling dependence comes from common GWAS, correlated traits, shared denominator estimates and LD. Multivariable LDSC/Genomic SEM already estimates joint genetic moments and their sampling covariance. With positive variances away from zero, ∇Δ′V∇Δ or valid joint deletions propagates uncertainty. A saturated/identified Genomic SEM model with a defined parameter can express the same target. This would be a scientifically useful application, not a new estimator. [Genomic SEM](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-019-0566-x), [primary supplement](https://labs.la.utexas.edu/tucker-drob/files/2019/05/Genomic-SEM-Supplement.pdf)

Do not confuse three matrices: SNP-estimate error dependence, the genetic covariance S, and sampling covariance V of estimated genetic moments. A bivariate LDSC intercept alone is not the covariance between two estimated r values. Overlap adjustment is already part of cross-trait LDSC under its assumptions. The intercept combines overlap and phenotype/error covariance, with possible confounding; it cannot identify a participant count or prove zero overlap. [LDSC primary derivation](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fng.3406/MediaObjects/41588_2015_BFng3406_MOESM38_ESM.pdf)

**C2 — Weak genetic variance and calibration.** The desired target is a confidence set/test for the same Δ that remains valid near small genetic variances and at the frozen extreme tail. This is the strongest potentially methodological candidate, but no distinct unresolved solution has been established. At zero genetic variance, r itself is undefined; computation cannot identify it. Near zero, delta/ratio inference can be unstable, even when a point estimate is finite. Fieller-type confidence-set inversion is established general ratio theory, although its elementary formula does not directly solve a difference of r values with square-root denominators. Joint-moment set inversion, covariance-based tests, likelihood/profile approaches and signal eligibility are strong baselines, not automatically calibrated replacements. Their adaptation would require a justified joint law and V, not just a new wrapper. [Primary ratio-confidence-set theory](https://www3.stat.sinica.edu.tw/sstest/j19n3/j19n312/j19n312.html)

SUPERGNOVA explicitly discusses unstable local r denominators and favors covariance inference; HDL/HDL-L provide likelihood alternatives. No cited validation certifies this project's joint contrast at its chosen extreme alpha under all overlapping case-control/device sources. That is a calibration obligation, not evidence that the denominator problem is newly discovered. Changing from r to covariance changes the scientific target and requires commensurate scales. h2 Z≥4 is neither uniform ratio coverage nor prospective power. [SUPERGNOVA supplement, section 5](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1186%2Fs13059-021-02478-w/MediaObjects/13059_2021_2478_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

**C3 — Reliability-corrected latent sleep sharing.** The proposed estimand r(T,D) for a true latent sleep construct T is different from r(Q,D) or r(M,D). Under Y_m=a_mT+e_m, a_m>0 and error genetically orthogonal to T and D, r(Y_m,D)=r(T,D): classical error reduces standardized heritability/precision without attenuating the genetic correlation. Dividing r by a square root of phenotypic reliability is invalid under that model. With genetically structured error or distinct sleep constructs, the latent loadings, error covariance and biological covariance cannot be separated from the available summary moments without anchors/restrictions. A factor model's imposed zero residual covariances are assumptions, not recovered biological measurement error.

PheMED already models relative multiplicative SNP-effect dilution using same-effect assumptions and a reference scale; it does not identify absolute truth, arbitrary clinical/device equivalence or genotype-dependent misclassification. It also requires a nonoverlapping pair when jointly modeling study dependence. Treat its retrieved 2023 record as a preprint, not a peer-reviewed guarantee. [Primary PheMED preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9882426/)

Repeated-measure error/participation research already tests covariance-aware heritability differences and distinguishes measurement accuracy from selection. Its time-invariant repeats are not a universal sleep reliability anchor: sleep can change over years. Re-estimating a standard error correction cannot support a methods priority claim. [Schoeler et al., online 2024/issue 2025, Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-024-02061-w), [exact supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41562-024-02061-w/MediaObjects/41562_2024_2061_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

**C4 — Clinical/questionnaire/device transport.** An observable target is a source-specific contrast Δ_source. A pure measurement effect at a common population/age/time is a different counterfactual target. Current sources combine measurement, cohort, age/time, inclusion/control rules and healthcare selection; a device subset measured years later does not isolate modality. MVP PheCode insomnia is a clinical-recorded construct while UKB insomnia is frequent complaints. More summary statistics for those same confounded cells do not identify the missing within-cohort cross-definition cells or selection mechanism.

Ascertainment-aware PCGC-s and EHR selection/misclassification sensitivity frameworks already address related problems under specific assumptions. PCGC-s needs specially constructed case-control summaries, prevalence and covariate information; generic public beta/SE/N is not automatically sufficient. EHR sensitivity models need credible selection/misclassification parameters. Neither turns a broad sleep complaint into a clinical diagnosis. [PCGC-s primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6035374/), [official required-input documentation](https://github.com/omerwe/S-PCGC), [Beesley et al. primary framework](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/sim.8524)

The strongest scientific gap is therefore an externally anchored, crossed measurement/cohort design that separates genuine construct differences from selection and diagnosis capture. The current data do not identify it. A narrower transport/robustness claim may be feasible and should use its actual observed definitions; it does not constitute a new method.

## Established baselines and important qualifications

| Method | Existing contribution relevant here | Limit that remains |
|---|---|---|
| LDSC | Global genetic covariance/r and overlap-related intercept modeling; genome block uncertainty | Architecture/LD/reference and ascertainment assumptions; intercept does not certify cohort identity |
| Genomic SEM | Joint S,V; identified multivariate/factor/regression models; defined-parameter uncertainty | Valid input covariance, constraints and block construction must be demonstrated |
| HDL | Full-LD likelihood for global covariance/r | Random-effect likelihood and reference/SNP compatibility; not automatic transport correction |
| HDL-L | Local likelihood, local variance/covariance inference | Distinct local estimand/null and LD assumptions; not a universal benchmark winner |
| SUPERGNOVA | Local covariance with estimated overlap term and uncertainty contribution | Weak local r denominator; locus definition and reference accuracy |
| LAVA | Local realized genetic components, bivariate/partial correlations and multiple regression; overlap correction | Requires local signal and valid LD/error structure; conditional association is not causal mediation |

The HDL and HDL-L primary supplements were inspected at their statistical model/simulation sections. The published HDL-L supplement generates random effects and uses 50 replicates per configuration; those settings do not alone validate extreme tails for a new joint contrast. [HDL supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41588-020-0653-y/MediaObjects/41588_2020_653_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), [HDL-L supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41588-025-02123-3/MediaObjects/41588_2025_2123_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

The 2025 LAVA-author technical note and HDL-L-author reply dispute benchmark interpretation. One target is realized component correlation β1′Rβ2/sqrt((β1′Rβ1)(β2′Rβ2)); a random-effect correlation parameter is a different target. The notes disagree about which null/performance interpretation is scientifically appropriate. They are not peer-reviewed resolutions. Do not call realization variation under a random-effect null a false positive for a fixed-effect target, or pool their reported detection counts as if the hypotheses were identical. Do not infer uniform method ranking. [LAVA-author note](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202507.0966), [HDL-L-author reply](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202508.1096), [LAVA exact methods/note supplement](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41588-022-01017-y/MediaObjects/41588_2022_1017_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

## Current implementation evidence does not certify V4 inference

The official Genomic SEM patch notes advertise standardized S/V output. The current `ldsc.R` actually computes `V_Stand = V * tcrossprod(scaleO)` after setting scaling factors from estimated S diagonals. That treats denominators as fixed and is not the full Jacobian of r. In contrast, current `usermodel.R` uses a Jacobian for `:=` parameters with sandwich-corrected parameter covariance. Therefore the established solution exists in principle, while the convenient standardized export cannot be accepted as proof of correct correlation uncertainty. [Current official ldsc.R, standardized block](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenomicSEM/GenomicSEM/master/R/ldsc.R), [current official usermodel.R, defined-parameter block](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenomicSEM/GenomicSEM/master/R/usermodel.R)

For a single r=c/sqrt(ab), the required derivatives are 1/sqrt(ab), −r/(2a), −r/(2b). Omitting the latter terms loses denominator variance and numerator/denominator covariance. This is standard nonlinear propagation. Current `ldsc.R` also computes row-quantile deletion cuts after trait/pair filtering; equal deletion row indices alone do not guarantee identical genomic boundaries across differing masks. Current `usermodel.R` has numerical covariance regularization paths, which do not restore identification or validate an invalid sampling matrix. These observations are limited source checks, not a V4 rerun or an instruction to replace its pipeline. Archived S/V stability and actual covariance calibration remain separate gates.

## Consequence for the integrated study

For the finite C1 application, freeze observed-construct contrasts, a small clinical outcome family, source/overlap documentation and minimum precision before inspecting new contrast outcomes. Use full-moment Jacobian/defined-parameter inference with independently checked sampling covariance, or a validated common-genome deletion procedure. Separate numerical reproduction, source robustness, outcome-only validation and fully independent two-trait replication. Do not declare these gates passed by this literature review.

No candidate currently shows a substantive estimator improvement or a supported beyond-atlas advantage. Reopening new-method development would require a precise target/null, a mathematical failure of strong established baselines under an admissible data-generating model, identifiable required inputs, and a prespecified improvement criterion transferable beyond sleep. C2 would additionally need coverage and the actual extreme-tail calibration under weak signals, overlap and realistic LD; C3/C4 would need external measurement/selection anchors or an explicitly justified restricted model. Do not create a sequence of alternatives or begin expensive simulations merely to seek a methods claim.

Prior Morrison/Goodman/Sleep Chart/multi-organ supplement audits remain the biological novelty foundation. They already constrain broad modality, conditional-sharing and atlas priority claims. This new review does not reopen their historical association results. A coherent integrated biological contribution can use established inference; its novelty, power, external validation and eventual Nature Communications GO/NO-GO must be decided separately.

Evidence boundary: exact primary supplements were read for LDSC, Genomic SEM, HDL, HDL-L, SUPERGNOVA, LAVA and the reporting-error study; some publisher main texts are subscription previews. The PCGC-s primary full-text fetch and PheMED supplementary fetch failed, so available primary indexed methods plus official documentation were used conservatively. No missing text was treated as verified. The companion candidate matrix and evidence ledger record these limits. Independent mathematical review agreement is an adversarial check, not empirical replication or human peer review.
