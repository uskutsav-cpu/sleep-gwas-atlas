# Independent Cell Genomics scientific drafting-readiness assessment

Reviewed 10 October 2026. The question here is whether the current science is sufficient to draft an accurately bounded single paper for investigator review. This is distinct from journal competitiveness, completed reporting/deposition or authorization to submit. This automated assessment reuses the direct completed-result and primary-prior-art review in `completed_results_scientific_interpretation_v1.md`, inspects four existing main result figures, and reads the final bounded source-status and device-duration wording. No analysis, new scientific source search, infrastructure or figure generation was performed. A limited live check of the journal's official scope was made.

**Decision: GO_TO_DRAFT_A_QUALIFIED_INTEGRATED_RESOURCE_PAPER.** There is enough settled quantitative evidence, source/construct interpretation and figure coverage for an honest manuscript. The completed evidence does not establish the program's originally sought important new primary discovery or new method. Drafting should proceed with that limitation explicit, rather than promising a result that the research did not deliver. The earlier Nature Communications submission verdict is not a universal prohibition on scientific drafting and should not be carried forward as a Cell Genomics drafting criterion.

## Strongest defensible single-paper contribution

The strongest contribution is an integrated, source-aware sleep–phenome association resource that retains complete testing families and distinguishes marginal genetic sharing, adverse sensitivity results and bounded external outcome-source support. It brings the 12 questionnaire, device and coded-clinical sleep constructs together across 33 core and 100 extension outcome-source entries, with operational definitions, marginal precision and source qualifications. The core and extension remain separate analysis families. These are 133 outcome-source entries, not necessarily 133 independent diseases or clinical experiments.

The resource can answer which observed source-defined sleep traits share common-variant architecture with which outcomes, and which historical associations retain support under alternate outcome sources and specified diagnostics. It does not identify a pure measurement effect, sleep-specific disease mechanism or optimal sleep prescription. Its useful scientific message is the uneven pattern of support and uncertainty across source-defined constructs. Numerical reconstruction supports that resource; process counts should not become the central biological argument.

The official Cell Genomics scope explicitly includes research, analyses, datasets, resources and methods across genetics/genomics. That makes a resource framing within the stated subject scope. It does not establish that this particular resource has the originality or importance required for favorable editorial selection; that remains unproven and should be assessed from the completed draft's demonstrated utility. This is an inference from the stated scope, not an editorial endorsement. [Official Cell Genomics scope](https://info.cell.com/cell-genomics-registration)

## What scientific drafting readiness does and does not settle

The manuscript can now state a stable question, show actual complete association families and figures, report external support and failures, explain the observed phenotype definitions, and delimit the unresolved inferences. Those ingredients are sufficient for drafting and accountable investigator review. The current final-source wording has corrected the blanket zero-device error and describes stopped partial source comparisons accurately. Retargeting the existing Nature Communications note/format to Cell Genomics is an editorial authoring change, not a new scientific experiment.

Scientific drafting readiness is therefore qualified but affirmative. It is not a statement that the broader novel-discovery mission has succeeded, that all source assumptions are certified, or that Cell Genomics is likely to accept the paper. Human scientific review, reference/source adjudication and accessible data/rights/declaration decisions remain necessary for finalization/submission. Those requirements should be completed around a concrete draft without withholding the already supported resource narrative until an unadmitted new result appears. This automated assessment is not human peer review or submission authorization.
