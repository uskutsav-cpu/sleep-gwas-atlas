# Independent scientific interpretation of completed association and historical-validation values

Reviewed 10 October 2026. This is an automated scientific review of actual coefficients, marginal uncertainty, stored testing-family labels, source definitions and existing primary-literature evidence records. It is not human peer review. No association fit, reanalysis, new BH calculation, simulation, source download, new literature search or tooling was performed. Physical TSV line numbers below include the header as line 1; keyed trait/outcome selectors are supplied because line order is not an estimand.

**Decision:** the completed tables support a qualified descriptive resource showing source-defined common-variant genetic sharing and bounded external outcome-side consistency. They do not establish a new primary biological finding, independent two-trait replication, causal pathway or calibrated measurement/source heterogeneity. The current Nature Communications submission NO-GO remains. This conclusion follows from the actual association evidence and its prior-art matches, not job receipts.

Thus, native unrounded coefficients reproduce the historical numerical findings at the available precision. They are actual association estimates, but they reuse the admitted historical inputs rather than supplying new cohorts or newly frozen hypotheses. Separate core and extension families must remain separate. These counts do not measure independent disease mechanisms or clinical breadth.

## Novelty from the actual prior-art evidence

`qualified_validation_prior_art23_v4_1.tsv` records 15 substantially similar published relationships, six related genetic/MR associations and two unresolved comparisons. No row establishes a first-ever relationship or admits a new primary claim. The six related classifications are not exact source-matched duplications: their record lacks a direct local-table comparator, so do not exaggerate their precision. Equally, an unresolved comparison is not positive evidence of priority.

The direct comparator records bind the major insomnia gastrointestinal and COPD patterns to Morrison's inspected primary supplements, and short/long disease sharing to inspected Sleep Chart evidence. The finite duration prior table supplies exact examples: Morrison `S13_Duration_LDSC` row 224 reports duration/reflux rg −0.1605, SE 0.04958; row 317 reports duration/COPD −0.1496, SE 0.05435. Goodman `SD24` row 41 reports composite sleep-health/reflux rg −0.4192, a related composite question rather than the same Q/device estimand. Sleep Chart `SF5` rows 96/130 and 266/280 already report short/long reflux/COPD marginal sharing. The records retain threshold, source-release and correction-version qualifications. These constrain broad disease-sharing novelty without proving every coefficient identical. [Morrison primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10851865/), [Goodman primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-07514-0), [Sleep Chart primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10524-5)

The multi-organ `SD11` named-source COPD comparisons already give Jones 2019 device duration near zero against two outcome sources (rows 2825/2961: −0.0244/0.0258) and Dashti 2019 questionnaire duration negatively correlated (rows 2856/2992: −0.1446/−0.1622). The two unresolved positive pairs are coded-apnoea/abdominal pain (validation line 19, rg 0.392011 [0.304656,0.479367]) and reported-snoring/cholelithiasis (line 30, 0.138571 [0.091464,0.185678]). These are actual threshold-positive historical associations with alternate outcome sources. The unresolved literature searches, reused sleep GWAS, clinical/source qualifications and lack of independent experiment prevent treating them as established novel discoveries. No outcome-driven primary selection is warranted.

No additional numerical inconsistency in the bounded current article claims was identified in this finite value review.
