# Bounded review of the proposed two-outcome duration contrast

Review date: 2026-10-10. Scope is exactly the recovered UKB continuous questionnaire duration source (`sleepdur`, Dashti 2019) versus Jones 2019 device duration (`accel_sleep_duration`, selected `ACC_SLEEP_DUR_RAW_SIN`), with FinnGen R13 `K11_REFLUX` and `J10_COPD`. This is an admissibility and prior-art review, not a new association analysis, protocol freeze or manuscript. Original supplements and earlier full-paper reviews were reused; only these target rows/aliases were rechecked. No new outcomes, fits, simulations, GWAS downloads or V4 modifications were performed.

**Decision: NO_GO_FOR_PRIMARY_ADMISSION_NOW; conditional feasibility of an established-method source/construct contrast.** A formal dependent contrast is a defensible target if its source, covariance, precision and validation gates pass. Its novelty is narrow and provisional. The current evidence does not establish an important original advance sufficient for the integrated Nature Communications objective. This is separate from the methods decision `NO_GO_FOR_NEW_METHOD`, and does not interrupt V4 reproduction.

## Target and interpretation

For each of the two fixed disease definitions D, define Δ_D = r_g(Q,D) − r_g(M,D), where Q is reported 24-hour duration including naps and M is inferred sleep summed within the main sleep-period window. This is a difference between two source-defined genetic correlations. It can test whether disease sharing estimated from one observed construct transports to the other; it does not identify an effect of measurement modality alone.

Q uses whole-hour baseline responses in 446,118 UKB participants. M uses a rank-normalized construct from up to seven wear days in 85,449 UKB participants, measured 2.8–9.7 years after baseline. Main-period device sleep excludes sleep outside the window and can include quiet-wake classification error. Nap inclusion, recall, transformation, elapsed time, age and device-participation selection all differ. The device sample overlaps the larger questionnaire cohort, but its exact intersection is not quantified by the reviewed source metadata. Linear scale invariance of r does not make rank normalization or these construct/population differences disappear. [Dashti duration study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08917-4), [Jones device study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6451011/)

The two diseases can support a finite clinically motivated design: respiratory symptom burden and reflux symptoms are plausible sleep-disruption contexts with different exposure and diagnosis-capture pressures. Smoking, adiposity, medication, healthcare contact and reporting can also affect the observed sharing. These are competing design explanations to review with a clinician, not mechanisms established by genetic correlation. They do not justify selecting this family from favorable estimates. Published and project historical values have already been seen, so the selection cannot be described as wholly outcome-blind; only the new contrast specification and later execution can be frozen prospectively.

## Admission gates and validation boundary

1. **Source and construct:** Freeze the actual Q/M effect columns, builds, ancestry, SNP mask and disease release/control definitions. Historical `EXACT` disease labels mean concept correspondence, not identical ascertainment. The historical FinnGen manifest gives 37,811/420,114 reflux cases/controls and 25,466/405,986 COPD cases/controls; those labels and counts are not substitutes for a current admitted source contract and endpoint/control audit. Accept the target as reported-24h versus main-period-device source transport only after human sleep-phenotyping review.
2. **Valid joint inference:** Obtain raw genetic moments S and their valid joint sampling covariance V, propagating numerator and all uncertain denominator terms. Sharing the disease GWAS and overlapping Q/M cohorts makes an independent-estimates formula invalid. The current official Genomic SEM `V_Stand` export is fixed-denominator scaling, so its presence is not evidence of a calibrated Δ. A full Jacobian/defined parameter or validated joint genomic deletions is established methodology. Archived covariance instability and filter-specific block boundaries remain actual validity gates. [Current official source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenomicSEM/GenomicSEM/master/R/ldsc.R), [defined-parameter implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenomicSEM/GenomicSEM/master/R/usermodel.R)
3. **Precision and finite family:** Before new contrast values are inspected, fix a scientifically meaningful Δ range, precision/power criterion, two-outcome correction and treatment of unstable variances. Published point differences, marginal P values and historical h2 eligibility do not establish prospective power for the new FinnGen-source contrasts. A null result is informative only if its interval can exclude the predefined meaningful range; otherwise retain it as imprecise. Do not remove a disease or change construct because it fails.
4. **Validation:** FinnGen is an external disease source while Q and M remain UKB sources. This supports outcome-source robustness, not a fully independent reproduction of the two sleep measurements. FinnGen releases overlap one another, and Sleep Chart already used those endpoints. No exact independent device GWAS and matching Q/M disease system is currently admitted. MVP clinical insomnia is a different sleep construct and cannot replicate this duration contrast. A narrower source-transport claim needs an explicit compatible validation design; the required validation has not yet been demonstrated.
5. **Substantive conclusion:** A calibrated nonzero Δ could establish that particular disease sharing is sensitive to these observed source/construct definitions. It would not prove biological mechanisms, quantify measurement error, identify true sleep or distinguish modality from participation/time. A tightly bounded near-zero Δ could support limited robustness, not equivalence of measurement systems. These conclusions would sharpen published parallel estimates, but importance beyond an incremental established-method application remains unsupported.

Result-independent stopping rule: do not admit this as the integrated study's primary discovery if any mandatory source, covariance/calibration, prospective precision, construct or compatible-validation gate fails. Do not add outcomes, latent factors or local/molecular analyses to rescue its novelty. If the gates pass, it can become a transparently retrospective-motivated, prospectively frozen secondary or narrower primary contrast application; that still requires an independent judgment that the conclusion is important enough for the publication objective. This review neither authorizes a novelty claim nor treats standard covariance correction as a new method.
