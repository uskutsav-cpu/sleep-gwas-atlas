# Bounded result-level statistical review

Reviewed 10 October 2026. This is an independent automated reading of actual reported results, not human investigator approval. Scope is the original 190-analysis result set (158 standalone h² and 1,637 r_g records), the 62 sensitivity results, their complete correction/classification tables and existing figure inputs. No estimator, source preprocessing or controller was run or inspected; no GWAS body, source download or new infrastructure was used.

**Conclusion:** the observed tables support a qualified resource description of source-defined common-variant sharing and diagnostic robustness. I found no materially sized scalar arithmetic or BH inconsistency in the inspected results. One substantive reporting error was found and corrected: the validation table has one device-derived midpoint result, although it has zero device-duration results. The unperformed duration-contrast decision is unchanged. Existing source-quality and uncertainty limitations continue to prevent new discovery, calibrated difference/heterogeneity, causal, clinical-equivalence or fully independent replication claims.

**No new scientific evidence beyond the already reported results was found.** The observations below are checks, descriptions and qualifications of existing estimates. The newly noticed device-midpoint row is an existing historical result, not a new association analysis or independent validation.

## What the actual results support

The three correlation tables contain 396 core, 1,200 extension and 41 external estimates. All 1,637 reported estimates, SEs, Z scores, P values and interval bounds are finite, and every SE is positive. Reported Z equals estimate/SE on binary64 parsing. Re-evaluating the two-sided normal tail from the reported Z gives maximum absolute P differences of 4.39×10⁻¹⁵, 4.44×10⁻¹⁵ and 3.50×10⁻¹⁵ for core, extension and validation, respectively. These are negligible numerical differences, not estimator failures. All 158 standalone heritability records have finite scalar results and positive SEs; the stored heritability Z and scale/SE relationships reconcile.

The native correlation tables' intervals use the normal critical value 1.959963984540054. Their bounds reproduce exactly from the stored estimate and SE. The sensitivity table uses the conventional rounded multiplier 1.96. This produces at most about 4.5×10⁻⁶ difference in a bound if the conventions are interchanged; it does not materially change the displayed conclusions. “Marginal normal 95% interval” is accurate. Neither convention provides simultaneous family coverage, a confidence interval for a difference between related estimates, or demonstrated coverage under an unstable heritability denominator.

Recalculating BH values from each complete stored P family gives exact agreement with the stored native q values. Core retains 153 primary-tier and eight excluded sensitivity-tier positives; extension retains 603 positives. All stored `fdr_boundary_status_changed` flags are false. These are nominal BH labels from the frozen historical screens, not evidence that the empirical false-positive rate, source validity or independence of the findings has been established.

The clearest quantitative illustrations remain insomnia–frailty, insomnia–MDD and coded apnoea–BMI: precise positive source-defined correlations, with large separation from zero under the reported within-fit model. Insomnia–BMI and the GI examples further illustrate heterogeneous sharing. Their evidentiary strength is for the analysed constructs and sources. None identifies a causal pathway or clinical equivalence, and differences between their point estimates were not tested jointly.

## Publication-relevant qualifications and internal issues

**Device coverage correction.** `validation_native_full_precision_rg_v4.tsv` line 42 is `sleep_timing`–`finngen_r13_K11_REFLUX`: r_g −0.055389180346499096, SE 0.059997565029310175, P 0.35590795356773663, CI −0.17298224696404688–0.062203886271048676. The `sleep_timing` row in the completed sleep-source dictionary identifies this as rank-normalized Jones device SPT midpoint. Thus the 41 estimates include six questionnaire constructs, coded apnoea and device midpoint, not eight questionnaire constructs and not zero device traits. The blanket zero-device sentence and C47 binding were corrected to **zero device-duration estimates**, retaining the exact one-midpoint/zero-duration counts. The finite duration feasibility table still has absent device-duration estimates for both FinnGen reflux and COPD. No inference, power or new contrast was supplied by this correction.

**Excluded positives must remain excluded.** All eight FDR-positive core sensitivity-tier results are T2D rows, with `original_interpretation_status=EXCLUDED_FROM_PRIMARY_INFERENCE`. T2D's standalone intercept is 1.315324964600841 (`core_native_full_precision_h2_v4.tsv`, `trait_id=t2d`). They cannot be folded into “161 primary associations” or promoted because their marginal P values are small. The 12 melanoma pair estimates remain excluded and none is a core BH positive. Melanoma's standalone heritability Z is 3.568187736137023; the reported liability-scale h² is 3.730992444540936. MS's corresponding h² is 5.982278329852824 despite Z 5.002904722174046. Those above-one presentations are not valid population variance fractions. N/scale diagnostics preserve the signal-to-SE ratio and do not certify a population prevalence.

**Standalone and bivariate diagnostics differ.** Core has 15 pair-specific heritability-Z failures: 12 melanoma pairs and three ovarian-cancer pairs (`insomnia`, `napping`, `snoring`; core rg lines 34, 232 and 265). The ovarian outcome's pair-specific Z is 3.97817, 3.98830 and 3.99664 in those rows. None is BH positive. Their imprecise estimates cannot support biological negatives. There are also 48 core pair-intercept failures: the 36 lipid fits and 12 T2D fits. Twenty nominal primary BH positives have a pair-intercept failure. These flags are not a new correction family or grounds for silently changing the frozen standalone-source tiers; they are reasons to preserve diagnostic qualifications.

Four external cholelithiasis fits have pair outcome intercepts above 1.2, although the historical standalone source gate passed: external rg lines 9, 17, 25 and 30. The snoring row (line 30) is a qualified external positive, r_g 0.13857126657655658, SE 0.024034653215880174, P 8.142667936737145×10⁻⁹, pair outcome intercept 1.210460535344209. The other three are directional-below-threshold results. “Qualified positive” therefore denotes the frozen standalone/source/family criterion, not certification that every bivariate component passes the intercept rule. Keep that distinction explicit; do not invent retrospective exclusions or present all 23 as unrestricted replication.

**External evidence has a bounded meaning.** The complete `reviews/independent_whole_validation_classification217_v4.tsv` supports 23 qualified, 18 directional, 17 QC-ineligible and 159 unavailable pairs. Joining its 17 QC rows by `replication_source_id` to `validation_native_full_precision_h2_v4.tsv` `trait_id`/`original_qc_reason` gives nine heritability-signal and eight intercept exclusions. The correction is 0.05/217, not BH over the 41 observed estimates. All 41 reuse the sleep source; no fully independent two-trait replication is established. All-direction concordance is selected and correlated evidence, not 41 independent Bernoulli replications. Unavailable, low-signal and intercept-excluded pairs are not numerical null tests.

**Covariance-zero displays remain diagnostic only.** The existing figure inputs `tables/shared_sleep_cov0_diagnostic41_v4_1.tsv` explicitly set covariance to zero while `sampling_covariance_established=False`, `confirmed_effect_difference=False` and `shared_sleep_source=True`. For example, its insomnia–abdominal-pain difference is −0.14923614001304264, with a conditional covariance-zero P of 0.002279756435983974. That P cannot establish cohort heterogeneity, attenuation, measurement effects or winner's curse. Retaining the two supplemental diagnostic pages with these limitations does not turn them into calibrated inference.

## What the 62 sensitivities establish

The actual sensitivity table has 60 correlation rows and two standalone h² rows. All 60 have `difference_P_value=NULL`, `cross_fit_delete_alignment_certified=False` and `new_BH=False`. Scalar Z/P relationships are coherent. No between-fit statistical difference or new multiplicity screen is available.

For the 36 lipid fits, both pair intercepts meet 1.2 in 0 baseline and 35 sensitivity fits, while both heritability Z values meet four in all 36 throughout. The remaining apnoea–HDL outcome intercept is 1.2066970994564596. The largest absolute correlation change is 0.029183837309218363 (device midpoint–HDL; sensitivity table line 58). This shows regression-specification dependence in diagnostics and generally smaller absolute point changes, not harmless inflation or corrected source validity.

Device-duration–HDL changes from −0.049704881043005175 (CI −0.09545123597531417 to −0.003958526110696178) to −0.02843833846370568 (−0.06429927733983187 to 0.007422600412420516), line 53. Its marginal sensitivity interval includes zero. Insomnia–HDL remains negative at −0.11754337908154319, SE 0.01622814370845609 (`lipid_two_step_insomnia`,`hdl`). Neither observation tests whether the two fits differ. The most defensible robustness claim is descriptive persistence or sensitivity of individual estimates, with the remaining source flags attached.

For the 24 binary-outcome correlations, maximum absolute point and SE changes are 5.023759186428833×10⁻¹⁵ and 2.5396351688300456×10⁻¹⁵. The two standalone diagnostic rows (lines 62–63) change h² scale but retain MS Z about 5.00290 and melanoma Z about 3.56819. `independent_sensitivity_liability_presentations_v4_6.tsv` supplies the ten controlled presentations. The observed numerical invariance is specific to these source streams and conventions; it does not validate every binary GWAS N or a population liability interpretation, and it does not rescue melanoma eligibility.
