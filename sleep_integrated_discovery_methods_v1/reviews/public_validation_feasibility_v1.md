# Public sleep data and independent validation feasibility

Review date: 2026-10-10. Scope: the new integrated sleep measurement/ascertainment workstream; Atlas V4 is preserved. This finite check reuses exact source identities and clinical/cohort audits and updates public primary documentation. It adds no GWAS bodies, fits, simulations, participant contact, restricted access requests or pair outcomes. The companion source and design matrices distinguish public availability, recovered bodies, necessary estimator precision and scientific source admission.

**Decision: NO_GO_CURRENT for a new independently validated subjective/clinical/device primary.** No publicly accessible, sufficiently documented cohort-disjoint full-summary device sleep GWAS was established in this bounded check. No presently completed result is independent both-trait replication. A smaller observed-construct robustness question is identifiable in principle, but its new primary source and statistical contracts are not met. Public access and successful literal replay do not change this decision.

## Finite source choice

The single narrower candidate is UKB questionnaire sleep duration (Dashti 2019) versus UKB accelerometer SPT-window sleep duration (Jones 2019), with **exact FinnGen R13 K11_REFLUX and J10_COPD** as the two outcome sources. These are a candidate clinical family for review, not an admitted testing family or a biological finding. No substitution of releases, trait thresholds or populations is allowed. Choosing them after historical results have been seen cannot be represented as prospective independence; the biological rationale/novelty review must justify their inclusion independently of P values and retain all tests in any frozen family.

The questionnaire is hours of sleep in 24 h, whereas the device column `ACC_SLEEP_DUR_RAW_SIN` is a rank-normalized inactivity-based sleep duration within a main sleep-period window. The exact deposited source columns, transformations and inclusion counts matter. Neither is a clinical insomnia diagnosis. The public publications and original header metadata support these distinct observed definitions. [Dashti et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08917-4), [Jones et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09576-1)

For each fixed disease D, the candidate estimand is Δ_D = r_g(Q,D) − r_g(M,D), where Q and M denote those literal GWAS constructs and populations. It is a source/construct contrast. The device participants form a selected UKB subset assessed at a different time; the source design does not separate measurement modality, quiet-wake error, age/time, participation or clinical ascertainment causes. There is no latent true-sleep or reliability parameter identified by these public moments. Use established joint-moment inference for Δ, with full sampling covariance and denominator uncertainty, as set out in the separate methodological novelty review. [Genomic SEM](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-019-0566-x), [cross-trait LDSC](https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.3406)

The bounded matrix contains five main sleep source entries, a GERA clinical alternative, two public-release boundary entries, one external-device follow-up boundary, two fixed disease endpoints and one already used MVP disease route. Jones sleep efficiency is recorded with device duration in one source entry, but is outside this candidate's primary contrast family. Portas 2026 is an optional same-UKB algorithm/construct alternative, not a device replication source. Existing continuous MVP duration research and public Yale short/long files do not fill the independent device cell.

## Clinical alternatives and independent-validation boundary

FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA has a completed observed-scale h² diagnostic: 0.0535357591 (SE 0.0040705199, Z 13.1521), intercept 1.077872499, using explicitly assumed constant N_eff 185146.703773 and 1,156,359 retained SNPs. This is actual completed precision evidence, not the earlier failed acquisition attempt or a source-admission certificate. INFO/per-variant N, clinical interpretation, LD/reference adequacy and overlap remain unresolved. It creates no new r_g or independent replication. Its F51.0/G47.0 recorded-insomnia construct is a related clinical transport source; it is not chronic-insomnia-disorder equivalence to UKB complaints. The preserved V4 protocol/actual scientific review receipt are the evidence for these numerical facts; the [exact R13 endpoint](https://r13.risteys.finngen.fi/endpoints/F5_INSOMNIA) supplies the underlying phenotype rules.

MVP clinical insomnia `GCST90475826` is the EUR GIA Phe_327_4 source (78,566 cases, 329,572 controls), rather than the different HARE stratum. It changes the named sleep cohort relative to original UKB discovery, and FinnGen R13 changes the named disease cohort relative to Pan-UKB. All four original-to-new cohort-source intersections are source-distinct, but individual/relative intersections remain unverified. The FinnGen outcomes were already used in the original validation wave. Accession-specific CI/effect/P semantics, source QC and precision still block new confirmatory inference; this is possible related-construct transport, not automatic independent replication. ## Eligible claim and exact unmet contract

At present, eligible reporting is the completed qualified atlas/reproduction evidence, source availability/feasibility, and the explicit absence of an admitted independent replication. No new primary inference is admitted by this review. If all gates later pass, Δ_D could support: “The estimated genetic correlations of the declared questionnaire-duration and device SPT-duration constructs with a fixed disease source differ/do not differ at the specified precision under the stated model.” It would not identify why, validate a latent measure, prove clinical interchangeability, infer causality, or establish absence from a nonsignificant result.

Before a new contrast, the finite data contract is: exact source bodies and new derivatives; release-specific phenotype/control/event/observation rules; source-specific effect/SE/P, INFO and N decisions; declared reference/build/SNP compatibility and Finnish LD suitability; an explicitly qualified individual/relative overlap model; jointly valid S/V or genuinely common genomic deletions; nonlinear denominator propagation and frozen-tail calibration; minimum meaningful Δ with uncertainty/power planned without choosing P values; one bounded family retaining failures; and rights consistent with research reuse. h² Z≥4 alone is neither joint coverage nor contrast power. A difference must use Var(Δ)=Var(r_QD)+Var(r_MD)−2Cov(r_QD,r_MD), including shared outcomes, shared UKB participants and estimated denominators. The old covariance-zero diagnostics do not supply that missing covariance.

For independent replication, document discovery-sleep∩validation-sleep, discovery-sleep∩validation-outcome, discovery-outcome∩validation-sleep and discovery-outcome∩validation-outcome, together with within-validation sleep/outcome overlap and prior-validation reuse. Distinct cohort names are source-level evidence only. Source changing on both sides can admit a related-phenotype transport test under assumptions; it does not certify person-level independence. Independent clinical validation would validate that clinical transport estimand only, not the device-versus-questionnaire contrast.

Exact human review needs are finite: a sleep measurement/clinical reviewer for the related observed constructs and meaningful Δ; a FinnGen clinical/source reviewer for each R13 endpoint's rules and INFO/N; a GERA source reviewer for accession linkage/control handling/build; an MVP source statistician for accession-specific CI/effect/test semantics; and a cohort epidemiologist for the four cross-stage intersections/relatives/previous-wave reuse. This review sends no questions or contacts. The preserved R9 apnea ICD9 3472/3472A anomaly remains unresolved; R9 apnea is excluded from the proposed duration family and cannot be a pure-OSA or independent R13 replication anchor.

This bounded source recheck does not prove that no external device GWAS exists worldwide. It establishes that none of the inspected public sources presently meets this study's finite independent-validation contract. Additional arbitrary traits or a GERA precision-only experiment would not close the decisive integrated-design gap.
