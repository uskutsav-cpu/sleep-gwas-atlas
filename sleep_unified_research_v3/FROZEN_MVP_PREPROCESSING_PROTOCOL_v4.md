# MVP GIA insomnia preprocessing protocol v4

Scope: a new, result-free materialization of GCST90475826 clinical insomnia. This is not exact replication of the UKB symptom phenotype. It admits no h2, correlation, local or molecular test and selects no primary scientific question. The historical families, thresholds and all sealed v1/v2 outputs remain unchanged.

Input is the acquired 575,504,178-byte GRCh38 European GIA source, SHA256 46b62349ba0d98b22bf302be973bb54a80707cfb02ce953426f8224910761835. Source definition is bound to the v2 definition contract and amendment. Cases 78,566, controls 329,572, total 408,138; every row must match. N_eff = 4/(1/cases+1/controls). Unexpected rows, header, nonmissing supplied SE, or changed input hashes fail the materialization, with evidence preserved.

Use the frozen code-47 rules: MAF strictly > .01, R2 strictly > .9 (no new upper bound), valid OR/CI/P, nonambiguous canonical alleles, nonambiguous HapMap3 rsIDs, GRCh37 extended MHC exclusion inclusive chr6:25,000,000–34,000,000, allele orientation to pinned A1/A2 and historical-rule Z = log(OR)/[(log(CI_upper)-log(CI_lower))/(2*1.959963984540054)]. This assumes 95% log-scale Wald intervals; accession-specific confirmation remains pending. No P/CI discrepancy filter is permitted.

Prospective additions for this new source: reject rows with multiple distinct nonambiguous HM3 rsID matches. Require unique GRCh38 one-based point mapping through the pinned hg38ToHg19 chain and exact GRCh37 HM3 chromosome/BP agreement. On negative-chain mapping complement both alleles; apply sign only from effect-allele alignment, with no extra chain sign flip. Apply MHC exclusion on the final GRCh37 coordinates. Retain the first eligible occurrence of a duplicate rsID after mapping/filtering; count all later eligible duplicates. These additions do not replace the original method retrospectively.

Record whole-file OR inside-CI and log-CI centering diagnostics, literal missing versus finite/nonfinite SE, R2 range and R2 > 1, plus P-versus-CI-Z descriptive buckets on retained eligible rows. Centering uses absolute discrepancy in SE units, with descriptive cut points .001/.01/.05/.1/1/10; no diagnostic selects rows. Reconcile source rows = mutually exclusive exclusion counts + output rows. Require at least 700,000 retained HM3 rows; failure retains the partial/final source-derived output without admission.

Resource plan: one stdlib worker, no network, SSD temporary and output namespace only. Launch requires >=512 MiB internal and >=5 GiB SSD free. Stop at observed worker RSS >768 MiB, internal free <128 MiB, elapsed >3,600 seconds or combined new output/temporary bytes >128 MiB. Poll every two seconds; RSS is observed, not guaranteed instantaneous peak. The full native LDSC guard remains >=3 GiB internal free. Freeze this document, code and dependency hashes before worker launch; verify dependency hashes afterward, complete gzip stream CRC, and final output hashes. Original inputs are read only.

Success means only geometric/allelic concordance and reproducible historical-rule preprocessing. It does not establish biological allele semantics, independent uncertainty calibration, h2, power, participant disjointness or an independent replicated association. Null and failed outputs remain available. Independent methods and numerical reviewers must examine the receipt before any downstream admission.
