# Result-free MVP preprocessing methods review v3

**Verdict: admissible for the v4 preprocessing scope only.** No necessary implementation fix remains in the reviewed worker and runner. A successful run would establish reproducible historical-rule materialization and concordance with the pinned locus/allele references. It would not pass source h2/intercept, uncertainty calibration, power, participant-overlap or pair-admission gates. This is a technical scientific review, not manuscript prose.

The review read bounded local code, protocols and existing diagnostic receipts; it did not read the raw GWAS body, rehash the real reference bodies, execute native preprocessing, estimate h2, inspect a new pair outcome, or edit sealed v1/v2 evidence. Exact reviewed-file hashes and compact independent diagnostics are in the companion JSON.

## Source and method admissibility

The v2 definition contract, lines 3–12 and 37–55, documents the exact EUR GIA accession GCST90475826 / pha010373.1 and the clinical coding rule. Its lines 57–64, 86–93 and 113 retain unresolved executable phenotyping, allele and overlap limits. It supports clinical-insomnia transport, without exact equivalence to UKB frequent self-report insomnia. All six files bound by the v2 protocol-amendment receipt independently match their hashes.

The v1 acquisition receipt, lines 4–9 and 23, pins 575,504,178 bytes, upstream MD5 `2e9c12624b653aac527444fc426e037b` and SHA-256 `46b62349ba0d98b22bf302be973bb54a80707cfb02ce953426f8224910761835`. The schema receipt, lines 32–64, reports 19,703,815 rows, no finite supplied SE, constant per-row cases 78,566 / controls 329,572 and constant total 408,138. Its acquisition and diagnostic-code hashes also independently match. Direct arithmetic gives N_eff = 253768.6150468714; reciprocal evaluation gives 253768.61504687142, a floating-point evaluation-order difference. The worker uses the latter and the inherited 12-significant-digit output `253768.615047`.

Historical code 47, lines 248–270, supplies the frozen CI rule, strict MAF > .01 / R2 > .9, physical P validity, nonambiguous allele orientation and 12-significant-digit Z/N serialization. The new worker, lines 70–106, preserves those rules. R2 > 1 is counted, without a new upper-bound filter; the inherited schema reports a maximum of 1.113. P/CI differences and CI centering are descriptive and do not select rows.

The CI construction remains an assumption: Z = log(OR) / { [log(CI_upper) − log(CI_lower)] / [2 × 1.959963984540054] } represents a 95% Wald interval on the log-OR scale. A positive OR and ordered positive CI alone do not establish that interpretation or log-center consistency. GWAS-SSF v1.0.2 defines the CI fields generically, without specifying confidence level or construction. The worker now records OR outside its CI, log-CI centering in SE units and retained P/CI discrepancies; it does not remove rows using those diagnostics. These summaries can identify a source-level methods problem, but cannot independently prove the upstream SE or EBI conversion. [Official GWAS-SSF specification](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gwas-catalog/summary-statistics-standard/main/gwas-ssf_v1.0.2.tex).

## Prospective identity and operational gates

Worker lines 82–90 reject multiple distinct HM3 rsID matches, require a unique mapped locus and exact chromosome/BP agreement with the pinned GRCh37 HM3 reference, and apply inclusive chr6:25,000,000–34,000,000 MHC exclusion on those final coordinates. The helper's lines 62–82 correctly convert a one-based source point into zero-based chain offsets and back; negative-strand coordinates subtract from chromosome size. Worker lines 96–106 complement both alleles for a negative chain, then change Z sign only on tested-allele alignment to A1/A2. A negative chain does not introduce an additional sign reversal. [Official UCSC chain format](https://genome.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/help/chain.html).

Ten synthetic coordinate cases pass exactly, including both block ends, unmapped points, reverse-strand endpoints, different-target ambiguity, invalid coordinates and nonautosomal targets. Three independently constructed Wald-CI cases reproduce known Z values within relative/absolute 1e-14. Six isolated pure historical-orientation cases give the expected same/swapped/complemented signs or reject ambiguous/mismatched alleles. These checks exercise local implementation arithmetic; they are not real-source mapping validation or independent upstream allele certification. Multiple chain records mapping to the same locus and strand collapse to one geometric hit; uniqueness therefore means a unique mapped locus/strand, not a unique chain ID.

The first eligible occurrence of an rsID is retained and later eligible duplicates counted (worker lines 102–107); this is the explicitly frozen code-47 policy, not exclusion of every occurrence of a duplicated rsID. The threshold of 700,000 output rows is inherited. Source cardinality and mutually exclusive exclusion counts must reconcile before a pass (lines 109–126). Full-stream gzip CRC and output hashes are part of the receipt. Missing finite SE and per-row sample-count changes are hard source gates.

Three diagnostic/contract issues were corrected before this verdict: the worker now requires the exact pinned source header (lines 55–57) and recognizes `#NA` as literal missing SE and fails on unexpected nonmissing invalid/nonfinite supplied SE (lines 65–69). The runner freezes input/code/protocol hashes, checks the four registered source/reference hashes, binds the plan hash and requires both plan stability and the worker's plan binding for success (lines 34–72 and 97–111). It launches one worker with SSD outputs, no network and a bounded resource monitor. Its 512 MiB preprocessing guard is specific to this streaming task; the full native 3 GiB internal guard remains in force. Two-second RSS polling is an observed maximum, not proof of instantaneous peak RSS.

## Receipt review required before downstream use

The execution receipt must match the reviewed code/protocol hashes, preserve all dependency hashes, reconcile 19,703,815 source rows, establish output uniqueness/allele/locus agreement, and provide all exclusions and diagnostics. CI centering, P/CI agreement, R2 > 1 and eligible duplicate counts must be interpreted at the source level without favorable filtering. Large unexplained discrepancies require methods clarification, not selection of favorable variants or a changed threshold. The final receipt remains pending at this prelaunch review.

The public-definition linkage and geometric concordance do not reconstruct the historical phenotyping implementation, resolve the accession MAC<30 versus final-method MAC>40 discrepancy, or certify zero participant overlap. Source/outcome h2 Z >= 4 and intercept <= 1.2, prospective power, the full native resource guard and every locked-family admission requirement remain pending. No new test, significance threshold, phenotype replacement or independent association is admitted by this report.
