Why lambda cannot clear admission
Genomic inflation lambda is a global GWAS distribution diagnostic, not an LD-score regression intercept. It does not supply the relationship of statistics to LD scores, SNP-h2, its uncertainty, the required h2 Z statistic or a correlation sampling variance. Published LDSC methods distinguish contributions of polygenicity and confounding rather than equating genomic control with the intercept (Bulik-Sullivan et al., Nature Genetics47,291-295,2015; https://www.nature.com/articles/ng.3211).
The additive annotation0.964 is slightly below1. It cannot certify absence of confounding, genetic signal, a positive/zero h2, adequate correlation precision or a failed power gate. No conversion from lambda to an h2 estimate/SE/intercept is justified by this figure. The other inheritance-model lambdas are diagnostics for those different tests; they cannot substitute for an additive source assessment. A near-null QQ distribution is compatible with several signal, information and calibration conditions. Lack of an obviously extreme plotted locus is not a genetic-correlation power calculation.
A future source-only observed-scale h2 feasibility analysis, if separately admitted after complete-source QC, must use the fixed necessary Z>=4 and intercept<=1.2 gates. It would still not establish adequate precision for a clinically meaningful pair or contrast, clinical equivalence, zero sample intersections or a fully independent replication. Population liability scale requires a compatible cited prevalence/ascertainment convention; none is added here.
