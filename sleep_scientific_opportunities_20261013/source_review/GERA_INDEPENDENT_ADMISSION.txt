Decision
GO for bounded public metadata/phenotype/analysis-contract review. Future full-source preprocessing is scientifically defensible only after its explicit assembly/allele/N rules and resource plan are admitted. NO_GO now for a native fit or confirmatory result: source-wide QC, insomnia signal/precision, exact cohort intersections and a fixed inference family remain unresolved. Current df inspection reported1,229,295,616 available internal bytes (~1.145GiB), below the existing3GiB launch guard; SSD capacity does not waive it. No fit, GWAS-body download, production-panel change, manuscript change or private participant-key search was performed. This is an independent judgment, not a new discovery or replication result.

What changed
The independently reviewed official harmonised route is587,496,274bytes, MD5 66e3c3b915b65006ab2027c8e2279f79; its metadata explicitly declares GRCh38,1-based and the exact accession. That is a materially better-defined delivery contract than the older2,019,290,168-byte raw route with an empty assembly field. It is new to this project, not a newly released study. The reported checksum has not been verified against a complete local body. Header presence and upstream counts are not downstream QC admission. [S5,S6]

Phenotype and transport
The source is a GERA ICD-9-coded INSOMNIA endpoint. The dbGaP code document lists307.41,307.42,327.0,327.00,327.01,327.02,327.09 and780.52. Its code list mixes transient/persistent and unspecified or condition-related insomnia categories. It does not certify chronic primary insomnia, DSM/ICSD disorder, symptom duration/severity or a sleep-laboratory diagnosis. The Catalog/portal label "Frequent insomnia symptoms" cannot replace the primary code definition. Qualifying event multiplicity, observation window and control exclusion rules remain undocumented in the inspected materials. Controls without a recorded diagnosis must not be described as symptom-free or healthy sleepers. [S3,S5,S6,S8]
Historical UKB frequent trouble falling/staying asleep and this coded endpoint have different ascertainment. A future comparison could study transport of source-defined common-variant sharing from questionnaire complaints to coded insomnia in insured GERA participants. It could not isolate a pure measurement effect: age, healthcare contact, ancestry/reference adequacy, selection, coding and measurement change together. Population-wide or cross-ancestry transport is not established. Positive or negative coefficients would pertain to the exact code-defined estimand, not all insomnia.

Effect and SE lineage
The primary analysis used additive logistic regression with age, sex and seven principal components, alongside four separately reported inheritance models. Source imputation chose the best-INFO reference-panel result, rather than the most significant inheritance model. [S1]
The provider README explicitly defines alleleB as effect allele, frequentist_add_beta_1 and frequentist_add_se_1 as additive beta and SE, all_total as sample size, and separate chromosome-X columns. The exact Catalog record names the additive model; bounded raw and harmonised headers supply beta/standard_error directly. This is a supplied additive log-odds coefficient/SE route, rather than a CI-derived uncertainty route. These linked statements justify further lineage review, not yet whole-file certification. [S4,S6]
Before preprocessing, freeze the additive columns and signed beta/SE statistic; do not choose the best model or signed P by effect strength. Verify full-file finite beta, strictly positive finite SE, alleles, frequency semantics and hm_code disposition. Source all_maf is not an oriented effect-allele frequency. A difference between beta/SE normal-tail P and source P is a diagnostic requiring test documentation, not permission to replace supplied SE. Harmonisation may change effect orientation; allele/Beta/frequency transformations and accepted harmonisation codes require an explicit upstream-version rule. Do not infer those rules from a header or aggregate log.

N and QC
Publisher SupplementaryData1, '1. GERA samples'!A16:C16, literally reports Insomnia3972cases and52665controls after QC. Total N is56637, case fraction0.07013083320091107. Single-cohort design arithmetic gives N_eff=14773.761322103925, or0.260849997742 of total N. This is an imbalance calculation, not observed power, per-SNP information or an imputed sample size. [S2]
Although all_total is present and documented as sample size, its distribution, per-variant case/control missingness and effective-N interpretation have not been audited. Do not treat all_total automatically as N_eff, or assume per-variant case fraction is constant. Any design-N approximation must be declared and contrasted with source-specific per-variant information when available. Sample case fraction is not population prevalence; no liability conversion is admitted without a compatible cited prevalence/ascertainment convention.
The primary source filtered INFO<0.7 and MAF<0.001 and used explicit sample/variant QC. These source thresholds do not certify the project's common-SNP/INFO, HapMap3 or LDSC gates. Relatedness removal within GERA does not prove disjointness from external cohorts. [S1]
The harmonised GRCh38 file cannot silently enter a GRCh37 workflow or inherit GRCh37 MHC coordinates. Freeze a compatible reference/build disposition before preprocessing; do not repair an assembly mismatch opportunistically. Report every allele, harmonisation, MAF, INFO, N and reference-identity exclusion. Source15.5million-site counts do not determine the retained common-SNP universe. No threshold relaxation to achieve eligibility is justified.

Published heritability and power evidence
I inspected the primary article XML main body, all10 publisher workbook sheets and the supplementary-file descriptions. No insomnia-specific SNP h2, h2 SE/Z, LDSC intercept, genetic-correlation SE or pair-level power was identified. The workbook's power comparisons concern T2D and macular degeneration; they do not quantify insomnia or correlation power. The main article's selected additive/recessive locus calculations also cannot supply that missing evidence. [S1,S2,S7]
This is a bounded 'not found' finding, not a claim that no published estimate exists anywhere. The large supplementary-information PDF exceeded the10MB fetch cap and was not downloaded or exhaustively inspected. Generic twin heritability, UKB/FinnGen insomnia h2 or another disease's power must not fill the gap. The absence of an insomnia-specific genome-wide significant row is not a power calculation or evidence of no genetic component.
No native h2 or correlation is admitted now. If full-source QC and resources later clear, a source-wide, result-free prespecified observed-scale h2 feasibility check would be the first scientific estimator step; it must retain the fixed Z>=4/intercept<=1.2 necessary gate and distinguish signal failure from confounding. Passing is necessary for the proposed correlation workflow, not sufficient for meaningful precision, clinical transport or independence. A failing/uncertain source is ineligible, not a biological null. Any subsequent pair-power/precision target must use a prespecified meaningful effect/difference and the actual eligible source uncertainties and joint covariance, not counts alone.

Inference and family boundaries
GCST90086097 is a GERA-only additive data product in the exact Catalog record. UKB/FinnGen follow-up elsewhere in the paper does not make this accession pooled. Nevertheless, a cohort label or geographic recruitment contrast is not a zero-overlap certificate. Account for participant and relative intersections with the exact original UKB source and every proposed outcome release using public cohort metadata first; unresolved intersections stay explicit. [S6]
Replacing only the sleep source while reusing a historical outcome would supply sleep-source transport/robustness, not fully independent two-trait replication. Reused outcomes also induce dependence between compared correlations even if sleep cohorts are disjoint; marginal SEs alone cannot test a questionnaire-versus-clinical difference. A two-source external design would still require documented overlap and compatible phenotype contracts. Any new tests need a prospectively fixed source/estimand/family; the historical396/1200 BH and217-candidate denominator remain unchanged. No positive or negative association claim can precede those gates.
The MVP abdominal-pain outcome accession GCST90479148 (phecode785) retains its existing CI/SE-authenticity hold covering five analyses and the exploratory fit. The MVP clinical-insomnia candidate is GCST90475826. Neither GERA availability nor reuse of an MVP outcome repairs the abdominal-pain outcome hold. All automated reviewers remain AI assistance and are not accountable human scientific approval or evidence of three independent provider families.

Finite next step and exact unresolved questions
Metadata-only work is justified now: bind the additive beta/SE mapping to the harmonisation-version documentation; establish the exact ICD-event/control/window contract; document all_total/case-control semantics; and declare the GRCh38-to-reference disposition. Keep the body unacquired and do not launch preprocessing or native estimation under the present guard. Once resources and the frozen source contract permit, a bounded full-source preprocessing review may assess checksum/EOF, finite-statistic, allele/N/INFO and reference retention without selecting disease pairs by their coefficients.
Publicly answerable questions: (1) What event count/window and control exclusions define INSOMNIA? (2) Does all_total equal per-variant analysed subjects, and are variant-specific cases/controls available? (3) Which documented hm_codes and Beta/allele transformations are admissible for this release? (4) Is an exact published insomnia SNP-h2/SE/intercept estimate available outside the inspected materials? (5) What exact cohort/release intersections are documented for a fixed future comparison? Human source-owner clarification is needed only for specific fields that remain unavailable after those public checks; no private key is presumed necessary.
