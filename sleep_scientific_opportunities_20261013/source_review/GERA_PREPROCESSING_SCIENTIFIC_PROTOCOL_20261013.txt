Why a source-specific adapter is necessary
The old MVP19 pathway targets GRCh38, derives uncertainty/Z from CI and assigns effective N. The old fixed-source pipeline contains source-specific assumptions and rsID extraction. Neither can be reused unchanged here: GERA supplies additive beta/SE, documented total sample counts and position/allele identifiers. Copying those mappings would change the statistic, sample normalization or variant identity. Reuse the reviewed orientation function unchanged and the authenticated GRCh37/HM3 allele/reference inputs. A small input adapter implementing this protocol is justified; a new estimator/controller or inheritance-model search is not.

Statistic and observed-N estimand
The sole supplied statistic is signed Wald Z=beta/standard_error, with finite beta, finite strictly positive SE and finite resulting Z. Output orientation multiplies beta/Z by+1 or-1; SE and all_total are unchanged. Source P and the Wald tail probability are diagnostic only. No row is selected by P, absolute beta/Z, SE magnitude, statistical significance, P-versus-SE discordance or inheritance model. No source-P inverse-normal magnitude, CI-derived SE or uncertainty repair is allowed.
Use N_j=all_total under its documented per-variant total-analysed-subject convention, conditional on actual census. Accept only mathematically integer-valued finite source numbers in1..56637; an encoding such as56637.0 is integer-valued, while56637.1 is not. Determine integer-valuedness from the exact decimal source text, including exponent notation, without a floating-point rounding tolerance. Reject missing, fractional, zero/negative and above-cohort N with separate reasons; do not round, clip, fill or replace them. Preserve source N exactly as an integer in final data. Source-semantic anomalies are a source-census HOLD for independent review before h2.
This total-N convention can support qualified observed-scale h2 for the ascertained coded source sample under LDSC assumptions; it is not population liability h2 or a balanced-sample normalization. The design N_eff14773.761322103925 is distinct from total N56637 and is not assigned to rows. Per-SNP case/control composition remains unknown; substantial missingness therefore needs source ascertainment review. Sample case fraction is not population prevalence. No effective-N, prevalence, inferred case counts or power assumption is substituted.

Fixed record processing and identity
1. Keep each original physical row's bytes, including delimiters and its line terminator, available by immutable-file offset/length or exact payload. Parse the declared columns, not a regex rsID or whitespace-collapsed label. Malformed field counts are held/rejected with counts. Normalization of case for allele/chromosome comparison must not erase original bytes used for duplicate comparison.
2. Structural candidates require integer autosome1..22, positive integer1-based BP, two distinct single-base A/C/G/T alleles, and no A/T or C/G palindrome. ChrX/Y/MT/nonstandard contigs and indels are excluded. Preserve the historical GRCh37 MHC exclusion: chromosome6 with25,000,000<=BP<=34,000,000 is excluded. No boundary shift or alternate-build MHC interval is used.
3. Build a collision-preserving reference multimap (CHR,BP)->all reference rsIDs/allele pairs. Reference duplicate keys cannot be overwritten by the old dictionary loader. Byte-identical reference duplicates may be collapsed with counts only after exact equality of identity/coordinate/alleles is established; conflicting rsID/allele-list/coordinate reference groups are held, with no first/last-winner rule. Multiple distinct compatible rsIDs at one coordinate remain unresolved even if their alleles agree.
4. For each structurally valid source candidate, apply the unchanged reviewed orientation(effect,other,referenceA1,referenceA2). Direct/complement matches give+1; swap/complement-swap gives-1; incompatible alleles giveNone. Require exactly one distinct compatible reference rsID. No match is classified as outside-reference or allele mismatch as applicable. Multiple matches/tainted reference groups are held/excluded; no lexicographic or row-order rescue. Source rs_id_all remains a position/allele provenance key, not the output SNP identity. A documented source-ID coordinate disagreement is separately flagged and not repaired.
5. Group all uniquely mappable source rows by final SNP identity BEFORE INFO/MAF/N/beta/SE eligibility filtering. Thus, an ineligible or malformed-statistic counterpart cannot disappear and permit another differing row to win. Different original row bytes mapping to one SNP cause the COMPLETE group to be excluded as conflicting duplicates. Byte-identical full-row copies collapse to one representative only, with the extra copies counted. Digest equality alone is not byte-identity proof: compare the original payload if a digest is used. No group member is preferred by INFO, N, P, beta, SE or source order. Structural/reference-unmappable rows are separately dispositioned and cannot receive invented group identity.
6. For nonconflicting unique representatives, apply valid-field and strict source eligibility: finite INFO>0.9; finite all_maf in(0.01,0.5]; finite beta/strictly positive SE and Z; valid documented integer total N. INFO>1 still satisfies the declared numerical threshold but is specifically flagged for post-run source-range review; it is never capped or treated as automatically certified. Missing/invalid fields, failing INFO/MAF and invalid N are separate disposition categories. Source all_maf is MAF, not EAF. No source-P requirement silently replaces the Wald-Z contract.

Exact sample-N missingness algorithm
The quantile population is ONLY unique, nonconflicting, uniquely mapped autosomal representatives satisfying every preceding eligibility rule, including valid integer total N and the MHC exclusion, BEFORE the N-quantile cut. Identical-copy multiplicities do not weight this population. Source unmatched/ambiguous/conflicting or otherwise invalid/ineligible rows do not enter it. No P or Z-magnitude condition is used.
Let m be the number of these candidates and n[0]..n[m-1] their total N values sorted ascending. If m=0, HOLD with no native input. R7 interpolation uses u=0.9*(m-1), j=floor(u), g=u-j, and q90=(1-g)*n[j]+g*n[min(j+1,m-1)]. Threshold T=q90/1.5. Retain candidates with N_j>=T; equality passes. For m=1, q90=n[0]. Do not recompute q90 after this cut or change its population to raw rows, significant SNPs, duplicate copies or the eventual LD-score merge.
Exact deterministic arithmetic can use a=9*(m-1), j=a div10, r=a mod10, Q=(10-r)*n[j]+r*n[min(j+1,m-1)]. Then q90=Q/10, T=Q/15 and retain iff15*N_j>=Q. Record m,j,r,Q,q90,T, numbers below/equal/above threshold and retained N range. This removes floating-point ambiguity without changing R7. The cutoff is a total-N missingness rule, not N_eff scaling or a power gate.

Outputs, census and mandatory post-run review
Only after the previous steps may a final five-column native input be written: SNP,A1,A2,Z,N. A1/A2 are the authenticated reference pair, Z is the directly supplied beta/SE statistic signed to output A1, N is source total N. Use round-trip numerical representation; no generic P-to-Z munging or silent three-decimal reconstruction. Require one row per SNP, finite Z, valid N and complete reference allele compatibility. Existing authenticated EUR LD inputs and eventual native runtime remain separate dependencies.
The full census must reconcile every input physical row into retained or an explicit mutually exclusive terminal disposition. Duplicate copies and complete conflicting groups must be counted without losing their members. Also provide overlapping diagnostic counts where a rejected group had multiple problems; do not confuse those masks with the conservation ledger. Record source/header/checksum, structural/MHC exclusions, mapping orientation/mismatch/collision categories, INFO/MAF/N missingness and ranges, duplicate-copy/group counts, exact R7 parameters, final SNP/coordinate uniqueness and HM3/LD-reference intersections.
Classify INFO>1, noninteger/out-of-range N, source-ID/coordinate inconsistency, reference conflicts and unusually large or structured N/INFO/MAF/mapping loss for independent actual-output review BEFORE any h2 fit. A large outside-HM3 count from a dense source is not by itself a build mismatch; distinguish absent reference positions from allele conflicts at present positions. No arbitrary 'acceptable mismatch' fraction, expected retention percentage or missingness tolerance is invented here. Actual reviewer adjudication must concern source semantics/counts, precede estimates and remain explicit.
