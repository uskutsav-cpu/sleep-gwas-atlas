INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE SIX-ITEM MULTIROW UTILITY DRAFT
13 October 2026; no task execution

Decision: The draft is scientifically admissible for pre-registration of a narrow finite source-resolution experiment. I found no concrete current packet/gold correctness defect. It is not ready for execution or for broad scientific-interpretation, production-interface or human-utility claims. This decision concerns the six authored operations, not the amount of code written.

Actual independent checks
I reconstructed the disjoint factual tables from C's joined cards for all six tasks, including conventional source metadata, and checked common evidence equality. All six B/C factual comparisons pass. Twenty-two estimate/source-identity joins and 74 frozen source-row projections/hashes pass. I independently derived all twelve B/C gold answer objects from supplied facts and shared policy, including QC ratios, adjusted-family screening, sorted numerical recomposition, publication/sample-total role binding, and validation/discovery source reuse. There is zero exact overlap with the old 66 records and declared public human/example records. No private human key, complete HGA table reread, model call or native fit was used. Conventional bibliographic gold was checked against the supplied frozen candidate metadata, not certified by a new complete HGA-source audit.

D04's actual positive bibliographic candidate binds matching publications and sample totals in the same role orientation, so the current gold is supported. The builder tests PMID and N equality separately under alternate orientations. A future candidate could produce a false conjunction if each matches in a different orientation; bind the two tests in one consistent sleep/outcome orientation before any future extension. This is a future guard recommendation, not a detected error in these six present candidates.

Scientific value and limits
The substantive improvement is that the solver must choose among several real measurement/source candidates and combine independent numerical/source facts. D01 and D05 bind measurement and analysis roles; D02 synthesizes corrected-family, numerical QC and contract requirements; D03 recomposes and orders selected estimates; D04 distinguishes positive publication/count agreement from exact analysis identity; D06 calculates scale-specific QC ratios. Those operations are more informative than copying a single requested exact-key record and its stored qualification flag.

C is an entity-aligned factual prototype, not the unchanged production query. It retains every candidate and introduces no precomputed task-specific selection, sorted contrast, QC gate flag or independence decision. B carries the same factual content. Repeated metadata and explicit entity-role grouping are part of the representation intervention and should be reported as such.

Removing answer-key names does not remove every cue. Shared policy explicitly says joint covariance is not supplied and bibliographic agreement does not authenticate an exact source/estimator contract. Thus the negative D03/D04 interpretation components largely reproduce shared rules. Source-contract facts also support D02's exclusion decision. The harder new components are role binding, arithmetic and combined screening. Call the endpoint factual source-resolution and shared-rule admissibility, not unaided broad scientific reasoning. The historical source-direction and MVP uncertainty qualifications remain operative; no positive score would clear them.

Selection is purposive scenario construction after the earlier feasibility experiment. Seeded candidate order and no observed effect/P/q sorting are useful controls, but they do not establish random task sampling, author blinding to known data, or independent confirmatory utility validation. New pair keys do not make the already exposed source dictionary and policies independent information. Six tasks give no measured researcher utility, no population-level interface advantage, no independent-provider evidence and no raw-source validity certification.

Before any execution
Write and freeze a separate finite-operation estimand, strict and component scoring rules, model/settings registry, per-cell schedule, bounded resource plan and exact failure/retry/provenance policy. A new scorer must handle set-valued IDs, ordered arrays, nested reason maps and numeric tolerance faithfully; the previous flat-field scorer cannot simply be reused. Preserve complete packet/attempt evidence and improve provenance controls after the prior digest/truncation problems. Solver-supplied digests and self-declarations alone do not authenticate read access. Accountable human semantic gold adjudication remains pending and is distinct from this AI packet review.

Keep this design in DRAFT status with no models run. Any broader utility or interpretation claim needs a separately designed and independently adjudicated user-relevant instrument. No task, gold, old response, old frozen inventory or human instrument was altered by this review. No private gold values or protected source numeric rows are reproduced here.
