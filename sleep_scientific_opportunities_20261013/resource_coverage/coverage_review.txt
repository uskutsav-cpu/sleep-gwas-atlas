Scientific meaning and remaining limitations
B denotes related context, not exact source/release, phenotype/coding or estimator equivalence. The original SF5 categorical tails are not interchangeable with atlas continuous reported duration or actigraphy-derived duration. Legacy short/long titles alone cannot authenticate all source contracts. Some related disease candidates use qualified diagnoses or medication proxies, so even B does not imply identical case ascertainment or a directly transportable estimate. The new colorectal result is related categorical-duration/FinnGen context, not four newly discovered biological associations.

D is a dated finding within the declared complete original-table scope and fixed alias/clinical-code mapping. It is not exhaustive semantic ontology matching or a global literature-negative result. Relevant additional synonyms and subtypes may exist outside that mapping. A blank declared slot, an unresolved code or an unavailable applicable table requires E rather than invented absence. Explicitly excluded/compound outcomes require incompatibility adjudication rather than positive name coincidence. The audit corrections illustrate why unchanged alias-only tables should not be described as comprehensive scientific clearance.

The 354 count is not a count of significant or novel associations. It combines historical source/QC eligibility with bounded mapped-snapshot absence and excludes direction holds; it does not authenticate raw source uncertainty, causal interpretation, independent replication or source redistribution. The original four annotations were reused to reconstruct the union and checked for preservation, not comprehensively re-audited for their own phenotype semantics. State that limitation directly whenever these union totals are reported.
