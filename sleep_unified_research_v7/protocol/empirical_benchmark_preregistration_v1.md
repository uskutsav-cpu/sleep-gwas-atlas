# Preregistered empirical resource benchmark

**Protocol version:** 1.0
**Date frozen:** 2026-10-10
**Branch:** `research/resource-benchmark-v7-2026-10-10`
**Base commit:** `192965d6a730c3b62b3bdbf2e99a4772abeb8c23`
**Status:** Frozen before V7 comparator-result extraction and before any baseline/atlas-assisted task answers are generated.

## Question and hypothesis

The primary hypothesis is that adding the V5/V6 Sleep GWAS Atlas's source-linked definitions, fixed-family results, uncertainty qualifications, and validation-status records increases the proportion of sleep–phenome research questions answered completely and correctly, compared with the strongest accessible public-resource workflow plus the same original source documents.

This evaluates evidence interpretation, not biological novelty, causal inference, or human usability. If automated language-model agents are the only evaluators, the result will be labeled an **automated evidence-interpretation benchmark**, not an independent human-user study.

## Frozen populations and comparators

The V7 atlas population is every record in the preserved V4 full-precision core, extension, and external-validation tables: 396 + 1,200 + 41 = 1,637 correlation records. These families remain distinct. No row will be removed because of its effect, p-value, QC status, or match result.

Experiment A will evaluate these comparator snapshots when the relevant full result tables or direct exports are legitimately accessible:

1. Human GWAS ATLAS release 3, `v20191115`, including its released full pairwise genetic-correlation table and source metadata.
2. CTG-VL, only where an actual permitted export or documented query result is accessible without restricted credentials or new user registration.
3. Morrison et al. SLEEP supplementary genetic-correlation tables.
4. Goodman et al. Communications Biology supplementary genetic-correlation tables.
5. SleepChart 2026 and Fan 2026 tables if their complete relevant tables are already available in the project or through permitted official downloads.
6. LD Hub or another V6-identified comparator only if its relevant full table/export is actually accessible; historical description alone is not a comparator result.

For each comparator, the release date and completeness of the searched table are recorded. A row absent from a 2019 release is counted only as absent from that dated release; it is never evidence of biological novelty. Inaccessible or incomplete comparator data are classified as insufficient evidence, not as an atlas advantage.

## Experiment A: source-level match rules

The matching unit is one atlas correlation record × one comparator result set. Candidate label matches may be generated by normalized trait names and declared synonyms, but labels alone never determine the final class. Reviewers will verify the original sleep and outcome GWAS source, accession/release, phenotype measurement and ascertainment, cohort and ancestry, effect direction/scale, estimator/reference, sample frame, and intended estimand using original publications and official release metadata.

Each comparison receives exactly one class:

- **A — exact source/estimand match:** both GWAS sources/releases and phenotype definitions, cohort/ancestry frame, effect coding, and estimand are sufficiently identical; the estimation method/reference is compatible with the stated estimand.
- **B — related, nonidentical:** the scientific question is close, but at least one source, release, ascertainment, measurement, sample frame, or estimator changes the estimand.
- **C — incompatible:** a superficial label match hides a materially different phenotype/source or a comparison that cannot answer the same question.
- **D — not represented in the accessible comparator snapshot:** the searched release/table is complete for its declared scope and contains no counterpart. This is a release-specific coverage result only.
- **E — insufficient evidence:** source identity, definition, result access, table completeness, or comparability cannot be established.

`label_match` is recorded independently of this classification. Its primary rule is exact normalized label after Unicode NFKC normalization, lowercasing, punctuation/whitespace normalization, and removal of no phenotype-bearing terms. A synonym match is separately tagged and must cite the source supporting the synonym. The strict-match failure proportion is the number of label/synonym candidates classified B, C, or E divided by all adjudicated label/synonym candidates; D is not a failed source match because no comparator row exists.

The table will include atlas record/family IDs, comparator and release, comparator table/sheet/row or query reference, source/accession evidence, definition/ancestry/method evidence, class, label-match type, adjudication notes, and direct citations. It will not copy full comparator tables or protected GWAS bodies. Any needed comparator statistics will be read in place or held in temporary analysis files; V7 public outputs will use source references and generated classifications/aggregates.

The following are reported separately for each comparator and for the union of accessible comparators: counts A–E, exact source/estimand-match coverage (A / 1,637), related nonidentical count (B), qualified atlas-only combinations, ambiguous/unresolvable count (E), and strict-match failure among apparent label matches. “Qualified atlas-only” requires (i) an atlas row that met its original eligibility rule, (ii) no A/B counterpart in the complete accessible comparator snapshots, and (iii) sufficient source metadata to interpret it. It is a coverage description, not a novel association claim.

## Experiment B: blinded task benchmark

The task target is 60, with up to 12 randomly sampled tasks from each prespecified stratum after the frozen Experiment A matching annotations are created:

1. source/estimand-matched positive correlations;
2. similar labels with different ascertainment or measurement;
3. relationships with statistical/QC/uncertainty limitations;
4. external validation, cohort reuse, or replication-status questions;
5. unavailable or QC-ineligible relationships.

Candidate pools and task selection rules are recorded before solver answers are generated. Use random seed `20261010`, sample without replacement within strata, and do not reallocate an undersized stratum to a better-performing one. If a stratum has fewer than 12 eligible candidates, take its census; total task count may consequently be below 60. Each task is a concrete source-defined question, with clinically relevant cardiometabolic, psychiatric, and aging tasks included where the eligible pool supports them. Task selection is not based on V7 solver or adjudication outcomes.

Two workflows answer every selected task:

- **Baseline:** the strongest accessible comparator tables/tools and the same primary source documents supplied to the other workflow, without the V5/V6 atlas package.
- **Atlas-assisted:** exactly the same external evidence and primary source documents, plus the V5/V6 atlas release and its source/uncertainty/validation metadata.

Both workflows receive the same task wording, source-document set, answer template, and maximum response length. No live browsing or additional sources are allowed after the evidence packet is frozen. The output must state phenotype, source, estimate or abstention, uncertainty/QC, whether a contrast was actually tested, cohort reuse/replication status, and citations. Retrieval time is omitted because tool latency is not a fair proxy for researcher time.

Two independent automated adjudicators, blinded to workflow labels, score anonymized answer pairs against a task-specific source-document gold standard. Each of the seven answer dimensions is marked correct, incorrect, or not applicable with evidence citations. A task is **fully evidence-supported** only when both adjudicators find all applicable dimensions correct and no material unsupported claim. Any adjudicator disagreement is retained, flagged as unresolved, and counted as not fully supported in the primary analysis; majority/adjudicated scores are sensitivity summaries only. The study will state that this is automated, not human peer review.

## Endpoints and statistical analysis

**Primary endpoint:** proportion of tasks yielding a fully evidence-supported and correctly qualified answer in each workflow.

**Primary effect:** paired absolute difference in task success, atlas-assisted minus baseline, in percentage points. Report the task counts, the paired discordance table, the estimate, and a 95% confidence interval.

Because tasks can share sleep or outcome GWAS, the prespecified interval uses a stratified two-way source-cluster pigeonhole bootstrap (10,000 replicates; seed `20261010`): resample sleep-source clusters and outcome-source clusters independently within the task set and weight each task by the product of its two cluster multiplicities; compute a stratum-balanced paired difference. Report the number of unique clusters in each dimension. If either dimension has fewer than 10 unique clusters or more than 5% of bootstrap replicates are degenerate, label the interval unstable and interpret the primary contrast descriptively; do not substitute an independent-task interval as confirmatory evidence. An unclustered paired interval and exact McNemar result may be shown only as explicitly secondary sensitivity summaries.

Secondary outcomes are the seven dimension-specific accuracy rates, source-identification accuracy, correct-estimate retrieval when available, unsupported-inference rate, and abstention accuracy. These are descriptive with Holm adjustment across the seven dimension tests where p-values are reported. Error type and adjudicator disagreement are tabulated. There is one primary endpoint; no outcome-based task replacement or post-hoc reallocation is allowed.

The inference population is the frozen, stratified benchmark task set, not all researchers or all possible sleep-genetics questions. No claim of general user benefit follows automatically from model performance. Any conclusion must separate integration benefit from information already accessible by ordinary inspection of comparator tables and original papers.

## Data integrity, exclusions, and deliverables

V5/V6 estimates, source records, rights decisions, and negative findings are immutable. No LDSC/GWAS reruns, new association scans, or restricted access requests are permitted for V7. Protected GWAS bodies and publisher supplement workbooks will not be copied into V7 outputs. Generated V7 data will contain source references and matching/adjudication annotations, with source rows and hashes sufficient for re-verification where rights allow.

Prespecified deliverables are: (1) source-matching annotation dataset; (2) per-comparator A–E and coverage summaries; (3) sampled task set and evidence manifest; (4) anonymized answers and blinded adjudication records; (5) primary and secondary performance tables with uncertainty; (6) error/disagreement analysis; (7) one publication-quality figure; (8) reproducible build/analysis scripts and test fixtures; (9) claim-to-evidence table; (10) interpretation and limitations; and (11) independent adversarial review.

No results from comparator matching or task performance have been inspected at protocol freeze. Any necessary deviation will be timestamped, justified, and labeled before the affected outcome is opened. The exact protocol file SHA-256 and commit will be recorded before Experiment A extraction.
