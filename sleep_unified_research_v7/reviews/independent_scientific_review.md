# Independent scientific review of the integrated evidence

**Scope:** read-only review of actual V4 result tables, source definitions, prior-art comparisons, and V7 comparator rows. This is an automated independent-agent review, not human peer review or empirical replication.

## Scientific assessment

The V4 sleep–phenome result package is coherent as a source-resolved resource: it retains separate core and extension testing families, explicit phenotype/source definitions, marginal uncertainty, exclusions, and qualified outcome-side validation. Its demonstrated use cases are bounded retrieval examples, not new biological findings. The 41 external estimates reuse discovery sleep GWAS; they do not establish independent two-trait replication. The intervals are marginal, the discovery/validation covariance is not calibrated, and the 62 sensitivity rows are not tests of between-fit differences.

The prior-art comparison includes Morrison's multidimensional sleep genetics, Goodman's composite sleep-health work, SleepChart's duration/disease and organ-level resource, Fan's multi-sleep/multi-organ panel, Human GWAS ATLAS pairwise correlations, and established LDSC workbenches. The evidence does not support a first-of-kind resource/method claim, a major new biological result, a causal mechanism, or a distinct subjective-versus-device inference. V7 adds bounded snapshot-level source-coverage annotations only.

## Review of final V7 scope

The corrected result package has no HGA A classifications; source/estimand candidates that lack release/coding/estimator evidence are E. The union is limited to HGA release 3, Morrison S10–S15, Goodman SD24, and Fan SD2/SD4/SD11. The 361 qualified match-absent rows are explicitly bounded to those snapshots. SleepChart remains prior audit context rather than a V7 row-level search; CTG-VL has no captured direct export. The planned paired AI-answer task benchmark was deferred before task sampling. The previous automated candidate-consumer review does not substitute for a baseline comparison or human usability study.

Representative source-level checks support cautious classifications: Fan insomnia–MDD is related coverage (B), while insomnia–Alzheimer's remains insufficiently comparable (E). They do not establish agreement or disagreement of effect sizes because Fan numeric estimates were not exported into V7.

## Publication decision

One integrated, carefully qualified resource paper is plausible for human investigators to draft. The current evidence does **not** establish Nature Communications submission readiness or competitiveness: it adds no validated method, independently replicated biology, or measured user benefit. Public release also remains contingent on source-specific rights, final source/phenotype/overlap approval, metadata, ethics and institutional review, authorship and contribution approval, funding/conflict declarations, AI-use disclosure, and a durable data/code-access plan.

No manuscript prose was written, no journal was contacted, and no submission was made.
