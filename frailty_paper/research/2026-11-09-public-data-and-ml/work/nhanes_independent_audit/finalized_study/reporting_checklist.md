# Observational reporting and remaining review

The participant selection, years, exposure, score construction, covariates, design, missingness, model specifications and source links are documented in `methods.md`; flow, weighted baseline characteristics, missingness, results, sensitivity analyses and uncertainty intervals are in the supplement tables. `results.md` and the figures present the estimated associations and uncertainty. `limitations_and_corrections.md` addresses bias, generalizability, alternative interpretations and unresolved source issues. The package contains the data, dictionaries, code, source hashes and figure data for reproducibility. The study uses public data; investigator confirmation of institutional requirements is still needed.

Before journal submission, investigators should reconcile this text with the full manuscript and journal-specific checklist, confirm original score allele orientation with source authors, determine any ethics/funding/conflict statements, review all variable definitions and clinical interpretation, and verify that the actual manuscript's reported numbers and multiplicity families match these outputs. The observational study does not establish causation, external validity of the FI, or genetic-score validity.
