CURRENT MODEL RESEARCH UPDATE

Completed one new, bounded comparison: 16 backpropagation fits, the same 654,612 development observations, and the same data partitions. A 20,749-parameter head compared the existing scalar inputs with 192 fixed projections retaining additional channel details from the three frozen DNA readers. Actual full DNA was used; no padded sequence, detection target, prior test reuse as untouched, or AlphaGenome imitation was introduced. Independent fitted mathematics and new-input gradients passed.

The additional channel version did not earn promotion. Its best common-epoch source-omission error improvement was 0.385% over the typical-effect baseline, versus 0.668% for its matched scalar control. Both selected epoch 1. Nearby ordering was 54.76% versus 54.11%; variant-specific cell-difference correlations stayed weak. These are selected development diagnostics, not external validation. The earlier full-reader candidate and all its adverse external results remain unchanged.

Measurement investigation: many source estimates have uncertainty as large as their measured magnitude, and the cell-wide uncertainty order changes across libraries. This can distort naive comparisons of absolute effects, but does not show effects are zero. In the previously revealed blood source, the median magnitude correlation between repeat subsets was 0.668 in K562. There is reproducible signal that the model failed to learn; noise alone does not excuse that failure. Some between-cell repeat comparisons are less stable. No original target was replaced by this ratio diagnostic.

Original source identity check: all four released A/B RNA/control means exactly matched the corresponding normalized Ref/Alt count averages for 93,754 centered-forward comparisons in each of HepG2 and SK-N-SH. This strengthens source count/allele-activity mapping. It does not establish the original paired GLM Log2Skew contrast convention; unknown signed targets were not filled.

New free test candidate: original GEO GSE87711 supplies 145 bp designs, 14 barcode/count rows per allele construct, two DNA samples, six basal K562 RNA samples and four GATA1-overexpression RNA samples. Reciprocal genome mapping, full actual reference DNA, complete-source literal-DNA/history and whole published pretraining-library checks leave 1,317 designs from 447 variants in 51 one-megabase windows. Original files were downloaded and CRC/hash checked. The new source has not been used to fit coefficients or judge a changed model. Count-to-effect reconstruction, repeat/QC rules and a fixed candidate/prediction protocol remain before it becomes an eligible new validation. GATA1 manipulation is not an ordinary additional cell type.

The project still has no strong validated DNA-effect model, no authorized biological rankings, and no human insomnia/frailty score validation. The prior AVI rejection, genetic analyses, NHANES observational study and manuscript remain unchanged.
