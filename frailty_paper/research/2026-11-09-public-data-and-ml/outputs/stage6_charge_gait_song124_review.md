# Full gait GWAS screen of the published insomnia–FI candidate list

I screened the 124 rsIDs reported in the project's [published insomnia–FI cross-trait candidate inventory](published_insomnia_fi_124_cross_trait_candidates.csv) against the full [NHLBI GRASP gait-speed summary file](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/grasp/full-results). The full stream contained 2,474,503 rows and reproduced the independently recorded normalized-stream hash from the earlier 22-candidate scan. Forty-five candidate rsIDs had one source row and 79 were absent. This is **rsID-only matching**: the source lacks positions, and build, effect allele equivalence, reference alleles and multiallelic representation have not been independently established for these 124 contrasts. Do not use the source effects for direction inference without resolving those issues.

Two of 45 returned rows had unadjusted p<0.05: rs1032524 (p=0.04313) and rs9268557 (p=0.04356). Neither meets the descriptive 0.05/124 threshold (approximately 0.000403). The latter lies in the major histocompatibility complex according to the original candidate inventory, where complex LD complicates interpretation. The candidates were selected by earlier cross-trait analyses, the source phenotype is walking speed, and exact participant overlap with all prior studies is unverified. The 79 missing IDs are not null effects; 43 returned nonsignificant p-values are not proof of absence. This screen does not validate an individual genetic score, demonstrate a shared causal variant, or establish a gene or cell mechanism.

The [lookup](stage6_charge_gait_song124_lookup.csv) preserves source allele order, beta, SE, p-values, per-row weight and heterogeneity p-values without reorienting them; the corresponding [receipt](stage6_charge_gait_song124_receipt.json) records source, row counts and normalized-stream hash. The earlier [22-candidate analysis](stage6_charge_gait_full_candidate_review.md) includes more detailed allele-frequency checks for that subset. Reproduce the new screen with `python work/gait_charge_2017/full_song124_lookup.py`.
