# Molecular follow-up of RBM6-region variants

## Hospital-frailty marker and RBM6 brain expression

The FinnGen hospital-frailty marker rs2282749 (GRCh38 chr3:50213404:T>C), selected as the smallest hospital-frailty p value **within the five-GWAS common-variant set**, appears in the public SingleBrain browser as an RBM6 eQTL in seven major brain cell categories. Those seven records are repeated analyses of correlated cell types, not seven independent replications; differences in p values do not establish cell-type specificity. `stage6_rbm6_singlebrain_exact_matches.csv` contains portal-returned records and `stage6_rbm6_singlebrain_raw.zip` preserves the responses. The set and the source of the selected marker are described in `stage6_rbm6_regional_shared_risk_review.md`.

The source's full excitatory-neuron file was independently checked by a targeted byte-range extraction. For rs2282749, it explicitly reports C as the effect allele, fixed beta -0.120533 (SE 0.012637; fixed-effect p=1.45e-21; random-effects p=1.92e-20). In the earlier aligned FinnGen hospital-frailty GWAS, the same C allele has beta +0.052102 (SE 0.012657; p=3.85e-5); in Jansen insomnia, its beta is +0.013903 (p=0.00664). These are different effect scales and postselected marginal associations. Their directions suggest a hypothesis of lower RBM6 expression linked with higher trait values, but matching at a marker does **not** establish a common causal variant, disease mediation, or a sleep–frailty mechanism. The source brain effect is in excitatory neurons; the separate RBM6 prediction model is for mural cells and should not be conflated with it.

For the influential model SNP rs2353586 (chr3:50008613:G>T), the SingleBrain excitatory-neuron full file contains an exact-allele row with fixed SE=0 and beta=0, plus a reversed-allele row with the same zero statistics. These are unusable as signed estimates and may reflect missing or unestimable statistics; they are **not evidence of no biological effect**. The browser did not return an exact RBM6 association for this marker in the seven queried categories, which likewise is not a negative finding. The extracted source file `stage6_singlebrain_ext_rbm6_full_regional.tsv.gz` and `stage6_singlebrain_ext_rbm6_regional_receipt.json` record the checked window, range hash, ordering and BGZF block checks. Because only a range was downloaded, the full source file's checksum was not verified. Reproduce with `stage6_singlebrain_rbm6_regional_extract.py Ext` and `stage6_rbm6_singlebrain_query.py`.

## rs2353586 immune-cell context

The [FinnGen CASCADE browser](https://cascade.finngen.fi/) returned the exact GRCh38 G>T variant, marked **partial**: it has no CASCADE classification at the position. The response contains 66 top-feature records across cell contexts and molecular assay types, saved in `stage6_rs2353586_cascade_top_qtl.csv` and `stage6_rs2353586_cascade_raw.json`. Top eQTL features vary by cell type; the broad PBMC top feature is SEMA3F-AS1 (nominal p=3.20e-24), while RBM6 is the top eQTL feature in several reported contexts, including broad NK cells (nominal p=0.000339). These are source-selected top features, not an exhaustive per-variant gene screen, and the response's q-values need not represent correction of these individual nominal p values. The current portal beta allele convention has not been fully established for these rows; their signs should not be directly compared with brain or disease effects.

The CASCADE resource studies immune cells rather than brain mural or neuronal cells, and FinnGen immune donors may overlap its phenotype GWAS. These molecular associations cannot validate a patient outcome, identify the causal target gene or prove that the immune-cell and brain signals share a cause. Conditional or multi-signal colocalization with complete matched statistics and independent phenotype data remain required. Sources: [SingleBrain study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02541-x) and [full-statistics release](https://zenodo.org/records/14908182); [CASCADE study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2) and [portal](https://cascade.finngen.fi/).
