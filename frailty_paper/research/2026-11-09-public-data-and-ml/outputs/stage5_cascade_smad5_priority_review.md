# SMAD5: preliminary CASCADE multiome cross-check

The public [FinnGen CASCADE browser](https://cascade.finngen.fi/) reports results from paired immune-cell RNA and chromatin profiling of FinnGen donors. The peer-reviewed [multiome study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2) was published in 2026. It is a separately measured dataset from the previously used OneK1K molecular study, but donor overlap with FinnGen disease GWAS is possible; it is not automatically independent phenotype replication. The model's live version and full training provenance are not independently pinned, so publication timing alone does not prove an independent model evaluation.

For the exact GRCh38 variant `chr5:136202653:A>C` (rs7734045), the public API's **top** naïve CD4 eQTL entry is SMAD5 (ENSG00000113658), p = 1.0145 × 10⁻⁵¹, reported beta = +0.34181, SE = 0.022463 and allele frequency = 0.28864. The same variant's top naïve CD4 chromatin QTL is peak `chr5-136132565-136133590`, p = 7.53 × 10⁻¹¹⁸, reported beta = +0.7107121 and SE = 0.0268119. These are browser-selected marginal associations, not proof that expression and accessibility share a causal signal, nor proof of a link from that peak to SMAD5. The peak is about 69 kb from the marker and does not overlap it.

The previously recorded OneK1K naïve CD4 SMAD5 eQTL ALT effect is +0.672672 (p = 1.55 × 10⁻⁴⁴). AlphaGenome's matching-label naïve CD4 total RNA score is +0.0004586 while its polyA RNA score is -0.0014015. Its local ATAC and DNase scores are +0.0099125 and +0.0233555, respectively. Do not compare those local 501-bp accessibility scores directly to the remote chromatin peak's QTL effect. CASCADE's API beta is preserved **as reported**; SAIGE-QTL documentation describes Allele2 as the effect allele, but this portal's exact downstream harmonization has not yet been independently traced, so directional agreement with the AlphaGenome REF/ALT score remains provisional. QTL and model scales, donors, cell states and experimental modalities differ.

The API response is explicitly partial: the browser reports no CASCADE classification at this position while showing available molecular signals. The full retrieved JSON is retained under `work/stage5_cascade_raw` and the selected top-per-cell results are compiled in `stage5_cascade_top_qtl_by_cell.csv`; as the queue run continues these files and the corresponding coverage table may grow. Top-per-cell output is not the full set of tested variant–gene/peak associations. Earlier single-signal FOXP2 and SMAD5 colocalization reviews remain applicable, with their ancestry, incomplete-coverage and model limitations. A matched-locus, suitable-LD multi-signal colocalization and an independent allele-specific experiment are still required for a causal interpretation.

Sources: [CASCADE data access](https://cascade.finngen.fi/data/), [SAIGE-QTL output documentation](https://weizhou0.github.io/SAIGE-QTL-doc/docs/single_step2.html), and the [model scoring definitions](https://google-deepmind-alphagenome.readthedocs-hosted.com/variant_scoring.html).

## Fine-mapped alternative

For `chr5:136207208:G>A`, CASCADE reports the same top naïve CD4 gene, SMAD5, with p = 3.1354 × 10⁻⁴⁶, beta as reported +0.31912, and a top caQTL at `chr5-136132565-136133590` with p = 3.60 × 10⁻¹⁰³ and beta as reported +0.6671954. AlphaGenome's matching-label naïve CD4 RNA scores are -0.0004606 for total RNA and -0.0020425 for polyA RNA; the local ATAC and DNase scores are +0.0083566 and -0.0183401. The portal's effect-allele convention is not yet independently confirmed, so the apparent sign difference cannot be classified as agreement or disagreement. The earlier SK-N-SH reporter result for this allele was a raw-library observation outside that study's selected subset and does not establish a naïve CD4 mechanism. These are postselected, correlated variants in one locus, not independent replications.

A subsequent audit of the published FinnGen SAIGE-QTL workflow supports ALT orientation of the eQTL beta, but the browser's full transformation path remains unverified. Under that conditional interpretation, the original marker's total-RNA model score has the same sign as its portal eQTL and the polyA score the opposite; both RNA scores for the alternative have the opposite sign. See `stage5_cascade_effect_allele_crosscheck.md`. This does not verify caQTL orientation or establish causal sharing.
