# Remaining molecular-QTL worklist

The [worklist](stage3_remaining_molecular_worklist.csv) covers the 14 provisional marker groups not already represented by a regional QTL comparison when this batch was prepared. Twelve have one selected exact-marker GTEx v10 expression-QTL gene/tissue pair for follow-up in the related, reprocessed GTEx v8 datasets of eQTL Catalogue release 7. Two groups, rs9845387 and rs4886140, had no exact-marker eQTL or sQTL row returned by the earlier GTEx v10 query; they currently have **no gene supported by that screen**, not proof that the regions lack molecular associations. Proxy variants, other tissues, distal contacts and untested molecular phenotypes remain possible.

The rule is a transparent way to choose a first analysis, not a claim that the selected gene is causal: among exact-marker eQTL rows with a corresponding release-7 dataset, prioritize brain, skeletal muscle, whole blood or artery; then tibial nerve; then pituitary; then other available tissues. Within a tier, choose the smallest source p-value with stable tie breaking. Thus a less significant brain or artery QTL may be selected over a more significant unrelated-tissue QTL. P-values are post-selection and do not rank colocalization or prove shared risk. Gene IDs are stable Ensembl IDs without version suffixes; v10-to-v8 annotation and dataset differences can prevent a match.

The selected [variant–gene–tissue table](stage3_initial_variant_gene_table.csv) retains the broader exact-marker evidence. This worklist does not replace it or complete the requested locus analysis. Each follow-up needs to verify the actual allele and GRCh38 position, regional variant support, molecular fine mapping, sample and ancestry matching, and prior/model limitations. GTEx v10 and the Catalogue's v8 reprocessing share source material and are not independent replications. The [regional GWAS cache receipt](stage3_gwas_regional_cache_receipt.json) documents the source files and candidate-centered extraction windows; filtering to a region does not authenticate builds or alleles.

The two no-exact-QTL groups were subsequently evaluated with [positional fallback genes](stage3_positional_fallback_colocalization.md). Neither candidate had a significant nominal cortex eQTL in that selected release, and no molecular credible sets were reported. This does not identify a supported causal gene or rule out other mechanisms.
