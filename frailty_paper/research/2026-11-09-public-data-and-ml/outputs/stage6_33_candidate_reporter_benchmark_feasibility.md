# Can the 33 project variants supply an independent reporter benchmark?

We matched the 33 distinct selected project variants by GRCh38 position and exact unordered allele pair to the existing [Siraj et al. selected-library reporter coverage](stage5_siraj2026_allcell_reporter_review.md), separately for each of six cell lines. The resulting [coverage matrix](stage6_33_candidate_reporter_coverage.csv) has 198 variant–cell entries. Five variants have selected records in five lines each (25 entries); none of those entries met the source's active-element-and-allelic-skew rule. The remaining **173 entries were not assessed in this selected subset**, including entries absent from the original 488-variant queue or its reference-checked mapping. These are missing values, not negative results.

The observed 25 entries are not validated negative controls: an experiment may lack power, the element may be inactive in the tested construct or cell line, and the study's selected-library and coverage filters apply. The 25 entries are repeated measures on five variants, not 25 independent variants. Even the recorded absence of an active-and-skew call is limited to the reported assay and selected source. Normal brain or primary immune cell contexts are not represented by an immortalized cell-line result. Candidate rsIDs and cross-build mappings were inherited from earlier project tables; exact coordinate and pair matching does not resolve all multiallelic representations.

This subset therefore has **no positive reporter labels and insufficient independent variants for a useful supervised benchmark**. Do not train an embedding classifier on these rows, convert not-assessed cases to zero, or claim a model-performance result. A valid future benchmark needs a larger, independently defined assayed-variant universe with source QC, separate active/inactive/uncertain designations, matched controls and region- or chromosome-held-out evaluation. Check study and donor independence and source licenses; the available records and genomic training overlap do not establish independence from AlphaGenome. Project AlphaGenome outputs and derivatives were not used in this match.
