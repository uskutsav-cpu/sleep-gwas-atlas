# Reference LD among the 12 discordant RLS-region variants

The [three-source screen](stage6_rls_three_study_region_review.md) identified 12 nearby variants whose reported effect directions agreed between FinnGen and INTERVAL but differed from the Akçimen meta-analysis. Those rows should not be counted as 12 independent findings. To make this qualification more concrete, we mapped their GRCh38 positions to GRCh37 using the local UCSC chain and matched rsID and exact biallelic allele pairs in the [1000 Genomes Phase 3 reference](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/gbdb/hg19/1000Genomes/phase3/ALL.chr2.phase3_shapeit2_mvncall_integrated_v5a.20130502.genotypes.vcf.gz).

All 12 had unique matches and variable called dosages in the 503-person EUR reference. Of 66 pairwise comparisons, 10 had r² ≥ 0.8. Connecting variants with r² ≥ 0.8 produced six descriptive connected groups: three groups contain multiple variants and three contain one each. The 99-person FIN subset produced the same group membership, but is **contained within the EUR panel**, so this is not an independent replication. Some lower-frequency estimates are unstable: rs187012067's ALT allele appeared only 18 times among 1,006 EUR chromosomes, including nine among 198 FIN chromosomes.

The groups are a visualization of a reference-panel threshold, not a count of independent causal signals. Connected groups need not be cliques; pairs below 0.8 can still be correlated; a different threshold, target population, imputation, reference sampling or conditional analysis could give different groupings. The reference may contain relatives and structure and is not the Akçimen or FinnGen cohort. Allele-matched reference records do not settle the source-level effect coding or explain the discrepancy. The selected source variants and outcomes were examined after earlier results.
