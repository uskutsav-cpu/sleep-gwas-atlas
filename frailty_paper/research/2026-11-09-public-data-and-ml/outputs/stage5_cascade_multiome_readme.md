# Public FinnGen immune multiome follow-up

The 2026 [FinnGen CASCADE study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2) profiles paired single-nucleus RNA and chromatin accessibility in peripheral blood immune cells. Its public [browser](https://cascade.finngen.fi/) returns cell-specific molecular associations, fine-mapping and mechanism annotations. This is a separately generated molecular dataset from the earlier OneK1K analysis. FinnGen donors may overlap participants in FinnGen phenotype GWAS, so these records are not an independent disease-cohort replication. Publication after the original AlphaGenome paper is useful context, but the precise training and benchmark provenance of the live API version is not independently pinned; do not claim proven model independence.

We queried all 488 reference-checked GRCh38 queue alleles against the public variant endpoint. For 460 requests, the response's variant ID exactly matched the requested REF/ALT. The portal reported no available position-level result for the other 28; those responses do not independently confirm an allele-level negative. Of the 460 exact-ID records, 458 explicitly indicated a partial response and two did not provide that flag. A portal response or absence of one is not a test that a variant has no molecular effect.

- `stage5_cascade_query_coverage.csv` retains the 488 per-request statuses, partial flags and portal reasons.
- `stage5_cascade_top_qtl_by_cell.csv` contains 26,171 returned marginal rows: 14,742 eQTL and 11,429 caQTL. The portal provides the **top feature per cell and QTL type** in this view, not every tested variant–gene or variant–peak pair. Missing FOXP2 or another gene as the top feature is not evidence that it has no QTL.
- `stage5_cascade_credible_qtl_by_cell.csv` contains 7,868 portal-returned credible-set eQTL/caQTL rows for 338 variants. A molecular PIP is not a disease PIP; source-specific credible sets, peak links, partial responses and colocalization assumptions matter. Marginal and credible rows can describe the same underlying data and must not be counted as independent replications.
- `stage5_cascade_model_eqtl_comparison.csv` links 3,903 model RNA rows across 450 variants to same-allele, same-Ensembl-gene portal top eQTLs using explicitly mapped cell labels. The labels indicate similar populations, not identical samples, activation or assay. Rows include nonsignificant associations, multiple tracks and correlated variants; they are not a calibrated validation set. CASCADE beta and allele frequency are retained **as reported**, with no sign classification until the portal-specific effect-allele transformation is verified. [SAIGE-QTL documentation](https://weizhou0.github.io/SAIGE-QTL-doc/docs/single_step2.html) defines Allele2 as the effect allele, but does not by itself establish how this browser transformed each summary.
- `stage5_variant_phenotype_experimental_multiome_index.csv` appends CASCADE coverage and pointers to the existing 488-variant phenotype and reporter evidence index. It does not duplicate all cell-level results or make QTL and phenotype associations causal.
- `stage5_cascade_raw_responses.zip` and `stage5_cascade_query_receipt.json` preserve all 488 API response objects as reserialized compressed JSON, query URLs, and hashes. Hashes apply to these saved copies, not the original HTTP byte streams. This is a reproducibility receipt, not proof that the portal is a complete archive of all possible molecular evidence.

## Priority SMAD5 findings

At rs7734045 (`chr5:136202653:A>C`), the portal's top naïve CD4 eQTL is SMAD5, p = 1.01 × 10⁻⁵¹ and beta as reported +0.34181. The top naïve CD4 caQTL is at `chr5-136132565-136133590`, approximately 69 kb from the variant, p = 7.53 × 10⁻¹¹⁸. The fine-mapped alternative `chr5:136207208:G>A` also has top naïve CD4 SMAD5 eQTL and the same top caQTL peak (p = 3.14 × 10⁻⁴⁶ and 3.60 × 10⁻¹⁰³, respectively). These are associated, postselected variants, not independent locus replications; the chromatin peak and eQTL are not automatically a shared causal pathway. In particular, a local 501-bp AlphaGenome ATAC score at the variant is not a matched prediction for a distant peak. Model RNA assays give different small scores across tracks; source effect-allele orientation remains unresolved. See `stage5_cascade_smad5_priority_review.md` for the earlier OneK1K results and full caveats.

The public 2026 resource adds meaningful immune-cell triangulation, not a definitive mechanism or independent validation of every model prediction. In particular, it does not prove that a molecular eQTL colocalizes with insomnia or frailty, and it does not supply a phenotypically independent cohort. The original FOXP2 and SMAD5 colocalization and reporter reviews remain separate, with their assumptions and unresolved source coverage.

## Reproduction

From the project root, `python3 outputs/stage5_query_cascade_multiome.py` saves per-allele gzipped response objects. Then run `python3 outputs/stage5_compile_cascade_multiome.py`, `python3 outputs/stage5_compile_cascade_credible.py`, `python3 outputs/stage5_compare_cascade_model.py --store LIVE_SCORE_DIRECTORY`, and `python3 outputs/stage5_integrate_cascade_index.py`. The public portal and its data can change; repeat queries are not guaranteed to match the archived responses. Standard scientific limitations on cell-type labels, allele alignment, sample overlap, model training and multiple testing apply throughout.

A follow-up source-code audit found that the published FinnGen SAIGE-QTL workflow specifies reference-first allele order and reports effects for Allele2, supporting ALT orientation. The deployed browser's complete transformation pipeline has not been independently pinned, so the eQTL interpretation remains conditional; do not transfer it automatically to caQTL betas. A within-locus OneK1K consistency screen is documented in `stage5_cascade_effect_allele_crosscheck.md`.
