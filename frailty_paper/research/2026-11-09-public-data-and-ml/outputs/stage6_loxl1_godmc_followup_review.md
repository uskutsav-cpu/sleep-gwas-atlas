# Follow-up of the LOXL1-proximal methylation site

The previous [frailty EWAS proximity screen](stage6_frailty_ewas_candidate_overlap_review.md) found the whole-blood CpG cg12594244 83,871 bp from the 22-marker candidate rs11636381. We queried the [GoDMC public methylation-QTL database](https://mqtldb.godmc.org.uk/about) for this CpG on 29 October 2026 UTC. Its two-stage database describes blood methylation associations initially selected for follow-up; it is not an exhaustive set of all tested SNP–CpG estimates. Cohort overlap with our frailty or sleep studies has not been determined.

The endpoint returned 133 selected association records for this CpG. None had rsID rs11636381 or its GRCh37 position 15:74134883. The strongest reported record by the returned `pval` is rs4077284 at 15:74228391, with reported p = 1.56×10⁻⁹², A/G alleles, and 27,222 samples. This is an association between the marker and methylation, not an association with insomnia or a causal effect on frailty. The API reports one record per selected association; absence of the candidate does **not** establish that it was tested, null, or unrelated to the CpG.

We further matched 89 of the 133 returned records to the MAGMA 1000 Genomes European reference by GRCh37 position and unordered allele pair, and calculated pairwise dosage correlations with rs11636381 in 503 samples. The strongest reported mQTL rs4077284 had r² = 0.031; the largest r² among the 89 evaluable returned records was 0.111, for rs2165241. These reference LD estimates give no strong support for using those returned variants as a single-marker proxy for the candidate in this reference panel. They do not exclude an unreported mQTL, multiple independent signals, or ancestry-specific differences; nor do they constitute colocalization. Forty-four returned associations could not be matched to this biallelic reference by the stated criteria and were not evaluated. No result validates a sleep–frailty score.

GoDMC's documented API was reachable here over HTTP, while HTTPS returned an error. Thus the retrieved response was not authenticated in transit; this limits provenance and calls for a verified download or a secure mirror before relying on the quantitative values. The local response SHA-256 is `0e22c5aee70791ca60f76251332d1069575f94b9623ffc3baa94cedd62f9ac16`. A negative exact-match result is especially conditional on the database's selection and coverage. Seek a secure full summary source with the exact candidate contrast and then, if adequate regional data exist, assess distinct signals and colocalization with ancestry-matched LD.
