# Neuronal reporter exploratory ML baseline

Written before fitting this analysis, after inspecting the published workbook's column structure and group-label categories. Use the published neuronal MPRA baseline condition and sequence library from Retallick-Townsley et al. (2026), Supplementary Data 2. Pair only two uniquely named REF and ALT sequences for the same reported variant, require consistent author `emVar.group` across both rows and A/C/G/T 199-bp sequences, and retain labels `fdr <= 0.05` as detected, `n.s` as not-detected. Exclude `NA`, intermediate FDR groups and conflicting or incomplete pairs. A not-detected result is not proof of a nonfunctional allele; the target is **assay detection classification**, influenced by power and study selection, not biological truth or disease risk.

Represent the allele pair by absolute differences in 1–3-mer counts (84 features); standardize using training chromosomes only, fit a fixed L2-regularized logistic regression with no hyperparameter search. Hold out chromosomes 1 and 2 in their entirety, train on the other autosomes. Compare held-out AUROC and average precision with the held-out positive prevalence as the constant-score baseline. A fixed random seed and fixed gradient descent ensure reproducibility. One held-out split provides a single exploratory estimate; it is not a formal uncertainty interval or clinical test. Exclude labels/predictions from AlphaGenome, project GWAS or other tools; no pretrained model features. No holdout study, external donor, data source, LD block beyond chromosome, or study ascertainment independence is guaranteed. The models and derived sequence-level data are not redistributed; source article specifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and terms should be assessed for use beyond local analysis. The experiment does not validate insomnia–frailty project scores.
