# Reference LD between the handgrip lead and nearby project candidate

The exploratory handgrip lookup identified rs7731706 G/T, near TRPC7, at nominal p=0.009. The project also includes rs7734045 C/A, about 32.6 kb away on chromosome 5 and previously followed up in the TenK10K immune-cell accessibility analysis. These are two preselected project markers; physical proximity does not make them the same signal.

I retrieved their exact biallelic GRCh37 contrasts from the public 1000 Genomes phase 3 VCF and calculated phased-haplotype correlations. In the 661-person reference group labeled AFR, the correlation between rs7734045 **C** and rs7731706 **G** was r=−0.357 (r²=0.128; 1,322 complete phased haplotypes). In the 503-person group labeled EUR, r=+0.380 (r²=0.144; 1,006 haplotypes). The positive EUR estimate reproduces the project's earlier check. The opposite sign in these pooled reference groups is a useful warning against assuming that the two counted alleles travel together across populations. Both squared correlations are modest; neither establishes whether the GWAS signals share a causal variant.

The AFR label combines genetically diverse populations, and this panel is not proven representative of the eight-country Hansen cohort; pooled population structure and finite sampling may affect correlations. The rs7731706 identifier is multiallelic in a broader reference even though the checked 1000 Genomes record was biallelic. Phasing, imputation and allelic coding may differ in the actual studies. The handgrip GWAS itself had a near-zero rs7734045 C estimate (beta 0.0201, SE 0.2198, p=0.927), but it is too imprecise to demonstrate no effect. The rs7731706 result is selected and not significant under 0.05/22. Neither comparison validates the score, establishes causality or identifies TRPC7 as the effector gene.
