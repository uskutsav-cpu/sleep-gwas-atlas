# Twenty-two-marker count against depression-symptom GWAS

We extended the [case-enriched symptom screen](stage6_mdd_clinical_candidate_screen_review.md) to the full 22-marker equal-weight count using previously saved 1000 Genomes European reference summaries. All 22 exact coordinate-and-allele contrasts were present in each source GWAS. The approximate weighted marginal Z was **1.05** for the insomnia symptom and **−1.10** for fatigue. Both estimates are statistically uncertain; they do not demonstrate a null, compare the scores' predictive abilities, or validate the count.

The source outcomes are symptoms in depression-enriched cohorts, not general-population insomnia or FI/FFS. The marker list and analyses were selected after prior exploration, and target participant/relative overlap with all discovery sources remains unverified. The results are not a joint-outcome analysis or a prespecified replication.

The calculation aligns each GWAS A1 effect to the score-counted allele. Its numerator sums the aligned marginal Z values weighted by √[2p(1−p)], using reference counted-allele frequencies. Its denominator is the previously saved empirical standard deviation of the complete count, 3.03694 in 503 European reference individuals; the score file hash matched its original receipt. This denominator includes the sample's observed cross-chromosome correlations, whereas the numerator uses Hardy–Weinberg variance approximations rather than actual marker-level reference variances. The difference is a further approximation. Target LD, allele frequencies, per-variant effective samples and Z covariance are unknown, and GWAS contributors vary by marker. SE × reference HWE SD ranges 0.0138–0.0169 for insomnia and 0.0203–0.0271 for fatigue. These ranges provide a rough diagnostic only, not proof that the score approximation or its nominal p-values are calibrated. Score variants with multiallelic representations and the target effect coding require further verification.

The [15-marker check](stage6_mdd_15_weighted_sensitivity_review.md) used cached individual reference genotypes and a different denominator convention; its Z values must not be compared to these to rank scores. Appropriate evaluation still needs a frozen score and independent linked genotype–phenotype data.

Reproduction: `work/stage6_mdd_22_weighted_sensitivity.py`, [results](stage6_mdd_22_weighted_sensitivity.json), `outputs/22_score_reference_group_allele_frequencies.csv` and [reference score receipt](shared22_reference_diagnostic.json). Full source GWAS files were streamed and checked against Figshare's compressed-file checksums; the PGC's scientific-use and redistribution restrictions apply as described in the original screen review.

## Target-frequency and within-chromosome LD sensitivity

We subsequently retrieved the 22 required genotype records in 503 1000 Genomes EUR reference participants. Using a case-count-weighted approximation of the source's case/control allele frequencies for HWE genotype SDs, the reference within-chromosome correlation matrix and zero cross-chromosome correlations, the count's approximate Z is **1.079** for the insomnia symptom and **−1.088** for fatigue, versus **1.047** and **−1.097** in the earlier calculation. The sign and broad interpretation are unchanged; neither result excludes a modest association or evaluates a frailty phenotype. This is a post hoc combined sensitivity: it changes both the frequency and the denominator convention, so the difference cannot be attributed to either change alone.

The frequencies may not be exact pooled frequencies across a meta-analysis, and a common reference LD matrix and common score-statistic approximation may not reflect per-marker sample composition, case-control information, ancestry or actual target LD. Imputed calls, allele harmonization and discovery/target relatedness remain unverified. The indexed remote reference VCF was not full-file checksum verified. These nominal statistics are not patient-level score regression, calibrated score-level tests, prespecified replication, or clinical prediction. Reproduce with `python3 work/mdd_shared22_frequency_ld_sensitivity.py`; the [receipt](stage6_mdd_shared22_target_freq_ld_sensitivity.json) records the aggregate results and input hashes, without redistributing the source candidate data.

As an additional precision sensitivity, replacing the target-frequency weights with inverse standard-error weights gives approximate Z values of **1.048** for insomnia and **−1.069** for fatigue under the same reference LD convention. The coefficient of variation of SE multiplied by the target HWE genotype SD is **0.031** and **0.037**, respectively. Frequency weighting is proportional to inverse-SE weighting only under uniform precision; this diagnostic does not establish that condition. The inverse-SE calculation also assumes that the external LD matrix approximates covariance between the target summary statistics. The small differences here do not establish equivalence, calibration, or predictive validity.
