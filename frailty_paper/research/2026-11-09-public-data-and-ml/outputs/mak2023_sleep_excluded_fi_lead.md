# A published GWAS of a frailty index without insomnia and fatigue

**Mak et al. ran a GWAS of a modified FI that removes both insomnia and fatigue**, according to the methods and Supplementary Tables 4 and 16 of their [2023 study](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13868). This is a more directly relevant lead for the project's measurement-overlap question than a purely theoretical item-removal plan. It does not, by itself, answer whether insomnia and frailty have a genetic correlation after item removal.

The paper's 49-item UK Biobank FI groups eight items under mental wellbeing. Supplementary Table 4 identifies them as self-rated general health, fatigue, insomnia, feeling depressed, self-described nervous personality, severe anxiety/panic attacks, feeling lonely and sense of misery. Supplementary Table 16 labels a **modified FI excluding all eight mental-wellbeing items**. Thus, under the reported category construction, its full item set has 41 remaining items; the individual denominator can depend on missing items. It removes six other items as well as insomnia and fatigue, so a change in correlation could not be uniquely attributed to those two items. Other items may retain indirect links to sleep or mental health.

The paper says it ran a GWAS for each of 11 category-stripped indices and reports Mendelian-randomization estimates for metabolic biomarkers against those outcomes. The supplement does **not** provide an insomnia–modified-FI genetic correlation in the inspected table, and the reported biomarker MR coefficients are not SNP-level summary statistics for LDSC. The [authors' Figshare dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24591573.v1) lists 19 files, including full, random-half and non-metabolomics FI and physical-frailty GWAS; none of their filenames identifies the mental-wellbeing-stripped FI. This is an inventory of that specific release, not proof that the file is unavailable anywhere else. The paper's data statement says further data are available upon reasonable request.

Rechecking the [Figshare article API](https://api.figshare.com/v2/articles/24591573) on 28 October 2026 returned version 1 and the same 19 filenames; a newly released stripped-FI file was not identified in that article. This does not rule out releases elsewhere or establish availability by author request.

## What would make this useful

Obtain the full mental-wellbeing-stripped FI GWAS summary statistics, item coding and phenotype/sample definitions, including effect/other alleles, genome build, per-variant N, beta, SE, allele frequency and QC. Check whether the released full-FI and stripped-FI GWAS use the same individuals and the same model; the paper describes the same method, but this does not by itself prove identical participant sets. For a matched-support LDSC comparison with insomnia, verify h² precision, LD-reference suitability, sample overlap and paired uncertainty. Comparison of the full and stripped versions would remove eight items at once and therefore test a broader construct change, not isolate the contribution of insomnia or fatigue. A more specific same-person GWAS dropping only insomnia, then insomnia plus fatigue, would be needed for that question.
