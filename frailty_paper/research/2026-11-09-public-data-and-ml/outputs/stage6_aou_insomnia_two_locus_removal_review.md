# Sensitivity to two insomnia-associated loci

The [public All of Us candidate follow-up](stage6_aou_full_candidate_association_review.md) identified rs113851554 and rs12536335 as the two project variants passing BH in its selected 33-variant insomnia analysis. We asked how the previously calculated *conditional weighted-Z* statistics change if those specific variants are omitted. This is a post hoc dependence check in the same data, not a new cohort, a causal partition, a test of individual prediction, or a proposal to change the score.

In the EUR genetic-similarity analysis, omitting both variants from the 15-marker sum leaves 13 variants and produces conditional insomnia Z values from 4.34 to 5.10 across the three weight sets, versus 5.00 to 6.79 for the respective full sums. For the 22-marker constructions, rs113851554 was never included; omitting rs12536335 leaves 21 markers and Z values from 2.69 to 3.34, versus 3.56 to 4.20 for the respective full sums. These statistics stay positive under the model assumptions, indicating that the prior *approximate statistic* does not arise solely from those omitted variants. The ranges aggregate different weight sets and must not be interpreted as confidence intervals, cross-score comparisons or evidence of independent replication. The numerator, LD covariance and denominator all change when a marker is removed. For example, the inferred FFS statistic can increase when a marker is omitted; that does not establish improved prediction or justify pruning.

The [complete 40-row table](stage6_aou_insomnia_two_locus_removal.csv) also includes single-marker omissions and AFR-similarity-group calculations. Group Z values have different precision, frequencies and reference LD; no test of a group difference or inference about equity or transportability is made. The 15-marker two-omission result and 22-marker one-omission result are different research sums, and none is a patient-level score effect. Clinical insomnia is a coded outcome, not frailty; All of Us contributed to a previously inspected meta-analysis, discovery/relative overlap remains unresolved, and the broader project has many post hoc analyses. Do not treat nominal p values as confirmatory.

The computation reuses each group's saved source frequencies and marginal Z values, project weights and 1000 Genomes EUR/AFR reference LD, including the source-ALT, biallelic diploid reversal and HWE assumptions documented in the [EUR](stage6_aou_eur_weighted_z_sensitivity_review.md) and [AFR](stage6_aou_afr_weighted_z_sensitivity_review.md) reports. Marker-level allele representation, FFS weight orientation, per-variant sample overlap and target LD remain uncertain; different nominal results do not establish differences between associations. Reproduce with `work/stage4_env/bin/python work/aou_complete/insomnia_locus_removal_sensitivity.py`. The script checks that every full-score result reproduces the earlier output before omissions; the [receipt](stage6_aou_insomnia_two_locus_removal_receipt.json) records the output hash. All 40 rows and their unique identifiers were verified.
