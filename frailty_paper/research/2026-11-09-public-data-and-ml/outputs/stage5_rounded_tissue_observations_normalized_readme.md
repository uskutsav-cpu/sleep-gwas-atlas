# Rounded tissue-specific Atlas observations

`stage5_rounded_tissue_observations_normalized.csv` combines 12 manually recorded tissue-specific website rows for four queued variants from the three named source CSVs. It preserves each source row, assay, biosample, gene label where supplied, displayed values and source caveat. Rows are observations, not necessarily unique experimental tracks: separate Atlas rows may have the same visible labels, and no unique track ID was recorded. The separate whole-genome AVI observation for rs323509 is deliberately excluded because it is not a tissue-specific score.

These selective observations cover pituitary, naive CD4 T cells and testis; they do not establish complete brain, pituitary, muscle or immune coverage. Tissue-name similarity does not prove sample identity or that the measured and predicted traits are identical. In particular, testis findings in the rs323509 region should not be presented as sleep- or frailty-relevant tissue evidence without additional support.

The website displayed raw values rounded to two decimals. A displayed value of 0.00 is not evidence of an exact zero or a reliable sign. The website quantile scale has not been reconciled with the API's quantiles. The observed expression rows are not event-matched splice-site, splice-usage or junction predictions; accessibility and promoter scores are not direct gene-expression measurements. Therefore no prediction–QTL concordance is assigned. These observations are partial model predictions, not functional validation, disease risk estimates or evidence of causal mediation.

Reproduce with `python3 outputs/stage5_compile_web_tissue_observations.py`. The source files retain the original transcriptions. For association and QTL context, use `stage5_variant_gene_tissue_phenotype_integrated.csv` and its README, and interpret the source-specific reviews `stage5_initial_tissue_prediction_review.md`, `stage5_rs323509_atlas_initial_observations.md` and `stage5_rs60271_atlas_tissue_review.md`. The required full-precision, systematic multi-modality scoring remains pending API access.
