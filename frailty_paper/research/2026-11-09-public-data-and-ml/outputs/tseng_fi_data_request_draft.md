# Draft request: diagnosis-and-laboratory frailty-index GWAS

**Draft only; not sent.** Corresponding-author details are available on the [publisher's article page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11357-023-00874-7). Adapt the affiliation and data-use details before sending.

Subject: Request for FI GWAS summary statistics and phenotype definitions

Dear Dr. Chattopadhyay and Dr. Lee,

We are studying the relationship between insomnia and different frailty measures. Your paper, “Utilizing multimodal approach to identify candidate pathways and biomarkers and predicting frailty syndrome in individuals from UK Biobank,” describes a 52-item diagnosis-and-laboratory frailty index and an FI GWAS in 178,566 participants. We would like to evaluate it as an alternative frailty construct in a comparison with published insomnia GWAS.

If available and permitted under the relevant data-use terms, could you share or direct us to the **complete genome-wide FI summary statistics**, rather than only the significant variants? To support harmonization and LD score regression, we would need chromosome and position, genome build, rsID or allele-specific variant identifier, effect and other alleles, effect estimate, standard error, p-value, allele frequency, and sample size per variant where available. A README specifying effect-allele and phenotype scales, ancestry and quality filters would be very helpful. If the Fried-type phenotype GWAS is also available, we would welcome the equivalent file and its case/control counts and definition.

Could you also share the phenotype-construction code or full mappings for the 52 items, including the exact ICD codes, laboratory thresholds and units, missing-data rules, transformations, covariates, and participant inclusion criteria? The main text groups the items as 38 ICD deficits, 10 blood tests and four status measures, while the supplementary table numbers 36 named diagnosis-type deficits, 12 biomarker/anthropometric measures and four other measures. We would appreciate guidance on the implemented grouping and on whether any sleep, insomnia, tiredness or fatigue diagnosis codes entered the FI.

Could you clarify the FI scale as well? The text describes a proportion but reports a maximum of 29.67, while Table 2 reports 0–29.6 with a mean of 0.104. For the FP, the text describes cases as having more than three criteria, while the supplement uses at least three; the reported robust counts also differ between the results text and Table 2B. Which scale, cutoff and final counts were used in the analyses?

We would also appreciate clarification of the FI association thresholds: the text reports 31 loci below 10⁻⁸ and 21 below 10⁻⁹, while our count of Table 3A's printed values is 20 below 10⁻⁸ and 10 strictly below 10⁻⁹, with one further value printed as exactly 1.00 × 10⁻⁹. Is a different threshold or set of results intended?

We recognize that this FI differs from other frailty indices and that UK Biobank sample overlap may affect comparisons. We will follow the conditions you specify and report the definitions and limitations. Please let us know whether the files are available, any applicable access conditions, and whether there is a preferred citation.

Thank you for your consideration.
