# RBM6 brain-expression follow-up with larger reference LD

## Interpretation

An exploratory analysis found a **candidate** pairwise overlap between the RBM6 excitatory-neuron eQTL and each of the insomnia and FI signals in a restricted 541-variant set. The results **do not establish a causal RBM6 mechanism or independent validation**: the restriction removes most of the probability mass of the earlier disease credible sets, the FI and insomnia GWAS use overlapping UK Biobank data and an FI containing an insomnia item, the brain effective sample size is uncertain, and LD is external. There is no joint three-trait posterior. HFRS and FFS returned no credible sets on this subset, so no eQTL–HFRS or eQTL–FFS SuSiE-coloc posterior was computed; that does not prove no shared signal.

For the source-wide 757-donor working-N setting and p1=p2=1e−4, p12=1e−5, the pair represented by eQTL lead rs2526751 and insomnia lead rs2071206 had PP.H4=0.980; the eQTL and Mak FI pair with those same reported lead IDs had PP.H4=0.984. These are **two separate pairwise model results**, not independent evidence or the probability that all three phenotypes share a causal variant. For shared prior p12 from 1e−6 to 5e−5, the respective PP.H4 values ranged 0.830–0.996 and 0.863–0.997. Other signal pairs favored distinct associations at the illustrated prior. The values are only conditional on the selected SNPs and modeling assumptions, and the very incomplete support below precludes a confident interpretation even when PP.H4 is high.

## Coverage and signal sensitivity

The source SingleBrain RBM6 excitatory-neuron regional extract has 2,088 rows; 1,126 have a positive fixed-effect standard error and a resolved effect allele. Only 541 matched the exact position and allele pair of the existing 1,034-SNP five-GWAS/gnomAD input. The marginal hospital-frailty marker rs2282749 is present: its C allele had fixed eQTL beta −0.120533 (SE 0.012637) in the source and a positive HFRS association in the project's prior lookup. Marginal alignment does not prove colocalization or mediation. The RBM6 model SNP rs2353586 is not usable here: its source fixed-effect SE is zero, which is missing or unestimable evidence rather than a zero biological effect.

When compared with the previous 1,034-SNP gnomAD fits, the 541-SNP set retains **22.9% and 25.0%** of the component mass within the two Jansen insomnia credible sets, and 0%, 71.1% and 30.1% within the three Mak FI sets. It retains none of the prior FFS set. These are coverage measurements of earlier fitted sets, not true causal-variant probabilities. Reduced SNP support can force apparent agreement or alter the number and identity of signals. The new signal-pair posterior must therefore not be compared as though it were estimated over complete credible sets. `stage6_rbm6_brain_signal_coverage.csv` contains the sizes and component masses.

Using 757 (the release's unique-donor count) as working N, the brain fit returned one credible set; using 983 (samples) returned three. The release-wide counts are not the gene-specific effective N, and the different results underscore sensitivity to the assumption rather than identify the correct number of signals. Under both settings the Jansen fit returned two sets, Mak FI two, and HFRS and FFS zero; all fits converged. The brain Z/LD consistency diagnostic was approximately 0.0134–0.0140. There is no universal threshold, and convergence or a small diagnostic does not establish adequate LD, N, allele harmonization or model calibration.

A preliminary direct QTL–Jansen exact position/allele intersection found 563 non-palindromic, positive-SE variants in the same 1-Mb window before reference matching, compared with the present 541 five-trait/reference matches. The reproducible feasibility count is in stage6_rbm6_brain_jansen_direct_feasibility.json. This suggests that requiring all five traits accounts for only a small part of the loss; it does not establish full disease credible-set coverage or make the 563 an automatically valid coloc input. Additional data with more complete, reliable regional QTL statistics and appropriate ancestry-matched or in-sample LD are needed. Missing fixed-SE records were not assigned zero effects.

## Methods and reproducibility

`stage6_rbm6_brain_gnomad_prepare.py` aligns the full-statistics **fixed beta and fixed SE** by genomic position and non-palindromic allele pair to the previously aligned reference set; it does not mix the browser's random-effect p values with fixed effects. `stage6_rbm6_brain_gnomad_coloc.R` uses 95% SuSiE credible sets, L=10 and fixed residual variance, NFE signed LD for the neuronal eQTL and UK Biobank-based GWAS, and FIN LD for FinnGen HFRS. It writes the diagnostics, set members, fits and all available signal pairs across three shared priors. `stage6_rbm6_brain_signal_coverage.R` compares retained SNPs and component masses with the earlier gnomAD fit. The aligned tables and source receipts preserve allele and data provenance.

The SingleBrain file was retrieved as a checked regional byte range; its whole-file checksum was not verified. GnomAD block and index receipts are described in `stage6_rbm6_gnomad_coloc_review.md`; the custom index decoder remains unverified against an official Hail read. None of the calculations use individual patient outcomes or validate either genetic-score prototype.
