# Public blood-expression dataset GSE140358: sample-count audit

[GSE140358](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE140358) is an open blood-expression microarray dataset on platform GPL16686; it offers possible observational frailty biology context, not genetic-score validation. Its submitted GEO series matrix contains **25 sample accession columns total**. Its condition metadata labels are `Frail` for 4, `Robust` for 13, and `Dep` for 8. The meaning of `Dep` and its relationship to any formal classification should be checked in source documentation; it should not be automatically recoded as a frailty or control label. The submitted description says 25 individuals were studied using Timed Up and Go, gait speed and Tilburg Frailty Indicator measures. The available metadata and study descriptions do not establish a linked participant-genotype dataset, clinical insomnia outcomes or this project's FI/FFS scores.

A later [published analysis](https://doi.org/10.1002/brb3.70657) describes this dataset as containing 25 frail and 25 robust people. That description conflicts with the **25 total accession columns** and the specific condition labels in the retrieved GEO matrix. Different selection criteria or an unreported additional source are possible, but the claim of 50 samples cannot be supported from this file. A [doctoral thesis](https://addi.ehu.es/bitstream/handle/10810/61137/TESIS_SAENZ_ANTO%C3%91ANZAS_ANDER.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1) describes 25 participants and different consensus group counts (7 and 5), reinforcing the need to verify which classification and subset were used instead of equating them with GEO's labels.

The very small frail-labeled group, potential group-selection and cellular composition differences, multiple probes and unknown genetic linkage make this dataset unsuitable for score performance validation or causal/cell-specific claims. No exploratory differential expression test was performed. Reproducible metadata audit: `work/geo_gse140358/audit_metadata.py`; aggregate result `outputs/stage6_gse140358_metadata_audit.json`.
