# PREDICT-NEURODEGEN: open sleep and activity data, no score inputs established

Checked 29 October 2026. The [open Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/20734280) describes a neurodegeneration research project involving sleep–wake monitoring, frailty and selected genetic markers, and its metadata reports CC BY 4.0. We downloaded its sole 846.7 kB ZIP and verified MD5 `c324b026822df5630c1e126cb141de17`. Its two workbooks contain three sheets: global EEG features, videogame task metrics, and smartwatch-derived sleep, step and heart-rate fields. The archive is a potentially useful open wearable/brain phenotype resource, but its contents do **not** establish usable genetic-score validation data despite the broader project description.

A read-only schema audit found 238 nonempty EEG rows with 238 distinct nonempty IDs, 231 videogame rows with 231 distinct IDs, and 231 smartwatch rows with 231 distinct IDs. The smartwatch sheet's `TimeSleepTotal_mean_total` column is nonblank in 138 rows. These are nonblank counts, not verified valid observations, clinical insomnia cases, independent participants, or a sample size for a specific analysis; spreadsheet dimensions alone are larger because of empty formatting. Exact IDs overlap between the two sheets, but this does not independently establish a validated linkage, authorized secondary linkage, comparable dates, clinical diagnoses or complete cases. No participant rows, dates or identifiers are exported in our audit.

The displayed workbook headers contain **no named genotype, genetic score, explicit frailty index, Fried phenotype or clinical-diagnosis column**. This is a header-level inventory of the downloaded files, not proof that the broader project collected no such data or that unstructured notes have no information. Therefore, the archive does not provide the necessary documented participant genotypes, score variants and frailty outcomes for our validation. Sleep-duration and device quality fields are not a clinical insomnia diagnosis, and step measures alone are not an FI or FFS. Device details, calibration, study protocols, participant diagnoses, age, missing-data mechanisms, phenotype ascertainment, permitted linkage and score-variant coverage have not been established; these must be resolved before any secondary analysis or ML claim. The selection of patients with neurodegenerative disease can also limit applicability to general older populations.

The [aggregate schema receipt](stage6_predict_neurodegen_schema_audit.json) records worksheet names, headers, nonblank counts and the archive digest. Reproduce with the bundled workspace Python and `work/predict_neurodegen/schema_audit.py`; no participant data were copied into the receipt. No application or external message was sent. If data custodians publish an explicitly documented linked genotype-and-clinical release, recheck it against the fixed score alleles, phenotype definitions and permitted use; the present archive does not establish that release.
