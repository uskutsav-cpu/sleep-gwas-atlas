# Selected candidate genes: biological context and evidence boundaries

These examples explain why a gene could be interesting to investigate. A known gene function does not establish that an exploratory marker changes that function or that it explains insomnia, frailty, or their overlap. The genes below were selected from the existing evidence inventory, not by an unbiased pathway screen.

| Gene or molecular ID | General biological context | What this project actually supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ENSG00000251574 / LOC105379109 | [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/105379109) describes an uncharacterized noncoding RNA with expression restricted toward testis. Its function in sleep or frailty is not established. | A selected testis splice event has a four-variant molecular credible set and qualified pairwise FI and insomnia colocalization evidence. Testis relevance, sample overlap and prior sensitivity remain unresolved. [Identifier context](stage3_rs323509_gene_identifier_context.md); [credible-set LD](stage3_rs323509_molecular_credible_set_reference_ld.md). |
| SMAD5 | [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/4090/) describes a signaling protein activated by bone morphogenetic protein receptors and involved in TGF-beta/BMP-related signaling. This is broad biology, not a specific insomnia or frailty mechanism. | rs7734045 has bulk molecular associations involving SMAD5 and separate naïve CD4-cell molecular evidence. Splice-event multiplicity, incomplete credible-set coverage and the limitations of cell colocalization prevent a causal claim. [Muscle splicing review](stage3_smad5_splice_coloc.md); [CD4 review](stage4_cd4_smad5_colocalization_review.md). |
| FOXP2 | FOXP2 is a transcription factor; [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/93986) links pathogenic dosage changes to a speech and language disorder. That clinical association does not imply the candidate SNP has the same effect. | Exact-marker tissue eQTLs and separate FI/FFS gene-level associations are available. The selected pituitary molecular comparison omits a high-PIP indel and should not be treated as decisive. [FOXP2 molecular review](stage3_foxp2_pituitary_colocalization.md); [gene comparison](stage4_candidate_fi_ffs_gene_comparison.md). |
| NEGR1 | [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/257194/) catalogs neuronal growth regulator 1 and evidence for cell-adhesion processes. This broad annotation is not proof of a specific sleep circuit. | The caudate analysis was a fallback after a different candidate query returned no data; no molecular credible set was reported. [NEGR1 follow-up](stage3_negr1_brain_colocalization.md). |
| ADARB1 | [NCBI Gene](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/104) describes an RNA-editing enzyme that edits glutamate receptor pre-mRNA. Its general RNA-processing role does not establish a disease mechanism for the candidate marker. | Bulk-tissue expression and splicing QTLs and an exploratory skeletal-muscle splice-event analysis exist; credible-set coverage is incomplete and the selected model is single-signal. [ADARB1 review](stage3_adarb1_muscle_splice_finemapping.md); [region inventory](stage3_region_evidence_inventory.md). |

These examples should be interpreted alongside the full [variant–gene table](stage3_initial_variant_gene_table.csv), including alternative genes, tissues and negative or unavailable tests. Functional follow-up would require allele-specific evidence and appropriate disease-relevant cell models.
