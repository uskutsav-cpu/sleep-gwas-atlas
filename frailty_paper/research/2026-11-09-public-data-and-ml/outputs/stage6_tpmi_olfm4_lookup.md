# TPMI sleep-disorders lookup: rs1909650

[TPMI PheWeb](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/13:53217760-G-A) displays rs1909650 as GRCh38 chr13:53217760 G/A. The project prototype uses GRCh37 chr13:53791895 G/A and counts G; its recorded Ensembl lift reaches the same GRCh38 position on the positive strand. The rsID has additional alleles in other reference data. The source effect allele and the exact multi-allelic contrast have not been independently established, so the displayed sign cannot yet be assigned to the project's counted allele.

For the broad [sleep disorders phenotype](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327), the browser displays effect −0.022 (SE 0.011), p = 0.026, with 45,836 cases and 287,785 controls. Values are rounded. This is a nominal association for a postselected single-marker check, without a prespecified multiple-testing adjustment; it is not evidence that the effect is robust, concordant with a project allele, or specifically due to insomnia. Sample or relatedness overlap with all discovery and selection sources is unverified. OLFM4 is the site's nearest-gene label and is not evidence of gene mediation.

The finding does not validate either score, shared causal risk, or individual prediction. A score-level analysis would need all candidate effects with verified allele and build harmonization, suitably matched LD and a defined testing plan. Individual prediction additionally needs linked genotypes and outcomes. Related prior lookups and download limitations are in `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.

TPMI requests acknowledgement and citation of [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x).
