# Diabetes Prevention Program: genotype, sleep and frailty feasibility

**Decision:** DPP/DPPOS is a disease-focused, controlled-access lead, not a verified free public individual-level validation dataset. Its linked sample and permission for this insomnia–frailty project are unconfirmed. No records were downloaded or scored.

## What is documented

* The [NIDDK DPP repository](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/study/38) links to [dbGaP phs000681.v2.p1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs000681.v2.p1) for candidate-gene and SNP-array data; a [2026 DPP genetics paper](https://diabetesjournals.org/diabetes/article/75/7/1329/172040/Global-and-Partitioned-Polygenic-Risk-Scores) also describes a DPP genome-wide dataset there. These descriptions do not prove that every project marker was measured, imputed or available in compatible releases.
* A [published DPPOS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8087265/) assessed five Fried physical frailty criteria in 2,385 participants at year 8 and 2,289 at year 10, approximately 12 and 14 years after randomization. Grip and 15-foot walking were measured; activity and exhaustion used adapted questionnaires, and unintentional weight loss was included. Frailty was present in about 3–6% depending on treatment and visit. These numbers do **not** establish the usable genotype/sleep/frailty intersection. Participants were originally recruited for elevated diabetes risk, intervention and subsequent follow-up; selection, treatment, follow-up attrition, ancestry and generalizability require attention. Baseline frailty was not measured in that study.
* The public [DPPOS phase 3 dictionary](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/public/study_document/DPPOS_V6/DPPOS_Phase3_Data_Dictionary.pdf) lists `BDSLEEP`, labeled feeling about sleep in the past week; the [bridge dictionary](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/public/study_document/DPPOS_V6/DPP_Bridge_Data_Dictionary_Screen_Reader_V1.pdf) labels a `BDSLEEP` item about sleeping as well as usual. These are related measures, but we have **not** verified their coding, equivalence across files, collection timing, release availability, or intersection with the frailty and genotype records. A single depression-inventory sleep item is not a clinical insomnia diagnosis. The frailty study also drew exhaustion from depression-inventory questions; test whether sleep and exhaustion variables share wording or related measurement context before claiming independence.
* A separate [phase 3 respiratory form](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/media/studies/dppos/Forms/DPPOS_PHASE3_Forms/Q24.pdf) includes `Q24SLSDY`, sleep disruption attributed specifically to lung condition, at visits 15A and 18A. It should not be substituted for general insomnia, and its relation to years 8 and 10 requires visit-specific investigation.

## Access and next decision

The [NIDDK DPP repository](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/study/38) states permitted uses including diabetes, related complications and heart disease and separately describes genetic typing restricted to non-insulin-dependent diabetes. Those statements are a **warning about research scope**, not a definitive interpretation of all consent groups or the separate dbGaP genetic release. The [dbGaP data-access process](https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/beta/FAQs/data-content/) reviews study-specific consent restrictions. Before an application, inspect the exact release and consent-group use limitations and ask the responsible data-access authority whether the proposed research falls within them. Do not assume that reframing the question makes an otherwise prohibited use permissible. No access application or contact was made.

Cost, project eligibility, genotype and phenotype linkage across DPP and DPPOS, ancestry-specific usable sample, candidate allele coverage, and discovery/selection overlap all remain unknown. Even if data access is free, approvals and institution requirements remain; the low frailty prevalence and selection make precision a serious concern. A feasible future analysis would freeze score coding and prespecify the sleep item, five-component outcome, handling of treatment arm, ancestry and loss to follow-up, and comparison with age/sex baseline. If those conditions cannot be met, do not claim independent validation.
