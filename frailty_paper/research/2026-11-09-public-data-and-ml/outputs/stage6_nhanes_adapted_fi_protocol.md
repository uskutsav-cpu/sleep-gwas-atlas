# NHANES adapted deficit index: analysis plan

The original plan was written before fitting associations but mistakenly specified interview weights; after results were inspected, the analysis was corrected to MEC weights in accordance with CDC guidance. The matched-sample sensitivity was added post hoc. Exploratory secondary analysis of publicly released NHANES 2011–12 and 2013–14; not a preregistered study. Adapted from the 30 items in https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13246825/ and its supplementary table. Four source items unavailable or unsuitable in one or both cycles (ENQ010, OSQ010A, PFD069H, OHQ011) are omitted for a common index; the source PFD069H records days with hearing problems, not a simple hearing-loss diagnosis. Source sleep trouble SLQ050 and fatigue DPQ040 are omitted to avoid direct overlap with the exposure. This is not the published 30-item FI, is not a validated clinical index, and no frailty classification threshold will be applied. Other cognitive, mood and functional deficits may still reflect sleep problems.

Index of 24 common variables: CDQ010 CDQ001 BPQ020 MCQ160E MCQ160F MCQ160B DIQ010 MCQ160A MCQ160L MCQ220 MCQ084; DPQ050 DPQ070 DPQ080 DPQ020 DPQ010 DPQ090; PFQ061F PFQ061G PFQ061K PFQ061J PFQ061A PFQ061B PFQ061R. Binary yes=1/no=0, other responses missing, including borderline diabetes. DPQ 0=0, 1–3=1; refusal/unknown missing. PFQ 1=0, 2–4=1, 5/7/9 missing. No skip is imputed as absence. Index is mean of observed items, requiring at least 20 of 24. Analyze adults >=60, comparing SLQ050 yes versus no (told doctor of trouble sleeping), adjusting for age and sex; survey strata, PSUs and 2-year MEC examination weights. Report each cycle separately as mean index differences and design-based 95% CIs. Sensitivity: remove concentration and psychomotor items DPQ070/080, requiring at least 18 of 22 items. Treat missing responses as missing and describe eligibility; complete-case comparisons are not assumed unbiased. This phenotype is not insomnia diagnosis and the analysis is cross-sectional, not genetic validation or evidence of causation.
