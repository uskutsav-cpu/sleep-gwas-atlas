# rs7734045: direct TenK10K CD4-naïve chromatin association

The TenK10K CD4-naïve full cis-QTL table contains an exact GRCh38 `5:136202653:A:C` row for the project marker rs7734045 and peak `chr5:136132117-136134284`. For ALT C the source reports beta = **+0.7922**, SE = 0.0373 and nominal p = 6.96 × 10⁻⁸¹; ALT frequency = 0.2959. The [study methods](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.27.25334533v2.full) describe TensorQTL slopes per ALT allele; ALT C is also the minor allele at this locus in the source. This is an observed chromatin-association estimate for this peak, not a disease risk effect or individual prediction.

The peak-level permutation table reports a different lead, `5:136187682:T:G`, with FDR q = 5.25 × 10⁻⁶⁸ and an associated nominal threshold of 6.85 × 10⁻⁶. The candidate's nominal p falls below that source-provided threshold. This observation should not be confused with an independently adjusted candidate-specific p-value; many correlated variants can pass for one peak. The source's broader correction and selection apply at the peak level. The candidate lies approximately 69 kb from the peak and does not physically overlap it.

The same cis table reports `5:136131620:C:G`, the top SNP named in the source's SMAD5 eQTL–caQTL colocalization, with beta +0.7980 and p = 1.54 × 10⁻⁸¹. Both raw-table variant IDs give REF and ALT; however, C/G is palindromic and the source's precise underlying genotype harmonization has not been audited. The previously measured EUR and EAS external reference LD between these variants is high (r² ≈ 0.98 in each), which explains why the two associations are correlated; the source does not establish their target-sample LD. [Full molecular review](stage6_tenk10k_smad5_molecular_review.md).

The peak overlaps the independently generated CASCADE naïve-CD4 peak at `chr5:136132565-136133590`, and both sources use GRCh38. Source-defined peaks have different boundaries, so this is a coordinate overlap, not proof that regulatory signals, disease signals or individuals are independent. TenK10K donors overlap OneK1K, and potential CASCADE–FinnGen GWAS sample overlap remains. Colocalization with SMAD5 expression is model-dependent and **does not establish colocalization with insomnia or frailty**; no causal disease mechanism is inferred.
