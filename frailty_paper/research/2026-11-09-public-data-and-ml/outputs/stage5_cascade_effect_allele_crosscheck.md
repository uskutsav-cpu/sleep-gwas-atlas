# CASCADE eQTL effect-allele check

The publicly available [FinnGen single-cell pipeline](https://github.com/FINNGEN/singlecell-pipeline/blob/f185d93232307c42b8c03da32ec74b118017a021/wdl/saige_qtl/saige_qtl.tasks.wdl#L643) invokes SAIGE-QTL with `AlleleOrder=ref-first`. The pipeline's [fine-mapping export](https://github.com/FINNGEN/singlecell-pipeline/blob/f185d93232307c42b8c03da32ec74b118017a021/wdl/saige_qtl/saige_qtl.export_zfiles.wdl#L80-L94) parses the third and fourth marker-ID components as allele1 and allele2 and renames `BETA` to `beta`. [SAIGE-QTL documentation](https://weizhou0.github.io/SAIGE-QTL-doc/docs/single_step2.html) defines Allele2 as the effect allele. Together, these sources support an ALT-oriented eQTL effect when the pipeline and marker IDs are applied as described. This repository commit is a public snapshot, not a verified deployment commit for the CASCADE browser; the browser's downstream transformations have not been fully traced. Therefore the portal beta's orientation is **strongly supported, but not independently confirmed end to end**. The caQTL pipeline is different and this conclusion should not automatically be transferred to its beta values.

As a supplementary consistency check, 65 of the 70 SMAD5 naïve-CD4 OneK1K molecular credible-set alleles had an exact-ID CASCADE top eQTL for SMAD5 in the matching-label naïve-CD4 population. All 65 CASCADE reported betas and the OneK1K ALT-oriented betas were positive. Eight of these are indels. These are correlated variants at one locus, selected using molecular evidence; the agreement is not 65 independent replications, a proof of the browser's allele convention, or a colocalization test. The remaining five lacked this exact top-feature match; this does not prove absence of their eQTL. The results are in `stage5_cascade_onek1k_smad5_crosscheck.csv`, reproducible with `python3 outputs/stage5_crosscheck_cascade_onek1k.py`.

For rs7734045 (`chr5:136202653:A>C`), the portal's SMAD5 beta is +0.34181 and AlphaGenome's naïve-CD4 total and polyA RNA scores are +0.0004586 and -0.0014015, respectively. If the supported ALT convention is retained by the deployed browser, total-RNA and portal eQTL have the same sign and polyA and portal eQTL have opposite signs. The variant, gene and broad cell label match; the donors, protocols, transcript measures and scales do not. These tiny model scores and opposite assay-track signs make the sign check exploratory and not a clinical or causal conclusion.

For chr5:136207208:G>A, the portal beta is +0.31912 and the total and polyA model scores are -0.0004606 and -0.0020425. Under the same conditional orientation assumption, both are opposite in sign to the portal eQTL. This variant and rs7734045 are correlated alternatives, not independent evidence. The previously observed reporter result for this variant was not part of the source study's selected subset.

Raw, cell-label-matched eQTL/model rows are preserved in `stage5_cascade_model_eqtl_comparison.csv`; betas remain labeled as reported. Full browser transformation provenance, sample overlap, current-model training provenance and suitable disease colocalization remain unresolved. The ATAC/caQTL comparison requires separate assessment because the reported chromatin peak is distant from the variant and the local model score is a different feature.
