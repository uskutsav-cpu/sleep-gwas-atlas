# rs34290943 and INKA1: skeletal-muscle molecular evidence

GTEx portal `gtex_v10` reports an exact-marker skeletal-muscle eQTL between rs34290943 (`chr3:49124227 T/C`, GRCh38) and **INKA1** (ENSG00000185614), p = 5.87 × 10⁻¹⁷. An independently processed GTEx v8 skeletal-muscle dataset, eQTL Catalogue **release 7, QTD000281**, also has a nominal exact-marker eQTL (p = 1.15 × 10⁻⁷). These are different processing versions of related GTEx data, not independent biological replications. They link the marker to expression, not necessarily to the insomnia or frailty association.

In the Catalogue muscle molecular fine-mapping file, INKA1 has one **reported** credible set with 88 distinct variants (90 rows because some variants have multiple rsIDs). The 88 variants span GRCh38 chr3:49352409–49694428. The largest reported molecular PIP is 0.1322 for rs9836291 at chr3:49660026 G/A. **rs34290943 itself is not in this reported set.** Its lack of membership does not by itself rule out association or a GWAS shared signal; the molecular and trait fine-mapping models answer different questions. PIPs are molecular-model quantities and must not be interpreted as individual disease risks. [Full molecular credible-set rows](stage3_inka1_muscle_credible_set.csv).

The original 1-Mb candidate-centered extraction missed part of the molecular set. A subsequent 2-Mb extraction spans the Catalogue's fitted region, `chr3:48803261–50803261`, retaining 3,483 INKA1 variant rows ([receipt](stage3_catalogue_inka1_2mb_extract_receipt.json)). This dataset and the separate `gtex_v10` API are version-pinned; remote regional extraction and index have not been checked against an independent provider checksum. Candidate coordinates/build and molecular allele coding remain subject to source verification. [Exploratory GWAS–QTL colocalization](stage3_inka1_muscle_colocalization.md) has since been performed; no shared causal variant, distal regulatory interaction, or trait mediation is established.
