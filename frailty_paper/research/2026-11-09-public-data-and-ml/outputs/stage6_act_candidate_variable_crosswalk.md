# ACT: candidate variables for a future score-validation analysis

This crosswalk uses ACT's public [2025-freeze data dictionary](https://www.actagingresearch.org/download_file/f1058bf6-f034-46ba-94bf-96af632448a4/9) and the historical [Gray et al. frailty definition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3738027/). It is a feasibility map, not executed phenotype derivation or proof of access, linkage, completeness, or sample size. ACT and NIAGADS must authorize any relevant linkage and data use.

| Target | Dictionary sheet and fields | Proposed use and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Insomnia symptoms | `Form66 - Sleep`: `ISI_item1`–`ISI_item7`, `ISI_NumberValid`, `ISI_Score`, `frm66` | Seven-item ISI can be available starting 2022; the provided sum is missing unless all seven items are valid. This measures symptom severity, not a clinical diagnosis. The take-home form is optional. |
| Sleep-disturbance alternative | `Form66 - Sleep`: `Sleep_Tscore`, `Sleep_NumberValid` | PROMIS T-score can be available from 2016 when at least six items are valid; coding of individual questions differs for some items. Do not equate it to ISI. |
| Weakness | `Form85 - Grip`: `Grip_Strength`, `Grip_Able`; `Form85 - Anthropometry`: `BMI`; appropriate sex field | Historical definition applies sex- and BMI-specific thresholds to average dominant-hand strength in kg. Dictionary strength averages up to three nonmissing attempts; the historical analysis describes three attempts. Inspect completeness and inability reasons before coding. |
| Slowness | `Form85 - Gait`: `Gait_Time`, `Gait_Able`, `Gait_Aid` | Historical cutoff is gait speed <0.6 m/s for a 10-foot test: 3.048 / `Gait_Time` <0.6 for positive seconds. The dictionary permits an average based on one completed test; the historical paper describes two. Unable, not assessed, and missing must not all be coded as slow or non-slow. Timing precision changed toward end of 2022. |
| Low activity | `Form77 - Exercise`: `ExerDays_Sum`, `Exercise_Reg` | `Exercise_Reg=0` describes fewer than three activity-days of at least 15 minutes; any of eight missing component fields makes the derived value missing. Historical definition uses the same type of exercise frequency, but confirm coding and timing. |
| Weight loss | `Form85 - Anthropometry`: `Weight` at current and preceding visits | Compare with a valid previous measured value, allowing for visit timing. Dictionary weight is in rounded pounds; the historical paper conflicts between ≥7.5% in its table and >7.5% in text. Resolve source code and rounding before choosing a threshold. Missing prior weight is not absence of loss. |
| Exhaustion | `Form73_Form23 - CESD`: `cesd_item7`, `cesd_item20` | These describe effort and inability to get going. Values 0–3 encode frequency, 9 means don't know/refused and blank means missing. Verify the source's exact positive-frequency threshold and missing-item rule before implementation. |
| Earlier sleep symptom | `Form73_Form23 - CESD`: `cesd_item11` | Restless sleep is a single item, not the ISI or diagnosis. The same instrument supplies exhaustion items, so account for related symptom content. |
| Linkage | Each visit-level form: `SUBJECT`, `VISIT`; genotype source key not established | The ACT dictionary uses subject and visit keys internally. It does not prove the NIAGADS or dbGaP genetic IDs can be linked or that permission is granted. `VISIT` approximates follow-up years and is not a substitute for verified calendar dates. |

For a future analysis, first request a permitted joint sample flow by genotype QC, adult participants with complete relevant sleep and frailty variables, phenotype timing, and genotype availability for each of the 33 exact allelic contrasts. Obtain per-score complete-case counts and an explicit overlap and relatedness audit with all discovery sources. Compare FI and FFS weights only after validating their original allele orientation; the above map supports a modified Fried phenotype and does not establish a valid FI. These observational outcomes do not establish that a score adds predictive accuracy until evaluated in a genuinely independent sample.
