# Independent frailty GWAS options for candidate follow-up

An additional [2026 Super Seniors assessment](stage6_super_seniors_validation_feasibility.md) identifies a healthy-aging case–control GWAS, but its data are available on request with approvals rather than as a verified unrestricted download, and the outcome differs from FI and FFS. A separate article's mention of UKB-excluded summary statistics concerns suicide attempt, not frailty.

## Canadian Fried phenotype GWAS

[Borhan et al. (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41514-026-00363-z) report a Fried-style phenotype GWAS in 23,105 European-ancestry Canadian Longitudinal Study on Aging participants (mean age 63), separate from the UK Biobank discovery cohorts in the project. Frailty was classified as non-frail, pre-frail or frail and analyzed using ordinal regression. The phenotype includes self-reported exhaustion, so it is not a sleep/fatigue-free sensitivity phenotype. Its 3-category model differs from the original 0–5 FFS GWAS, so it can provide cross-phenotype support, not identical-measure replication.

The authors report one genome-wide significant lead, rs147311617 (p=4.98×10⁻⁸), near PLXNC1. It is not one of the current 15 candidates. The study reports no independent replication sample and no effect estimates, confidence intervals or SNP heritability estimates; it is therefore not immediately usable for signed allelic replication, score weights or the project's LDSC heritability gate. The analysis adjusted for sex, genetic PCs, batch and number of chronic conditions, **not age**, citing potential multicollinearity. That model differs materially from the project’s sources and could alter interpretation. A nominally significant lead just below 5×10⁻⁸ also needs replication; proximity or eQTL annotation alone does not establish its causal gene or a connection to insomnia.

The article’s data statement refers to applying for access to CLSA data and does not identify a downloadable full summary-statistics file. The [CLSA public publication listing](https://www.clsa-elcv.ca/our-publication-type/gwas-summary-data/) includes this paper under “GWAS Summary Data,” but the visible entry links to PubMed; it does not establish that full signed results can be downloaded. An author or CLSA request would need to confirm whether per-variant test statistics, effect alleles, sample sizes, genotype QC and signed effect estimates/SE are available or can be computed. Non-overlap with each sleep discovery cohort should be checked rather than assumed. No file or access request was sent.

## FinnGen hospital frailty score

The [Mak et al. (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43587-025-00925-y) paper used FinnGen R12 to build HFRS and a dementia-excluded variant, with independent UK Biobank phenotype replication. Correction: the repository already contains the full FinnGen HFRS GWAS, [GCST90707291](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90707001-GCST90708000/GCST90707291/), and its checksum matches the catalog metadata. The supplement gives threshold-selected variants, including rs12536335 for the full and dementia-excluded HFRS. A subsequent lookup of all 15 candidates in the full HFRS GWAS is in `candidate_hfrs_full_gwas_review.md`. The full dementia-excluded GWAS file has not been verified.

FinnGen HFRS and FinnGen insomnia may share individuals; UK Biobank HFRS and the original UKB FI, FFS and insomnia inputs may share participants. HFRS also includes broad diagnostic categories that can capture drowsiness or vital exhaustion (`hfrs_sleep_overlap_audit.md`). It is a useful distinct frailty measure but does not settle sleep-item overlap or independently validate shared insomnia–frailty causation. All 15-candidate analyses remain subject to post-selection and multiple-testing concerns.

## Practical next action

Request complete, signed summary statistics and phenotype definitions from the corresponding investigators for each study; for CLSA also ask for age-adjusted sensitivity results if available. If only p-values are obtainable, they can support a limited two-sided association lookup but do not identify risk-allele direction, support score construction or enable standard signed cross-trait LDSC. Perform any candidate lookup only after build, alleles and sample overlap have been verified; do not interpret absent hits from thresholded tables as null associations.
