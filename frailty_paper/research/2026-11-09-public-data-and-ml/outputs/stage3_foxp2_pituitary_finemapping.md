# FOXP2 pituitary eQTL: molecular fine-mapping evidence

For FOXP2 expression in pituitary, eQTL Catalogue release 7 lists one molecular SuSiE credible set, `ENSG00000128573_L1`, containing **four unique variant IDs** (reported minimum within-set r² 0.965). Its leading three variants each have a posterior inclusion probability of approximately 0.312 in the published molecular model; this represents shared uncertainty across correlated variants, not three independently proven causal variants. The fourth has PIP approximately 0.038. [Selected source rows](stage3_foxp2_pituitary_susie_credible_set.csv); [source receipt](stage3_foxp2_pituitary_susie_receipt.json).

None of the three project FOXP2-labeled markers—rs12536335, rs12671330 or rs17312861—matches any of the four source credible-set positions. This does not mean their PIPs are zero or that their marginal associations are invalid: credible sets include only variants selected into the reported set, and tagged variants can lie outside it. The GTEx v10 portal separately reports exact-marker FOXP2 eQTLs for rs12536335 and rs12671330 in pituitary. These two resources are related GTEx releases/reprocessing, not independent replications. Neither the credible set nor a marginal eQTL establishes that FOXP2 mediates insomnia or frailty.

The Catalogue source is **QTD000301**, reprocessed GTEx v8 as represented in release-7 metadata; the sample size is 237 pituitary samples. The selected compressed file was read to completion and locally SHA-256 hashed, but no provider hash was verified. Five input rows collapse to four unique variant IDs because one variant has more than one rsID; deduplication recovers the source-reported set size and total PIP approximately 0.972. Such duplicate identifiers are not independent variants. The [Catalogue's data guidance](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/eqtl/Data_access/) describes duplicated rsID rows.

No GWAS credible set, regulatory enhancer-to-gene link, causal direction or cell type has been established by this check. An [exploratory GWAS–FOXP2 colocalization](stage3_foxp2_pituitary_colocalization.md) has since been performed; it excludes a credible-set indel and is highly qualified. The source region is approximately 2 Mb; trait-specific full summary statistics and appropriate ancestry-matched LD are needed for a comparison with the FI, FFS and insomnia signals. The availability of a molecular credible set alone does not justify combining its PIPs with the project GWAS results.
