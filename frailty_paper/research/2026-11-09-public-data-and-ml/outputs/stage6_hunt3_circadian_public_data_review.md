# Open HUNT3 circadian-gene insomnia results

Checked 29 October 2026. [Bragantini et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30862195/) analyzed 73 variants in nine circadian genes across seven combinations of insomnia symptoms in HUNT3. The abstract reports 6,029 people with symptom information, including 3,577 cases, within the larger cohort. Sleep onset, maintenance and early awakening are distinct outcomes; this is not a frailty study or a clinically interchangeable measure of every insomnia phenotype.

A [freely accessible CC BY 4.0 supplementary dataset](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.7834895.v1) includes a spreadsheet with 511 variant-by-symptom-group rows (73 unique rsIDs, seven combinations each) and allele labels, odds ratios, confidence intervals and p-values. This is a targeted set of published summary results, **not individual genotypes or a full genome-wide association file**. The authors' original paper and documentation should govern allele interpretation, outcome definitions, multiple-testing handling and eligible uses. Their HUNT participants may overlap other HUNT research; independence from any particular project study and relative overlap remain unverified.

We compared the 73 unique spreadsheet rsIDs to the variant IDs in both project scoring files and found **zero exact ID matches with the 15-marker and zero with the 22-marker list**. This does not rule out alternative rsIDs, nearby variants or LD proxies and does not establish a null association. Since the data do not give the selected variants' complete aligned results, participant-level scores, or a frailty outcome, they cannot validate either score. They are useful public background for the mentor's circadian-gene and symptom-specific questions; they do not show that our candidate variants act through these clock genes.

The downloaded file is `work/hunt_circadian_supp.xlsx`, SHA-256 `0a4e4aa589e652c22e1e914378e131e9b0500f0c6c06d9e54fd48787fbd57272`. The Figshare API response is `work/hunt_circadian_figshare.json`. No HUNT individual-level data were accessed.
