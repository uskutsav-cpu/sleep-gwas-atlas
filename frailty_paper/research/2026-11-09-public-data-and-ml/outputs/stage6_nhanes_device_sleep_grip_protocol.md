# Fixed local analysis: device-classified sleep and grip in NHANES

Before loading the device data, analyze cycles 2011–2012 and 2013–2014 separately in adults aged 60 or older. Use CDC's PAXDAY valid sleep-wear minutes per calendar day (PAXSWMD), not idle sleep mode. Exclude partial days by requiring PAXTMD≥1439 minutes, and require at least 1,200 minutes classified as valid wake or sleep wear (PAXWWMD+PAXSWMD). Require at least four eligible days; use each participant's mean sleep-wear minutes divided by 60, with no imputation for the rest of a day. These thresholds are working QC choices, not formally validated definitions of sleep or NHANES standard thresholds. Check their impact on exclusions and sensitivity to requiring at least five days.

Fit the same survey-weighted linear model in each cycle: combined grip (MGDCGSZ, kg) regressed on mean predicted sleep-wear hours per calendar day, age and sex, using MEC weights and PSU/strata with complete-case domain analysis. Report coefficient per hour, t-based confidence interval, p-value and complete-case sample and design degrees of freedom. Do not select the preferred cycle on outcome. Model and thresholds are local pre-analysis choices, not a registered protocol; prior NHANES sleep and grip analyses and external studies are known. Sleep-wear includes predicted sleep during the 24-hour day and is not a clinical diagnosis or a nightly sleep-period measure. Grip is a frailty-related component, not full FI/FFS. Neither cross-sectional phenotype association nor the data files test a genetic score.
