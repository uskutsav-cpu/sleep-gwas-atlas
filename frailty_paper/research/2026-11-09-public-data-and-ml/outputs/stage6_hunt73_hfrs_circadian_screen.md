# Exploratory HUNT3 circadian-variant screen in FinnGen hospital frailty

This is an exploratory **candidate-panel coverage and association screen**, not a genetic score test or a test of shared causality. We took the 73 circadian-gene rsIDs in the [public HUNT3 insomnia-symptom supplement](stage6_hunt3_circadian_public_data_review.md) and scanned the project's complete FinnGen hospital frailty risk score (HFRS) GWAS, GCST90707291. The full source scan read 21,294,560 data rows and reproduced its expected MD5 `38d9434f50a4493def9d2c9d1cc1b4b3`.

All 73 rsIDs appeared in the frailty source; 72 had at least one row whose two allele letters matched the HUNT table. The one unmatched pair was rs4795424; it must not be coded as a null result. This is an identifier-and-allele-string check: the HUNT spreadsheet did not establish each genomic coordinate in this comparison, so build, strand and multiallelic representation are **not fully harmonized**. No effect-direction comparison was performed. Source results are in [the screen table](stage6_hunt73_hfrs_rs_allele_screen.csv); the [script](../work/hunt73_finngen_hfrs_screen.py) reproduces the scan when the source file is mounted.

Among the 72 matching rows, ten HFRS p-values are below 0.05; the lowest is 0.00352. For this descriptive panel alone, none passes Benjamini–Hochberg 5% across 72 p-values (the minimum adjusted p is approximately 0.135). The variants can be correlated, and the panel was selected for circadian-gene relevance and contains seven HUNT symptom contrasts per SNP; the 72-test calculation is not a formal joint insomnia–frailty multiple-testing correction. No claim of shared effect or gene prioritization follows from nominal p-values. An allele-pair match and a nearby gene label do not establish a causal variant, gene or cell type.

HFRS is derived from hospital diagnostic codes and is not the Fried score or deficit FI; its diagnostic components can include sleep-related categories. HUNT insomnia and FinnGen frailty differ in phenotype and ancestry context, and participant/relative overlap has not been conclusively audited. The panel contains no exact rsID from either project score, so it does not validate those scores. A rigorous next analysis would require verified locus/build/alleles, harmonized HUNT effect definitions and uncertainty, a prespecified independent-sample joint hypothesis, LD-aware adjustment and appropriate phenotype overlap assessment.
