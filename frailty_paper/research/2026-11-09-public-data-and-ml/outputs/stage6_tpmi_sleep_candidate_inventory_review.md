# Taiwan sleep-disorders candidate inventory

**Update, 28 October 2026:** TPMI also provides an [insomnia-specific phecode 327.4](stage6_tpmi_insomnia_specific_lead.md). This inventory reports broad phecode 327 and should not be presented as results for that insomnia-specific outcome. Twelve variants have subsequently been inspected for the more specific outcome, with limitations documented in the linked note.

The [inventory](stage6_tpmi_sleep_candidate_inventory.csv) brings the 15-marker prototype checks into one table. Twelve variants have manually transcribed, rounded [TPMI PheWeb](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/) results for [sleep disorders](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327). Their displayed allele pairs match the prototype and the 12 GRCh37 coordinates each map uniquely on the positive strand to the displayed GRCh38 positions using Ensembl. Source effect-allele direction and multi-allelic contrasts remain unverified; the displayed signs must not be interpreted as effects of the score's counted alleles. The target phenotype is a broad diagnosis grouping, not insomnia alone.

The two nominal p < 0.05 values among these 12 are rs11790060 (0.011) and rs1909650 (0.026). They were not independently prespecified, and testing/selection involved more than these 12 lookups; these are not multiplicity-adjusted replication findings. The others have statistically unclear estimates, which do not establish absent or zero effects. Manual browser values are rounded and have not been checked against full precision source data.

Three variants remain unverified: rs113851554, rs323509 and rs78206187. Searches returned no exact rsID matches; focused attempts for rs113851554's GRCh38 G/T and rs323509's A/C returned browser errors, while the rs78206187 GRCh38 A/G search had no verified result. These observations do not prove that the variants were absent or unassociated. No complete-file checksum or source-variant QC has been obtained. Full-score weighted association was not calculated, and a partial score must not be reported as the full score. The TPMI cohort's participant and relatedness overlap with all discovery and selection studies has not been independently established.

The results are exploratory, separate-population single-marker context; they cannot provide individual prediction, discrimination, calibration or validate the 15- or 22-marker score. Full-score summary analysis would require complete verified effects and allele coding, suitable target LD and an explicit analysis plan; individual-level validation additionally requires linked genotypes and outcomes. The study's clinical cohort and broader sleep outcome are distinct from the original FI and FFS. Source-method and individual-marker notes are linked from `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.

TPMI requests acknowledgement and citations to [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x). To reproduce, run `stage6_tpmi_sleep_candidate_inventory.py` to reconstruct the manual transcriptions and then `stage6_tpmi_liftover_audit.py` to check the 12 mapped coordinates and add the mapping-status column. The latter saves response hashes and a receipt; its checks do not establish source effect alleles or score validity.

A subsequent [effect-allele audit](stage6_tpmi_effect_allele_audit.md) found that PheWeb's documented input convention expects ALT effects and TPMI's published analysis code is consistent with that convention. The exact production conversion into this browser was not traced, so this is supporting evidence rather than definitive source-specific confirmation; direction remains unverified.

The [EAS reference-frequency check](stage6_tpmi_missing_eas_frequency_review.md) found zero and one counted-allele copies among 1,008 called chromosomes for rs113851554 and rs78206187, respectively, but 69 for rs323509. The first two may be difficult to study under TPMI's reported frequency filter; these are external-panel frequencies and do not prove target availability or absence.
