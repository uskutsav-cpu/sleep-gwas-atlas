# CHARLS: new controlled genetic-data lead

Checked 28 October 2026. CHARLS is relevant to longitudinal sleep and frailty, and its [genetic-data access page](https://charls.charlsdata.com/pages/Data/2026-charls-geneticdata/en.html), dated 26 October 2026, explicitly describes controlled access to individual-level human genetic data including whole-genome sequencing. This is **not unrestricted public genotype data**. The page's text is embedded in a JavaScript variable and may not appear in simplified page views; the public HTML was inspected directly.

A prospective [CHARLS sleep-duration and frailty study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41514-026-00466-7) reports 18,282 participants and a 44-item FI. It uses sleep duration, not a clinical insomnia diagnosis. The study does not establish that its analytic participants have usable genotypes, that its FI excludes sleep or fatigue, or that the genetic and longitudinal survey datasets can be linked under the proposed project. Check the supplement and raw field definitions before interpreting phenotype overlap.

## Access restrictions and unresolved items

CHARLS states that the principal investigator must hold a doctoral degree or equivalent and be affiliated with an eligible research institution in China; international participation requires formal collaboration and applicable approvals. The page also describes ethics approval, research proposal and security requirements, review by its access committee, and review of outputs before public release. Consult the current custodian guidance rather than assuming these conditions are exhaustive. No request has been submitted, and no email has been sent.

The number of sequenced participants, overlap with sleep/frailty observations, raw variant availability, quality, project-specific permissions, restrictions on cross-border access, and any application or compute fees are unverified. The study could provide a useful non-European evaluation if these matters are resolved, but it cannot be called freely downloadable or confirmed no-cost. Establish cohort/relative overlap with discovery sources and assess ancestry-specific score transfer; a small score derived largely from European GWAS may not transfer well. Do not equate sleep duration with insomnia, or a 44-item index with Fried physical frailty.

No CHARLS participant data were accessed. Independent prediction remains unvalidated. If the research team has a qualified Chinese collaborator, a project-specific application can ask for genotype–phenotype overlap counts, allele/assembly documentation, sample-level principal components, FFS and sleep-free FI measures, linkage and output rules, and all fees before proposing a full evaluation. The existing [NHATS access lead](stage6_nhats_genetic_validation_feasibility.md) has a stated no-fee restricted access route and is a separate alternative.

A separate [2025 CHARLS sleep–frailty FI item audit](stage6_charls_sleep_fi_overlap_audit.md) found a CESD-10 depression deficit that can indirectly incorporate restless sleep and effort. This requires source-code confirmation, and must not be assumed to describe every CHARLS FI or the 2026 study.
