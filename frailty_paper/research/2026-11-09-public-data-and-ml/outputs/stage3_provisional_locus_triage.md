# Provisional locus work queue for variant-to-gene analysis

The [19-row queue](stage3_provisional_locus_triage.csv) groups all 22 earlier markers plus rs323509 and records the available FI, FFS, three insomnia and hospital-frailty p-values for a representative marker, along with GTEx v10 exact-marker molecular genes. It is a **candidate-centered working order**, not a genome-wide discovery list, a ranking of causal genes, or an independent test of 19 loci.

To make the order reproducible, the representative within each group has the smallest **maximum** of three unadjusted p-values: Atkins FI, Ye FFS and FinnGen insomnia. Groups are ordered by this same maximum. At a single prespecified marker, max(p) can serve as an intersection–union test of whether all three traits have marginal associations if the inputs are valid; here the markers and approach are post-selected, tests are correlated, samples overlap or may overlap, and the ordering was not preregistered. We use it **only as an exploratory work-queue rule**; it does not establish a common causal variant, account for all tested markers or control selection bias. The other GWAS p-values are provided for context rather than counted as independent replications.

The first five representatives under this rule are rs323509 (max p = 0.000158), rs34290943 (0.00110), rs12671330 (0.00170), rs2838788 (0.00400), and rs817509 (0.0223). rs323509 was explicitly requested from the older 15-marker list; its MVP-insomnia p = 0.05089 and it did not pass the later 22-marker joint nominal screen. Its high position in this particular queue should not be confused with discovery or with a genome-wide significant three-trait result. rs7734045 is also retained, with its muscle SMIM32 and SMAD5 molecular associations and the [first exploratory colocalization](stage3_smim32_skeletal_finngen_coloc.md).

Markers are grouped by same-chromosome GRCh37 positions connected by gaps ≤1 Mb. This creates 19 descriptive groups; a 1 Mb rule is not LD clumping, conditional analysis, a biological boundary or proof of signal independence. The smallest p across multiple markers in a group adds further selection. The GTEx v10 columns list genes and association-row counts at the exact markers; abundance of QTL rows is not used to rank or equate a gene with causality. No QTL row is a statement about all variants in a region. Mak FI/FP p-values are provided when present in the 22-marker matrix; they are not available there for rs323509.

The local [PLACO+ audit](stage3_existing_placo_ffs_audit.md) found potentially relevant FinnGen-insomnia/FFS shards but could not authenticate their configuration or gating, so those p-values are **not** used in this work queue. As full regional summary statistics, fine-mapping, molecular colocalization, tissue and regulatory evidence become available, update the evidence table and replace this preliminary rule with a documented, multidimensional assessment. No rank establishes individual risk, a causal variant or a causal gene.
