# Initial AlphaGenome Atlas observation: rs323509

The public [AlphaGenome Atlas variant view](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr5%3A104746478%3AA%3EC) was inspected for the verified GRCh38 A→C allele on 27 October 2026. The website displayed **AVI Phred 1.0** and “Top 78.7% predicted impact of all genome-wide SNVs.” These are rounded website values; AVI is a molecular impact prioritization score, not an insomnia or frailty risk probability.

The web track table listed testis RNA-seq entries for ENSG00000251574, with displayed raw scores of **0.00** for both polyA-plus and total RNA tracks at the site's two-decimal precision. Their signs and unrounded magnitudes cannot be inferred from this display. The website also showed values in a column named “Quantile score,” but their website display scaling has not been reconciled with the API's signed quantile definition; they are preserved as displayed without interpretation in [the observations](stage5_rs323509_atlas_initial_observations.csv). A search for “splice” in the add-track dialog returned no rows; this is not evidence that the model predicts no splicing change, as that dialog may not expose every scoring modality. No junction-specific prediction was obtained.

The measured testis QTL is a **splicing** association for this gene. Rounded RNA-expression scores do not test agreement or disagreement with that splice junction, and the relevance of testis to sleep and frailty is unresolved. Full-precision gene, splicing and tissue-specific scores remain to be retrieved through the official API, which requires an account-specific key. The [molecular credible-set and LD review](stage3_rs323509_molecular_credible_set_reference_ld.md) remains separate from the model's predictions.
