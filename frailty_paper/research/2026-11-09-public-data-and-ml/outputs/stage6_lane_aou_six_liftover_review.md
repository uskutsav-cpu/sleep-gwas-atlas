# Coordinate and allele-pair check for six insomnia candidates

29 October 2026. Taking the Lane supplementary-table coordinates as GRCh37, all six candidates map to their All of Us GRCh38 positions with a single forward-strand Ensembl assembly mapping. In each case the pair of bases in Lane's effect/other column is identical as an unordered pair to the All of Us REF/ALT pair. The [per-candidate audit](stage6_lane_aou_six_liftover_audit.csv) includes source and target positions, pairs, mapping response checksums and query URLs. For example, the rs113851554 coordinate maps from 2:66750564 to 2:66523432, and the compared alleles are T/G and G/T.

This validates consistency **conditional on the source-build assumption**; the supplementary workbook does not itself establish that assumption in the extracted table. Mapping coordinates and matching letters do not prove that effect estimates refer to the same allele, or solve the representation of multiallelic variants, strand conventions in underlying source studies, phenotype or sample overlap, or validity of either genetic score. The returned mapping is an assembly mapping, not independent confirmation of causal variant identity. In particular, the [MEIS1 multiallelic check](stage6_meis1_aou_multiallelic_lookup_review.md) shows both G/T and G/A results at the mapped position; G/T is the extracted comparison.

The lookup was made through the [Ensembl assembly mapping endpoint](https://rest.ensembl.org/documentation/info/assembly_map). Reproduce with `python3 work/cross_cohort/verify_six_liftover.py`; it requires an active connection to the Ensembl GRCh37 REST server and curl. Output CSV SHA-256 `cb1043235516b6e0b6bfddad2c6ffbf78a90b2377f696e8f164e7ec2bad6f9dc` records the response obtained for this run; Ensembl data and services may change. The [cross-cohort results](stage6_lane_aou_six_variant_comparison.md) remain postselected associations, not individual-score validation.
