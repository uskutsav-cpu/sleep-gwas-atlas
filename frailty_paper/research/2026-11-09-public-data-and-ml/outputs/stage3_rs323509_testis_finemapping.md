# rs323509: testis splicing QTL fine mapping

GTEx v10 reports rs323509 (`chr5:104746478 A/C`, GRCh38) as an sQTL for several introns of `ENSG00000251574` in testis. For the junction chr5:104739412–104739497, the portal p-value is 5.09 × 10⁻²⁷. In eQTL Catalogue release 7 QTD000340, reprocessed GTEx v8 testis splicing reports the same junction with a different cluster label (`5:104739412:104739497:clu_21568_-`) and nominal rs323509 p = 5.65 × 10⁻²¹. These are related GTEx releases and reprocessing, not independent replication. The Catalogue source metadata lists 322 testis samples.

The Catalogue's molecular fine mapping lists a four-variant credible set for this junction; rs323509 has reported molecular PIP **0.291**, while rs6421926 has 0.311, rs60271 0.346 and rs161645 0.0334 ([source rows](stage3_rs323509_testis_splice_credible_set.csv)). The reported minimum within-set r² is 0.967 in the molecular fine-mapping source, consistent with high LD among those four variants in that reference. These values describe uncertainty under the molecular model and are not probabilities of insomnia or frailty. Other introns in the same gene have separate reported sets; the different junctions are not independent replications. The rs323509 allele has multiple reported alleles in reference resources, so full allele contrast verification is necessary.

This is a testis molecular observation and does not establish a causal disease gene, a relevant sleep or frailty tissue, or a shared trait variant. An exploratory [regional comparison](stage3_rs323509_testis_colocalization.md) has since been completed for FI, physical frailty, FFS and insomnia. Even a regional colocalization would not by itself prove mediation or relevance of this tissue.
