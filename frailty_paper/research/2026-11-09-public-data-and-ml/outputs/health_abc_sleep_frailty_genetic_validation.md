# Health ABC: potential controlled-access validation cohort

Checked 29 October 2026. The Health, Aging and Body Composition study is a scientifically relevant lead for the project's sleep and frailty outcomes, but linked data access, consent, cost and usable sample size are **not established**. It is not an openly downloadable participant-level dataset.

## Evidence for the required domains

* The [NIA study description](https://www.nia.nih.gov/healthabc-study) says Health ABC enrolled 3,075 initially well-functioning older Black and White men and women, ages 70–79, at Memphis and Pittsburgh. Its data include genetics, physical performance and questionnaires. This selected U.S. community cohort should not be assumed representative of all older adults.
* The [dbGaP genome-wide study `phs000169.v1.p1`](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs000169.v1.p1) describes 2,932 people evaluated with a genome-wide scan; the same page lists 2,802 consented subjects for the shown release. These are distinct source counts, not a verified count of records authorized for this project. Although the study focuses on visceral adiposity, its [study-specific data-use addendum](https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/aa/wga.cgi?stacc=phs000169.v1.p1&view_pdf=), checked 29 October 2026, lists consent group 1 as General Research Use (GRU), with use for research and no individual identification. This removes the earlier concern that the study title itself implies an obesity-only restriction; it is not approval of our project or evidence that all requested phenotype linkage is permitted. Recheck the current release and agreement when applying.
  The [public GRU distribution manifest](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000169/phs000169.v1.p1/manifest/manifest_phs000169.CIDR_VisceralAdiposity.v1.p1.c1.GRU.pdf) documents genotype calls and a phenotype table in the controlled package. Its [phenotype data dictionary](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000169/phs000169.v1.p1/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000169.v1.pht000729.v1.HABC_Phenotype.data_dict.xml) lists baseline demographics, smoking, drinking, activity, BMI, education and visceral fat but **no listed sleep, grip, walking or frailty variables**. Therefore this specific package alone does not document the outcomes needed here; an approved additional clinical data source and authorized linkage are necessary unless a different release supplies them. The manifest records files, not public access to participant rows.
* A [year-5 sleep study](https://academic.oup.com/biomedgerontology/article-pdf/63/10/1069/1489392/1069.pdf) reports 2,264 older participants and insomnia symptoms. This is separate from the genotyped count and does not establish their intersection. Its year-5 timing must be aligned with the chosen frailty assessment.
  [Goldman et al.'s methods](https://academic.oup.com/biomedgerontology/article/63/10/1069/559215) describe separate questions for sleep onset, nighttime awakening with difficulty returning to sleep, early awakening with difficulty returning to sleep, and sleep medication. Responses use five frequency categories; the paper dichotomized each as frequent at 5–15 or 16–30 times per month versus never, rare or 2–4 times per month. This threshold is not equivalent to CLSA's three-times-per-week threshold, and medication use is not itself an insomnia symptom or diagnosis. Exact Health ABC field identifiers, codes, missingness and access are still unverified; prespecify symptom definitions after checking the relevant codebook. The paper's year-5 sample required an in-person clinic visit and fatigue data, so its sample size and prevalence cannot be applied to the prospective genetic validation subset.
* A [published study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9037366/) constructed physical frailty at the second annual visit; another used the [10-point SAVE measure](https://academic.oup.com/biomedgerontology/article/78/11/2152/7228041), adapted from the Fried phenotype. SAVE and simple gait/chair-rise definitions are not the Ye five-component FFS; choose and document the exact measure. Whether the relevant year-5 or later frailty components are available in the same participants as insomnia and genotypes remains unverified.
  A separate [Health ABC longitudinal analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5644033/) reports its two-component gait/chair-rise outcome in years 1, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 11—not year 5. Thus it cannot simply be labeled contemporaneous with the year-5 insomnia survey. The year-6 measure could in principle follow year-5 sleep, but participant-level dates, baseline status, survival, follow-up, availability and authorized linkage must be checked, and this endpoint is not a full Fried score. Its 2,000 participants and 631 incident events describe a hearing-selected analysis through year 11, not our year-5 eligible sample or its event count.

## Access, overlap and priority

NIH [dbGaP guidance](https://www.grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/sharing-policies/accessing-data/dbgap) requires an eligible institutional investigator, institutional signing official and approval. NIH educational material describes dbGaP as a no-data-fee route, but that does not establish that all relevant Health ABC clinical data are included in the genetic release, that cross-resource linkage is allowed, or that the full project has no cost. NIA's study page calls for a proposal; whether a separate clinical release, sponsor, fees or agreement is required needs confirmation from the current access terms. No proposal or communication has been sent.

The [NIA proposal instructions](https://www.nia.nih.gov/healthabc-study) explicitly call for a Health ABC sponsor for an outside first author, a proposed analysis, variables, mock tables and timeline, with approval before analysis and clearance before reporting. A [working proposal draft](health_abc_proposal_research_draft.md) supplies the research sections but has no named sponsor, institutional attestations or submission.

Health ABC was included in the [CHARGE gait-speed GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5310665/) already examined in the project. This is prior cohort-level overlap, not proof that its participants contributed to score selection or the future target sample. Audit all discovery and selection sources, participant/relative overlap and the chronology of the gait lookup before treating a future analysis as held out. The different ancestral groups are useful for studying transferability, but small within-group sample sizes, ancestry-matched QC and LD, and unequal performance uncertainty matter; avoid treating self-identified race as genetic ancestry.

Before application, verify the current release and agreement, approved genotype–phenotype linkage, precise questionnaire and frailty items by visit, 33 distinct score variants and imputation quality, and counts for the joint eligible sample and events. This is a controlled-access candidate, not evidence that either score predicts a phenotype.

The dbGaP listing identifies the `Human1M-Duov3_B` array. [Illumina](https://sapac.support.illumina.com/downloads/human1m-duo_product_support_files.html) lists a v3 manifest, and an [Oxford annotation index](https://www.chg.ox.ac.uk/~wrayner/strand/sourceStrand/index.html) names a build-37 annotation for that exact revision. An attempt to retrieve the latter on 29 October 2026 returned HTTP 403. Presence on an array manifest would not prove a variant passed cohort QC or was available in imputed data; absence from the typed array would not rule out imputation.

**NCBI platform inventory follow-up:** The public [GEO GPL6984 platform table](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL6984) names the exact `Human1M-Duov3_B` array. A complete streamed scan of its `SNP_ID` field found exact rsID matches for **5/15** 15-marker variants and **8/22** 22-marker variants, 12 of the 33 distinct rsIDs across both lists. The source uses build 36 and array strand conventions; only identifiers were compared, not coordinates or alleles. Alternative IDs or probes can be missed, and a listed probe does not establish that it was present or usable in the Health ABC release after QC. A nonmatch is not evidence that the SNP cannot be imputed. These counts indicate that using only the exact typed markers found would be a partial score, not the validated prototypes. [Machine-readable scan](healthabc_geo_platform_screen.json); [streaming script](../work/healthabc_geo_platform_stream.py). The completed scan reached the table-end marker and did not retain participant data.

**Coordinate follow-up:** All 33 distinct project GRCh37 positions had one positive-strand GRCh36 mapping under the UCSC hg19-to-hg18 chain. A second complete scan found only the same 12 candidates by their mapped chromosome/position; each also had the same rsID. Thus no additional coordinate alias was found in this table, and the 5/15 and 8/22 counts are unchanged. This is a coordinate and identifier screen, not an allele/strand harmonization or target QC check. Other probe naming, genome mapping errors, platform revisions and imputation remain possible. [Coordinate-screen receipt](healthabc_geo_platform_coordinate_screen.json); chain SHA-256 `fc312e924e970dee936a69972768c6f84591d3a6daddbe598e49321c5fb1d253`. The streaming script now performs this coordinate check and requires `pyliftover` and the local chain file.

**Array allele-label comparison:** For the 12 matched rows, comparing each GEO `SNP` allele pair as written with the project GRCh37 pair gives three direct string matches (`rs11152363`, `rs12671330`, `rs7734045`) and nine complementary string matches. None of these 12 pairs is strand-palindromic. This is a textual comparison only: [Illumina explains](https://knowledge.illumina.com/microarray/general/microarray-general-reference_material-list/000002837) that TOP/BOT is defined by local sequence and does not by itself identify the reference-genome plus strand. Therefore do not automatically flip the nine or treat the other three as proven aligned. Sequence-to-reference mapping, actual dbGaP release coding, allele contrast and genotype QC must be checked first. No participant-level data were accessed.

**Probe-flank alignment:** The GEO table's `TopGenomicSeq` flanks for all 12 probes matched the UCSC hg18 sequence exactly in one orientation at their listed positions (seven forward, five reverse-complement). After orienting the bracketed TOP-sequence alleles to the reference, all 12 pairs match the corresponding GRCh37 score allele pairs; the hg18 reference base occurs in each pair. This is a platform-level sequence alignment using the mapped coordinates, not a determination of how genotypes are exported in the controlled Health ABC file. It also does not establish target QC, allele frequencies, imputation of missing markers, the GWAS effect contrast or score validity. [Per-probe alignment and request hashes](healthabc_probe_reference_alignment.json); [reproduction script](../work/healthabc_probe_reference_alignment.py).

**Imputation precedent:** A [published longevity GWAS](https://academic.oup.com/biomedgerontology/article/72/10/1411/3003200) reports Health ABC imputation using 1000 Genomes reference haplotypes for 1,663 subjects in its analysis. A [separate adiposity study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3660116/) reports imputed loci in both European- and African-ancestry Health ABC analyses. These establish that imputation has been performed in research on this cohort; they do not establish that those imputed files are currently distributed, that the 21 unmatched score rsIDs were imputed accurately, or that the respective subjects overlap the sleep/frailty cohort. The publicly inspected `phs000169` manifest lists genotype-call files but does not clearly identify an imputed dosage release. A later authorized release or project-specific imputation would require confirmation of permissions, computing resources, reference and variant-level quality; its cost is unverified.

The [reference-only subset sensitivity](healthabc_partial_count_reference_review.md) finds that the 8 typed-array count markers correlate 0.656 with the complete 22-marker count in 503 EUR reference samples and 0.568 in 661 AFR reference samples. These are not Health ABC participants or outcome associations and do not justify substituting a partial score for the full prototype.
