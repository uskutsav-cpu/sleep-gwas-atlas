# Remaining two marker-overlapping ENCODE elements

**rs1909650, chr13:53217760 G>A:** The allele overlaps EH38E3067913, a CA-CTCF element. SCREEN lists PCDH8P1 among the nearest genes, and exact-allele GTEx v8 associations include PCDH8 in tibial nerve and ENSG00000279325 in anterior cingulate cortex. PCDH8 and PCDH8P1 are different gene annotations. The six displayed solo STARR-seq reporter results have nominal p-values greater than 0.05; this does not exclude activity in other contexts. [SCREEN gene evidence](https://screen.wenglab.org/GRCh38/ccre/EH38E3067913/genes).

**rs2604551, chr4:15089577 T>G:** The allele overlaps distal-enhancer candidate EH38E3574753. SCREEN reports a K562 Hi-C loop to CPEB2 and CPEB2-DT (experiment ENCSR479XDG), plus GTEx v8 exact-allele eQTL associations with CPEB2 in esophagus mucosa and whole blood and with LINC00504 in skin. These are tissue-specific contact/association records, not proof that the variant alters the contact or mediates disease. A CPEB2 association on the same page at chr4:15089514 A>G belongs to a different variant. Of seven solo STARR-seq records, MCF-7 displays nominal p = 0.03 but FDR = 0.27; none of the remaining six has nominal p below 0.05. [SCREEN gene evidence](https://screen.wenglab.org/GRCh38/ccre/EH38E3574753/genes).

The [reporter table](stage5_remaining_marker_ccre_reporter_results.csv) preserves all 13 records. The assays concern regulatory fragments, not REF-versus-ALT tests of these markers, and model-training independence is unverified. GTEx release versions share data. Candidate genes and cell types therefore remain hypotheses requiring tissue- and allele-matched AlphaGenome predictions, colocalization and independent functional tests.
