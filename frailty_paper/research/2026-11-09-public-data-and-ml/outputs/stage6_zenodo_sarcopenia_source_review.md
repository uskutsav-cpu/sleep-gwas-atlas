# Zenodo latent sarcopenia summary data: source under review

The open [Zenodo record 17069328](https://zenodo.org/records/17069328) describes genome-wide summary data for a Genomic SEM sarcopenia-related factor using muscle mass, strength and physical function, with UK Biobank as the claimed source. Sarcopenia is a distinct construct from FI and Fried frailty, and participant overlap with our UK Biobank-derived discovery/selection data is likely; it is not an independent outcome-validation cohort. No linked participant genotypes or sleep outcomes are provided.

Several metadata issues require clarification before interpreting associations. The record explicitly states 522,632 participants from UK Biobank, while [UK Biobank reports 503,317 recruited volunteers](https://www.ukbiobank.ac.uk/about-our-data/our-participants/). The figure could reflect an effective, summed or other count, or inaccurate wording; its meaning is unverified and must not be reported as the number of distinct UK Biobank participants. The underlying trait studies, factor loadings and sign convention, effect-allele semantics, genetic build, derivation, variant-specific information and external study documentation are not supplied in the record as checked. A preliminary comparison of one file row with [Ensembl GRCh37](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/variation/human/rs72631875?content-type=application/json) agrees with the rsID's chr1 position, but one row does not verify the assembly genome-wide. The Zenodo API metadata reports a CC BY 4.0 license; cite the dataset and assess any additional applicable data-use terms.

A complete streamed scan of 7,319,145 rows passed the Zenodo-reported compressed-file MD5 `d9b5c1e7513e209bc57326264c95c356`; see [receipt](stage6_zenodo_sarcopenia_receipt.json) and reproduce with `work/stage4_env/bin/python work/stream_sarcopenia_zenodo.py`. Of the project candidates, **14/15** and **22/22** match an rsID, inferred GRCh37 coordinate and unordered allele pair; rs11152363 is absent by exact rsID, and the two lists share four markers. [Match audit](stage6_zenodo_sarcopenia_match_audit.json); [candidate source rows](stage6_zenodo_sarcopenia_candidates.csv). Exact matching does not settle the source build, allele coding, multiallelic context, latent factor orientation or cohort makeup. Absence does not establish a null or an unmeasured SNP. The extracted variant values are postselected, with overlap to UK Biobank and uncertain source metadata. They are not validation of either score or a measured predictive effect, and no gene or direction of risk should be inferred from these data until the source documentation is resolved.

## Frequency provenance check

A follow-up compared the reported `effect_allele_frequency` for **all 22 score markers**, after orienting the independently stored 1000 Genomes phase-3 EUR reference frequencies to the source effect allele. Every one of the 22 differs from the project's 503-person reference frequency by less than 0.00005, the half-unit rounding tolerance for four decimal places; the largest absolute difference is 0.000049702. The source values are thus all compatible with those reference values rounded to four decimals. This striking agreement strongly motivates checking whether the column was filled from that or a similar reference panel; the comparison alone cannot establish the source of the field or prove the other association columns are incorrect. Treat the frequencies as **unverified study frequencies**, and do not use them to assert cohort ancestry, confirm effect-allele coding or perform study-specific frequency QC. The 22 markers were already selected for this project, not randomly sampled across the genome. [Per-marker comparison](stage6_zenodo_eaf_reference_check.csv), [receipt](stage6_zenodo_eaf_reference_check.json); reproduce with `python3 work/check_zenodo_reference_frequencies.py`. The existing [reference-panel audit](22_score_reference_group_diagnostic.md) identifies 503 EUR individuals with complete diploid calls for these markers. Clarification of the source column and the reported 522,632 count is needed before weighted score-level or LD analyses.

As a basic internal consistency check, the two-sided normal p-value calculated from beta/SE agrees with the reported p-value within 0.01 log₁₀ units for each of the 32 extracted unique markers. This checks arithmetic only; it cannot verify phenotypes, sample independence, standard errors, or genuine genotype–outcome associations.

## Systematic noncandidate check

To test whether the frequency agreement was restricted to postselected project markers, we streamed the entire source file a second time (again matching its published MD5) and selected row numbers 123,456 plus every 300,000th row before inspecting their frequencies, yielding **24 systematic rows** across the file. This is a systematic sample, not a random genome-wide sample. All 24 had unique matches by GRCh37 chromosome, position, rsID and biallelic allele pair in the public UCSC 1000 Genomes phase-3 reference, with complete calls for the 503 EUR-labeled reference individuals. After orientation to the source effect allele, **all 24** source frequency values matched the corresponding reference frequency within 0.00005, the half-unit tolerance for four decimal places. Maximum absolute difference was 0.00004712. This extends the finding beyond the candidate markers, supporting the hypothesis that the reported frequencies are reference-derived or otherwise share the same origin. It does not prove the data pipeline, indicate that the association effects are wrong, or resolve the stated sample size or factor sign. Treat these frequencies as unverified rather than independent cohort measurements.

Reproduce the source selection with `work/stage4_env/bin/python work/zenodo_genomewide_sample.py` and reference comparison with `python3 work/zenodo_reference_sample_audit.py`. The [systematic comparison](stage6_zenodo_systematic_reference_audit.csv) and [receipt](stage6_zenodo_systematic_reference_audit.json) include each tested row and matching status. Source reference VCF is accessed remotely via UCSC; panel membership is the locally recorded phase-3 panel. No participant genotypes are saved. The systematic rows are evenly spaced in file order, not a representative or exhaustive audit of all variants; independent repeat access or alternative reference content could change availability. This is source-QC evidence, not score validation.
