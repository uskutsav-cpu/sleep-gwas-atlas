# Exploratory SMIM32 skeletal-muscle eQTL versus insomnia colocalization

**Subsequent QC finding:** The [molecular credible-set audit](stage3_skeletal_molecular_finemapping_review.md) found incomplete credible-set coverage, including zero of 18 SMAD5 L2 variants in the filtered FinnGen input, and two SMAD5 molecular signals. The single-signal H3/H4 estimates below are not a reliable conclusion of shared or distinct signals.

For the rs7734045 region, I compared a publicly available **SMIM32 skeletal-muscle eQTL** with FinnGen clinical-insomnia GWAS summary statistics over a 1 Mb candidate-centered window. At the default illustrative priors (`p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴`, `p12 = 10⁻⁵`), a single-signal approximate Bayes-factor analysis gave **H1 = 0.908, H3 = 0.045 and H4 = 0.047**. H1 denotes a QTL association only within this model, H3 two distinct trait-associated variants, and H4 one shared variant. These are model-dependent posterior probabilities, not proof that insomnia has no association or that SMIM32 is unrelated to sleep or frailty. H3 + H4 is only 0.092, so the conditional H4/(H3+H4) value should not be read as strong evidence of sharing.

The [prior sensitivity table](stage3_smim32_finngen_coloc_abf_sensitivity.csv) varies the shared-signal prior and the eQTL effect-size prior. For example, H4 ranges from approximately 0.0049 to 0.197 when p12 ranges from 10⁻⁶ to 5 × 10⁻⁵ at an eQTL prior-effect SD of 0.15. This locus was selected after earlier results, multiple gene–tissue pairs remain to be tested, and no multiplicity or selection adjustment is implied.

## Data and matching

- QTL: [eQTL Catalogue](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/eqtl/Data_access/) release-7 metadata, GTEx skeletal-muscle gene-expression dataset QTD000281, 702 samples per metadata, gene **ENSG00000271824 (SMIM32)**. This is the Catalogue's reprocessed dataset, not the GTEx v10 portal results used for the initial table. The catalogue's [methods](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/eqtl/Methods/) describe inverse-normal transformation of gene counts; the 0.15 quantitative prior SD is an illustrative standard-normal-scale assumption and source-specific phenotype scaling still warrants review. The variant at rs7734045 has eQTL p = 6.18 × 10⁻¹⁸ in this dataset.
- GWAS: the project's FinnGen R13 `F5_INSOMNIA` source, modeled as a case-control log-odds association with prior effect SD 0.20. Source files and checksums are documented in the [harmonization receipt](stage3_smim32_harmonization_receipt.json) and preceding project source records. Sample overlap with the QTL donors has not been verified.
- Window: GRCh38 chr5:135702653–136702653, inclusive, centered on rs7734045. After restricting QTL records to unique rsIDs with nonpalindromic biallelic SNP alleles and MAF ≥ 0.01, 2,212 FinnGen variants passed unique ID, position and exact allele-pair matching, finite positive-SE and GWAS-frequency filters. rs7734045 was present. This restricted support omits indels, ambiguous SNPs, duplicate IDs, missing variants and rare variants and does not cover the gene's entire possible cis window. The [QTL extraction receipt](stage3_catalogue_smim32_extract_receipt.json) records the source URL and indexed extraction; the index was hashed locally, but neither full-source nor index checksum was verified against the provider.

## Model and quality limits

A custom implementation of the standard single-causal-variant approximate Bayes-factor calculation uses beta and variance, rather than selected significant rows. Its H3 calculation was checked against explicit enumeration in a small numerical example. [Method description](https://chr1swallace.github.io/coloc/reference/approx.bf.estimates.html); [calculation receipt](stage3_smim32_finngen_coloc_abf_receipt.json). The default-prior H0–H4 calculations were subsequently checked against the unmodified `approx.bf.estimates` and `combine.abf` functions from coloc 5.2.3 on the same real inputs; the full package workflow was not run. QTL beta is ALT-oriented according to the Catalogue, and GWAS beta was aligned to the same ALT; standard single-signal ABF posterior calculation itself uses squared Z scores. This does not verify every multiallelic source representation or all genotype quality fields. QTL and FinnGen LD/population mismatch and possible multi-signal architecture can invalidate the single-causal-variant model. The FinnGen regional maximum |Z| in the matched set was 3.33, indicating a modest observed insomnia signal in this window; limited power is an important possible reason for the result.

This is a **first exploratory test**, not a genome-wide or gene-wide conclusion. Separate [exploratory FI and physical-phenotype comparisons](stage3_smim32_skeletal_mak_coloc.md) have since been run with data-derived trait-scale assumptions; they remain separate two-trait tests. No splicing colocalization or three-way shared causal variant has been established. A multi-signal method with ancestry-appropriate LD, broader cis coverage, source-validated sample sizes, prior sensitivity and independent replication is needed before making a gene-level claim.

[Reference-formula validation](stage3_coloc_reference_formula_review.md) checks arithmetic only; credible-set coverage and single-signal model limitations still govern interpretation.
