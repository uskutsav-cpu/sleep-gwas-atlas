# RBM6 region: multi-signal fine-mapping feasibility

## Outcome

A public-reference multi-signal fine-mapping attempt did **not produce a defensible insomnia–hospital-frailty colocalization**. On the 2,186 aligned variants with usable 1000 Genomes European-reference genotypes, SuSiE-RSS converged for both inputs: it returned two Jansen insomnia candidate credible sets but **no reported FinnGen hospital-frailty credible set** under the package's default purity filter. Maximum variant posterior inclusion probability in the hospital-frailty fit was approximately 0.021. Without a usable pair of credible sets, a SuSiE-coloc result cannot be calculated here. No reported set is not proof of no association or that the traits have different causal variants.

This is a feasibility analysis of a postselected locus and a restricted set of variants, not independent confirmation or a final fine-mapping result. Convergence only means the numerical algorithm met its stopping rule; it does not establish model fit, statistical power, true coverage, correct LD, or causal validity. The resulting insomnia candidate sets are saved in `stage6_rbm6_Jansen_insomnia_susie_candidate_sets.csv` for diagnostic review and should not be presented as definitive disease variants. The hospital-frailty output is `stage6_rbm6_FinnGen_HFRS_susie_candidate_sets.csv`; `stage6_rbm6_susie_attempt_status.csv` records convergence and counts.

## Reference and diagnostics

We intersected the existing five-GWAS, two-megabase common-variant set (2,256 SNPs) with exact biallelic 1000 Genomes Phase 3 reference genotypes, requiring reference minor allele frequency ≥1% and at most 1% missing calls. The resulting LD matrix contains 2,186 SNPs and 503 European reference donors. Fifty-one variants failed the MAF filter and 19 had no unique exact reference record; absent reference records are not biological negatives. The reference is external to both GWAS and only approximates their LD; the common-variant filter, selected window and omission of indels/palindromic SNPs further limit coverage. After using double precision, the minimum eigenvalue was -6.19e-13, consistent with numerical error, but the matrix has rank at most 502 because it has only 503 centered reference samples. `stage6_rbm6_ld_reference_receipt.json`, `stage6_rbm6_ld_variant_coverage.csv` and `stage6_rbm6_1000g_eur_reference_ld.npz` record coverage and LD. The genome-wide reference VCF was not downloaded and checksum-verified.

Among 49,473 reference SNP pairs with |r|>0.98, no pair had an LD-aligned Z-score difference exceeding 2 for Jansen insomnia, Mak FI or FinnGen hospital frailty. Pairs are highly correlated, not 49,473 independent tests; this check can miss more subtle incompatibility. SuSiE's `estimate_s_rss` returned 0.0174, 0.0212 and 0.0161 for those three traits, respectively, with working sample sizes of 313,750, 402,910 and 500,737. Mak per-variant N varies (385,687–403,041 among the retained SNPs), and the Jansen value comes from its provided effective-N field. The [susieR documentation](https://stephenslab.github.io/susieR/reference/estimate_s_rss.html) says larger estimates indicate stronger Z/LD inconsistency; no universal cutoff or exact source of the discrepancy is established here. Pairwise agreement and convergence do not override the rank, out-of-sample LD, coverage and mismatch limitations.

The two-trait exploratory fit used susieR 0.14.2, L=10, 95% coverage and `estimate_residual_variance=FALSE`, using the full 2,186-SNP EUR reference for both GWAS; the hospital-frailty cohort is Finnish, so that reference is not ancestry-matched to it. Scripts: `stage6_rbm6_eur_reference_ld.py`, `stage6_rbm6_reference_ld_qc.py`, `stage6_rbm6_susie_ld_diagnostic.R` and `stage6_rbm6_susie_attempt.R`. This fit does not address the separately measured brain eQTL, does not use in-sample LD, and is not a direct three-trait analysis. Improved ancestry/study-matched LD, broader variant coverage and sufficient signal strength are needed before interpreting a shared signal. The aligned common association inputs are `stage6_rbm6_five_gwas_common_region.csv.gz`; the statistical diagnostics and worst pairwise discrepancies are `stage6_rbm6_susie_ld_consistency.csv`, `stage6_rbm6_high_ld_z_diagnostics.json` and `stage6_rbm6_high_ld_z_mismatch.csv`.
