# Public Japanese knee-strength GWAS: pending candidate comparison

[Ito et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-024-06108-6) report knee-extension strength GWAS results for 3,452 Japanese participants aged at least 60 from the Shimane CoHRE and Bunkyo Health studies. The named study cohorts differ from UK Biobank, making this a useful external muscle-strength association lead, although participant and relatedness overlap with all prior project sources has not been exhaustively verified. Knee-extension strength is a continuous lower-limb strength measure, not FI, the Fried frailty score, or a joint insomnia–frailty outcome. Small sample size and ancestry differences limit power and transferability. The study is not an individual-level score-validation cohort.

The authors identify [GWAS Catalog GCST90319502](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90319001-GCST90320000/GCST90319502/) as the public summary source. Its downloaded metadata (`work/GCST90319502.yaml`) describes GRCh37, 1-based positions, 3,452 Japanese participants, an effect-allele frequency and beta/SE file, and an expected MD5 of `0fc2688cf786984fb5f147f6eb52628a`. It marks the file unharmonized and unsorted; do not assume coordinate sort, harmonization, or reference-allele orientation. Verify coding against the full header and study methods, and check exact chromosome, position, allele pair, strand and multiallelic representation before orienting effects. Higher knee strength and higher frailty are not the same direction of outcome.

Its candidate matching is conditional on source coding and does not by itself resolve strand, multiallelic representation, phenotype direction or cohort overlap. It also extracts the paper's lead rs10749438 as a source-specific allele and effect check.

The apparent fastGWA/METAL discrepancy is explained by the [paper's methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-024-06108-6.pdf): fastGWA was used for each dataset, followed by an inverse-variance fixed-effects METAL meta-analysis. The phenotype was mean left/right knee strength divided by body weight, residualized for age and sex and inverse-rank normalized; cohort devices differed. Thus a source beta is on the transformed higher-strength scale, not kg or an FI/FFS effect. The paper excluded variants with imputation Rsq < 0.3 and MAF < 0.005, which may affect candidate coverage; a missing match alone does not identify its cause. The paper reports the lead effect for rs10749438 A (ALT) as −0.148, SE 0.027, p 4.24×10⁻⁸; compare the completed source file before relying on its orientation.

As a build check, the 1000 Genomes phase 3 hg19 reference VCF contains rs10749438 at chromosome 10 position 123,810,832 with alleles G/A, consistent with the control used in the extraction script. This single-site reference check does not establish how the unfinished source file represents the variant or independently verify the full reference VCF.
