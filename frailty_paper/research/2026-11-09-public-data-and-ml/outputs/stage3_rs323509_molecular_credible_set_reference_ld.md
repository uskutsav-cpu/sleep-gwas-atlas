# LD among the rs323509-region molecular credible-set variants

The four variants in the eQTL Catalogue testis splice-junction credible set all matched one 1000 Genomes European reference-panel record by rsID and allele pair; their coordinates also had a consistent GRCh38-to-GRCh37 offset in this small region. The six pairwise unphased dosage correlations appear in [the reference LD table](stage3_rs323509_molecular_credible_set_reference_ld.csv). All 503 reference individuals had nonmissing genotypes for each pair. Pairwise r² ranges from 0.982 to 1.000; rs60271 and rs323509 have identical alternate-allele dosages in this sample. The same alternate alleles as the molecular source were counted for signed r.

This is consistent with poor resolution among these variants in the reference panel. It is not independent validation of the molecular credible set or a disease fine map. The eQTL Catalogue source reports its own credible-set minimum r² of about 0.967, based on its own methodology/data; the two values are not expected to be identical. LD in the molecular and disease GWAS samples may differ from this small European panel, especially across ancestries. The published molecular PIPs pertain to the selected testis splice event, not to an insomnia–frailty joint causal probability. The selected locus and pairwise coloc caveats are detailed in [the gene context](stage3_rs323509_gene_identifier_context.md) and [the molecular fine-mapping review](stage3_rs323509_testis_finemapping.md).
