# Natural short sleepers cohort: screening result

[Investigating Genetics of Human Natural Short Sleepers, dbGaP phs001270.v1.p1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs001270.v1.p1) is a sleep/circadian lead with symptom questionnaires, but not a viable validation cohort for the current sparse score. The public study description reports 117 screened participants, 38 blood samples, and whole-exome sequencing for 10 people. It requires participants to be related to another individual in the study, and selects extreme short or long sleep patterns; it does not document a standard FI or FFS for those sequenced people. Genetic data require authorized dbGaP access.

The project variants are predominantly noncoding; whole-exome targeting may not cover them. Exact per-variant callability, genotype–phenotype linkage, frailty measures, and independent sample status have not been verified. Even complete marker availability in this small, related, selected sample would not provide a credible generalizable test of score performance. This screen does not establish that the broader sleep study lacks additional usable participants or that the data have a specific access fee. No individual data were downloaded.
