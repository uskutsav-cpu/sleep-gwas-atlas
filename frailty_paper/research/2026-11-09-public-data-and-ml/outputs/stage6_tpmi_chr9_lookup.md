# TPMI sleep-disorder lookup: rs11790060

The [TPMI PheWeb page](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/9:93440650-T-C) displays rs11790060 at GRCh38 chr9:93440650 T/C. The project 15-marker prototype uses GRCh37 chr9:96202932 C/T and counts C; Ensembl maps that coordinate to the same GRCh38 position on the positive strand. The rsID has additional alleles in another reference, and the source effect-allele convention is unconfirmed. Do not assign the displayed sign to the project's counted C allele yet.

For broad [sleep disorders](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327), the browser displays effect −0.020 (SE 0.0087), p = 0.011, in 45,836 cases and 287,785 controls. These values are rounded. This is a nominal result in a postselected single-marker screen with no prespecified multiplicity correction; it does not establish an insomnia-specific effect, a concordant effect direction, or a shared sleep–frailty mechanism. The browser labels FAM120AOS as the nearest gene, not as a proven mediator. Cohort and relatedness overlap with every discovery and selection dataset remains unverified.

The site search displayed no matches for rs78206187 and rs113851554 during this check. Search nonmatches are not proof that variants are absent, unmeasured, or unassociated; alternate exact representations and source files remain to be checked. The 15-marker score and the separate 22-marker count remain unvalidated in individuals. Other TPMI checks and the download limitation are listed in `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.

TPMI requests acknowledgement and citation of [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x).
