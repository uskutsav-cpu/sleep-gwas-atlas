# SMAD5 excitatory-neuron expression versus frailty and insomnia: exploratory colocalization

We compared the [open NIAGADS SMAD5 single-nucleus eQTL](stage6_niagads_xqtl_candidate_review.md) in the ROSMAP excitatory-neuron mega-analysis with the local Mak frailty-index (FI), Mak physical-frailty (FP), and FinnGen insomnia GWAS regional caches. This is a postselected, single-signal approximate Bayes factor sensitivity, **not** validation of either score or proof of a causal variant, gene or cell type.

After exact allele-pair matching, positive-strand liftover, removal of palindromic SNPs and exclusion of matches whose QTL and GWAS allele frequencies differ by more than 0.1, we obtained the following results. H1 means an expression association without a detected GWAS signal in the model; H3 means distinct expression and GWAS signals; H4 means a shared signal. These are conditional model probabilities, not p-values or direct probabilities that SMAD5 causes disease.

| GWAS phenotype | Matched SNPs | H1 | H3 | H4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mak FI | 4,613 | 0.0027 | 0.9964 | 0.00094 |
| Mak FP | 4,613 | 0.9513 | 0.0387 | 0.0101 |
| FinnGen insomnia | 4,215 | 0.8684 | 0.0878 | 0.0438 |

At this configuration the FI comparison favors distinct signals. The FP and insomnia comparisons favor the expression-only hypothesis, so they do **not** establish either shared or distinct disease signals. The calculations set p1=p2=10⁻⁴, p12=10⁻⁵, and expression-effect prior SD 0.15; GWAS-effect prior SDs were inherited from the earlier project analysis (FI 1.1004, FP 0.12325, insomnia log-odds 0.2). Those effect-scale choices are illustrative and must be checked against the source models. Across expression prior SDs 0.075–0.30 and shared priors 10⁻⁶–5×10⁻⁵, H4 ranged 0.000094–0.00471 for FI, 0.00101–0.0484 for FP and 0.00456–0.187 for insomnia. These are sensitivities, not calibrated confidence intervals. Removing the frequency-difference filter is retained in the [full result table](stage6_niagads_smad5_exc_abf_sensitivity.csv).

The compared variants retained the leading expression SNP and approximately 99.7% (FI/FP) or 97.7% (insomnia) of **single-signal expression ABF support among eligible non-palindromic, common SNPs in the extracted cis window**, using expression prior SD 0.15. This is not genome-wide or disease-signal coverage and excludes untested, palindromic, rare and indel variants. The source also uses a wider TAD-enhanced window outside this cis interval, and coverage of the GWAS signals across their full regions has not been established. The significant marginal result at rs7734045 therefore cannot be used to infer a shared causal signal.

Major limitations remain: the analysis assumes at most one causal variant per trait, does not model target-study LD or multiple signals, and uses separately ascertained cohorts and potentially overlapping source samples. QTL association and genotype characteristics, expression normalization, ancestry, imputation, sample overlap and disease phenotype differences require additional review. The frequency filter is a sensitivity rule rather than proof of correct coding, and the regional source files were retrieved by indexed byte range without full-member checksums. An independently sourced larger ancestry-matched LD reference and multi-signal models with diagnostics, full regional support, and additional datasets are needed before any firm conclusion. The earlier [CD4 chromatin comparison](stage6_tenk10k_expanded_fi_susie_review.md) is a different molecular measurement and cannot be treated as an independent replication of this brain expression test.

A [reference-frequency follow-up](stage6_niagads_smad5_frequency_discrepancy_review.md) checked the four Mak FI matches excluded for a frequency difference above 0.1. For each, the 1000 Genomes EUR reference frequency was closer to the Mak value than to the brain-QTL value. The cause remains unresolved and this does not alter the filtered analysis.

Reproduce with `python3 work/niagads_smad5_abf_sensitivity.py`; [receipt](stage6_niagads_smad5_exc_abf_receipt.json) includes hashes and support checks. `Rscript work/niagads_smad5_coloc_formula_check.R` independently reproduced the three baseline calculations using coloc 5.2.3 formula code to within 3.2×10⁻¹⁶. This validates arithmetic only, not model assumptions or biological conclusions. The NIAGADS archive and source acknowledgment are described in the [atlas review](stage6_niagads_xqtl_candidate_review.md).

## Reference-LD multi-signal sensitivity for FI

We reused the existing 503-person 1000 Genomes European reference LD matrix, matched 3,261 non-palindromic variants with QTL/reference and QTL/GWAS allele-frequency differences at most 0.1, and fit SuSiE RSS with L=10 and fixed residual variance. The subset retains the marginal expression lead and the project candidate. Relative to the already matched, frequency-filtered cis inputs, it captures approximately **96.1% of the expression** and **88.0% of the FI** single-signal ABF support at the chosen effect priors; these are model-dependent approximations, not comprehensive coverage or sample representativeness.

Both fits converged and each returned one credible set. For the pair of fitted signals, coloc.susie at p1=p2=10⁻⁴ and p12=10⁻⁵ gave H3 **0.99945** and H4 **0.00045**; H4 ranged from 0.000045 to 0.00223 as p12 varied from 10⁻⁶ to 5×10⁻⁵. This is consistent with the earlier single-signal FI sensitivity under these models, but does not prove distinct causal variants or rule out shared signals outside the fitted set. The fact that L=10 was allowed does not establish that all real signals were detected. FP and insomnia were not fitted in this analysis.

The LD checks are a material limitation: `estimate_s_rss` returned approximately **0.0203 for each trait**, the 3,261-SNP matrix has a maximum sample rank of 502, and small negative eigenvalues (minimum −1.52×10⁻⁶) were set to zero by the diagnostic. The reference is not the target-cohort LD; ancestry, imputation, sample selection, omitted variants and finite reference size can produce mismatches. The QTL sample size of 737 is reported in the retrieved source rows; FI N=403,041 is the prior study-level approximation, not a verified per-variant sample size. Because this fit relies on imperfect external LD and postselected regions, a larger target-matched LD panel and improved coverage are needed for a firmer interpretation.

Reproduce with `work/stage5_env/bin/python work/niagads_smad5_susie_prepare.py`, `Rscript work/niagads_smad5_susie_fit.R`, and `Rscript work/niagads_smad5_susie_coloc.R`. Outputs are the [coverage and matrix hash](stage6_niagads_smad5_susie_coverage.json), [LD diagnostics](stage6_niagads_smad5_susie_ld_diagnostics.csv), [fit summary](stage6_niagads_smad5_susie_fit.csv), and [prior sensitivity](stage6_niagads_smad5_susie_coloc_sensitivity.csv). No individual genetic-score validation was performed.

## Wider TAD window sensitivity

We subsequently used the same indexed-range method to retrieve the source-reported TAD-enhanced interval, GRCh38 chr5:134,640,001–137,760,000, including **11,372 SMAD5 eQTL rows** rather than 7,755 in the prior cis interval. The tabix index checksum and archive-member header were verified; the full large archive and compressed chromosome member were not. Reproduce with `python3 work/niagads_smad5_remote_region.py --tad`; [extraction receipt](stage6_niagads_smad5_exc_region_tad_receipt.json).

After the same allele and frequency-difference filters, the expanded single-signal analysis included 6,642 matched variants for each Mak phenotype and 6,060 for FinnGen insomnia. At the same baseline priors, FI H3/H4 were **0.99639/0.00094**, FP H1/H3/H4 were **0.933/0.057/0.0099**, and insomnia H1/H3/H4 were **0.615/0.354/0.031**. FI retains the earlier model-dependent pattern; FP and insomnia do not establish shared or distinct signals. The larger interval changes the insomnia posterior substantially (prior cis-window H1 0.868 and H3 0.088): the strongest absolute matched GWAS Z in the larger interval is at `chr5:137615744:G:A` (Z≈4.44), beyond the smaller cis window. This is a descriptive result in an overlapping postselected dataset, not a formal test of a window effect or a new independent replication. The wider interval increases the risk that more than one signal is present, which the single-signal model cannot resolve.

The [wider-window sensitivity table](stage6_niagads_smad5_exc_abf_sensitivity_tad.csv) and [receipt](stage6_niagads_smad5_exc_abf_receipt_tad.json) record the full prior grid and matching. `work/stage5_env/bin/python work/niagads_smad5_disease_harmonize.py --tad`, then `python3 work/niagads_smad5_abf_sensitivity.py --tad`, reproduce it. `Rscript work/niagads_smad5_coloc_formula_check.R --tad` independently reproduces the baseline formulas within 1.3×10⁻¹⁵. The existing multi-signal LD fit above is for the narrower cis subset; it has **not** been rerun for the larger TAD interval. Full target-cohort LD, complete disease-signal coverage and source harmonization are still unverified.

### Reference coverage before a wider-window multi-signal fit

The local 503-person European reference has 5,856 uniquely matched, non-monomorphic variants for the 6,060 frequency-filtered FinnGen insomnia rows, including the wider-window insomnia lead and the expression lead. These matched variants account for approximately 96.0% of expression and 98.8% of insomnia ABF support **conditional on the already matched and filtered set and chosen priors**; this is not complete source coverage. The reference is general European rather than Finnish and cannot establish target LD quality. The [coverage receipt](stage6_niagads_smad5_tad_ld_coverage.json) is reproduced by `python3 work/niagads_smad5_tad_ld_coverage.py`.

An attempt to construct the full 5,856-variant matrix did not complete: writing the matrix failed with an operating-system storage error, and free local space fell to roughly 341 MiB at inspection. The empty failed output was removed. No wider-window multi-signal fit or colocalization result was produced. A future fit also needs appropriate binary-outcome sample-size handling and LD diagnostics; adequate reference-marker coverage alone is not sufficient.

### Pairwise reference check of the wider-window insomnia lead

As a lightweight check, we calculated genotype correlations in the same 503-person 1000 Genomes European reference for the project candidate `chr5:136202653:A:C`, the SMAD5 excitatory eQTL marginal lead `chr5:136206558:C:T`, and the wider-window insomnia marginal lead `chr5:137615744:G:A`. The candidate and eQTL lead had **r²=0.996**, while the wider-window insomnia lead had **r²=0.0044** with the candidate and **0.0036** with the eQTL lead. This helps explain why a stronger distant insomnia association can change the single-signal regional model without indicating that it tags the brain-expression lead in this reference. These are postselected marginal leads and external-reference LD: they do not establish Finnish target LD, conditional independence, the number of signals, causal variants or a shared disease mechanism. Strong reference LD with the expression lead also does not override the low PIP for the candidate in the source's molecular credible set.

All 503 reference samples had complete calls at the three checked sites. [The receipt](stage6_niagads_tad_lead_reference_ld.json) records the pairwise signed correlations and input hash; `python3 work/niagads_tad_lead_ld.py` reproduces the check. No large LD matrix or individual outcome data were produced.

### Finnish reference-panel comparison

The [FinnGen public LD server](https://docs.finngen.fi/working-outside-the-sandbox/ld-browser), queried with the SISu v4.2 Finnish reference, gave r² **0.9834** for the candidate–expression-lead pair. For the wider-window insomnia lead it returned r² **0.0001** with the candidate and **0.0000** with the expression lead (as displayed at server precision). This supports the same broad tagging pattern as the small EUR panel, but the returned zero is rounded and does not establish literal independence. SISu is an imputation reference, not the actual target-sample LD matrix, and these postselected pairwise checks do not resolve multiple causal variants or validate the genetic score.

The initial query using a 2,000,000 window parameter did not reach either comparison SNP; absence from that response was **not** treated as zero LD. A 4,000,000 window returned both exact GRCh38 variant IDs. The [pair receipt](stage6_niagads_sisu_pair_audit.json) includes response hashes, anchors, parameters and returned values; `python3 work/niagads_sisu_pair_audit.py` checks the saved API responses. The API responses were collected via HTTPS on 29 October 2026; downloaded files are `work/niagads_tad_finnish_ld_anchor_4m.json` and `work/niagads_smad5_finnish_ld_anchor.json`. The server returns r², not the signed LD matrix needed for SuSiE.
