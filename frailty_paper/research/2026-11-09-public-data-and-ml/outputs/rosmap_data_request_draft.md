# ROS/MAP data request: draft for an eligible principal investigator

**Not submitted.** The [RADC process](https://www.radc.rush.edu/requests/data.htm) requires an account, committee review and executed institutional agreements. Confirm the total cost and genetic-data access route before applying.

**Working title:** Evaluation of prespecified sleep–frailty genetic scores in ROS/MAP.

**Research question:** Are previously frozen 15- and 22-variant research scores associated with separately defined sleep symptoms and frailty outcomes in eligible older adults, and what uncertainty surrounds their incremental performance beyond baseline covariates? This is a potential association/prediction validation, not a causal test.

**Requested data:** Participant-level permitted linkages among genotype dosages and imputation quality, genome build and array/batch; ancestry PCs and relatedness indicators; demographics; longitudinal sleep questionnaire items and actigraphy; individual deficits and coding necessary to reconstruct the published 41-item MAP FI; follow-up timing, missingness and consent. Request exact allele-level coverage for both score manifests, including multiallelic representations. Confirm cohort, study visit, permitted use, transfer restrictions and any separate genome-data authorization.

For precise inventory, a [ROSMAP data descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata2018142.pdf) lists `ROSMAP_arrayGenotype.bim` as Synapse `syn3221155` alongside array genotype files and chromosome-level imputed dosages; [Synapse metadata](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn2426141) describes an Illumina imputed dosage resource with a 1000 Genomes reference. These published IDs are leads, not proof of current permissions, full participant coverage, a shared file version, or 15/22-marker presence. Ask the custodian for the current version-specific marker manifest, imputation-quality information and associated dataset documentation; check actual rsID, build, alleles and contrasts, and do not use a BIM list as proof that phenotype-linked samples have usable calls.

**Direct access check, 29 October 2026:** Synapse's [public entity metadata endpoint](https://repo-prod.prod.sagebase.org/repo/v1/entity/syn3221155) identified the BIM file as latest version 2. An unauthenticated request to its [file endpoint](https://repo-prod.prod.sagebase.org/repo/v1/entity/syn3221155/file) returned HTTP 403, stating access requirements were unmet. The file contents and marker coverage could not be inspected. This is an unauthenticated access result, not a determination that an eligible researcher cannot obtain it.

**NIAGADS alternative and restriction:** [ROSMAP2 NG00095.v1](https://dss.niagads.org/datasets/ng00095/) lists 533 samples and GRCh38 PLINK genotypes and TOPMed-r2-imputed BGEN files, with application required and phenotypes separately requested from RADC. Its stated consent is `DS-ND-IRB-PUB-NPU`: [NIAGADS defines](https://niagads.scrollhelp.site/support/data-use-limitations) DS as disease-specific, ND as neurodegenerative disorders, IRB as local IRB approval, and NPU as not-for-profit use. Whether this particular sleep–frailty project qualifies must be determined by the NIAGADS access committee; do not assume it is permitted. The 533 are not the questionnaire–FI intersection or additive to the 401 MAP availability flags. This release is an alternative request lead, not an unrestricted marker list or evidence of score coverage.

The [public NG00095 file manifest](https://st1.niagads.org/portal/download-public/NG00095.v1/fm) identifies `rosmap2.NHW.fwd.hg38.postqc_ALL.bim` (MD5 `76f2354a0a89d2ca55a62867c0b25f1b`), a TOPMed-r2 imputation INFO archive, and a BGEN index among its release files. The manifest lists filenames and checksums, not the marker rows. The BIM is marked `ALL` in its consent column; the INFO archive is labeled `NONE`, but that label alone does not establish public download access. Ask NIAGADS whether the INFO file can be provided without controlled-data approval and whether it contains variant-level INFO and alleles. Do not infer marker coverage or override the dataset's access and use rules.

The public [MAP baseline interview dictionary](https://www.radc.rush.edu/docs/dictionary/show.htm?formno=mp2int) identifies candidate sleep-onset `q1slp`, nighttime-waking `q2slp`, and fatigue questions `q1md` (effort) and `q10md` (could not get going). It also lists restless sleep `q2md`. Request the corresponding baseline and follow-up fields and source form/version mapping; the baseline `q1slp`/`q2slp` are not automatically the same as the separate 32-item sleep substudy flagged in the 401-person report. The raw-form 1=yes, 2=no coding for `q1md` and `q10md` must be verified against the delivered dataset. Avoid deriving the proposed FI from a depression total that includes restless sleep. These field descriptions do not establish that the requested variables or usable genotypes overlap within participants or visits.

The public [MAP follow-up interview dictionary](https://www.radc.rush.edu/docs/dictionary/show.htm?formno=mp2intfu) lists the same `q1slp`, `q2slp`, `q1md`, `q10md` and `q2md` names with corresponding question wording and response labels. This supports requesting repeated items, but the dictionary does not establish collection at every visit, harmonized delivered-variable names, exact dates, completeness, or genotype linkage. Obtain actual visit-level data and verify form versions before specifying a longitudinal analysis.

**Phenotype plan:** Use the published 41-item MAP index only after verifying scoring and data availability. Its displayed item list has no explicitly named sleep item, but includes two fatigue-related items. Prespecify a sensitivity removing both fatigue items and recalculating the denominator and completeness rule; compare on a common sample. Other items may still reflect sleep or health-seeking. Define a sleep questionnaire outcome from its original wording and coding, without calling a symptom score a clinical diagnosis. Treat actigraphy as a distinct phenotype. If timing or item coding cannot support these definitions, revise the plan before examining score associations.

**Additional documented outcome:** Request RADC's longitudinal [`frailty_z`](https://www.radc.rush.edu/docs/var/detail.htm?category=Frailty&variable=frailty_z) and its underlying grip average, timed walk, BMI, `q1md` and `q10md`, plus baseline means, SDs and derivation code. RADC describes it as a four-domain standardized continuous composite, not the 41-deficit FI or the original five-component Fried phenotype. Its fatigue domain uses the two named mood items; it therefore is not fatigue-excluded. If the underlying variables and scale parameters are available, a prespecified three-domain no-fatigue sensitivity may be constructed and compared on a common sample, but it is a new measure whose direction, scaling, missingness and validity must be verified. RADC notes an exception to its general sign description: longer timed walks increase frailty and should not be flipped. Its published summary requires grip and walk to be nonmissing; confirm handling of missing BMI/fatigue and actual released coding. Never call either composite a sleep/exhaustion-excluded frailty GWAS.

**Feasibility gates:** Establish joint counts for genotype QC, selected sleep item, FI item completeness, age, ancestry and consent at compatible visits; sleep-outcome and frailty event counts; marker-level alleles, missingness and INFO; overlap with discovery cohorts, relatives and any prior selection or follow-up; and total access and computing costs. RADC's public 401-person MAP questionnaire/genetic flag is not this joint analysis count. Small samples and low outcome counts may preclude reliable calibration or clinical discrimination.

**Analysis if gates pass:** Freeze score files and harmonization decisions, evaluate prespecified outcomes with appropriate ancestry, age, sex, genotyping batch and kinship adjustment, report effect sizes and uncertainty and compare clinical baseline models with versus without each score. Specify missing-variant handling, model selection, multiplicity and subgroup restrictions before target analysis. Do not retune on the same sample and claim independent validation. Use only approved data infrastructure and publish no participant records.

**Suggested note to mentor:** “Could we discuss a ROS/MAP data request for independent testing of our experimental sleep–frailty scores? The public availability report shows a possible sleep-questionnaire/genetics intersection, but we need linked FI data, exact marker coverage, outcome counts, access eligibility and cost before deciding whether the analysis is feasible.”
