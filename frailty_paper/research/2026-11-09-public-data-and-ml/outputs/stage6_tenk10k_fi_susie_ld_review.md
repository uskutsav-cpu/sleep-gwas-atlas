# TenK10K chromatin–FI multi-signal sensitivity

We assessed whether the published TenK10K CD4-naïve chromatin peak and the Mak FI signal could be compared using SuSiE with the project's existing 1000 Genomes European LD reference. This is an exploratory **reference-LD sensitivity**, not score validation, a causal result or a replacement for same-sample LD.

The pre-existing 503-person LD matrix covers 1,743 of 3,877 frequency-filtered matched FI/peak variants. It includes the top variant for each trait in the preceding single-signal ABF calculation, but excludes the palindromic top SNP from the TenK10K molecular–molecular colocalization. At the earlier ABF priors it retains 78.4% of the total peak-QTL single-signal ABF support and 88.0% of FI support; these are model-specific diagnostic fractions, **not** credible-set coverage or a probability of validity. The same reference captures only 38.0% and 40.1% of the corresponding FP and insomnia ABF support and misses each trait's top variant. We therefore did not fit or interpret those comparisons with this matrix. [Coverage receipt](stage6_tenk10k_ld_support_coverage.json).

Using susieR 0.14.2, both FI and peak models converged, each returning one credible set in the restricted input. This does not rule out additional signals in missing variants or under other settings. The coloc 5.2.3 SuSiE comparison of the two fitted sets gave, at illustrative p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴ and p12 = 10⁻⁵, H3 = **0.9985** and H4 = **0.00122**; H4 ranged 0.00012–0.00605 across p12 10⁻⁶–5×10⁻⁵. Under these settings, the fitted signals favor distinct variants, consistent with the preceding single-signal sensitivity. This is a conditional finding rather than proof that no shared FI mechanism exists. The coloc model addresses chromatin accessibility and FI, not SMAD5 expression mediation or insomnia–frailty sharing.

The result is fragile in several ways. The external matrix has at most rank 502 for 1,743 variants; its minimum float-derived eigenvalue was −9.45×10⁻⁷. `estimate_s_rss` warned about non-positive-semidefiniteness and truncated negative eigenvalues, estimating λ = 0.0081 for the QTL and 0.0051 for FI. These diagnostics depend on the model and are not pass/fail thresholds; they do not prove orientation or LD consistency. SuSiE sample sizes were approximated as 921 for the peak and 403,041 for Mak FI. The former is the rounded modal value inferred from published minor-allele count and frequency, not verified per-variant sample counts; the latter is the study-wide N, while per-variant N can differ. Both reference ancestry differences and these N assumptions affect results. The earlier alignment's genomic build, palindromic SNP handling, imputation-quality limitations, study/relative overlap and reduced locus coverage still apply. Source correction status of the molecular data remains provisional.
