# Public full gait-speed GWAS: 22-candidate follow-up

**Update to the earlier feasibility screen:** the full [Ben-Avraham et al. 2017 gait GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5310665/) is publicly hosted on [NHLBI GRASP's full-results page](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/grasp/full-results), even though the GWAS Catalog entry does not list full statistics. The outdated URL cited in the ELSA report redirects to GRASP's overview; following its current Full Results link reveals the actual [Gait-Aging-All.txt](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/grasp/downloads/FullResults/2017/2017_Ben_Avrahim/Gait-Aging-All.txt) file and a [source README](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/grasp/downloads/FullResults/2017/2017_Ben_Avrahim/CHARGE_Gait_speed_README.docx). The source terms prohibit reposting the full catalog or significant subsets or commercial use without permission and specify citation requirements; this project retains a small, nonselected candidate lookup and source links.

A complete streamed scan read 2,474,503 rows, matching the size reported in ELSA's documentation. Of the project's 22 previously selected variants, 11 had a matching rsID and unordered allele pair and 11 rsIDs were absent. No matched candidate had nominal p<0.05 (smallest p=0.07056). This is **not evidence that these variants have no effect**: estimates may be imprecise, candidate and source traits differ, and an absent rsID does not establish absence of a variant under a different representation. This is an exploratory post-selection comparison; no score, individual-level prediction or causal interpretation follows.

The source README identifies `Allele1` as the coded allele and `Effect` as its beta. I reversed signs and frequencies where needed to use the project's specified candidate allele. All 11 allele pairs are nonpalindromic. For 10, the absolute candidate-allele frequency difference from the project's small European 1000 Genomes reference was at most 0.045; **rs4886140 differs by 0.299** and has a multiallelic broader reference. Its matching strings are not enough to establish the same allele contrast; exclude it from directional interpretation pending source-specific resolution. The file does not include chromosomal coordinates, so exact position, genome build mapping and multiallelic representations were not independently validated. The direction string in the CSV retains source orientation and must not be treated as reoriented.

For the nearby handgrip candidate rs7731706 G/T, the combined gait estimate was +0.0002 (SE 0.0016; p=0.8875) in the paper's gait-speed units (m/s), with source heterogeneity p=0.00411. This point estimate is imprecise and applies to gait, not grip; it does not formally test or contradict the separate handgrip result. The source's 21 cohorts include HRS and Lothian, which appear elsewhere in this project's background and evaluation; overlap with all discovery, selection and comparator samples has not been individually established. A public summary GWAS for a physical component is not an independent evaluation of the project's score against FI or FFS.
