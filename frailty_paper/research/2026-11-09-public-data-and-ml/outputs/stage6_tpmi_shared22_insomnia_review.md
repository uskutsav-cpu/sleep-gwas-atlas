# TPMI insomnia follow-up for the 22-marker count

The [candidate inventory](stage6_tpmi_shared22_insomnia_inventory.csv) records rounded [TPMI PheWeb](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/) associations for 17 of the 22 markers in the postselected, equal-weight count. Four rows reuse the earlier 15-marker insomnia lookup; 13 further markers were inspected by rsID using the site's search. Each displayed GRCh38 position and allele pair matched the project's existing reference-checked AlphaGenome queue and the score's allele pair. This is an internal consistency check, not an independent confirmation of target effect-allele coding or all source transformations. The five remaining rsIDs and their positions returned no exact autocomplete match; this does not prove that the variant is absent, unassociated, or absent from the full summary statistics. All five were rare or unobserved in the East Asian reference panel; see the [missing-marker review](stage6_tpmi_shared22_missing_marker_review.md) for the distinction between reference and TPMI data.

For the 17 displayed markers, the smallest rounded p-value for [insomnia (phecode 327.4)](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327.4) was 0.055; for organic or persistent insomnia (327.41), it was 0.13. Neither screen had a nominal p below 0.05. This is a descriptive, postselected, incomplete screen, not a test of the score, and it cannot establish no effect. The outcomes share controls and can share cases; results are correlated within and across phenotypes. Summary statistics are rounded, and the production PheWeb effect allele remains insufficiently verified for assigning the signs to the score's counted alleles. Sample/relative overlap with every discovery and selection cohort is not fully established.

TPMI's insomnia outcome is clinical coding in a Taiwanese hospital-based cohort, not FI, FFS or a joint sleep–frailty measure. Differences in ancestry, phenotype and ascertainment limit direct comparisons with the European-ancestry discovery results. Individual prediction, calibration and discrimination cannot be assessed from these marginal lookups. The five unverified variants, full-precision data and effect orientation must be resolved before even considering an explicitly labeled, approximate full-score summary test. The screen does not evaluate the 15-marker weighted scores.

In a separate [reference-panel comparison](stage6_shared22_cross_population_reference_review.md), the full count and the 17-marker subset were highly correlated in the EAS panel because the five unresolved markers showed little variation there. This genotype-only result does not establish equivalence in TPMI or validate the subset.

Run `python3 outputs/stage6_tpmi_shared22_insomnia_inventory.py` from the project root to rebuild the table from the documented browser transcriptions, the 15-marker inventory, the score definition and reference queue. This verifies internal coordinate and allele agreement; it does not retrieve or independently recheck the browser data. For case definitions and sample counts see [the insomnia source note](stage6_tpmi_insomnia_specific_lead.md).
