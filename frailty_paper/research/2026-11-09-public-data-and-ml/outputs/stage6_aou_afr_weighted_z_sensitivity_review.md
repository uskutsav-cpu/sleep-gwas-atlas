# All of Us AFR-similarity subgroup: conditional score sensitivity

The public [All by All browser](https://allbyall.researchallofus.org/) returned whole-genome association results in its AFR genetic-similarity group for all 33 distinct project variants and both insomnia (`NS_333.2`) and generalized weakness (`SS_807.21`): 66 exact position/REF/ALT matches. AFR is the resource's genetic-similarity label, not a synonym for self-identified race and not a homogeneous population. This is aggregate association data, not genotypes and outcomes of individuals.

Using 661 1000 Genomes Phase 3 AFR reference samples for LD, we repeated the [earlier EUR-group conditional weighted-Z method](stage6_aou_eur_weighted_z_sensitivity_review.md). All seven score constructions had nominal p > 0.10 for each outcome; the conditional Z values ranged from −0.90 to 1.61 for insomnia and 0.43 to 1.15 for generalized weakness. These uncertain results **do not demonstrate absence of associations or lack of predictive value**. No formal between-group heterogeneity test was performed. Different group sample sizes, allele frequencies, LD, phenotype ascertainment and uncertainty can change Z values; contrasting significance between this result and EUR results does not establish a difference in effect or performance. The outcomes do not measure individual score accuracy, FI, Fried frailty, or a joint sleep–frailty phenotype.

The published candidates and score weights were selected post hoc using primarily European discovery data, and the current analysis was not prespecified. Two of the 33 matched variants had source minor-allele frequency below 1% in each phenotype; sparse information can increase uncertainty. All 15 and 22 listed biallelic contrasts had nonzero variation in the 661 reference samples, but this does not establish adequate target-cohort effective sample size or target genotype quality. Individual and family overlap with the discovery and selection sources is unverified, and All of Us data also contributed to a subsequently inspected insomnia meta-analysis. Avoid claiming independence or fair/equivalent transportability.

The formula, ALT-effect assumption, biallelic sign reversal, source-frequency HWE scaling, cross-chromosome-zero convention and cohort/variant limitations are the same as the [EUR analysis](stage6_aou_eur_weighted_z_sensitivity_review.md). In particular, the reference LD need not approximate the AoU subgroup's covariance, even when both carry an AFR label; reference participants may be related, remote VCFs lack independently verified full-file checksums, some rsIDs are multiallelic in broader references, and the FFS weights' direction is inferred. `SE × HWE dosage SD` has coefficient of variation about 1.2–2.7% in these four sets; that is a limited diagnostic, not proof of common sample size, calibrated p values or adequate LD.
