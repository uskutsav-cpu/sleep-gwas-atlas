# Human muscle stem-cell insomnia analysis: status

The public [Kedlian et al. human muscle aging atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00613-3) provides a processed muscle-stem-cell subset with 17,528 cells, 19,668 genes and 12 represented donors. It is a separate resource from the 387,000-profile multimodal atlas. [Input receipt](stage4_human_musc_input_receipt.json) and [cells by subtype and donor](stage4_human_musc_donor_cell_counts.csv).

An initial scDRS insomnia scoring run used the authors' published Jansen insomnia GWAS gene set, the processed expression matrix, 1,000 expression-matched control sets, and covariates for gene detection and donor indicators. It retained 691 gene-set genes after filtering; one of 17,528 cells passed the scDRS cell-level FDR < 0.10 threshold. This is a single-cell gene-program result, not evidence of frailty, common genetic risk, causality, clinical insomnia diagnoses, or independent replication. Four inspected cells' values were consistent with log1p(counts scaled to 10,000); exact source transformation has not been independently verified throughout the file. No provider checksum was available for independent comparison, though the file opens and a local SHA-256 is recorded.

The four cell-subtype association tests have now completed; see the [human muscle scDRS results](stage4_human_musc_insomnia_scdrs.md). The first attempt encountered a Scanpy/SciPy sparse-array compatibility error when constructing neighbors. A workspace-local wrapper supplies the equivalent sparse-array nonzero-count method, checked against `csr_matrix` on a small example, and the downstream calculation finished. No subtype passed BH q < 0.05 across the four association tests.

A descriptive [donor score-contrast table](stage4_human_musc_donor_score_contrasts.csv) compares mean normalized scores within each donor between a subtype and the other cells in this same subset, requiring at least 10 cells in each side. It is not a disease-control comparison or an independent genetic test. The smallest MYOG+MuSC group meets that threshold in only three donors. The scDRS scores are computed jointly and adjustment for donor indicators does not make its standard cell-level or group-level p-values donor-aware; any result needs independent donor-aware validation.
