# Exploratory comparison of two older-adult sleep expression studies

We took the 244 genes reported with adjusted p <0.05 in [Spektor et al.'s whole-blood middle-insomnia analysis](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27062771) and looked up those gene symbols in the project's public [GSE208668](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE208668) PBMC expression matrix. This selection was made after the project had already explored GSE208668; it is post hoc and is **not a preregistered replication**.

Of 244 source gene symbols, 221 matched a matrix row; 23 did not. For the 221 matched rows, we fit the same insomnia-group + age + sex linear model as the previous project analysis, using HC3 robust standard errors and BH adjustment across these 221 tests. **167 of 221** passed BH q <0.05 in this PBMC analysis; **140 of those 167** have the same estimated sign as Spektor's reported log2 fold change and 27 have the opposite sign. Across all 221 matched genes, 169 signs agree. These are descriptive counts, not independent confirmations of 140 genes or an estimate of a reproducible biological effect. Genes are correlated, the source panel was selected for statistical significance, the sample size is small, and we did not test the wider universe of project hypotheses. A missing symbol is not evidence that a gene was absent biologically.

Most importantly, **GSE208668 insomnia status is perfectly confounded with recruitment study**: the 17 cases and 25 controls came from different studies. The original authors report one laboratory batch, but recruitment-source effects and insomnia cannot be separated here. Spektor used whole blood and a different middle-insomnia definition, with 26 middle-insomnia cases among 236 people selected to have BMI <30. Donor overlap between the two studies has not been formally verified. Different cell composition, case definitions, laboratory methods and covariates may influence agreement. Neither dataset demonstrates that these genes mediate genetic effects or provides an individual genetic-score validation. The 244 source genes are not the project's four nominated genes, none of which appeared in the source's nominally filtered table.
