# Same-sample sleep–deficit-index and grip comparison

Exploratory plan written after the separate index and grip findings were known, before computing this matched-sample analysis. For each NHANES 2011–12 and 2013–14 cycle, restrict to examined adults aged 60+ with valid SLQ050 sleep response, MGDCGSZ combined grip, age, sex, and at least 20 of the 24 adapted deficit-index components specified in `stage6_nhanes_adapted_fi_protocol.md`. Reuse MEC weights and survey strata/PSUs. Fit separate survey-weighted linear models for the 24-item index and grip strength, each with SLQ050, age and sex. Report sample sizes and separate estimates with 95% confidence intervals. These two coefficients have different units and cannot be compared as if they measure the same outcome. This is a partial physical-component comparison, not a Fried frailty score, a direct test of a difference between outcomes, or an independent genetic replication. It is a post hoc selected comparison; neither p values nor differences in significance can demonstrate a difference in effects.
