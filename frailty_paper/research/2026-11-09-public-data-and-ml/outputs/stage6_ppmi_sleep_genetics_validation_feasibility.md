# PPMI sleep, mobility and genetic data: secondary validation lead

Checked 29 October 2026. The Parkinson’s Precision Medicine Initiative (PPMI) is a longitudinal study including Parkinson's disease, at-risk and healthy-control groups. It is a **disease-focused secondary lead**, not a verified free public dataset for independently validating the project’s frailty scores.

## Evidence and limitations

* [A published PPMI sleep study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7815601/) used MDS-UPDRS item 1.7 to evaluate insomnia complaints over follow-up. [Another PPMI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5441947/) used a score of at least 2 as suggestive of insomnia. This is a symptom measure, not necessarily the same definition as our discovery GWAS; studies use different cutoffs. Sleep data, cohorts and timepoints must be specified before comparison.
* A [published PPMI progression study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10578214/) evaluated walking/balance and functional-dependence milestones. These are not FI, FFS or a direct substitute for measured frailty. The searches inspected did **not establish** that all components exist to construct either FI or FFS, nor prove that such measures are absent. Disease-related motor impairment could distort a physical-frailty endpoint and limit generalization; healthy-control and Parkinson-specific analyses should be distinguished.
* The [PPMI access page](https://www.ppmi-info.org/access-data-specimens/download-data) describes genomic data, including genomic VCFs for authorized investigators, alongside clinical data. The [April 2026 data access guidelines](https://www.ppmi-info.org/sites/default/files/docs/PPMI%20Data%20Access%20Guidelines.pdf) list genetic data as Tier 3 and require a genetic request form, supplemental data-use agreement and approval; a general application and agreement also apply. “Public” portal descriptions should not be interpreted as an anonymous participant-level genotype download. The exact cost, permitted use for this particular project and genetic/clinical linkage have not been established. No access application, data download or contact was made.
* The PPMI page notes that raw genome files can be extremely large. No raw files were downloaded. Whether smaller variant-level dosage files or indexed data can be accessed and used under the agreement is unknown.

## Decision

Do not prioritize this over cohorts with documented FI/FFS and a potentially no-fee access route. PPMI could support a **separately defined Parkinson-specific sleep–mobility analysis** if permission, age distribution, participant overlap, availability, adequate sample size and 33 distinct variant contrasts are established. It does not currently meet the requirement for validating the 15- or 22-marker scores against observed FI or FFS. Before any use, establish the phenotype dictionary and usable joint genetic–sleep–frailty sample, exact clinical and genomic linkage, data cost, quality metrics, discovery and selection overlap, case selection, age, ancestry and impact of Parkinson-related motor impairment. Do not treat a published sleep study’s sample count as the genotyped overlap.
