# Post hoc transfer to inflammatory-cue assay conditions

After inspecting the baseline model result, retain its fixed training split, features and weights. In the held-out chromosomes 1 and 2, match baseline-eligible allele pairs to the IL-6 and IFN-alpha result sheets by exact within-workbook ID. Require one ref and one alt row, same author statistical call on both rows, and the same project-independent source criterion as the baseline (`fdr <= 0.05` detected, `n.s` not detected). Require the same exact CRS identifiers as the paired baseline rows; do not equate missing or intermediate FDR calls to non-detection. On the identical baseline–cue intersection, report baseline and cue classification AUROC, average precision and prevalence for the fixed baseline-trained sequence model; do not retrain or tune. This is post hoc same-study, same-variant model transfer, not an interaction model, independent donor validation, disease test or evidence of no effect. No AlphaGenome output used. Nonsignificant assay calls are not true-negative biological ground truth.
