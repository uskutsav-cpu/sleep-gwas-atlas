# ALBION: genetics, sleep and physical-function cohort lead

The [ALBION study's public data description](https://albionstudy.github.io/data.html) lists imputed genome-wide association data for 297 participants and whole-genome sequencing for 64, as of July 2026. The same study describes questionnaires, actigraphy and WatchPAT in subsamples, handgrip dynamometry and a four-metre walk. Those counts are totals for separate assays, not verified numbers with all necessary genotypes, sleep and physical-function measurements together. Sleep quality and sleep-disordered breathing are not automatically an insomnia diagnosis, and grip and gait are components, not a validated complete FI or FFS.

The [study website](https://albionstudy.github.io/) invites enquiries for data access or partnership; the [public catalogue](https://healthdatagateway.org/en/dataset/1978) points to a separate access service and does not state whether the participant-level data are unrestricted or free. No linked participant-level genotype/phenotype file, per-candidate dosage coverage, data use agreement, cost, valid joint sample size or discovery/relative independence was established. Given the small genetic sample, statistical precision could be poor even if access is possible. ALBION is a potentially relevant future aging-cohort lead, **not a confirmed free public dataset or score validation**. No access request was sent and no participant records were retrieved.

## Public assay-availability overlap

The study's [July 2026 assay inventory](https://albionstudy.github.io/ALBION_Biomarkers_JUL2026.xlsx) is publicly downloadable. In its `Biomarkers` sheet, counting rows with numeric `1` in both indicated fields yields **189 rows** with `Imputed GWAS` and baseline `7 day Actigraphy`, and **128 rows** with `Imputed GWAS` and baseline `PAT administration`. The corresponding row counts for `Whole Genome Sequencing` are 15 and zero. These are flags indicating listed assays, not proof of actual data linkage, suitable scores, data quality or usable outcomes. Zero means no row met this exact flag rule; it does not prove that no person was ever assessed using another column or visit.

The inventory has 436 nonblank-ID rows but 435 distinct listed identifiers, with one identifier repeated; we report row counts rather than claiming exact participant counts. Numeric `1` is counted and both missing and `0` are not counted. The inventory has 63 rows flagged for whole-genome sequencing, while the website's descriptive summary says 64; the reason was not established. Likewise, baseline and follow-up flags differ from broad totals on the website. Visit alignment, missing values, duplicate identifiers, sample identity, assay quality and whether the `Imputed GWAS` field corresponds to accessible individual-level imputed dosages must be clarified. The publicly available spreadsheet is an assay inventory, not the underlying genotype or sleep data. Its small overlap sets would give imprecise predictive estimates, and sleep, grip or gait availability for these same records was not established.

## Further access evidence

The data-availability statement for a [2026 ALBION WatchPAT sleep study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1389945725023664) says the data used in that study were licensed and not publicly available, with access possible on reasonable request and permission. This is stronger evidence against immediate public access to **that study's sleep dataset**, but does not establish the access terms, fees or availability for every ALBION release or its imputed genotype file. The linked external dataset catalogue was not reachable in this environment. No request was made.
