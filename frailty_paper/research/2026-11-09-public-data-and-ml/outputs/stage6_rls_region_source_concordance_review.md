# Regional RLS summary-statistic direction audit

The [rs78206187 check](stage6_rls_cross_study_direction_discrepancy.md) found opposite reported G-allele directions between the Akçimen meta-analysis and several other RLS GWAS. To see whether this is confined to that row, we retrieved both the [Akçimen harmonized results](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90435001-GCST90436000/GCST90435387/harmonised/GCST90435387.h.tsv.gz) and [FinnGen R13 RLS results](https://storage.googleapis.com/finngen-public-data-r13/summary_stats/finngen_R13_G6_RLS.gz) for GRCh38 chr2:66,500,000–66,820,000. The interval was chosen around the two previously examined variants, not independently selected or preregistered.

After requiring a unique exact coordinate/ref/alt match, a non-palindromic single-base pair, recognized Catalog harmonization status, and finite estimates, 746 rows remained. Beta values were aligned to the same stated ALT allele. Of these, 42 had p < 5 × 10⁻⁸ in **both** datasets: 30 had the same effect sign and 12 had opposite signs. Nine of the 12 discordant rows lie within 25 kb of rs113851554. No rows in that doubly significant subset fell within 25 kb of rs78206187; that is a statement about this filtered subset, not all tested variants or the rs78206187 result itself, whose Akçimen p was above the genome-wide threshold.

These are correlated variants in a postselected region, **not 42 independent tests**. Selecting for small p values in both studies changes the composition of the comparison, and absence from the matched set may reflect filtering, representation, imputation or data coverage. Matching the same two alleles does not rule out additional alleles at a position. Differences in population, LD, case definition, sample size, cohort overlap, allele processing and models could contribute; this audit cannot identify the cause, establish which file is correct, or prove biological heterogeneity. It also does not test whether the disagreement is genome-wide or a single common error. Do not treat direction counts or their raw effect-size correlation as statistical evidence for independent replication, a causal gene or score performance.

The [matched row table](stage6_rls_region_source_concordance.csv) and [receipt](stage6_rls_region_source_concordance_receipt.json) document the filters, counts, sources and output hash. `python3 work/rls2024/region_direction_audit.py` queries indexed ranges and reproduces the comparison; complete source-file checksums were not verified. A definitive investigation would audit per-cohort input alleles and estimates and matched sample/phenotype definitions, including imputation and cohort overlap. That information was not available in the checked public summaries.

A subsequent [allele-frequency spot check](stage6_rls_region_allele_frequency_spotcheck_review.md) found that the reported ALT frequencies of two common, opposite-sign variants are similar between the meta-analysis and FinnGen. This makes a simple frequency-complement reversal less obvious but cannot verify source-level effect coding or explain the disagreement.

A [third-source INTERVAL lookup](stage6_rls_three_study_region_review.md) found matching signs with FinnGen for all 12 variants in the selected discordant set. Their LD, postselection and possible cohort overlap prevent counting this as 12 independent replications or concluding that the Akçimen source is wrong.
