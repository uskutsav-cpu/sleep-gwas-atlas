# Selected molecular-QTL associations at LD proxies

The [proxy QTL table](stage3_selected_molecular_qtl_ld_proxies.csv) intersects the previously downloaded eQTL Catalogue release 7 summaries for selected regional gene–tissue pairs with nonself candidate proxies from the 1000 Genomes European r² ≥ 0.8, ±250 kb inventory. It contains 379 matched candidate–proxy–molecular-trait records across 12 markers. These are selected molecular tests, not a comprehensive catalogue-wide or genome-wide search. Per-pair counts and skipped ambiguous joins are in [the audit](stage3_selected_molecular_qtl_ld_proxy_audit.json).

For rs323509, the selected testis splice event matches eight reference proxies, including the three nonself variants in its molecular credible set. For other markers, the matches refer to the specific previously selected gene and tissue in the table. The reported QTL p-values are **nominal and unadjusted**; the selected gene/tissue pairs and candidate markers were not chosen independently of the data. Nearby proxies in LD are highly correlated and their p-values are not independent confirmations. A nominal molecular QTL, even combined with marker–proxy LD, does not establish that the same allele drives the insomnia or frailty association.

The reference proxy is GRCh37 and the Catalogue variant is GRCh38. Joins require a matching rsID, chromosome and unordered biallelic allele pair, and exclude ambiguous multiple QTL matches, but do not independently lift over and verify each proxy coordinate. Multiallelic sites and reference differences therefore warrant particular review. The QTL effect is reported on the source ALT allele and was not oriented to the index marker's disease effect; no mediation or effect-direction claim is made. The evidence network records these as separate `selected_proxy_molecular_QTL` edges, with the reference LD and disease associations retained separately.
