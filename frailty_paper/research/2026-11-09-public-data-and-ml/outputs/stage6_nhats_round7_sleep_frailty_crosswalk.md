# NHATS Round 7: same-wave sleep and frailty feasibility

Checked 29 October 2026 using public documentation, not participant records. Round 7 (2017) is the biospecimen collection wave used for the genetic release, making it a practical candidate for a same-wave, cross-sectional score evaluation. Existence of questions in the wave does **not** establish participant-level linkage, complete-case sample size or permission to combine files.

The [Round 7 crosswalk](https://nhats.org/sites/default/files/public-documentation/NHATS_R7_Crosswalk_between_Instruments_and_Codebook_02152024.pdf) identifies the following fields in the public Sample Person file; consult the codebook and access conditions for the actual data:

| Measure | Variable | Question and response distinctions |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep initiation | `hc7aslep30mn` | How often taking more than 30 minutes to fall asleep in the last month; every night, most, some, rarely or never. |
| Sleep maintenance | `hc7trbfalbck` | On nights waking earlier than desired, how often difficulty returning to sleep in the last month; the response includes “don't wake up/not a problem” as a separate category. |
| Sleep medication | `hc7sleepmed` | Frequency of taking medication to help sleep in the last month. |
| Energy | `ss7lowenergy`, `ss7loenlmtat` | Low energy/exhaustion and whether it limits activity, used for the physical frailty low-energy component. |
| Physical activity | `pa7evrgowalk`, `pa7vigoractv` | Walking for exercise and vigorous activity; both feed the published low-activity component. |

The [Round 7 instrument](https://nhats.org/sites/default/files/public-documentation/NHATS%20Round%207%20Data%20Collection%20Instrument_022024_0.pdf) specifies categories 1 = every night, 2 = most nights, 3 = some nights, 4 = rarely, 5 = never for both sleep questions; HC21 additionally has code 7 for “don't wake up/not a problem.” Refused and don't-know options are shown but their numeric file encodings must be confirmed from the codebook. Proxy and sampling rules require checking.

**Candidate prespecification:** for the sleep-initiation endpoint, compare responses 1–2 (most or every night) with 3–5 (less often), treating refused, unknown and inapplicable responses according to the codebook as missing. Analyze HC21 separately, explicitly defining how code 7 is treated; do not silently treat it as a missing value or assume it means the same thing as “never” without documenting that decision. Medication is a separate descriptor and is not itself a diagnosis. These are symptom-frequency measures, not clinical insomnia diagnoses; collapsing categories discards information and a prespecified ordinal sensitivity analysis may be useful if feasible.

For the frailty endpoint, use the [2026 NHATS construction guide and code](stage6_nhats_frailty_2026_implementation.md), including its explicit uncertain categories and a prespecified energy-excluded sensitivity. The sleep and frailty questions coexist within the wave, but their timeframes differ and cross-sectional data do not establish temporal direction or causality. Estimates should use a fixed score, a prespecified model and an ancestry-appropriate analytical subset; confirm genetic-subset sampling or selection treatment and appropriate design-based uncertainty before analysis. Participant overlap, target sample, alleles, imputation quality, and access are unresolved.

Public source files were downloaded locally as `work/nhats_round7_crosswalk.pdf` and `work/nhats_round7_instrument.pdf`; no restricted or public-use individual NHATS data were acquired or uploaded.

## Later-wave option for prospective evaluation

A published [NHATS longitudinal analysis](https://academic.oup.com/biomedgerontology/article/79/9/glae174/7712721) reports constructing a Fried frailty phenotype at Round 8 (2018) in its 2015 cohort, including rules for participants physically unable to complete grip or walking tests. This establishes a later-wave implementation in that study, not the number of Round-7 genotyped participants with valid Round-8 frailty, identical item definitions, or permission to link their data. Other NHATS waves and participant cohorts may differ in collection and instrument rules.

Subject to the actual approved files and joint sample, a prespecified one-year analysis could take genotype and sleep at Round 7 and frailty at Round 8. An incident-frailty analysis requires excluding definite baseline frailty using the same prespecified classification, handling uncertain baseline status explicitly, and counting Round-8 events. Death before follow-up, attrition, sampling design, proxy participation and exclusion of physically unable participants must be addressed rather than treated as ordinary healthy survivors. Round 8 frailty includes exhaustion, potentially overlapping with sleep complaints; report the standard phenotype and the separately labeled energy-excluded sensitivity. This is a proposed longitudinal test, not evidence that sufficient independent cases exist or that scores predict future frailty.

### Genetic-sample selection and Round-8 overlap

The official [NHATS genotyping QC report](https://nhats.org/sites/default/files/public-documentation/NHATSGeneticsQCandImputationReport_08282023.pdf) says all Round-7 participants with a completed sample-person interview were eligible for dried blood spots, but self-response was required for consent; some proxy respondents were consequently not invited. It reports 4,903 consents, 4,691 with a usable sample card, and 4,091 with material and genotyping permission before subsequent QC. [NIAGADS](https://dss.niagads.org/datasets/ng00134/) reports 4,006 post-QC samples. These successive counts describe different stages, not the number with Round-8 sleep/frailty data. The study incorporated a 2015 sample replenishment as well as the continuing cohort, but public aggregate documentation does not give the intersection of the genotyped set with the published Round-8 frailty study's analysis population. In particular, a published Round-8 phenotype in the 2015 cohort cannot be counted as verified overlap with each genetic sample. Before analysis, derive a consent-safe participant flow and examine selection and proxy-related differences using the permitted variables; obtain appropriate weights or justify the target population rather than assigning national representativeness to the 4,006 genotyped sample.
