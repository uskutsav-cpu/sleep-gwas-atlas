# Draft request: exact Ye FFS phenotype construction

**Draft only; not sent.** The corresponding-author details are listed on the [published article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00771-z).

Subject: Request for UK Biobank Fried frailty score construction details

Dear authors,

We are comparing the genetic architecture of insomnia with the frailty index and the 0–5 Fried Frailty Score (FFS) in your GWAS, “A genome-wide association study of frailty identifies significant genetic correlation with neuropsychiatric, cardiovascular, and inflammation pathways.” We would like to interpret how the underlying UK Biobank questionnaire items overlap with the insomnia and fatigue items in other frailty indices.

If available, could you share the UK Biobank phenotype-construction code or a specification with field IDs, assessment instances, response recoding, units and thresholds for all five criteria? We are particularly interested in the exhaustion question and threshold, weight-loss question and whether its intent was measured, grip-strength cutoffs, activity and walking-pace definitions, and how missing or “do not know/prefer not to answer” responses were handled. Please also clarify the number and selection of participants contributing to each component and whether any transformations were applied before the GWAS.

The article describes a 0–5 score and cites a UK Biobank frailty adaptation, but our review of the article and checked supplements did not establish every source-specific field or cutoff. We would cite your work and follow applicable data-use conditions.

Thank you for your consideration.
