# FI–FFS candidate gene comparison

The [comparison table](stage4_candidate_standard_fi_ffs_gene_comparison.csv) joins the Stage 3 candidate gene IDs to two separately run MAGMA v1.10 SNPwise-mean gene analyses using the same 1000 Genomes European LD reference and Ensembl v92 protein-coding gene boundaries extended 1 kb. It contains 182 candidate gene IDs, including genes added through ABC proxy predictions; 147 were tested in both models, and 40 of those passed BH q < 0.05 in each trait-specific gene family (19,129 FI and 19,015 FFS genes). Genes absent from a result are not evidence against association; the tested set is constrained by the annotation and available variants.

A large share of the 40 candidates map to the rs34290943 region; correlated neighboring gene tests cannot be counted as independent signals. FOXP2 is among the genes passing both thresholds (Mak FI q = 3.55 × 10⁻¹³; Ye FFS q = 0.0477), but a positive gene test combines SNP evidence within a gene region and does **not** prove that either exploratory marker causes the association or that FOXP2 mediates insomnia or frailty. The DIAPH3 positional fallback also passes both gene-level tests, but its tested exact-marker cortex QTL was not significant, illustrating why gene-level association and a variant-to-gene link should be kept separate.

This is a descriptive juxtaposition, not a formal test that FI and FFS differ or share a causal variant. FI and FFS have different definitions, studies, sample composition and GWAS filtering; overlap and phenotype composition may affect their relationship. The matching model and reference help interpretation but do not eliminate these differences. Q-values were calculated separately across all genes in each analysis, and candidate genes were selected before this comparison. Do not interpret overlapping significance as replication, colocalization, or evidence of direction of effect. Original full gene results: [Mak FI](stage4_mak_fi_magma_gene_results.csv) and [Ye FFS](stage4_ye_ffs_magma_gene_results.csv). Methods and quality checks are in [MAGMA preparation](stage4_magma_preparation.md).
