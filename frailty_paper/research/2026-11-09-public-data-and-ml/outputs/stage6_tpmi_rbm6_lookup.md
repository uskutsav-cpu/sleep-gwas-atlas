# TPMI sleep-disorder lookup: rs10865954

The [TPMI PheWeb variant page](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/3:49174556-T-C) displays rs10865954 at GRCh38 chr3:49174556 T/C. The project's 15-marker prototype uses GRCh37 chr3:49211989 with the same pair and counts T; the [Ensembl mapping](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/map/human/GRCh37/3:49211989..49211989/GRCh38?content-type=application/json) returns the GRCh38 coordinate on the positive strand. The rsID is multi-allelic in other reference sources. The TPMI effect-allele convention and exact contrast remain unconfirmed; the displayed sign should not yet be assigned to the counted T allele.

For [sleep disorders](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327), the browser reports effect size 0.037, SE 0.021 and p = 0.084 (45,836 cases and 287,785 controls). These are rounded, and the diagnostic grouping is broader than insomnia. The result is not statistically clear at nominal 0.05 and does not establish the absence of an association. This postselected single-marker screen does not validate the score or an individual joint outcome. TPMI displays an allele-frequency range of 0.96–0.97 without establishing here which allele that refers to; study-specific imputation, exact representation and target overlap require checking. The website labels KLHDC8B as nearest gene, which does not establish any causal gene.

TPMI requests acknowledgment and citation of [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x). The main source and other candidate checks are in `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.
