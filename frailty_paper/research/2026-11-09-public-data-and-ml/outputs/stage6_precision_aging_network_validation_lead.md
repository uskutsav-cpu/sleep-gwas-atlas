# Precision Aging Network: a candidate for future sleep–frailty genetic research

Checked 29 October 2026 against the [Precision Aging Network data-access page](https://precisionagingnetwork.org/access-pan-study-data-for-research/) and its [laboratory and biomarker dictionary](https://pandb.org/pan_support_files/data_dictionary/labs_biomarkers_dd.html).

**Decision: retain as a controlled-access lead, not an immediately usable public validation dataset.** The page describes an application with a research plan, researcher identity and affiliation, and a data-use agreement. It does not establish that the requested project will be approved or that all required resources are free. We have not submitted an application, accepted terms, or accessed participant data.

The Healthy Minds for Life (HML) portion provides a promising combination: a Medical Outcomes Study sleep measure, an upper-extremity-function (UEF) frailty measure, wearable data, and precomputed polygenic scores. The listed baseline counts are 1,063 HML participants, 902 with sleep survey data, 726 with UEF frailty, and 1,022 with polygenic scores. These are marginal counts and do not give the number with all three, suitable quality, or nonmissing covariates. A different, much larger MindCrowd count on the same page must not be used as the HML genetics sample size.

The dictionary lists `sleep_duration`, with source label `Sleep_duration_GCST007561`, and `snoring` among its 149 polygenic-score table fields. The source label identifies an underlying study, not sufficient detail to recreate or validate the score; ascertain the scoring weights, ancestry, training overlap, quality control, and interpretation before analysis. Sleep duration is a different trait from insomnia. A text check found no field labeled insomnia or frailty in this dictionary, which does not prove that no related score exists under another name. These precomputed scores are not our 15- or 22-variant prototypes and do not allow reconstruction of them. The site lists two APOE variant calls; that does not establish access to genome-wide individual genotypes or any of our 33 candidate variants.

The site's UEF frailty scores are video-derived single- and dual-task measures. They are not automatically the project's deficit-accumulation frailty index or Fried frailty phenotype. The HML sleep survey is also not necessarily a clinical insomnia diagnosis. Before using the cohort to compare FI and FFS, verify the full item definitions and whether either construct can be derived, participant-level overlap, age distribution, ancestry, and discovery-sample overlap. The listed baseline and two-year follow-up counts do not establish adequate paired genetic/frailty/sleep observations.

If access is ever pursued, first establish whether participant-level imputed genotypes for the exact alleles are distributed under the same request, their cost, imputation quality and genome build; identify the allowed computing environment, permitted users and data-transfer restrictions. Without project variants, the study could support a distinct exploratory association of its available sleep-duration PGS with UEF and sleep outcomes, but that would not validate our insomnia–frailty scores. No individual-level score evaluation was performed here.
