# Diagnosis-code overlap in All of Us fatigue and weakness follow-up

The public [PhecodeX vocabulary](https://github.com/PheWAS/PhecodeXVocabulary) identifies `SS_807` as malaise and fatigue and `SS_807.21` as generalized muscle weakness. In both the v1.0 and v1.1 **unrolled ICD-CM mapping files**, the weakness category's two mapped codes—ICD-10-CM `M62.81` and ICD-9-CM `728.87`—are also mapped to the broader malaise/fatigue category. The parent category maps to 17 ICD-CM codes in v1.0 and 22 in v1.1; examples also cover malaise, fatigue and post-viral fatigue. The two code lists for `NS_333.2` insomnia and `SS_807` have no identical ICD-CM code in either version.

This is a **vocabulary-level check**, not a reconstruction of All of Us participant cases. The exact vocabulary version, any local mappings, OMOP transformations, exclusion rules and participant-level category assignment in the deployed CDR v8 tables have not been independently confirmed. The [All by All documentation](https://support.researchallofus.org/hc/en-us/articles/27049847988884-Overview-of-the-All-by-All-tables-available-on-the-All-of-Us-Researcher-Workbench) identifies PhecodeX as a phenotype source; the [authors' methods supplement](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/medrxiv/early/2026/05/12/2026.05.08.26350964/DC1/embed/media-1.pdf?download=true) describes its EHR phenotypes, but neither finding establishes the exact case-set intersection here.

**Interpretation:** fatigue and weakness should not be treated as independent external outcomes: the public vocabulary makes code nesting plausible and the phenotypes were derived from the same health-record resource. Differences between their association results may reflect definitions, overlapping patients and sample size. A lack of identical codes between insomnia and fatigue does not imply patients are independent or that sleep and fatigue are clinically distinct; one patient can have both diagnoses. No overlap percentage or actual participant counts are inferred here, and neither category is a measured FI or Fried frailty score.
