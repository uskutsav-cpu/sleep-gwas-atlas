# Variant-level SingleBrain follow-up

## Scope and reproducibility

The public SingleBrain browser was queried for SMAD5, FOXP2, INKA1 and TMEM161B-DT in the seven major brain-cell categories when the gene appeared in the downloaded top-association files. The source-specific, versioned gene IDs were used, and chromosome, GRCh38 position, REF and ALT were matched exactly to the 488-variant project queue. The reproducible query script is `stage5_singlebrain_variant_query.py`; archived portal responses and request counts are `stage5_singlebrain_priority_gene_portal_raw.zip` and `stage5_singlebrain_priority_query_receipt.json`. The join to the existing six-study GWAS matrix is `stage5_singlebrain_priority_variant_phenotypes.csv`; `stage5_singlebrain_priority_variant_matches.csv` contains the raw matched portal columns.

The portal returned 54,114 rows across 23 gene–cell queries, with 584 exact variant–gene–cell matches corresponding to 104 distinct queue alleles. Five queries returned zero rows. Some gene–cell combinations were not queried because the gene was absent from the local top-association file. A zero response, an unqueried combination or a nonmatch is not evidence that a variant has no effect. The portal returns a selected set of nominal records; its completeness relative to the source full association files has not been established. Disease and molecular rows repeated for different cells, genes or correlated variants are not independent replications.

## Illustrative result

For SMAD5 in excitatory neurons, the allele chr5:136159634:A>G is the source's top gene association: portal beta +0.15061, random-effects p approximately 1.96e-21 and source gene q approximately 2.13e-18. For this exact allele, the previously harmonized project matrix reports insomnia p=2.72e-6 in Jansen and p=0.0240 in FinnGen R13, frailty-index p=0.000942 in Mak and physical frailty score p=0.015 in Ye. These disease associations are nominal, selected after inspecting the locus, and do not establish a shared signal. The different trait definitions, scales, possible cohort overlap and multiple testing all matter.

The browser's beta agrees with the source top-file fixed-effect beta in all seven exact top-allele matches; their explicitly identified effect allele equals ALT. This small check does not establish effect-allele orientation for every other portal row. The browser presents random-effects p values rounded to a few significant digits alongside fixed-effect beta and an absolute Z score; it does not provide a matched standard error in this table. Do not derive an SE from the rounded random-effects p and fixed-effect beta, or use these fields as if they were a complete colocalization input. Full source statistics and the source's allele column are needed for signed regional analysis.

A separately generated brain molecular association supports investigation of the variant–gene pair, not independent phenotype replication or validated model accuracy. The apparent variation across cell types needs a direct cell-by-cell interaction or heterogeneity analysis: different p values or lack of a portal result alone do not establish specificity. SingleBrain's underlying cohort overlap with disease studies and model training has not been confirmed. The original rs7734045 SMAD5 marker and chr5:136207208:G>A alternative were not returned as exact matches in these portal results; this is not a negative QTL finding.

Sources: [SingleBrain study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02541-x), [public download record](https://zenodo.org/records/14908182), and [public browser](https://singlebrain.nygenome.org/eqtl/).
