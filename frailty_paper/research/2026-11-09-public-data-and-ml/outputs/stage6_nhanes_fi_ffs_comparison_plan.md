# Exploratory matched deficit index / modified physical phenotype

Written after observing prior sleep–index and sleep–grip results, before fitting the models below; not a preregistration. Use NHANES 2011–12 and 2013–14 adults 60+ examined with complete sleep exposure, 24-item index (>=20 components) and all five modified physical phenotype domains. Fit survey-weighted age/sex-adjusted linear models separately for the adapted index and the five-domain count (range 0–5), plus the same count omitting exhaustion (range 0–4). Same participants for all models and weights WTMEC2YR. Report counts and uncertainty; do not compare coefficients on unlike scales or infer difference from different p values. Post hoc sensitivity excludes exhaustion, not a validated Fried frailty classification. Assess missingness and threshold choices.

Domains are adapted from https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9100462/: self-reported difficulty carrying 10 pounds PFQ061E 2–4; self-reported difficulty walking between rooms PFQ061H 2–4; exhaustion DPQ040 2–3; sedentary minutes PAD680 in upper group; unintentional weight loss >=10 lb WHD050−WHD020 >=10 and WHQ060=2. PFQ 1=0 and 5/7/9 missing; DPQ 0/1=0, 2/3=1, other missing. Valid sedentary range 0–1439, refused/unknown missing. Because published thresholds are not fully specified, use 80th percentile computed separately in each cycle from examined adults 60–69, 70–79, or 80+ with valid sedentary responses, using unweighted empirical type-1 quantiles, with all ties at or above threshold flagged. This may differ from the publication and from exactly 20%. For weight, valid reported current and previous weights 50–750 lb; if loss <10 lb score 0 without intentionality, respecting the documented skip; if loss >=10 lb score 1 for unintentional and 0 for intentional, unknown/refused missing. This is a modified physical phenotype, not the original performance-based Fried phenotype: weakness and slowness are self-reported, sedentary time substitutes for activity, and weight relies on recall. Deficit index and physical phenotype share conceptual physical domains; no causal, genetic or clinical validation inference.
