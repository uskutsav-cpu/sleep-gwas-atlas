# Possible Zunpeng source: brain-cell cCRE overlap screen

A possible match for the mentor's “Zunpeng” reference is [Zunpeng Liu et al., Cell 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.06.031), but **the mentor has not identified the person or paper**. Do not describe this as the intended source or as a prior project study without confirmation. The paper describes 3.5 million single-cell epigenomic/transcriptomic profiles from 384 postmortem brain samples across six regions and 111 Alzheimer's disease and control individuals; cells and brain samples are not independent donors. Its [processed atlas](https://compbio.mit.edu/AD_Multiomic_MultiRegion/) lists accessible candidate cis-regulatory-element (cCRE) peak files. The paper states that raw data and metadata require controlled-use agreements; no controlled data were accessed.

Assuming the posted peak BED files use GRCh38, we compared the 22 score-candidate GRCh38 positions against all **67 subtype-labelled peak files**. Four of 22 candidate positions fell strictly inside at least one subtype peak, comprising 15 candidate–file pairs:

| Candidate | Subtype-file overlaps | Examples of source file labels |
|---|---:|---|
| rs4886140 | 9 | Ast_DCLK1, Exc_L6b, OPC_TPST1 |
| rs7734045 | 2 | Exc_L5_6_IT_Car3, Exc_RELN_COL5A2 |
| rs28528997 | 1 | Exc_L6b |
| rs9536347 | 3 | Inh_ENOX2_SPHKAP, Oli_DPP10, Oli_OPALIN |

These are **physical position overlaps in a brain atlas**, not a genetic association, single-cell eQTL, allele-specific functional assay, cell-type enrichment test, causal-gene assignment or independent validation of insomnia, frailty or a score. No matched background set or multiple-testing framework was used; number of overlapping files is not a measure of statistical significance, tissue specificity, or number of independent experiments. The atlas is based on Alzheimer's-disease and control brains, not a sleep–frailty cohort, and the brain-region/cell-state relevance to sleep and physical frailty has not been established. Prior project brain analyses or reference data may share donors or source material; independence from those sources and from AlphaGenome model training is unverified. The 18 candidates without an interior match are not proven inactive or absent in all brain cells.

**Coordinate caution:** The paper reports mapping its sequencing to GRCh38 and using liftOver to obtain hg19 annotations for LDSC. Its [published code](https://github.com/ZunpengLiu/Multi-region_AD/blob/main/01_snATAC/08_GWAS_enrichment/Liftover_hg38_to_hg19.sh) explicitly converts hg38 BED inputs to hg19-suffixed outputs, which supports, but does not definitively establish, the assembly of each file posted in the atlas directory. Some published peak-export code writes the first three columns of R `GRanges` directly, so source-specific start-coordinate convention should also be confirmed. To avoid interpreting boundary cases, this screen required `start < one-based variant position < end`; zero candidate–peak pairs hit either boundary. GRCh38 score mappings and pair identities were inherited from prior project work, not independently re-established for this screen. All overlaps remain **conditional on source-file assembly and coordinate semantics**.

The public `Union_PeakSet.bed` overlapped only three of the four candidates. For rs9536347, subtype files overlap but the union file does not; the nearest union intervals ended about 243 bases before or began about 198 bases after the variant by the reported coordinates. The [interval audit](stage6_liu_union_subtype_audit.md) shows that each subtype interval overlaps a nearby union interval even though the variant is outside that union interval. The paper uses ArchR peak merging, whose documented iterative overlap removal provides a plausible explanation; the specific selection history is not verified. Do not use the union to assert the three subtype overlaps are false, nor assume it preserves every base of every subtype interval. The locally retained union file matched the hash captured in the scan. No donor-level or per-region signal was analyzed.

The [overlap table](stage6_liu_brain_ccre_candidate_overlaps.csv) contains exact source intervals and candidate mappings, and the [receipt](stage6_liu_brain_ccre_candidate_overlaps.json) records the source index, each file's hash/size and boundary checks. `python3 outputs/stage6_liu_brain_ccre_candidate_screen.py` retrieves and scans the 68 posted BED files (67 subtype-labelled plus union); it does not retain the subtype files or compute enrichment. Code in the author's repository has its own license; confirm separate terms before redistributing data. Individual-level independent score validation and FI-without-sleep genetic analysis remain outstanding.
