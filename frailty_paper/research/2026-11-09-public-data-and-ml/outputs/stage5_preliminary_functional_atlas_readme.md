# Stage 5 preliminary functional atlas and execution status

The [preliminary functional atlas](stage5_preliminary_functional_atlas.csv) has **488 unique GRCh38 REF→ALT inputs**: all 23 exploratory markers, 444 distinct molecular credible-set variants, and 23 matched comparison SNVs, with two marker/credible-set overlaps deduplicated. It records separate GWAS, molecular-QTL and credible-set context and marks every model prediction as obtained or missing. The table is a working evidence inventory, **not** the finished full-precision functional atlas. The [preliminary integrated network](stage5_preliminary_evidence_network.csv) preserves the Stage 4 evidence edges and adds explicitly labeled, rounded AlphaGenome website observations and allele mappings; graph paths are not causal proof.

## Inputs and quality control

The [Stage 3–4 freeze](stage5_frozen_stage3_stage4_inputs.tar.gz) contains 437 source deliverables, allele/credible-set files, accessible selected molecular inputs and scripts; each archive member's hash was verified against the [manifest](stage5_frozen_input_manifest.json). Large external reference datasets are not included; their existing receipts and hashes are preserved in the archive. The [freeze receipt](stage5_frozen_input_receipt.json) records the archive checksum.

All 23 markers had unique positive-strand GRCh37→GRCh38 mappings in the [independent Ensembl mapping check](stage5_marker_grch37_grch38_liftover_audit.csv), and the GRCh38 reference sequence matched the specified study allele pair. All 444 distinct eQTL Catalogue molecular credible-set variants had REF sequence matching Ensembl GRCh38. The [variant manifest](stage5_variant_input_manifest.csv), [source/set provenance](stage5_molecular_credible_set_provenance.csv) and [reference receipt](stage5_variant_reference_check_receipt.json) record these details. Among the credible-set variants, 28 are indels; the [normalization check](stage5_indel_normalization.csv) checked each against 100 bp of flanking reference and confirmed equivalent edited haplotypes without a position shift. This validates representation within the checked context, not the disease-causal identity or the original GWAS allele orientation.

The FOXP2 insertion is `chr7:114534306 T→TA` (molecular-set aliases rs66823671 and rs397734598). It has molecular PIP 0.312 in the selected pituitary eQTL model, not a disease PIP. An independently available [JoGo FOXP2 variant record](https://jogo.csml.org/haplotype_detail?alt=A&chr=chr7&hapid=a0001c0001t0004g0171&pos=114663134&regionname=FOXP2_chr7_114409815_114698765) also lists the same position and T/TA representation; its functional consequence for insomnia or frailty is not established.

The 23 [comparison variants](stage5_matched_controls.csv) are locally selected, reference-frequency-matched and low-LD controls that do not pass nominal thresholds in three input GWAS; they are not proven biological negatives. Two required 1 Mb rather than 250 kb search windows. Details are in [the AVI control review](stage5_avi_controls_review.md).

## AlphaGenome evidence obtained and outstanding

The official Atlas website was used to obtain rounded AVI scores for **all 23 markers**, **23 controls**, and **11 selected credible-set SNV alternatives** (57 distinct variants). See [marker scores](stage5_23_markers_atlas_avi.csv), [controls](stage5_avi_matched_control_comparison.csv), and [credible-set alternatives](stage5_priority_credible_snv_atlas_avi.csv). These are broad variant-impact scores, not phenotype risk or complete molecular predictions. Atlas currently covers SNVs, so it cannot supply the 28 indel predictions in this workflow.

The preliminary [tissue-specific web review](stage5_initial_tissue_prediction_review.md) and [rs323509 review](stage5_rs323509_atlas_initial_observations.md) preserve the website's rounded values. They do not establish agreement or disagreement with the measured eQTL/sQTL. The [measured evidence inventory](stage5_measured_molecular_evidence.csv) records GTEx v10, selected Catalogue r7 proxy QTLs and OneK1K observations, with event, tissue and ALT effect where available. GTEx data overlap model training resources; eQTL Catalogue reprocessing and GTEx releases are not independent replications. sQTL beta signs cannot be compared to the model's unsigned gene-level maxima without event-specific alignment.

The [score queue](stage5_score_queue.csv) contains the full set. The official AlphaGenome client v0.10.0 is installed and its [offline validation](stage5_alphagenome_client_validation.json) confirms valid Variant objects, one-based input coordinates, 1,048,576-bp windows and availability of the recommended RNA, three separate splicing, ATAC, DNase, histone, TF, CAGE and PRO-cap scorers. **No API inference or full-precision tissue-specific comparison has yet been performed.** A live API key is required for that work; the local research model also requires specialized hardware and acceptance of separate weight terms. Full model outputs, tissue availability audits, matched eQTL/sQTL comparisons, and an integrated final evidence atlas remain outstanding.

This is research-only work. The source scores, QTL associations and network edges do not establish a causal variant→gene→cell→phenotype pathway or an individual's disease risk.
