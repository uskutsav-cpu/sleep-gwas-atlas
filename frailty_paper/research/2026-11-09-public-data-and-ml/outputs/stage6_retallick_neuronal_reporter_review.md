# Neuron and immune-cue reporter data: candidate coverage

[Retallick-Townsley et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-77114-x) report a lentiMPRA in human induced glutamatergic neurons from two donors, with vehicle, IL-6 and interferon-alpha conditions. The published analysis reports 267 variant-specific effects and 61 variant-by-cytokine interaction effects among its selected psychiatric-risk library. This dataset may be relevant to the project's proposed brain–immune functional benchmark, but the experimental system is a neuronal model and the tested variants were chosen from other trait studies; it is not a sleep–frailty outcome dataset.

I searched the **version-of-record Supplementary Data 2** by exact rsID text for the 33 project candidates. One, rs10865954, appears in the library-design and GRCh38 mapping sheets; its two allele sequences are listed in the library sheet at chromosome 3 position 49,174,556 (C and T), consistent with the project’s recorded contrast. Exact text matches are not independent confirmation of all allele representation. None of the 33 rsIDs appeared in the published baseline, IL-6 or interferon-alpha result sheets, nor in either listed interaction-result sheet. **This does not establish absence of an effect:** the reason a designed variant is missing from a result sheet is not established, and names or QC filters may differ. We did not classify rs10865954 as a reporter negative or attach a result to the graph.

The published workbook contains thousands of assay-result rows and a designed sequence library, so it is a useful lead for a broader held-out assay benchmark. Before using it for training or testing, establish whether the reported rows include a complete tested universe and measured non-hits; obtain per-condition QC and the exact allele-effect convention, determine genomic/study/model training overlap, group replicated constructs and donors, and use locus-held-out splits. A small selected 33-variant lookup cannot by itself evaluate an embedding model. The paper is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; review permitted reuse and redistribution of any derived data before sharing or training.

[Coverage receipt](stage6_retallick_candidate_coverage.json) contains sheet row numbers, file SHA-256 and exact search scope; [script](../work/retallick_reporter_coverage.py) operates on the publisher's Supplementary Data 2. The Europe PMC archive also returned an older preprint supplement with different conditions; it was not used for the published result. This is molecular assay context, not independent validation of the project scores or evidence that an allele causes either disease.
