# NCDS age-62 physical frailty: component feasibility

Reviewed 29 October 2026. The public survey instruments identify candidate information for several physical-frailty domains. **They do not establish a valid, complete Fried frailty phenotype or a linked genetic test.** The exact original study's operational definitions, approved field release, observed joint sample and participant-level missingness remain to be checked.

| Fried domain | Age-62 evidence | What remains |
|---|---|---|
| Weakness | Measured grip in kg, two trials per hand (`n10mmgsl1`, `n10mmgsr1`, `n10mmgsl2`, `n10mmgsr2`). | Confirm field access, valid test selection and any sex/body-size adjusted thresholds; account for hand injury and inability/refusal. |
| Slowness | Two timed 8-foot walks (`n10mmwlka`, `n10mmwlkb`, plus outcome codes). | Prespecify handling of aids, inability, invalid times, trial choice, distance and height/sex thresholds; the protocol may differ from the discovery phenotype. |
| Low activity | Interview asks regular participation and frequency; a supplementary self-completion inventory asks frequency and average duration of many activities. | Verify available release, routing, and how intensities and seasonal frequencies are mapped. These are not automatically the original Fried kilocalorie thresholds; occupation-only activity excludes people not working. |
| Exhaustion | Self-completion asks how often the person feels full of energy. | This is not the same question or timescale as the original Fried exhaustion items. A low-energy proxy would need a specified threshold, separate validation and explicit sleep/fatigue overlap assessment. |
| Unintentional weight loss | Current self-reported and health-visit weight are available; age-55 self-reported weight was also collected. The age-62 interview asks whether the person is currently trying to lose, gain or maintain weight. | None of those observations establishes unintentional weight loss during the previous year. A 7–12-year difference between measurements is a different quantity, and current intention does not establish the intention behind a past change. Search the complete release for a direct question before asserting this component is available. |

The [health-visit guide](https://cls.ucl.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/ncds_sweep10_age62_user_guide.pdf) documents grip and walking methods. The [main interview](https://cls.ucl.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NCDS_sweep10_age62_questionnaire.pdf), pp. 354–355 and 361–362, describes exercise frequency, current weight and weight-management intention; its strenuous-exercise question changed across fieldwork stages, and the interview asks frequency only for those reporting regular activity. The [self-completion instrument](https://cls.ucl.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NCDS_sweep10_-age-62_-Your-Life-Now-self-completion-questionnaire.pdf) contains leisure-activity frequency/duration and the energy question as well as sleep questions. [Prior age-55 weight source](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44197-023-00093-5).

For the immediate project, separately analysing measured grip and walking as continuous outcomes is better supported than declaring a five-item score available. If missing components lead to a modified phenotype, describe it as such; do not use original 0/1–2/3–5 category labels as if all five Fried criteria had been measured. Confirm item weights, response modes, sample timing, field use conditions and complete-case counts before any composite or comparison. The original FFS includes exhaustion, so an association with insomnia can involve shared symptom constructs even without identical wording.

The linked-genotype pathway is distinct from the unlinked EGA release: [CLS](https://cls.ucl.ac.uk/data-access-training/data-access/accessing-data-via-public-repositories/) says linked genetic and survey data require a direct application, and [genetics documentation](https://cls-genetics.github.io/docs/access.html) says genetic data are available without a data charge upon approval. Those statements do not confirm that age-62 biomeasures will be approved in the requested extract, that there are no incidental institutional costs, or the number of participants with outcomes and the 15 or 22 markers. No application or participant analysis has been performed.
