# Mexico City Prospective Study: potentially linked genetics and aging measures, access conditional

Checked 29 October 2026. The Mexico City Prospective Study (MCPS) is a large cohort of adults recruited at age 35+ in two Mexico City districts. Its [public data showcase](https://datashare.ndph.ox.ac.uk/mexico/exinfo.cgi) reports 140,831 genotyped, 141,046 exome-sequenced and 9,950 whole-genome-sequenced participants; these are source-wide counts, not the number with joint sleep, frailty and target-score data. A [published MCPS analysis](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.maturitas.2025.108656) measured self-reported sleep duration and physical activity in 153,866 participants. Sleep duration is not insomnia, and activity alone is not FI or Fried frailty.

The study's [public register of approved/completed projects](https://www.ctsu.ox.ac.uk/research/prospective-blood-based-study-of-150-000-individuals-in-mexico-old/data-access/data-and-sample-sharing-policy/completed-and-protected-projects-with-currently-available-data) lists a project titled “Frailty index and its association with cardiovascular disease and all-cause mortality in Mexican adults,” dated 9 September 2024. This is evidence that a frailty-index project was registered, **not** confirmation that an existing FI variable, its item definitions, the current project's use, or a linked insomnia–genotype–FI sample is available. It also raises a project-overlap/protection question. The precise items must be examined for direct sleep/fatigue content and appropriate exclusions; no FI/FFS measure is assumed here.

**Access and cost:** The [MCPS version 6.4 data-sharing policy](https://www.ctsu.ox.ac.uk/research/mexico-city-prospective-study/data-access/data-and-sample-sharing-policy-6-4) describes managed access for bona fide researchers with a data-sharing agreement; it describes open-access data as free to applicants from low- and middle-income countries, while researchers in high-income countries face a data application charge plus usage costs above $1,000. Its listed application charge range is £2,500–£7,500, depending on requested data. Collaboration costs may also arise, and specific rates and eligibility require confirmation. “Open access” in this policy means access after review and agreement, not an anonymous download; eligibility for any exemption and our institution's location are not established. Genetic data and newer releases can have additional availability and exclusivity conditions. This lead is **not established as free for this project**. The study's public variant browser summarizes variation and is not participant-level outcome data.

**Why it could matter:** An appropriately authorized linked-data analysis could assess transportability outside the predominantly European discovery population, with a published sleep-duration measure and an FI only if available and appropriate. This is not a replication of insomnia diagnosis or physical Fried frailty. The study population is admixed; use continuous ancestry information and appropriate local-ancestry/relatedness and imputation-quality handling instead of treating all participants as genetically homogeneous. Prespecify model, score allele representations, covariates and sample overlap; quantify completeness and outcome events. A score association in this cohort would not automatically generalize to all Mexican or Latin American populations.

Before prioritizing, confirm participant-level permission for genotype plus relevant phenotype linkage, eligible no-fee route and all computing costs, sleep question and wave, whether an FI can be supplied or reconstructed and whether it includes sleep/fatigue items, Fried components, post-QC marker coverage and allele alignment, ancestry/kinship covariates, event counts and discovery/selection overlap. No request, external message, participant-level access, or score validation was performed.

## Public dictionary follow-up

[Field 28](https://datashare.ndph.ox.ac.uk/mexico/field.cgi?id=28) records sleep duration in integer hours, with 159,373 baseline responses and 10,045 resurvey responses; these counts are not the linked genetic sample. The range reported for baseline is 1–22 hours, so an analysis needs prespecified plausibility and missing-data checks. Duration is not an insomnia diagnosis. The public project register's FI project remains a lead, not verification of accessible FI items, linked records, eligibility or free access for this team.
