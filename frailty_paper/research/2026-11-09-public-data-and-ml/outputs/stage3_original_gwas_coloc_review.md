# Original Atkins FI and Jansen insomnia: exploratory molecular colocalization

I compared the project's original public Atkins frailty-index and Jansen UK Biobank insomnia summary statistics with four molecular traits already investigated. At one illustrative set of priors, the **rs323509-region testis splice QTL** has H4 = 0.791 with Atkins FI and H4 = 0.978 with Jansen insomnia. These are two separate pairwise comparisons, not a formal three-trait test. They nominate the region for follow-up but do **not** prove that one variant drives all three associations, that the testis junction mediates either trait, or that this is an independent replication.

| Molecular trait and region | Molecular credible-set coverage, Atkins / Jansen | Atkins FI H1 / H3 / H4 | Jansen insomnia H1 / H3 / H4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Testis splice junction near rs323509 | 4/4; 4/4 | 0.005 / 0.204 / **0.791** | <0.001 / 0.022 / **0.978** |
| FOXP2 pituitary expression | 3/4; 3/4 | <0.001 / 0.999 / 0.001 | 0.057 / 0.875 / 0.068 |
| INKA1 skeletal-muscle expression | 68/88; 68/88 | <0.001 / 0.307 / 0.693 | 0.390 / 0.604 / 0.007 |
| ADARB1 skeletal-muscle splice junction | 13/15; 13/15 | 0.938 / 0.054 / 0.008 | 0.949 / 0.035 / 0.017 |

These values are model-dependent posterior probabilities, not p-values. H1 is QTL only, H3 distinct signals, H4 a shared signal; H0/H2 are small in these comparisons. All comparisons use QTL effect prior SD 0.15, p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴ and p12 = 10⁻⁵. Jansen's log-odds prior SD is 0.20. Atkins FI prior SDs are approximately 0.147 on a working phenotype scale estimated using per-row SE, allele frequency and the source's constant total N of 175,226; that constant is not a verified per-variant sample size. The QTL prior assumes an approximately standardized molecular phenotype. These scales are assumptions requiring review.

For rs323509, varying the trait prior SD by 0.5–2 and p12 from 10⁻⁶ to 5×10⁻⁵ gives H4 ranges **0.269–0.950 for FI** and **0.817–0.996 for insomnia**. For INKA1 FI, H4 ranges 0.184–0.919, and only 68 of 88 molecular credible-set variants are represented. FOXP2 excludes a molecular credible-set insertion with reported PIP 0.312. ADARB1 covers 13/15 molecular credible-set variants. More complete matching, alternative priors and LD-aware multi-signal analyses are required. [All sensitivity results](stage3_original_gwas_coloc_sensitivity.csv) and [coverage](stage3_original_gwas_coloc_coverage.json).

At rs323509, these Atkins and Jansen results differ from the separate Mak and FinnGen comparisons in the [testis review](stage3_rs323509_testis_colocalization.md). INKA1 also differs between Atkins and Mak FI. Phenotype construction, cohorts, sample size, variant coverage, scale assumptions and multiple signals may contribute; no formal test of differences between studies was performed. Atkins FI includes UK Biobank and TwinGene, Jansen insomnia uses UK Biobank, and the FI construction includes a sleep-related item. Their sample and phenotype overlap can induce dependence; the two pairwise results are not independent confirmation of a shared mechanism. Testis is not established as a relevant tissue for insomnia or frailty, and a colocalization result is not mediation evidence.

## Inputs and checks

The source FI is the project's harmonized public Atkins GCST90020053 meta-GWAS; Jansen is the 386,533-person public UK Biobank-only insomnia GWAS, not the restricted full meta-analysis. Source documentation describes Jansen OR per A1, converted by the project harmonizer to log(OR); the project's output has constant effective N rather than a per-variant sample size. These project harmonized inputs already excluded MHC, palindromic sites, and MAF ≤1% and have source-specific QC; they cannot support conclusions about excluded variants. Source GWAS and molecular builds/allele representations still need independent authentication.

We matched unique rsIDs, chromosome, uniquely positive-strand-lifted positions and allele pairs, excluding palindromic, non-biallelic and low-frequency variants; QTL effects and GWAS effects were aligned to ALT. The QTL data are Catalogue r7, GTEx v8 reprocessed: testis QTD000340 junction, pituitary QTD000301 FOXP2 expression, muscle QTD000281 INKA1 expression, and muscle QTD000285 ADARB1 junction. Matching receipts are `stage3_*_original_gwas_harmonization_receipt.json` in this output folder. The underlying regional cache preserves source rows within 2.5 Mb of candidate markers and does not independently establish genome builds or allele semantics. Candidate and region selection were post hoc.

Reference [coloc v5.2.3](https://github.com/chr1swallace/coloc/blob/v5.2.3/R/claudia.R) functions agree with the custom single-signal ABF formulas on these matched inputs to <5×10⁻¹⁵ ([numerical check](stage3_original_gwas_coloc_formula_check.csv)). This does not validate source QC, prior assumptions, tissue relevance, sample independence or causal interpretation. The source beta/SE values are rounded; in the rs323509 region the median absolute difference between supplied −log10(p) and the value implied by beta/SE is about 0.004–0.005, with larger differences for some variants. The ABF uses beta and SE, not the supplied p-value. GWAS imputation quality and molecular credible-set completeness remain important limitations.
