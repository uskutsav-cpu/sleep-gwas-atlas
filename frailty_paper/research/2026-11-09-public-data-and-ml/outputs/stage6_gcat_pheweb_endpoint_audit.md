# GCAT PheWeb: outcome availability

On 29 October 2026 UTC, the public [GCAT PheWeb phenotype inventory](https://pheweb.igtp.cat/api/phenotypes.json) returned ten GWAS phenotypes: psoriasis, lipoprotein disorders, asthma, migraine, type 2 diabetes, glaucoma, hypertension, obesity, anxiety disorders and a single episode of major depressive disorder. None is labelled sleep disorder, insomnia, frailty index or Fried frailty. The [PheWeb methods page](https://pheweb.igtp.cat/about) describes 4,987 participants, ICD-10 outcomes and GRCh37 coordinates. Its positive-control catalog entries include the ten diagnoses above; the lack of a relevant label does not show that GCAT lacks these measurements elsewhere or that particular SNPs have null effects.

This matters because the separate [PolyGenie](https://polygenie.igtp.cat/) PRS interface displays many more EHR outcomes, including sleep-disorder and fatigue codes. A PolyGenie score–phenotype result is not evidence that its corresponding full SNP-level GWAS is available in the ten-phenotype PheWeb. The public PheWeb catalog checked here therefore does not currently offer a direct insomnia or frailty candidate-level summary validation. The PolyGenie analysis used 4,988 QC-passing participants according to its paper; the 4,987 in this separate PheWeb should not be assumed identical without verifying sample selection.

The institution's [resource page](https://www.igtp.cat/en/strategic-projects/gcat/1/gcat-genomes-for-life) says summary statistics are directly available in one section and available on request in another. Those statements do not establish availability of full sleep or frailty GWAS results. Individual-level genetic data require formal approval; no access or score validation was performed. Other GCAT studies or data releases may differ from this inventory.
