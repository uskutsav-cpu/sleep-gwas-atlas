# Two regions without exact-marker GTEx v10 QTL returns

For rs9845387 and rs4886140, the earlier GTEx v10 exact-marker query returned no significant eQTL or sQTL rows. To assess whether a regional comparison was feasible, I selected **positional fallback genes**, not QTL-supported targets: LSAMP, whose gene body overlaps rs9845387, and DIAPH3, a protein-coding gene whose body is approximately 406 kb from rs4886140. Ensembl's GRCh38 region output also reports a lncRNA overlapping rs4886140 and the positional pseudogene RPP40P2 about 3.3 kb away. Proximity alone does not make any of these genes causal.

In the related eQTL Catalogue r7 GTEx v8 reprocessing, brain-cortex expression data were available for both positional genes, but their exact marker–allele nominal p-values were **0.102 for rs9845387–LSAMP** and **0.581 for rs4886140–DIAPH3**. These are not evidence of a significant marker eQTL and do not exclude a true association. Neither gene had a reported molecular credible set in the selected cortex fine-mapping file. The source is bulk cortex, not a single-cell or disease-specific tissue test.

I nevertheless ran exploratory 2-Mb regional single-signal comparisons against six public GWAS inputs, using the full matched variant patterns rather than the marker p-value alone. For rs9845387–LSAMP, baseline H0 ranged 0.267–0.684 and H4 0.003–0.049 across the six traits, indicating limited evidence under this particular model. For rs4886140–DIAPH3, baseline H4 ranged 0.018–0.045; some H3 values were high, but without a reported molecular credible set or a validated single-signal model that does not definitively establish distinct signals. The [full sensitivity results](stage3_positional_fallback_coloc_sensitivity.csv) include all H0–H4 probabilities and prior settings. These are post hoc screens, not evidence that either selected gene is the true trait gene or that the regions lack other relevant regulatory genes.

The cortex source is eQTL Catalogue r7 QTD000171, reprocessed GTEx v8, with 205 samples in metadata. Candidate positions/alleles were checked against Ensembl GRCh38 variation records, including the A/G contrast at multiallelic rs4886140; GWAS builds, allele coding, reference matching and source completeness still need independent authentication. [Regional extraction](stage3_positional_fallback_extract_receipt.json), [GWAS harmonization](stage3_positional_fallback_harmonization_receipt.json), and [model coverage](stage3_positional_fallback_coloc_coverage.json) record source and matching details. The candidate-centered windows are 2 Mb; no LD-proxy search or complete distal-enhancer mapping was performed. Other tissues, genes and variants remain plausible.

Models used p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴, p12 = 10⁻⁵, QTL prior SD 0.15 and trait-specific effect prior SDs; the sensitivity grid scales the latter by 0.5–2 and varies p12 from 10⁻⁶ to 5×10⁻⁵. Atkins FI and Jansen insomnia share UK Biobank participants and the FI has a sleep-related item; Mak and Ye also use UK Biobank. The 12 baseline calculations agree with [coloc v5.2.3](https://github.com/chr1swallace/coloc/blob/v5.2.3/R/claudia.R) reference formulas to <10⁻¹⁵ ([check](stage3_positional_fallback_coloc_formula_check.csv)). This checks arithmetic, not missing variants, signal multiplicity, tissue relevance, overlap, or causal interpretation.
