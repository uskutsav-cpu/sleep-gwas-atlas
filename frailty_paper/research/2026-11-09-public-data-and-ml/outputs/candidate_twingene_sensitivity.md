# Cohort-specific FI lookup for the 15 exploratory candidates

I scanned the entire publicly released Atkins cohort-association file already held by the project and matched the 15 selected rsIDs by rsID, GRCh37 chromosome/position and the same unordered allele pair. Each of the 15 had one source row and one such match. The full results are in `candidate_twingene_fi_cohort_lookup.csv`. This is a descriptive, post-selection follow-up, not a new validation study or a genetic-correlation analysis.

## A candidate worth treating with more caution

For **rs12536335**, counted allele G, the UK Biobank FI effect in the source file is −0.01889 (SE 0.00345, p=4.6×10⁻⁸) and the TwinGene FI effect is +0.03922 (SE 0.01454, p=0.006997), after the project’s source-backed TwinGene direction adapter described below. The approximate 95% interval for the TwinGene effect is +0.0107 to +0.0677. Both FIs were quantile-normalized in the discovery analysis, but they differ in item composition and cohort characteristics. The opposite signs are a **warning against describing the variant as a consistently signed FI signal**. They do not establish why the estimates differ, prove that the insomnia item causes the difference, or demonstrate an independently replicated shared insomnia–frailty variant. The selection and this follow-up were not prespecified independently, participant relatedness across cohorts was not verified here, and the genetic sources need further review.

Of these 15 rows, rs78206187 also had TwinGene p<0.05: counted G UKB +0.02209 (SE 0.00732), TwinGene +0.07480 (SE 0.03490, p=0.0321). The interval is wide, the threshold is unadjusted, and the marker's prior FFS p was 0.75. This is not evidence of a confirmed three-trait shared risk. The remaining 13 had TwinGene p≥0.05; this is not evidence of zero effects or lack of sharing. This small, result-selected set cannot support an unbiased genome-wide sign comparison.

A later exploratory comparison computed direct UKB–TwinGene beta differences for all 15 candidates under an independent-estimate approximation. rs12536335 and rs323509 had the two smallest unadjusted p-values; the contrasts are post-selected and depend on comparable phenotype scales and the TwinGene direction adapter. They do not establish phenotype-specific causation. See `fi_candidate_cohort_difference_sensitivity.md` and `.csv`.

## Coding and interpretive limits

The archive README labels the same EA for UK Biobank and TwinGene, but project audits found that TwinGene's raw coefficient sign conflicts with that convention: comparisons with the source's published cohort direction strings and 16,780 paired reconstruction witnesses supported `beta_TG_on_shared_EA = -BETA_TG_raw`. The output retains both the original raw beta and the project-adapter result. It then switches the sign again if the candidate counted allele is the source OA. That evidence supports this adapter but does not resolve why the original archive is inconsistent; it is not an author-confirmed correction. No effect-allele frequency was inferred from the ambiguous TwinGene frequency column. Coordinates and allele strings alone do not resolve all multiallelic representations; nine prototype rsIDs have multiple listed reference alleles.

The TwinGene cohort contributed to the original FI meta-GWAS and the candidate selection used earlier cross-trait results. The cohorts have different age distributions, sample sizes, ascertainment and FI items; the two betas are not an experiment removing the insomnia item from the same people. Selection, small-study variation and technical artifacts remain possible. The source p-values and intervals are unadjusted. No clinical genotype or outcome data were analyzed.

The project's prior TwinGene LDSC heritability-precision gate remains failed (h² Z=1.68 against ≥4), so no cohort-specific insomnia genetic correlations were fitted here. The SNP lookup does not overturn that gate. See `fi_ffs_component_crosswalk.md` and `insomnia_frailty_risk_briefing.md` for the larger FI–FFS picture.

Source: [Atkins cohort-specific summary-statistics archive](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Genome-Wide_Association_Study_of_the_Frailty_Index_-_Atkins_et_al_2021/9204998), original README and published [Atkins et al.](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/acel.13459). The lookup script is reproducible from the preserved project file and records all source and matched row counts. Source effects were not modified.
