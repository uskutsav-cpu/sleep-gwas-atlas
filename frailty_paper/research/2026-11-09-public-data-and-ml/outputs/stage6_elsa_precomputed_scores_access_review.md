# ELSA precomputed polygenic scores: access and feasibility distinction

ELSA releases several distinct genetic data products. The UK Data Service [catalogue entry for study 8773](https://datacatalogue.ukdataservice.ac.uk/studies/study/8773) identifies its 2022 precomputed PGS dataset as **safeguarded**: available to registered UK Data Service users under an end-user licence; commercial use requires approval. The page offers an account basket, not an unrestricted anonymous download. ELSA says [most UK Data Service data are free](https://www.elsa-project.ac.uk/faqs), but the exact current cost and permitted linkage of this specific PGS release to phenotype files were not verified. No login, registration, application or data download was performed.

The public [ELSA PGS methods report](https://doc.ukdataservice.ac.uk/doc/8773/mrdoc/pdf/8773_full_report_elsa_gwas_pgs_2022.pdf) lists precomputed insomnia-complaints (2017), insomnia (2019), sleep-duration, morningness and gait-speed PGSs. Its Table 18 reports 7,183 score observations for each named sleep score; this is not the number with complete frailty outcomes or a verified analysis sample. The 2017 insomnia score uses UK Biobank GWAS data; the report describes the 2019 insomnia source as a UKB–23andMe meta-analysis. Check the exact released score version, allele weights, cohort and relatedness overlap, and trait measurement before any interpretation. The report is descriptive; it does not provide the participant-level analysis of insomnia PGS against FI or FFS that this project needs.

This product might permit a future **different-score** validation of an insomnia genetic predictor against ELSA frailty outcomes if the user can meet its access conditions, linkage is permitted and no fee applies to the intended data combination. It does **not** provide the individual SNP dosages needed to calculate this project's 15- or 22-marker experimental scores. ELSA's separate [genetic-data-access page](https://www.elsa-project.ac.uk/genetic-data-access) expressly requires approval and charges for provision of GWAS or candidate-polymorphism data linked to phenotypes. Its FAQ also states a fee for genetic data linked to phenotypes; whether that statement covers the particular precomputed PGS/standard survey linkage offered through UK Data Service is not resolved by the available descriptions. Do not assume either a fee or a fee exemption for that linkage without a definitive data-service ruling.

The [2017 gait GWAS correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5559176/) corrects a gene name in a prose description; it does not identify a change to the 536 selected-variant table used in our screen. The ELSA report says its gait PGS was based on 772,690 overlapping variants, which demonstrates that its creators had broader summary statistics but does not provide a public download URL for those input weights. The report's link to GRASP was not established here as a presently working full file. The Catalog metadata says full gait summary statistics are unavailable there; this is not proof that another copy does not exist.
