# Croatian longevity cohort: public data feasibility

The [Šetinc et al. study](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0279971) makes its anonymized participant data available on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/7421684). It reports 314 older participants and longevity outcomes (age at death), with genotypes for 43 selected loci. It does not establish FI, Fried physical frailty or insomnia outcomes. Some genetic variables were dominant/recessive recodings, and missing genetic values were replaced by the study's median, so they should not automatically be treated as original per-allele dosages.

We obtained the paper's public Supplementary Table S1 and checked its 43 distinct rsIDs against both project score lists. There were **zero exact rsID matches** for either the 15- or 22-marker prototype. This is an identifier-only screen: genomic proxies and participant-level genotype contents were not checked. It does not establish absence of nearby variants or associations, but the targeted 43-variant panel offers no straightforward way to compute either score. The small and selected sample and longevity outcome would also prevent interpreting the study as frailty prediction validation. No participant records were downloaded for this screen.
