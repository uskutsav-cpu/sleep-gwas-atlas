# Naïve CD4-cell SMAD5 eQTL and insomnia/frailty: exploratory colocalization

I tested whether regional associations in the OneK1K naïve CD4 T-cell SMAD5 expression QTL resemble those in three publicly available GWAS. At one illustrative set of priors, the **frailty-index comparison favors separate signals**, while the physical-frailty and FinnGen insomnia analyses are dominated by the hypothesis of a QTL signal without a detected GWAS signal. These are provisional regional results; they do not establish causal genes, variants, or shared insomnia–frailty risk. No direct three-trait colocalization was performed.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mak frailty index | 1,944 | 53/70 | 0.0027 | 0.9964 | 0.0009 |
| Mak modified physical frailty | 1,944 | 53/70 | 0.9760 | 0.0138 | 0.0102 |
| FinnGen R13 insomnia | 1,760 | 51/70 | 0.9140 | 0.0307 | 0.0553 |

These are posterior probabilities **conditional on the model and specified priors**, not p-values. They use QTL prior effect SD 0.15, phenotype prior SD 1.1004 (FI), 0.12325 (physical frailty), or 0.20 log-odds (insomnia), and p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴, p12 = 10⁻⁵. H0/H2 are negligible here. The quantitative phenotype SDs are working estimates reused from the prior local-region analysis, and the QTL prior assumes an approximately unit-normalized expression scale; both assumptions need independent validation. Over the tested QTL effect SDs 0.075–0.30 and p12 10⁻⁶–5×10⁻⁵, H4 ranged 0.00009–0.00467 for FI, 0.00099–0.0494 for physical frailty, and 0.00577–0.231 for insomnia. [All sensitivity results](stage4_cd4_smad5_coloc_abf_sensitivity.csv).

The 70-variant QTL credible set has total reported PIP 0.9504; the variants represented after matching sum to 0.7773 for each Mak phenotype (82% of set mass) and 0.6686 for insomnia (70%). This incomplete coverage may distort the comparison. Neither a high H3 nor a low H4 excludes a shared signal, especially if key variants are missing or GWAS and molecular associations contain more than one causal signal. The public QTL credible-set file reports one SMAD5 set in its 2-Mb fitted region; this does not prove that each GWAS has only one signal, and the present analysis is limited to a 1-Mb window centered on the candidate. An ancestry-matched LD-based multi-signal analysis and improved variant coverage are needed. These selected, post hoc analyses are not discovery-level tests.

## Inputs and checks

The QTL input is [eQTL Catalogue release 7 OneK1K QTD000612](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/spot/eQTL/sumstats/QTS000038/QTD000612/), donor-level pseudobulk expression for naïve CD4 cells; region `chr5:135702653–136702653`, GRCh38. Release metadata records 949 QTL samples, whereas the source row for rs7734045 has AN 1962. These measures may refer to different sets or quantities; the discrepancy is unresolved and must not be treated as a verified sample count. The candidate's QTL imputation r² is 0.95163. Mak FI and modified physical frailty are different phenotypes from Atkins FI and Ye FFS; FinnGen is a separate insomnia input. The Mak phenotypes share a cohort, so their results are not independent. Other ancestry and donor overlaps remain unverified.

We required one-to-one rsID, chromosome, GRCh38 coordinate and allele-pair agreement; Mak positions were lifted from GRCh37 with positive-strand unique mapping. We retained non-palindromic biallelic SNPs with QTL and GWAS MAF ≥ 1%, finite beta/SE, and QTL imputation r² ≥ 0.8. These restrictions improve basic matching but omit variants, including indels and palindromic SNPs; GWAS imputation quality was not independently checked. QTL and GWAS effect alleles were aligned to ALT. The source release, regional download and harmonization details are in the [extraction receipt](stage4_catalogue_cd4_smad5_extract_receipt.json), [harmonization receipt](stage4_cd4_smad5_harmonization_receipt.json), and [coverage report](stage4_cd4_smad5_coloc_coverage.json).

The approximate-Bayes-factor calculation was independently compared on the identical filtered inputs with the `approx.bf.estimates` and `combine.abf` functions from [coloc v5.2.3](https://github.com/chr1swallace/coloc/blob/v5.2.3/R/claudia.R); all H0–H4 differences were < 2 × 10⁻¹⁶ ([check](stage4_cd4_smad5_coloc_formula_check.csv)). This validates the numerical formulas, not preprocessing, prior choice, input accuracy, the single-signal assumption, or the biological conclusion. No full coloc package pipeline or cell-type enrichment test was run.
