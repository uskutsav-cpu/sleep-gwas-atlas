# Canadian CLSA frailty GWAS: selected-results audit

[Borhan et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41514-026-00363-z) analysed an ordinal, three-category Fried-type phenotype in 23,105 European-ancestry Canadian Longitudinal Study on Aging (CLSA) participants. Its measurements include grip, a four-metre walk, physical activity, weight loss and exhaustion; its categorisation differs from the project's 0–5 FFS and the cumulative-deficit FI. Exhaustion used two CES-D questions, not the same sleep item as the index. The study adjusted for sex, batch, ten ancestry PCs and chronic-condition count; the methods state age was omitted. Discovery/target participant and relative independence has not been established.

The paper reports one genome-wide significant ordinal-frailty signal, rs147311617, and four suggestive loci in supplementary table S2. These are selected signals, so nonappearance of any project candidate is not evidence of no association. The paper states it could not report effect estimates or 95% CIs from the ordinal GWAS. Consequently the selected table is not adequate to calculate a signed, weighted candidate-score association, even before addressing marker coverage, LD and cohort independence. It also cannot test predictive discrimination or calibration. The authors did not have a replication sample for their own finding.

**Source integrity issue:** supplementary table S2 labels rs73034008 once on chromosome 12 (p = 5.9970505e-7) and again on chromosome 2 (p = 6.5112046e-7). A lookup against the [Ensembl GRCh37 variation record](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/variation/human/rs73034008?content-type=application/json) places this rsID at chromosome 12:202,574, in addition to alternate sequences. The chromosome-2 row is therefore ambiguous and should not be assigned an inferred rsID or used in an overlap test without correction from the source or full study data. This check concerns the supplementary table and does not establish which part of the row is wrong.

The ordinal-frailty lead rs147311617 maps to chromosome 12:94,666,601 (GRCh37 Ensembl primary chromosome; the paper uses GRCh38). Neither the 15-marker nor 22-marker project list contains any chromosome-12 candidate, so this lead does not directly match a project marker. The other unambiguous selected IDs also are not among the lists. These identity checks are descriptive only; they cannot rule out regional LD, non-selected candidate associations, or relevance of other variants.

The paper's MAGMA tissue analysis reported no tissue reaching statistical significance. Nearby PLXNC1 and eQTL-linked SOCS2 are candidate annotations and should not be presented as validated causal genes for the shared sleep–frailty traits. The [CLSA PheWeb availability audit](stage6_canadian_public_gwas_feasibility.md) records the separate public-summary lead and its maintenance period. If access returns and contains this exact outcome, verify actual file coverage, signed effects, alleles, model and overlap before further analysis.
