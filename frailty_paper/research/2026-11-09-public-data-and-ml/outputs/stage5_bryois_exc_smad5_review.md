# Bryois excitatory-neuron SMAD5 check

The publicly released Bryois et al. single-nucleus brain eQTL results were checked to determine whether the SMAD5 locus missing from three SingleBrain full-statistic files was measured in an underlying study. Bryois is one of the cohorts incorporated into SingleBrain, so the results are **not an independent replication of SingleBrain**. The study reports 196 genotyped donors across major brain cell types; cell-type sample counts and quality control should be checked in the paper for specific analyses.

The chromosome 5 excitatory-neuron results and the SNP-position/allele file were downloaded in full, verified against the release's published MD5 hashes, and then removed after extraction to conserve disk space. `stage5_bryois_smad5_extract.py` reproduces this process; `stage5_bryois_exc_smad5_extract_receipt.json` records the hashes. The resulting source tables contain 3,776 SMAD5 association rows and 3,776 uniquely keyed SNP-position rows. The study contains 1,621 SMAD5 association rows with positions inside the apparent SingleBrain-file gap. This demonstrates that the region was present in this component release, but it does not explain the gap in the meta-analysis release.

The original marker rs7734045 was tested for SMAD5 expression: chr5:136202653, reported effect allele C and other allele A, beta +0.093267, nominal p=0.038079. The project's exact GRCh38 allele is A>C, so the reported effect is oriented to the project ALT allele under the source's allele/coordinate convention. The alternative chr5:136207208:G>A (rs13187218) was also present (effect A, beta +0.084443, nominal p=0.058026). These are selected, unadjusted tests; neither establishes a significant independent replication, a cell-specific effect, disease causality, or a shared causal variant. The different variants are correlated and must not be counted as independent experiments.

Another allele, rs7730723 (chr5:136159634:A>G), has reported ALT beta +0.090554 and nominal p=0.043501 in Bryois. The SingleBrain meta-analysis also reports positive ALT beta for this allele, but the meta-analysis includes Bryois: direction agreement is not independent validation. Source record and project queue matching used GRCh38 coordinate and the unordered pair of alleles, with effect orientation recorded separately. There are 127 exact allele-pair matches to the project queue, 107 non-palindromic; see `stage5_bryois_exc_smad5_queue_matches.csv` and `stage5_bryois_smad5_compare.py`. Palindromic variants require particular strand care.

The Bryois release supplies nominal p values, effect estimates, alleles, positions and MAF, but no standard error in the examined association file. It cannot simply be combined with the SingleBrain meta-analysis or used as though it supplies independent evidence. A reliable multi-signal disease colocalization still requires appropriate full statistics, variant coverage, LD and a documented approach to cohort overlap and uncertainty. Source: [Bryois et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-022-01128-z), [summary-statistics release](https://zenodo.org/records/7276971), and [SingleBrain study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-026-02541-x).
