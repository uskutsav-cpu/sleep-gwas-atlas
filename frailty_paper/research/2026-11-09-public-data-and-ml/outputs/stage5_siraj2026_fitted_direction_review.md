# Reporter fitted-effect direction and AlphaGenome comparison

The published [Siraj et al. (2026) methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10121-6) define their fitted allelic contrast as alternative versus reference, comparing reporter RNA relative to plasmid DNA using a DESeq2 design. Their [public preprocessing code](https://github.com/julirsch/finemapped_mpra/blob/main/code/utils.R) retains the `Log2Skew` result column as the reported allelic effect. This provides a source-grounded basis to interpret the selected `Log2Skew` values in the analysis as ALT relative to REF, after the project's GRCh37-to-GRCh38 allele checks. The aggregate count ratio used in earlier reviews is a different descriptive calculation; its disagreement with the fitted effect in nine groups does not show that the source fitted-effect direction was reversed.

`stage5_siraj2026_glm_direction_screen.csv` records the fitted reporter effect, its study FDR field, whether it passes the authors' active-and-skew rules, the matched-label AlphaGenome DNase ALT-minus-REF score, and the descriptive sign relationship. The 284 groups cover 42 unique variants, but group rows repeat related loci, cell types, source subsets and library measurements. Across all groups, 138 have the same sign and 146 opposite signs; this is **not an accuracy estimate or independent sample count**. Many source groups are not classified as expression-modulating and their fitted signs are not significant evidence of an allelic reporter effect. Among 17 selected active-and-skew result groups, 14 have the same sign and three opposite; those groups represent only five variants and are not independent trials.

Both ALT orientations are aligned to the project's checked alleles, but the measured RNA reporter and the model's DNase score measure different molecular features and are not expected to have identical effects in all cases. SK-N-SH and the other cell lines are not normal brain or primary tissue. The model's same-name cell track need not represent the reporter samples or conditions. Selection used measured activity, model and study training or genomic-region overlap remains unverified, and genomic LD and shared sources preclude treating groups as independent. This comparison does not establish model accuracy, gene mediation, disease colocalization, or a shared insomnia–frailty mechanism.

Reproduce with `python3 outputs/stage5_siraj_glm_direction_screen.py` after rebuilding the all-cell count table as described in `stage5_siraj2026_allcell_orientation_review.md`. The count-ratio and fitted-effect signs remain separately visible; missing or zero effects remain distinct from same/opposite categories.
