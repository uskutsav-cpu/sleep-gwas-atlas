# CD83-region marker rs12524473: regulatory-element evidence

The exploratory marker rs12524473 (chr6:14105537 A>G, GRCh38) overlaps ENCODE candidate distal enhancer EH38E2447954 (chr6:14105480–14105790; zero-based, half-open). SCREEN lists CD83 as the nearest gene, with a displayed 11,621 bp distance to its TSS. Proximity does not prove this element regulates CD83.

[SCREEN's functional-characterization page](https://screen.wenglab.org/GRCh38/ccre/EH38E2447954/functional-characterization) lists seven solo-fragment STARR-seq records. The element shows positive log₂ fold changes of 2.02 in A549, 1.95 in SH-SY5Y, and 1.56 in MCF-7, with p-value and FDR each displayed as 0.00; those are rounded displays, not exact zeros. HepG2 has nominal p = 0.02 but displayed FDR 0.82. The remaining K562 and HCT116 records have higher displayed p-values. All rows and experiment identifiers appear in the [reporter table](stage5_cd83_ccre_reporter_results.csv).

These are element-level assays in cell lines, not direct A-versus-G experiments, and SH-SY5Y is a neuroblastoma line rather than a primary sleep-related neuron. The evidence supports activity of the tested element in those assay contexts but does not establish a variant effect or a CD83-mediated insomnia/frailty mechanism. Independence of these assays from AlphaGenome training remains unverified.

For the exact allele, [SCREEN](https://screen.wenglab.org/GRCh38/ccre/EH38E2447954/genes) reports a GTEx v8 CD83 eQTL in skeletal muscle (slope -0.315, p = 1.69 × 10⁻¹⁰). The existing project table records a GTEx v10 ALT-oriented normalized effect of -0.3013, p = 1.35 × 10⁻¹³ in skeletal muscle; these releases overlap and should not be counted as independent replications. SCREEN also displays a different variant in the same element; it is not the exact marker. Full-precision tissue-matched AlphaGenome predictions, a formal disease–QTL colocalization assessment and allele-specific functional validation remain necessary.
