# Mugello nonagenarian sleep–frailty genetics: feasibility

The [Mugello CLOCK study](https://flore.unifi.it/bitstream/2158/1148931/1/CLOCK.pdf) reports FI-34, sleep-related measures and genotyping of three CLOCK-gene variants (rs1801260, rs11932595, rs4580704) in an older nonagenarian cohort. These three IDs do not occur in either of our prototype lists. This targeted assay, even if participant-level linkage were permitted, does not by itself support calculation of either 15- or 22-marker score. Genotypes for any other project markers are not established; no claim is made about proxies or additional arrays. The table's age-90-plus sample is highly selected and should not be assumed to generalize to younger cohorts.

A [separate Mugello sleep study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36967638/) reports 178 wearable-sleep participants, not necessarily the intersection with the targeted genotyping or full FI. A [later Mugello paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8918304/) says its clinical data were available by request rather than public download; this does not automatically determine access rules for all other datasets. Whether linked genotypes and FI/sleep data exist, individual coverage, study overlap, permission and cost are unknown. This is a background and biological hypothesis source, not a verified no-fee public score-validation dataset. No participant data were obtained.
