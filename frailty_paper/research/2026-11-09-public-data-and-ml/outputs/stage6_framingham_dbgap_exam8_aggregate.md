Checked 29 October 2026. The [exam 8 dataset page](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/dataset.cgi?pht=747&study_id=phs000007.v20.p8) describes `ex1_8s`, `pht000747.v4.p8`, with one row per person. Its linked [public variable dictionary](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000007/phs000007.v20.p8/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000007.v20.pht000747.v4.ex1_8s.data_dict.xml) and [aggregate variable report](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000007/phs000007.v20.p8/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000007.v20.pht000747.v4.p8.ex1_8s.var_report.xml) provide the following counts, extracted reproducibly in `work/framingham_dbgap_exam8_aggregate.py` and [CSV](stage6_framingham_exam8_dbgap_aggregate.csv).

This is a **superseded 2013 metadata release**, not a current eligible sample. The report counts 3,001 Offspring records. There are 2,919, 2,922 and 2,922 nonmissing responses for trouble falling asleep (H730), nighttime waking (H731) and early waking (H732), respectively. For ever being told of insomnia (H745), there are 2,881 nonmissing responses, of whom 109 answered yes. Those are separate marginal counts: they do not reveal their overlap, age, consent eligibility, genotypes, later follow-up or event rates in the intended analytic sample. In particular, the 109 are not a power calculation or the number available for validation. A symptom outcome could have more observations than reported diagnosis, but its threshold and clinical meaning must be specified before examining results.

The dbGaP dictionary confirms `H745` uses `0=no`, `1=yes`, and `.=unknown` in this release; the paper questionnaire's `9=don't know` has therefore been recoded as missing in that dictionary. Check the actual release rather than applying questionnaire codes blindly. H401 distinguishes intentional and unintentional weight loss; H605 is first right-grip trial in kilograms and H617 is first walk time in seconds. These are ingredients, not a reconstructed five-component Fried phenotype.

The later public [v31.p12 variable list](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/GetListOfAllObjects.cgi?object_type=variable&study_id=phs000007.v31.p12) lists H730–H732 and H745 in `pht000747.v6.p12` with accessions `phv00072560.v5.p12` through `phv00072562.v5.p12`, and `phv00072575.v5.p12`. It also contains separate same-named fields in another dataset (`pht004815`), so variable names alone are insufficient for a cross-file join. v31 is also superseded; verify the actual current release, source dataset, eligibility and coding before requesting or using data.

No participant-level data were accessed. Linked genotypes, consent, sample or relative overlap, frailty components, attrition and prediction performance remain unverified. The resource is a controlled-access no-data-service-fee lead, not an openly downloadable validation cohort. See [study feasibility](framingham_no_fee_validation_route.md) and [exam 8–9 crosswalk](stage6_framingham_exam8_exam9_variable_crosswalk.md).

## Current public release check

On 29 October 2026, the latest version listed by dbGaP was `phs000007.v35.p16`. Its [exam 8 report](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000007/phs000007.v35.p16/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000007.v35.pht000747.v10.p16.ex1_8s.var_report.xml), generated in July 2025, lists 2,976 Offspring records. H730 has 2,894 nonmissing responses, H731 and H732 each 2,897, and H745 2,856, including 107 yes. These replace the v20 counts as the better current public snapshot; reasons for changing counts are not established.

The same release lists [exam 9 / Omni 1 exam 4 dataset `pht005140.v6.p16`](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/dataset.cgi?pht=5140&study_id=phs000007.v35.p16), whose [public dictionary](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000007/phs000007.v35.p16/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000007.v35.pht005140.v6.e_exam_ex09_1b_0844s.data_dict.xml) confirms J765–J770 grip trials, J785/J790 walk times, and J944 insomnia diagnosis. Its [variable report](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000007/phs000007.v35.p16/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000007.v35.pht005140.v6.p16.e_exam_ex09_1b_0844s.var_report.xml) reports 2,678 records in this combined cohort dataset, 2,587 nonmissing first right-grip observations and 2,518 first-walk times. These counts include both cohorts and are marginal: they do not establish repeated measurements in the same people, score-variant coverage, eligible ages, genetic linkage, or outcome completeness. Missing functional tests may reflect physical limitations and cannot automatically be treated as healthy or discarded without considering the published frailty protocol.

The [current-release aggregate extract](stage6_framingham_exam89_dbgap_v35_aggregate.csv) is generated by `work/framingham_dbgap_exam89_v35_aggregate.py`. A prior Framingham [methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6909900/) describes 4-meter walks at usual speed, three grip trials per hand, and averaging trials for its functional analysis. These methods clarify the measured variables, but do not prove the exact cutoffs or missing-test handling used by a different Fried frailty analysis.
