# Public brain-cell prediction models: feasibility check

The [PsychAD single-nucleus transcriptome-wide association study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10836-6) released ancestry- and cell-type-specific expression-prediction models and SNP covariance matrices on [Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/17435917). These are trained statistical gene-expression models, not AlphaGenome sequence predictions. They could support hypothesis-generating transcriptome-wide association analyses with appropriate GWAS harmonization and matched model covariance; they do not by themselves establish causal genes or clinical predictions.

To test feasibility without downloading the 10.3 GB model archive, two European-ancestry brain vascular cell-class model databases were retrieved as individual ZIP members by byte range: endothelial and mural. `stage6_psychad_extract_small_models.py` verifies each extracted member's ZIP CRC and records its SHA-256; the archive as a whole was not downloaded or checksum-verified. The two databases report 779 training samples each. Individual donor overlap with other molecular or disease datasets has not been established. The model files are under `work/`, and the retrieval receipt is `stage6_psychad_small_models_receipt.json`.

The study defines an imputable gene using cross-validated R² ≥ 0.01, FDR-adjusted prediction p ≤ 0.05 and at least one SNP in the model. Applying those thresholds to database-reported fields for the 97 unique gene IDs present in the project's molecular-evidence atlas, the endothelial database contains 9 candidate gene entries and none passes; the mural database contains 22 entries and two pass: RBM6 and ENSG00000286863. Counts do not imply that the other genes have no biological role; they are absent or do not meet this model-performance filter in these two cell classes. `stage6_psychad_model_qc.py` recreates the full table, `stage6_psychad_priority_gene_model_qc.csv`.

SMAD5 is present in both tables but fails the thresholds in both; FOXP2 is absent from endothelial and fails in mural; INKA1 is absent from both. These results do not assess excitatory or inhibitory neurons, other brain cells, other ancestries or other prediction models. The two passing mural models are eligibility results, not associations with insomnia or frailty. They were drawn from a broad, already-selected gene list; proximity, passing model quality, and cross-validated expression prediction do not identify a disease effector gene. This QC is not phenotype validation or proof that the model was trained on an independent cohort. A subsequent exploratory RBM6 TWAS is reported separately in `stage6_psychad_rbm6_twas_review.md`.

A future analysis can retrieve the corresponding model and covariance members, prespecify eligible cells and genes, harmonize full GWAS summary statistics to model alleles and ancestry, account for multiple testing and compare against colocalization and other molecular evidence. The phenotype cohort overlap and the reported limitations of TWAS effect sizes also need explicit assessment. Neuronal model files are much larger and have not been downloaded or tested in this feasibility screen.
