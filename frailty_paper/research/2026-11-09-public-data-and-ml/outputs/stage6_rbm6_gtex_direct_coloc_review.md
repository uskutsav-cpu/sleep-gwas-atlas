# RBM6 cortex expression and frailty: expanded-coverage follow-up

## What the public data show

After recovering rs10049477 and additional variants, a 1,006-SNP GTEx bulk-cortex RBM6 eQTL–Mak frailty-index (FI) analysis still favors a shared signal for one pair of modeled components. At p1=p2=0.0001 and p12=0.00001, PP.H4=0.948 and PP.H3=0.052; PP.H4 ranges from 0.647 to 0.989 as p12 varies from 0.000001 to 0.00005. Two other FI component pairs favor distinct signals. This is a conditional, postselected, pairwise model result—not proof of a causal gene or an independent test of the project's score, and not a joint insomnia–FI–expression analysis.

For the Ye Fried frailty score (FFS), the modeled cortex expression–FFS pair gives PP.H4=0.0039 and PP.H3=0.996 at the same illustrative prior, with PP.H4 0.00039–0.019 across the three tested p12 values. This is evidence about this particular pair, locus, and model; it does not prove that FI and FFS have no shared biology or that one is clinically superior. The FFS regional source did not provide allele frequencies in the cached aligned data, so the FFS/reference frequency match could not be checked. Allele-pair and build matching were performed.

The expanded model contains 44 of the 49 unique variants in the eQTL Catalogue's published cortex RBM6 credible set; their published PIPs sum to 0.917 of 0.953 within that set. That includes rs10049477, the published set's largest-PIP variant (0.503), which the earlier five-study intersection omitted. In the new fit its cortex PIP is 0.210; its FI PIP is 0.042 and FFS PIP 0.0097. These values come from different fine-mapping runs and are not probabilities of gene causality. Published and local PIPs differ because the input set and model/reference choices differ. Five published variants remain absent, including an indel excluded from our non-palindromic SNP analysis.

The earlier 947-variant model gave cortex–FI PP.H4=0.938 for one selected pair at the illustrative prior, but it excluded the published leading expression variant. The new calculation uses a different variant set and signal composition; its similar posterior is a sensitivity observation, not an independent replication or proof that omitted variants have no effect. The insomnia result has not been recomputed on this new support because this FI/FFS intersection does not contain matched insomnia statistics. The two prior UK Biobank-based sleep and FI analyses have overlapping samples and FI includes an insomnia item.

## Methods and limits

The GTEx v8 eQTL Catalogue bulk brain cortex source has 205 samples. The available FI and FFS regional data were matched to the RBM6 cortex QTL by GRCh38 position and exact non-palindromic SNP allele pair, converted to GRCh37 for gnomAD reference matching, and restricted to variants present in both frailty datasets. The resulting input has 1,006 variants, including rs10049477; one common candidate failed the NFE reference match. Source reference/alternate allele orientation was checked and QTL ALT and frailty ALT betas aligned. Absolute allele-frequency differences versus gnomAD NFE were at most 0.0618 for cortex and 0.0491 for FI, using a maximum allowed difference of 0.20; FFS frequency was unavailable. These checks do not prove that LD or imputation matches the study samples.

SuSiE-RSS used gnomAD NFE signed LD, 95% credible sets, L=10, fixed residual variance and working N=205, 402,910 and 386,565 for cortex, FI and FFS respectively. Variant-specific N can vary. All fits converged, yielding one, three and one credible sets. The local LD matrix was symmetric with unit diagonal and minimum eigenvalue 1.20e−5. Reference LD is external, and the custom Hail index decoder has not been independently verified with Hail. The window, variants and priors were selected through prior work; cross-signal and prior comparisons do not establish independent validation. Bulk cortex cannot identify the relevant cell type; donor overlap and the relationship to independent functional assays have not been established.

Reproduce from `stage6_rbm6_gtex_direct_prepare.py`, `_nfe_index.py`, `_align.py`, `_ld.py` and `_coloc.R`; the aligned inputs, diagnostic, credible-set members, coloc table and receipts share the `stage6_rbm6_gtex_direct_` prefix. The LD extraction downloads the specified public gnomAD blocks, hashes the retrieved blocks, and does not verify the complete remote reference matrix. The published QTL credible-set coverage receipt documents duplicate rsID aliases and local hashes, without a publisher checksum comparison. Source and caveat details are in [the earlier cortex coverage review](stage6_rbm6_gtex_cortex_coloc_review.md).

A stronger next step is to build a separate QTL–insomnia input with broader source support and appropriate ancestry-matched or in-sample LD, and evaluate molecular signals in well-characterized cell types. Independent validation of the actual 15-/22-marker scores still needs participant-level genotype and outcome measurements in a non-overlapping cohort; neither these summary-statistic results nor the AlphaGenome predictions establish performance in people.
