# RBM6 immune-cell colocalization: related phenotypes, not sleep–frailty validation

Two overlapping 500-kb CASCADE portal windows centered on rs2353586 and rs2282749 returned 582 and 559 selected rows respectively; 685 exact distinct rows remain after combining the responses. Fifteen are RBM6 expression-QTL/FinnGen GWAS signal-pair results, across six phenotype codes. Four concern the FinnGen `PAIN` code, four `M13_DORSALGIA` (dorsalgia), four other arthrosis, and one each arthrosis, a codeine/tramadol prescription endpoint, and an unresolved numeric code. These rows are neither independent cell-type replications nor independent disease cohorts: broad and nested cell populations, phenotype definitions and genomic signals can overlap. See `stage6_rbm6_cascade_selected_coloc.csv`.

For illustration, the returned RBM6 broad-CD4 eQTL and FinnGen PAIN signal pair has PP.H4=0.884; the corresponding dorsalgia pair has PP.H4=0.873. The [CASCADE methods](https://cascade.finngen.fi/about) describe SuSiE-coloc for pairs of fine-mapped molecular and R12 disease signals. PP.H4 is a model-conditional posterior for the signal pair under its assumptions, not the probability that RBM6 or either selected marker causes pain. The portal's lead variants for these two examples differ from the two markers used to define the windows, so the results cannot be assigned to rs2353586 or rs2282749 without further credible-set and allele-level evidence. They are immune-cell eQTL results, not excitatory-neuron or brain mural-cell colocalizations.

No `trait1` code in the 685 displayed rows literally contained `insomn`, `sleep` or `frail`. The endpoint returns only browser-selected results, and numeric or other coded labels are not all resolved. The absence of a literal code is **not** a negative sleep/frailty result and does not establish that those phenotypes were tested. PAIN includes overlapping pain phenotypes in FinnGen, so it is not independent of dorsalgia; neither endpoint is an insomnia or frailty GWAS. FinnGen immune and phenotype participants can overlap, and the QTL and disease allele conventions and causal effects have not been independently re-estimated. These results motivate a pain–sleep–frailty hypothesis but do not prove one.

`stage6_rbm6_cascade_coloc_inventory.py` reproduces the request, exact-record deduplication and RBM6 filter; source URLs, response hashes and counts are in `stage6_rbm6_cascade_coloc_receipt.json`. Individual source responses are archived as `stage6_cascade_49758613-50258613.json` and `stage6_cascade_49963404-50463404.json`. Regional boundary conventions are not independently verified; these are broad contextual windows. Because source results are filtered and the project has not inspected matched full statistics, LD, prior choices or participant overlap for these pairs, treat them as evidence reported by the resource, not an independently validated colocalization analysis. Source context: [CASCADE study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11078-2), [methods](https://cascade.finngen.fi/about), and [FinnGen dorsalgia endpoint](https://cascade.finngen.fi/phenotype/M13_DORSALGIA).
