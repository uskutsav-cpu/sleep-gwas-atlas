# TenK10K immune multiome: SMAD5 molecular colocalization

The public [TenK10K multiome release](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anglixue/TenK10K_multiome) includes colocalization results between immune-cell chromatin accessibility QTLs and expression QTLs. Its dataset card warns of allele-flipping issues in older caQTL–eQTL files. We used the full `coloc_results_caQTL2eQTL.zip` at repository revision `a8af2b48c3e9c3a9daac9fb8d5396b377fdb929b`; its local SHA-256 `0a2f7afb74b27150f883018389b204aff93e1f1443adfcd6091d301987dd1c5f` matches the repository LFS object hash. The 26 June upload replaced an earlier full-file object; the 27 June correction notice calls for the updated version. Repository history confirms that the small filtered ZIP was unchanged, so we did not use it. Although this evidence supports using the later full file, the exact time relationship to the correction notice is not explicitly documented; its corrected status remains provisional.

Among 133,163 chr5 rows across the archive's cell-type files, **73 report SMAD5** (`ENSG00000113658`) and 10 have H4 ≥ 0.8 under the source's model. These are source-reported *molecular–molecular* colocalizations, not independent insomnia or frailty GWAS colocalizations. For CD4 naïve cells, peak `chr5:136132117-136134284` and SMAD5 expression have H4 = 0.9077 and H3 = 0.0923 across 4,143 variants; the reported top SNP is `5:136131620:C:G`. This peak overlaps the previously observed [CASCADE naïve-CD4 caQTL peak](stage5_cascade_smad5_priority_review.md), `chr5:136132565-136133590`. Both datasets document GRCh38, and the peak intervals overlap; different peak-calling boundaries prevent treating them as identical elements. The project candidate rs7734045 is ~68 kb away and is not the reported top SNP; this result does not establish its molecular effect or causal status. Neither H4 nor a shared peak establishes the direction of effect, a disease-gene mechanism or the link from insomnia to frailty.

The TenK10K and OneK1K resources overlap in participants, so this is not a fully independent molecular replication of prior OneK1K evidence. CASCADE uses a separately measured Finnish resource, but independence of its donors from FinnGen disease GWAS is not guaranteed. Different ancestry, cell labels, peak boundaries, source QC and underlying models matter. The TenK10K H4 value depends on model assumptions, priors and the set of variants tested; it does not by itself distinguish multiple signals or validate the project scores. The 10 H4 ≥0.8 rows are post hoc, share genes and peaks, and must not be counted as ten independent confirmations.

The [complete SMAD5 row extraction](stage6_tenk10k_smad5_molecular_coloc.csv) and `python3 work/tenk10k_smad5_extract.py` preserve all 73 chr5 source rows, including low-H4 results, with source cell-type paths. Full disease-QTL colocalization requires independent sleep and frailty summary statistics, harmonized molecular variant effects and suitable LD, with source version and overlap checked. [TenK10K study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11173-4); [FinnGen CASCADE access and terms](https://cascade.finngen.fi/data/).

The source’s reported top SNP `5:136131620:C:G` maps uniquely on the positive strand to GRCh37 position 5:135467309, corresponding to rs9327743 in the 1000 Genomes phase 3 VCF. Its genotype correlation with candidate rs7734045 is r² = 0.983 in 503 EUR and 0.981 in 504 pooled EAS reference individuals. See the [reference-LD receipt](stage6_tenk10k_smad5_reference_ld.json) and `python3 work/tenk10k_smad5_ld_check.py`. This is strong reference linkage, not a demonstration that the candidate and source top variant have the same effect in TenK10K or disease cohorts. The top SNP is C/G palindromic, so study strand and effect alleles require verification before assigning an effect direction. The remote VCF and index were not independently checksum-verified.

An [exact variant-level caQTL lookup](stage6_tenk10k_smad5_variant_caqtl_review.md) now finds rs7734045 ALT C associated with this peak in CD4-naïve cells. The result remains molecular and does not by itself establish an insomnia or frailty mechanism.
