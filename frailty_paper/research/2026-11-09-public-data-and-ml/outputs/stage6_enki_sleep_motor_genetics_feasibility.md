# Enhanced NKI-Rockland Sample: sleep and motor phenotype lead

The enhanced Nathan Kline Institute–Rockland Sample (eNKI-RS) is a community-based lifespan neuroimaging study. A [published analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12796161/) used sleep-quality, depressive-symptom and motor measures, including grip strength, in the same study and compared younger and mid-to-older samples. Its older eNKI subgroup was too small for stable canonical-correlation interpretation in that analysis. That finding concerns the authors' multivariate model, not a direct estimate of power for our proposed scores. Sleep quality is not a clinical insomnia diagnosis, and grip strength alone is not an FI or Fried frailty phenotype.

The [original study description](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3472598/) planned genetic sample collection, and the [study site](https://rocklandsample.org/science) describes samples being processed through the NIMH Genetics Repository. These statements do **not** demonstrate a currently downloadable genome-wide genotype dataset, candidate SNP coverage or a joinable set of older adults with insomnia and frailty outcomes. The site's [phenotypic access page](https://rocklandsample.org/phenotypic-data) says full phenotypic data follow a data-use agreement; an [older access page](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/enhanced/access.html) says it has not been maintained since July 2025. Neither confirms present genomic file access, conditions or project-specific costs.

The older access page links to instructions for ordering genetic **samples**, rather than identifying a variant-call dataset; that target returned HTTP 404 when checked. A broken historical link does not establish that the repository or samples are unavailable, but ordering biospecimens would not by itself supply genotype calls or establish a no-cost analysis route.

This lead may eventually support a targeted sleep–grip association study if genotype release and linkage are verified, but it is **not established as a free, fully public, independent validation cohort** for either score. Before use, establish current genetic dataset accession and contents, consent and linkage to the phenotype release, complete frailty components, outcome counts among older participants, candidate allele quality and coverage, independence from all selection cohorts, and fees or computational costs. No account, request, participant download or validation was performed. The MIVAC study is a related but distinct extension, already documented in [its separate assessment](stage6_mivac_sleep_grip_genetics_feasibility.md); do not add participant counts across them without a linkage audit.
