# Reference-panel screen of nearby array markers

This is a feasibility screen for eventual NHATS access, not an evaluation of NHATS genotypes or of either genetic score. The actual NHATS array revision, sample ancestry and variant-level imputation quality remain unverified. No NHATS participant data were used.

We compared the generic Illumina GSA-24v3-0_A1 GRCh37 manifest with the 503-person European 1000 Genomes reference distributed for MAGMA. For each candidate, reference-panel markers within 100 kb had to match a manifest assay by coordinate and inferred genomic-plus allele pair. The score variant also had to have exactly one coordinate-and-allele match in the reference. We calculated squared Pearson correlation of unphased dosages using at least 400 paired calls, and reported the best matching marker. A reference-panel correlation of 0.8 was used as a descriptive threshold, not an imputation-quality threshold.

All 33 distinct candidates had a resolvable reference anchor and at least one eligible nearby marker. Twelve had a best reference correlation of at least 0.8: 7 of the 15-marker list and 6 of the 22-marker list, with overlapping variants counted in both lists. For rs12536335, the best nearby marker had r² = 0.719; for rs113851554, the best had r² = 0.153. These latter values describe this limited panel, array manifest, and 100-kb search only; they do not demonstrate failed imputation.

Correlations here do not establish allele direction, phenotype association, causal genes, imputation INFO, or score validity. A marker can be accurately imputed from multiple markers and a larger reference panel even when no single nearby array marker is strongly correlated. Conversely, strong reference LD does not establish good imputation in NHATS. The European reference may not represent NHATS participants. The generic manifest differs in assay count from the NHATS dataset description, so real array coverage must be checked from actual cohort metadata. The analysis also excludes non-SNP and ambiguous-strand manifest records and relies on the listed GRCh37 coordinates and inferred strand pairs.

Next, if linked NHATS access becomes available, inspect its actual genotype manifest and imputation INFO, marker allele coding and per-ancestry frequencies, then assess score coverage and validate against prespecified outcomes without substituting these tags for score variants. See [Illumina product files](https://support.illumina.com/downloads/infinium-global-screening-array-v3-0-product-files.html) and [NHATS genetic dataset](https://dss.niagads.org/datasets/ng00134/).
