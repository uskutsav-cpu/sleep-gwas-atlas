# Expanded reference-LD sensitivity for the TenK10K peak and frailty index

This is exploratory molecular follow-up, not independent validation of an insomnia–frailty risk score. The TenK10K naive-CD4 accessible-chromatin peak was selected after reviewing molecular associations, so these results are post-selection and do not establish a disease-associated gene or cell type.

We expanded the 1000 Genomes European reference from 1,743 to 3,754 allele-matched SNPs for the Mak frailty-index (FI) comparison (503 reference individuals; at most rank 502). The expanded set contains 3,754 of 3,877 previously frequency-screened SNPs and includes the molecular lead absent from the earlier LD subset. Using the existing approximate single-signal ABF calculations, the set covers 93.6% of the molecular support and 88.1% of FI support. These percentages refer to posterior support under that calculation, not a guarantee that all causal signals are included. The matching uses genomic position and alleles, with reference versus molecular ALT-frequency differences required to be at most 0.1.

With `susieR` 0.14.2, both summary-statistic models converged, each producing one credible set. For `coloc` 5.2.3 at p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-5, the pairwise posterior was H3=0.99940 and H4=0.00049. H4 ranged from 0.000049 to 0.00243 as p12 varied from 1e-6 to 5e-5. H3 describes two distinct signals under the model, while H4 describes a shared signal; these are conditional model probabilities, not proof that the signals are different. The earlier smaller-panel sensitivity also favored H3.

**The increased LD mismatch is a material limitation.** The `estimate_s_rss` diagnostics were 0.0316 for the peak and 0.0218 for FI, larger than approximately 0.0081 and 0.0051 with the smaller subset. The 3,754-by-3,754 matrix is necessarily rank deficient because it comes from 503 people, and tiny negative eigenvalues (minimum -1.74e-6) were set to zero by the diagnostic. The QTL sample size is approximately 921 inferred from molecular summary statistics; FI N=403,041 is the study-level approximation, not verified per variant. Ancestry and imputation differences, sample overlap, post-selection, missing variants and conditional model assumptions remain concerns. A larger ancestry-matched LD resource and source-verified sample sizes are needed before drawing a firm locus conclusion.

For planning, the expanded matrix covers 3,754 of 3,877 frequency-screened physical-frailty variants and 3,721 of 3,879 insomnia variants, corresponding to approximately 97.1% and 95.7% of their respective single-signal ABF support. Three insomnia variants were excluded because molecular and reference ALT frequencies differed by more than 0.1; one had a difference of about 0.51. These are coverage checks only: expanded multi-signal models for physical frailty and insomnia were **not** run, and Finnish-specific LD is not established here.
