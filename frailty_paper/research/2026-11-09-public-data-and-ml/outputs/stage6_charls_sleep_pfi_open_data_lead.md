# Public sleep and psychological frailty dataset: CHARLS study lead

Checked 28 October 2026. [Ouyang and colleagues' Sleep-PFI CHARLS repository, version 2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/76zz3d74r6/2), describes a CC BY 4.0 release of processed analytical data, code, and supplementary documentation for 9,476 CHARLS participants aged at least 45. The described variables include baseline nighttime and nap duration and a 15-item psychological frailty index measured at follow-up. We downloaded the R data file and verified its SHA-256 against the repository metadata (`97afcc8845f543d5fcc0c2d617949099e3da6812009cec1bb2626a501e61689a`). Its object `g` has 9,476 rows and 29 columns, including `PFI`, `night`, `snap`, `total_sleep`, age and covariates; it has no genotype or genetic-score columns. The author script and supplement were also inspected for the separate [published-result reproduction](stage6_charls_pfi_public_reproduction_review.md). We have not established that any genetic linkage is permitted. The dataset is a phenotype-analysis lead, not a candidate-score validation cohort.

The associated [Ouyang et al. 2026 paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42873651/) reports baseline short sleep under six hours versus 6–8 hours associated with 2018 psychological frailty (adjusted odds ratio 1.27, 95% CI 1.08–1.51) and persistent short sleep associated with psychological frailty (1.44, 1.15–1.81). These are the authors' observational findings; our separate reproduction matches their principal short-sleep estimate and reported sensitivity, but they do not establish causation. Sleep duration is not equivalent to insomnia, and psychological frailty is not interchangeable with the cumulative-deficit FI or the Fried physical-frailty score in this project. A direct comparison of their odds ratios with genetic score weights would be misleading. The item list and overlap between sleep measures and the outcome require inspection before any new analysis.

For genetic validation, the main CHARLS genetic-access route is separate and controlled, as documented in [the existing access review](stage6_charls_genetic_access_2026.md). Do not attempt to link the public processed file to any restricted genetic records without verified permission and allowed identifiers. Even with approved linkage, adequate candidate variant coverage, phenotype alignment, overlap with discovery samples and independence remain to be checked.

This source can improve the mentor background and inform the choice of phenotype in a future study, but provides no demonstrated held-out validation for the 15- or 22-marker prototypes. The separate reproduction also compares adjustment strategies and sample selection, while keeping the phenotypic exercise separate from genetic prediction. The downloaded file is cached as `work/sleep_pfi/data.rda`.
