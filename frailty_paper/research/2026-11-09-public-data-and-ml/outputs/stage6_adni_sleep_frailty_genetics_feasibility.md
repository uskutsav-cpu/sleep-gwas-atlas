# ADNI: potential no-fee, application-based score-validation lead

The Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) is a longitudinal cohort with clinical, imaging and genetic data. [NITRC](https://www.nitrc.org/projects/adni) describes SNP-genotyping data as freely available to authorized investigators. The [ADNI access instructions](https://support.adni4.org/hc/en-us/articles/31090030057236-I-am-a-researcher-how-do-I-access-ADNI-research-data-or-samples) require a data-use agreement and application; they do not establish an unrestricted public download. This project has not applied or accessed participant records. Whether there are fees for the specific data or infrastructure, whether the team qualifies, and applicable data restrictions must be confirmed.

A published [ADNI frailty-index comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9886733/) describes three indices: a 93-item standard FI, a 26-item data-driven FI, and a previously published 40-item FI, in development and validation samples of 819 and 815 respectively. Its table marks **self-reported insomnia as a component of the 93- and 40-item indices**, but not the 26-item index. The 26-item index also includes NPI sleep disturbance, self-reported drowsiness, and low energy. The 93-item index contains all four items; the 40-item index includes insomnia, drowsiness and low energy but not the NPI sleep item. **None of these three published indices is free of explicitly named sleep or fatigue-related items.** Shared item content can inflate an observed sleep–frailty relationship. An index reconstructed after removing these items would be a new adapted measure requiring separate validation; the exact mapping between NPI/NPI-Q across ADNI phases must also be checked. The [reproducible item audit](stage6_adni_fi_sleep_item_audit.json) checks all 26 refined-index rows.

A separate [sleep and biological-aging study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13370888/) describes ADNI's NPI-Q informant-reported sleep question combining night awakening, early rising and excessive daytime naps. This is **not a direct clinical insomnia diagnosis**. No exact intersection of sleep, frailty and genome-wide genotypes, usable outcome events, score-variant coverage or sample/relative independence has been verified. ADNI's cognitive-impairment-focused ascertainment limits generalization. Its FI- and sleep-study sample counts must not be added or assumed to describe linked genetic subsets.

ADNI may be an instructive future no-fee validation lead if its actual terms permit use and the required data are linked, but it does not satisfy the current unrestricted-public-data constraint. No score was computed. The FI item audit used the public Europe PMC full-text XML at `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC9886733/fullTextXML`; the sleep definition used `https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC13370888/fullTextXML`.

## Candidate 11-item alternative

[Chi et al.](https://doi.org/10.3233/JAD-231111) report a modified 11-item frailty index and NPI/NPI-Q assessments in 2,155 ADNI participants. The [author manuscript](https://adni.loni.usc.edu/adni-publications/Chi_2024_Associations%20of%20Frailty%20with%20Neuropsy.pdf) lists functional dependence and 10 comorbidity/clinical-deficit items; none is explicitly named sleep, insomnia, drowsiness or fatigue. It is comorbidity-dominant and does not include exhaustion, slowness or reduced activity, so it is distinct from the project's FI and FFS. No claim of complete construct independence follows: its items can still relate to sleep, and ascertainment and common causes can induce associations.

The paper supplies a better candidate for a no-explicit-sleep-item sensitivity if future authorized linked data are available; confirm the full variable coding, timing, missingness, score-marker coverage, population overlap and actual number with genotypes before analysis. The 2,155 participants are assessed for frailty and NPI/NPI-Q in the paper, not a verified genotype intersection. This does not resolve access or provide validation.
