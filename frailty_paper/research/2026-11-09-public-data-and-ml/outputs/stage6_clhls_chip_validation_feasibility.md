# CLHLS frailty and genotype feasibility: targeted-chip coverage

The Chinese Longitudinal Healthy Longevity Survey (CLHLS) includes older adults and has been used for [frailty and genotype analyses](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10791136/). Survey files are described as free to researchers under a data-use agreement by [Duke's CLHLS resource](https://cpha.duke.edu/node/3596). That description does **not** extend to individual genetic data: a [genetic-data study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7963337/) describes the data as controlled under accession CNP0000792. Genetic-data access costs, phenotype linkage and eligibility are unverified.

A published CLHLS resource describes two genotyping datasets. The larger dataset used a custom **27,656-marker** chip, and the smaller dataset used an approximately **900,015-marker HumanOmniZhongHua** array; 2,054 people overlapped between them. They are not interchangeable. The 2023 frailty paper mentions about 27,000 SNPs and the HumanOmniZhongHua array, so its exact genotype source and marker/imputation inventory require clarification; do not assume that its complete data equal either 2021 dataset.

We compared both project candidate lists to the 2021 study's publicly supplied Supplementary Table S3 inventory of **27,656 custom-chip markers**. There were zero exact rsID matches among 15 weighted-score markers and zero among 22 count-score markers. The [per-marker table](stage6_clhls_chip_candidate_coverage.csv), [receipt](stage6_clhls_chip_candidate_coverage.json), and [script](../work/clhls_chip_candidate_check.py) preserve the source and check. This is a comparison to the custom-chip **design**, not the separate 900k array, imputed data, or an individual's QC-passing calls. It does not rule out an identifier alias, imputed coverage or nearby proxies; it does not imply a null genetic effect. Exact build, strand and alternate identifiers still need checking. On this evidence the 27k design alone is not established as suitable for calculating either score; whether the 900k or imputed data cover the scores remains unknown.

Before considering CLHLS for validation, verify the precise genetic cohort and array, access/fees, overlap of genotypes with sleep and a well-defined FI or FFS, variant coverage and quality including imputation, ancestry, and discovery-sample or relative overlap. The frailty paper's 39-item FI is distinct from the UKB discovery measures. Its own score was selected in CLHLS and is not our project score. No participant data were accessed and no independent score validation has been performed.

## Follow-up: representative larger-array manifest

We downloaded the public [HumanOmniZhongHua-8 v1.1-B manifest](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GPL19864) and scanned all 894,517 assay rows. Four of 15 weighted-score markers and one of 22 count-score markers had exact rsID **and GRCh37 coordinate** matches; one additional marker in each list had a coordinate match under a different probe name. This is a probe-design comparison, not a genotype or allele match: Illumina strand conventions, SNP contrasts, array revision, sample QC and imputation were not verified. The v1.1 manifest is representative; the CLHLS studies do not establish from the cited evidence that this precise revision was used. Other revisions and imputed markers could differ. Thus this check still cannot determine whether either score can be calculated in the actual cohort.

[Manifest match table](stage6_clhls_omni_v11_coverage.csv), [receipt](stage6_clhls_omni_v11_coverage.json), and [script](../work/omni_zhonghua_manifest_check.py) include raw manifest strand labels, counts and the downloaded-file hash. Do not use raw manifest allele strings as confirmed forward-strand score alleles without resolving Illumina's conventions.

### Allele-orientation follow-up

Using [Illumina's manifest guidance](https://knowledge.illumina.com/microarray/general/microarray-general-reference_material-list/000001489), we inferred genomic-plus allele pairs from the manifest SNP and RefStrand fields. All four exact-name/coordinate matches in the 15-marker set, the one such match in the 22-marker set, and the two coordinate-only probe names have inferred plus-strand pairs matching their respective project contrasts: **5/15 and 2/22 at the coordinate-and-pair level**. Coordinate-only matches do not prove rsID identity or that probes work, and inferred plus alleles still require validation against the actual CLHLS array revision and data. This supersedes the earlier statement that no manifest allele orientation had been attempted; the biological-sample genotype and imputed coverage remain unknown. The updated [match table](stage6_clhls_omni_v11_coverage.csv) and [receipt](stage6_clhls_omni_v11_coverage.json) include the derived pair and raw strand fields.
