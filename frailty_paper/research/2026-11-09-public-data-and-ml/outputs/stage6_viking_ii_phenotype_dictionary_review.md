# VIKING II questionnaire: sleep and physical-function feasibility

Checked 29 October 2026. The [VIKING II cohort](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10476511/) collects online questionnaire information and saliva, and describes prospective linkage to health records. Its [open data dictionary](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/4036) is documentation, not participant data. The study says individual-level genomic and other research data are not publicly released and require managed access. Its [current access page](https://viking.ed.ac.uk/our-data-and-samples/access) describes VIKING II/III questionnaire data through DPUK and genomic access for different cohort subsets under separate arrangements. We did not establish which genetic product covers VIKING II, individual-level linkage or cost; clinic exomes and other VIKING cohorts must not be assumed to represent VIKING II.

We downloaded the 2020-03-02 workbook and examined all 23 sheets' populated rows. The [14-field selected dictionary extract](viking_ii_sleep_frailty_dictionary_selected.csv) records names, response coding and exact question text. Useful candidate measures include:

- Workday and free-day categorized sleep-onset latency (`q51c_How_many_minutes_fall_asleep`, `q51f_Free_day_minutes_asleep`). These measure usual time to fall asleep, not an insomnia diagnosis or the same case definition as the project's GWAS. The dictionary includes several work/free-day sleep timing questions.
- Tiredness or low energy over three months and over two weeks (`qSAF_Tired_3_Months`, `qSAF_Tired_2_Weeks`) and self-reported work, transport and leisure activity. These are potential exhaustion/activity proxies, not the original Fried criteria without an explicit validated mapping.
- `cQ7a_Trouble_falling_asleep` and `cQ7a_Waking_too_early` appear alongside questions about periods of depressed mood or loss of interest in the `Clinical` sheet. They must not be treated as population-wide insomnia questions without verifying the actual screen routing, eligibility, time frame and missing-value codes. The sample [questionnaire PDF](https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/bitstreams/a2e1910d-476e-4605-b0af-e82c3121783a/download), page 19, displays the initial mental-health screening questions, but its screenshot does not establish all dynamic follow-up routes.

A keyword scan of this workbook found no explicitly named grip-strength field. This is not proof that grip or other frailty measures are absent from the wider VIKING data or linked clinics. We have not established a complete FI, Fried phenotype, prospective outcome, age distribution, common sample, usable markers or independence from discovery sources. The workbook's existence and the nominal sampling target cannot establish the count of genotyped participants with all required data. A custom sleep-free frailty index would require a defensible item list and evaluation; tiredness and mood symptoms cannot simply be treated as interchangeable with FI or FFS items.

The present result supports a possible sleep-onset/circadian exploratory analysis if an eligible research team secures linked data, but it is not evidence that our score predicts insomnia or frailty. Before prioritizing, establish genotype platform and dosage availability for each exact allele contrast, imputation QC, phenotype and genotype joint counts, age and relatedness, sleep question routing, possible cross-cohort overlap, access terms and fees. Account for kinship and founder-population structure if an analysis becomes possible. No application, external message, or participant-level data analysis was made.
