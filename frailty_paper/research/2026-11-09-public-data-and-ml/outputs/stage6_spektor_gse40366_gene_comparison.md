# Sleep-expression panel in a public frailty cohort

We compared an insomnia-associated blood-expression panel with a separate publicly available frailty expression dataset. In [Spektor et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27062771), 244 genes in the authors' supplementary Table S5 had BH-adjusted p < 0.05 for middle insomnia versus controls. We looked up these selected symbols in the [GSE40366 Vitality 90+ dataset](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE40366), which provides PBMC expression and frailty categories in nonagenarians. This is a cross-phenotype exploratory comparison, not independent replication of insomnia, and it does not evaluate or validate genetic scores.

Of 244 source genes, 222 had an exact symbol match with the historical GPL15988 platform annotation and usable expression. Among those 222, 14 had nominal p < 0.05 for frail versus non-frail, but **none remained significant after BH correction across the 222 tested genes** (smallest q = 0.723). The estimated directions matched the source insomnia comparison for 101 of 222 genes. This descriptive fraction is not a calibrated test of a shared biological signature and does not establish a null effect. Twenty-two genes did not map exactly; outdated symbols and probe coverage could contribute.

We compared 29 frail with 38 non-frail participants, excluding 84 pre-frail participants. We selected the highest mean-expression probe per gene across all 151 category-labeled participants without using their group labels, then fitted expression on frailty status and sex, with HC3 standard errors and t reference degrees of freedom 64. BH adjustment was applied to all 222 matched genes. The source panel itself was selected for significance in another dataset; this cross-study follow-up and direction comparison were not prespecified. The source study used whole blood in older adults, while GSE40366 used PBMCs in selected survivors aged 90 or older. Differences in cell composition, cohort recruitment, frailty definitions, exposures and other unmeasured covariates can affect the results. The GEO metadata do not support adjustment for exact age or cell composition; participant overlap with other project cohorts remains to be resolved.

Reproduce with `work/stage4_env/bin/python work/geo_vitality/spektor_gene_followup.py`. The [gene-level results](stage6_spektor_gse40366_gene_comparison.csv) and [analysis receipt](stage6_spektor_gse40366_gene_comparison_receipt.json) document estimates, probe selection, mapping and denominators. The script streams the public GPL15988 platform and uses the previously downloaded GEO matrix and source supplementary table. Treat the nominal signals as leads, not validated risk genes.
