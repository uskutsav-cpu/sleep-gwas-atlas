# Remaining candidate regions: QTL and exploratory colocalization batch

From 14 previously outstanding candidate-centered groups, the [selection worklist](stage3_remaining_molecular_worklist.md) identified 12 exact-marker GTEx v10 expression-QTL gene/tissue pairs for follow-up. In the corresponding eQTL Catalogue release-7 GTEx v8 reprocessing, **11 selected gene/tissue pairs had regional statistics and an exact marker–allele match**. RPL31P12 in cerebellum for rs2815764 returned no gene rows in the queried region; this is not proof that the gene or region has no association in other releases or tissues. Two other groups, rs9845387 and rs4886140, had no exact-marker GTEx v10 QTL returned by the original screen, and proxy-variant or distal analysis remains incomplete.

For each of the 11 pairs I calculated six pairwise, single-signal ABF comparisons: Atkins FI, Jansen insomnia, Mak FI, Mak modified physical frailty, Ye FFS and FinnGen insomnia. The [66 baseline comparisons](stage3_remaining_coloc_baseline_summary.csv) contain H0–H4, matched variant counts, molecular credible-set coverage when available, and the observed H4 range across a small prior-sensitivity grid. The [complete 594-row sensitivity table](stage3_remaining_coloc_sensitivity.csv) contains all settings. Each row is **post-selection and exploratory**; none is a multiple-testing-controlled discovery, a three-trait test, or evidence of gene mediation.

At the baseline prior, the anterior cingulate expression QTL for `ENSG00000279325` at the rs1909650 region has H4 = 0.850 with Atkins FI and 0.697 with Jansen insomnia. This is a hypothesis for deeper work, not a confirmed shared causal gene: no molecular credible set for this gene was reported in the selected Catalogue file, the single-signal assumption is unverified, and the FI and insomnia GWAS have sample and phenotype overlap. The source-v8 exact-marker QTL p = 0.000732 is nominal; it and the GTEx v10 selection use related data, not independent replication. Other gene/tissue choices could yield different results.

The [fine-mapping summary](stage3_remaining_molecular_finemapping_summary.csv) shows that six of the 11 tested genes have no reported molecular credible set in the corresponding file. Two others, the rs6452792 and rs2155292 selected gene/tissue pairs, have **two reported molecular credible sets**; a one-signal H3/H4 model cannot decisively describe them. The remaining three tested pairs have one reported set, but some fine-mapped variants are absent from the matched GWAS. For example, rs7691487/SNCA artery has 22/30 variants in the Atkins and Jansen inputs. Even one reported set does not prove that all QTL or GWAS signals have been captured. The [molecular-set rows](stage3_remaining_molecular_credible_sets.csv) and [coverage by trait](stage3_remaining_coloc_coverage.json) give the actual counts. Reported variant PIPs are molecular-model quantities, not disease probabilities. No selected candidate itself appeared in these reported sets; this does not establish zero PIP or disprove tagging.

## Methods and limitations

Selection was based on post hoc exact-marker eQTLs and a preference for brain, muscle, blood or artery where available; this is not a comprehensive gene or tissue screen. The source datasets are the related GTEx releases described in the [worklist](stage3_remaining_molecular_worklist.md). We extracted candidate-centered 2-Mb GRCh38 windows from the Catalogue's complete `all` QTL files, retaining the selected gene only. [Extraction receipt](stage3_remaining_catalogue_extract_receipt.json), [source-download receipt](stage3_remaining_catalogue_download_receipt.json). Local hashes were recorded, but the provider's full source/index checksums were not independently validated. Some molecular fine-mapping windows differ from these candidate-centered windows.

GWAS matching required unique rsID, chromosome, coordinate and allele-pair agreement, non-palindromic biallelic SNPs, QTL MAF ≥1%, finite beta/SE, and unique positive-strand GRCh37-to-GRCh38 liftover where applicable. Mak, Atkins, Jansen and FinnGen GWAS MAF ≥1% was also checked; Ye source frequencies were unavailable and its filename specifies an upstream 1% filter. The original Atkins and Jansen project files have additional source QC and exclude the MHC. QTL and GWAS imputation quality, sample overlap, source build and allele semantics are not fully verified. [Matching receipt](stage3_remaining_qtl_harmonization_receipt.json).

Baseline priors are p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴, p12 = 10⁻⁵ and molecular effect SD 0.15 on an assumed standardized scale. Jansen and FinnGen insomnia use a log-odds prior SD of 0.20; Mak FI/FP use working SDs from another region. Atkins FI uses an estimate based on SE, GWAS allele frequency and source constant total N; Ye FFS uses total N and QTL MAF as a proxy for its unavailable GWAS frequency. These estimates are approximate, and GWAS sample overlap, measurement definitions and effective sample size remain uncertain. The sensitivity grid varies trait effect-prior SD by 0.5–2 and p12 from 10⁻⁶ to 5×10⁻⁵; it does not test other QTL priors, model misspecification or all missing variants. H1 is QTL-only, H2 GWAS-only, H3 distinct signals and H4 one shared signal; they are model-dependent posterior probabilities.

For all 66 baseline calculations, the custom ABF implementation matched the `approx.bf.estimates` and `combine.abf` functions from [coloc v5.2.3](https://github.com/chr1swallace/coloc/blob/v5.2.3/R/claudia.R) to <3×10⁻¹⁵ ([numerical checks](stage3_remaining_coloc_formula_check.csv)). This validates arithmetic only, not the underlying data, prior, biological interpretation or single-signal assumption. Fine-mapping with ancestry-matched LD, improved variant support, multiple independent signals, tissue relevance and independent datasets are still needed.
