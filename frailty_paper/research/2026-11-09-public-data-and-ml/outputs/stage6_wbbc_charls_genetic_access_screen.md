# WBBC and CHARLS: access and phenotype screen

Screened 29 October 2026 against the requirement for free participant-level genotype data linked to sleep and frailty outcomes. Neither is currently an established free, openly downloadable validation cohort.

## Westlake BioBank for Chinese (WBBC) pilot

The [cohort profile](https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/11/6/e045564) reports sleep questionnaires and grip-strength measurements in the pilot, but states that its data are not freely available in the public domain and describes approved collaboration. The pilot was focused on adolescents and young adults, limiting its suitability for validation of an aging-related frailty phenotype. Grip is not FI or the full Fried frailty phenotype. Separate participant-level overlap among sleep, grip, score markers and sequencing is not established by the marginal counts.

The [genomic study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30526-x) describes 10,376 sequenced or array-genotyped participants, including a separately recruited older Parkinson's disease case/control sample. Its public availability statement places sequence and VCF data in GSA-Human HRA001385 and explicitly describes restricted access for FASTQ. Do not infer that the complete participant genotype-plus-phenotype package is open or that the separate older sample has pilot sleep and grip data. The [WBBC download page](https://wbbc.westlake.edu.cn/downloads.html) specifies permissions for raw genetic data and offers aggregate allele-frequency and selected summary resources. Fee and approved cross-data linkage are not established. Public frequencies or summary statistics cannot alone validate these participant scores.

## CHARLS

[CHARLS's genetic access guidance](https://charls.charlsdata.com/pages/Data/2026-charls-geneticdata/en.html) says individual-level genetic data, including WGS, are controlled and subject to cost-recovery charges; it also describes institutional and other eligibility conditions. Public questionnaire availability does not make the linked genetic data freely accessible. Under the user's no-fee constraint this is not currently a confirmed route; actual score marker coverage and linked sleep/frailty sample remain unverified.

No application, collaboration request, or participant-level analysis was performed. Continue prioritizing demonstrably no-access-fee resources with permitted linkage and do not equate genotype-free phenotype analyses or single-variant summary associations with score validation.

### Public WBBC GWAS inventory check

The [linked Chinese GWAS summary inventory](https://wbbc.westlake.edu.cn/downloads_GWAS.html) was inspected directly. It lists hip and femoral-neck bone mineral density (BMD), and does not list an insomnia, sleep, grip, FI or FFS summary dataset. The parent download page calls the release 5,369 samples, while the two linked entries say 5,679; resolve this discrepancy from the original files and study documentation before using either number. BMD is a bone-health measure, not the requested frailty or sleep outcome. These aggregate resources cannot be used to evaluate an individual score's prediction. This inventory check does not rule out other unpublished or separately distributed WBBC traits.
