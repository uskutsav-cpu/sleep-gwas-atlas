# Device-measured sleep and the project's candidate variants

## Findings

[Portas et al. (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71252-y) analyzed accelerometer-derived sleep traits in 80,013 UK Biobank participants of European ancestry. We looked up the project's 15 candidates, the FOXP2-region disease lead and a sleep-study lead in the public, harmonized **sex-combined** REM and NREM summary statistics. The 15 project candidates and rs35624276 had exact position-and-allele matches in both files. These results provide distinct sleep-phenotype context; UK Biobank overlaps or may overlap project discovery studies and the lookups were postselected, so they are not independent replication of an insomnia–frailty score or a causal mechanism.

- **MEIS1-region rs113851554**, counted T versus G: REM beta −9.051 minutes/night (SE 0.394, p=1.22e−116) and NREM beta +6.917 minutes/night (SE 0.602, p=1.58e−30). Portas et al. discuss this locus in connection with restless legs syndrome (RLS) and report associations persisting after excluding identified or probable RLS; residual underdiagnosis remains possible. The study's main-table REM beta is +9.051 for G (G frequency 0.943); the harmonized file's T effect is the opposite sign (T frequency 0.057). Its harmonisation code 11 denotes flipped alleles, and the allele-specific values are consistent with that table. This does not establish that MEIS1 mediates frailty or the sleep–frailty relationship. The locus and alleles are multiallelic in broader reference data, and the exact original-study representations still merit scrutiny.
- **FOXP2-region rs12536335**, counted G versus A: combined-sex NREM beta +1.068 minutes/night (SE 0.274, p=9.61e−5); combined-sex REM beta +0.271 (SE 0.179, p=0.129). The NREM association is postselected and nominal, and the study phenotype is algorithm-inferred sleep stage rather than insomnia. Its direction cannot by itself establish the same causal signal as the HFRS or insomnia associations.
- **FOXP2-region rs35624276**, counted G versus A: NREM beta −0.798 (SE 0.271, p=0.00327), and REM beta −0.248 (SE 0.177, p=0.161). This is the lead of the previously reported disease–disease SuSiE signal, not proof of shared biology with NREM.

The paper separately reports a **female-only** NREM association at rs1799252 (beta −2.16 minutes/night, p=6.4e−9). Its sex-specific result must not be conflated with our combined-sex lookups or described as independent of the UK Biobank discovery work. Ensembl maps rs1799252 T/A to chr7:114227537 (GRCh38), but the sex-combined harmonized files did not return that exact T/A allele pair at that position. The 1000 Genomes Phase 3 VCF we queried contained a different deletion at the position and did not contain an rs1799252 SNP record; the Ensembl pairwise LD service returned empty results for comparisons with rs12536335 and rs35624276. These are coverage limits, **not evidence of zero association or zero LD**. We cannot determine whether the female-specific lead and our disease signal are shared from this lookup.

## Regional signal-sharing test

For combined-sex NREM, MVP clinical insomnia and FinnGen HFRS, 1,225 exactly aligned SNPs passed the target/reference frequency-difference filter in the previously studied 1-Mb gnomAD window. Mean absolute NREM/gnomAD NFE frequency difference was 0.0103 (maximum 0.0706 after filtering). We used public gnomAD NFE signed LD for NREM and MVP, FIN for HFRS, SuSiE-RSS L=10, 95% coverage, fixed residual variance and working N=80,013, 253,769 and 500,737, respectively. All three fits converged. The NREM fit returned **no reported credible set** (maximum PIP 0.679); it therefore cannot be paired in this SuSiE-coloc analysis. This result does not imply separate or shared causal variants. The NREM marginal peak in the retained window was rs12535428 (p=2.93e−7); strongest marginal association alone does not establish a causal variant. The lack of an NREM credible set is a property of this model and selected SNP set, not proof of no regional association.

On these 1,225 variants, the MVP/HFRS pair still gives PP.H4=0.979 at p1=p2=1e−4 and p12=1e−5 (0.826–0.996 over p12 from 1e−6 to 5e−5). A separate expanded-prior check of the earlier 1,257-variant fit shows substantial prior dependence; the lower-prior range was not rerun on this 1,225-variant fit. This is the *same two disease cohorts and locus* as the prior result, with slightly different SNP support, and is not new replication. NREM was not involved in that disease–disease posterior. The gnomAD matrices are external, the custom variant-index decoder was not independently checked using Hail, source overlap is not fully excluded, and the 1-Mb window omits a distal HFRS signal. Credible-set convergence, allele-frequency checks and a high conditional posterior do not establish a causal gene. The algorithm-inferred REM/NREM stages show only moderate polysomnography agreement in the paper, so sleep-stage misclassification is another limitation.

## Source and files

The [GWAS Catalog](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90824001-GCST90825000/) source files are sex-combined REM `GCST90824106` and NREM `GCST90824107`; original builds are GRCh37 and these indexed harmonized files are GRCh38. The downloaded tabix index MD5s matched the respective published index MD5 entries. We retrieved regional/indexed subsets only; the complete source files were not downloaded or checksum-verified. Missing and nonmatching allele pairs were retained as missing. The 15 candidates were defined before this lookup, but examined in prior analyses and selected using related data. Comparing p-values across traits, loci or sex strata does not test differences between their effects.

Reproduction files: `stage6_portas_sleep_candidate_lookup.py` and `.csv`, `stage6_portas_sleep_candidate_receipt.json`; `stage6_portas_nrem_foxp2_region_extract.py` and receipt; `stage6_portas_nrem_foxp2_prepare.py`, aligned CSV and receipt; `stage6_portas_nrem_foxp2_coloc.R`, SuSiE diagnostics, candidate members and pairwise coloc CSV. The records do not contain individual-level data. The full project limitations remain in `stage6_foxp2_gnomad_matched_coloc_review.md` and `stage5_validation_conclusions.md`.
