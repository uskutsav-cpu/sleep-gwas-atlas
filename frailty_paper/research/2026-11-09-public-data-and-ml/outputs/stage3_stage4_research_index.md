# Insomnia–frailty genetics: Stage 3 and Stage 4 working results

These are public-data research analyses of 22 postselected exploratory markers plus rs323509. They are hypotheses for follow-up, not a validated risk score, proof of a shared causal variant, or evidence that a gene or cell mediates disease. Some GWAS samples overlap, and an FI measure contains a sleep-related item. The region grouping, selections and some follow-up thresholds were chosen after observing the data.

## Start here

- [Variant–gene table](stage3_initial_variant_gene_table.csv) and [how to read it](stage3_first_variant_gene_table_readme.md): source-aligned trait effects, molecular associations, selected colocalization, gene and regulatory evidence, and explicit evidence status. Multiple rows per marker are expected. Predicted targets are labeled.
- [19-region evidence inventory](stage3_region_evidence_inventory.csv) and [prioritized work order](stage3_evidence_informed_work_order.md): a practical post hoc queue, not independent discovery or calibrated causal-gene ranking. Each region has at least one exploratory molecular comparison, not comprehensive gene/tissue coverage.
- [Gene–cell–phenotype evidence network](stage4_variant_gene_phenotype_network_scaffold.csv) and [network explanation](stage4_network_and_resource_preparation.md): each record names the source and evidence type. Paths combine different associations; they do not prove a causal pathway.

## What the current analyses show

- The strongest selected pairwise follow-up by the stated screening rule is an rs323509-region testis splicing QTL: the selected molecular credible set was covered, and separate FI and insomnia colocalization posteriors were high under one prior. FFS support was low, the testis context is of uncertain relevance, and the cohorts overlap. See [the work order](stage3_evidence_informed_work_order.md) and [original-GWAS colocalization review](stage3_original_gwas_coloc_review.md).
- [Selected gene biology](stage3_selected_candidate_gene_biology.md) separates general gene function from the evidence actually observed in this project.
- The [LD proxy inventory](stage3_candidate_reference_ld_proxies.md) and [proxy regulatory predictions](stage3_abc_ld_proxy_regulatory_review.md) broaden the target hypotheses. The 1000 Genomes European reference is small, and ABC predictions, TSS-based gene-ID matches, and LD are not variant-effect validation. [Selected molecular QTLs at LD proxies](stage3_selected_molecular_qtl_ld_proxy_review.md) are separately labeled.
- The [four-source candidate gene comparison](stage4_candidate_brown_four_trait_gene_comparison.md) adds matched-model insomnia, FI and FFS gene tests, with overlap and LD cautions.
- The [FI–FFS candidate gene comparison](stage4_candidate_fi_ffs_gene_comparison.md) and [cell-type comparison](stage4_standard_fi_ffs_cell_type_comparison.md) use the same standard MAGMA gene model and reference. They are side-by-side results rather than a formal trait-difference or shared-signal test. A [secondary adult prefrontal-cortex analysis](stage4_adult_pfc_neuron_subtype_review.md) tests more neuronal subtypes. The [FFS model-sensitivity review](stage4_ye_ffs_magma_model_sensitivity.md) shows how results change with the approximation.
- [Published FI TWAS supplementary results](stage3_published_fi_twas_supplement_review.md) add selected gene-model associations; the [published FI/insomnia comparison](stage3_published_fi_insomnia_twas_review.md) remains limited and potentially overlapping. Matching a gene does not connect the TWAS signal to a specific candidate variant.
- The [human muscle cluster analysis](stage4_hpa_human_muscle_cell_type_review.md) tests FAP, satellite-cell and myonuclear expression properties, using an atlas from a PAD study and controls. None of its 100 post hoc trait–cluster tests passed the joint FDR threshold.
- The [four-system cell expression review](stage4_four_system_candidate_expression_review.md), [human muscle stem-cell expression](stage4_human_musc_candidate_expression_review.md), [human muscle insomnia scDRS](stage4_human_musc_insomnia_scdrs.md), and [mouse brain insomnia scDRS](stage4_mouse_brain_insomnia_scdrs.md) have different sample and method limits. Expression is not enrichment; insomnia-only scDRS does not test shared frailty risk.

## Remaining limitations

The current colocalizations use selected gene–tissue pairs and mostly a single-signal model, with incomplete credible-set or variant coverage in several regions; multi-signal, relevant-tissue and wider candidate-proxy analyses are still needed. The LD/ABC target mapping has unresolved symbols and no allele-specific functional validation. Cell-type references are incomplete, include mouse datasets, and generally do not provide donor-aware disease tests. Neither the network nor FDR overlap establishes a variant→gene→cell→phenotype mechanism. [Matched Brown-1df cell-type analyses](stage4_matched_brown_cell_type_comparison.md) are available for FFS, original insomnia and original FI; they do not substitute for independent-cohort replication.
