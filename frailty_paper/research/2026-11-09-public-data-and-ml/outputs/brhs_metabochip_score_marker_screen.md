# BRHS UCLEB Metabochip: score-marker coverage screen

Reviewed 29 October 2026. A published UCLEB design paper describes a BRHS case-control selection of 1,095 coronary heart disease/stroke cases and 1,358 controls matched on town and age band; this is the historical UCLEB design, not the count eligible for sleep–frailty validation. A later diabetes analysis reports 2,317 BRHS participants with Metabochip data and complete incident-diabetes information, again a different selected analytic count. The consortium used cardiometabolic chip genotyping and imputation. [UCLEB design](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0071345); [diabetes paper](https://diabetes.diabetesjournals.org/content/diabetes/64/5/1830.full.pdf); [cardiovascular risk analysis](https://heart.bmj.com/content/heartjnl/102/20/1640.full.pdf).

## Public manifest check

I compared the existing prototype variant coordinates with Illumina's public **Cardio-Metabo Chip 11395247 C** manifest. The 15-marker list has **1/15** exact coordinate matches (rs1032524); the 22-marker shared-direction list has **2/22** (rs6058750 and rs6938026). Each of these three also has the exact same rsID as the manifest marker. The lists are overlapping project prototypes, not independent sets. See [per-marker results](brhs_metabochip_score_marker_screen.csv), [script](brhs_metabochip_coverage.py) and [checksum and counts](brhs_metabochip_score_marker_screen.json).

The manifest contains mixed GenomeBuild values; the script compares manifest rows labelled 36, 36.2 or 18 with coordinates converted from the project's inferred/assumed GRCh37 positions to hg18 using the UCSC hg19-to-hg18 chain, and rows labelled 37 or 37.1 with the original positions. Each of the 37 input rows mapped to one hg18 location. Twenty-five build-35 and 23 unplaced/null manifest records were not compared by coordinate; exact rsID matches were checked across all entries. The build-label interpretation, input-coordinate identities, chip revision used in BRHS, alleles, strand and imputation require verification from the actual released data. An array-coordinate match by itself is not a verified biallelic genotype or a passing-quality call.

These figures describe **direct array design coverage only**, not the number of project variants available in UCLEB: imputation can recover additional variants, and other genotyping datasets may exist. Conversely, a manifest marker can fail quality control or be absent from an individual. The UCLEB design paper notes limited imputation in regions lacking nearby chip coverage, which makes actual variant-level imputation quality essential. This check is not a score test or validation. Public inputs: [Illumina product page](https://emea.support.illumina.com/downloads/cardio-metabochip_product_support_files.html), [manufacturer CSV](https://webdata.illumina.com/downloads/productfiles/cardio-metabochip/cardio-metabo_chip_11395247_c.csv), [UCSC conversion chain](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/goldenPath/hg19/liftOver/hg19ToHg18.over.chain.gz).

## Practical implication

The Health Data Research Gateway describes a UCLEB steering-group application specifying required studies and datasets, giving a more concrete genetic-data route than the BRHS UK Data Service phenotype deposit alone. The catalogue lists **cost recovery** as the access-request cost and **no linkage** among its data-use limitations. It does not specify an amount or whether this limitation applies to joining BRHS genotype and study phenotype files already inside UCLEB; do not assume either is free or permitted. Eligibility, release of person-level genotypes and a viable linked participant count remain unconfirmed. [UCLEB catalogue](https://healthdatagateway.org/en/dataset/831).

Before investing in access, establish whether a suitable BRHS imputed or other genotype release covers each score variant with adequate INFO and matching alleles, whether linkage to 2010–12 and 2018–19 sleep and frailty data is allowed, and the participant-level joint counts. The historical cardiovascular case-control sampling and survival to later waves must be taken into account for any association, calibration or performance estimate; do not assume the genotyped subset represents the whole cohort. Request the sampling design and full analysis documentation before choosing any correction. No enquiry or application was sent.

A [reference-panel sensitivity](brhs_chip_subset_reference_review.md) shows that sums restricted to these direct-chip markers correlate only weakly with the full prototypes in 503 European-labelled reference participants. This does not measure actual BRHS coverage or score performance.
