# SMIM32 skeletal-muscle expression with original FI and insomnia GWAS

For SMIM32 expression in GTEx v8 skeletal muscle (Catalogue r7 QTD000281), the single-signal baseline model gives Atkins FI H1 = 0.442, H3 = 0.531 and H4 = 0.027, and Jansen UK Biobank insomnia H1 = 0.005, H3 = 0.249 and H4 = 0.746. These are pairwise probabilities under the specified model, not a three-trait test or confirmation of a causal SMIM32 mechanism. Both comparisons cover only **74 of 98** reported molecular credible-set variants, so missing variants can materially distort the estimates. The FI includes sleep-related content and overlaps Jansen UK Biobank participants; its contrast with insomnia is not an independent replication.

At baseline p1 = p2 = 10⁻⁴, p12 = 10⁻⁵, QTL effect prior SD = 0.15, the FI prior SD is estimated from GWAS SE, frequency and a constant source N, while the insomnia log-odds prior SD is 0.20. Across tested trait-prior multipliers 0.5–2 and p12 from 10⁻⁶ to 5×10⁻⁵, H4 ranges 0.0019–0.152 for FI and 0.223–0.937 for insomnia ([sensitivity table](stage3_smim32_original_gwas_coloc_sensitivity.csv)). This grid does not resolve missing variants, multiple signals or prior misspecification. The separate [FinnGen and Mak results](stage3_smim32_skeletal_finngen_coloc.md) use different inputs and are not a formal test of differences between studies.

The source window is GRCh38 chr5:135702653–136702653; it is narrower than the molecular fine-mapping region. The [matching receipt](stage3_smim32_muscle_original_gwas_harmonization_receipt.json) records allele/build and QC restrictions; the [coverage](stage3_smim32_original_gwas_coloc_coverage.json) and [source credible-set rows](stage3_smim32_muscle_credible_set.csv) record model support. The original project harmonized GWAS exclude palindromic variants, MHC and low-frequency SNPs, and source build, sample overlap, imputation quality and QTL scale require further review. The custom ABFs match [coloc v5.2.3](https://github.com/chr1swallace/coloc/blob/v5.2.3/R/claudia.R) reference functions to <4×10⁻¹⁵ on these inputs ([check](stage3_smim32_original_gwas_coloc_formula_check.csv)); this is not biological validation.
