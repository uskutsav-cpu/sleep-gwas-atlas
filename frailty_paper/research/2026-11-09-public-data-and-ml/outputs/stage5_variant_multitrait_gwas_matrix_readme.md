# Variant-level FI, FFS and insomnia comparison

The [matrix](stage5_variant_multitrait_gwas_matrix.csv) has one row per Stage 5 queue allele and separate match status, ALT-oriented beta, standard error and unadjusted p-value columns for six sources: Atkins FI, Jansen insomnia, Mak FI, Mak physical frailty, Ye FFS and FinnGen R13 insomnia. It joins the existing exact-allele extracts without multiplying results across tissues or QTL records. Molecular credible-set genes, tissues and maximum PIP are context only; maximum PIP across distinct sets is not a disease PIP and should not be used as a cross-trait causal probability. The [audit](stage5_variant_multitrait_gwas_matrix_audit.json) gives source coverage.

For example, rs60271 (chr5:104742532 A>C) has Jansen insomnia p = 5.159 × 10⁻⁹ and Ye FFS p = 1.2 × 10⁻⁹ in these selected datasets, while its testis sQTL credible-set status concerns a different molecular phenotype. Its similar associations to the rs323509 marker do not resolve causality or demonstrate shared colocalization; linkage disequilibrium and correlated cohorts matter. The FOXP2 insertion has FinnGen, Mak and Ye results; blank cells are missing or excluded, not null results.

Sources use different phenotypes, analysis methods, effect scales and samples. Mak FI and physical frailty are same-cohort analyses, Ye FFS and several other inputs involve UK Biobank, and v8/v10 molecular QTL resources may overlap. Beta signs alone do not establish a shared mechanism; risk-direction interpretation requires verified phenotype and allele coding and should not use the Ye direction as definitive because its A1 convention was inferred. Source p-values are not adjusted here for selection or multiple tests. Mapped GRCh37-source SNVs require the assumptions and exclusions in the [matching notes](stage5_grch37_gwas_exact_queue_readme.md); FinnGen requires the separate [exact-match notes](stage5_finngen_insomnia_exact_queue_readme.md).

AVI is a broad model-based impact score rather than disease risk. Full tissue-resolved AlphaGenome predictions, event-matched validation and fine-mapping/colocalization appropriate to the phenotype remain unfinished.

A subsequent GRCh37 reference-checked indel lookup added 26 exact indel matches each for Mak FI, Mak physical frailty and Ye FFS. The FOXP2 insertion is present for those sources. See the [indel mapping notes](stage5_indel_grch37_gwas_readme.md). These results do not update the earlier colocalization analyses.
