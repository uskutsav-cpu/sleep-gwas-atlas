Subject: Request for summary statistics for the mental-wellbeing-excluded frailty index

Dear Dr. Mak,

We are studying shared genetic associations of insomnia with the frailty index and the Fried-style physical frailty phenotype. In your 2023 Aging Cell study, Supplementary Tables 4 and 16 describe an FI excluding eight mental-wellbeing items, including insomnia and fatigue, and the paper reports a GWAS of each category-excluded FI. This phenotype could help us examine the impact of item overlap on the genetic correlation between insomnia and frailty.

Would it be possible to obtain the full genome-wide summary statistics for the mental-wellbeing-excluded FI, together with the corresponding full-FI GWAS results from the same sample and model? For each file, it would be helpful to have the genome build, chromosome and position, rsID, effect and other alleles, beta, standard error, p-value, allele frequency, per-variant sample size, quality filters and ancestry. Please let us know any access conditions or restrictions.

We would also appreciate clarification of:

- Which samples were used for the modified and full-FI GWAS, whether they consist of the same individuals, and whether results exist for the full UK Biobank sample and/or the random-half and non-metabolomics subsets used in your main analyses.
- The exact item coding, missing-data rules, FI denominator, phenotype scaling and covariates for each version, and whether any participants or close relatives overlap the insomnia discovery cohorts.
- Whether separately derived FI GWAS removing only insomnia, or only insomnia and fatigue, are available. We understand that removing the entire mental-wellbeing category changes additional components of frailty.

We would use the data for harmonized, quality-checked genetic-correlation and candidate-variant sensitivity analyses, with appropriate acknowledgment and adherence to any data-use terms. Thank you for considering the request.

Best regards,
