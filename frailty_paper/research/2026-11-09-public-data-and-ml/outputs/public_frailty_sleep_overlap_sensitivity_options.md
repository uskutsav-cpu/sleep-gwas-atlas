# Public-data options for testing sleep-item overlap

Research review, 27 October 2026. The question is whether the genetic association between insomnia and frailty persists when frailty is measured without a direct insomnia item. A different frailty definition provides useful triangulation but cannot isolate the effect of deleting that item: cohorts, ascertainment and the rest of the phenotype also change.

| Frailty source | Sleep-related content and interpretation | Public data and practical use |
|---|---|---|
| Mak mental-wellbeing-stripped FI | Removes insomnia and fatigue, but also six other mental-wellbeing items. The closest identified published comparison of FI with and without these domains; it does not isolate insomnia alone. | The inspected [author release](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24591573.v1) includes full FI and physical-frailty files but does not identify the stripped-FI genome-wide file. Full summary statistics not located in the inspected sources. [Item and availability audit](mak2023_sleep_excluded_fi_lead.md). |
| Tseng diagnosis/laboratory FI | Published item list does not explicitly name sleep, insomnia or fatigue; broad diagnoses may still contain related information. It is a different 52-item FI, not the same FI with one item removed. Published item classifications and reported scale require clarification. | Full summary statistics not located in targeted searches. The selected lead-variant table is insufficient for genome-wide LDSC. [Item, availability and lead-variant audit](tseng_diagnosis_lab_fi_gwas_lead.md). |
| FinnGen hospital frailty risk score (HFRS) | No F51, G47 or R53 three-character category in the 109 listed categories. R40 includes somnolence and Z73 includes possible exhaustion-related subcategories; HFRS is hospital-diagnosis based, not FI or FFS. | Public full summary statistics have already been analyzed. MVP insomnia–HFRS LDSC r_g = 0.566 (approximate 95% CI 0.502–0.630). This is an exploratory comparison of different constructs, with an elevated HFRS intercept and non-Finnish-specific LD reference; it cannot establish sleep-item removal or causation. [Code audit](hfrs_sleep_overlap_audit.md); [LDSC diagnostics](hfrs_insomnia_ldsc_intercept_audit.md). |
| Foote latent residual factors | Multimorbidity and disability have no insomnia among their named defining indicators, but belong to a model whose general factor includes insomnia. They are not stripped FI scores. | Public, Q-filtered factor summary statistics and indexed marker lookups are available. Seven-factor lookup is hypothesis-generating only; filtering, model-specific N-hat, overlap and marker coverage require method-specific review before LDSC. [Factor review](foote_latent_frailty_gwas_availability.md). |

## Decision

Use the existing HFRS analysis as **triangulation**, with its phenotype and LDSC limitations stated alongside the estimate. Keep Mak's stripped FI as the first choice for a direct comparison *if* a free full summary-statistics release becomes available, and Tseng's FI as a second, different-definition sensitivity. Neither has been located in the inspected sources, and a broader search is not proof that a file does not exist. The public searches repeated for this review returned the [Mak-related literature](https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13868), [Tseng paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10828416/) and existing FI/FFS studies, rather than a verified downloadable stripped-FI genome-wide file.

If suitable public files become available, prespecify a matched-support comparison of full versus stripped FI against the same insomnia GWAS, confirm allele harmonization and ancestry-appropriate LD, assess heritability and intercept diagnostics, and use paired block-jackknife uncertainty when block definitions align. Report the change as a difference between composite definitions, not the isolated contribution of one item. Shared participants and differences in ascertainment remain material. Do not use a selected lead table or Q-filtered latent-factor file as a substitute for conventional full summary statistics without method validation.

No controlled-access cohort, author contact, new LDSC calculation or individual-level prediction was used in this review. Public summary statistics alone do not validate or calibrate the project's 22-marker score.
