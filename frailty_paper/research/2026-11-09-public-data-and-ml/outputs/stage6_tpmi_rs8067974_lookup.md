# TPMI sleep-disorder lookup: rs8067974

The [TPMI PheWeb variant page](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/17:45155199-A-G) reports rs8067974 at GRCh38 chr17:45155199 A/G. This matches the two alleles in the 15-marker prototype, which counts G, but source-specific effect-allele orientation and multi-allelic contrasts need confirmation before comparing directions. The project's association sources were primarily European ancestry; TPMI is a Han Chinese clinical cohort. Sample or relative overlap with all candidate-discovery and selection datasets has not been verified.

For the browser's broad [sleep disorders phenotype](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327), the page displays effect size 0.013, SE 0.0078 and p = 0.11, with 45,836 cases and 287,785 controls. These are rounded browser values, not a downloaded full-precision record. The result is imprecise and does not show a clear association at a nominal 0.05 level; it is not evidence of no effect, and the broader diagnostic outcome differs from insomnia. A single candidate check does not validate the 15-marker score, joint insomnia–frailty prediction, or individual predictive performance. Marker and phenotype selection was exploratory; no multiplicity adjustment or independent-replication claim is made. The positional nearest-gene label HEXIM1 does not establish mediation.

The site states that coordinates use GRCh38 and requests TPMI acknowledgement plus citations to [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x). No full summary-statistics file was obtained; the output is a manual browser lookup. A separate direct URL attempt for the A/C representation of rs323509 returned a browser error; this is not evidence that the marker is absent or null. Full-source effect coding, phenotype codes, QC, precise estimates and the rest of the frozen candidates remain to be checked.
