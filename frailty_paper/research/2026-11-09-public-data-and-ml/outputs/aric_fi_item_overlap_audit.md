# ARIC Visit 5 frailty index: public item-label audit

On 29 October 2026, the public [dbGaP dictionary for pht012825.v2 FINDEXCOMP51](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000280/phs000280.v9.p3/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000280.v9.pht012825.v2.FINDEXCOMP51.data_dict.xml) listed **59 fields named FDI1–FDI59**, a variable `FDICOMPPRES51` (components present), and `FRAILTYINDEX51` (cumulative index). A literal search of descriptions found no `sleep`, `insomnia`, `restless`, or `circadian` wording. This is an item-label audit only; it does not prove sleep independence, rule out sleep-related constructs hidden in other items, establish the exact formula/denominator or missing-item rules, or verify that all listed fields are used in every version of the index.

Two listed items are explicitly effort/exhaustion-related: `FDI10` (“Everything is an effort”, CES3) and `FDI14` (“Trouble getting going”, CES11). These are the same question constructs used for exhaustion in the [Visit 5 ARIC Fried-style frailty phenotype](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5458397/). The index also includes gait speed (`FDI8`), weight loss (`FDI24`), no regular exercise (`FDI27`) and grip strength (`FDI29`), which overlap conceptually with the physical phenotype, though cutpoints and construction cannot be assumed identical from labels. The public [BioLINCC 2026 data dictionary](https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/studies/aric/) separately lists `FRAILTY51`, `FRAILTY52`, and components `EXHAUSTCOMP`, `WTLOSSCOMP10`, `PACCOMP20`, `GRIPCOMP`, `WALKCOMP`. `FRAILTY52` includes a derived likely value when `FRAILTY51` is missing according to its description; do not silently substitute the two.

If an approved linked sample becomes available, a paired study could compare the original FI and physical phenotype in the same people, then run a prespecified sensitivity FI excluding FDI10 and FDI14. That adaptation requires validation of exact coding, item availability, original denominator, threshold for missing data, and the score distribution; it is not the original FI and cannot be assumed valid merely by dropping two columns. Separate comparisons of specific physical domains may explain overlap. Ensure survival and selection between sleep Visit 4 and frailty Visit 5 are addressed. Public labels do not establish sample linkage, genetic variant coverage, permission, no-fee access or participant independence.

The complete label inventory is [here](aric_fi_public_item_inventory.csv); `python work/aric_fi_public_dictionary_audit.py` reproduces the count, keywords and flags. Local dbGaP XML SHA-256 `1608aa535b9c10a027e32304001c1e5f4cb3beee003465dd1329247e8d3b173b`; BioLINCC dictionary PDF SHA-256 `e688977e8b8ceea16086ce0d045d6828621c3a329d0551e81893f1886e0748b0`. No participant data were accessed. See [cohort feasibility](aric_sleep_frailty_genetics_feasibility.md) for sample and access limits.

## Public value and missingness summaries

The separate [dbGaP aggregate variable report](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/dbgap/studies/phs000280/phs000280.v9.p3/pheno_variable_summaries/phs000280.v9.pht012825.v2.p3.FINDEXCOMP51.var_report.xml) supplies marginal value summaries, not person-level records. For `FDI10`, it reports 6,298 non-null observations, 132 nulls, and observed values 0, 0.5 and 1; for `FDI14`, 6,297 non-null, 133 nulls, and the same three possible values. These differ from the dichotomous exhaustion component of the [Fried-style definition](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5458397/), which combines the two questions using its own threshold. Sharing question constructs does not mean the two indices give the questions equal weight or code them identically. Value labels and derivation rules must still be verified before calculating a modified FI.

For `FDICOMPPRES51`, the report gives 6,430 non-null observations, median 44 and range 7–59; `FRAILTYINDEX51` is also reported for 6,430. These are separate marginal summaries in the public report, not the number of people with linked genetic data, sleep measures or valid derived sensitivities. The marginal range and counts do not establish the specific individual-level relationship between number of observed components and availability of the FI, or its derivation. Therefore do not assume a complete 59-item denominator, impose a missingness threshold retroactively, or infer the common complete-case size. Other variables also contain fractional values and substantial missingness.

The value report is stored locally as `work/aric_fi_public_variable_report.xml`, SHA-256 `b81fa5b256f7564974ad3d89b79855f2d37a474320b1232bc23c18c265a387c5`. These are public aggregate summaries only. Source field categories, unreported null semantics and the appropriate minimum completeness threshold should be checked in study methods and source documentation before any participant-level computation.
