# Japanese knee GWAS: reference allele and frequency audit

The 26 matched score rows represent 23 distinct variants; three occur in both score definitions. For all 23, the allele order encoded in the source variant identifier matched the hg19 1000 Genomes reference record's REF/ALT order, and none of these matched pairs was A/T or C/G palindromic. The source effect allele was present in every matching biallelic reference record. This improves confidence in the matching but does not prove each effect's strand or coding, especially at sites represented as multiallelic in other resources.

The largest absolute difference between the source effect-allele frequency and the same allele's frequency in 104 1000 Genomes Japanese (JPT) samples was **0.0634** (rs8067974: 0.4270 versus 0.4904); no difference exceeded 0.10. These descriptive comparisons are not a formal test or guarantee of harmonization: the JPT panel is small and not the GWAS sample, and target cohort composition and imputation can differ. The reference VCF was accessed by indexed region without a full-file checksum. Missing target markers are outside this comparison.

The [per-row table](stage6_japanese_knee_allele_frequency_audit.csv), [summary](stage6_japanese_knee_allele_frequency_audit.json) and `python3 outputs/stage6_japanese_knee_allele_frequency_audit.py` record the source identifiers, effect alleles and frequencies. This audit supports the conditional assumptions of the [partial-score analysis](stage6_japanese_knee_weighted_z_review.md); it does not make those analyses independent score validations, establish target LD, or resolve the inferred FFS weights.
