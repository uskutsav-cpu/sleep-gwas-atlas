# CIMA-Q: insomnia and physical frailty phenotype lead

The [Canadian Open Neuroscience Platform catalog entry](https://portal.conp.ca/dataset?id=projects/CIMA-Q) describes the CIMA-Q aging and cognition cohort. Its assessment inventory lists chronic insomnia, the Insomnia Severity Index, a sleep-quality questionnaire, grip strength, walking speed and a measure named “Fried frailty index.” This is a potentially useful **phenotype** combination. Its exact operational definition, timepoints, missingness, usable sample and overlap of all instruments are not established by the catalog entry. Fried's phenotype and the accumulation-of-deficits FI are different constructs; the catalog's terminology alone does not prove that this measure is identical to the project's discovery FFS.

The entry reports 419 recruited participants as of September 2024 and a portal record of 412 subjects; these are different dated metadata fields, not a verified number with all relevant variables. Its sample is enriched for cognitive impairment and is not a general population. The published inventory lists DNA specimens and APOE genotype, but we did not find genome-wide genotype calls or imputed dosages for our 15/22 markers in the inspected catalog description. An available biospecimen or APOE call does not establish availability of a custom project score. No individual data or scores were accessed.

**Access:** The catalog says data and sample access is restricted to CIMA-Q members and requires approval by its user-access and executive committees, plus independent project ethics approval. Costs and the possibility of joining or obtaining genome-wide data were not established. It is not a confirmed free public validation cohort; the modest total size would also require careful assessment of events and precision. A future authorized feasibility inquiry would need to establish whether appropriate genome-wide data already exist, exact marker/allele coverage and sample overlap, questionnaire and frailty definitions, permitted linkage, study selection, and all costs. No inquiry or application was sent.
