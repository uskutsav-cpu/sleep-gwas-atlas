Between-cell difference error improved by only 0.103% versus a culture-median difference over 122,543 pairs.
The overall between-cell correlation was 0.217, but the mean correlation within a fixed study/cell contrast was only 0.023. Much of the overall agreement therefore reflects broad cell differences rather than correctly explaining which DNA change responds differently.
The new test measures one cell context and cannot independently validate between-cell differences. Calibration-region coverage is descriptive because those outcomes also informed interval scaling through source-omitted predictions. No project or paired ranking is authorized.
