# Candidate-gene associations across insomnia, FI and FFS

The [candidate comparison table](stage4_candidate_brown_four_trait_gene_comparison.csv) contains separate MAGMA Brown-1df gene tests for the original Atkins FI, Ye FFS and Jansen insomnia summary statistics, alongside Mak FI as an additional FI source. The same 1000 Genomes European reference and Ensembl v92 coding-gene annotation were used. These models include approximate gene p-values, and their input filtering and sample-size information differ: Atkins and Ye use constant reported sample sizes, while Jansen and Mak use per-variant sample-size fields. The FI and insomnia UK Biobank samples can overlap, and FI contains sleep-related information. Candidate selection was post hoc.

Among 182 candidate gene IDs, 147 were tested in each of the original FI, FFS and insomnia models. Ten had BH q < 0.05 in all three separately corrected gene families (19,034, 19,015 and 19,007 genes respectively). Nine of those ten are candidate genes in the same rs34290943 region; gene associations in an LD-rich region can be correlated and should not be counted as nine independent shared loci. The tenth is FOXP2, with q = 3.67 × 10⁻⁵ for Atkins FI, 0.0291 for Ye FFS and 9.53 × 10⁻⁵ for Jansen insomnia under the Brown model. FOXP2 is a follow-up lead, not an assigned causal gene: the selected molecular comparison omits a high-PIP indel, and a gene-level test does not establish the causal variant. [FOXP2 molecular review](stage3_foxp2_pituitary_colocalization.md).

The overlap of separately significant gene tests is not a joint shared-risk test, GWAS colocalization, evidence of direction, independent replication or proof of mediation. The gene-level results may reflect different variants or shared LD, and the distinction between FI and FFS cannot be inferred from significance alone. The FFS gene discoveries are model-sensitive: the Brown approximation and default method differ in how many genes pass BH correction; see [FFS model sensitivity](stage4_ye_ffs_magma_model_sensitivity.md). FI and insomnia cell-property analyses are available in [the matched-model cell comparison](stage4_matched_brown_cell_type_comparison.md).

The Jansen console capture ended before its final progress messages, but its MAGMA analysis log explicitly records normal completion and 19,007 genes, and the gene output contains exactly 19,007 rows; the intermediate gene-results file is additionally read by the downstream MAGMA analysis. The gene-family BH calculations were checked independently against R `p.adjust`. These checks do not establish that GWAS samples are independent or that their input alleles, LD or assumptions are perfect.
