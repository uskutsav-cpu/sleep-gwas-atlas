# MAP phenotype definitions: a sleep-item-free FI candidate

[Ward et al. (2025), Table 1](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2826165) lists 41 deficits for a frailty index constructed in MAP. No item in the displayed MAP column is explicitly named sleep or insomnia. Two listed items are explicitly fatigue-related: “everything I did was an effort” (item 12) and “I could not get going” (item 40). Several other clinical, functional and mood items may relate indirectly to sleep. This is an item-label inspection of that specific index, not proof that every MAP frailty index excludes sleep, that its underlying questions are independent of sleep, or that its coding has been verified.

This specific index is a practical candidate for a **no explicitly named sleep item** primary FI, with a separately prespecified sensitivity excluding the two fatigue items and recalculating the available-item denominator and inclusion rule. Access to item-level data and confirmation of each original coding, missingness rule, time alignment, relevant variable names, and event counts are necessary. Reconstructing the phenotype and testing the project scores has not been done. The paper's MAP analytic sample of 1,451 is not the 401 sleep/genetics availability subset, and their overlap is unknown.

A separate [MAP sleep study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12046567/) describes a 32-item sleep-health and sleep-disorder-symptom questionnaire in annual assessments from 2012, with selected items from recognized sleep instruments, as well as wrist actigraphy collected in 2005–2018. Its published analytic samples and overlap with genotyped participants are separate from RADC's current availability flags. The information checked does not establish a validated clinical insomnia case definition, or that any particular insomnia item is complete in the publicly reported 401-person intersection. These two studies supply phenotype-design evidence, not a genetic score-validation result.

The [ROS/MAP feasibility review](stage6_rosmap_sleep_frailty_genetics_feasibility.md) documents public availability-flag counts, controlled access and unresolved fees, exact candidate variant coverage, and discovery-cohort overlap. In a permitted dataset, first establish whether FI items and sleep data were measured at relevant times, whether the chosen exposure overlaps FI components, whether sample size/events support the prespecified analysis, and whether any use is independent of prior score development.
