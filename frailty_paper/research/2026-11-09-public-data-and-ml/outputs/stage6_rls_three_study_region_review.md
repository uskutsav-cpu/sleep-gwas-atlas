# Third-source check of regional RLS direction differences

In the [postselected chromosome 2 comparison](stage6_rls_region_source_concordance_review.md), 12 of 42 exact non-palindromic variants significant at p < 5 × 10⁻⁸ in both the Akçimen meta-analysis and FinnGen R13 had opposite reported effect signs. We checked those same 42 variants in the public [INTERVAL RLS GWAS](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/studies/GCST90399571) from [Schormair et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01763-1). The file describes 3,491 cases and 23,741 controls, with GRCh38 harmonized summary statistics.

All 12 previously discordant variants had an exact coordinate/ref/alt result in INTERVAL, and their listed rsIDs also agreed. All 12 INTERVAL beta signs matched FinnGen rather than Akçimen; each had an unadjusted INTERVAL p < 0.05. This finding prioritizes an audit of the Akçimen regional input and cohort coding, but **does not establish that its file is wrong** or show which explanation is correct. The 12 variants can be strongly correlated, were selected because of significant results and sign differences in the first two sources, and cannot be counted as 12 independent replications. Comparisons are affected by differing phenotype definitions, ancestry, LD, ascertainment and possible participant/relative overlap. Nominal p values are descriptive and have not been adjusted for project-wide exploration.

Effect directions were aligned to the same reference/alternate alleles in the three harmonized files. This does not independently verify original per-component coding, imputation quality, phenotype construction or the cohort overlap. Source-level inputs and cohort-specific estimates remain necessary to understand the discrepancy. RLS is not FI, FFS or insomnia, and these summary rows do not test either score in individuals.

The query uses indexed byte ranges, so full-file checksum verification was not performed. The sample is a summary association dataset, not a free linked participant-level validation cohort.

A [reference LD check](stage6_rls_discordant12_reference_ld_review.md) matched all 12 discordant variants and found 10 pairwise r² values ≥ 0.8 among 66 EUR-reference pairs. This demonstrates reference correlation within the selected set, not 12 independent signals or target-cohort LD.
