# ARIC: a controlled-access sleep, genotype and frailty lead

Checked 29 October 2026. **Potentially useful linked-data route; not a publicly downloadable individual-genotype cohort, and no fee-free project-specific release or independent validation is established.**

[Wyss et al. 2026](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/advance-article/doi/10.1093/sleep/zsag200/8739310) analyzed several insomnia phenotypes and existing sleep polygenic scores in four cohorts. In ARIC, their sleep-and-genetic-score analysis included 1,550 people at Visit 4; 1,539 had a 3-question insomnia score. These counts already show that sleep and genetic score were jointly studied, but they do not establish how many of these people survived, attended and had complete frailty data at Visit 5, nor whether our 15 or 22 markers are usable. Study-specific phenotype/score sample counts can vary.

A separate [ARIC frailty study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6075198/) used a five-component Fried-style phenotype at Visit 5 (2011–2013); among 6,080 participants in that analysis, 393 were frail, 3,066 pre-frail and 2,621 robust. These are **not** counts in the sleep/genetics intersection. The phenotype includes exhaustion; an FI and a full Fried phenotype are distinct outcomes. The public [dbGaP variable catalog](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/variable.cgi?phv=515904&study_id=phs000280.v9.p3) lists Visit 5 cumulative FI components, including effort. Availability of the full FI item set and explicit sleep-item overlap must be audited before creating a sleep-excluded sensitivity measure. The Visit 4 sleep and Visit 5 physical-frailty measurements are temporally separated, with survival and attendance selection; no claim of an incident or baseline-frailty-free cohort follows.

The insomnia-score study reports that ARIC genetic and phenotype datasets are available through application to dbGaP accessions [phs000280](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs000280.v9.p3) and phs000090; sleep data are also available through the National Sleep Research Resource. [BioLINCC ARIC](https://biolincc.nhlbi.nih.gov/studies/aric/) offers data on application and states registration is free; this statement does not establish that linked genotypes, data transfer and project infrastructure are fee-free or that all desired files can be linked. dbGaP participant-level genomic data require approval and an eligible institutional investigator. Check consent group, cross-repository identifiers, fees, release coverage, approval and analysis environment. No application or data download was made.

The published PGS019928 score is not the project's selected-marker scores. Wyss et al. reported a fixed-effect meta-analysis of this score and insomnia symptom definitions across ARIC, MESA, HCHS/SOL and BHS (OR 1.17 per score SD, 95% CI 1.11–1.22). The [PGS Catalog](https://www.pgscatalog.org/score/PGS019928/) says this score has 1,069,748 variants and provides its weights; in HCHS/SOL the reported increment in variance explained for WHIIRS≥10 was 0.27 percentage points (95% CI 0.11–0.48). That incremental estimate is cohort-specific, not the meta-analysis estimate or validation of our scores, and the 2026 study did not test frailty. Its code is [public](https://github.com/tamartsi/PRS_sleep_consensus_definitions), but the linked patient data require authorization.

**Selection and overlap:** ARIC contributed to the [CHARGE gait-speed meta-analysis](https://www.aging-us.com/article/101151/text), whose results were already examined for our selected candidate markers. ARIC therefore cannot automatically be called untouched by our prior analyses. Determine the actual participants, variants and analysis history and evaluate consequences before using it as a held-out test; neither participation in CHARGE nor cohort membership proves exact subject overlap with any other source. Also check UKB, MVP, other discovery sources, relatives and phenotypic overlap. Ancestry/relatedness, imputation quality and sampling differences must be addressed. Outcome testing on a cohort used to make subsequent candidate or score choices would require a new untouched test cohort.

**FI item-label update:** the public FINDEXCOMP51 dictionary has 59 `FDI` component labels and no explicit sleep-related keyword; it includes the two exhaustion-question constructs also used in the physical phenotype. Exact FI computation and linkage remain unverified. See the [item audit](aric_fi_item_overlap_audit.md).
