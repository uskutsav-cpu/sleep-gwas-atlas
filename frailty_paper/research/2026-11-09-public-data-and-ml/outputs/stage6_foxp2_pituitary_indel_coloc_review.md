# FOXP2 pituitary colocalization: inclusion of the credible-set insertion

## Result

The previously omitted FOXP2 pituitary eQTL insertion chr7:114534306:T>TA (rs66823671) was present in both public disease GWAS and the larger gnomAD FIN and NFE LD references. It was added to the earlier 1,191-SNP analysis, giving 1,192 variants. All four unique variants in the published pituitary molecular credible set are now represented; their original source PIPs sum to approximately 0.972. Coverage of this particular set does not guarantee coverage of every possible causal variant or other signal in the window.

At p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-5, the pituitary/MVP-insomnia comparison changed from PP.H3=0.969, PP.H4=0.030 to PP.H3=0.970, PP.H4=0.029. Pituitary/FinnGen-HFRS changed from PP.H3=0.9933, PP.H4=0.00350 to PP.H3=0.9935, PP.H4=0.00337. The disease-to-disease pair remained at PP.H4≈0.980 on these inputs. Under this model, the expression signal still favors being distinct from the disease signals. These are postselected, pairwise, prior-dependent analyses with external LD; they do **not** prove that FOXP2 is uninvolved, identify another causal gene, or validate a risk score.

The insertion changes the lead of the recalculated pituitary credible set to rs66823671, with PIP≈0.292; the other two leading SNPs have PIPs≈0.289 and 0.288. Thus the source credible set is represented by several correlated alternatives, not an independently proven causal insertion. Across p12=1e-6 to 5e-5, the insertion-inclusive pituitary/MVP PP.H4 ranges 0.0030–0.131 and pituitary/HFRS PP.H4 ranges 0.00034–0.0166. The disease-pair PP.H4 ranges 0.834–0.996 over the same chosen priors. These posteriors are conditional on the chosen models and cannot be combined into a three-trait posterior.

## Matching and quality checks

The eQTL Catalogue reports identical FOXP2 eQTL estimates for two rsIDs for this exact indel; they were treated as one allele, not two independent observations. The MVP release has one T/TA record, while the HFRS source lists both rsID aliases on its one matching T/TA record. All three effects were aligned to the T allele. Position conversion and GRCh37 reference sequence were previously checked in `stage5_indel_hg19_mapping.csv`. gnomAD FIN and NFE variant indices independently contained the matching GRCh37 T>TA record. Allele-frequency differences between sources remain population- and QC-dependent; matching does not establish causal identity.

The newly retrieved signed reference LD between the insertion and rs35624276 is r=-0.665 in gnomAD FIN and r=-0.660 in gnomAD NFE after allele alignment. The FIN r²≈0.442 is similar to the separately queried SISu pairwise r²=0.468, an external plausibility check rather than proof of every matrix entry. The two expanded reference matrices are positive semidefinite to numerical tolerance (minimum eigenvalues about -2.2e-14 for FIN and 8.3e-6 for NFE). All three SuSiE-RSS fits converged; Z/LD inconsistency estimates were approximately 0.00730 for pituitary, 0.00155 for MVP and 0.00182 for HFRS. These checks have no universal pass threshold and do not establish that the external reference represents either study's in-sample LD.

The pituitary dataset has 237 samples in release metadata; its precise ancestry and overlap with disease cohorts were not independently established, and variant imputation quality was unavailable. MVP SE values are missing in the public file, so insomnia Z uses the signed reported p value and an approximate effective N. HFRS is an ICD-based construct distinct from the original FI and FFS. The analysis is restricted to a narrow window, candidate selection was post hoc, and full genome-wide source and independent clinical-prediction validation are not supplied by this test. The custom gnomAD Hail variant-index decoder remains unverified by the official Hail reader, although partition counts, sequential indices and external LD agreement were checked.

## Reproduction

`stage6_gnomad_fin_indel_index_extract.py` and `stage6_gnomad_nfe_indel_index_extract.py` match the insertion to the public gnomAD index; `stage6_foxp2_indel_gnomad_ld.py` extracts signed correlations; `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_prepare.py` builds aligned inputs and validates the two expanded matrices; `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_coloc.R` runs susieR and coloc. Results are in `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_coloc.csv`, fit diagnostics in `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_susie_diagnostics.csv`, and set members in `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_susie_members.csv`. Retrieval hashes, match information and source PIP coverage are in `stage6_foxp2_gnomad_indel_ld_receipt.json`, the per-population indel index receipts, and `stage6_foxp2_pituitary_indel_prepare_receipt.json`. Raw disease/source details and the unexpanded comparison are in `stage6_foxp2_expression_coloc_review.md` and `stage6_foxp2_gnomad_matched_coloc_review.md`.
