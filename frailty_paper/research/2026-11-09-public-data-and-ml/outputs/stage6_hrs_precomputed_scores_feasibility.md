# HRS precomputed polygenic scores: source and access check

The Health and Retirement Study (HRS) publishes a [polygenic score data product](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/polygenic-score-data-pgs), with a public [list of included scores](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products/polygenic-score-data-pgs/included-pgs) and [codebook](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/sites/default/files/documentation/codebooks/1731015343/PGENSCORE.txt). At the time checked (29 October 2026), neither the included-score list nor the codebook contained the text “sleep”; the list also had no “insomnia” or “frailty” label. It includes other phenotypes such as neuroticism and longevity. This is a label search, not proof that no indirectly related score exists or that a different release elsewhere is unavailable.

Even if accessible, existing broad scores for other traits do not reconstruct our specific 15- or 22-variant scores. Evaluating them against sleep or frailty could address a different background question but would not independently validate the project prototypes. HRS was used for prior frailty-locus and Fried-score follow-up in the project; its overlap or relatedness with all other discovery and candidate-selection sources must still be checked for any new analysis.

The product page calls the PGS release public and lists a 2024 version, but its data-files section currently instructs users either to log in with access already granted or submit a Sensitive Health Data Order Form. The older [HRS 2025 user guide](https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/sites/default/files/new-user-guide/HRS-New-User-Guide-July-2025.pdf) describes the PGS data as public. These statements do not establish the currently applicable permission steps or whether any costs apply; the product-specific conditions should be checked before use. HRS's [file-merge reference](https://hrs.isr.umich.edu/data-products/file-merge-reference) describes genetic-data linkage to HRS data products, but this does not prove all phenotypes or the intended score are present in one accessible release. No account, order, application, download or participant analysis was made.
