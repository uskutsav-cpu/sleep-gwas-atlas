# TPMI sleep-disorders lookup: chromosome 2 candidates

The TPMI PheWeb displayed two candidates from the 15-marker prototype. Their GRCh38 positions below match positive-strand Ensembl mappings of the prototype's GRCh37 coordinates; the displayed allele pairs match, but the source's effect allele has not been verified and rs35881094 has additional reference alleles elsewhere.

| Variant | Project GRCh37 position, counted/other | TPMI GRCh38 position, pair | Browser effect (SE) | P |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| [rs35881094](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/2:58695786-T-G) | 2:58922921, G/T | 2:58695786, T/G | 0.0012 (0.0080) | 0.89 |
| [rs17550821](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/2:58751388-G-A) | 2:58978523, A/G | 2:58751388, G/A | −0.040 (0.029) | 0.16 |

The browser's [sleep disorders outcome](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327) has 45,836 cases and 287,785 controls. Values are rounded. Neither is statistically clear at nominal 0.05; this does not establish no effect. This broad diagnosis differs from insomnia. Both markers were postselected, the multiple-testing burden is not prespecified, and participant/relatedness overlap with discovery and selection has not been fully verified. A single-marker screen cannot validate the score or individual prediction. Do not infer direction relative to the project counted alleles until the TPMI effect-allele convention is verified.

Browser searches for four other markers did not produce a verified exact suggestion in this pass; this is a search outcome, not proof that those variants are absent, unmeasured or null. Further direct source checks are needed. Previous lookups and download limitations are in `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.

TPMI requests acknowledgement and citation of [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x).
