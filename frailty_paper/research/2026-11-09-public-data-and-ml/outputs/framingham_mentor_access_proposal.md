# Mentor proposal: Framingham sleep–frailty genetic-score validation

**Status:** Draft for discussion; no application, restricted data access, or validation has occurred. Public metadata checked 29 October 2026. The current top-level dbGaP release is [phs000007.v36.p17](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs000007.v36.p17); verify the version and access conditions at submission.

## Proposed study

Request authorized FHS Offspring data through `phs000007` to test frozen 15- and 22-variant research score definitions against prespecified sleep and frailty outcomes. Prefer exam 8 sleep symptoms and subsequent exam 9 frailty, with baseline frailty and a same-person FI versus physical-frailty comparison if feasible. Report each endpoint separately and treat this as predictive/association validation, not proof of causality. Prespecify models, sample flow, missing variants, ancestry, family structure and all tests before inspecting target associations. Compare the frozen scores with age, sex and ancestry covariates and, if feasible, established genome-wide score comparators; estimate incremental performance and uncertainty. A score needs usable events and adequate variant quality before testing.

The existing separate NHANES result is observational and does not validate genetic scores. The proposed FHS work is not a replacement for a sleep/exhaustion-excluded frailty GWAS, which would need different scale and power.

## Variant and independence checks

The public related Omni5-4v1 probe report has position matches for 9/15 and 12/22 score markers, under an assumed GRCh37 coordinate match; it is not the actual FHS manifest. Probe name/position is not validated allelic identity, successful genotyping or imputation. Inspect the [marker crosswalk](stage6_framingham_omni5_nexus_coordinate_matches.csv), exact release build, alleles, strand, multiallelic contrast, missingness, INFO and ploidy for every candidate. Do not silently substitute proxies; any proxy-based score is a different model needing a prespecified policy and evaluation. [Full coverage audit](stage6_framingham_omni5_nexus_review.md).

The FI discovery includes UK Biobank and TwinGene; FFS discovery is UK Biobank, and insomnia discovery includes UK Biobank. FHS is a different named cohort, but participant and close-relative independence must be verified for every source and every selection/follow-up step. FHS also contributed to a CHARGE gait analysis examined after candidate selection; audit the full decision history before claiming a fully untouched test. Use pedigree or kinship-aware models, and do not count related individuals as independent.

## Illustrative power and access questions

For a single continuous FI score-association test with ten covariates, alpha 0.05 and independent complete observations, approximate power at partial R² of 0.1% / 0.5% / 1% is 17% / 61% / 88% at n=1,000 and 23% / 78% / 97% at n=1,500. These are assumptions, not estimates of the actual score, target sample, discrimination, calibration or binary-outcome power. Relatedness, missing data, sparse events and multiple testing can lower effective power. The 107 exam 8 insomnia disclosures are an upper bound only for that documented marginal field, not the number of linked genetic cases. Perform cohort-specific precision and power calculations after an approved feasibility count.

An eligible PI should confirm: (1) institutional eligibility, NIH authorization, permitted research use and data-use agreement; (2) whether requested genotype, phenotype and pedigree releases can be linked under relevant consent; (3) cohort- and exam-specific intersection, event counts and exact FI/FFS coding; (4) variant-level coverage and source/target allele conventions; (5) family and discovery overlap and any target-derived selection; (6) secure-computing and staff costs. FHS states [dbGaP and BioLINCC have no data-access service fees](https://www.framinghamheartstudy.org/fhs-for-researchers/data-available-overview/); this does not guarantee zero total cost or access approval.
