# Second public sleep-study expression inventory: GSE39445

[GSE39445](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE39445) is a public human blood transcriptome experiment including sleep extension and sleep restriction, with repeated sampling during extended wakefulness. A [prior methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5329904/) describes it as an independent young-adult sleep-expression dataset and identifies sleep extension as its control condition. This offers context for the clock-gene analysis, but has no documented FI/FFS outcomes or genotype–frailty validation set. Its PER3 genotype grouping is not equivalent to the project's 33 candidate markers.

I downloaded the public series matrix and platform metadata and constructed a descriptive probe inventory for the eight clock genes and the two score lists' positional nearest-gene labels. Exact annotation matching yielded 24 of 40 distinct requested labels and 44 probes. I restricted this preliminary calculation to the 221 sleep-extension samples with phase labels from 24 subjects. Six samples lack an explicit `subject` characteristic, but their titles contain a subject ID and `SE` condition code. Across all 427 samples with explicit subject fields, the title IDs matched those fields, with no discrepancies; the six missing IDs were therefore recovered from their titles. The script checks title–subject and title–condition consistency and fails on a mismatch. This changes the initial 215-sample output to 221 samples; confirm all metadata and original QC before a definitive analysis.

For each annotated probe I standardized expression within each person's selected observations and fitted a 24-hour harmonic with subject-centered sine and cosine terms against the supplied `circadianphase` field. The [inventory](stage6_gse39445_candidate_clock_probe_inventory.csv) includes amplitudes and peak times relative to that field's zero as **descriptive quantities**, not tests of rhythmicity; no uncertainty, multiple-testing adjustment, cross-validation, or probe-specific detection threshold was estimated. Phase-zero meaning, sampling, normalization and probe specificity require verification. MEIS1, FOXP2 and TCF4 have annotated rows, but those labels are nearest-gene annotations and this is not evidence that the candidate variants regulate them. Array platform, phase reference, subject selection and expression scales differ from GSE56931; these outputs cannot establish replication or compare numerical amplitudes across studies.

Reproduce with `python3 work/circadian_gse39445_probe_screen.py` (NumPy required).
