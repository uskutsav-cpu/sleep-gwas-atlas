# AlphaGenome Atlas: marker and matched-comparator scores

The [marker table](stage5_23_markers_atlas_avi.csv) contains the Atlas website's displayed AVI Phred scores and genome-wide SNV rankings for all 23 exploratory markers, accessed 27 October 2026. Values are as rounded by the interface. AVI is a broad predicted variant-impact score incorporating multiple features, including conservation; it is **not** an insomnia or frailty probability, a trait-specific association, proof of gene regulation, or a clinical risk score. A low score does not exclude a real molecular or disease effect.

The highest displayed marker score was rs34290943 at 11.9 (website: top 6.5% of genome-wide SNVs). This is a postselected marker in a complex LD region and must not be interpreted as a confirmed causal SNP. The selected rs323509 A→C allele displayed 1.0; this does not contradict or validate its measured testis splice QTL because AVI is a broad aggregate score.

For context, I selected [23 reference-frequency-matched comparison SNVs](stage5_matched_controls.csv). Within a 503-person European reference, all had r² ≤ 0.05 with their matched marker, MAF difference ≤ 0.05, and unadjusted p > 0.05 in each of the three available FI, FFS and insomnia GWAS inputs. Twenty-one are within ±250 kb; two required windows up to 1 Mb. Three-way p-value matching uses potentially overlapping studies. These are **comparators, not proven neutral or independent negative controls**; similar MAF and proximity do not guarantee matched local regulatory context. Selection was post hoc and SNP test resolution and LD depend on ancestry and the small reference panel.

The [paired displayed scores](stage5_avi_matched_control_comparison.csv) have medians 1.8 for the markers and 2.4 for comparators; 11 of 23 markers have higher displayed scores than their paired comparator. This is descriptive, not an inferential test of enrichment, predictive accuracy or the existence of biology. Rounding, dependence among regions and nonrandom comparator selection matter.

I also checked [11 selected molecular credible-set SNV alternatives](stage5_priority_credible_snv_atlas_avi.csv) in the rs323509, FOXP2 and SMAD5 regions. The FOXP2 insertion is not covered by the current SNV-only Atlas, despite its molecular credible-set PIP; it needs the model API. Molecular PIPs and AVI scores quantify different things and should not be substituted for one another. Full tissue-resolved gene expression and splicing model results remain necessary.
