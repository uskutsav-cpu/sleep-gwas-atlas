# CHARGE sleep-duration lead: public selected results, not a score validation

[Dashti et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405943/) report follow-up of UK Biobank sleep-duration loci in a CHARGE adult consortium GWAS (up to 47,180 individuals in 18 cohorts). The underlying [CHARGE study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4430294/) uses self-reported usual sleep duration, which is not insomnia, FI, or FFS. CHARGE was used by Dashti et al. as a replication sample for UK Biobank sleep-duration loci; that does not establish independence from all datasets and individuals used throughout this project.

The paper's publicly downloadable Supplementary Data 6 includes CHARGE estimates or proxies for selected UK Biobank sleep-duration lead loci, not arbitrary variants. An exact-identifier scan of the 124 Song candidate IDs against its 78 UKB lead rows and 47 reported CHARGE proxy identifiers found **zero matches**. This says only that these candidate IDs do not appear in those two identifier columns. It does not test the candidates in the full CHARGE GWAS, rule out associations, evaluate nearby proxies, or demonstrate a null result. Results selected on UK Biobank significance are not appropriate for assessing a full unselected score.

The [NH​LBI GRASP full-results index](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/grasp/full-results) lists a separate pediatric EAGLE sleep-duration dataset but did not identify the Gottlieb adult CHARGE full results in the searched listing. The [CHARGE results page](https://www.chargeconsortium.com/main/results) says some full summary-statistic files require authorized access through dbGaP; this does not establish the access status or availability of this exact study. No full adult file, participant-level genotype–frailty data, or score evaluation was obtained from this lead.

[Machine-readable intersection](stage6_charge_sleep_supp_intersection.json) and [reproduction script](../work/charge_sleep_supp_candidate_intersect.py) retain file hashes and counts. Supplement workbook is from the Europe PMC supplementary archive for PMC6405943. This is a useful potential data source if full association results can be located, but the selected supplementary results do not advance independent genetic score validation on their own.
