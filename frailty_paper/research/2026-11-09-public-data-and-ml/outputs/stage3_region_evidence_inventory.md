# Region-level evidence coverage

The [inventory](stage3_region_evidence_inventory.csv) summarizes the current 19 candidate-centered working groups, covering all 22 exploratory markers and rs323509. It preserves the existing provisional ordering; a separate [evidence-informed work order](stage3_evidence_informed_work_order.md) incorporates selected molecular comparisons and their limitations. The original ordering is not a set of independent loci, a causal-gene ranking, or a discovery threshold. Detailed trait statistics and the selection rule are in the [triage review](stage3_provisional_locus_triage.md).

Seventeen groups currently have at least one exact-marker GTEx v10 molecular-QTL gene in the initial table. All nineteen groups now have at least one exploratory GWAS–QTL colocalization comparison, and all nineteen have a fine-mapping note for a gene or molecular phenotype; some notes state that no credible set was reported. These are coverage counts only. They do not indicate that every gene, tissue or event in those groups has been analyzed, or that any region has a validated causal gene. ADARB1 comparisons now include the original FI and insomnia GWAS and the later Mak, Ye and FinnGen inputs. All groups need broader candidate-gene and proxy coverage beyond these selected molecular traits; the existence of one exploratory comparison does not establish complete regional analysis.

Gene lists and tissues in this inventory come from precomputed significant exact-marker GTEx queries, not all genes in the region; a blank result is not proof of no QTL or gene. Regulatory-feature counts refer to Ensembl features within 10 kb of candidate markers and do not imply an enhancer-to-gene link. The ABC columns distinguish exact-position predicted target labels from targets predicted through reference LD proxies; both are model predictions, not established allele effects or causal genes. See [the LD-proxy ABC review](stage3_abc_ld_proxy_regulatory_review.md). The [variant–gene table](stage3_initial_variant_gene_table.csv) retains tissue, eQTL/sQTL, molecular fine-mapping notes and the status of the specific comparisons. Different model windows, reference panels, phenotype definitions, missing variants and sample overlaps preclude treating all rows as equally strong evidence. No evidence count is used as a causal score.

The [batch follow-up](stage3_remaining_molecular_batch_review.md) reports the additional 66 pairwise comparisons and their limitations. Several have multiple or unreported molecular credible sets, so they must not be read as definitive colocalizations.

For rs2815764, the selected RPL31P12 query returned no rows, but a separate [NEGR1 caudate fallback](stage3_negr1_brain_colocalization.md) has since been assessed; it is a different gene/tissue choice and has no reported molecular credible set.

The two groups with no exact-marker GTEx v10 QTL return were assessed using [explicit positional fallbacks](stage3_positional_fallback_colocalization.md), whose exact-marker cortex p-values were not significant and whose molecular fine mapping remains unsupported.
