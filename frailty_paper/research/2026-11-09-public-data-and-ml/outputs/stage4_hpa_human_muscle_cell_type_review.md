# Human muscle myonuclear and fibro-adipogenic cluster analysis

To cover the requested human muscle populations, I used publicly downloadable [Human Protein Atlas cluster summaries](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/single+cell/single+cell+type/data) from **GSE233882**. The source [study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/pmid/37823262/) collected gastrocnemius biopsies from people with peripheral artery disease and non-PAD controls. The HPA cluster summaries used here are aggregated; their donor and disease contributions were not separately resolved. They are not the 387,000-cell frailty atlas and do not constitute an independent frailty-cohort analysis.

The [source metadata](stage4_hpa_skeletal_muscle_cluster_metadata.csv) lists 28 muscle clusters. I selected the 25 included in HPA's own aggregation, including **c-9 fibro-adipogenic progenitors** (738 nuclei), **c-14 myosatellite cells** (379 nuclei), and multiple myonuclear groups; some other clusters contain only tens of nuclei. All 20,162 source genes had a value for every source cluster. The [candidate expression table](stage4_hpa_muscle_candidate_expression.csv) reports 143 matched candidate IDs across the 25 selected clusters; expression is descriptive, not a genetic association. HPA's nCPM values and source labels were retained there.

For a secondary exploratory MAGMA analysis, I used log1p(nCPM) as each cluster's gene property, conditioned on the equal-cluster mean of these transformed values and the standard internal gene covariates. The equal-cluster mean is **not** a donor- or cell-weighted tissue mean. Four Brown-1df GWAS gene models were each tested against 25 clusters; BH correction used all **100 trait–cluster tests together**. All 100 regressions were estimable and none had q < 0.05. For the FAP cluster, p/q were 0.274/0.966 for Jansen insomnia, 0.584/0.966 for Atkins FI, 0.403/0.966 for Mak FI, and 0.0763/0.756 for Ye FFS. The [full property results](stage4_hpa_muscle_four_trait_cell_properties.csv) include the satellite-cell and myonuclear groups. BH was independently checked with R `p.adjust`.

These post hoc results are sensitive to source selection, the transformation and baseline, small cluster sizes, sample disease status, expression sparsity, shared LD and GWAS input differences. A lack of significance is not proof of no biological involvement. No candidate variant, single donor or mechanism is tested here, and there is no formal between-trait or frailty-status comparison. The [preparation receipt](stage4_hpa_muscle_preparation_receipt.json), [source receipt](stage4_hpa_muscle_source_receipt.json) and [analysis receipt](stage4_hpa_muscle_gene_property_receipt.json) record inputs and commands.

The multiple-testing correction in this report applies to its stated exploratory family only; it does not correct across all of the project's prior models, matrices, locus selections or other analyses.
