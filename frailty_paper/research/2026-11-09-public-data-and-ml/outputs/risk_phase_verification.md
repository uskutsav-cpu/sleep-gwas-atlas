Outstanding scientific work is not hidden by this verification: the modified sleep-excluded FI summary statistics were not found in the inspected releases, causal signals and genes have not been established, important multiallelic representations require source-level confirmation, and the score has not been evaluated or calibrated in independent participants. Detailed single-cell and functional analyses remain future work under the user's sequencing instruction.
