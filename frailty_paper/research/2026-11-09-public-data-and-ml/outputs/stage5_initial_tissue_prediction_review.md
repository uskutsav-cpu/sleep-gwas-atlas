# Initial tissue-specific Atlas observations

These are **website-displayed rounded values**, transcribed from the official AlphaGenome Atlas on 27 October 2026. They are targeted checks, not complete API results. The exact rows and source links are in [the observations](stage5_initial_atlas_tissue_observations.csv).

- **rs12536335, FOXP2, pituitary:** the web interface lists a polyA RNA-seq gene score of `0.00` at two-decimal display precision. GTEx v10 reports a positive eQTL normalized effect for the G alternate allele (p = 4.70 × 10⁻⁸). Because the displayed model value is rounded, it is not possible to determine the predicted sign or assess directional agreement. GTEx is also not fully independent of the model's training resources.
- **rs7734045, SMAD5, naïve CD4 T cells:** the interface lists polyA and total RNA gene scores of `0.00` at two-decimal precision; neither establishes a zero effect or allows a signed comparison. The OneK1K cell eQTL reports ALT-C beta 0.673 and molecular PIP 0.095. In the previous regional test, the Mak FI comparison favored **distinct** signals under its baseline model, whereas other disease tests were largely QTL-only; missing variants and assumptions limit that conclusion. [CD4 colocalization review](stage4_cd4_smad5_colocalization_review.md).
- For the same rs7734045 variant, the interface lists a naïve CD4 ATAC score of `0.01` and DNase score of `0.02`, again rounded to two decimals. These are local predicted accessibility effects, not direct predictions of SMAD5 expression or proof that accessibility mediates the QTL or disease phenotype. The cell label is similar to the OneK1K cell label but exact sample identity is not established.

The web UI's “Quantile score” column displays values whose scaling has not been reconciled with the official API quantiles. The raw and displayed values are preserved without converting them to probabilities. Full-precision RNA and three splicing scorers, with explicit track metadata, are still needed before classifying prediction–QTL agreement. The [rs323509 testis observation](stage5_rs323509_atlas_initial_observations.md) likewise cannot be compared directionally to its measured splice event from rounded expression scores.
