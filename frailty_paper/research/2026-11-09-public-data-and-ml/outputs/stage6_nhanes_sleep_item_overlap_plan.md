# NHANES sleep-item overlap illustration

Written after prior results were observed, before fitting this calculation. Restrict each NHANES 2011–12/2013–14 examined age-60+ sample to valid SLQ050, valid DPQ040 fatigue response, and >=20 observed items of the previously defined 24-item adapted index. Compare two age- and sex-adjusted survey-weighted regressions in the identical participants: (1) 24-item index with sleep and fatigue excluded, and (2) a 26-item index obtained by adding SLQ050 (yes=1, no=0) and DPQ040 (0=0, 1–3=1). Divide each count by available items; no additional missingness allowed for the two added items. In the second index the exposure is itself an outcome component: this is a deliberately circular descriptive illustration of measurement overlap, not independent validation or evidence of causality. It is not a reconstruction of the original Atkins FI or an estimate of the difference in genome-wide genetic correlations. Other items can also overlap symptoms. Do not treat the two scales' coefficients as a formal causal decomposition or compare statistical-significance labels.
