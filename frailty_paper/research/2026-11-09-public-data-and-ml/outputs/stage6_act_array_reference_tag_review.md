# ACT array: reference-panel tag feasibility

The prior [manufacturer-manifest screen](stage6_act_array_manifest_review.md) found direct rsID-and-position matches for 4 of the 15-score markers and 5 of the 22-score markers on one Illumina Human660W-Quad v1_H design. To assess whether array probes near the other candidates might provide a starting point for a future authorized analysis, I searched probes within ±100 kb in that manifest and compared their genotypes with each target in the existing 503-person 1000 Genomes European reference used by the project.

Among the **11 candidates without a direct match in the 15-marker set**, six have a nearby manifest probe with reference dosage r² ≥ 0.8. Among the **17 without a direct match in the 22-marker set**, 12 have such a probe. Including direct manifest matches, that is 10/15 and 17/22 candidates with either type of *design-level* match. These sets overlap across scores and are not independent findings. [Candidate-level table](stage6_act_full_reference_tags.csv); [receipt](stage6_act_full_reference_tags.json); reproduce from the project directory with `python3 work/act_660w_reference_tag_screen.py`.

Each candidate anchor matched the reference by GRCh37 position and unordered allele pair. Candidate tags were required to match array and reference by position and allele pair under either direct or complementary strand, with at least 400 paired nonmissing reference genotypes. The strongest tag was selected in the same reference panel used to estimate r²; this descriptive selection is not an independent evaluation. r² does not give the signed allele relationship. Manifest allele orientation is unverified, especially for palindromic pairs, and neither the exact array revision, post-QC probe availability, sample calls, ACT ancestry-specific LD nor imputation quality is established. Several variants in the complete scores remain without a direct match or a selected r² ≥ 0.8 tag under this limited search; a wider window or imputation could differ.

The 0.8 threshold is a heuristic, not a guarantee of an acceptable substitute. Swapping tags changes the score and requires a separately specified, allele-oriented method, target-level quality checks and validation. The 1000 Genomes reference is not ACT and contains no project outcomes. This analysis does not validate prediction, estimate the performance of an imputed score, establish ACT linkage or demonstrate free access.
