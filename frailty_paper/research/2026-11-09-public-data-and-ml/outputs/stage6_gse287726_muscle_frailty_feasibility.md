# Cardiac-surgery frailty muscle dataset: public-data feasibility

[GSE287726](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE287726) provides public RNA-seq counts for quadriceps muscle from 70 patients aged at least 70 undergoing cardiac surgery or TAVI. The [source study](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.13846) assessed physical function, including gait speed, timed up-and-go and grip strength. It is a potentially informative but clinically selected cohort, not a general-population sample or an FI/FFS genetic-score validation cohort.

The downloaded count matrix and GEO series metadata contain 70 matching, unique sample titles. The matrix includes rows with matching Ensembl IDs and symbols for all four prior candidate genes: FOXP2, SMAD5, RBM6 and MEIS1. However, the GEO sample-characteristic fields supply only tissue and `age: 70+`, not per-sample frailty/physical-function measurements or sex. A search of the publisher supplement's Word XML for the first GEO sample identifier did not find it; this limited check does not prove that phenotype data are unavailable elsewhere. Inferring individual frailty from titles or sequencing counts would be invalid. We therefore did not perform a case–control comparison or claim this cohort replicates the two previous muscle analyses.

There is no established linked individual-level genotype or insomnia data in these files, so even with physical-function labels the RNA-seq study would provide expression context rather than score discrimination or calibration. A usable follow-up would need documented, permitted per-sample phenotype and covariate linkage, a prespecified analysis appropriate to count data, and study/participant independence checks. Differences in cardiovascular disease, frailty ascertainment, tissue and processing also matter when comparing to the prior studies.

Metadata and count files were retrieved from the official GEO FTP directory; SHA-256 values and aggregate inventory are in `outputs/stage6_gse287726_data_audit.json`. Reproduce with `work/geo_muscle/audit_gse287726.py` using `work/geo_muscle/GSE287726.txt.gz` and `work/geo_muscle/GSE287726_counts.tsv.gz`. The publisher supplementary archive was inspected separately; no per-participant result was retained.
