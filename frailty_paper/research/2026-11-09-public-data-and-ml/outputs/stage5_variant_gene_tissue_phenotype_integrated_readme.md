# Integrated variant–gene–tissue–phenotype evidence table

`stage5_variant_gene_tissue_phenotype_integrated.csv` joins the existing molecular evidence atlas to the six-study GWAS matrix on the exact GRCh38 REF/ALT variant key. It has 1,351 molecular-evidence or placeholder rows for all 488 queued variants; 1,325 rows include a gene ID or symbol. Each row retains its original QTL, tissue, credible-set, regulatory and prediction-status fields and adds match status, ALT-oriented beta, standard error and p-value for Atkins FI, Jansen insomnia, Mak FI, Mak physical frailty, Ye FFS and FinnGen R13 insomnia. Molecular records for the same variant, including records possibly duplicated across collections, inherit the same GWAS information; these repeated values must not be counted as independent disease observations.

GWAS p-values are available for 394, 394, 429, 429, 430 and 474 variants, respectively. Empty fields mean unavailable or unaligned, not absence of association or an effect of zero. Source-specific matching, exclusions, genome-build conversion and allele-direction limitations remain as documented in `stage5_variant_multitrait_gwas_matrix_readme.md` and `stage5_indel_grch37_gwas_readme.md`. Mak physical frailty and Ye FFS are related physical-frailty measures from separate analyses, not the deficit-accumulation FI; effect estimates across studies use different scales and sample sets, which can overlap. Do not compare beta magnitudes directly or treat correlated results as independent replications.

The joined table reports measured associations and context, not a test that a molecular and phenotype signal share a causal variant. A nearby or QTL-linked gene is a candidate, not an established effector; cell or tissue labels are those of the source measurements, not proof of the disease-relevant cell type. In particular, the rs323509-region testis splice evidence is tissue-specific. Interpret FOXP2 and SMAD5 regional colocalization results using the linked review files; molecular credible-set PIP is not disease PIP. No full-precision AlphaGenome RNA, splice or chromatin outputs have been generated, so prediction-versus-QTL concordance remains unassessable. The AVI score is a general variant-impact measure, not predicted disease risk or a tissue-specific effect.

To reproduce, run `python3 outputs/stage5_integrate_qtl_and_phenotypes.py` from any directory. The script checks exact equality of the queue, GWAS and molecular variant-key sets before performing a many-to-one join. Its inputs are `stage5_variant_gene_tissue_evidence_atlas.csv`, `stage5_variant_multitrait_gwas_matrix.csv`, and `stage5_score_queue.csv`; the source readmes contain provenance and deeper limitations. Do not use the duplicated rows as independent observations in statistical tests.
