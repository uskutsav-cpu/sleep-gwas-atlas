# Sensitivity of the projected FI–FFS contrast to individual variants

The [previous All of Us EUR summary-data comparison](stage6_aou_eur_fi_ffs_paired_contrast_review.md) gave an exploratory projected insomnia contrast Z = 2.82 for the 15-marker FI- versus FFS-weighted sums. To test sensitivity, we recomputed both projected statistics and their reference-LD covariance after removing each variant in turn. This changes and restandardizes both sums; it is not a test of the original fixed score, an optimization exercise, or evidence that a marker should be removed.

Omitting rs113851554, near MEIS1, reduces the contrast from 2.82 to 1.39 (nominal two-sided p from 0.0048 to 0.165). The corresponding projected insomnia Z values change from 6.79 to 6.38 for the FI-weighted sum and from 5.00 to 5.58 for the FFS-weighted sum. In the original inputs, the T allele has opposite signs in those two weight sets and a strong insomnia association, so its removal changes their contrast substantially. This is consistent with the original result being sensitive to that marker; it does not establish a causal mechanism or the effect of removing it on individual prediction. The FFS sign is inferred, and rs113851554 has multiallelic representations requiring exact contrast verification. Its prior FI and FFS marginal associations were not statistically clear; its sleep association alone is not proof of shared frailty risk.

For context, omitting all four chromosome 2 variants yields contrast Z = 1.78 (nominal p = 0.075); those four are not one established causal locus. Across single-variant omissions the Z range is 1.39–3.16. The selected omissions and p values are post hoc; no distinction based on crossing 0.05 demonstrates an actual difference between models. Remaining linked variants can tag an omitted variant, and the 503-person European reference need not match the target LD. Results also inherit all assumptions and limitations of the original projection, including external LD, approximate common effective sample size, HWE, allele orientation, and unknown discovery overlap. They neither compare actual FI against FFS nor validate prediction, discrimination or calibration.

[All omission results](stage6_aou_eur_paired_contrast_omission.csv) and the [hash receipt](stage6_aou_eur_paired_contrast_omission_receipt.json) are reproducible with `/opt/miniconda3/bin/python3 work/aou_complete/eur_fi_ffs_leaveone_sensitivity.py`. Missing or non-significant outcomes are not evidence of no shared effect. An independent linked-genotype cohort remains necessary.

A [reference-only bootstrap](stage6_aou_paired_reference_bootstrap_review.md) found that the decrease in the contrast after omitting rs113851554 persisted across 5,000 resamples of the 503-person reference. This does not account for uncertainty in the target GWAS, postselection, or mismatch between the reference and target cohort.
