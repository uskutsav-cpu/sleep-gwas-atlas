# KARE aging cohort: validation feasibility

29 October 2026. The [Kunshan Aging Research with E-health (KARE) profile](https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyaf041) describes an older Chinese cohort with linked genotyping-array and annual electronic-health records. The profile reports 55,943 people as of December 2023 and approximately 650,000 directly genotyped array markers. It lists age-related activities of daily living and clinical diagnoses, among other data. The cohort is scientifically relevant to aging and could potentially inform evaluation outside the predominantly European discovery samples.

It is **not an established free public validation dataset**. The [Atlas of Longitudinal Datasets](https://atlaslongitudinaldatasets.ac.uk/datasets/kare), based on information available in May 2026, says access requires contact with the study team and that a secure platform under a data-access agreement is being developed. It lists no data-access link or fee information. This source reports 135,787 participants as of 2025, but neither this total nor the earlier profile count proves the number with usable genotypes and the necessary outcomes. The profile's list of aging measures does not itself establish a validated FI or Fried frailty score, a usable insomnia phenotype, individual overlap, candidate-variant coverage or imputation quality. An EHR sleep-disorder code, if available, would not automatically be equivalent to the discovery insomnia measure. Ascertainment and relationship to discovery data also need evaluation.

A later [KARE genetic analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12870439/) says its data can be requested subject to ethics and data-protection rules, which does not establish an anonymous download or a no-cost arrangement. KARE is a future collaboration lead only; it cannot currently be used to validate either project score under the user's free-public-data constraint. No contact or data request was made.
