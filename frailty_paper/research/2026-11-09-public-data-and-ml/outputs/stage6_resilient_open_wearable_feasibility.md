# RESILIENT open wearable dataset: phenotype and coding audit

Checked 29 October 2026. The [RESILIENT Zenodo release](https://zenodo.org/records/16755408) is openly downloadable; its API identifies a CC BY 4.0 license. It describes an aging-related comorbidity study with sleep-mat states and physiology, watch steps and heart rate, and mental-health and cognitive assessments. It does not list individual genotypes, an insomnia diagnosis, FI or Fried frailty. It cannot validate the project's genetic scores or support a direct FI-versus-FFS comparison. The released PHQ-9 sleep question covers trouble falling/staying asleep **or sleeping too much**, so it is not a clinical insomnia diagnosis. Its tiredness item is not a full frailty phenotype. Self-report and devices measure different constructs; selecting a fatigue item from the same questionnaire as a proxy for frailty risks circular interpretation.

We retrieved the public demographics CSV and the metadata ZIP and checked their MD5 hashes against Zenodo. The demographics file has 73 rows; all 73 have a nonempty PHQ-9 sleep item and 72 have a nonempty tiredness item. These are availability counts, not a validated analytic sample or insomnia/frailty case counts. Their intersection, timestamps and device coverage need verification for any actual analysis. The release page describes participant-linked device data, but we did not download or examine the time series. Per-person repeated observations are not independent samples, and temporal and participant-level splits are essential for any model evaluation. The release warns that the six-month cognitive scores were undergoing further QC; later release status should be checked.

## Data-dictionary inconsistency

The released dictionary describes “nearly every day” as 3 for PHQ item 1 but 4 through 11 for items 2–9. The standard [NIH PHQ-9 scoring](https://cde.nlm.nih.gov/deView?tinyId=Qy_ihKG8km) assigns 3 to that response for every item. In the actual CSV, all 69 rows with all nine item values present have values in 0–3; all 69 available totals equal the nine-item sum. This supports—but does not prove—that the inconsistent dictionary labels are a documentation error rather than special coding. Do not automatically recode values 4–11 from that dictionary; obtain authoritative clarification and check all missing values before analysis. Counts do not verify every item response, instrument version, assessment process or clinical interpretation.

The [aggregate receipt](stage6_resilient_phenotype_metadata_audit.json) records hashes, availability and total-score checks without participant identifiers. Reproduce with `python3 work/resilient/audit_demographics.py` after downloading the listed files into `work/resilient/`. This resource may inform future sleep and wearable-feature methodology within its terms, but is small and does not address the central genetic-validation bottleneck. No outcome association or predictive model was fitted.
