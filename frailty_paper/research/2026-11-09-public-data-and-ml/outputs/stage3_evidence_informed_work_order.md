# Evidence-informed locus work order

The [ranked work order](stage3_evidence_informed_work_order.csv) brings together all 19 provisional groups, including all 22 exploratory markers and rs323509. It lists the previous marginal-GWAS triage statistics, the selected molecular gene and tissue, reported molecular credible-set coverage, and separate baseline H4 values for Atkins FI, Jansen insomnia and Ye FFS where available. It is a **post hoc order for follow-up work**, not a ranking of causal genes, confirmed shared loci, individual risk or clinical importance.

The first priority is **rs323509**, because the selected testis splice junction has one reported four-variant molecular credible set, all four variants matched both original GWAS inputs, and the two separate baseline H4 values were ≥0.7. This rule is a practical screening choice, not a calibrated probability of three-trait sharing. The FI H4 is prior-sensitive, the Ye FFS baseline H4 for the same junction is only 0.087, and the testis context is not established as relevant to either trait. FI and insomnia also overlap in UK Biobank participants and sleep-related phenotype content. These limitations argue for multi-signal and independent-data checks, not a causal or shared-risk claim.

The second priority is the **rs9536347/rs1909650 group**, because the selected anterior-cingulate `ENSG00000279325` QTL has Atkins FI and Jansen insomnia baseline H4 values both above 0.5. This is weaker molecular support: **no credible set was reported** in the selected Catalogue file, signal multiplicity is unknown, and the Ye FFS baseline H4 is 0.008. It is a gene/tissue hypothesis for follow-up, not a replicated shared locus. The GTEx sources are related and the GWAS are not independent.

All other groups with an exact-marker molecular-QTL lead or a separately assessed fallback are in tier 3, ordered by the earlier candidate-centered three-p-value triage rule rather than an invented causal score. rs9845387 and rs4886140 are in tier 4 because the exact-marker GTEx v10 QTL screen returned no significant rows and the selected positional cortex fallbacks had non-significant nominal marker p-values and no reported molecular credible sets. That does not establish absence of other genes, tissues or LD-proxy effects.

The thresholds, tissue selections and ordering were chosen **after inspecting these data**. H4 values depend on priors, selected windows, credible-set coverage and the single-signal assumption; two pairwise H4 values do not form a joint posterior. Some groups contain multiple candidate markers: the `representative_gwas_marker` for the earlier p-value triage may differ from the `molecular_anchor_marker`; their rows must not be read as one variant-specific result. The table uses the source's original effect and p-value evidence, not a cross-phenotype sum, and makes unavailable FFS molecular comparisons explicit. See the [original-GWAS comparison](stage3_original_gwas_coloc_review.md), [batch review](stage3_remaining_molecular_batch_review.md), and [positional fallbacks](stage3_positional_fallback_colocalization.md) for sensitivity ranges and limitations.

A stronger priority assessment needs unbiased regional fine mapping with ancestry-matched LD, multiple-signal colocalization and relevant cell-type evidence, with sample overlap and the FI sleep item addressed. For rs323509 in particular, resolving allele representation and evaluating whether a relevant non-testis tissue shares the molecular effect are higher priorities than interpreting the model score as risk.

For the selected rs323509 gene, [identifier context](stage3_rs323509_gene_identifier_context.md) links ENSG00000251574 to an uncharacterized noncoding RNA in NCBI; this does not supply a known sleep or frailty mechanism.

The four reported rs323509-region testis molecular credible-set variants have pairwise reference dosage r² of 0.982–1.000 in 503 European reference individuals; see [the LD check](stage3_rs323509_molecular_credible_set_reference_ld.md). These are molecular, not shared-trait, credible variants.
