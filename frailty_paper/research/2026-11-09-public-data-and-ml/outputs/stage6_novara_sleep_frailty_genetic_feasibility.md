# Novara Cohort Study: sleep, frailty and genetic data lead

Checked 29 October 2026. The [2026 Novara Cohort Study (NCS) profile](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-109328) describes a population-based Northern Italian aging cohort with protocol measures relevant to this project. This is **not an open genotype download or a confirmed free score-validation sample**.

* **Sleep:** The protocol lists Pittsburgh Sleep Quality Index, Epworth Sleepiness Scale and STOP-Bang measures; the [earlier NCS paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12102150/) also describes sleep questionnaire items. These are sleep quality, sleepiness and apnea-related measures, not automatically equivalent to the insomnia GWAS phenotype.
* **Frailty:** The current protocol lists a 40-item deficit FI and Clinical Frailty Scale, as well as grip strength, gait speed and other functional tests. The profile does not establish a fully derived five-component Fried phenotype or how many participants have all the required inputs. Before a sleep–FI analysis, check whether any of the exact 40 deficits include sleep, exhaustion or related symptoms and define an excluded-item sensitivity if needed.
* **Genetics:** The profile reports SNP-array genotyping completed for **500 participants** and describes planned whole-genome sequencing for 1,000, not completed sequencing. It reports about 1,000 total enrolled as of mid-2025 and a planned 10,000; neither figure establishes the current genotyped sleep–frailty overlap, score-marker imputation quality or event counts. The cohort includes adults from age 18, so the number of genotyped older adults is unknown; small effective sample size may severely limit precision.
* **Access:** The profile describes approved proposals for qualified researchers, ethical approval, a data-use agreement, and possible restrictions on sensitive data. It does not establish a no-fee route or an unrestricted public download. Current costs, eligibility, supported analysis environment and whether linkage includes the genetic subset must be verified. No application or contact was made.

## Decision

NCS is a promising **future controlled-access lead** because the protocol includes the relevant modalities within one study. It is not yet a solution to the user's free-public-data requirement. Before any score evaluation, establish cost and authorization, exact common participants and measurement waves, FI item definitions, candidate variants and imputation quality, source-allele contrasts, ancestry and discovery-cohort/relative overlap. Freeze score weights and primary endpoints before analysis; report uncertainty and avoid training a flexible model on this small provisional sample. The score has not been computed in NCS.

## Additional age and platform check

Table 1 of the [2026 cohort profile](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2025-109328) lists 246 wave-1 participants aged 70–79 and 34 aged 80 or older: **280 of 961 wave-1 participants aged at least 70** (about 29.1%). It separately reports 46 participants aged at least 70 in the 133-person pilot. These are cohort counts, not counts among the 500 genotyped people; whether the pilot and wave-1 individuals overlap and which participants have genetic data require confirmation. The number with genotype, sleep and FI all present can only be smaller than or equal to its relevant underlying cohort and may be much smaller. The study was not designed solely for older adults, reinforcing the need for a precision and event-count assessment before claiming validation.

The profile specifies Illumina SNP arrays but does not identify an exact array model, manifest, imputation panel, genome build, or variant-level quality information. Consequently, a meaningful exact-marker coverage calculation cannot yet be performed. The article cites the published Italian frailty-index validation, but does not provide enough detail in its text to establish that Novara used exactly the same item set or whether the actual FI contains sleep-related deficits. The Europe PMC supplementary-file download attempt timed out, so no conclusion about its contents is drawn. These are specific information gaps, not evidence that the variants or measures are absent.
