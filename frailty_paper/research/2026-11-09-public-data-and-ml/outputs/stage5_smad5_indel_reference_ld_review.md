# Public reference LD for SMAD5 splice-set indels

The existing project-wide MAGMA 1000 Genomes European SNP panel did not have exact allele matches for the eight indels in the selected SMAD5 muscle-splice credible sets. A separate indexed lookup of the public [1000 Genomes Phase 3 GRCh37 chromosome 5 VCF](https://hgdownload.soe.ucsc.edu/gbdb/hg19/1000Genomes/phase3/) found unique exact REF/ALT records for seven of those eight. The eighth, chr5:136198951 C>CA in GRCh38, had no exact record at its mapped and normalized GRCh37 representation; another representation or a different reference panel has not been excluded. See the [inventory](stage5_smad5_indel_reference_inventory.csv) and [source receipt](stage5_smad5_indel_reference_ld_receipt.json).

For the seven matched indels, the [pairwise table](stage5_smad5_indel_reference_pairwise_ld.csv) reports ALT-dosage Pearson r and r² separately in 503 European-reference samples and 99 Finnish-reference samples. The European group includes the Finnish subset, so the two are not independent panels. Pairwise r² ranges approximately 0.032–1.000 in the European set and 0.006–1.000 in the Finnish subset. These ranges include different signal pairs and are descriptive; they do not prove a shared or distinct disease association. The [public reference dosages](stage5_smad5_indel_reference_eur_dosages.csv) are included, and all 42 signed correlations were independently recomputed from that table to within 2.3 × 10⁻¹⁶.

This is **not a full locus LD matrix** and does not support a completed multi-signal colocalization. One indel lacks an exact reference match, additional SNPs and their allele alignment are needed, and sample-specific LD may differ materially from 503 EUR or 99 FIN reference individuals. Low-frequency variants in the small Finnish subset have unstable LD estimates. The original QTL and GWAS sample ancestries, imputation and phenotype overlap still require assessment. No AlphaGenome prediction or disease mediation claim follows from reference LD.

The reproduction script, source index and public sample panel are saved alongside the outputs. It requires `pysam`, NumPy, the saved Stage 5 mapping/credible-set tables, and a network-accessible indexed source VCF; the script's `work/` paths should be adjusted if run elsewhere.
