# All of Us: full regional results for selected project variants

A public [All by All browser](https://allbyall.researchallofus.org/) regional endpoint provides more complete data than the significant-only endpoint previously queried. Using the **genome**, **meta** (within-All-of-Us across genetic similarity groups), **slow Hail** path, we retrieved exact chromosome/position/REF/ALT rows for all **33 distinct project variants** for each of two CDR v8 PhecodeX outcomes: insomnia (`NS_333.2`) and generalized muscle weakness (`SS_807.21`). This is a real summary-level association follow-up, **not individual-level score validation**. Browser-reported case/control totals are 26,227/253,797 for insomnia and 7,521/275,944 for weakness; actual per-variant and genetic-group contributions may differ.

| Outcome | Matched rows | Nominal p < 0.05 | BH q < 0.05 across 33 candidates |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Insomnia | 33 | 5 | 2 |
| Generalized weakness | 33 | 1 | 0 |

For insomnia, rs113851554 had p = 1.36 × 10⁻⁸ (q = 4.49 × 10⁻⁷) and rs12536335 had p = 3.32 × 10⁻⁵ (q = 5.48 × 10⁻⁴). They also pass BH within the combined family of 66 outcome/variant rows. No weakness result passed either correction. BH correction is descriptive for these **postselected** candidates and two chosen outcomes; it does not account for the project's many other searches and comparisons. Not passing a threshold is not evidence of no effect. A weakness diagnosis is not an FI, Fried frailty score, measured grip, or a joint sleep–frailty outcome; this analysis does not identify shared causal risk, compare score performance, or estimate prediction, discrimination or calibration.

These source results use a multi-group All of Us meta-analysis; ascertainment, genetic ancestry and case definitions differ from the project's predominantly European discovery sources. Candidate selection used UK Biobank, MVP and FinnGen results; All of Us was also subsequently included in the project's inspected clinical-insomnia meta-analysis, so the newly queried source and that meta-analysis are not independent of each other. Participant and family overlap with all discovery and selection sources remains unverified. The two insomnia hits require confirmation of discovery independence, allele contrasts, genomic build and assay quality before being called independent replication. The MEIS1-region rs113851554 has a strong separate association with restless legs syndrome and is multiallelic in broader references; its insomnia result is not proof of an insomnia-specific mechanism. The rs12536335 annotation near FOXP2 is a positional association, not evidence of a causal gene.

## Retrieval, checks and caveats

The endpoint was explicitly queried with `query_mode=slow`, `ancestry_group=meta`, `sequencing_type=genome`, using a short interval around each previously mapped GRCh38 coordinate. We required `storage_source=hail_gcs`, the requested phenotype, group and sequencing type, and exact REF/ALT variant identity, and checked valid p and positive SE. A manual positive-control request for rs12536335 agreed with a separate public region overlay. The [browser source at version 317fe0c](https://github.com/broadinstitute/all-by-all-aou-browser/blob/317fe0c/axaou-server/src/variants/annotations.rs) documents the slow path and explains why the prior lookup was incomplete. Its conversion replaces missing AF with 0.0; a zero AF must not be interpreted as an observed frequency without checking the original data. Source beta-to-effect-allele conventions and multiallelic representations require further verification before harmonizing directions or combining statistics. We did not fit a weighted-Z statistic using a mismatched population LD reference.
