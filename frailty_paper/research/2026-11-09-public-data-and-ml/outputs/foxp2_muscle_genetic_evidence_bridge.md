# FOXP2-region muscle follow-up: what the public evidence connects

This synthesis addresses the project's variant–gene–cell question without treating expression, molecular QTLs and disease associations as interchangeable.

| Evidence | What was observed | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| Disease-region follow-up | Prior regional modeling found conditional evidence for sharing an insomnia and hospital-frailty association signal under some settings. [Regional review](stage6_foxp2_gnomad_matched_coloc_review.md). | A common causal variant, FOXP2 as the effector gene, or individual score prediction. HFRS is distinct from FI and FFS, and results depend on LD, variant coverage and priors. |
| Type-I muscle-fiber chromatin | In Varshney's [public skeletal-muscle resource](https://zenodo.org/records/19670721), the project candidate rs12536335 at GRCh38 `chr7:114403104 A/G` occurs in two reported molecular credible sets. Its source PIP is about 0.0516 for the peak labeled `chr7:114416186:114416487` and 0.000804 for the peak labeled `chr7:114507657:114507958`. [Original screen](stage6_varshney_skeletal_muscle_qtl_review.md). | These PIPs are conditional on each molecular fine-mapping model, not disease-sharing probabilities. The candidate is outside both listed peak intervals. They do not establish that the candidate changes FOXP2 expression, whether the same signal drives disease, a direction of effect, or that the peaks act on FOXP2. |
| Bulk human muscle expression | In GSE167186, an exploratory age-adjusted edgeR model found higher FOXP2 expression in 24 sarcopenia-labeled versus 29 older healthy samples (log2 fold change +0.402; BH p=0.029 across four selected genes). [Analysis](stage6_gse167186_skeletal_muscle_frailty_review.md). | This is bulk expression in a small selected cohort, not a variant–expression association, cell-type result, FI/FFS outcome or an insomnia measure. Gene and analysis choices were postselected. |
| Separate muscle comparison | GSE144304's full older sample had an age/sex-adjusted FOXP2 log2 fold change of −0.00565 (p=0.959) using a similar edgeR model; a male-only post hoc sensitivity gave −0.226 (p=0.077). [Analysis](stage6_gse144304_muscle_candidate_expression_review.md). | No observed replication is not evidence that the effect is zero or a formal test of between-study differences. The groups, designs and adjustment differ; participant overlap remains unverified. |
| FOXP2 molecular colocalization elsewhere | Existing pituitary and brain eQTL follow-ups did not consistently support colocalization with the modeled disease signals under tested settings. [Review](stage6_foxp2_expression_coloc_review.md). | These other tissues and limited variant intersections cannot rule out a muscle mechanism or conclusively exclude FOXP2. |

The current public findings support a **testable hypothesis**: a FOXP2-region variant might influence muscle regulatory activity. They do not form a demonstrated variant → FOXP2 expression → cell function → frailty pathway. Next, obtain full cis-QTL statistics and peak-to-gene links for the same cell context, validate variant build, alleles, quality and sample overlap, and perform multi-signal disease–molecular colocalization with matched LD and coverage. Experimental perturbation would be needed to evaluate an allele-specific mechanism. A 29 October 2026 request to the linked [CMDGA search](https://cmdga.org/search/?searchTerm=stephen-parker%3AVarshney2024) returned HTTP 403 from this environment; that is an access observation, not evidence that the files do not exist. The openly downloaded credible-set archive remains usable, and no new molecular association was computed here.

Finally, even a convincing variant-to-gene result would not validate the project's 15- or 22-marker scores. That requires independent, permitted individual-level genotype and outcome data and a predictive evaluation.
