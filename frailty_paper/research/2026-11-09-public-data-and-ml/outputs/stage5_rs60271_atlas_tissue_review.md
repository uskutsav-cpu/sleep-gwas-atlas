# rs60271: a fine-mapped alternative in the rs323509 region

The reference-checked variant chr5:104742532 A>C (rs60271) is a member of the testis molecular sQTL credible set for ENSG00000251574, with molecular PIP approximately 0.346 in the project's retained source. This PIP concerns the molecular phenotype in that tissue; it is not a posterior probability of causality for insomnia or frailty.

On 27 October 2026, the [AlphaGenome Atlas](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?q=chr5%3A104742532%3AA%3EC) reported a rounded AVI Phred of 1.6. In its Add track table, three RNA-seq rows matched ENSG00000251574 and testis, with raw scores displayed as 0.00 and quantile scores of -6.75, -5.20, and -4.47. Two rows share the same visible polyA-plus assay label; they are retained separately, but the displayed labels do not identify their underlying track identities. Testis DNase and CAGE entries are also recorded. The exact visible fields are in [the observations table](stage5_rs60271_atlas_tissue_observations.csv).

**Interpretation:** The raw RNA values are rounded to two decimal places, so no effect direction, exact magnitude, or absence of effect can be inferred. The quantile column's meaning and comparability to API scores have not been reconciled; its sign should not be equated with the measured QTL's ALT effect. The available eQTL Catalogue record is a testis sQTL for intron event `5:104739412:104739497:clu_21568_-`, with ALT effect -0.767361 and p = 4.74323e-21. An RNA expression score and an event-specific sQTL measure different molecular outcomes; they are not an event-matched validation. Relevant brain or other sleep-related cell-type involvement is not established.

The Atlas website did not provide the required separate splice-site, splice-site-usage, and junction scores in this observation. Full-precision, tissue- and event-matched scoring remains needed, alongside evidence independent of model training and colocalization with the disease traits.
