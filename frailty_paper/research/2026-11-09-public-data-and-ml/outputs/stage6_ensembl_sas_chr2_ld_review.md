# Preliminary South Asian reference lookup for chromosome 2 markers

In the public Ensembl GRCh37 [1000 Genomes Phase 3 South Asian lookup](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/ld/human/pairwise/rs35881094/rs17550821?content-type=application/json;population_name=1000GENOMES:phase_3:SAS), the rs35881094–rs17550821 pair returned r² **0.057704**. The existing [European rsID-level lookup](selected_variant_pair_ld_reference.csv) returned r² 0.136945 for the same pair. These are descriptive results in different reference groups, not a formal population comparison. Ensembl's returned rsID-level record does not specify the counted alleles, uncertainty or signed r; multiallelic contrasts and differences in LD definitions or samples may affect comparison. It does not validate a genetic score or establish the variants' effects on an outcome.

For the MEIS1-region rs113851554–rs78206187 pair, the same [SAS endpoint](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/ld/human/pairwise/rs113851554/rs78206187?content-type=application/json;population_name=1000GENOMES:phase_3:SAS) returned an empty array. This is an absence of a returned result, **not an estimate of zero LD** or proof the variants are missing. A complete allele-specific LASI-DAD reference scan remains in progress; LASI-DAD and the 1000 Genomes South Asian reference are distinct samples, not interchangeable population labels. No sleep or frailty outcomes were analyzed.

Reproduce the two bounded lookups with `python3 work/lasi/check_ensembl_sas_ld.py`; the [receipt](stage6_ensembl_sas_chr2_ld.json) records source URLs, responses and response hashes.
