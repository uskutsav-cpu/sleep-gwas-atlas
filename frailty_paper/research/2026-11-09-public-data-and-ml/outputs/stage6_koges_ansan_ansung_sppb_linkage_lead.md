# KoGES Ansan/Ansung: a more specific sleep and physical-function genetics lead

Checked 29 October 2026. Two independently reported analyses now identify the **same named KoGES subcohorts**, although not necessarily the same people or data-release waves:

- [Ban et al. (2011)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3071826/) used the Ansan and Ansung cohorts, genotypes and self-reported insomnia. They reported 8,842 participants and 81,055 SNPs after their specific QC, including 1,439 insomnia cases and 7,280 controls.
- A [2022 ASHG poster abstract](https://www.ashg.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/ASHG2022-PosterAbstracts.pdf) by Kwak and colleagues, PB2622 (p. 1744), reports selection of 1,690 people with genotypes and a short physical performance battery (SPPB)-based frailty classification from 8,840 genotyped Ansan/Ansung participants. It reports 89 frailty cases (5.27%). SPPB measures physical performance and is not necessarily the cumulative-deficit FI or the five-component Fried frailty phenotype. The abstract does not document an insomnia measure in those 1,690 people or a genetic-score evaluation.

The shared subcohort names narrow the earlier [KoGES lead](korean_frailty_gwas_validation_feasibility.md), but the counts 8,842 and 8,840 do **not** establish that either study used the same individuals. Survey wave, insomnia timing and consistency, consent for linkage, attrition, ancestry/relatedness, data release and overlap with all our discovery and selection datasets must be established in approved data. With only 89 reported SPPB-defined cases before checking joint sleep and genotype availability, the opportunity for calibration, subgroup and joint-outcome analyses may be quite limited. Do not use these counts as predicted validation power or usable events. If this cohort is used, identify a separate sleep/fatigue-free endpoint where possible and verify all score alleles and imputation quality.

The 2022 abstract lists six selected suggestive GWAS loci (all p values above 5×10⁻⁸) and 14 preselected aging SNPs with nominal associations. An exact-ID comparison against the 15- and 22-marker project files found **zero matches** among those 20 listed IDs; [inventory](koges_2022_sppb_abstract_candidate_screen.csv). This is a narrow, selected-results check: it cannot establish that our variants were untested, absent or null; it does not test linked loci, harmonize alleles or assess a score. The abstract is conference evidence and does not supply a verified complete per-variant result file.

A [2026 KoGES profile](https://academic.oup.com/ije/article/55/5/dyag192/8812758) describes application and ethics processes and an international collaboration route. It does not establish an anonymous download, eligibility, price, or release of the exact linked data to this project. No application or message was sent. The key next access questions are: whether the historical insomnia questions and later SPPB/frailty components can be linked to the same current genotyped participants; usable sample and case counts by wave; required credentials and fees; candidate variant dosage, allele and imputation QC; and any participant/relative overlap with prior project GWAS. This is a candidate controlled-access route, not completed validation.
