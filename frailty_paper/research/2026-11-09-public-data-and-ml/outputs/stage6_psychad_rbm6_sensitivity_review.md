# RBM6 brain mural-cell model: sensitivity to variant coverage

The seven earlier S-PrediXcan analyses used 28–31 of the 31 model SNPs. This check recomputed the same uncalibrated association statistic in two ways: first, using the 28 model SNPs present in all seven harmonized GWAS; second, removing one available model SNP at a time and recalculating the covariance of the remaining model. The complete table, including each omitted SNP, is `stage6_psychad_rbm6_sensitivity.csv`; `stage6_psychad_rbm6_sensitivity.py` reproduces it. Recomputed full-model Z scores agree with the earlier MetaXcan results within 1e-6.

With the common 28-SNP set, Z was -3.761 for Atkins FI, -5.437 for Mak FI, -3.961 for Mak physical frailty, -3.629 for Ye FFS, -2.247 for Jansen insomnia, -0.670 for FinnGen insomnia, and -1.723 for FinnGen hospital frailty. The direction stayed negative in all seven, but SNP-set harmonization does not make the GWAS independent, equalize their power or definitions, or test differences between traits.

The most influential single removal in each of the seven calculations was rs2353586 (GRCh38 chr3:50008613:G>T in the model's allele labels). For Mak FI, Z changed from -5.585 to -3.203 (uncalibrated p 2.33e-8 to 0.00136); for Jansen insomnia, Z changed from -2.247 to -0.939 (uncalibrated p 0.0246 to 0.348). Removing the variant changed the Mak FI model's predicted-expression variance from 0.02829 to 0.01440. No leave-one-out result reversed sign, but the same data were used throughout and the analyses are correlated. The selected gene, cell type and seven traits were investigated after prior project results; these are descriptive checks, not prespecified independent validation or calibrated evidence of a difference.

Removing a SNP changes the prediction model and its variance; this is not a conditional GWAS, an intervention, or a causal test of rs2353586. Correlated SNPs and local LD may distribute or obscure signal. The finding does not establish that the SNP regulates RBM6 in vivo, mediates frailty, causes insomnia, or marks a shared causal signal. A comparison to genuinely independent outcome data and LD-aware multivariable fine-mapping/colocalization is still needed.

The archived PsychAD model's `extra` and `extra_full` tables contain no `phi` field required by the newer MetaXcan inflation-calibration routine. Supplying only GWAS heritability and sample size would not provide that missing model-specific quantity; do not fabricate a calibrated p value. The original result and source details are in `stage6_psychad_rbm6_twas_review.md`.
