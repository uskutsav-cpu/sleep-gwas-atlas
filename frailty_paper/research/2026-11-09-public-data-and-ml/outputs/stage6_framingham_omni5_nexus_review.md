# Omni5 marker-coverage screen for Framingham feasibility

Checked 29 October 2026. Framingham's [SHARe study description](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/study.cgi?study_id=phs000342.v23.p16) reports approximately 2,500 Offspring participants genotyped on an Illumina HumanOmni5M-4v1 array. We screened the public [GEO GSM2545742 NA12878 final-report file](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM2545742), which identifies its content as `humanomni5-4v1_c.bpm`. The exact Framingham manifest revision and released marker file were not verified; this is a related revision, not proof of FHS coverage. The GEO record describes GenomeStudio processing of an NA12878 sample and is **not Framingham participant data**.

The report contains 4,301,332 probe rows, matching its header count. Using the project scores' inferred/assumed GRCh37 positions as an **unverified coordinate-build match**, 9 of 15 markers and 12 of 22 markers have at least one same-chromosome, same-position probe in this report. Several have matching rsID names as well; others have `kgp` probe names and are position matches only. [Probe results](stage6_framingham_omni5_nexus_coordinate_matches.csv); [counts and SHA-256 receipt](stage6_framingham_omni5_nexus_receipt.json). Same-position matching is not sufficient to establish identical alleles, strand, variant identity or genotyping success. Probe SNP labels are TOP-strand, not necessarily forward alleles; do not use them to orient effects without strand and reference checks. The score coordinates, including possible multiallelic representations, also require verification.

The other 6 and 10 markers were not matched by position in this particular final report; that is not proof they are absent from the exact Framingham genotyping release or imputed data. Our scan relies on the report's position columns and does not separately search for renamed markers at discordant positions. The nominal panel coverage cannot support statements about sample genotype calls, imputation INFO, phenotype linkage, or predictive performance. FHS describes additional arrays and imputation panels; these could improve coverage but require direct inspection after authorization.

The [scanning script](../work/framingham_omni5_nexus_screen.py) reads all rows and checks the header's expected count. The compressed input SHA-256 is `6e361a4af8b11e145fcda2d4339715eb0a2c9308ef3401610d341491af774b4f`; to conserve disk space the downloaded source was removed after screening. Redownload it from `https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/samples/GSM2545nnn/GSM2545742/suppl/GSM2545742_HumanOmni5-4v1_NexusFinalReport1.txt.gz` to `work/framingham_omni5_na12878_nexus.txt.gz`, verify the checksum, then run the script from the project root. This screen is not independent score validation.
