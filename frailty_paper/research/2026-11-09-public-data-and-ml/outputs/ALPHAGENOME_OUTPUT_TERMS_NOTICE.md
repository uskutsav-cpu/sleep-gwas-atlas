# AlphaGenome output provenance and use notice

The project contains AlphaGenome API and Atlas outputs and tables or descriptions derived from them, including `stage5_live_raw_scores.zip`, `stage5_live_results_summary.md`, and functional-atlas tables. Their predictions are experimental research outputs from Google DeepMind, not measurements of an individual's risk or clinical advice. Derived tables may select, filter, annotate, aggregate, or transform the original model outputs; consult each file's provenance and the raw output before interpretation. Some requested outputs are missing and must not be represented as zero.

Use and distribution are subject to the applicable [AlphaGenome Services Additional Terms of Service](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/terms) and [AlphaGenome Output Terms of Use](https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/output-terms). The publicly displayed service terms were last modified 8 September 2026, and the displayed output terms are effective 9 October 2026; the latter say the version effective when output was generated applies. Check the governing version and any separate terms applying to a particular dataset or downloadable artifact before sharing or using outputs. This notice is not an exhaustive license determination or a substitute for reviewing those terms.

For users of AlphaGenome Services, the displayed Additional Terms restrict training models that predict genetic variant effects similarly to AlphaGenome, except for specified permissive-use downloadable artifacts. The separately displayed Output Terms have a broader machine-learning-training restriction with a stated exception for certain similar models under the Model Parameters Terms; the Output Terms state that, for users who agreed to the service or model-parameter terms, their use is governed by those terms. They also define Output to include predictions and related information such as embeddings, and Output Derivatives to include substantially derived adaptations. Because the scopes and exceptions are specific, do not assume the proposed variant-function prediction model can be trained using these API outputs, embeddings or derivatives. Review the applicable terms and obtain any needed institutional or rights-holder clarification before an output-derived training use. The displayed service terms describe the AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score and certain designated downloadable artifacts separately; do not assume that all model tracks or feature breakdowns have that status.

For the present project, build ML baselines and embeddings using independently sourced, appropriately licensed non-AlphaGenome features and labels; preserve the AlphaGenome predictions as separately labelled research annotations for post-hoc comparison. Do not include API outputs or their derivatives as model-training inputs in the proposed variant-effect prediction. This is a conservative project design, not a claim that every conceivable downstream use is forbidden.

The service terms require conspicuous notice of the relevant Output Terms and modifications when covered outputs or derivatives are published or distributed; consult the terms for the exact requirement and the stated artifact exceptions. Individual service credentials are personal and must not be shared. Source-linked original outputs and independent datasets may carry additional terms.
