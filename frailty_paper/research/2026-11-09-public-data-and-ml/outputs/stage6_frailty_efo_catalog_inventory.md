# Current GWAS Catalog frailty-category inventory

A direct [GWAS Catalog v2 study query for EFO_0009885 (frailty measurement), including child traits](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies?efo_id=EFO_0009885&show_child_traits=true&size=500) returned 11 curated studies on October 28, 2026; the response reported one page. Nine entries have Catalog full-summary availability: the Atkins UKB–TwinGene FI meta-analysis, the FinnGen hospital frailty risk score GWAS, and seven Foote genomic-SEM frailty factors. All are already considered in this project. The reported factor N values are genomic-SEM effective estimates, not counts of distinct participants; the Foote results are not an independent conventional single-cohort FI GWAS. The two other entries are the Ye Fried score and Mekli HRS FI studies, for which this query marks Catalog full summaries unavailable. The Ye results are nevertheless available separately on Figshare and were already analyzed, illustrating that a Catalog flag is not a definitive statement about all public copies.

The query does not prove that no other relevant dataset exists. It only covers studies curated under this ontology term and its descendants; phenotypes indexed under different terms, prepublication releases, other repositories, newly deposited studies or study-specific files may not appear. Catalog cohort labels marked `NR` are unknown, not proof of non-overlap. It identifies no new immediately usable independent full FI or FFS summary GWAS within this query and does not address individual-level score performance.
