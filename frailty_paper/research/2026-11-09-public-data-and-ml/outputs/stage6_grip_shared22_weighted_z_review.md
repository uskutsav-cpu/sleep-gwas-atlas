# Full 22-marker count: low-grip summary association screen

All 22 markers of the equal-weight allele count have exact coordinate and allele-pair matches in the checksum-verified [Jones et al. low-grip GWAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7844411/). Combining their marginal effects with EUR 1000 Genomes reference LD gives an approximate weighted **Z = 2.51**, nominal two-sided p = 0.012. The GWAS includes UK Biobank, which also contributes to candidate discovery, so this is **not independent replication**. Low grip strength is one component of physical frailty, not FI or the complete Fried score. The count was selected and analyzed post hoc, and the nominal p-value is not multiplicity-adjusted or demonstrated to be well calibrated.

In post hoc leave-one-marker-out calculations, Z ranged from 1.89 when rs28528997 was omitted to 2.92 when rs9536347 was omitted. This is an influence check, not a confidence interval or evidence that removing a marker improves prediction. The varied cohort participation and meta-analysis heterogeneity documented in the [15-marker grip review](stage6_grip_sparse_score_review.md) limit the assumption that target Z-score covariance equals the external reference LD. Differences across score statistics also do not measure differences in predictive performance.

The calculation uses `aᵀz / sqrt(aᵀRa)`, with `z` the published beta/SE oriented to each score allele and `a=sqrt(2p(1−p))` from the target counted-allele frequency. `R` is constructed from the previously recorded 503-person European reference dosage covariances, with cross-chromosome correlations set to zero. It assumes compatible marginal models, target frequencies, adequate source allele orientation, and reference LD appropriate to the target meta-analysis. The reference VCF was not full-file checksum verified. No individual genotypes or outcomes from the GWAS participants are used, so this does not estimate score odds ratios, discrimination, calibration, clinical utility, or the joint sleep–frailty outcome.

Reproduce with `python3 outputs/stage6_grip_shared22_weighted_z.py`; the [receipt](stage6_grip_shared22_weighted_z.json) records the score hash, source file MD5, assumptions and results, and the [sensitivity table](stage6_grip_shared22_weighted_z.csv) lists all leave-one-out calculations. Exact candidate matches and per-variant effects are in `stage6_grip_gwas_candidate_aligned.csv`. The linked phenotype analysis, independent target access and full validation remain unresolved.
