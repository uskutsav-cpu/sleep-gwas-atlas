# Korean frailty: candidate proxy check near NCAM1

The 22-marker count includes rs2155292, 25,256 bases from rs10891490 in the GRCh37 1000 Genomes reference. The latter appears in the 14 preselected-marker tables of [Kwak et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11357-026-02154-6). In 1000 Genomes phase 3, their dosage correlation squared is 0.541 in 504 East Asian reference individuals, versus 0.943 in 503 European reference individuals. The EAS panel is not the Korean study's in-sample LD and includes multiple East Asian populations. Its r² is below a commonly used 0.8 threshold for a strong proxy, so European LD alone would be misleading for this follow-up.

The source table reports for rs10891490 a meta-analysis beta −0.02 (SE 0.02, p = 0.38) for its FRAIL scale and −0.03 (SE 0.03, p = 0.33) for the CHS index. Its FI row reports a KoGES-only beta −0.00809 (SE 0.00743, p = 0.28) with no meta-analysis estimate. These are reported estimates for a different SNP and different frailty definitions; lack of statistical significance does not establish no effect. The source lists C as an allele, but the complete allele and effect-direction semantics have not been independently verified; the study-table build is not explicitly established here. Do not transfer these betas or signs to the project's counted G allele.

The other nearby lead, rs2396766, has no Korean estimates in the selected source tables. Those tables are selective and do not establish whether the project's markers were tested in the full Korean study. This screen neither validates the 22-marker count nor excludes associations. Cohort and participant non-overlap with all candidate-selection sources is unverified; the project has no individual-level genotype/outcome data from this study. NCAM1 is a positional annotation, not an established effector gene.

`stage6_korean_ncam1_proxy_ld.py` and its JSON output record the reference genotype checks. The remote VCF and index were not independently full-file checksum verified. Source rows and phenotype definitions are documented in `korean_frailty_gwas_validation_feasibility.md` and `kwak2026_supplementary_candidate_inventory.csv`.
