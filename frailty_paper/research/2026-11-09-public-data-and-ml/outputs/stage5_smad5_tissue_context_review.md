# SMAD5-region rs7734045: tissue context

The official AlphaGenome Atlas feature-breakdown view for chr5:136202653 A>C on 27 October 2026 displays predicted DNase-seq scores of 0.06 in glutamatergic neuron, 0.03 in brain, 0.02 in cerebellum, 0.03 in psoas muscle, 0.02 in skeletal muscle cells, 0.03 in T-helper 17 cells, and 0.02 in naïve CD4-positive T cells. Additional selected contexts are in the [observations table](stage5_smad5_dnaseseq_contexts.csv). Values are rounded to two decimals; these are predictions of local chromatin accessibility, not gene-specific RNA or phenotype effects. The rows are selected examples, not a systematic statistical test of differences between tissues, and sample provenance and exact cell-type correspondence require metadata review.

At the same allele, an eQTL Catalogue OneK1K record links ALT C to higher SMAD5 expression in a CD4_Naive cell population (beta 0.672672, SE 0.0456211, p 1.55 × 10⁻⁴⁴; molecular PIP 0.0952). The Atlas's similarly named naïve CD4 RNA rows display raw values rounded to 0.00 in the [earlier observations](stage5_initial_atlas_tissue_observations.csv), preventing directional comparison. The predicted DNase signal is a different molecular modality and cannot be called validation of the expression QTL or proof that chromatin mediates disease. OneK1K's independence from the model training and disease GWAS must be established before use as independent validation.

The [previous colocalization review](stage4_cd4_smad5_colocalization_review.md) describes differences across trait datasets and model assumptions; the Mak FI baseline favored distinct signals, while other tests were largely QTL-only. This does not establish a shared causal insomnia–frailty variant. Separate event- and tissue-matched AlphaGenome expression and splicing scores, and independent experimental evidence, remain necessary. The Atlas examples above do not establish pituitary availability or rule out effects in any unexamined cell type.
