# Historical-symbol sensitivity: sleep-expression panel and frailty

The original [exact-symbol comparison](stage6_spektor_gse40366_gene_comparison.md) mapped 222 of 244 insomnia-associated genes onto the GSE40366 microarray. Here we used the [HGNC complete set](https://hgnc.genenames.org/download/) to investigate whether historical names could recover some of the remainder. We used a previous HGNC-approved symbol only when it uniquely resolved to the current source gene among all approved symbols and aliases in the downloaded file, and only if no exact-symbol probe was present. Existing exact-symbol results retained their original probe choices; each rescued gene used the highest-mean available probe across the 151 frailty-category-labeled samples, without using their labels.

This sensitivity analysis mapped **240 of 244 genes**, including 18 rescued using historical symbols. Of 240 tests, 17 were nominally significant at p < 0.05, and **none passed BH 5%** across the expanded panel (minimum q = 0.644). Frail-minus-nonfrail direction agreed with the original insomnia association in 109 of 240; this is descriptive, not a calibrated cross-phenotype concordance test. Four names remained unmapped: AP5B1, HELZ2, SRGAP2B and SRGAP2C. The source-selected panel and outcome comparison are post hoc, and current nomenclature cannot establish probe specificity or accurate mapping of the source transcript; paralogous genes and microarray cross-hybridization remain concerns.

The dataset and modeling limits remain those of the [original analysis](stage6_spektor_gse40366_gene_comparison.md): 29 frail and 38 non-frail nonagenarians, PBMC expression adjusted for sex, and no exact-age or cell-composition adjustment. Different phenotypes and populations, bulk expression, and post-selection limit inference. This is not genetic-score validation and does not establish a shared causal mechanism.

The [full sensitivity results](stage6_spektor_gse40366_alias_sensitivity.csv) and [receipt](stage6_spektor_gse40366_alias_sensitivity_receipt.json) identify historical symbols and probes, the HGNC SHA-256, unmapped genes, and all estimates. Reproduce with `work/stage4_env/bin/python work/geo_vitality/spektor_alias_sensitivity.py`; it uses the saved HGNC file `work/geo_vitality/hgnc_complete_set.txt`, the existing GEO matrix and source-gene list, and streams the GEO platform annotation.
