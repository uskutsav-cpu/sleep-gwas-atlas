# Initial live AlphaGenome results for priority markers

These are exploratory observations from the official API, using the recommended 1,048,576-base input and ALL_FOLDS, as selected examples from the 488-variant run. Scores are ALT minus REF on the relevant model scale; eQTL effects are the separately harmonized ALT effects. These are molecular predictions, not disease-risk scores. Matching an allele, gene and tissue label does not prove the samples or assays are identical, or establish colocalization, mediation or causality. GTEx is not a fully independent evaluation of models trained using related resources. Magnitudes use different scales and should not be compared directly.

- **rs323509, chr5:104746478:A>C:** the API returned RNA expression tracks for ENSG00000251574 in testis, including raw scores approximately +0.0000105 (ENCODE polyA), +0.001388 (ENCODE total RNA), and -0.000855 (GTEx polyA). It returned no rows for any of the three requested splice scorers for this variant. These expression tracks cannot validate the measured testis splice event, and testis results do not establish relevance to sleep or frailty.
- **FOXP2, rs12536335, chr7:114403104:A>G:** the pituitary polyA RNA score was -0.0007002, while the GTEx v10 pituitary eQTL ALT effect was +0.2479 (p = 4.70e-8): an opposite-sign screen. These are small model scores and differently scaled observations; the discrepancy requires follow-up and does not invalidate or establish either result.
- **SMAD5, rs7734045, chr5:136202653:A>C:** the naïve CD4 total RNA score was +0.0004586 and polyA RNA score was -0.001402; the OneK1K naïve CD4 eQTL ALT effect was +0.672672 (p = 1.55e-44). Thus the model's two assay tracks give opposite direction screens against the measured eQTL. Cell labels are similar, not necessarily the same samples; the published credible-set record and the Catalogue record refer to overlapping evidence and are not independent replication. No splice rows were returned for this marker. See `stage5_cd4_smad5_indel_colocalization_review.md` and `stage5_smad5_muscle_splice_indel_colocalization_review.md` for separate regional analyses and their assumptions.

The pilot result files and complete track metadata are saved under `/Volumes/Extreme SSD/codex-sleep-frailty-work/stage5_pilot_scores`; the first variant's receipt confirms 6,360 rows but no splice modalities. Absence of a row is not a predicted zero. Gene-based splice scoring is restricted by scorer-specific gene and variant context; why a given output was absent must be checked, not assumed. The official model paper describes gene and splice scorer restrictions: https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/papers/alphagenome.pdf. The GUI's previously transcribed rounded raw values did not permit these sign screens; website and API quantile labels/scales remain unreconciled.
- **FOXP2 insertion, chr7:114534306:T>TA:** the live run returned a pituitary FOXP2 polyA RNA score of +0.0045135 and a splice-site-usage score of 0.01171875, along with 32 pituitary splice-junction rows. The selected pituitary molecular credible-set record gives an ALT eQTL effect of -0.535275 (p = 8.16e-12), an opposite-sign expression screen. The splice scores require event-specific interpretation and cannot be treated as directional validation of the eQTL. The prior colocalization sensitivity, including this insertion, favored distinct rather than shared QTL and disease signals under its baseline assumptions; this prediction does not overturn that result or establish a disease mechanism.
