# FOXP2: larger European LD reference sensitivity

A public UK Biobank European-ancestry signed LD matrix was tested as a second reference for the MVP insomnia and FinnGen HFRS regional analysis. The streamed chromosome-7 resource reports 362,063 reference samples and contains HapMap3+ variants. It is a block-masked, int8-quantized matrix: correlations outside source LD blocks are set to zero, and it is not in-sample LD for the FinnGen HFRS or MVP GWAS. The resource's [documentation](https://shz9.github.io/viprs/download_ld/) and [methods paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12256920/) describe its construction.

Only **553** variants from the 3,019-variant EUR/FIN common analysis were present and passed exact GRCh37 position and allele-pair checks. Neither the original marker rs12536335 nor the previous full-set lead rs35624276 is directly present. A retained proxy, rs2894699, is strongly correlated with rs12536335 in the separately queried SISu panel (r²=0.9974); correlation with a marker does not establish it as causal. This reduced HapMap3+ subset cannot resolve all of the earlier signals or establish complete regional coverage.

On exactly these 553 variants, the same two-trait SuSiE-coloc procedure yielded one candidate signal pair with PP.H4=0.9876 using the UKB matrix, 0.9882 using 1000 Genomes EUR503, and 0.9842 using the nested FIN99 panel, at p1=p2=1e-4 and p12=1e-5. All fits converged. A second paired HFRS signal had much lower PP.H4 in each analysis. These high posteriors are conditional on a selected subset, references, priors and model, and do not establish a shared causal variant. In the earlier **3,019-variant** comparison the FIN99 fitted HFRS sets and colocalization results changed markedly; the apparent agreement on 553 variants therefore must not be treated as resolving the full-region discrepancy. FIN99 has only 99 donors and is contained within EUR503. The independent status of UKB LD with respect to the original locus-selection workflow is not established.

## Matrix and allele checks

The original UKB matrix is int8-quantized and, after extraction/dequantization, had 123 negative eigenvalues, minimum -0.0585. To allow the method to use a positive-semidefinite correlation matrix, negative eigenvalues were clipped to zero and the diagonal renormalized; maximum element change was 0.0124 and relative Frobenius change 0.00488. This is an explicit sensitivity approximation, not proof that the corrected matrix is the true LD. On 29,958 pairs with |r|>0.1 in both UKB and 1000G EUR, no signed correlations had opposite signs after orienting source A1 to the MVP effect allele; signed-r correlation was 0.991. Correlated pairs are not independent tests, and this check cannot settle every allele or sample mismatch. The UKB two-trait Z/LD inconsistency estimates were 0.0098 and 0.0274; FIN99 estimates were 0.242 and 0.224. These diagnostics have no universal cutoff and do not prove model validity.

The matrices and association statistics were analyzed with susieR 0.14.2 and coloc 5.2.3; L=10, 95% coverage, residual variance estimation disabled, and MVP effective N=253,769. Multiple shared priors are retained in `stage6_foxp2_ukbcommon_susie_coloc.csv`, and convergence and Z/LD diagnostics in `stage6_foxp2_ukbcommon_susie_diagnostics.csv`. The original phenotype releases, p-to-Z conversion, allele conventions, trait definitions, and prior sensitivity are described in `stage6_foxp2_independent_cohort_coloc_review.md`.

## Reproducibility

The UKB archive was streamed by byte range from the pinned Hugging Face dataset revision `3f177a475ec9cada341f9ca11c1cd20aafae7adc`; the entire remote archive was not downloaded or checksum-verified. The reader is the public `ld_browser` source at commit `264d33f5696405f160659cd51894685e14820491`, with extraction script `work/ld_browser/stage6_foxp2_ukb_extract.mjs`. `stage6_foxp2_ukb_input_qc.py` checks positions and alleles; `stage6_foxp2_ukb_ld_prepare.py` orients the matrix, calculates the PSD correction and prepares both same-support 1000G matrices; `stage6_foxp2_ukb_common_susie.R` performs the fits. Metadata and numerical checks are `stage6_foxp2_ukb_ld_metadata.json` and `stage6_foxp2_ukb_ld_qc_receipt.json`.

The fuller public UKB EUR reference with up to 18 million variants is distributed as a roughly 20.6 GB compressed archive; the local machine lacks space to download it. Even that larger EUR source would not be in-sample Finnish LD. Suitable study- or ancestry-matched signed LD with better coverage is still needed for a reliable regional conclusion, alongside independent validation of any individual-level risk model.
