# HPP public sleep association files: bounded feasibility check

On 29 October 2026 I examined the [HPP GWAS paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medj.2023.12.001), its [public dashboard](https://zacharylevine.shinyapps.io/GWASDashboard/) and its [source repository](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface). The live dashboard loaded and offered six feature domains without a sleep option. However, its repository includes an [ItamarSleep feature list](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface/blob/main/inst/lists/ItamarSleepLoader.csv) and public RDS association-result files. The existence of files does not prove that they are full GWAS summary statistics or linked participant data.

I downloaded the public [SleepLatency](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface/blob/main/inst/full_results_rds/SleepLatency.Rds) and [TotalSleepTime](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface/blob/main/inst/full_results_rds/TotalSleepTime.Rds) result objects. They contain 14,577 and 11,998 rows respectively, with maximum reported p-values approximately 0.001581 in both. The paper describes 8.9 million variants in its overall genetic set. The checked files therefore do **not** establish coverage of all tested variants and appear to be selected subsets; the exact selection rule has not been verified. Their `OBS_CT` values are 6,598 and 6,600 respectively, not the number of cases, score carriers or complete project-score records.

Neither file has an exact rsID match for any of the 15 or 22 project candidate IDs. This is only a **file-content observation**: an absent ID could reflect selection, QC, alternate identifiers, missingness or build/representation differences. It does not establish a null effect, failed replication, or even that the variant was tested. Exact phenotype processing, build and allele orientation have not been verified. Objective sleep latency and duration are related to sleep, but neither equals self-reported clinical insomnia or frailty. The project scores cannot be reconstructed or validated with these selected associations.

The inspection is reproducible with `Rscript outputs/stage6_hpp_sleep_subset_check.R` after downloading the two RDS files into `work/`. Results are in `stage6_hpp_sleep_subset_inventory.csv`.
