# NHANES genetic linkage check

Checked 29 October 2026. NHANES remains useful for our public phenotype-only analyses, but it is not currently an open linked-genotype route for testing the 15- or 22-marker scores.

The CDC's [NHANES DNA specimens and genetic data page](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/biospecimen/dna-specimens.html) says that most genetic repository datasets are restricted and that a limited number of anonymized genetic datasets, available on request under a data-use agreement, cannot be linked to public-use files or other NHANES data. Restricted genetic data can be requested through a Research Data Center proposal. The page does not establish that every score marker is available, that an appropriate linked sample exists, or that the requested analysis is free of access or computing charges.

**Implication:** Do not combine the public NHANES sleep–frailty analysis with anonymized genetic files or call it an independent genetic-score validation. A restricted-data proposal could be considered only after confirming the relevant variants, survey cycles, linked sample, permissible methods and full cost. Other no-service-fee or potentially free controlled-access leads are summarized in the [mentor brief](mentor_meeting_one_page_2026-10-29.md).
