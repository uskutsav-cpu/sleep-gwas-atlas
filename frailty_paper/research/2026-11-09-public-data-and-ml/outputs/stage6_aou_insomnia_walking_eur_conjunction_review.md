# All of Us insomnia and difficulty walking: shared-association follow-up

A subsequent [AFR-group sensitivity](stage6_aou_afr_walking_sensitivity_review.md) returned no nominal joint associations; its smaller case count and uncertainty preclude interpreting this as a between-group difference.

We joined the existing public All by All EUR genetic-similarity-group results for insomnia (`NS_333.2`) and difficulty walking (`NS_325.2`) by exact GRCh38 position, REF and ALT for all 33 project candidates. The public metadata reports 18,163 insomnia cases and 147,753 controls, and 4,002 walking cases and 170,048 controls. These are phenotype-group counts, not verified overlap or variant-specific effective sample sizes.

For each candidate we calculated `max(p_insomnia, p_walking)`, a two-sided intersection–union test against the null that at least one trait association is null. If the source p-values are valid, this max-p test does not require the two outcomes to be independent. One candidate, rs12536335 in the FOXP2 region, has unadjusted p < 0.05 for both (insomnia p = 5.53 × 10⁻⁶, walking p = 0.00361). Its max-p is 0.00361 and BH q across the 33 candidates is 0.119; **none passes q < 0.05**. Standard BH guarantees depend on the joint dependence structure, and the within-screen adjustment does not cover the project's broader searches. The candidates, outcomes and analysis were chosen post hoc.

The observed result is not a prespecified independent replication, proof of a shared causal variant or evidence that FOXP2 is the causal gene. The [existing regional analyses](stage6_foxp2_independent_cohort_coloc_review.md) address different datasets and assumptions; neither this test nor a nearby gene name establishes mediation. Difficulty walking is an EHR code, not a measured gait-speed threshold, Fried frailty or a joint sleep–frailty score. The direction of the two effects was not tested here. Participants and relatives may overlap discovery or other project follow-ups, and the source outcomes may be correlated. No nonsignificant result establishes absence of effects.

A separate comparison of the public [PhecodeX v1.0 and v1.1 mappings](https://github.com/PheWAS/PhecodeXVocabulary) found eight unrolled ICD-CM codes for difficulty walking and **no identical code** shared with the insomnia, broad malaise/fatigue, or generalized muscle-weakness lists in either version. That is a vocabulary comparison, not an All of Us case-set intersection: one person can have several codes, and the deployed vocabulary version, local mapping and exclusion rules have not been established. The [broader mapping audit](stage6_aou_phecodex_mapping_overlap_review.md) explains why fatigue and weakness may themselves overlap.

The [33-variant results](stage6_aou_insomnia_walking_eur_conjunction.csv) and [checksum receipt](stage6_aou_insomnia_walking_eur_conjunction_receipt.json) are reproducible with `python3 work/aou_complete/insomnia_walking_eur_conjunction.py`. Source association files and retrieval details are linked in the [walking follow-up](stage6_aou_eur_walking_followup.md). Neither this analysis nor a score-weight summary evaluates the score in independent individuals.
