# ACT 660W-Quad manufacturer-manifest screen

An [AD genetics supplementary table](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/medrxiv/early/2024/04/23/2024.04.22.24306094/DC2/embed/media-2.pdf?download=true) lists an ACT dataset on the Illumina Human660W-Quad and identifies NIAGADS NG00034 / dbGaP phs000234 as repositories. The reported ACT dataset and platform should be verified against the specific authorized release; the table's 2,790 samples are not a linked genotype–sleep–frailty count. It does not establish the exact manifest revision used for every person.

I downloaded Illumina's public [Human660W-Quad v1_H manifest](https://support.illumina.com/downloads/human660w-quad_v10_product_files.html), whose assay section reports genome build 37.1, and checked it against the project's GRCh37 candidate coordinates. The screen processed all **655,214** manufacturer assay records; the count agrees with the manifest header. Nine of the 33 distinct candidates have both the same rsID in the manifest `Name` field and the same chromosome/position: **4/15** for the 15-marker prototype and **5/22** for either 22-marker prototype. There were no additional coordinate-only or name-only hits. The [matched-record table](stage6_act_array_coordinate_matches.csv), [SHA-256 receipt](stage6_act_array_manifest_receipt.json), and [script](../work/act_660w_manifest_screen.py) document this check.

This result is an **array design screen**, not a coverage test in ACT participants. The manufacturer's allele field is in Illumina assay orientation; matching rsID and position alone does not establish the correct effect-allele contrast, assay strand, successful genotyping or post-QC availability. The project should check the source manifest version and actual dataset, alleles, sample call rates and quality. The 24 unmatched candidates may be available in imputed data or sequencing, or may have alternative identifiers or representations not found by this exact check; unmatched does not mean their genotypes are unavailable. Conversely, a hit does not guarantee its data exist. No claims can be made about imputation accuracy or score performance from this screen. The separately described NIAGADS ADSP whole-genome/exome resources use different technology and cannot be assessed with this array manifest.

A separate [reference tag screen](stage6_act_array_reference_tag_review.md) explores nearby array probes, but does not establish their use in ACT or validate the score.

A [reference-only partial-score comparison](stage6_act_direct_partial_reference_scores.md) examines how direct-manifest-marker sums relate to the full prototypes. It does not evaluate ACT outcomes or imputation.
