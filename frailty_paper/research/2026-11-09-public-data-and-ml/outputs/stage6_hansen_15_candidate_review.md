# African-ancestry handgrip GWAS: 15-marker prototype follow-up

The public [Hansen et al. handgrip summary GWAS](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90854001-GCST90855000/GCST90854255/) provides a physical-function follow-up in 1,652 individuals of sub-Saharan African ancestry, using an age- and sex-adjusted model with a genetic-relatedness random effect. This study is [published](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ajhg.2026.07.006), and its adults are aged 18 or older; the age distribution in the grip-specific subset was not established. Grip is one physical-frailty component, not FI, FFS or insomnia. Participant and relative overlap with every discovery and candidate-selection source has not been individually verified.

We used the project's existing GRCh37-to-GRCh38 position crosswalk and compared exact coordinates and unordered alleles with the unharmonized source. Eleven of 15 variants matched uniquely; four had no source row at their coordinates (rs113851554, rs11152363, rs78206187 and rs4886140). Missing variants may have been untested, filtered or represented differently; they are not null results. Source reference indicators were consistent with their variant IDs and independently queried UCSC hg38 reference bases for all 11 matched variants. This checks reference-base consistency but does not prove all harmonization, imputation, multiallelic representation or individual ancestry assumptions.

None of the 11 matched candidates had nominal p<0.05; the smallest was 0.112227. This is a postselected analysis with limited sample size, and lack of nominal significance does not exclude effects. The paper's preparation code labels original grip measurements in newtons and also constructs standardized traits, while Catalog metadata labels kilograms; the exact analyzed input remains unresolved. The recorded beta and SE are consequently in **unverified units** and must not be presented as kg. We did not compute a full score or compare FI and FFS predictive performance: four markers are missing, target ancestry-matched LD and individual genotypes are not available, and inferred FFS weight orientation needs confirmation. This does not validate the scores or test a shared insomnia–frailty mechanism.
