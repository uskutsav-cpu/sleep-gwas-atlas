# HPP public polygenic-score association matrix: sleep-score check

The [Levine et al. HPP study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medj.2023.12.001) tested UK Biobank-derived polygenic scores against many HPP phenotypes, and the paper states that its multiple-testing correction covered 4,116 scores and 4,772 traits. Its public dashboard repository supplies an aggregate [clinical corrected-p matrix](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface/blob/main/inst/prs_assoc_corrected_clinical.Rds) and [score-name dictionary](https://github.com/ZachLevine-11/gwasInterface/blob/main/inst/prs_name_dict.Rds). This is an analysis of published scores, **not** the project's experimental 15- or 22-variant scores.

The dictionary maps `Sleeplessness / insomnia` to UK Biobank field 1200 and `Non-cancer illness code, self-reported: insomnia` to 20002_1616. In the downloaded matrix, each of those two columns contains the value 1 for all 729 clinical-phenotype rows; the same is true for the checked sleep duration, daytime napping and chronotype score columns. No value for these five score labels is below 0.05 in this matrix. The [script](stage6_hpp_prs_matrix_check.R) and [aggregate output](stage6_hpp_prs_matrix_summary.csv) reproduce this narrow observation.

**Interpretation:** the matrix alone does not reveal uncorrected p-values, coefficients, uncertainty, usable sample per comparison, precise score weights or how missing/untested comparisons were represented. A value of 1 could reflect capped corrected p-values or encoding choices; do not infer a true absence of association, that every cell was tested, or a precise effect from it. FI and FFS are not verified outcome labels in this matrix. This check neither validates our scores nor establishes that HPP lacks a useful sleep–frailty relationship. HPP individual-level data remain controlled; the previously identified [access route and limitations](stage6_hpp_insomnia_score_validation_lead.md) still apply.

Reproduce after downloading the linked two files to `work/hpp_prs_corrected.Rds` and `work/hpp_prs_names.Rds`, respectively, and running `Rscript outputs/stage6_hpp_prs_matrix_check.R`.
