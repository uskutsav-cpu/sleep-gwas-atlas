# Korean and Japanese public phenotype catalog screening

Checked 28 October 2026. Searches below refer to the **displayed phenotype labels** in the named public GWAS browser, not all measurements made in the underlying cohorts or all publications. A zero-match search is not evidence that a cohort never measured the trait. GWAS summary statistics cannot provide linked individual-level prediction or a paired sleep–frailty outcome.

* [KoGES PheWeb phenotype catalog](https://koges.leelabsg.org/phenotypes) displayed 136 analysis entries. Searching `frail`, `grip`, `sleep` and `insomnia` returned zero matches; `BMI` returned two, a positive control that the interface was responding. Analyses can represent different populations or meta-analyses, so entries are not a count of distinct phenotypes.
* [PheWeb.jp phenotype catalog](https://pheweb.jp/phenotypes) displayed 345 entries. `frail`, `grip`, `muscle`, `fatigue` and `senility` returned zero, while `sleep` returned sleep apnea and `insomnia` returned [insomnia](https://pheweb.jp/pheno/Insomnia); a positive-control search for `body mass index` returned two. These counts describe the site at the time of checking.
* The Japanese insomnia page reports 389 cases and 177,864 controls, and identifies the [Sakaue and Kanai et al. cross-population atlas](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-021-00931-x). The page links the Japanese summary statistics. The small case count and hospital-based sampling restrict precision and generalizability. The browser states its coordinates are GRCh37. The Japanese GWAS may help as a targeted single-outcome association follow-up, not as independent validation of an individual score or a combined sleep–frailty phenotype. Check sample overlap and allele coding before inference.

The public catalog screening has not located an FI or Fried physical frailty outcome in either browser. It does not change the [individual-level validation gap](stage6_score_validation_evidence_summary.md).
