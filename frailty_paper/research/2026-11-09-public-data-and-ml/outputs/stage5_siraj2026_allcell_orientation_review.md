# Reporter allele-direction check across all selected cell-line results

We extended the author-labeled count cross-check from five selected SK-N-SH candidates to all 284 distinct selected result groups (42 variants) in `stage5_siraj2026_allcell_reporter_model_comparison.csv`. For each group, the authors' processed data provide mean reference and alternate plasmid counts and reporter RNA counts. We calculated `log2((RNA_alt / plasmid_alt) / (RNA_ref / plasmid_ref))` and compared its sign with the study's `Log2Skew`. Counts were present and positive in every group. The original variant, source, cell, library and selected-analysis fields were used for matching; repeated phenotype and gene source records are retained as counts, not independent experiments.

The sign agreed in 275 groups and differed in nine. The nine all failed the study's active-and-skew criteria, but that does not establish why the signs differ. For example, the rs60271 allele chr5:104742532:A>C in the HepG2 line has aggregate count log2 ratio -0.1266 while the fitted skew is +0.1252. A ratio of average counts is not the same statistic as the fitted generalized linear model; differences could reflect normalization, replicate effects, or allele conventions. We cannot use this simple check to declare a reversed allele or a valid direction for every row. Both measurements and the matching AlphaGenome DNase scores are preserved in `stage5_siraj2026_allcell_count_orientation.csv`.

The five previously highlighted SK-N-SH active-and-skew candidates each had matching count and fitted-skew signs; their direction review remains a narrowly scoped descriptive comparison. Even for those rows, model DNase and reporter RNA activity are different assays, the selected candidates are post hoc, and the model's training/benchmark independence is unresolved. The complete 284-group table includes nonpassing rows but still contains correlated variants, repeated contexts and potentially overlapping training regions; raw sign agreement with AlphaGenome is not an unbiased validation statistic. No clinical prediction or shared disease causality follows.

To reproduce, first generate the two selected all-cell analysis files as described in `stage5_siraj2026_allcell_reporter_review.md`, then run `python3 outputs/stage5_siraj_allcell_count_orientation.py --gtex GTEX_CSV --traits TRAITS_CSV`. The script joins on source, allele, cell, library and all selection/result fields; ambiguous or nonpositive counts remain unassessable rather than being interpreted as zero.

The authors' subsequently checked published methods explicitly define the fitted allelic contrast as ALT versus REF, and their preprocessing retains `Log2Skew` as the effect field. Therefore the differing aggregate ratios do not invalidate that source-reported fitted direction. See `stage5_siraj2026_fitted_direction_review.md`; generalization to the separate Ho study is not established.
