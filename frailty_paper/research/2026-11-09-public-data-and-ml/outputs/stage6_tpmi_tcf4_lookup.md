# TPMI sleep-disorder lookup: rs1942262

The [TPMI PheWeb page](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/variant/18:55206086-G-A) lists rs1942262 as chr18:55206086 G/A on GRCh38. The project's 15-marker prototype uses chr18:52873317 A/G on GRCh37 and counts A; [Ensembl](https://grch37.rest.ensembl.org/map/human/GRCh37/18:52873317..52873317/GRCh38?content-type=application/json) maps its coordinate to the TPMI position on the positive strand. TPMI's effect-allele convention has not been confirmed, so the sign cannot yet be assigned to the project's counted A allele.

For [sleep disorders](https://pheweb.ibms.sinica.edu.tw/pheno/327), the browser shows a rounded effect of 0.012 (SE 0.0084), p = 0.14, with 45,836 cases and 287,785 controls. This is not a statistically clear association and does not exclude a smaller effect. The diagnosis group is broader than insomnia. The candidate was selected after earlier analyses, and complete discovery/selection sample overlap remains unverified. The site's nearest-gene label TCF4 is positional, not evidence of mediation.

This single-marker result cannot validate a genetic score, shared causality, or individual predictions. Full candidate effects, effect-allele conventions, matched LD and non-overlap checks are needed for a defensible summary-level score comparison; individual outcome validation additionally needs linked genotypes and phenotypes. Prior checks and access limitations are linked in `stage6_tpmi_sleep_source_lead.md`.

TPMI requests acknowledgement and citation of [Chen et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09350-y) and [Yang et al. (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09680-x).

This imprecise, postselected single-marker result does not rule out smaller effects or validate the score. The site's nearest-gene label TCF4 does not establish mediation. The same sample-overlap, ancestry, diagnostic-definition and multiple-testing limitations above apply.
