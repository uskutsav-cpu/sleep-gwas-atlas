# FI versus FFS: paired comparison of their genetic correlations with HFRS

On a common set of 1,137,927 aligned SNPs, the exploratory LDSC estimates were **0.640** (SE 0.0257) for FI–HFRS and **0.550** (SE 0.0230) for FFS–HFRS. The estimated difference, FFS–HFRS minus FI–HFRS, was **−0.090** (paired jackknife SE 0.0265; approximate 95% CI −0.142 to −0.038; unadjusted two-sided normal p=0.00065). Under this model and data, the estimate for FI is higher.

The covariance matters because the comparisons reuse HFRS and FI/FFS share UK Biobank participants. I formed the difference for each of 200 matched delete blocks and used their paired jackknife variance. The estimated covariance of the two correlations is 0.000245 (sampling correlation 0.413). As checks, the native SNP order hashes and all 200 block boundaries match, the HFRS heritability and all its delete estimates are identical between runs, and the paired-difference variance agrees with the covariance formula. Each model ran with the project’s existing LDSC observer, European LD scores and free intercepts. The exact output and full source/output checksums are retained in `FI_HFRS_common.exact.json`, `FFS_HFRS_common.exact.json` and `hfrs_fi_ffs_paired_contrast.json`.

This is a **post hoc exploratory comparison**, with an unadjusted p-value, not a prespecified confirmation. The common set was derived from the project's earlier FI/FFS/FinnGen-insomnia support and then intersected with HFRS; the sleep-source support restriction is a filtering choice, not a new sleep correlation. The two frailty sources and HFRS have different definitions, cohorts, ages and ascertainment. A lower correlation does not imply that FFS is a worse clinical or genetic score, and no individual risk prediction follows.

The Finnish HFRS univariate intercept remains elevated (~1.188) when using the general European LD reference. The HFRS input assumes total N at each variant because variant-level N was unavailable. Potential LD mismatch and model or sample issues warrant specialist review and an authorized, suitably matched reference; the precision of this paired contrast does not eliminate those concerns. The exact UKB–FinnGen participant intersection is not established. HFRS is not fully free of sleep- or exhaustion-related categories, and no genetic causal interpretation or local shared-signal finding follows. The project’s earlier FI×sleep local-correlation QC failures remain failed.

A third matched-support FI–FFS fit and joint matrix checks were subsequently completed; see `frailty_three_way_common_support_ldsc.md`.
