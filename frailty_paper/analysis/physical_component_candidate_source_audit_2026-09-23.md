# Physical-frailty component source audit

Audit date: 2026-09-23. Purpose: determine whether public source documentation
now supports harmonizing the five acquired component GWAS or substituting
documented UK Biobank field-level GWAS.

## Acquired five-component release and phenotype-definition evidence

The registered Zenodo release (record 14011550) describes five component GWAS
in UK Biobank. Its archived `0_Readme.txt` identifies Regenie v3.5, gives
component case/control counts, and asks users to cite Chen et al. The publisher
supplement for the cited Chen et al. heart-failure study (Table S3) independently
lists the same five component names, UK Biobank field IDs, response coding, and
missing-value rules. It documents weight loss (2306), exhaustion (2080), low
physical activity (6164 and 1011), slow walking (924), and low grip strength
(31, 21001, 46, 47). The per-trait definitions and the source DOCX hash are
recorded in `physical_component_supplement_crosswalk.tsv` and
`physical_component_supplement_review_2026-09-23.md`. Thus the operational
phenotype mapping is now documented.

That supplement does not document the GWAS generation of the five separate
Zenodo files. The article abstract describes an individual-level analysis of
49,530 UK Biobank participants with cardiovascular disease; the Zenodo files'
case/control totals are 396,819–406,980. The article's Data Availability points
to a previous physical-frailty GWAS by Ye et al. (2023), which reports a single
ordinal Fried Frailty Score (0–5) in 386,565 unrelated European-ancestry UK
Biobank participants. Neither the visible article/supplement nor Zenodo
metadata explicitly maps that prior GWAS to each of the five component files.
The sample totals alone do not prove distinct or shared source cohorts.

The Zenodo record and README still do not document the five files' genome
assembly, ancestry and variant/sample QC, effect scale or model/coding,
covariates, or exact summary-statistic generation chain. The files remain
structurally valid, but their source-specific eligibility is unresolved. Keep
them separate from the Ye et al. overall Fried score and do not transfer Ye's
build, ancestry, or model metadata to the component files.

## UK Biobank field-level GWAS as possible proxies

The new supplement establishes the target component definitions, including a
multi-field low-activity algorithm and sex/BMI-thresholded binary low grip. A
single-field Neale Lab GWAS or raw field summary statistics cannot be assumed
to reproduce the exact acquired component release without a documented match
of phenotype recoding, source population, ancestry/QC, variant/build, and
association model. None is substituted into the frozen secondary component
family. The exact component definitions are retained in the crosswalk; the
remaining uncertainty is file-level GWAS provenance/eligibility.

## HFRS endpoint

The Nature Aging article reports HFRS discovery in FinnGen and UK Biobank
replication. Its data-availability statement points to FinnGen's results
access route and notes a one-year embargo for release of FinnGen summary
statistics. The R12 access documentation and endpoint-search sources consulted
here did not identify a file explicitly corresponding to the study's custom
109-code HFRS GWAS. Standard FinnGen endpoint files should not be treated as
that custom score without a matching endpoint definition and
case/control/sample metadata.

## Decision and next evidence needed

Keep all five acquired physical-component sources blocked before allele/build
harmonization. Table S3 has resolved the operational field definitions and
coding; it has not established that those component files were generated by
the cited article or documented their per-file build, ancestry and variant/sample
QC, beta definition/model, or covariates. Do not infer assembly from a coordinate
match or substitute the Neale field-level GWAS. The next sufficient evidence
is a written statement tied to Zenodo record 14011550 that supplies the
remaining per-file GWAS provenance and QC metadata. For HFRS, the next evidence is the exact downloadable endpoint
identifier and release documentation, or the authors' summary-statistics
release. An unsent author query is retained at
`review/author_queries/physical_component_gwas_metadata_request.md`.
