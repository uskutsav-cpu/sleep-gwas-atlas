# Sleep–frailty retraction-record audit

Audit date: 2026-09-24. Scope: verify the status and bibliographic relationship of two records already present in the frozen PubMed title/abstract queue. This audit is not screening or eligibility adjudication.

## Verified publisher record

Springer Nature labels “Depression is a mediating variable between sleep disorders and frailty” (PMID 38372839; DOI `10.1007/s11255-024-03980-3`) as a **Comment**, published 19 February 2024, and marks it retracted on 11 March 2026. Its publisher page links to the corresponding Retraction Note (PMID 41811627; DOI `10.1007/s11255-026-05089-1`). PubMed independently records the retraction notice as a Retraction Notice and explicitly links it to PMID 38372839.

The publisher preview provides the retraction date and linked notice, but not the notice's explanation. The reason for retraction is therefore not inferred or reported here.

## Review handling

Both PMIDs occur in the frozen title/abstract queue and both reviewer decision fields remain blank. Do not count the retracted comment as empirical sleep–frailty evidence. The comment cites a separate cross-sectional CKD study (PMID 38289547; DOI `10.1007/s11255-023-03910-9`), which is independently present in the frozen queue with its own abstract. Keep that cohort study as a separate unscreened record; its eligibility and risk of bias must be assessed through the registered dual-review process. No queue rows, PRISMA counts, screening decisions, or included-study counts were changed by this audit.
