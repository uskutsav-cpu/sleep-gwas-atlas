# Genetic score provenance and verification

The two score tables preserve the 15- and 22-marker project prototypes and their source FI, FFS and insomnia weights when present. They are weighted research scores, not independently validated clinical tools. Source paths and scoring details are recorded in the CSVs and copied `prototype_sources/`. Their 37 rows contain 33 unique rsIDs.

The reference audit queries Ensembl's GRCh37 mapping and reports that all 37 rows match the stated locus and that the stated allele pairs occur among the reference alleles. This supports mapping consistency but does not independently establish the original study build, the biological effect allele, or a biallelic contrast. Nine of 15 and 14 of 22 score rows have multiallelic reference entries, so the presence of a pair alone is insufficient to orient effects.

A separate audit matched all 111 score-by-trait entries against the three original project summary-statistic files by rsID, coordinates, and allele pair and found their stored weights numerically consistent with source BETA under the source A1-as-effect interpretation. FI and insomnia inputs were already harmonized; the original raw-study effect definitions and harmonization history still need author confirmation. The FFS source allele ordering was swapped relative to the score table for 10 of 15 and 22 of 22 rows; a numerical sign check does not resolve the source convention. The complete per-row audit and SHA-256 receipts are supplied; the large GWAS files remain on the original external drive.

No participant-level NHANES genotype matrix was available, so coverage of these markers and direct score validation could not be evaluated. The CDC genetic repository information sheet identifies 2011–2012 variants as forthcoming in its August 2025 update; stored specimens are not equivalent to available genotypes. Check current repository availability and obtain authorized genotype data and original effect-allele documentation before attempting a score analysis. The three source GWAS paths are recorded in `gwas_summary_source_receipts.json`.
