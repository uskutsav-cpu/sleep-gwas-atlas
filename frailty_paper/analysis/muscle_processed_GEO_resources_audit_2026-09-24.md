# Public processed human muscle single-cell resource audit

Audit date: 2026-09-25. This is a source-metadata audit only. No matrix or sequencing data were downloaded, and no cell-type/gene lookup was run because no frailty-specific project locus is eligible.

## Priority findings

1. **GSE268953 is the strongest newly verified directly usable processed multiome candidate.** The official [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE268953) lists 20 public sample records: GEX and ATAC records for five young and five aged biological replicates. The series and sample pages describe Cell Ranger ARC processed `barcodes.tsv.gz`, `features.tsv.gz`, and `matrix.mtx.gz` files; all 20 GEO MINiML processing records report hg38. The primary [Nature Communications article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61403-y) reports ten male donors (five aged 19–27, five aged 60–77) and 52,934 qualified nuclei. NCBI [BioProject PRJNA1119500](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/bioproject/PRJNA1119500) reports 1,877 MB total supplementary data. The article describes cell populations and senescence analyses, but a cell-level annotation-to-barcode file was not verified. This is a small, all-male age contrast with surgery indication confounded with age and no validated frailty measure. It is contextual tissue evidence, not genetic replication or a QTL resource.

2. **GSE167186 is the most directly relevant aging/frailty-adjacent processed snRNA candidate.** The official [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE167186) lists 17 snRNA sample titles: 11 `OldHM` samples (HM1, HM2, HM3, HM4, HM6, HM7, HM9, HM11, HM12, HM15, HM23; GSM5098737–GSM5098753) and six `YouHM` samples (HM5, HM8, HM10, HM13, HM21, HM22). This agrees with the primary [Aging article](https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.204435), which analyzes 17 independent biopsies as six young and 11 old. The snRNA subset is documented as an age contrast; no donor-level clinical frailty/sarcopenia classification for these 17 was identified. The parent bulk cohort contains sarcopenic groups, but those labels must not be assigned to the nuclei donors. GEO provides processed counts and filtered 10x H5 matrices; all 17 sample processing records report GRCh38. The primary article reports 143,051 nuclei transcriptomes after its quality control/preprocessing/alignment (93,406 old, 49,645 young) and seven broad clusters: type I and II fibers, FAPs, satellite, smooth muscle, endothelial, and immune cells. Later reanalyses report lower retained-cell counts under their own QC, so the counts are not interchangeable. NCBI reports 281 MB total supplementary files for the project. An official GEO [simplified metadata sheet](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE167186) and [MINiML sample archive](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE167nnn/GSE167186/miniml/GSE167186_family.xml.tgz) were inspected: donor ages are 19–22 for young and 70–90 for old. Sex and sample-level clinical frailty/sarcopenia fields are not listed in the GEO sample attributes. GEO gives OldHM23/GSM5098753 age 90, above the primary article’s reported 65–85 older-adult recruitment band; this conflict is preserved without silently changing the source value. The row-level crosswalk and source checksums are in `manifests/muscle_gse167186_snRNA_donor_crosswalk.tsv` and its provenance sidecar.

3. **GSE143704 is a useful secondary cell-reference atlas, not an aging contrast.** The official [GEO series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE143704) lists ten adult donor samples and public sample metadata (429.3 KB), normalized expression (2.1 GB), and raw count expression (38 MB). The [primary atlas article](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13395-020-00236-3) describes cell diversity across anatomically varied muscle samples. Numeric donor-age range and the exact cell-label-to-barcode crosswalk were not extracted in this audit, and the varied biopsy sites limit clean age comparisons.

The existing HLMA multimodal atlas remains the richest resource for age, fitness and frailty context, but its processed-object identity, per-file terms, coordinate build and archive-member integrity remain unresolved in the separate HLMA audit. The 2026 Gong study remains only an author-reported raw-data candidate at HRA009010; it has no verified processed matrix location. These resources are indexed separately so that public processed matrices are not conflated with listed portal paths or inaccessible raw data.

## GSE167186 sample-group crosswalk update (2026-09-25)

The official 9.9 KB sample sheet maps all 17 GEO accessions (GSM5098737–GSM5098753) to `OldHM`/`YouHM` labels and listed filtered 10x matrix filenames. The official 10.6 KB MINiML archive supplies a GEO group, age and tissue for each; all 17 accessions and labels cross-checked exactly. Ages are 19–22 for six young donors and 70–90 for 11 old donors. The crosswalk is `manifests/muscle_gse167186_snRNA_donor_crosswalk.tsv`; source snapshots and SHA-256 values are under `manifests/source_metadata/GSE167186/` and in `crosswalk.provenance.json`. GEO sample attributes do not list donor sex or clinical frailty/sarcopenia for these 17. OldHM23 is listed as age 90 in GEO, above the article’s 65–85 recruitment band; this source discrepancy remains unresolved. The broader study includes sarcopenic subjects in its bulk RNA-seq cohort; those categories were not assigned to snRNA donors. No expression files were downloaded.
## Decision and next step

Registered the three GEO resources in `manifests/muscle_single_cell_resource_index.tsv` and `manifests/targeted_resource_audit.tsv`. The GSE167186 and GSE268953 official sample-metadata archives are now registered as metadata-only acquisitions in `manifests/all_acquired_resources.tsv`; no expression matrices or sequence files were fetched. GSE268953’s 20-record donor-group/modality/build crosswalk and source archive checksum are recorded in `manifests/muscle_gse268953_sn_multiome_sample_crosswalk.tsv` and `manifests/source_metadata/GSE268953/`. Do not download the 1.9 GB GSE268953 or 2.1 GB GSE143704 expression matrices before a qualifying frozen locus exists. If an eligible locus emerges, first inspect only the required donor/cell-type matrix subset, verify cell annotations, genome/build or gene identifier semantics, checksum the files, and record reuse terms. Do not download raw FASTQs for this contextual lookup.

## GSE268953 donor-group, modality and build crosswalk (2026-09-25)

Downloaded and preserved the official 4,436-byte GEO MINiML archive (SHA-256 `02bdb147d2e1f8cf97c4461cba90269f44c8bbabc742b5534348877783e63cf1`). Parsed all 20 GEO records (GSM8303169–GSM8303188): five Young and five Aged biological replicates, each represented by distinct GEX and ATAC sample records. All 20 processing records list `Assembly: hg38`, Cell Ranger ARC v2.0.1, Seurat 5.0.1 and Signac 1.12.0; all list hamstring muscle, with the sample-level cell type only “muscle mononuclear cell.” Exact donor ages and sex are not reported per sample in the MINiML archive; the primary article’s 10-male-donor and age-band summary remains a study-level annotation, not an accession-level assignment. The paired-sample crosswalk is `manifests/muscle_gse268953_sn_multiome_sample_crosswalk.tsv` (20 unique GSMs; five donors per age group, two modalities per donor), with source and output hashes in `manifests/source_metadata/GSE268953/sample_crosswalk.provenance.json`. The 1.9 GB expression matrices and raw reads remain undownloaded; no barcode-level cell annotations were claimed. The primary [Nature Communications article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-61403-y) reports 12 publication-level clusters: MuSCs, FAP1/FAP2, EC1/EC2/EC3, pericytes, SMC1/SMC2, macrophages, B/T/NK, and a small number of mature skeletal muscle nuclei. The article-level cluster names do not establish a barcode-to-cell mapping for the deposited GEO matrices.
