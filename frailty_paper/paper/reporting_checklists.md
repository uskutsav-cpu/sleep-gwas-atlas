
## Provisional draft locators

Each checklist row now includes a current source-line locator in
`manuscript_page_line`. These ranges point to a provisional draft section (or
identify an absent section); they do not establish that the checklist item is
satisfied. The `status` and `remaining_action` fields remain authoritative for
completeness. Recompute and review locations after substantive manuscript
edits and again after final pagination; absent review-result, disclosure, and
reporting sections remain flagged rather than assigned invented page numbers.
