# Targeted resource source audit

Audit date: 2026-09-24. The row-level status and next action are in [`../manifests/targeted_resource_audit.tsv`](../manifests/targeted_resource_audit.tsv). This is a source audit, not a claim that resources have been downloaded or analyzed.

## Findings and decisions

- **Hospital Frailty Risk Score (HFRS):** the published FinnGen/UK Biobank study is a high-priority candidate. The publisher's supplementary workbook is now archived as a checksummed source and its first two tables provide reported genome-wide-significant HFRS and no-dementia hits with UKB and meta-analysis results. These are thresholded hit tables, not full summary statistics; exact custom-R12 genome-wide files remain unidentified. The reported UKB replication overlaps a project cohort source and is not independent replication. Finnish discovery may be more independent but still requires exact source, ancestry, and overlap checks. No web form was submitted.
- **Latent frailty factors:** the paper identifies eight Catalog accessions, GCST90624046 through GCST90624053, covering seven latent-factor GWAS and a pneumonia GWAS. At the initial page-only audit, the accession-to-phenotype map was unavailable and 050/052 were unresolved; the dated follow-up below supersedes that snapshot. Retrieved official Catalog YAMLs now provide the accession-specific labels and checksum-verified public TSVs.
- **Human muscle atlases:** the limb-muscle multimodal atlas is the better direct fitness/frailty context; its Nature article reports >387,000 combined cells/nuclei from ages 15–99 and distinct fitness/frailty levels, with processed data at the official HMA portal. A second intercostal atlas reports 90,902 cells and 92,259 nuclei from 17 donors (8 young, 9 aged) and exposes seven processed human H5AD objects. The object links and known/unknown metadata are indexed in [`../manifests/muscle_single_cell_resource_index.tsv`](../manifests/muscle_single_cell_resource_index.tsv). Neither atlas is acquired locally. Use for post-gate cell context only; do not treat either as independent GWAS or QTL evidence. The intercostal cohort has substantial hospital/ventilation context and no frailty measure was identified in the article text.
- **pQTL and eQTL context:** UKB-PPP and deCODE have published protein-QTL summary statistics and are registered in [`../manifests/pqtl_resource_index.tsv`](../manifests/pqtl_resource_index.tsv). UKB-PPP has substantial UK Biobank overlap with sleep/frailty inputs; deCODE is Icelandic and requires its own cohort-overlap check. These are molecular context, not frailty associations or independent replication. The deCODE 2023 download page lists a 19.7-TB combined resource and requires JavaScript in the current portal view. The AWS registry lists UKB-PPP as CC BY but marks its S3 resource Controlled Access; exact file-level routes, permissions and terms remain unverified. eQTL Catalogue has documented public tabix/full-file access; its R7 dataset-metadata index is acquired (758 rows), but no eQTL summary statistics are acquired. Use locus/assay-targeted queries only after eligible loci are frozen.
- **BrainSCOPE cell-type QTL context:** the official resource pages describe 388 adult DLPFC samples and link open cell-type-specific expression and QTL resources. The 388-row sample metadata file plus key-resource and eQTL link indexes are saved and checksummed. Sample records span 12 cohorts and include ancestry labels, age-at-death and disorder fields, but no sleep/frailty phenotype. No expression matrices, QTL archive, or cell-type summary statistics were downloaded. Treat these resources as optional functional context after eligible loci exist, not as independent frailty evidence.
- **FinnGen immune multiome:** FinnGen publicly released the CASCADE browser on 2026-08-13. Its public release page reports 27,294 genes and 297,024 chromatin peaks analyzed; current CASCADE browser v1.0.0/R12 metadata describes L0 PBMC + 8 L1 + 24 L2 labels and reports 20,829 cis-eGenes and 210,584 cis-caPeaks. The release page calls its 33 cell types 9 L1 + 24 L2; retain those source-specific labels because no crosswalk is provided. The 2025 preprint and current browser both report 119,094 fine-mapped variants, while enhancer/peak-gene link totals differ (496,488 in preprint v1; 593,765 in the browser) and remain unreconciled. These source-specific counts are documented in [`../analysis/finngen_cascade_release_metadata_recheck_2026-09-25.md`](../analysis/finngen_cascade_release_metadata_recheck_2026-09-25.md). The browser supports targeted locus, variant, gene and phenotype queries; bulk summaries remain form-and-email mediated. No form was submitted and no files were downloaded. With no eligible frailty loci, browser queries and downloads remain deferred; this is not a browser-access blocker.

## FinnGen release and HFRS endpoint check (2026-09-22)

The primary HFRS publication defines the score as a continuous sum of 109 weighted ICD-10 codes and reports a second GWAS with dementia removed. Its GWAS models include birth year, birth region, sex, and 10 PCs; the paper reports FinnGen discovery N=500,737 and UK Biobank replication N=407,463. The article's supplement labels the discovery data as FinnGen Release 12. The study N is specific to the HFRS analysis and should not be conflated with a release-wide participant count. The current official FinnGen access page lists both the R12 public release (2024-11-04) and R13 (2026-06-02; 500,186 participants and 2,755 endpoints). Current summary-statistics download instructions require an online form and emailed instructions. A full streaming scan of the official public R12 manifest found 2,469 entries and zero HFRS/frailty label matches; it searched manifest labels only and cannot rule out an opaque-named or separately delivered custom file (`../analysis/hfrs_r12_public_manifest_recheck_2026-09-24.md`). FinnGen standard R12 documentation describes 2,502 core endpoints/500,348 participants and a Regenie pipeline, while the paper describes its custom continuous HFRS analysis with SAIGE at N=500,737. The standard manifest therefore does not provide an identified equivalent HFRS endpoint. No R13 proxy or diagnosis endpoint is treated as equivalent.

The official FinnGen page records the CASCADE public release on 2026-08-13 and links the interactive browser. The browser methods page says its gene, variant, region and phenotype pages synthesize cell-type eQTL/caQTL, fine-mapping, peak-gene links and colocalization results. FinnGen separately requires a results-access form and emailed instructions for bulk summary-statistic downloads. These molecular results remain functional context, not independent frailty replication. No form was submitted and no summary files were downloaded. Use targeted browser queries only after eligible loci and downstream analysis gates exist.

## Latent-factor accession follow-up (2026-09-22; recheck supersedes the earlier Catalog snapshot)

The Nature Genetics data-availability statement confirms that GCST90624046–GCST90624053 comprise seven latent frailty-factor GWAS and a pneumonia GWAS. The paper names the factors as general frailty plus six residual constructs: limited social support, unhealthy lifestyle, multimorbidity, metabolic problems, poorer cognition and disability. A fresh read of current Catalog study pages/association rows identifies 046 as general frailty, 047 as Factor 1 (limited social support), 048 as Factor 2 (unhealthy lifestyle), 049 as Factor 3 (multimorbidity), and 051 as Factor 5 (poorer cognition); 053 is pneumonia. Checked the corresponding author analysis repository's public scripts for a post-publication accession crosswalk; the reviewed scripts do not provide one. The article's naming of Factors 4 (metabolic problems) and 6 (disability) does not identify which accession is which. The cited neighboring Zenodo record 15654249 is explicitly a software archive, described as the code repository release and containing one 88.4-kB ZIP (MD5 `772e939ccab2b63510d6e6d30bafab11`); it is not a missing GWAS data deposit. Do not assign the unresolved accessions by accession order, file size or sample-size coincidence. The required follow-up is an accession-specific authoritative record or a crosswalk from the authors/publisher.

Official GWAS Catalog FTP directory listings were checked for all eight accessions. Each contains a `.tsv` summary-statistics file plus a small metadata YAML, harmonised directory and checksum listing. The displayed rounded TSV sizes sum to approximately 5.0 GB (about 305 MB–1.0 GB per file). This verifies public file presence only: no summary-statistics bytes were downloaded or locally validated. Acquisition/use remains paused until the existing 20 GiB storage gate is met and file-level metadata, checksums, build, ancestry, sample size and license are checked. The paper's reporting summary cites Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15654248; the discoverable neighboring record 15654249 is an 88.4-kB software archive, not GWAS data.

### Source-structure follow-up (2026-09-22)

The bounded-memory audit in `../manifests/gwas_latent_deep_qc.tsv` found no invalid chromosome/position/allele fields across the eight accession files, and no adjacent exact variant-key duplicates. The seven factor files have nondecreasing numeric chromosome-position order. GCST90624048 reports per-variant `n=755864` throughout, one below its YAML study sample size of 755865; its expected-N semantics need checking against the study methods. GCST90624053 (pneumonia) has 1,045,468 numeric coordinate descents consistent with lexicographic ordering of the position strings. This is a file-order observation, not evidence of invalid variant coordinates; adjacent-key duplicate checks therefore do not establish global uniqueness in this file. The already-recorded 466,894 missing/non-finite beta/SE entries remain unfiltered.

The subsequent external-sort audit (`../manifests/gwas_latent_duplicate_audit.tsv`) compared the global key chromosome + numeric position + uppercase effect allele + uppercase other allele across every row. All eight accessions had zero duplicate key groups and zero excess rows, including the lexically ordered pneumonia file. This establishes uniqueness for that key definition only; rsID consistency, reference-allele orientation, strand compatibility, and harmonization have not been assessed. The audit used temporary sort files on the designated SSD, deleted after each accession.

## HLMA limb-muscle atlas metadata refinement (2026-09-23)

The primary Nature article and official Human Muscle Ageing Cell Atlas portal were checked directly. The study collected hindlimb biopsies from 31 participants (17 male, 14 female) in Spain and China: adults aged 15–46 years (n=12) and older adults aged 74–99 (n=19). The article reports 387,444 post-QC profiles from 22 individuals across modalities: 212,774 snRNA-seq nuclei, 79,649 scRNA-seq cells and 95,021 snATAC-seq nuclei. European participants had Barthel functional and Charlson comorbidity indices; individuals aged at least 84 included signs of sarcopenia. The official portal exposes processed data, sample metadata and a download route. The exact files/object sizes and checksums were not captured because the portal is dynamically rendered; no data were downloaded. The Nature article is CC BY 4.0, but repository-specific reuse terms were not established. The paper text does not identify the snATAC peak coordinate build. Both unknowns must be resolved before redistribution or variant-to-peak overlap. The atlas is the strongest currently indexed muscle cell-context resource, not independent GWAS/QTL evidence; defer locus queries until qualifying project loci are frozen.
