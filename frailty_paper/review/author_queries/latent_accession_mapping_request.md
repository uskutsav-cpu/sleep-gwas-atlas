**Subject:** Clarification of GWAS Catalog accessions GCST90624050 and GCST90624052

Dear authors,

I am auditing the public summary-statistics resources associated with your 2025 study of the multivariate genetic architecture of frailty. The current Catalog records I can inspect identify several accessions, but the records for GCST90624050 and GCST90624052 do not provide named traits or other study metadata.

Could you please confirm which of GCST90624050 and GCST90624052 corresponds to Factor 4 (metabolic problems) and which corresponds to Factor 6 (disability), or point me to an authoritative accession-to-factor crosswalk? I will not infer these labels from accession order or file size.

This would help ensure that any future use preserves the intended phenotype and coordinate interpretation.
