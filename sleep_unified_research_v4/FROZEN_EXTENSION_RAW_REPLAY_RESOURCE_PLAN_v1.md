# Frozen extension raw-source replay and resource plan

This historical provenance/reproduction task includes exactly the original 100 extension phenotypes, in locked panel order. It introduces no association, phenotype choice, release substitution, test-family change or new biological inference. Processed-input LDSC reproduction and this raw-to-processed replay have separate receipts.

Acquire the exact 100 versionId-pinned public Pan-UKB bodies in the original remote-object snapshot. Require original compressed size, original panel MD5, original S3 version and original snapshot ETag; compute SHA256. Total compressed network volume is 227610388647 bytes (approximately 212 GiB), largest body 2765036925 bytes. The versioned SSD namespace has approximately 972 GiB free at planning. Reserve 300 GiB for all bodies plus derivatives, logs and failure preservation. Retain exact bodies locally for reproducibility and never commit them. There is no tabix, full variant annotation, new phenotype or restricted dataset acquisition in this plan.

Use one sequential transfer, no automatic retries, immutable per-file receipts and before/after source hashes. A completed body may be skipped only after its sealed full hash and original identity are verified. Preserve partial/mismatched transfers and stop for explicit audit; do not admit them by filename. Maximum per-body transfer time is two hours and family wall-time is 96 hours. At the observed earlier single-transfer rate, the total may take roughly 20 hours; this is a planning estimate, not an ETA. Require >=300 GiB SSD at first family launch and keep the unchanged >=3 GiB internal / >=5 GiB SSD safety floors throughout. Monitor owned processes and observed aggregate RSS<=2 GiB. Network transfer contains no estimator and may overlap one historical estimator worker. Stop only owned processes on resource/identity/deadline failure.

After the original core, extension and validation stages finish, invoke unchanged historical `10_harmonize_panukbb.py` on each admitted local body, exact locked panel and archived provenance-matched Pan-UKB HM3 reference. This retains the original INFO>0.9 and<=1, MAF>0.01, allele, MHC, low-confidence, effective-N and P-floor semantics. Invoke the pinned historical LDSC munging command and reference alleles in new SSD outputs. One heavy worker and BLAS=1 apply. Do not write original core/extension directories.

Bind the exact script, reference, policy, panel and original streaming receipt identities in the operational pipeline plan before replay. Compare filter counts and full decompressed harmonized/munged records against available historical receipts and recovered processed files. Gzip timestamps/temporary names are serialization differences, not exact compressed-byte recovery. Record every full raw SHA/MD5, actual header/build/allele coding, QC count, native command, output hash and diagnostic. Compare content order as well as counts. Any source, harmonization or munging difference remains explicit and must be investigated before being attributed to an estimator.

The raw-source acquisition is admitted under the user's public-data research mission with this concrete resource plan. Harmonization/munging executor admission still requires its exact operational dependencies and independent numerical checks. This task does not resolve phenotype validity, cohort independence, covariance calibration, clinical interpretation or novelty.
