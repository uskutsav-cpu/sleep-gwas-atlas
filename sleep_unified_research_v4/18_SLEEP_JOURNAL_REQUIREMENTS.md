# SLEEP technical reporting preparation

The official SLEEP author-guidelines page was retrieved live on 2026-10-10 UTC: [Author guidelines](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/pages/author-guidelines). This file records technical requirements and research readiness only; it is not manuscript content or a submission checklist signed by the authors.

## Current journal requirements verified

SLEEP describes Original Articles as original sleep/circadian research and states no minimum or maximum length. It asks for an abstract of at most 250 words, with Study Objectives, Methods, Results, Conclusions and Keywords headings as the structured option, followed by 3–10 keywords. An Original Article also needs a Statement of Significance of at most 120 words.

The journal expects the applicable EQUATOR reporting guideline and a completed checklist at submission, with the selected reporting standard identified in the Methods. STROBE is given as an example for observational studies. The project’s STREGA crosswalk is a genetics-specific reporting aid; the official page does not name STREGA separately. The SLEEP-MR checklist, STROBE-MR checklist and SNP list are required only for an article that employs Mendelian randomization; the current study does not.

A data availability statement is required. Sharing underlying data and code is strongly encouraged where ethically feasible; code is required for work that depends on a new algorithm. If data or code cannot be shared for privacy, ethics or ownership reasons, authors must seek an editorial exemption and state the rationale. The journal requires authors to document ethics approval or give a full explanation, authorizing body and identifiers when approval was not required or an exemption was granted.

All authors’ relevant financial and nonfinancial conflicts, funding, authorship/contribution information, and related or overlapping publications must be handled truthfully. SLEEP requires disclosure of relevant AI use in both the cover letter and Methods or Acknowledgements; AI systems cannot be authors. Figure files are submitted separately; accepted formats include TIFF, EPS and PDF, with a minimum 300 dpi. Figure alt text is required. A graphical abstract is due no later than first revision.

SLEEP states it seeks high-impact, novel and methodologically rigorous work. Its examples of desk-rejection concerns include incremental science, lack of novelty, major methodological problems and limited depth of sleep measurement. This is a material scientific threshold for this project, not merely a formatting detail.
