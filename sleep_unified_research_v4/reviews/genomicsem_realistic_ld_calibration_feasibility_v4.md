## Decision

**Full realistic-LD calibration is not admitted.** Existing local assets are promising candidates, and a matrix-free representation can respect a 2 GiB worker limit in principle. Neither complete source compatibility nor a realistic population-LD generator has been established. The current guarded native executor also admits only 200-block deterministic controls; 100/400/shifted empirical arms need a separately reviewed executor and frozen manifests.

**A restricted 503-EUR source-factor simulation is scientifically executable as a named method qualification, after the pending source and generator checks below.** It supplies signed multivariate LD from actual genotypes, has an exact PSD Gram-factor model, and need not force independence at the jackknife boundaries. Its results would describe that finite-reference, Gaussian, restricted-universe model with the unchanged historical LD-score/weight/M inputs. It is not a replacement for the protocol's full realistic-LD scenarios and cannot open the biological 41-Cov release gate. No such simulation has run.

The original protocol's 8,000 primary replicates, acceptance margins, failure accounting, two distinct method arms, and partition sensitivities remain intact. If restricted qualifications are pursued, label them **S01–S04**, referencing the C01–C04 parameter structures, and keep their counts/results separate from unachieved full C01–C04 calibration. Do not rename a restricted success as full calibration.

## Proposed restricted generator and exact assumptions

Freeze J_S using **only** the raw genotype BIM, the hash-verified coordinate/allele map and consumed reference identities: exact ID/CHR/BP/allele-set intersections, ACGT biallelic/nonpalindromic records, and the original extended-MHC exclusion. Source allele flips are explicit signed transformations; ambiguous/conflicting records are failed/static exclusions recorded before simulation. Freeze the ordered SNP list/digest and row map after fresh BED verification. Do not use GWAS Z/P, actual native masks, new rg or successful deletions to choose J_S. The current probe counts support this recipe; they are not a sealed synthetic-input universe.

For each chromosome, decode the **same 503 samples** in bounded variant chunks. Center each selected SNP, impute missing dosage to its observed mean under a predeclared rule, and scale to `G_j'G_j/n0=1`; n0=503. Missingness limits and zero-variance handling must be fixed from reference QC before simulation, with any further exclusion explicitly versioning the universe. No variance floor or jitter. Define R=G'G/n0 within chromosome and zero between chromosomes. No additional mask/truncation is placed at canonical, LDetect or storage block boundaries.

For q<=4 traits, define Sigma_g from the frozen h2/rg structure and Sigma_e as the frozen **unit-diagonal GWAS-noise correlation** matrix. Draw independent standard normals U(m by q), H(n0 by q), with fixed keyed substreams for scenario/replicate/chromosome/trait role; beta=U*chol(Sigma_g)'/sqrt(m_total), epsilon=G'*H*chol(Sigma_e)'/sqrt(n0), and `Z_jt = sqrt(N_jt)*(R*beta)_jt + epsilon_jt`. A two-pass operator computes `v=G*beta/n0` and then `G'*v`, without forming R. Independent effects/noise are shared across the two pair fits exactly according to the frozen scenario; the shared sleep Z is the **same saved vector**, not an independent redraw. C03's rank-based missingness/N patterns reference the **whole historical master reference rank**, never the subset's renumbered rank; retain those ranks in J_S.

This defines a positive semidefinite **conditional-on-reference Gaussian summary-statistic model** with Cov(epsilon)=R tensor Sigma_e and Cov(signal)=(R^2) tensor Sigma_g/m_total. It is not simulation of 250,000 independent participant genotypes. In C03, rescaling the signal by variant N while holding this noise factor specifies a valid Gaussian experiment, but does not demonstrate a real cohort's variant-specific participant-overlap law. If physical nested-cohort overlap is wanted instead, prospectively freeze disjoint sample-component factors/weights and prove their joint PSD covariance; that is a different generator version, not an implicit correction after outcomes.

Specify the estimand before runs: this generator's integrated random-effect Sigma_g ratios are fixed at .30, whereas a finite draw's realized `beta'R beta` correlation can differ. Do not switch between conditional realized-effect truth and integrated model-parameter truth after seeing calibration. Empirical target/coverage calculations must match the chosen interpretation. Do not normalize effects to target realized h2/rg without a new documented generator, because that changes their distribution.

Keep the exact historical LD-score scalars, weight files and M for the S qualification; no source-generated replacements are spliced into the native path. The model's E(Z^2) depends on finite-reference R^2, not automatically on those historical unbiased/windowed L2 values. Source mismatch, finite-panel rank (<=502 after centering), and spurious chromosome-wide sample correlations are explicit stresses/limitations. A passing restricted S experiment cannot establish population LD decay, calibration for missing native rows, realistic LD-score estimation error, discovery selection, binary/liability transformations or conditional Schur models. A failing S experiment is recorded as failure of that stated model/estimator configuration, not evidence of biological heterogeneity or permission to choose a favorable subset/window.

The invented seven-sample fixture verifies the matrix-free/direct signal and noise identities, analytic joint covariance, signed allele flips, the four small PSD trait matrices and rejection of an indefinite matrix: **41 algebra checks pass**. It opens no source BED, performs no random replicate/fit, and establishes no empirical calibration.

## Resource feasibility and admission sequence

With m=1,290,028, dense double R requires 13,313,377,926,272 bytes (12.11 TiB); even a full m by 503 double genotype matrix needs 5,191,072,672 bytes. A 2,048-SNP by503 decoded double chunk needs only 8,241,152 bytes. For q<=4, full synthetic beta/Z arrays are tens of MB; keyed streaming can avoid storing all beta. Thus the factor **representation** is compatible with bounded memory, but total native parsing/regression RSS and throughput must be measured by a separately admitted one-worker pilot, with shared exclusive heavy lock, BLAS1, 2-GiB aggregate RSS and unchanged 3/5-GiB disk floors. No resource exception is inferred from a small algebra fixture.

8,000 draws reused across 200/100/400/shift partitions, two pair estimates and one/two-step methods imply **128,000 pair fits**, before audits/generation/summary bootstrap. At hypothetical 1/10/60 seconds per fit this is 1.48/14.8/88.9 days of serial fitting alone; these are arithmetic illustrations, not measured runtime predictions. Reduced/full masks and two-step filters must be checked for every fit; scheduled failures are retained, never replaced. Primary and sensitivity partition definitions/manifests must be frozen before the first outcome. Suggested result-free extension of the builder: Hamilton 1-per-chromosome +B-22 for B100/400 using the same static non-MHC source counts; shifted200 keeps its allocation and uses BP at floor((j+.5)*N/B_chr), rejects repeated cuts, and never splits BP ties. This is a **proposed rule requiring root review**, not an achieved partition sensitivity or mutation of the sealed primary200 manifest.

Storing every full weighted design or GWAS text for 128,000 fits is outside the current 8-GiB **16-control** cap. Future empirical preparation must declare its own output plan: retain every scheduled seed/status/full/deletion/diagnostic result with source/generator/partition hashes, retain prespecified independent-solve designs, and specify whether exact full input arrays are retained or reproducibly regenerated. A lower bound of ratio plus three h2/covariance delete arrays for all four partitions is about 921.6 MB; receipt/check arrays and full control designs add to that. Do not silently reinterpret the 16-control cap or preservation contract. Pilot resource/time failure produces INCOMPLETE, never PASS or a lowered quota.

Before any S simulation: fresh raw BED source hash and upstream provenance, reference dosage QC/universe seal, independent actual-source decode/dense-window/operator and allele-sign controls, exact truth/overlap semantics, source-only sensitivity manifests, reviewed generalized executor/generator, and explicit resource/storage admission. Numerical controls and all historical completion prerequisites are retained. These checks can be performed later under the shared lock; they have not been achieved here.

Before full C admission: a complete independently authenticated source/generator covering the **frozen required regression and causal universes**, or a prospectively reviewed full-model source definition; signed LD/allele/build/ancestry/sample eligibility; justified population LD/long-range dependence; matched score/weight/M interpretation and LD estimation error; unchanged frozen calibration quota/margins; complete result-independent validation. Capturing the actual native pair universes is necessary to assess coverage, but using them to choose favorable partitions is prohibited. Neither the missing-ID counts nor reference-only block occupancy decides that later fit gate.
