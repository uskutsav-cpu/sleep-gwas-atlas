## Archived S/V and failed-model implications

The original 45-trait S contains three negative eigenvalues, minimum `-0.00561344619486166`; its standardized correlation matrix has three negative eigenvalues, minimum `-0.0952280265983113`. Archived V is symmetric, finite and numerically positive definite, with minimum eigenvalue `8.41654092033112e-10` and absolute condition number `1457484301.65884`. These are hash-recorded original diagnostics retrieved here, not a new eigendecomposition. Absolute conditioning mixes trait units; a new small model must also inspect conditioning after diagonal normalization, numerical rank, eigenvalues and sensitivity of its actual contrast/solve. Positive definiteness alone does not certify valid sampling covariance.

The [pinned GenomicSEM source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenomicSEM/GenomicSEM/6b65ca5db39fdade08b0d811477be1cdd57b5039/R/ldsc.R) rescales V to V_Stand at local-source lines 521–532 without a ratio derivative or denominator jackknife. It also independently creates equal-row-count blocks after trait/pair SNP filtering (259–260; 402–403), then stacks slope pseudovalues (469). It automatically raises the block count above 18 traits and warns about dependency above 1,000. Thus its joint V requires block-alignment review in addition to the fixed-scale labeling issue. Coordinate sorting within each fit does not enforce common boundaries across fits.

The archived 42-trait odd/even exercise uses 903 covariance elements and 947 blocks per split. All ten frozen candidates are `NOT_VALIDATED`, all have negative observed residuals, and all retain the smoothing-change warning. The best-fitting salient six-factor candidate has CFI `0.96654834954593`, SRMR `0.0887840303563282`, and two negative residuals. The discovery-boundary variant still has a new negative Crohn residual. Their terminal status remains `NOT_APPLICABLE_NO_VALIDATED_MODEL`; factor GWAS and Q_SNP are not estimated. Chromosome splitting is a genomic architecture check, not participant-independent replication. Correlated split point estimates do not override failed model admissibility or uncertainty concerns.

Neither a units correction nor a smaller SE repairs these failures. A coherent liability rescaling uses `S*=D S D`, and `V*=T V T` for ordered covariance elements with `T_(ij)=D_ii D_jj`. Positive diagonal D preserves inertia; it cannot turn indefinite S into an admissible covariance matrix. Uncertainty in prevalence, effective-N conventions or phenotype ascertainment is not supplied by the old V. No smoothing, clipping, eigenvalue floor or diagonal-only replacement is approved as evidence recovery by this review.

## partialLDSC primary-source assessment

The paper describes Schur residualisation and a 200-block comparison of ordinary versus partial genetic correlation. It reports use of v0.1.1. This is established prior methodology; adjustment alone does not identify causal mediation. [Mounier et al. full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12873261/).

The pinned v0.1.1 tag resolves to `18b743baf609ac3898b00ee92f37783dc3b61347`. Its [exact R code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GEMINI-multimorbidity/partialLDSC/18b743baf609ac3898b00ee92f37783dc3b61347/R/partial_ldsc.R) computes local-source block starts independently from each retained `n.snps` (288–289; 426–427), after trait-specific removal and pairwise merges. It provides no common-boundary argument or jointly intersected SNP universe. Later it combines deletion rows by index to propagate ratio denominators (524), Schur terms (586), conditional denominators (625), and raw/partial sampling covariance (675). It therefore addresses denominator uncertainty algebraically but does **not** establish exact genomic alignment for this atlas. Default blocks cannot be treated as a ready covariance remedy. This assessment does not adjudicate every published result in that study.

The package admits one confounder only (55), reports observed-scale quantities, and relies on previously munged allele-compatible inputs; it is not an automatic BMI-plus-smoking implementation for the historical mixed-scale matrix. Its code and documentation are archived as small hashed forensic sources with a fetch receipt. No package was installed and no example GWAS was downloaded.

## Concrete executable method plan, still gated

The machine-readable companion `statistical_validation/genomicsem_covariance_admission_gates_v4.tsv` lists achieved versus pending gates. A methodological covariance experiment can be performed with existing pinned processed data and references after native execution releases the worker/resources. It does not require new dense raw downloads, all-158-trait SEM, a new clinical source, or redefinition of a historical testing family.

1. **Freeze scope and output identities before computation.** Separate (a) shared-estimator uncertainty for all 41 historically estimated source-matched comparisons; (b) a future small measurement or conditional model only if its question, source eligibility and novelty gates pass. Retain the 396/1,200 discovery families and the 217 validation-candidate denominator. Retain the historical zero-Cov calculations verbatim as diagnostics. Include unavailable and QC-ineligible candidates without treating missing estimates as zero. Do not select the seven positive-subset heterogeneity flags as the covariance experiment's test family.

2. **Build a reference-only common interval manifest.** Scan the same hashed 22 reference files sequentially and create a unique rsID→CHR/BP map plus master ordered reference IDs. Audit duplicate IDs, conflicting coordinates, coordinate ties, order and build. Proposed primary partition: 200 chromosome-contained intervals, at least one per chromosome, remaining interval counts allocated deterministically by reference SNP counts using largest remainders. Within chromosome split at quantiles of distinct reference BP groups, never split equal BP groups. Freeze one-based half-open endpoints, chromosome, block ID, full reference hash, reference SNP count and assignment digest. Require 200 nonempty master intervals or stop. Define 100- and 400-interval and deterministic shifted-boundary sensitivities in advance from the same reference alone. They diagnose dependence/balance; a favorable result cannot determine the chosen partition.

3. **Preserve each historical estimator's own SNP eligibility.** Apply its exact reference/weight merge, finite Z/N, allele compatibility, duplicate policy and configured filters, then join surviving rsIDs to the canonical intervals. Validate the existing genomic order and record exact ordered SNP digest; do not silently reorder the running historical estimator. Export block membership/counts, allele-flip audit, intercept choices, two-step status and any unmapped/conflicting rows. Never re-split filtered rows or subset blocks by index. Missing SNPs change counts inside fixed intervals, not interval identity. The bounded primary prototype requires every canonical block to contain rows in both full and relevant masked stages and fails before fitting otherwise. Predeclared severe imbalance/information loss must fail or route to a separately frozen sensitivity, not adaptive boundary repair or deletion of empty block indices.

4. **Use a small pinned-code adapter with independent verification.** For original pair rg, carry explicit genomic separators through `RG→Hsq/Gencov→LD_Score_Regression→IRWLS`; the existing IRWLS/jackknife layers accept separators but the upper layers do not. Preserve original point-estimation, weights, total M, N conventions, intercepts and default pair one-step control flow. The stock parser deliberately drops CHR/BP (`parse.py:170–171`), so use an audited external coordinate join. This is a separate covariance method even if full-data point estimates match. For two-step sensitivity, give both filtered first-stage and second-stage fits the same interval IDs, carry first-stage intercept uncertainty, and handle empty first-stage blocks explicitly; do not inherit trait-specific default first-stage quantiles. Do not patch the running original estimator.

5. **Recompute nonlinear estimands inside each deletion.** For A and B, obtain each pair's h2 numerators and covariance on the same canonical deletion event, then `r=c/sqrt(a*b)` using deletion-specific denominators. Combine aligned estimates as `p_b=B*theta_full-(B-1)*theta_(-b)` and `Cov(theta)=sampleCov(p_b)/B`. Compute the difference SE directly from paired difference pseudovalues and independently as `Var(A)+Var(B)-2Cov(A,B)`. For an admitted small conditional model, build `S_AA-S_AC solve(S_CC,S_CA)` separately for full data and every deletion, then standardize by its conditional diagonal. Positive h2, identified S_CC, positive conditional variances, finite deletions, appropriate matrix rank and stable unsmoothed S are required. Reject failure; do not suppress troublesome deletions. With q covariance elements, an empirical B-block V has rank at most B−1: a 200-block full 45-trait V cannot be invertible (q=1,035). Small prespecified 3–5-trait systems or direct two-estimate contrasts avoid that forced dimensionality problem.

6. **Freeze result-independent calibration and numerical acceptance before outcomes.** The proposed calibration uses LD-aware synthetic GWAS with known covariance and a common block structure, multiple h2/rg levels, unequal/variant-specific N, shared sources/overlap and differential missingness, including a zero true contrast. Independent additive block simulations check exact covariance algebra; they cannot alone certify LDSC's LD/reference/model assumptions. Compare repeated-sample empirical marginal variance, estimated covariance, contrast coverage, covariance-zero error and null rejection against the proposed method. Include weak-denominator, zero-residual, negative-variance and rank-failure fixtures and require fail-closed behavior. Use at least 2,000 replicates per frozen null setting where feasible; report Monte Carlo confidence intervals and freeze acceptance margins before observing them. Acceptance must assess variance/covariance error and 95% coverage, not just a non-significant calibration P value. Freeze feasible compute/time limits and record failed or incomplete scenarios; do not call an underpowered simulation pass. This calibration has **not been executed**.

7. **Verify two implementations, then unblind diagnostics.** Compare an explicit raw-row deletion/full-pipeline implementation against a fast sufficient-statistic implementation on synthetic data and prespecified small real controls. Verify points, block IDs, effective deletion SNP sets, h2/covariance/rg deletes, marginal variances and paired covariance. Fast stock LDSC holds final full-fit IRWLS weights for its deletion solves; fully re-estimating weights/Nbar per deletion is a different jackknife and must be compared and labeled, not silently equated. Include an identity comparison (same estimator twice: delta and SE(delta) zero), independent-source simulated control, and denominator-gradient checks. Proposed agreement tolerances and conditioning rejection limits must be frozen; high-level Schur agreement achieved here is only one small prerequisite. Release covariance-aware descriptive comparisons only after the full marginal/joint calibration passes. Stock marginal SE agreement is a regression check; it is not required equality for genuinely different canonical blocks.

`common_boundary_adapter_prototype_v4.py` now implements the result-free interface prototype: exact final optional Z-product filtering is AST-checked against the pinned `_rg`; context enters after that filter; Base regression supplies mask-specific interval counts to first-stage IRWLS and full-row counts to second-stage IRWLS, replacing incoming mapped/default index cuts. The adapter checks build, monotone genomic order, unique IDs, coordinate coverage and nonempty 200 blocks, restores original module bindings, and blocks native execution. Its fixture receipt passes five one-/two-stage calls and the optional final-filter fixture without importing native numerical libraries or fitting a regression. This is achieved interface scaffolding, not validation of native delete estimates. `COMMON_BOUNDARY_METHOD_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL_v4.md` freezes exact controls and proposed calibration limits before any of the 41 covariance outcomes.

The result-free membership recommendation derives all 41 exact comparison members solely from the frozen native job plan and source manifest, preserving its order and binding three input hashes per member. Two recommended native duplicate controls are the existing insomnia–BMI pilot identity and the first frozen validation member. Both await separate admission/execution. They check same-input zero contrast and stock-versus-canonical point consistency within each method arm, not independence of external cohorts or novel biology. The script uses no rg/SE/P values to select membership.

LD-score columns alone do not determine the signed LD matrix needed for realistic joint GWAS simulation. The inspected local reference folder contains partial LAVA UKB files, while the archived fine-mapping preflight records no complete verified LAVA seal. Neither is a source-matched, verified full LD covariance for this LDSC calibration. Reference-only coordinate reconstruction remains feasible now; realistic LD-aware calibration additionally needs an appropriately pinned signed LD/genotype asset or a scientifically justified equivalent simulation design. Artificial independent-block or distance-kernel simulations can check implementation but cannot waive that real model-calibration dependency. No large reference acquisition was undertaken during the active campaign.

8. **Preserve estimand and selection limits.** A common-SNP restriction may improve exact matching but changes the fitted SNP universe; give it a separate experiment ID and estimate/SE ledger, compare it with original-source estimates, and never overwrite original SEs. Canonical deletions estimate uncertainty around the new matched method, not retroactive certainty for rounded historical estimates. Genetic covariance of source-specific estimates can include shared genetic architecture as well as shared sampling noise; joint simulation must reflect the intended LDSC superpopulation/jackknife estimand. No covariance sign is assumed from study names or cross-trait intercepts. Existing discovery-selection and winner's-curse effects remain after a correct contrast SE: resulting heterogeneity is retrospective/descriptive unless an eligible independent prospective design is supplied. Equality failure/success does not establish phenotype equivalence, a causal pathway, or a difference in sleep biology.
