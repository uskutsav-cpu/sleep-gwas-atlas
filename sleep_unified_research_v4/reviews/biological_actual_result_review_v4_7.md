# Independent scientific interpretation of actual v4 results

Review date: 2026-10-09, America/Chicago. This review evaluates actual coefficients, stored uncertainty and actual sensitivities, rather than treating software verification as scientific evidence. Inputs are the 396-row core, 1,200-row extension, 41-row historical external-validation, 62-row sensitivity and 41-row covariance-zero diagnostic tables, the actual FinnGen insomnia h² result, and current phenotype/novelty summaries 07/08/10/16. No new statistical calculations, source searches, model fits or manuscript text were produced. The earlier v1 review remains separate.

**Scientific judgment: the actual data support a reproducible, heterogeneous pattern of global common-variant sharing. They do not yet establish a distinct new primary finding, independent two-trait replication, a sleep-specific mechanism or clinical actionability.** The lipid sensitivities provide useful scientific qualification; the FinnGen result establishes promising source feasibility under its assumptions. Essential raw-processing work remains unfinished, so this is not a package-completion verdict.

These are different constructs with different precision and selected assessment windows. They demonstrate why a generic objective-sleep factor or modality conclusion is premature. No direct correlated-estimate contrast is supplied; zero-crossing intervals cannot establish equivalence or biological absence.

## What the actual sensitivities change

The two-step lipid fits improve the recorded intercept gate in 35/36 pairs. Several substantive directions remain: insomnia–HDL is −0.1175 [−0.1494, −0.08574] and insomnia–triglycerides 0.1373 [0.1060, 0.1687]; snoring–HDL is −0.1687 [−0.2018, −0.1356]. This weakens the argument that every original lipid association is explained solely by the original elevated pairwise intercept. It does not establish freedom from confounding or authorize choosing the estimator because it yields more acceptable diagnostics.

**Marginal lipid support is not uniformly unchanged.** Device-duration–HDL moves from −0.04970 [−0.09545, −0.003959] to −0.02844 [−0.06430, 0.007423]. Its sensitivity interval includes zero. Sleep-apnea–HDL remains substantial, −0.2490 [−0.2922, −0.2057], but its outcome intercept remains 1.206697 above the retained 1.2 gate. These are descriptive sensitivity findings; frozen BH classifications and families remain unchanged, no new BH correction is supplied, and no calibrated between-fit difference P is established. The near-boundary HDL intercepts should not be described as uniformly robust source validity.

The binary N-scaling fits leave rg and its uncertainty essentially unchanged while changing the scale of h². Standalone melanoma h² Z remains 3.56819, below four, so this adjustment does not rescue its eligibility. A nominal long-sleep–melanoma interval below zero cannot promote the QC-failed row to a primary result. MS and melanoma h² rescaling demonstrates sensitivity to supplied N; it is not a new biological relationship or a certified population heritability.

The actual 41 estimates retain 23 qualified outcome-side positives, 18 concordant estimates below the locked 0.05/217 threshold, and shared original sleep inputs. Completed fully independent two-trait replication remains zero. Ordinary 95% intervals are pointwise, not 217-test simultaneous intervals: insomnia–cholelithiasis is 0.07534 [0.01831, 0.13238], yet P=0.009625 does not cross 0.05/217. ## Defensible allocation and claim readiness

The supplied actual evidence and current summaries establish no admitted new primary or local/molecular result. All ten latent-model candidates remain unvalidated with inadmissible residual behavior in the reported model assessment; they cannot explain this pattern as validated biological components. The prior comparison remains 15 substantially similar, six related and two unresolved historical positives, with no first-ever finding established. Reproduction adds confidence in the numerical resource, not priority over those prior findings.

One integrated, bounded sleep-genetics resource is defensible in principle, retaining the separate 396, 1,200 and 217 families and a visible separation of primary, sensitivity, outcome-side and unavailable results. Splitting overlapping screens into separate discovery papers would not supply novelty. The current evidence does not support a strong new SLEEP Original Article on mechanism, causality or clinical utility. A specialist resource/methods outlet may fit the eventual completed evidence better; investigator judgment is still required.

Human authoring around a new primary discovery is not cleared. A qualified resource interpretation requires completion of essential raw-processing/source work, statistical resolution or explicit qualification of the remaining lipid/source issues, sleep-clinician review of complaint/device/registry constructs, and current novelty review. Remaining essential work must not be replaced by optional models. This report identifies actual scientific content and its boundaries; it does not declare the research program finished.
