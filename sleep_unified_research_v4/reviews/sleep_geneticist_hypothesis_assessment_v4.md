# Independent sleep-geneticist review: primary-question admission, v4

Review date: 2026-10-09. This is a methods and research-design review, not manuscript text. No new project association was estimated, no phenotype was selected by its result, and no existing family or sealed v1–v3 evidence was changed.

**Decision: admit no new confirmatory primary question at present.** Joint adiposity and smoking residual sharing is the strongest conditional future lead of the three proposals. It still lacks a pinned smoking construct, one clinically justified outcome, defensible joint uncertainty, power evidence, and independent testing. Clinical insomnia transport is the most concrete near-term validation route, but does not automatically create a novel primary question. Objective/self-report contrasts remain useful measurement research; broad modality novelty is already constrained and independent device GWAS are not established. These rankings describe different purposes and cannot compensate for a failed mandatory gate.

The identifiers avoid ambiguity: Q1 corresponds to user candidate A and v1 hypothesis B; Q2 to user candidate B and v1 hypothesis A; Q3 to user candidate C and the independent clinical-validation route. The old v1 hypothesis C about previously unreported pairs remains a separate, unresolved priority question. The companion TSV records each gate and alternative failure reason.

## Q1: joint adiposity and smoking residual sharing

**Clinical question:** Does frequent insomnia share common-variant liability with one independently justified clinical disease after projecting out genetic liability represented jointly by adiposity and a precisely defined personal-smoking construct? COPD is a clinically coherent design example because both exposures are relevant; it is not admitted or selected here. Choosing a specific disease and explaining why it matters must precede its project estimate. A broad outcome-wide screen would be exploratory unless its family and purpose were frozen first.

For X = core frequent self-report insomnia, Y = the chosen disease, C = (BMI, smoking), and S the four-trait genetic covariance matrix, define

`R_XY = S_XY − S_XC S_CC^{-1} S_CY`, with the analogous residual variances `R_XX` and `R_YY`, and primary estimand `theta_Q1 = R_XY / sqrt(R_XX R_YY)`.

This is a linear projection in genetic covariance space. It is not a mediated fraction, exposure intervention effect, or evidence of an independent biological pathway. Unadjusted-minus-residual correlation can be a predeclared secondary contrast; it cannot quietly become a second primary after seeing attenuation. Validity requires positive and stable residual variances, an identified conditioning matrix, compatible scales/ancestry/LD reference, and uncertainty for all covariance and heritability terms. Near-collinearity or inadmissible residual variance is a failed estimator, not zero sharing.

BMI can be tied to an unadjusted adiposity GWAS after exact source confirmation. A smoking phenotype is not pinned. Initiation, lifetime smoking, cessation, nicotine dependence, tobacco-use disorder, and passive exposure are different liabilities. The extension's household-smoking construct excludes own smokers and cannot substitute for personal smoking. Covariate-adjusted snoring is likewise a different GWAS phenotype, not a residualized four-trait covariance.

Sampling variance must use a complete covariance matrix for `vech(S)` with the nonlinear derivative of theta, or genuinely common genomic deletion blocks across all inputs. Ordinary marginal SEs, fixed heritability denominators, and index-matched but genomically different blocks cannot establish this contrast. All sample intersections, including exposure cohorts, affect uncertainty; public meta-analysis labels often conceal UKB or 23andMe reuse. Statistical overlap correction and participant independence are separate requirements.

**Current admission:** NO_GO_CURRENT. An exact joint question is not proven already published, but its novelty is unresolved, not passed. Mounier's BMI-only analysis and Goodman sensitivities remove broad first-adjustment claims. Independent GWAS for all target constructs, a calibrated estimator, outcome-blind power planning, and a separate testing cohort remain necessary. A precise residual null would mean no detectable linear residual sharing at the selected resolution; it would not prove BMI/smoking explain all comorbidity.

## Q2: objective versus self-report sharing

**Clinical question:** Does the disease-sharing estimate differ for device-derived main-sleep-period duration and self-reported 24-hour duration? The defensible primary quantity is `theta_Q2 = rg(device duration, Y) − rg(self-reported duration, Y)` for one frozen disease target or explicitly frozen disease family. Its variance must include the covariance between both estimates, with uncertainty in their shared disease heritability and both sleep heritabilities.

The core Jones device GWAS has approximately 85,449 participants, while the Dashti continuous self-report source has approximately 446,118. Questionnaire duration includes naps; device duration sums sleep within the principal sleep period and uses a different scale/transformation. Devices were collected years later in a selected subset. Age, survival, participation, time period, reporting error, and construct differences are entangled. A difference is descriptive construct-dependent sharing, not an isolated effect of measurement modality. The devices are not polysomnography, and inferred REM/NREM phenotypes are not interchangeable with scored stages.

Morrison and multi-organ already compare objective/self-report architecture. Portas 2026 extends device constructs in about 80,013 UKB participants; a new label does not create an independent cohort. No adequately powered public non-UKB objective-duration GWAS and matching disease GWAS were established by this review. Smaller external cohorts supporting phenotypes or PRSs do not solve that requirement.

**Current admission:** NO_GO_CURRENT; lower future priority than Q1. Source availability for historical UKB contrasts is plausible, but phenotype comparability, direct joint uncertainty, prospective contrast power, source overlap, and independent reproduction remain unpassed. A wide null CI is inconclusive. Even a narrow contrast-null supports similar disease sharing for these constructs; it does not establish equivalence of their measurement, genetic effects, or clinical usefulness.

## Q3: independent clinically meaningful sharing

**Clinical question:** Does clinically ascertained insomnia in MVP show transport of previously frozen sleep–disease sharing to a FinnGen clinical outcome? The operational estimand is `theta_Q3 = rg(MVP clinical insomnia, FinnGen R13 Y)`; the frozen ten metadata-selected transport candidates remain inside the historical 217-candidate family. This review does not create a new ten-test correction or turn previously significant outcomes into a prospective primary. All ten must remain represented regardless of results or source-QC failures.

The v2 public GIA definition resolution is substantive: GCST90475826 → pha010373.1 → Phe_327_4.EUR.GIA, 78,566 cases and 329,572 controls; at least two mapped ICD instances for cases, zero for controls, without related-PheCode exclusions. The historical HARE-only definition objection has been resolved at the documented-definition level. Remaining distinct-day/interval/lookback/executable semantics must be described specifically. EHR insomnia lacks a demonstrated equivalence to chronic symptomatic insomnia with impairment or the core frequent UKB questionnaire symptom.

V3 verified source identity and retained 826,027 harmonized HM3 records from 19,703,815 source rows. It did not establish the upstream confidence-interval/effect conversion contract, source h2/intercept eligibility, pair power, or participant intersections. Those are the remaining gates; successful preprocessing alone is not pair admission. This assessment is a v3/v4 review snapshot: the parent native190 campaign may resolve estimator infrastructure separately without changing these source-specific facts.

MVP and FinnGen are distinct named cohorts from UKB/Pan-UKB discovery. Four intersections among discovery-sleep, discovery-outcome, validation-sleep, and validation-outcome require evidence or explicitly qualified unknown status. FinnGen was already used in historical external-outcome validation, so reuse cannot constitute two mutually independent validation waves. Clinical/symptom mismatch makes the initial label PHENOTYPE_SENSITIVITY or clinical transport; fully independent exact replication is not established. A cohort-distinct test may be useful with documented overlap limitations, but cannot be relabeled fully participant-independent merely because file names differ.

**Current admission:** conditional validation only; NO_GO_CURRENT as a new confirmatory primary. It is the nearest concrete source route, with clinically useful ascertainment assessment, but needs effect semantics, h2/intercept, prospective precision, and independent/overlap review. A nonsignificant result may reflect precision, ascertainment, health-care contact, sex/age structure, or real transport heterogeneity; QC exclusion is not a negative finding.

## Power, null interpretation, and a single-primary decision

Before selecting any primary, the professor must state a clinically motivated target and the smallest scientifically meaningful sharing or contrast on its own estimand scale. Genetic correlation is not a patient's risk change or intervention benefit. Clinical importance therefore needs an external disease rationale and a clearly stated research consequence, not a large P-value count or a generic correlation cutoff.

Plan power/precision using eligible source h2, realistic sample overlap, ascertainment and ancestry assumptions, conditioning stability, and a predeclared multiplicity rule. Under an adequately calibrated Wald approximation, the planning minimum detectable magnitude is roughly `(z_(1−alpha/2) + z_(desired power)) × SE_planning`; this is a planning relationship, not measured power. Use a range of plausible source uncertainties and effects fixed without screening candidate outcomes. Do not substitute h2-Z≥4, a positive historical result, or a published winner's effect for prospective power. Residualization can increase uncertainty despite large GWAS sample size. For the frozen transport work, the historical family and 0.05/217 criterion remain authoritative.

For a meaningful null, an appropriately calibrated CI must exclude the predefined meaningful effect or lie inside a prespecified equivalence interval; this requires its own declared inferential procedure. Otherwise report imprecision. A failed estimator, low source h2, or excessive intercept is INELIGIBLE, never null. Published prior evidence limits novelty even if our project estimate becomes significant.

Admit exactly one primary only if every mandatory gate passes: clinical target and exact estimand frozen; phenotype/source identities; calibrated joint uncertainty; acceptable source QC; prospective power/precision; overlap and independent-test classification; and a documented exact prior-art gap. No weighted ranking can override one failed gate. Preserve Q2/Q3 and the old latent-factor/local/molecular options as alternatives with their failure reasons. The earlier held-out broad factor failure is not repaired by choosing favorable indicators; local/molecular follow-up requires an eligible primary and remains conditional. If no question passes, the correct deliverable remains a qualified replication/transport analysis and an explicit no-new-primary decision.

The literature crosswalk distinguishes inherited full-text/full-supplement evidence, new live full primary text, indexed notices, abstract-only records, and retrieval failures. Search receipts retain primary URLs and dates. New Mounier bodies were hashed while read in memory and are not redistributed.
