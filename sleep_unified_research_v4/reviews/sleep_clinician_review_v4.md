## Clinical crosswalk and interpretation

`tables/sleep_clinician_core_crosswalk_v4.tsv` binds every core construct to its exact source, response/device definition, coding, time window, effect allele/units, sample counts, historical h²/SE/scale, ascertainment, overlap and remaining human question. The h² numbers are recovered rounded historical values, explicitly distinct from current native reproduction and from phenotype reliability coefficients. Liability- and observed-scale h² must not be compared as if they measured a common clinical construct.

The core UKB insomnia source measures usual initiation/maintenance complaints; diagnostic chronicity and impairment are not required. Duration covers a 24-hour day including naps, and short/long categories share the same normal-duration controls. Chronotype is preference; sleep midpoint is realized timing with the opposite increasing direction. Sleepiness is unintended dozing, while napping is behavior. Snoring is a participant's report of a close contact's complaint. These distinctions do not identify independent latent mechanisms. The official field help texts and source response version are carried in the table. [UKB field 1200](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1200), [field 1160](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1160), [field 1180](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1180), [field 1220](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1220), [field 1190](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1190), [field 1210](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1210).

Core wrist measures estimate main-period sleep and timing from movement. The recording occurs years after questionnaire baseline; transformed effects do not represent original hours, percentages or clock minutes. Quiet wake can be scored as sleep, and selected device participants overlap the UKB questionnaire cohort. Exact-source numerical test-retest reliability was not established. Differences in association significance cannot distinguish biology from ascertainment, timing, measurement error, h² or power. [Jones 2019 methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09576-1).

FinnGen R9 G6_SLEEPAPNO uses recorded G47.3/3472 with specified sleep-disorder control exclusions. It does not supply AHI, severity or universal polysomnographic confirmation. Rows overlap: that is neither a unique misclassification count nor proof that all those participants lack sleep apnea. Special outpatient HILMO is displayed separately from primary outpatient AVOHILMO, which the code table marks unused; this distinction prevents equating all outpatient events with primary-care ascertainment. Finnish clinical code-history and unique-case review remain necessary before a pure OSA interpretation.
