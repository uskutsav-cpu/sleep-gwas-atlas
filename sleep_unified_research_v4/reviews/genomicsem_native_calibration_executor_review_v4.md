Each of the stock Hsq1, Hsq2, and Gencov Base constructors receives the same final ordered pair context. The actual first-stage mask defines a separately recorded index map into that final context. The capture hook surrounds the exact `LstsqJackknifeFast` constructor call made after stock IRWLS finishes its weight updates. It saves the final weighted design and response without recomputing them. Per fit it also saves full-fit Nbar/M/M-total, the initial weight array returned by the original subclass method, the first-stage ordered row-index map, stock combined points/covariances/delete arrays, total and intercept deletions, and the actual two-step combination coefficient c plus both stage results. Pair N1/N2 and the raw reference design support an independently recomputed Nbar and c. The final pair ratio's numerator and denominator deletions and native pseudovalue covariance are retained at full precision. All arrays have identities, shapes, and dtypes; nonfinite arrays fail.

The independent arithmetic helper imports no LDSC implementation. For duplicate A of each of the eight combinations it solves every retained raw weighted-row design with `np.linalg.lstsq`, including all 200 deletions, checks rank, reconstructs the two-step intercept adjustment, and independently recomputes total scaling, ratio, pseudovalues, and covariance. It does not use LDSC block cross-product or deletion helpers. Point/delete tolerances are `1e-10 + 1e-8*abs(reference)` and covariance tolerances are `1e-14 + 1e-6*abs(reference)`, unchanged from the frozen protocol. Nonpositive individual deleted heritabilities or denominator failures stop the check.

Duplicate A/B controls compare ordered SNP identity, actual stage cuts, full weighted-row array hashes, marginal and ratio values/deletions, and paired covariance. The direct deleted contrast and the covariance-subtraction contrast must give delta and SE at most `1e-12`; no P value is assigned to the 0/0 identity. Default/canonical comparisons concern the uncorrected full point, total h2/covariance, and intercept within the same arm. Their jackknife corrections and variances are allowed to differ and are not incorrectly required to match.

The independent solve freezes the final full-fit weights and Nbar, exactly as the stock fast delete-block estimator does. Re-estimating weights or Nbar after each deletion would define a different estimator and is not silently substituted here. This arithmetic check also does not independently implement all stock IRWLS weight-update equations; retaining the exact pinned code and point controls addresses that narrower binding, while empirical sampling behavior remains a separate gate.
