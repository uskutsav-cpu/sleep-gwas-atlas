## Conditional CKB–BBJ route — admission screen, 2026-10-10

An outcome-blind design screen considered CKB broad self-reported snoring (GVP000023; 47,208 cases and 53,418 controls) paired with BBJ PheWeb Cholelithiasis (9,305 cases and 168,253 controls; ICD-10 K). The intended quantity would be East Asian genome-wide genetic correlation, interpreted as cohort-distinct transport of shared architecture. CKB's frequent-only “habitual snoring” is a distinct phenotype and must not be substituted after seeing results. CKB snoring is not an OSA diagnosis; BBJ cholelithiasis does not by itself resolve subcodes, severity, symptoms, or stone type.

**Current decision: NO-GO before source admission or pairwise analysis.** The live GVM record identifies PRJCA023790/GVP000023 as “Controlled access,” while the article describes deposited summary statistics. The available record lists GRCh37 and files but does not provide the versioned source contract/checksums needed for this analysis. BBJ's official phenotype/download pages give the case/control counts and broad ICD-10 K label; they do not establish exact coding fields, release checksums, or sufficient rg precision. CKB's published broad-snoring heritability does not supply the BBJ disease-side heritability/intercept or pairwise precision. Cohorts are distinct Chinese and Japanese biobanks, but individual-level overlap is not directly certified. No input was downloaded and no estimator or pair statistic was run.

Before reconsideration, an accountable source steward must resolve CKB access and permitted use; exact CKB and BBJ release/phenotype files and checksums must be sealed; build, alleles, SNP overlap and EAS LD compatibility must be checked; BBJ univariate heritability/intercept and prospective rg precision must pass a result-independent gate; then the one estimand, direction, and a new multiplicity rule must be frozen before pairwise inspection. Human sleep and hepatobiliary review is needed for terminology and clinical relevance. The existing 0.05/217 threshold belongs to the historical family and does not transfer automatically; preserving it would be a conservative choice, not an inherited requirement.

This is optional independent-validation work, not a new primary finding and not a publication-critical task while access and precision fail. Related genetic testing of sleep phenotypes including snoring and biliary outcomes already appears in a 2023 Research Square MR preprint, so the route cannot support “first genetic relationship” language. Source-review agents disclosed accidental local exposure to frozen pair rows during lookup; their source/clinical conclusions are feasibility-only and are not represented as blinded to the existing result. Primary sources: [CKB paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10924953/), [GVM accession](https://ngdc.cncb.ac.cn/gvm/getProjectDetail?Project=GVP000023), [BBJ phenotype](https://www.pheweb.jp/pheno/Cholelithiasis), [2023 preprint](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-2731162/v1).

## FinnGen R13 insomnia × MVP abdominal pain — source-pair qualification

The existing prospective matrix also lists FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA (51,643 cases; 446,273 controls) with MVP GCST90479148 abdominal pain (89,262 cases; 299,053 controls). The underlying MVP outcome series was already used in the historical 217-candidate external-validation family for insomnia–abdominal pain. This prevents overcounting it among independent replications; it does not change the completed 217 classifications or their frozen threshold.

## Large OSA meta-analysis source screen — independent-use ruling

The 2025 medRxiv OSA meta-analysis reports European summary statistics for 230,657 cases and 1,377,442 controls, and states that its METAL meta-analysis is available through GWAS Catalog. Its constituent sources include UK Biobank, FinnGen, MVP and MGB, together with All of Us, CLSA and Australian cohorts. The full meta therefore overlaps source cohorts used in this project and is not an independent OSA replication input for the existing UKB/FinnGen–Pan-UKB/MVP findings. The paper also reports putative OSA relationships with multisite chronic pain, which is related prior evidence for pain claims. It does not establish the exact OSA–abdominal-pain genetic-correlation estimand.

The paper's public access statement identifies the aggregate meta-analysis, while All of Us individual-level data are Controlled Tier and individual-cohort data require separate procedures. No cohort-excluded summary-statistics release was identified in this bounded screen. An independent subset cannot be assumed by subtracting source cohorts from aggregate results; no data or estimator was used. This route is **NOT_ADMITTED** for independent replication. Source: [García-Marín et al., medRxiv preprint](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.11.08.25339824).

## Additional conditional sleep source: MVP EHR-based OSA GWAS — 2026-10-10

A second concrete sleep-source lead is the single-cohort Million Veteran Program (MVP) OSA GWAS reported by Sofer et al. It analyzed 568,576 veterans; 91.3% were male and EHR-based OSA prevalence was 21.3%. The MAP phenotype combines ICD-9/ICD-10 codes, NLP-derived clinical concepts and record-note counts; it is not universal polysomnography. The paper reports BMI-adjusted and unadjusted results and states that full summary statistics are being deposited under MVP dbGaP accession phs001672. The current MVP access page says full summary statistics require a dbGaP application ([study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10065974/); [MVP access](https://www.mvp.va.gov/pwa/discover-mvp-data)).

The single-cohort MVP source is distinct from the project’s original FinnGen R9 OSA exposure, so it is a plausible sleep-side replacement if authorized. The aggregate MVP+FinnGen+MGB meta-analysis is not independent of FinnGen R9 and must not be substituted. The separate FinnGen R7 source used by the MVP paper is an earlier FinnGen release, not an independent cohort from R9. Even with MVP OSA, a qualifying test still needs an outcome source disjoint from both the new MVP sleep source and the original/history-selected outcome sources; no such frozen, adequately powered, novel pair is currently admitted. No dbGaP application was made, no data were accessed, and no estimator was run.

This expands the conditional-source inventory from one to two access-gated sleep GWAS leads (MVP and 23andMe). It does not change the count of completed independent two-trait replications (zero) or the current publication NO-GO.
