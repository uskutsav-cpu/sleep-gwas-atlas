# Machine-readable supplementary evidence

The native quantitative tables are complete for 396 core correlations, 1,200 extension correlations, 41 historical external-outcome correlations and their 158 standalone trait h2 estimates. Full-precision native values are the numerical source for current plots. Original reported rounded values are retained in separate historical tables; agreement with their printed precision is not exact recovery of unavailable historical unrounded estimates.

The independent 217-row classification retains 23 qualified external-outcome positives, 18 directional estimates, 17 QC/power-ineligible candidates and 159 candidates without eligible external data. Both traits have not been independently replicated. Missing or unestimated results are not biological nulls. The original 396- and 1,200-test BH families and validation threshold remain unchanged.

The 62-value sensitivity table includes original and sensitivity estimates, differences, marginal uncertainty, intercepts, sample-size conventions, warnings, exclusions and scope limits. The ten liability presentations retain original K values and describe transformations rather than certifying population variance fractions. The standalone baseline and sensitivity captures and all stock delete vectors remain on the SSD. Independent table arithmetic and seals are retained in this package.

`tables/supplementary_evidence_index_v4_1.tsv` records the exact file, SHA256, row count and purpose for each current table. It covers source/phenotype metadata, native estimates, original reported values, numerical comparisons, source-admissibility and claim gates, all 217 validation classes, source-design and overlap qualifications, lipid/liability/N QC, covariance validity, novelty, and clinical definitions. Rights, ethics, funding, contributions, related-publication and AI-use evidence remain in their separately reviewed ledgers; those ledgers will be included in the final consolidated supplement index.

Raw-to-estimator source reconstruction is still active. Ten core source pipelines are independently complete; the remaining 35 large-source pipelines are admitted and queued. The extension raw acquisition and FinnGen clinical-insomnia preprocessing are running. Thirteen original validation bodies have an independently reviewed and root-admitted acquisition plan, queued after the current single-transfer family. Their complete raw/filter/serialization replay has not yet been executed. These limitations remain distinct from the completed native estimator reproduction.

New primary, local, molecular, cohort-independent validation and calibrated measurement/covariance contrast evidence are not manufactured to fill supplement slots. Their admission decisions, explicit absences of eligible analyses, and failed/qualified experiments belong in the final machine-readable decision tables. The supplement index will receive an additive successor after the remaining admitted stages produce terminal receipts. No final completeness certification is claimed at this checkpoint.
