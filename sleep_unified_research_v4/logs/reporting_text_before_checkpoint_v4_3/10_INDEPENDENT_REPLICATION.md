The R13 clinical-insomnia work is governed by a source-feasibility protocol frozen before outcomes. It admits metadata, source preprocessing and one observed-scale h2 diagnostic after its prerequisite terminal producer passes. It admits no new disease pair, no substituted historical sleep source and no new replication test family. A successful diagnostic cannot supply clinical signoff or missing source fields.

The genetic-epidemiology design review separates four discovery/validation source intersections, prior-wave reuse, shared controls and relatives. Different cohort names do not establish participant disjointness. FinnGen R9 apnea and R13 insomnia cannot be treated as independent releases merely because their release labels differ. Validation using two FinnGen R13 outcomes would require within-validation overlap modeling as well as cross-stage qualification.
