# Source-aware harmonization crosswalk

Prepared 2026-10-10 from frozen local evidence. `02_source_harmonization.tsv` contains one record for each of the 12 sleep phenotype inputs in the construct dictionary. These are 10 source IDs and 10 archives: the three device phenotypes select different column families from the same GCST007803 archive. The table records source/version/member and archive versus selected-input hashes separately, construct and ascertainment, effect coding, ancestry/build, sample-size semantics, historical processing, QC counts, LDSC eligibility, overlap and rights. It supplies publication metadata with explicit qualifications; it does not release upstream summary-statistic bodies or admit a new source.

`UNKNOWN` means the inspected evidence does not establish the field. `NOT_APPLICABLE` identifies fields without a scientific meaning for that input, such as case/control counts for a quantitative trait. `ABSENT` is an observed missing source column, rather than missing documentation. No empty fields or substituted versions/populations are used. Counts labelled historical come from preserved QC and munging metadata; this pass did not scan the GWAS tables.

The transformations must remain visible when presenting the resource:

- Insomnia uses the exact public UKB-only Jansen release, excluding 23andMe. The harmonizer converts positive OR to log(OR) and retains source SE. The audited schema does not independently specify SE's formal scale. Stock LDSC derives signed Z from source P and the effect sign, rather than BETA/SE.
- Questionnaire duration retains hours including naps. The short/long tails retain published BOLT-LMM coefficients with the same 305,742 normal-duration controls. Their coefficients are not relabelled logistic log odds or clinical disorder effects. Chronotype retains the source's rescaled BOLT-LMM LOGOR/LOGOR_SE convention, with its documented mu*(1-mu) divisor.
- Sleepiness and napping retain numeric ordinal scales and their different constructs. Snoring retains its published BETA scale; logOR equivalence is not established. The sleepiness response-version issue remains unresolved.
- Device duration, efficiency and midpoint retain the exact `ACC_SLEEP_DUR_RAW_SIN`, `ACC_SLEEP_EFF_RAW_SIN` and `ACC_SLEEP_MIDP_RAW_SIN` transformed column families. Their effects cannot be read as hours, percent efficiency or clock minutes. Device main-period sleep and reported 24-hour sleep differ in definition, time of assessment and selected participants. A genetic contrast cannot identify separate effects of modality, reliability or selection.
- FinnGen R9 `G6_SLEEPAPNO` retains its hospital/cause-of-death coded endpoint and Finnish European ancestry. The ICD9 3472/3472A source-label anomaly and unique clinical contribution remain unresolved. A pure obstructive-apnea interpretation and universal sleep-test ascertainment are not established.

The historical harmonizer's ordered filter ledger is preserved in each row as JSON, including zero-removal steps. It removes non-rsIDs, non-SNP or equal alleles, A/T and C/G ambiguities, invalid P/BETA/SE, invalid or non-autosomal coordinates, nonmissing INFO outside (0.6,1], invalid nonmissing frequency or MAF<=0.01, the inclusive chr6:25–34 Mb MHC region, and later surviving duplicate IDs. It requires positive N at least 50% of its reference. The INFO and frequency threshold predicates allow missing values; this table does not silently strengthen them. FinnGen additionally reproduces the pinned hg38-to-hg19 point-liftover rule and explicit losses: 727,860 invalid coordinates, 102,969 unmapped points, zero multiple targets and 8,210 non-autosomal targets, with 54,564 reverse-strand allele complements.

All six binary inputs receive constant `N_eff=4/(1/Ncase+1/Ncontrol)` under the original pipeline. This includes insomnia: its released per-SNP N column is overwritten by that branch. Napping preserves source per-SNP N; the other five quantitative inputs receive configured constant N. These assigned values do not establish per-variant information or enrollment. Population-prevalence conventions used for historical liability presentation are recorded separately from sample counts and do not establish clinical disease prevalence.

Stock LDSC munging uses the pinned HapMap3 allele list, source-P-derived signed Z, its missingness/allele/frequency rules and N threshold `quantile90(N)/1.5`. INFO is omitted from the final harmonized schema. Therefore a stock message of zero INFO<=0.9 removals cannot certify an INFO>0.9 screen. Snoring and apnea have no per-variant INFO in their releases. Their source-level INFO qualification persists. Historical harmonization reconciles 161,962,898 input records to 79,259,222 retained records and 82,703,676 filter rejections across the 12 trait inputs; shared-archive rows are counted once per selected phenotype, not as independent source variants. The 14,074,718 nonmissing-N HM3 records are analysis-bearing counts from preserved logs. Each 1,217,311-row output is a complete left-joined template; missing template rows are not retained statistics. The table also records the smaller LDSC regression-SNP counts.

All 12 preserved native h² records are concordant at historical printed precision and pass Z>=4 and intercept<=1.2. This is qualified historical LDSC eligibility, not proof of construct equivalence, complete INFO/N validity, a newly completed raw-to-processed campaign or independent validation. Finnish ancestry versus external EUR LD remains unvalidated. UKB questionnaire/device overlap and relatives are not quantified; the shared tail controls are explicitly known. FinnGen R9 overlaps later releases; cross-enrollment with UKB/MVP remains unknown. No completed result is relabelled independent both-trait replication.

Rights fields reproduce the dated source-specific ledger, observed 2026-10-10. Jansen carries observed noncommercial/share-alike conditions; the Sleep Portal's analysis permissions do not establish underlying-file redistribution; Catalog defaults allow accession/owner exceptions; the inspected FinnGen pages do not provide a separate raw redistribution grant. The inherited raw-body publication restriction remains. This crosswalk does not contact source owners, grant permissions or replace accession-specific terms with CC0.
