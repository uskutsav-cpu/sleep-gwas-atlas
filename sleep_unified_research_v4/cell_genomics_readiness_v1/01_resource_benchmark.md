# Resource benchmark: sleep–phenome atlas

**Assessment date: 10 October 2026. Decision: qualified incremental resource contribution; no established first-of-kind resource or method claim.** This finite benchmark compares seven direct published/interactive resources and two upstream access resources. It does not use the historical 15/6/2 prior-art categories. Counts below describe datasets, estimands or table records, not interchangeable numbers of independent diseases. No estimates were generated.

## What this package actually provides

The inspected V4 numerical tables contain 396 core rows (12 × 33), 1,200 extension rows (12 × 100) and 41 historical external-outcome estimates. They include marginal LDSC rg, SE, P, 95% intervals, h²/intercept diagnostics, SNP overlap and original-family correction/classification fields. The 217-row availability/admissibility table distinguishes 23 threshold positives, 18 below-threshold directional results, 17 QC-ineligible candidates and 159 without eligible external data; all 217 have fully independent two-trait replication = False. The 62 completed sensitivity outputs are diagnostics, not a newly validated disease model.

The twelve constructs comprise eight UKB questionnaire traits, three later UKB actigraphy constructs and FinnGen R9 coded apnoea. The dictionary specifies coding, controls, effect columns and assessment window. Questionnaire duration reports total 24-hour sleep including naps; Jones device duration/efficiency/midpoint concern the algorithmic main sleep period and selected rank-normalized source columns. Short duration is ≤6 versus 7–8 hours; long duration is ≥9 versus the same 7–8-hour controls. The external table includes **one device-midpoint row, zero device-duration/efficiency rows**. No calibrated joint covariance or between-construct difference test is present. Source-specific raw replay/INFO/N/LD qualifications remain; native numerical concordance is not complete source reconstruction.

Evidence: V4 `tables/core_native_full_precision_rg_v4.tsv`, `extension_native_full_precision_rg_v4.tsv`, `validation_native_full_precision_rg_v4.tsv`, `independent_validation_admissibility217_v4_1.tsv`, `independent_sensitivity_62_full_precision_with_CI_v4_6.tsv`; integrated-package `manuscript/sleep_construct_source_dictionary_v1.tsv`. These are local research products. The [public project repository](https://github.com/uskutsav-cpu/sleep-gwas-atlas) exposes the historical pipeline; a stable public release of this exact current V4 package, DOI, query interface or API was not verified.

## Closest resources and capability differences

**Morrison 2024 SLEEP.** Six domains combine three GenomicSEM factors with three single indicators, using questionnaire and actigraphy inputs. Its CTG-VL screen covers 1,403 external traits per domain; 181 were selected for focused presentation. Public S10–S15 expose rg/SE/P and cohort/source metadata; figures show intervals. It already addresses multidimensional sleep and discusses questionnaire/device duration differences. Its factors and reverse-coded health orientation are different estimands from individual atlas traits. The inspected material did not establish a current factor-statistics/code release route. [Primary article and supplement landing page](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/47/2/zsad320/7477860).

**Goodman 2025 Communications Biology.** UKB N=413,904 yields an additive score plus five phenotypic PCs from five questionnaire items. The declared external family is 375 heritable Pan-UKB traits × six scores. SD24 displays 380 trait rows with six rg/P pairs and phenotype/sample/h² metadata; it does not expose SE columns. Its original SNP GWASs, PRS validation and locus follow-up exceed this atlas's association-only depth; those validation experiments should not be equated with independent replication of every disease rg. Statistics are declared public through the sleep portal; customized code is available on request. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-07514-0), [full primary text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11760956/).

**SleepChart 2026 Nature.** Its new UKB short <6/long >8 versus normal 6–8-hour GWASs and FinnGen **R9**/PGC disease screen differ from Dashti's selected duration categories and from this atlas's multiple constructs. SF5 represents the declared 527 × two new-source family; the inspected workbook has 1,054 labelled slots, 1,050 populated rg/SE/P estimates and four blank slots (D3_BLOODOTHER and AB1_VIRAL_NOS, both duration categories), plus legacy source blocks. Public organ/omics results, interactive pages, named Synapse GWAS entities and MIT-licensed GAM code already exist. [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10524-5), [portal](https://labs-laboratory.com/sleepchart/), [named GWAS deposit](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn68602065/wiki/633329), [code](https://github.com/anbai106/SleepChart). A July 2026 correction exists; portal GWAS pages still cite an under-revision 2025 title. These checks verify pages/entities, not full downloaded-body identity.

**Fan 2026 Communications Medicine.** Ten observed sleep traits and 623 imaging traits accompany 34 sleep GWAS datasets and a declared 113 datasets/50 disease-trait panel. SD2 distinguishes questionnaire/device and binary/ordinal sources; SD11 exposes rg/SE/P/FDR. The inspected SD11 has 4,046 populated rows with 34 sleep labels and 119 disease-source labels, so 113 must not be silently substituted for its table denominator. Jones and Dashti duration–COPD pairs are already included. Daytime dozing sources labelled “narcolepsy” require phenotype correction, not acceptance as diagnosed narcolepsy. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-01656-w), [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13458312/), [open abdominal-GWAS deposit, v1](https://zenodo.org/records/19460500). No custom-code release was verified in the inspected primary material.

**CTG-VL (Complex Traits Genetics Virtual Lab).** This is a distinct, broader post-GWAS workbench, not the Human GWAS ATLAS below. The current portal reports an evolving tool/data collection, including 1,190 MVP GWAS added for LDSC batch correlations in November 2024; that is an addition count, not the current total. Its 2019 platform report described over 1,000 public GWAS summaries and post-GWAS results for more than 1,500 traits. The portal documents LDSC and a broad downstream suite, while the current homepage requires Google login for new users and says uploaded GWAS are periodically deleted after six months. This breadth exceeds the atlas's association-only workflow. The atlas instead packages its fixed 12-construct × 133-outcome-source result configuration, frozen families and explicit validation availability; no exclusive usable-pair advantage has been measured. Exact overlapping sleep-pair coverage and reproducibility of the same prespecified query were not benchmarked here. [Current portal](https://vl.genoma.io/), [platform report](https://doi.org/10.1101/518027).

**Human GWAS ATLAS.** The current official page advertises release 3 **v20191115**, 4,756 GWAS datasets, last curation August 2019—not a 2026 updated catalogue. It already offers genetic-correlation comparison, full pairwise exports, original-source metadata, SNP/gene PheWAS and plot/JSON downloads. Its rg eligibility uses h² Z>2, whereas this package's policy uses ≥4; differing filters are not proof of better estimates. h² SE is documented; the full rg export's SE schema was not independently downloaded here. [Release page](https://atlas.ctglab.nl/), [methods/metadata](https://atlas.ctglab.nl/documentation), [reuse interface](https://atlas.ctglab.nl/multiGWAS).

**LD Hub.** The primary platform paper described a centralized lookup/database/test/share service built around LDSC. Its original inventory contained 963 public GWAS datasets from 36 consortia; after the paper's stated selection criteria, its worked screen included 173 traits, of which 18 were diseases. The test center automated h² and rg, returned QC/log outputs, and advised interpreting low-h²-Z rg cautiously (recommended h² Z>4); the paper also documents lookup and source-link/PMID features. Those are established provenance and invalid-estimate safeguards, so the atlas does not claim to introduce them. The historic LD Hub endpoint could not be verified as a live service in this assessment; the current OpenGWAS page says its database began as the underlying database for LD Hub, which is evidence of data-platform lineage, not proof that every historical LD Hub function or release remains available. No exact pair-level coverage comparison was possible. [Primary LD Hub paper](https://academic.oup.com/bioinformatics/article/33/2/272/2525718), [current OpenGWAS description](https://opengwas.io/about).

The **Sleep Disorder Knowledge Portal** is an upstream sleep-summary-statistics access point named by Morrison/Goodman, not a newly verified complete disease-rg atlas. [Its legacy address](https://sleepdisordergenetics.org/) redirects to [sleep.hugeamp.org](https://sleep.hugeamp.org/), whose current catalogue is JavaScript-only in this access session; no dataset inventory/release or failed availability claim is inferred. **Pan-UKB** already supplies public GWAS/phenotype/h²/QC/LD manifests, flat files/Hail formats and CC BY 4.0 results. Its official record documents 2022 updates and cites the 2025 paper. The project's “Pan-UK Biobank 2025 EUR” label must not be promoted into proof of a distinct 2025 regenerated data release. [Downloads and terms](https://pan.ukbb.broadinstitute.org/downloads/index.html), [dated update](https://pan.ukbb.broadinstitute.org/blog/2022/04/11/h2-qc-updated-sumstats/index.html).

## Exactly what is added—and its limit

1. **A specific curated combination:** individual symptom/preference/24-hour duration/tail traits, device duration/efficiency/midpoint and coded apnoea are exposed together against the fixed core and extension panels. No inspected comparator supplied this exact source-defined configuration. This is a combination-level addition, not proof any constituent pair is new or absent from every public dataset.
2. **A project-specific interpretation layer:** full-family numerical rows, literal construct/source definitions, exclusions, unavailable candidates and shared-sleep outcome-side validation are connected in one package. A reader can distinguish an unestimated/QC-ineligible candidate from a numerical null and a marginal estimate from an unperformed measurement contrast. Comparable metadata/QC/download practices already exist elsewhere; their use is good resource practice, not methodological innovation.
3. **A reproducible finite benchmark dataset:** these sources and exact testing families support reuse for marginal-association comparison. They do not currently support causal mechanisms, corrected reliability, clinical transport, calibrated heterogeneity or fully independent replication. More significant cells and small P values cannot establish unique utility.

The strongest defensible resource claim is therefore **a source-resolved, qualified marginal sleep–disease/trait panel that integrates the project's two fixed families and exposes evidence limits**. It is smaller and narrower than several existing screens and general post-GWAS workbenches, and does not yet demonstrate a user task that cannot be achieved by combining their public tables. A Cell Genomics resource argument is plausible for drafting only if this bounded utility is made concrete and the exact reusable package is released with durable versioning and permitted aggregate data; this benchmark does not establish editorial importance or submission readiness. No additional fits are required to reach this benchmark verdict.

Public access does not establish unrestricted redistribution. Goodman, SleepChart and Fan article materials carry CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 notices; SleepChart's separately licensed code is MIT, and Pan-UKB results are CC BY 4.0 subject to stated UKB terms. Original GWAS/cohort conditions still govern source reuse. Publisher supplement workbooks here are review caches, not proposed atlas release files. A reusable project release must state its own code/aggregate-output license and source attribution/conditions; no permission to republish protected source bodies or adapted third-party figures is certified by this review. [Goodman notice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-07514-0), [SleepChart notice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10524-5), [Fan notice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-01656-w), [SleepChart code license](https://github.com/anbai106/SleepChart), [Pan-UKB terms](https://pan.ukbb.broadinstitute.org/downloads/index.html).

## Verification boundary

Primary articles/official pages were checked live on the assessment date; the CTG-VL page and LD Hub primary paper/current OpenGWAS description were added to the comparison. Public supplement workbooks previously acquired from their publishers were inspected directly for schemas and coverage (Morrison S10–S15, Goodman SD24, SleepChart SF5, Fan SD2/SD4/SD11). Local table schemas and fixed-family counts were read directly. Cached supplement inspection is not a checksum certification of today's publisher binary. No SNP-body downloads, new estimates, access requests, manuscript changes or frozen-output edits were made. Current CTG-VL/LD Hub pair-level availability and cross-resource query concordance were not measured; those remain benchmark limits. The adjacent TSV records releases, scope, uncertainty, access, reuse and limits separately.
