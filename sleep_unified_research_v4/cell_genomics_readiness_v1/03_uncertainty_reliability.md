# Uncertainty and phenotype reliability: scientific release framework

**Release decision:** existing estimates support qualified, fit-conditional marginal uncertainty summaries. They do not identify phenotype measurement reliability or support calibrated tests of differences between fits. Release these three dimensions separately; neither a precise estimate nor reproducible execution clears the other two. This is a technical release framework based on existing actual results, not a new analysis or final scientific approval.

## What is supported now

| Evidence dimension | Supported representation | Current limit |
|---|---|---|
| Sampling uncertainty within a fit | Native h² or rg, its returned marginal SE, and stored nominal pointwise 95% CI where available; exact fitted scale, N convention and options | Conditional on the fitted LDSC model, reference, SNP eligibility, weights, intercept choices and source conventions. Arithmetic is independently verified; empirical finite-sample coverage and absence of systematic bias are not certified by reproduction. |
| Comparisons between fits | Each fit's marginal interval and a descriptively labeled point difference/sensitivity change | Joint sampling covariance is not calibrated. No confirmatory difference SE, CI, P, heterogeneity or equivalence claim. |
| Phenotype measurement reliability | Source-defined construct, instrument/coding, window, direction, selection and known ascertainment limitations | Exact-source test-retest/ICC/kappa or sensitivity/specificity is not identified by the GWAS summary statistics. Reliability coefficient and reliability-adjusted estimates remain missing. |

The actual native tables contain 396 core rg values, 1,200 extension rg values, 41 external-outcome rg values and 158 standalone h² estimates. Existing within-fit independent arithmetic checks support the returned marginal estimates and SEs. The completed sensitivity review covers 62 values, including 60 paired rg values and two standalone h² fits. These are separate from the older Genomic SEM standardized-covariance export: its fixed h² denominators do not propagate complete ratio uncertainty, and its indefinite S, V conditioning/block-alignment concerns and ten failed latent models do not supply a validated measurement model. Use the actual native rg uncertainty fields for their own fits; do not substitute V_Stand errors as complete rg errors. [Native/sensitivity evidence](../reviews/independent_sensitivity_whole62_review_v4_6.md); [Genomic SEM review](../reviews/genomicsem_specialist_independent_review_v4.md).

## Sampling intervals and thresholds

The stored rg intervals are nominal, pointwise normal intervals centered on the table's reported native `rg` field, using its within-fit jackknife SE. Preserve that point-estimator identity: `rg_jknife_bias_corrected` is a separate field and must not silently become the center of the original interval. Existing h² SEs can be displayed on their recorded fitted scale; a nominal interval uses the same estimate and scale. A transformed liability point/SE is conditional on the supplied sample prevalence, population K and N convention. Fixed positive scale factors propagate point and SE together; they do not propagate uncertainty in K, ascertainment, measurement error or N. This framework creates no additional intervals.

Do not clip an estimated interval to [−1,1] for rg or [0,1] for h², replace an undefined estimate with zero, or turn physical-boundary violations into plausible population parameters. Retain numeric results and flags with the appropriate interpretation hold. Historical MS liability h² 5.9823 and melanoma 3.7310 diagnose N/presentation problems; the controlled presentations below one do not certify population variance fractions. Melanoma h² Z remains 3.5682, below four, after N rescaling. The observed FinnGen R13 coded-insomnia diagnostic is h² 0.0535357591, SE 0.0040705199 and stored interval [0.0455576867,0.0615138315], under assumed constant effective N 185,146.7038. This is a necessary feasibility result, not source or population-heritability admission. [Actual FinnGen diagnostic](../statistical_validation/finngen_observed_h2_actual_diagnostic_v4_3_9.tsv); [independent interpretation](../reviews/independent_finngen_h2_actual_scientific_result_review_v4_3_9.md).

Retain the exact frozen decision rules and their purpose:

- h² Z ≥4 is a precision/eligibility diagnostic; h² intercept ≤1.2 is a retained point-estimate QC gate. For rg, inspect both **pair-specific** h² estimates/intercepts from the fitted SNP universe; standalone PASS is not a substitute. Passing these necessary gates does not prove absence of stratification, reference mismatch, INFO/N problems or selection bias. An intercept interval is sampling uncertainty, not a replacement gate or proof of no inflation.
- The 396- and 1,200-member BH families remain their complete historical discovery families. Show their family IDs, native full-precision q values and original classifications separately. Do not form a new BH family from the 36 lipid or 24 binary sensitivity rows, selected positives, or plotted rows. Calculated BH decisions are not individual probabilities that a finding is false or simultaneous confidence intervals; inferential control remains conditional on valid tests and the required dependence assumptions.
- External-outcome validation retains the prespecified P <0.05/217 threshold even though only 41 correlations were estimated. The 23 qualified positives and 18 additional directional estimates remain distinct from 17 QC/power-ineligible and 159 unavailable candidates. A pointwise 95% CI is not a 217-test simultaneous interval. Insomnia–cholelithiasis has rg 0.0753427, CI [0.0183064,0.1323789], P 0.0096245, yet fails the frozen threshold. A zero-crossing CI or absent estimate establishes neither equivalence nor a biological null.

The 41 validations reuse the original sleep GWAS: the insomnia–GCST90479148 estimate 0.4098272 [0.3447144,0.4749399] is a qualified external-outcome-side positive, not a fully independent two-trait replication. Finite, narrow CIs do not remove clinical/source or overlap qualifications. [Actual validation table](../tables/validation_native_full_precision_rg_v4.tsv); [217-member admissibility crosswalk](../tables/independent_validation_admissibility217_v4_1.tsv).

## Between-fit uncertainty stays separate

For estimates A and B, `Var(A−B)=SE_A²+SE_B²−2Cov(A,B)`. Marginal SEs alone do not identify the covariance. Shared sleep participants, reused outcome sources, overlapping releases, common reference/LD and selection can matter. Different default delete-vector row numbers do not certify common genomic deletions; matching SNP order alone does not make independently chosen one-/two-step blocks aligned. No current sensitivity or 41-pair comparison has achieved the joint calibration required for a biological contrast.

The current sensitivity evidence demonstrates the distinction. Device duration–HDL changes from −0.0497049 [−0.0954512,−0.0039585] to −0.0284383 [−0.0642993,0.0074226]. Both are marginal intervals; the second includes zero, but this is not a significance test of their difference. Apnea–HDL retains a sensitivity rg of −0.248954 [−0.292224,−0.205683] while the outcome intercept is 1.206697, still above the retained gate. Across the 36 lipid estimates, the both-intercepts gate improves from 0 to 35 passes, with one unresolved; this does not establish uniform source validity. The MS/melanoma rg rescaling is essentially unchanged at floating-point scale, yet changes h² scale and does not rescue melanoma eligibility. [Stored sensitivity values/intervals](../tables/independent_sensitivity_62_full_precision_with_CI_v4_6.tsv).

The preserved [41-pair Cov0 diagnostic](../tables/shared_sleep_cov0_diagnostic41_v4_1.tsv) explicitly sets covariance to zero. Its contrast SE/CI/P and historical seven annotations remain retrievable as **uncalibrated diagnostics**, not validated uncertainty or confirmed heterogeneity. Do not use CI overlap, a significance/non-significance difference, a zero-Cov P, or counts of significant rows as a cross-modality or replication-difference test. A future scientifically necessary comparison requires a prespecified question, eligible sources/constructs, denominator-aware joint uncertainty on genuinely aligned deletions or another justified joint model, and independent calibration/implementation evidence. Deferred finite-reference operator or duplicate controls alone cannot supply the actual 41 estimators' covariance. No covariance simulation or contrast is added here.

## Reliability requires phenotype evidence

The [12-source phenotype crosswalk](../tables/sleep_clinician_core_crosswalk_v4.tsv) records distinct measurements. Questionnaire insomnia is a frequent complaint item, not verified chronic insomnia disorder. Reported duration covers 24 hours including naps and whole-hour recall. Device duration is inferred sleep within a main sleep period; efficiency shares that numerator and period boundaries, and quiet wake can be scored as sleep. The device assessment covers up to seven wear days in a selected later subset, 2.8–9.7 years after baseline. Chronotype morning preference and a later main-period midpoint have different constructs and positive directions. Registry apnea depends on recognition/coding/health-care capture, not universal polysomnography; its source-specific code ambiguity remains qualified.

A larger h², smaller GWAS SE, stronger rg, objectively labeled modality or greater sample size is not a reliability coefficient. Low SNP heritability can coexist with reliable measurement; genetic correlation measures genetic components, not individual agreement or test-retest stability. Different constructs, environmental variance, transforms, N conventions, ascertainment and selected cohorts can all change these summaries. No attenuation or reliability correction is admissible from these data alone. Evidence sufficient for such a claim would identify the exact release/instrument, coefficient type, validation population, repeated assessments or reference standard, uncertainty and transport assumptions. Existing source-defined limitations are reportable; an absent coefficient stays `null`, not zero or one.

## Compact user-facing schema

Use one result card with seven clearly labeled groups: **source/construct**, **estimate and scale**, **pointwise sampling interval**, **frozen threshold and eligibility**, **descriptive sensitivity**, **comparison uncertainty**, and **measurement reliability**. A provenance link supplies exact table/row/field, fit identity and source-definition evidence. The accompanying [field dictionary](03_uncertainty_reliability_field_dictionary.tsv) specifies types, missing values, current field mappings and release rules; it is a proposed interface contract, not a newly populated result table.

Always display source/coding/window rather than a generic “sleep” label. Show the CI as “nominal pointwise sampling interval, conditional on this fit.” Show comparison status as “not calibrated” and current contrast CI/P as `null`; link Cov0 only behind its diagnostic qualification. Show reliability as “not identified from these summary statistics,” without a numeric score. Keep missing-source, QC-ineligible and nonfinite cases distinct. A source/pipeline PASS is reproducibility evidence, not evidence that a phenotype is reliable or a clinical/causal claim is valid. Preserve current source-replay completion states in their own ledgers, without treating a prepared plan as a completed result.

The release layer may expose qualified marginal estimates and source-defined limitations now. Confirmatory heterogeneity, modality superiority, reliability-adjusted h², new latent traits, population-liability interpretation and fully independent replication remain withheld until their separate scientific evidence exists. No original estimate, classification, manuscript section, controller or frozen input was modified.
