# Independent sleep-phenotyping adversarial review

Review date: 2026-10-08. Technical evidence review for Phase 10; no manuscript text, new association estimates, or confirmatory outcomes. The fixed 12-core definitions and 217 validation candidate identities were audited separately from source admission. Frozen labels and files remain unchanged. This is an automated review; it does not substitute for a human sleep specialist.

**Decision: qualified descriptive use of the exact core constructs is reasonable; exact clinical replication and a generic objective-versus-subjective measurement interpretation are not established.** Proposed MVP insomnia validation is clinical-to-symptom phenotype transport. The sleep-apnea code anomaly below requires human code-history review before a pure obstructive-sleep-apnea interpretation. Neither a positive nor a negative result would resolve these measurement issues.

## Core constructs and coding

The accompanying `sleep_phenotyping_v1_core.tsv` records all 12 trait/source identities, coding, effect columns, direction and clinical restrictions. The panel has eight participant questionnaire traits including the complaint-about-snoring item, three wrist-actigraphy traits and one registry diagnosis. Chronotype remains a distinct preference construct within the questionnaires. This grouping is descriptive and does not establish a latent clinical factor.

1. **Insomnia means frequent complaints.** UKB field 1200 contrasts “usually” having difficulty initiating or maintaining sleep with never/rarely or sometimes. It does not require chronicity, adequate sleep opportunity or daytime impairment. The selected public Jansen release is UKB only, not the article's larger UKB/23andMe meta-analysis. Interpret higher effects as complaint liability; avoid diagnostic-disorder or treatment claims. Fresh publisher access exposed the abstract/public-data statement, not full Jansen methods; exact source coding is supported by fixed audited metadata rather than a new full-methods reread. [Jansen et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-018-0333-3)

2. **Reported duration and its tails are selected categories.** Continuous duration concerns whole-hour reported sleep over 24 hours including naps. Short sleep is ≤6 hours against 7–8; long sleep is ≥9 against the same 7–8 controls, excluding the opposite tail. The source excludes implausible durations, missing answers and reported sleep medication; additional shift-work/illness exclusions belong to sensitivity analyses and should not be attributed to the primary release. Shared controls and a selected normal category do not define two independent mechanisms or a causal U-shaped risk curve. The released tail effects are linear mixed-model betas, not logistic sensitivity ORs. [Dashti et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-08917-4)

3. **The selected chronotype GWAS is binary.** GCST007565 has 252,287 morning versus 150,908 evening participants; unknown/uninformative answers are omitted. The article's primary ordinal encoding and larger meta-analysis are different estimands. Use the registered LOGOR/LOGOR_SE convention, with increasing morning preference. Neither this binary contrast nor an ordinal questionnaire is a measured circadian phase or a disorder of phase. [Jones et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-08259-7)

4. **Sleepiness and napping are ordinal questionnaire responses analyzed as continuous.** Sleepiness has four response levels; napping has never/rarely, sometimes and usually. Do not describe effects as minutes, nap duration, an Epworth score, MSLT sleep latency, hypersomnia or narcolepsy. Napping does not distinguish planned from involuntary naps. The napping source's sensitivity models and its 23andMe replication use different questions/covariates; they cannot silently replace the selected UKB no-BMI member. [Wang et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-11456-7), [Dashti et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-20585-3)

5. **Snoring is respondent-reported complaint status.** A participant answers whether a partner/relative/friend complains. The historical `PARTNER_OR_CLOSE_CONTACT_REPORT` label and rationale can imply direct proxy administration; qualify them in the new namespace. The item lacks frequency, acoustic measurement or apnea severity and can depend on cohabitation/recall. It is not a clinical apnea diagnosis. [Campos et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14625-1)

6. **Device constructs require exact names.** ACC_SLEEP_DUR is summed detected sleep within the main SPT window; it is not elapsed window length. Efficiency divides detected sleep by window length; midpoint uses the window boundaries. Episodes derive from sustained wrist-angle inactivity. All selected RAW_SIN effects are inverse-normal transformed. Collection occurs 2.8–9.7 years after baseline. [Jones et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09576-1)

   Accordingly, expand the ambiguous historical “SPT-window sleep duration” wording in new metadata to “summed estimated sleep time within the main SPT window.” Clinical interpretation requires caution about quiet wake and the shared duration/efficiency numerator. Raw effect differences are not hours or percentage points. Later midpoint and greater morningness point in opposite timing directions; direction alignment requires a declared orientation. Midpoint is neither L5/M10 timing nor melatonin phase. A disease contrast across these measures also varies collection time, nap inclusion, selection, sample size and ascertainment; it cannot isolate measurement modality alone. This is the reviewer's inference from the recorded constructs, not a conclusion that every contrast is invalid.

7. **FinnGen apnea is a coded registry endpoint.** G6_SLEEPAPNO uses G47.3/3472, hospital-discharge and death records; controls exclude G6_NARCOCATA/G6_SLEEPDISOTH. Broader primary-care-inclusive endpoints are different sources. The fixed counts are 38,998/336,659. Subtype, PSG severity and undiagnosed disease are not established by this definition. [Official R9 endpoint](https://r9.risteys.finngen.fi/endpoints/G6_SLEEPAPNO)

   **Unresolved code-table anomaly:** the official detailed table lists INPAT_ICD9(3472A), named “Cataplexy and narcolepsy,” with 1,574 case-code entries. Counts across codes can overlap. This is not proof that 1,574 unique cases are erroneous, but it directly prevents certifying a pure OSA phenotype without Finnish coding/history review. Retain the exact endpoint and qualify its interpretation; do not repair case membership from a summary table. [Official detailed code counts](https://r9.risteys.finngen.fi/table_case_counts/G6_SLEEPAPNO.html)

Liability prevalence conventions for duration tails, morningness and symptom complaints are analyzed-category approximations, not diagnostic prevalence. The apnea 5% convention comes from an external OSA study, which does not establish that this broad registry endpoint has that population prevalence. Sensitivity of liability-scale h2 to prevalence belongs in statistical review; genetic correlation does not become a clinical risk ratio.

## The 217 historical validation candidates

The result-free companion matrix retains every pair identity and historical match label: 37 EXACT, 21 COMPARABLE_WITH_DOCUMENTED_DIFFERENCES and 159 PENDING, representing 13 distinct external outcome sources. “EXACT” was copied from curated source configuration and mostly means a named/code concept match. It does not certify matched ICD/PheCode version, number of events, encounter source, observation period, case exclusions or control exclusions. Some stored discovery definitions explicitly say they are truncated. No source is certified here as identically ascertained.

The eight EXACT sources require ascertainment qualification: diaphragmatic hernia, reflux, cholelithiasis, COPD, gonarthrosis, intervertebral disc disorders, abdominal pain, and drug-allergy history. Z88 versus MVP PheCode 977 especially requires an explicit cross-taxonomy code map before an exact claim. Shared PheCode 785 for abdominal pain is stronger concept evidence but still does not establish identical healthcare capture or controls.

The five documented nonidentical mappings should remain qualified: arthrosis is broader than osteoarthrosis; gastroenteritis/colitis adds colitis; unspecified chronic bronchitis selects a subtype; smoking dependency is a narrower diagnosis proxy for tobacco use disorder; kidney/ureter calculus narrows urinary calculus. Smoking dependency cannot validate a smoking-initiation, heaviness or generic smoking-liability question merely through the label “smoking.” These findings follow the fixed source definitions; full endpoint-specific registry rules and Pan-UKB control implementations were not reconstructed here.

All 217 rows retain `YES_SAME_INPUT_PATH` for the sleep GWAS in the historical design. Even a genuinely equivalent external outcome therefore supplies an external-outcome check with the original sleep estimate, not independent sleep replication. Keep unavailable rows distinct from negative tests. Source/participant independence is separately adjudicated by the provenance reviewer, and the unchanged family denominator remains 217.

## Proposed MVP insomnia validation

The source review identifies GCST90475826 as EUR MVP PheCode 327.4, 78,566 cases/329,572 controls. It is EHR-diagnosis ascertainment, unlike the UKB frequency item. The authors' resource uses collated diagnosis codes and acknowledges limitations of automated PheCode phenotyping. The source reviewer records ≥2 mapped instances for cases and zero for controls; exact mapping version and exclusions remain unresolved. [Primary MVP resource](https://www.med.upenn.edu/syspharmatt/assets/user-content/documents/science.adj1182.pdf), [primary phenotype manifest](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/document.cgi?phd=8759&study_id=phs002453.v1.p1)

**Admit only as a related-phenotype transport test if the parent's result-free source gates pass.** The estimand must be named clinical-recorded insomnia liability versus frequent-complaint liability. Differences may reflect healthcare utilization, diagnosis/coding practice, symptom burden, age/sex or clinical comorbidity selection. Neither concordance nor a heterogeneity statistic proves phenotype equality, and failure cannot distinguish weaker power from construct mismatch. Freeze this classification before any pair outcomes. This review does not independently certify participant overlap, full-body QC or power and does not authorize a clinical intervention interpretation.

## Human sleep-specialist review questions and stopping rules

1. Does G6_SLEEPAPNO's Finnish ICD-9 3472/3472A rule genuinely admit narcolepsy/cataplexy records, or does its code-table label reflect a mapping artifact? What fraction also has G47.3? Until resolved, stop pure-OSA/subtype claims.
2. Does the MVP PheCode 327.4 implementation require adequate opportunity, chronic symptoms and impairment, or only diagnosis encounters? Which sleep-disorder/medication exclusions apply to cases and controls? Until documented, stop exact-insomnia-replication claims.
3. Is a binary morning/evening preference contrast clinically meaningful for the proposed question? Are neutral/unknown exclusions and later midpoint orientation acceptable? Require a signed construct rationale before combining timing traits.
4. Can duration tails against 7–8-hour controls answer the intended question without treating tail liability as a quantitative sleep deficit or causal optimum? Require separate tail definitions and correlated-estimate modeling.
5. Do quiet wake, short monitoring and shared efficiency/duration components permit the proposed latent component? Require measurement rationale and model-identification checks; reject a generic “objective sleep quality” factor solely from these three columns.
6. Are daytime sleepiness, voluntary napping and reported snoring sufficiently specific for each clinical claim? Reject diagnostic labels or severity/treatment conclusions that their questions cannot identify.
7. For each of 13 external outcomes, which complete diagnosis maps, encounter thresholds and control exclusions make the historical exact/comparable label defensible? Until supplied, stop stronger identical-phenotype wording; preserve descriptive concept-level qualification.

These are result-independent interpretation stops, not post-result opportunities to replace sources. Descriptive analysis of the pinned constructs can proceed under the parent's statistical/provenance gates. Any changed clinical question, factor definition or narrower endpoint needs a new declared protocol and novelty review.
