# Genetic provenance and cohort-independence adversarial review

Review date: 2026-10-08, America/Chicago. This technical report reviews source admissibility before new pair results; it does not provide manuscript text. It is separate from the parent's native input-hash and LDSC reproduction audit. Read-only review covered core source/schema/panel manifests, measurement modes, extension source/schema contracts, the frozen replication manifest/contract, existing publication provenance/rights/source-integrity evidence and the live SSD inventory.

## Findings that constrain interpretation

1. **The primary insomnia input is UKB only, N=386,533, not the 1,331,010-person published UKB+23andMe meta-analysis.** The selected CNCR release, phenotype counts and source header agree on this. The current [official CNCR page](https://cncr.nl/research/summary_statistics/) explicitly excludes 23andMe from its public 2019 summary statistics. The clinical-to-symptom distinction must accompany any new source choice: core cases answered “usually” to trouble falling asleep/waking at night; controls include “sometimes.” This is not a clinically diagnosed insomnia-disorder GWAS.

2. **Eleven of the twelve core sleep traits originate in UKB.** Subjective sleep, morningness, snoring and the three actigraphy traits therefore cannot be considered mutually independent cohorts. The objective sample is a UKB subset rather than a disjoint device cohort. All 100 extension outcomes are selected EUR Pan-UKB results, also creating within-discovery cohort sharing with those sleep GWAS. Cross-trait intercept estimation can address some overlap covariance under LDSC assumptions; it cannot prove zero overlap. Measurement mode is not a random intervention, and differences in sample size/phenotyping cannot be interpreted as biological differences from counts of significant associations.

3. **FinnGen R9 sleep apnea is the one non-UKB sleep source.** Its exact endpoint is G6_SLEEPAPNO, hospital/cause-of-death coded sleep apnea, N=38,998 cases +336,659 controls, GRCh38, beta/log-OR for alt. It is not a quantitative polysomnographic severity measure or verified exclusively obstructive sleep apnea. A newer FinnGen R13 sleep-apnea file shares the FinnGen population and cannot serve as independent replication of R9. The Finnish founder-population LD/reference fit must be evaluated in native QC; the EUR label alone does not certify a reference match. The locked population prevalence 0.05 is an external convention, not the cohort case fraction or a newly verified prevalence of the exact endpoint.

4. **Historical validation reuses the discovery sleep source.** This is established by both scripts passing `data/munged/$sleep.sumstats.gz` and the historical logs/audit, rather than by assuming that different outcome labels imply independent estimates. The frozen 217 candidate manifest contains 159 unavailable outcomes and 58 externally available-source entries, with 41 historically estimable and 17 QC-ineligible. Existing source curation puts the available sleep-apnea pairs with MVP outcomes, not FinnGen R13 outcomes. This avoids direct FinnGen R9 sleep × R13 outcome pairing in those historical pairs but still reuses exactly the sleep GWAS. The 23 historical qualifying positives retain the **qualified external outcome-side validation** interpretation; this review produces zero new independent results.

5. **Source-level independence and estimator independence are different checks.** For a proposed UKB insomnia × Pan-UKB discovery versus MVP insomnia × FinnGen outcome validation, both validation traits come from cohort-distinct resources by reported design. Exact person-level intersection remains unknown. For core FinnGen R9 apnea discovery, an R13 outcome in validation would share participants with discovery sleep even if the validation sleep GWAS were MVP. Check all four discovery-to-validation cohort intersections, rather than only same-trait discovery/validation comparisons. Within a new MVP × MVP validation pair, shared controls/participants require the bivariate LDSC overlap model, although the pair can remain cohort-distinct from a UKB discovery. Treat the old `NON_OVERLAPPING_CONFIRMED` metadata as evidence of cohort design, not participant-ID certification.

6. **The old zero-covariance effect-difference tests cannot become confirmed heterogeneity.** Reuse of the sleep source couples the discovery and outcome-side validation rg estimates. Existing seven nominal findings require reconstructed joint block-level covariance or a justified equivalent, which the parent/statistical reviewer is investigating. An ancestry label, a small cross-trait intercept, or a successful command cannot resolve this covariance problem.

7. **Hash scopes must remain distinct.** The upstream 2.5-GB gzip-wrapped accelerometer ZIP is not the extracted 10-column SSD `sleep_efficiency.txt.gz`, `accel_sleep_duration.txt.gz` or `sleep_timing.txt.gz`. Do not compare the public archive SHA-256 against an extracted canonical file and call the difference a source-version failure. Conversely, a hash of a provenance JSON receipt does not verify the body it describes. The parent's current full-file hash audit is authoritative for the live SSD bytes; the old publication audit's `UNAVAILABLE` markers are historical, not an assessment of the reattached SSD.

Read-only header inspection of SSD insomnia returned `SNP UNIQUE_ID CHR BP A1 A2 MAF OR SE P N INFO`; SSD FinnGen apnea returned `#chrom pos ref alt rsids nearest_genes pval mlogp beta sebeta af_alt af_alt_cases af_alt_controls`; the objective extracted files returned `SNP CHR BP A1 A2 FRQ BETA SE P INFO`. These match the expected canonical schemas. Header matching is only a source-schema check and does not replace full hashes, numeric validation or original preprocessing receipts.

## Result-free independent-source search

`provenance_v1_source_admissibility.tsv` contains fifteen candidates and blockers, including exact release/build/effect/N/access/overlap evidence, with no new rg values. Searches used current primary public resources: Catalog v2 disease/EFO queries; FinnGen R13 phenotype API and official manifest; CNCR; published primary articles; Yale's author-hosted sleep statistics; CHARGE/EAGLE publications; PGC's official results inventory; and All of Us official access documentation. Raw API/HEAD/header receipts are retained alongside the table. The legacy Catalog REST search path returned HTTP410, and Python's local trust store failed verification; successful requests used system curl with verified TLS and current v2 paths. TLS verification was not disabled. API failures were not interpreted as absence of data.

The most feasible source is **MVP EUR clinical insomnia GCST90475826**. The detailed source contract, complete 217-row proposed admission table and bounded approximately 549-MiB acquisition plan are in `provenance_v1_mvp_insomnia_priority.md`, `provenance_v1_mvp_insomnia_source_contract.json` and `provenance_v1_mvp_insomnia_217_admission.tsv`. It is conditional related-phenotype validation, not an exact swap of the original symptom source. A metadata-derived set of ten existing FinnGen outcome rows remains eligible for further preflight independent of previous significance; all original 217 rows and 0.05/217 must persist. No source was selected because it gave a favorable new correlation.

Other concrete routes:

- **FinnGen R13 F5_INSOMNIA:** exact public manifest N=51,643/446,273; generation-pinned full source and header verified. Clinical definitions differ from symptoms, and exact R13 phenotype rules remain unpinned. It shares FinnGen with R9 apnea and existing R13 outcomes, so independence claims must be pair-specific. The currently indexed unversioned Risteys page has different case counts and must not silently supply R13 counts or rules. [Official manifest/download documentation](https://finngen.gitbook.io/documentation/data-download), [schema](https://finngen.gitbook.io/documentation/data-description).
- **GERA additive insomnia GCST90086097:** Catalog verifies EUR 3,972/52,665, CC0 and a complete public file; exact header/MD5/size are retained. Catalog's “frequent insomnia symptoms” label does not justify claiming questionnaire equivalence: the [primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21952-4) uses GERA disease phenotyping with ICD-9-CM definitions. Exact insomnia coding remains a gate. The raw filename says GRCh37 while the source YAML assembly is blank; sentinel validation is required. N alone does not establish h2/power. Only the additive model is an ordinary additive-LDSC candidate; dominant/recessive/genotypic alternatives are not interchangeable.
- **MVP sleep apnea:** primary [dbGaP phenotype manifest](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/document.cgi?phd=8759&study_id=phs002453.v1.p1) distinguishes GCST90475824's EHR PheCode327.3 (152,031/278,027) from GCST90475257's survey DoApnea (79,571/236,097). Identical Catalog disease names conceal distinct ascertainment; never choose or swap these based on new results. R13 FinnGen outcomes would share the R9 discovery-sleep cohort, so that proposed paired design would fail full across-estimator independence.
- **CHARGE adult duration:** independent sleep-cohort rationale exists for N=47,180, but exact public downloadable full release/build/allele/N/rights were not verified; Catalog full-summary flag is false. LDHub availability is not equivalent to possessing an exact downloadable source. Some CHARGE component cohorts could overlap original core disease consortia, which requires full component review. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/mp2014133).
- **Published independent device validation:** the Jones2019 CoLaus/WhitehallII/Rotterdam sample totals 5,819, but a public dense all-SNP external GWAS file was not verified. Published lead-SNP validation is insufficient for a genome-wide rg. The released large UKB archive is already the core source. [Primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-019-09576-1).
- **Other independently measured duration cohorts:** HCHS/SOL and J-MICC are different cohort/ancestry strata, but the [primary 2023 paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10656946/) states their summary statistics are available upon request. No request/contact is authorized. EAGLE childhood duration changes the adult estimand and lacks a verified exact public full release.
- **MVP short/long duration:** the [author-hosted public EUR source](https://medicine.yale.edu/lab/gelernter/stats/) is a UKB+MVP meta-analysis, not verified MVP only. The [primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41249-y) uses ≤5/≥10-hour extremes and excludes reported OSA, unlike the locked <7/≥9-hour traits. A public URL and large N therefore do not establish independent, phenotype-compatible replication.
- **New 2026 objective GWAS:** [Portas2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71252-y) uses 80,013 UKB participants. It is a same-cohort measurement-method source, not independent objective replication. Inferred REM/NREM are model-derived from accelerometry, not directly recorded polysomnography.
- **PGC/All of Us:** no exact independent sleep dataset met all source gates in this bounded search. The [PGC inventory](https://pgc.unc.edu/for-researchers/download-results/) has research/attribution/no-cross-posting terms; a consortium label would still require a component-cohort check. [All of Us access](https://www.researchallofus.org/data-tools/workbench/) requires an authorized workbench for participant-level work; no current authorization or exact public dense device-GWAS source is established. This is not a claim that such research/data do not exist.

## Required disposition

Continue native reproduction with the parent's verified original inputs. Preserve all historical statistical families and outcome-side categories. A newly frozen clinical-insomnia transport experiment is scientifically feasible to preflight; it cannot establish exact symptom-phenotype replication by changing a source label. The review has not admitted a new confirmatory biological question or certified adequate power. Appropriate result-independent failure conditions include full-hash mismatch, unresolved effect/SE convention, inadequate shared SNPs, h2/intercept gate failure, phenotype transport not justified, or failed cohort-independence requirements. Report each explicitly and retain unavailable/null/failed classifications.

Human scientific review must decide clinical-to-symptom phenotype transport, whether ascertainment/severity/comorbidity differences materially change the estimand, UKB/core-consortium and cross-enrollment overlap assumptions, Finnish LD/reference suitability, sample/prevalence conventions for liability-scale h2, source-owner reuse rights, and whether any successful new validation supports a genuinely distinct finding. No mechanism, causal mediation, effector gene or clinical recommendation follows from this provenance review.
