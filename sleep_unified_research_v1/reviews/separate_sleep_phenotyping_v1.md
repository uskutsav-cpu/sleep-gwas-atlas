# Independent sleep phenotype adversarial review

Reviewer: separate_sleep_phenotyping_review. Review date: 2026-10-08 America/Chicago. Scope: locked 12-trait panel and proposed MVP GCST90475826 insomnia comparison. Research review only; no GWAS body download, pair-outcome inspection, native estimation, protected access or historical-output edits were performed.

**Verdict:** The panel provides several distinct sleep-related constructs, rather than interchangeable measurements of one sleep condition. MVP insomnia is a plausible clinical phenotype transport experiment, not exact replication of the locked UKB insomnia definition. No new independent biological validation is established by this metadata review.

The accompanying 13-row evidence table gives each locked definition, direction, window, effect convention, sample size, ancestry, build and qualification. Definitions were formed from fresh official field documentation, primary papers and the locked source/schema contracts. The prior sleep-phenotyping and novelty reports were not read. Contract-level GWAS allele/unit facts and some original sample counts were not independently rederived from full source files; those facts retain that boundary.

## Core phenotype challenges

UKB insomnia contrasts usual initiation/maintenance symptoms with never/rarely or sometimes symptoms. The field's help text points to the preceding four weeks when symptoms vary. It does not itself require chronicity, a minimum number of nights, daytime impairment, clinical diagnosis, or exclusion of another sleep disorder. Thus a clinical insomnia label needs qualification. [Official field 1200](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1200), [response coding](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/coding.cgi?id=100343).

Self-reported duration covers a full 24-hour day and includes naps. Short and long sleep are tails of that same report, with the same 7–8-hour control group; they are neither independent traits nor diagnoses of insufficient sleep and hypersomnia. Source preprocessing excluded reported sleep medication use and extreme duration responses. A new source using nocturnal duration, <=5 hours, or >=10 hours would change the phenotype. [Official field 1160](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1160), [Dashti primary methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6405943/).

The locked chronotype source is the UKB binary morning/evening analysis (403,195 participants), distinct from the paper's larger continuous/combined analyses. Higher values mean morningness, whereas higher sleep midpoint means later sleep timing. Preference and realized timing therefore require both construct and sign alignment. The source clock-time representation handles midnight wrapping. [Jones chronotype methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6351539/).

Sleepiness asks about unintended daytime dozing; napping asks about having a daytime nap. Both are ordered response categories fitted numerically, not measured minutes or equivalent continuous physiology. The sleepiness paper codes four categories, but the official field notes that “all of the time” was removed during initial assessment. This is a measurement-version question, not evidence that the archived GWAS is invalid. For napping, the locked UKB no-BMI member must remain separate from BMI-adjusted and 23andMe analyses. [Wang methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6692391/), [field 1220](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1220), [Dashti napping methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7876146/).

Snoring is answered by the participant about complaints from another person. The existing collection-mode label can misleadingly suggest that a partner supplied the response. The question does not quantify nightly frequency; “habitual” should not imply a measured frequency threshold. It cannot substitute for sleep apnea. [Campos methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7021827/), [field 1210](https://biobank.ndph.ox.ac.uk/ukb/field.cgi?id=1210).

Accelerometer duration sums detected sleep episodes within the main sleep window; efficiency divides that sum by elapsed window length, and midpoint locates the window in clock time. The selected effects are inverse-normal units. Recording occurred up to seven days, 2.8–9.7 years after baseline. These estimates are not PSG stages, physiological circadian phase, 24-hour duration including naps, or original-unit effects. Algorithm, elapsed time, participation and substantially smaller samples are alternative explanations for differences from questionnaire associations. [Jones accelerometer methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6451011/).

FinnGen R9 G6_SLEEPAPNO is a registry endpoint with G47.3/3472 rules and sleep-disorder exclusions in controls. It does not directly provide AHI or subtype. Its official case-code table labels ICD9 3472A as cataplexy/narcolepsy (1,574 code-related cases), while the endpoint is labeled sleep apnea. The table also displays outpatient counts despite the endpoint's rule display describing hospital/cause-of-death registries. These require Finnish clinical/coding review. Counts overlap and are not a count of uniquely misclassified GWAS cases; neither wholesale contamination nor an OSA-only phenotype is proven here. [R9 endpoint](https://r9.risteys.finngen.fi/endpoints/G6_SLEEPAPNO), [official code table](https://r9.risteys.finngen.fi/table_case_counts/G6_SLEEPAPNO.html).

## MVP transport boundary

Fresh Catalog v2 metadata independently confirms MVP, European-only ancestry, 78,566 cases and 329,572 controls. Fresh YAML confirms GRCh38, total 408,138, source MD5 and unharmonized status. [Catalog metadata](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gwas/rest/api/v2/studies/GCST90475826), [source YAML](https://ftp.ebi.ac.uk/pub/databases/gwas/summary_statistics/GCST90475001-GCST90476000/GCST90475826/GCST90475826.tsv.gz-meta.yaml).

The official MVP documentation distinguishes HARE and GIA analyses; its attached HARE methods define cases using at least two mapped ICD occurrences and controls using none. That is useful resource-level evidence, but this review could not inspect the GIA supplement or accession-specific ICD map. It must not silently certify their exact equivalence. Clinical recording also depends on health-care contact, and no coded diagnosis does not mean absence of symptoms. The primary paper cautions about standardized phenotyping and the older, predominantly male Veteran population. [MVP official documentation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/projects/gap/cgi-bin/GetPdf.cgi?id=phd008760.1), [primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12857194/).

MVP and UKB are distinct reported cohorts; individual cross-enrollment has not been excluded by phenotype metadata. Any eventual MVP–FinnGen comparison must retain explicit clinical-transport wording, separate source QC/power gates, exact outcome compatibility and cohort checks. Agreement would support transport across ascertainment systems. Disagreement could reflect ascertainment, sex/age mix, coding, power or sampling uncertainty, rather than falsifying a single identical phenotype.

## Required corrections and human decisions

1. Use locked recovered analysis_panel metadata for native runs. Provisional config/traits.tsv still has different FinnGen case/control counts, chronotype prevalence and additional unselected aliases. Preserve the old file but do not let it redefine the frozen panel.
2. Clarify snoring collection mode as participant-reported complaints from a close contact. Retain distinct symptom, behavior, preference, actigraphy and registry categories without treating them as independent latent biological dimensions.
3. A sleep clinician should judge which clinical claims survive the question definitions, especially insomnia chronicity, sleepiness versus fatigue, and sleep-apnea subtype.
4. A Finnish clinical coding expert should reconcile 3472/3472A labels, unique-case contribution and registry display versus extraction rules. No investigators were contacted.
5. A source specialist should confirm GCST90475826 GIA versus HARE identity, exact ICD map, repeated-code timing, single-code exclusions, observation window and sex/age composition before detailed clinical claims or new pair tests.
6. A measurement specialist should document the sleepiness response-option change, source GWAS coding and whether its time pattern matters for comparisons.
7. A statistician must use verified shared-estimator covariance and power-aware contrasts. Numbers of significant correlations cannot establish objective-versus-subjective biological differences; neither exact phenotype equivalence nor cohort independence can be inferred from matching labels.

Source retrieval failures are recorded in the receipt: urllib TLS verification failures were resolved using system curl without disabling certificate checks; the deprecated Catalog API returned 410, while v2 succeeded. The PMC supplement returned an HTML challenge and publisher supplement returned 403. No inaccessible supplement was treated as inspected.
