## Competitors that materially constrain the question

**Morrison 2024.** Three multivariate factors and three single indicators are distinguished; duration and reverse-coded non-insomnia are close univariate comparators. S10–S15 each contain 1,403 external-trait rows. Insomnia comparisons already include reflux, diaphragmatic hernia, COPD and disc disorders; signs need reversing from healthy coding. The article explicitly compares actigraphy with self-reported duration, including batch LDSC and molecular architecture. Thus “objective and subjective duration differ genetically” is already anticipated. A formal, prespecified covariance-aware disease contrast could remain an extension, but the broad concept is not novel. The accompanying comparative Figure DOCX returned HTML on attempted retrieval; numerical contents of that unavailable file were not recertified. [Primary study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10851865/).

**Goodman 2025.** Six composite scores derived from five self-reported traits are related to broad health outcomes. The declared phenome family is 375 heritable traits × six scores, despite 380 nonblank rows in SD24. BMI and smoking sensitivity models were already considered. Composite-score correlations and covariate-adjusted GWAS effects do not equal residual genetic covariance jointly conditional on smoking and adiposity. Nevertheless, a general claim that health liabilities differ across sleep components would be incremental. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-07514-0).

**Sleep Chart 2026.** SF5 contains 1,054 original short/long comparisons and selected displayed Dashti/Austin comparator blocks of 309/361 records. The Dashti block already pairs short and long sleep with FinnGen reflux, COPD and intervertebral endpoints. A later FinnGen release does not create first-priority science. Missing entries in the selected comparator blocks cannot establish that an association was never screened. Original-study duration cutoffs and sources must not be treated as identical to Dashti. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10524-5).

**Multi-organ 2026.** SD11 supplies objective and self-reported sleep–disease estimates alongside BMI-adjusted snoring/sleepiness constructs. It includes published COPD estimates for device and self-report duration and both BMI-adjusted/unadjusted snoring. These rows are not themselves formal difference tests. The article also models disease-conditional sleep–imaging relationships in Genomic SEM. Consequently, neither modality comparison, adiposity sensitivity, conditional decomposition nor colocalization is generally unexplored. Its source label “narcolepsy” includes the UKB daytime-dozing field; it must not automatically be equated to a clinical narcolepsy diagnosis. [Primary study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-01656-w).

**Additional prior art recovered by this review.** Morrison's online-2022/issue-2023 study already used conditional genetic regressions of sleep domains on psychiatric factors. Lawrence 2026 models 73 physical diagnoses across eight systems and a common medical factor; sleep disorders are included among its indicators, so importing that factor without inspecting sleep-content overlap risks circular interpretation. Portas 2026 uses 80,013 UKB participants for new device sleep constructs, not a cohort-disjoint replacement for Jones UKB. Its inferred REM/NREM constructs require measurement qualifications. [Conditional sleep study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10382696/), [physical illness components](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-69218-1), [device GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71252-y).

Zhang 2025's objective multidimensional profile, a 2026 multivariate poor-sleep-quality study and Chen 2026's proteomic atlas further weaken generic multidimensional or molecular novelty claims. Their complete numerical supplements were not independently audited here, so they are priority alerts rather than exact-pair certificates. Clinical objective-versus-subjective comparisons also exist and cannot be converted into genetic contrast evidence. [Objective sleep profile](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-62338-0), [poor-sleep-quality study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41613264/), [proteomic study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-026-03678-1), [2026 clinical comparison](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42454954/).

## Formal feasibility assessment

The matrix uses judgment scores 0–4: 0 unavailable or unsupported, 1 weak/blocked, 2 plausible but unverified, 3 supported, 4 strong. Scores are not empirical power calculations or acceptance probabilities. Mandatory gates are vetoes; no aggregate total compensates for a failed gate. Ranking prioritizes future admissibility work and does not use this project's historical significance pattern.

**B, rank 1: joint smoking/adiposity residual sharing.** Credible rationale: determine whether genetic sharing remains beyond two named liabilities. The exact smoking construct must be specified: initiation, heaviness, cessation and tobacco use disorder are different traits. For genetic covariance matrix S and covariates C, the residual cross-covariance is `S_sleep,disease − S_sleep,C S_C,C^−1 S_C,disease`; normalization also requires positive residual variances. Uncertainty must propagate the joint sampling covariance through this nonlinear calculation or a validated common-block jackknife. A separately adjusted GWAS is a different estimand and can introduce selection/collider biases. Conditional attenuation is not causal mediation. No current complete smoking/adiposity/disease system or independent conditional validation is admitted. The source reviewer found a promising MVP clinical-insomnia route, but its PheCode definition differs from UKB frequent symptoms and QC/power remain untested. This is a lead, not replication.

**A, rank 2: covariance-aware modality contrast.** Use a genuinely commensurate continuous-duration comparison, a fixed clinically selected outcome family and `Var(rg_device − rg_self) = Var_device + Var_self − 2Cov`. Joint covariance must include shared sleep/outcome samples and all denominator estimates. Counting significant correlations is invalid evidence of a difference. Even a valid genetic contrast reflects measurement, age at assessment, device selection, time window and population composition; the existing sources cannot identify a pure measurement effect. No eligible independent objective GWAS is verified. Thus neither novelty nor validation gate passes presently.

**D, rank 3: common versus specific clinical disease components.** This is defensible only with clinically coherent indicators, identified factors, positive residual variances and independent validation. The prior 42-trait odd/even-chromosome exercise failed its own held-out residual gates; it cannot supply a validated latent phenotype. Chromosome splitting addresses model stability within the same GWAS, not cohort independence. A smaller prespecified model might work, but it must not be selected by retaining outcomes that give desired sleep associations. Recent physical-illness modeling increases the novelty burden.

**E, rank 4: local/molecular architecture.** No specific new primary signal with eligible dense summary statistics, correctly oriented alleles, matched build, effective sample size, overlap model, signed genotype-derived LD and independently supported molecular test is admitted. Tissue/pathway enrichment or nearby genes cannot manufacture a mechanism. Do not select a locus because it rescues an otherwise incremental global screen. Locus-level work is downstream of an admissible scientific question.

**C, rank 5: relationships absent from multivariate screens.** Absence from a selected table, prior nonsignificance or a different composite's null result cannot establish first discovery. A diluted composite and a stronger univariate estimate are not a formal contrast without joint uncertainty and phenotype comparability. Two unresolved historical pairs remain unresolved. Selecting them because they currently lack identified comparators would be retrospective result-driven selection. This is not an admissible primary rationale.

## Result-independent stopping and falsification rules

These are admissibility recommendations, not a frozen new confirmatory protocol or replacements for historical thresholds. Before new outcomes are inspected, fix exact sleep/outcome/covariate sources, clinical selection rationale, estimand, joint covariance estimator, complete test family and correction, negative controls, independent validation design, and a scientifically justified minimum effect/contrast. Assess power from sample sizes, heritability and planned estimator uncertainty rather than observed winner effects; an a priori 80% planning target is a possible new protocol criterion requiring investigator agreement.

Stop confirmatory admission if any required input is unavailable/incompatible, overlap assumptions cannot be supported, residual variance or estimation is unstable, the full prespecified family is underpowered, or a compatible independent test cannot be frozen. Retain failures and null outcomes. Do not change constructs, remove diseases, add model residual paths or retune local regions after outcome inspection to obtain success. Successful native code execution does not waive these gates.

Adversarial challenges remain: (1) already-published coding-aligned disease estimates; (2) broad medical factors containing sleep diagnoses; (3) correlated UKB sleep and disease sources; (4) reused FinnGen releases; (5) source-dependent heritability/intercepts and unstable covariance; (6) measurement/selection differences mistaken for modality biology; (7) power-induced differences in significance; and (8) adjusted GWAS or conditional covariance interpreted causally. No hypothesis passed all these challenges in the material reviewed.

## Human review and publication allocation

A sleep phenotyping specialist must approve equivalence across symptoms, clinical diagnoses and device constructs. A statistical geneticist must approve joint covariance propagation, source overlap, residual stability and outcome-family/power rules. Investigators must determine whether the exact B estimand changes interpretation enough to merit a new study after a source-admissibility review. Literature specialists should complete the unavailable García-Marín and newly identified supplements and check aliases before any priority claim.

The core and extension can form one transparently integrated numerical resource, with their 396- and 1,200-test families kept separate. The current evidence does not establish an important new advance for SLEEP, nor justify two papers by splitting overlapping screens. A specialist statistical-genetics or sleep resource outlet may be more suitable after native/source validation. This is an automated scientific assessment, not a journal decision. The present package is **not cleared for human authoring around a claimed novel SLEEP discovery**; a qualified resource handoff is a different decision requiring investigators.
