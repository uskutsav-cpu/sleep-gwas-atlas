# Shared-estimator covariance audit

Original validation reused the sleep GWAS. The diagnostic difference variance was SE(discovery)^2+SE(validation)^2, which assumes zero covariance. A valid contrast requires subtracting 2*Cov(discovery,validation). The covariance is not known merely from the study names, cross-trait intercept, or an arbitrary grid.

The original historical set contains 41 estimated pairs. Sixteen have nominal zero-covariance heterogeneity P<0.05; seven of these are among the 23 threshold-positive validation rows. Thus seven is a selected-positive-subset count, not the number across all 41. Discovery and validation selection/winner's curse further prevent interpreting these as calibrated confirmations of heterogeneity. No multiplicity or selection correction is silently introduced into the historical outputs.

Native LDSC now successfully exports 200 block-delete estimates for the core pilot. These reproduce its own ratio-jackknife marginal SE. They omit genomic boundary coordinates, and different outcome intersections yield different index-based deletion blocks. Appending vectors by block number is not sufficient alignment evidence. The original core S/V does not contain the Pan-UKB extension/FinnGen validation sources.

A distinct numerical issue exists in the core multivariable export: GenomicSEM V_Stand rescales covariance uncertainty with h2 diagonals held fixed. The older export labels its diagonal square roots as full genetic-correlation SEs. Independent review finds the fixed-denominator identity across all 1,035 exported rows; diagonal rg=1 still has positive SE. A separate delta-method diagnostic propagates denominator uncertainty from S/V and materially differs for some correlated pairs, but that diagnostic remains conditional on the original high-block-count/ill-conditioned V and is not a validated covariance correction for this extension.

Current status: **covariance not calibrated; heterogeneity remains a qualified zero-covariance diagnostic**. A new admitted covariance experiment must share verified genomic boundaries across the relevant GWAS, validate marginal and contrast uncertainty, include numerical calibration and independent implementation agreement, and preserve the original estimator versus any common-SNP/method change as separate experiments. No new covariance estimate or confirmed heterogeneity is claimed here. See `reviews/statistical_validity_v1.md` and both independent numerical reviews.
